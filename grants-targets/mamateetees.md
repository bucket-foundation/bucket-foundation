# MamaTeeTees — Grant Targets

**Venture profile:** Registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. West Africa (Cameroon-region) education + housing programs. **Has its own c3 status — does NOT need Bucket as fiscal sponsor.** This is the venture with the cleanest legal posture and the broadest grant universe.

The grant story: girls' education + community housing in West Africa is one of the most heavily-funded philanthropic verticals globally. The constraint is not opportunity volume — it's relationship-based access. **Most of these foundations are invitation-only or referral-only**; the work is cultivation, not application.

## Top 3 Picks

1. **GlobalGiving Accelerator (immediate, $5K–$25K + permanent platform).** Quarterly cycle, open to any c3, low-friction. The real prize isn't the cash — it's permanent placement on the GlobalGiving platform, which becomes a recurring revenue channel and credibility marker for every other application after this. **Should be the #1 priority this quarter.**
2. **USAID small grants / Cameroon mission RFAs.** Federal money is real money. Requires SAM.gov registration and active monitoring of Grants.gov + USAID Cameroon mission solicitations. Even one $250K small grant transforms operating capacity. The bottleneck is solicitation timing, not eligibility.
3. **Together Women Rise (formerly Dining for Women).** Annual featured-grantee cycle ($35K–$50K), explicitly funds women/girls programming in the Global South. Cameroon is in scope. Application is process-driven, not relationship-driven — meritocratic.

## Pre-Application Prep Needed

- **SAM.gov registration** — required for USAID. 4–8 weeks. Start now.
- **Grants.gov account.**
- **Audited financials** — most foundations require 1–3 years of audited financials. If MamaTeeTees doesn't have these, prioritize a CPA review immediately.
- **Form 990s** — public-record. Make sure last 3 years are filed and accessible on Candid/GuideStar.
- **Theory of change document** — 2-page narrative: who served, what changes, how we know, what scale needs.
- **Logic model** — outputs → outcomes → impact, with measurement approach.
- **Country registration in Cameroon** — most West Africa funders require local registration / MOU with Cameroonian government.
- **GuideStar / Candid Platinum profile** — free, but signals operational seriousness.
- **References / board diversity** — foundations check.
- **Letters of support** — Cameroonian local government, partner schools, community elders. Cultivate now.
