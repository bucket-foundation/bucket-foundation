## Top 3 Picks

1. **NSF POSE / PESOSE Phase I (~$300K).** Bucket's mission statement (build a sustainable open-source ecosystem for citeable primary research) is verbatim what POSE funds. Phase I is a 1-year scoping award — we don't need to be operating at scale yet, just credible. Gating issue: needs an IRS-recognized prime applicant. Either (a) wait for c3 determination, or (b) team with an academic co-PI who can prime, or (c) confirm whether HCB-as-fiscal-sponsor satisfies NSF eligibility (most NSF programs require the prime to be an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) directly — fiscal sponsorship may not qualify; verify with NSF program officer).
2. **Sloan Exploratory Grantmaking in Technology ($50K–$250K).** Two-page LOI to digitaltechnology@sloan.org. No registration overhead. Sloan has explicitly funded digital infrastructure for research (e.g., the Ford-Sloan Critical Digital Infrastructure work). Fastest possible "yes." Send LOI Q3 2026.
3. **Ethereum Foundation ESP + Gitcoin OSS round (combined ~$50K–$200K).** Crypto-native funders accept individual contributors and LLCs, no c3 required, fast turnaround (ESP weeks–months; Gitcoin per-round). Gitcoin in particular builds public legitimacy via community match. Both can be applied to *now*, no determination letter needed.

## Blocked-by-501(c)(3)

These are gated until Bucket has its IRS determination letter (or a confirmed fiscal sponsor that the funder explicitly accepts):

- **NSF POSE/PESOSE & Safe-OSE** — federal grants generally require the prime applicant to be IRS-recognized; fiscal sponsorship is a gray area NSF tends to disallow as prime. Verify case-by-case.
- **Mellon Public Knowledge** — invitation-only; in practice they only invite c3s.
- **Templeton OFI** — accepts fiscal sponsorship in practice but prefers c3.
- **MacArthur 100&Change** — round 4 not yet announced; round 3 closed Sept 2024. Will require c3 (or international equivalent).

## Pre-Application Prep Needed

- **HCB onboarding** (~1 week): apply at hackclub.com/fiscal-sponsorship/apply. 7% donation fee. Gets us a c3 legal entity wrapper today.
- **501(c)(3) determination filing**: separate track; 3–9 months IRS turnaround on Form 1023.
- **SAM.gov registration**: required for any federal grant (NSF, USDA, NOAA). 4–8 week turnaround. Start now regardless — applies to AGFarms LLC and Bucket separately.
- **Grants.gov registration**: requires SAM. Adds ~1 week.
- **NSF Research.gov account**: for POSE submission.
- **Biosketches** (NIH/NSF format) for Gian + any co-PIs.
- **Data Management Plan**: NSF requires 2-page DMP. Bucket's Walrus + Story Protocol architecture *is* the DMP — easy to write.
- **Budget justification template**: standard NIH/NSF format.
- **Letters of support / collaboration**: 2–3 from canon-tier figures (the contributor index in canon-figures/) would massively strengthen Templeton + NSF.
