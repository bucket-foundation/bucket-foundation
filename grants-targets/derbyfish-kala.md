# DerbyFish / Kala — Grant Targets

**Venture profile:** Competitive fishing platform (DerbyFish) + marine-science arm (Kala). Tournament data is de facto fisheries science: catch records, location, species, length, release status. Verified Data Sessions (VDS / BHRV — Bump, Hero, Release, Validate) produce wallet-signed citation-grade catch records. Held under AGFarms LLC.

The grant story: **DerbyFish is a citizen-science instrument** that produces stock-assessment-grade data at zero cost to NOAA. That framing unlocks the entire NOAA + NFWF + Sea Grant universe.

## Top 3 Picks

1. **NOAA Saltonstall-Kennedy ($25K–$500K).** LLC-eligible, no c3 hassle. The program's stated purpose — fisheries promotion + development — maps onto DerbyFish 1:1. Annual cycle, predictable. Pitch frame: *"DerbyFish is private-sector infrastructure for catch-record verification; we want to deliver assessment-grade data to NOAA stock managers at near-zero marginal cost."*
2. **NOAA Citizen Science for Stock Assessments ($75K–$200K).** This is the most thematically perfect program in the entire portfolio. Three to eight projects funded; we'd be a top-quartile applicant if we frame BHRV correctly. Eligible as for-profit. Partner with NOAA Fisheries scientist as encouraged co-investigator.
3. **NSF SBIR Phase I (up to $305K).** AGFarms LLC route — no c3 needed. DerbyFish has a real deeptech component (CV-based length verification, wallet-signed citation records, x402-paid scientific access). Project Pitch first, decision in 3 weeks. **Lowest-friction federal money for an LLC.**

## Blocked-by-501(c)(3)

Almost nothing here is c3-blocked — NOAA + NFWF + NSF SBIR all accept for-profits. The only blocked items:
- Pew Marine Fellows (individual fellowship, nomination-based — not really a c3 issue)
- Some private-foundation marine programs (Walton, Bloomberg) prefer nonprofits but accept LOIs

**Net: DerbyFish is the most grant-ready of the four ventures because federal fisheries money flows freely to LLCs.**

## Pre-Application Prep Needed

- **SAM.gov registration for AGFarms LLC** — required for all federal grants. 4–8 weeks. **Start immediately.** Even without a target, this is foundational.
- **Grants.gov account** — depends on SAM.
- **NOAA NOAA Grants Online (eGrants)** — if applying to NOAA.
- **DUNS / UEI number** — assigned via SAM.
- **NSF Research.gov account** — for SBIR Project Pitch.
- **Indirect cost rate** — federal grants use indirect cost rate; AGFarms LLC needs to negotiate or use de minimis 10%.
- **Letters of support** — 1–2 from a NOAA Fisheries scientist or state F&W biologist would massively de-risk Citizen Science + S-K applications. Cultivate now.
- **Data sharing plan** — describe how VDS records flow into stock assessments. Easy write — already core to product.
- **Stock-assessment use case** — pick 1–2 species (e.g., striped bass, red drum) where DerbyFish data could meaningfully inform an existing NOAA stock assessment. This is the strongest single de-risker.
- **Budget** — justify ~$200K Year 1 (engineering + biologist consultant + outreach to tournaments).
