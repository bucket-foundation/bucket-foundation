# AGFarms LLC, Grant Targets

**Venture profile:** For-profit umbrella LLC (Delaware). 16+ ventures under it. Self-funded, pre-revenue.

**The answer first:** for-profit LLCs are eligible for a *narrow* set of grants, primarily federal SBIR/STTR, state economic development, and industry-specific (USDA, DOE, DARPA, etc.). Most large foundation grants will not fund AGFarms directly. **The strongest grant strategy for AGFarms-the-umbrella is to route the charitable/research portion of specific ventures through Bucket Foundation as fiscal sponsor (once Bucket is operational), or to apply for SBIR-style federal money on behalf of specific deeptech ventures.**

Don't pad this list. The realistic AGFarms grant universe is SBIR + state EDA + USDA. Everything else flows through ventures with appropriate legal wrappers.

## Top 3 Picks

1. **NSF SBIR Phase I, multi-venture campaign (up to $305K each).** Submit Project Pitches for 2-3 ventures: DerbyFish (computer-vision fish length verification + wallet-signed records), Nucleus Brain (multi-agent enterprise infra with brain quality metrics), DataSoar (AI for HVAC field operations). Each one is independently eligible; each takes ~2 weeks to draft and 3 weeks for NSF decision. **Volume is the strategy.** Even a single $300K Phase I underwrites 6-9 months of one venture.
2. **NIH SBIR (Sep 5 2026 deadline), only if a health-aligned product exists.** If Longevity Hub crystallizes as a health product, NIH SBIR is plausible. If not, skip, NIH won't fund infrastructure plays.
3. **Route public-good work through Bucket once it's operational.** This isn't a single grant, it's the structural play. Several AGFarms ventures have charitable/research components (Kala marine science, Bucket-adjacent canon work). Spinning those components out under Bucket's c3 opens Knight, Mellon, Sloan, Templeton, none of which fund LLCs directly.

## Blocked-by-501

For AGFarms-the-LLC, *most* large foundation grants are blocked. This is just structural:
- Knight, Sloan (most programs), Mellon, Templeton, MacArthur, Open Society, Pew institutional, Walton, Bloomberg
- Mastercard, Echidna, Segal, all closed to for-profits

The move is: route the relevant portion through Bucket (or another c3) as fiscal sponsor.

## Fiscal-Sponsor-OK

For-profits *cannot themselves* be fiscally sponsored, fiscal sponsorship is a c3-to-project relationship. The reverse direction is the play: spin out a *project* (not the LLC) under Bucket's c3 wrapper.

Example pattern:
- "AGFarms Marine Citizen Science Project" lives under Bucket as fiscal sponsor → eligible for Pew, Walton, Bloomberg
- DerbyFish-the-product stays under AGFarms LLC for SBIR + commercial revenue
- Two structures, same team, two grant universes

## Pre-Application Prep Needed

- **SAM.gov for AGFarms LLC**, required for SBIR. 4-8 weeks. **Start immediately.**
- **Grants.gov + SBIR.gov accounts.**
- **NSF Research.gov account.**
- **eRA Commons account** (for NIH).
- **Per-venture Project Pitches** (1-2 pages each). Drafts can be reused as later product memos, so this work isn't sunk.
- **DUNS / UEI** via SAM.
- **Indirect cost rate**, use de minimis 10% as small business unless we negotiate.
- **Commercialization plan template**, SBIR Phase I requires a path to Phase II + market.
- **Bibliography of related research**, for any NIH submission.
- **Confirm AGFarms LLC NJ vs DE operating status**, relevant for NJ EDA eligibility.
