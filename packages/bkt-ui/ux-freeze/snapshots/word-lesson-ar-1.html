قدم/qa.dam/noun

foot

Other senses
  • foot (a measure)
  • step
  • to precede
  • to return from a journey
Etymology

From Proto-Semitic *q-d-m (“to precede, come before”). Compare Hebrew קדם and Classical Syriac ܩܕܡ.

قلب/qa.la.ba/noun

heart (organ)

Other senses
  • heart (the symbolic seat of human emotion)
  • pith, heart of palm
  • to turn
Etymology

Derived from قَلَبَ (qalaba, “to turn”). Alternatively, from Proto-Semitic *libb- (“heart”), with the initial ق possibly being a phonological insertion to break a biconsonantal cluster and avoid *∅alb or *∅ulb, thus *lb → *∅alb → qalb.
