How citing works

Paid once, cited forever.

  1. Read free

    Every entry is open: the primary text, the derivation, the context.

  2. Cite it

    Press cite in the app, the CLI or an agent request.

  3. Author is paid

    One payment settles over x402 on Base, about a cent.

  4. Keep the receipt

    The receipt is a citeable envelope that never expires.
