From the Earth to the Moon by Jules Verne 





From the Earth to the Moon by Jules Verne 


Translated by Lewis Page Mercier and Eleanor E. King 


From the Earth to the Moon (French: De la Terre à la Lune) is a humorous science fantasy 
story written in 1865 by Jules Verne and is one of the earliest entries in that genre. It tells the 
story of three well-to-do members of a post-American Civil War gun club who build an 
enormous sky-facing columbiad and ride a spaceship fired from it to the moon. (Summary 
from Wikipedia) 

Note: From the Earth to the Moon ends in a bit of a cliffhanger. The story continues with 
Round the Moon.
