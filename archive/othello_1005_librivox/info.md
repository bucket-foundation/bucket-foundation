## Description

<a href="http://librivox.org/" rel="ugc nofollow">LibriVox</a> recording of <i>Othello</i>, by William Shakespeare.

<i>Othello</i> is the story of a cross-cultural romance between the title character, a noble moor who is a general in the Venetian army, and Desdemona, a beautiful and virtuous Venetian lady.  The newly-married couple fall prey to the machinations of Iago, Othello's jealous ensign, who plots to destroy their union. (Summary by Elizabeth Klett)
