An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding 
By David Hume (1711-1776) 


The Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding is a shortened and simplified version of Hume’s 
masterpiece A Treatise of Human Nature. It sought to reach a wider audience, and to dispel some of 
the virulent criticism addressed toward the former book. In it, Hume explains his theory of 
epistemology, and argues against other current theories, including those of John Locke, George 
Berkeley, and Nicolas Malebranche. (Summary by Kirsten Ferreri) Total running time: 05:54:04.
