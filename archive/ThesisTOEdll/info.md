Contextual implications (epistemological and ontological vision of reality and its different contexts). <div><br />I affirm that the many-worlds interpretation of quantum mechanics is incapable of deriving objective probabilities and Born's rule without falling into circular arguments, and on the other hand, that current cosmological models, such as inflationary or cyclical ones, are an epistemological and incomplete vision of reality, each presenting its own problems, without being able to incorporate quantum gravity and where the hypothesis of the block universe fails, causing the appearance of infinities. However, with a quantum cosmology model, and with a universal wave function with a closed time curve (CTC), and that applies Everett's interpretation, all these problems disappear, offering a complete, objective and ontological vision of universe, which gives rise to a model of a block universe, spatially finite, without edges, and apparently static, which provides an ontological vision to the meaning of the arrow of time and probabilities within the framework of quantum mechanics. Furthermore, it is combined with current theories that try to solve the problem of quantum gravity, privileging a type of discrete space-time. This “quantum multiverse” model manages to connect with relativistic cosmological models through the MWI=Cosmological Multiverse hypothesis, applying a holographic method and the principle of complementarity, emerging both space-time and the Born rule naturally.
