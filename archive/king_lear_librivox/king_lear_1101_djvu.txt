King  Lear 

By  William  Shakespeare 


King  Lear  is  widely  held  as  the  greatest  of  Shakespeare's  tragedies;  to  some,  it  is  the  greatest  play  ever  written. 
King  Lear  abdicates  the  British  throne,  to  divide  his  kingdom  among  his  three  daughters  in  proportion  to  their 
professed  love  of  him.  His  plan  misfires  when  Cordelia,  his  youngest  and  favourite  daughter,  refuses  to  flatter  her 

father;  she  is  disinherited  and  banished.
