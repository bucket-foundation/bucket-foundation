## Description

<p>

  Ibogaine is a drug being tested as a treatment for drug addictions of 
most types. Patients who have tried it claim that it not only prevents 
withdrawal but eliminates craving for a period as well. The effects of 
ibogaine include the re-experiencing of past memories, a property which 
has interested some psychotherapists, but also the Drug Enforcement 
Agency (USA) which has placed the drug on Schedule I. Nevertheless, the 
Food and Drug Administration (USA) has approved human trials which have 
stalled due to the unavailability of funding.</p><p>

  This document was created to promote the study of ibogaine because it has the potential to alleviate so much suffering.</p><p>(this is a back up from <a href="http://www.puzzlepiece.org/ibogaine/literature/iborefs.html" rel="nofollow">http://www.puzzlepiece.org/ibogaine/literature/iborefs.html)</a><br /></p><p>
