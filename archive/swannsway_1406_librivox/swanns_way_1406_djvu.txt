Swann's  Way  is  the  first  book  in  the  seven  volume  work  In 
Search  of  Lost  Time,  or  Remembrance  of  Things  Past,  by 
Marcel  Proust.  It  is  a  novel  written  in  the  form  of  an 
autobiography.  Proust's  most  prominent  work,  it  is  popularly 
known  for  its  length  and  the  notion  of  involuntary  memory,  the 
most  famous  example  being  the  "episode  of  the  madeleine." 
(Summary  from  Wikipedia)
