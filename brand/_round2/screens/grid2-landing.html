Proposition I.1

On a given finite straight line, construct an equilateral triangle.

  1. With centre A and distance AB, draw the circle BCD. Postulate 3.
  2. With centre B and distance BA, draw the circle ACE. Postulate 3.
  3. From C, where the circles cut, join CA and CB. Postulate 1.
  4. CA equals AB and CB equals BA, so all three sides are equal. Common notion 1.
