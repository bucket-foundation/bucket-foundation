# Flag atelier

Clickable objects connect a place to a regional craft. Bucket supplies the shared system; Polingual uses its country records alongside explicit language labels.

The frame carries texture and material. The inset preserves the source flag's geometry and colors. A native button selects the tile; the detail panel exposes the craft source and its context. Country names and language specimens remain separate fields.

The outer body has a 1.12 width-to-height ratio, 19% corner radius, and padding of 14% at the top, 13% at the sides, and 18% at the bottom. The face adds 3% padding around a contained flag image. Textile bodies use a 16% radius. Artwork widths of 40, 56, and 80 pixels demonstrate compact use. Interactive wrappers retain a 44-pixel minimum target.

Use warm ivory `#f5f3eb`, forest ink `#183d35`, and muted green `#646d61` for the gallery. Material accents belong to the frame. Georgia supplies display type; Noto script specimens use the local licensed subsets in `fonts/`.

The collection includes 249 ISO countries and territories. Sixteen regional craft studies carry researched attribution, native-script names, and custom object icons. Six public-domain Met photographs retain their object captions; ten detail panels use original vector studies. Every craft has an original illustration available in `illustrations/`.

[Research and references](reports/Flag%20tile%20craft%20references.md) ground the material choices. [Usage and asset contract](BRAND-SYSTEM.md) defines the production files. `material-study.png` is a generated material concept; the interface uses the versioned SVG artwork.
