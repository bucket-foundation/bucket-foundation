Files you own: brand/_directions/src/logos.py (mho, lyre, goat, vessel, mascot, guides, construction) and a new icons.py in the same folder exposing `icon(d, name) -> inner SVG on a 24 viewBox` for the 8 names home, search, library, cards, listen, cite, profile, settings. build2.py imports both; after your change run `python3 build2.py` there and check http://127.0.0.1:8777/_directions/index.html. Direction specs: lib.py (D) and made.json in the same folder.

1. References first. Save real sources with their URL and licence to brand/_directions/licensed/marks/ plus a sources.json: heraldic goat and ibex charges, the Capricorn glyph, Greek coin goats (for example the Ainos tetradrachm), Japanese animal mon (crane, carp), Islamic animal motifs, a coopered bucket, amphora profiles, the Marianne Brandt MT49 teapot, the igeta crest, and the Shukhov tower. Use Wikimedia Commons, Met Open Access, Smithsonian or Cooper Hewitt.
2. Geometry. Rebuild every mark per direction from explicit geometry: circles, tangent lines, golden or root-2 proportions, cubic beziers with G2 continuity, the hyperboloid as true ruled lines (line pairs between two rotated circles projected), and the ℧ horns as two concentric arcs joined by real tangents. Generate the path data in Python from the constructions, with clean numbers and no auto-trace noise. guides(d) must draw the actual construction (circles, tangents, proportion rectangles) for each mark, and construction() must show it.
3. Goat and vessel quality. Proportions and silhouette are traced from the references; front-facing goat head whose horns form ℧; per-direction vessel (Swiss ∪, Kamon igeta or masu, Memphis cornucopia, Constructivism Shukhov hyperboloid, Girih astrolabe or ewer, Bauhaus Brandt teapot); mascot per direction. Each must hold up at 32px.
4. Icons. Give each direction a distinct icon grammar: d1 Carbon filled, square terminals, 2px counters; d2 SF Symbols-like in mon roundels; d3 Duolingo chunky with lip; d4 Material Sharp with diagonal metaphors; d5 Fluent-like outlined at 1.5px with filled accent; d6 primitives only. Vary stroke versus fill, corner logic, metaphors and grid between directions. Each icon reads at 24px, with internal contrast of 3:1 or better.
Render a contact sheet at 24, 32 and 96px for all directions and view it before finishing. No code comments, no commits. Report under 80 words.
