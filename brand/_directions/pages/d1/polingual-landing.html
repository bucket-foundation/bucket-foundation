How it works

  1. Pick a script

    Latin, Hebrew, Greek, Cyrillic, Devanagari or Han.

  2. Study one root

    See hundred beside cent, cien, מאה, εκατό and сто.

  3. Reveal the link

    Each card shows the shared Proto-Indo-European root *ḱm̥tóm.

  4. Keep the streak

    Five minutes a day. Reviews space themselves.
