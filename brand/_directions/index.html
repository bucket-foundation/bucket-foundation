Grid

Swiss International Style · IBM Carbon
How it is madeHand-set SVG on a 16-column 8px grid; every shape snaps to integer grid units. Motion: Hard cuts and kinetic type: 0ms state swaps, type slides on steps of 8px, Carbon productive cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0.38,0.9) 110ms. Accents: Square buttons with right-aligned arrow; 1px hairline dividers; 2px inset focus ring in blue; crosshair cursor on canvas, underline on hover; sound: single 1kHz tick; photos none, data plots only; icons filled sharp; no texture; flush-left ragged-right type; 16-col grid.

Accents: Pill buttons with seal dot; double hairline rule dividers; vermilion ring focus with 4px offset; brush cursor on art; hover darkens indigo; sound: wooden clack; woodblock illustration, photos as washi-texture duotone; icons filled in mon roundels; washi fibre texture 4%; centered scroll axis, vertical rhythm 8px.

Euclid

Classical Greek: Attic red-figure, stoichedon inscriptions and Byrne's Euclid · Wikimedia Codex
How it is madeEuclidean construction in code: every figure is a numbered proposition built from labelled points, circles and lines, colored in Byrne's red, yellow and blue; headings set on a stoichedon letter grid. Motion: Proof steps: construction lines draw in sequence, 240ms each, cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1), then the conclusion and QED fade in. Accents: 2px-radius Codex buttons with a terracotta fill; meander (Greek key) dividers; 2px blue focus ring with a key corner; pointer cursor, hover underlines in terracotta; sound: a single lyre pluck; red-figure silhouettes for figures, Byrne diagrams for ideas; icons filled on a 20px grid; parchment grain texture; polytonic Greek alongside English; numbered proposition layout with proof column.
