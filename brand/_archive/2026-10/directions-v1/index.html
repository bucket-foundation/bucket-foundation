Bucket design directions

Five art directions for the Bucket Foundation refresh. Direction 0 reworks the current tan, gold and green. Directions 1 to 4 are new. Each one shows the bkt mark at 1024, 64 and 32px, iOS and Android icons, a Polingual study card front and back, and measured WCAG contrast for every action and label. Generated art is marked as such and is a concept sample.
