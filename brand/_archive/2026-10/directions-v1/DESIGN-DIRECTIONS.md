# Bucket Design Directions

Brand-kit Step 1, concept only. Rendered page: `brand/_directions/index.html`. Founder picks one direction, or two to blend, before Step 2 generates marks. Shared study-card content: Italian "rinascita", rebirth, shown front and back with an example sentence and a reveal step. Every action and label on the page carries a measured WCAG ratio, and all pass AA.

## Mark

The mark is lowercase "bkt" in each direction's display face. The b is drawn as a path whose counter is a bucket with a rim, so the bucket survives at 32px. The app icon is the b alone plus one direction detail: a gilt rim, a rubric dot, an orange index tab, a vermilion cut-paper rim, or a brass orbit ring. Android icons sit at 42% of the circle.

## 0 Forest and Gilt

Directives: forest is the app surface and limestone the reading surface. Gilt is a fill with forest text on one action per screen; gilt text uses #6E520E. Instrument Serif at 32px and up only. The bkt mark has a gilt rim inside the bucket counter.
Anti-slop: no tan or parchment, no gold gradients or gold text on green, no Kala globe or stone clip art, no serif in controls.
References: Wellcome Collection identity, Stripe Press covers, Kinfolk typography, Aesop signage.
Study screen: forest frame, limestone card, gilt "knew it" button.

## 1 Codex

Directives: rubric red marks the one action. Margin column on every page for roots and citations. In-house drop caps, pilcrows and a printer's-device bucket. Ruled-line texture under 6% contrast.
Anti-slop: no parchment photos or burned edges, no handwriting body fonts, no pasted Leonardo sketches, no blackletter.
References: Codex Arundel facsimiles, Aldine italics, Tufte's book typography, Folger digital collection.
Study screen: vellum card with a red margin rule, lapis root note. Icon: italic b on vellum with a red point.

## 2 Archive

Directives: accession numbers on everything (BKT-0001, PL-IT-0412). 8-column grid, 2px black rules, square corners. One signal orange for action and live state. Set-type wordmark.
Anti-slop: no shadows, gradients or glass, no icons where a word fits, no centered layouts, no second accent.
References: Experimental Jetset Whitney identity, Cooper Hewitt collection, Müller-Brockmann grids, Are.na.
Study screen: white sheet with a 2px black border, word filed with a date, "file it" button. Icon: black tile, orange tab, "bkt".

## 3 Pigment

Directives: four pigments, each owning a language or canon branch. Cut-paper shapes with hand edges. Rounded cards, pill buttons, 48px targets. Card flip and stamp under 300ms, off under reduced motion.
Anti-slop: no mascot, confetti or streak flames, no pastels, no 3D blobs or pigment gradients, no text under 4.5:1.
References: Matisse cut-outs, Giotto's Scrovegni blue, Headspace flat color, Penguin Modern Classics.
Study screen: lapis frame, ochre card, vermilion pill. Icon: ochre bucket with vermilion rim on lapis.

## 4 Observatory

Directives: near-black with one brass accent, meridian cyan only for links. Constellation diagrams for the canon graph. Newsreader italic for studied words, Geist for the rest. One brass bloom behind the focal card under 15% opacity.
Anti-slop: no purple or aurora, no AI sparkles, no starfield stock, no heavy blur, no neon text.
References: Sidereus Nuncius plates, Linear dark UI, Griffith Observatory wayfinding, Stellarium.
Study screen: dark card with a constellation of related words. Icon: bucket outline inside a brass orbit with one star.
