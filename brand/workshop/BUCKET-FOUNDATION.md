## Phase 1: Foundation

### Purpose

The world will be a better place if primary research is paid for once, citeable forever, and its authors are paid when others build on it.

### Enemy

There is no way to cite a foundation and pay its author at the moment of use. Citation incentives point at the cheapest paywalled article, so the strongest derivations go uncited and their authors go unpaid. That gap is the enemy.

### Audience

- **Core:** a person who works with a frontier model and an axiom, already at work, who needs a canon to build on without re-checking every citation.
- **Power niche:** the canon-tier contributors in `canon-figures/`, and the first learners in Polingual who arrive knowing one language and study a second.
- **Not for:** people who want the next viral consumer app, an enterprise SaaS suite, an agent that books flights, or a governance token. The manifesto says so and offers no apology.
- **Feeling:** standing on ground that holds.

### Onlyness

Bucket is the only open research workspace where a citation is designed to pay the author and where the canon, the protocol and the code are free to copy.

Competitor test: Zenodo and arXiv host without paying authors. Google Scholar indexes without a rail. Anki and Duolingo teach without a citable canon. Notion stores without a thesis. None can say the sentence. The fee rail is specified and does not settle yet, so public copy says "designed to".

## Phase 2: Character

### Personality

Four traits. Precise leads in canon and research copy. Humble leads in anything about money or limits. Credible leads in launch copy. Playful appears only inside Polingual.

### Story

- **Hero:** the person doing genius work with AI.
- **External problem:** foundations sit behind paywalls or in unsorted corpora.
- **Internal problem:** doubt that the work will outlive the project.
- **Guide:** Bucket, a commons with a known direction.
- **Plan:** read free, build on the canon, cite, and the author is paid.
- **Stakes:** a second Renaissance on a new substrate, or another decade of closed loops.

### Primal code and archetype

Archetype: Sage, with a Creator second. Creation story: the first Renaissance was a change of substrate, and the second is under way now. Sacred words: foundation, canon, cite, bucket. Rituals: the daily quiz and the daily item. Leader: the founder, posting raw sessions.

## Phase 3: Expression

### Positioning statement

For researchers and learners who work with AI at the edge of a field and cannot trust what they cite, Bucket is an open research workspace and canon of foundations that is free to read and designed to pay the author on every citation. Unlike journals, publishers and closed learning apps, it ships the protocol, the code and the data under open licenses, so no host can lock the work back up.

### SOCO and SOCA

- **SOCO:** a small canon of foundations you can trust and cite, free to read.
- **SOCA:** never "another AI research app", never "a token", never "payments settle today", never "neutral".

### Tagline family

- Primary: free to read. paid to cite.
- Closer: written in stone.
- Slogans, always in order: build the past. build history. Bucket is the new renaissance.
- Polingual: every word in every language, one meaning.
