The ground is parchment and ink. Everything loud comes from somewhere real: a flag, a pigment, a tile, a cloth. The words sit at the center, and the world crowds in around them.

Marks

The ℧ holds the fire.

Bucket's mark is an inverse omega: a bowl. It carries the streak as a living flame. Polingual uses the Po tile; its accent is still open, with a diacritic over the o as the lead option.

DOKeep the ℧ upright with its feet level. Give it a third of the icon as padding. Keep the drop at the center of the bowl.
DON'TDraw the lyre, a plain U, or a bucket pail. Outline the mark. Put it on a busy collage without its purple tile.

Scenes

Find it in the world.

A scene is a place from one culture, drawn in the collage style: flat pigment fills, a 4px ink keyline, a hard ink offset shadow, no gradients. The learner hears a word and taps the object. Paris holds a croissant and a baguette; Tokyo holds salmon and a lantern.

RuleValue
ObjectsFive to eight per scene, each at least 120px across at 1200 wide
Keyline and shadow4px ink stroke; offset 6, 8 at 28% ink
PalettePigments only; food may use its own warm tones
FeedbackCorrect pops and shows the word; a miss shakes and says the word it is
SourceEach scene names its place and language in the footer
