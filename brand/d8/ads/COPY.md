## Organic Posts

### r/languagelearning

Title: I built a free iPhone app that shows one word across many scripts, looking for beta testers

Body: I kept learning words in one language and forgetting how they connect to the others I know. Polingual puts one word on tiles in many languages at once, each in its own script, with IPA and audio. "Hundred" is cent, מאה, εκατό, مئة, सौ and 百 on one screen. It is a free TestFlight beta on iPhone. I would love blunt feedback on which languages you want next: https://www.polingual.com

### r/polyglot

Title: For people juggling three or more languages: one word, eighteen languages, one screen

Body: Polingual lines up the same word across the languages you study, with script, IPA and a recording for each. There is a streak, and every tile plays when you tap it. Free iPhone beta, no account needed to look around: https://www.polingual.com
