## The Multiplier

The multiplier is m_p = J_p / max(H_p, A): a person's score with the model's answer shown, over the better of the person alone and the model alone. Inputs are three guess-corrected scores on matched four-choice items and an unaided retest after seven days. Dependence is a positive gain with learning at or below zero.

Proved in Lean: score bounds, two consequences of the dependence definition, the minimum learning path. Measured: nothing. Assumed: prerequisite edges are correct, nothing is forgotten mid-path, FSRS default weights fit, 0.7 means mastered, seven days is the right interval.
