## Agreement

- Retrieval and spacing beat restudy at a delay: Roediger, Dunlosky, Bjork.
- Step-level feedback drives tutoring gains: VanLehn, Kulik, Hattie, Black.
- Novices need guidance that fades with skill: Sweller, Kalyuga, Renkl, Lazonder.
- Self-explanation helps: Chi 1989, Chi 1994.
- Answer-giving AI lowers unaided performance: Bastani, Kazemitabaar, Fan, Stadler.
