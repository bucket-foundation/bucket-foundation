Every queued item staged as MVP, near-term, or later, with the reason, so the founder decides what ships next by reading one page. Bead: `ros-roadmap`, priority 0. The standing rule from `ros-frontend` applies, so the staging lands inside Research OS where people work.

## Stage: research

Underway. This file carries the finished memo; the notes below say what it will answer and where the answers come from.

## What it has to answer

1. What is queued. `BEADS-PENDING.jsonl` holds 206 rows, and the Research OS ones span the `ros-prime`, `ros-truth`, `ros-import`, `ros-workbench`, `ros-patents`, and `ros-frontend` epics plus loose tasks. Each needs a line that says what it delivers and what it depends on.
2. What "MVP" means for a research OS with 1,903 public nodes and no outside users yet. The stage names have to rest on a test a reader can apply.
3. What each item depends on, so the staging is a possible order. An item whose input is blocked on the founder cannot sit in MVP.
4. What the founder decides against what the loop decides. The page's job is the founder's yes or no on order, and the loop keeps the ordering inside a stage.
5. Where the staging lives in Research OS, and what a reader does with it there.
