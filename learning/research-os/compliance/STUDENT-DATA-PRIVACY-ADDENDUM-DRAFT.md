## 1. What this addendum is

This addendum is the district-specific supplement that pairs with the base NDPA v2.2, the model contract SDPC and A4L publish for exactly this purpose. Signing the base NDPA once and filing it in SDPC's Resource Registry, then offering this addendum per district, is the procurement-speed path `_intake/research-os-k12/04-compliance-distribution.md` section 2 recommends over negotiating a one-off contract with every district.

## 2. Parties

Bucket Foundation ("Provider") and [District name] ("District, ""LEA""). Provider acts as a **school official** under FERPA (20 U.S.C. 1232g, 34 CFR Part 99) for the sole purpose of delivering Research OS for K-12 and Bucket Academy to the District's students and staff.

## 3. Data elements covered

Every table and field named in `learning/research-os/compliance/DATA-INVENTORY.md`, current as of this addendum's effective date. The District may request the current inventory at any time; Provider will supply it within [X business days], matching Illinois SOPPA's public-disclosure-burden obligation, which sits on the District but which this Provider commitment is meant to support.

## 4. Purpose limitation

Provider uses District student data only to: route a learner through the knowledge graph, grade a learner's own submitted explanation or transfer answer, let a District-designated teacher review and approve or return a learner's submitted work, and maintain the account and consent record described in `learning/research-os/compliance/DATA-INVENTORY.md`. No other use, including product-improvement analytics outside this purpose or the founder's own research use (see section 8), is authorized without a separate, explicit written agreement.

## 5. No Sale or Advertising

Provider will not sell District student data, use it for targeted advertising, or build a non-educational profile of any student. This matches the baseline every state statute researched in `_intake/research-os-k12/04-compliance-distribution.md` section 2 (California SOPIPA, New York Education Law 2-d, Illinois SOPPA, Colorado HB 16-1423, Texas Student Privacy Act) independently requires.

## 8. Research Use

This section keeps research use separate from the operational data-sharing terms above. Provider's founder is conducting human-subjects research using this product as a testbed (`_intake/research-os-k12/04-compliance-distribution.md` section 10). **This addendum's operational data-sharing terms do NOT authorize that research use.** Any District that participates in the research study does so under a separate, IRB-approved research data use agreement, with its own consent process, described in `_intake/research-os-k12/04-compliance-distribution.md` section 10's "Data governance for research use." FERPA's studies exception (34 CFR 99.31(a)(6)) is a narrow, school-initiated pathway that does not, on its own, cover a vendor's own dissertation research; the compliance plan flags this directly (risk register item 3) and this addendum does not rely on that exception.

## 9. Access and Deletion

A parent, an eligible student (per applicable state law, commonly 13 or older), or a District official acting on a family's behalf may request access to, correction of, or deletion of a student's data at any time. Provider will act on a verified deletion request within [X days], through the technical route described in `learning/research-os/compliance/README.md`. Illinois SOPPA and the Texas Student Privacy Act both name this right independently of FERPA; this section commits to it directly rather than relying only on FERPA's own weaker default.

## 10. Data destruction on contract end

On termination of this addendum, Provider will destroy or return all District student data within [X days], and confirm destruction to the District in writing, matching Colorado HB 16-1423's explicit destruction-and-confirmation requirement.
