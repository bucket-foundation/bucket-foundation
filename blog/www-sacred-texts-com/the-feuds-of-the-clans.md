The Feuds of the Clans Index | Internet Sacred Text Archive Sacred Texts Legends/Sagas Celtic The Feuds of the Clans by Alexander MacGregor [1907] Contents Start Reading Page Index Text [Zipped] This is an account of the feuds of the Highlanders of Scotland. While most feuds stem from trivial causes, they have always been a source for high literature, from the Iliad to the Mahabharata. These Scottish feuds take on an epic quality of their own, as they play out their grim logic of retribution.
