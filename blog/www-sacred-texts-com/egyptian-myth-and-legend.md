Egyptian Myth and Legend Index | Internet Sacred Text Archive Sacred Texts Egypt Buy this Book on Kindle Egyptian Myth and Legend by Donald Mackenzie [1907] Contents Start Reading Page Index Text [Zipped] This highly readable book covers Egyptian religion, history, and culture through its entire civilization. We are accustomed to history measured in decades or centuries. Egypt requires thinking in terms of millenia. There was not one monolithic Egyptian belief system; it went through profound changes over time; this book describes this evolution in great detail. Mackenzie includes many extracts from religious texts, folk tales, and historical documents.
