The Complete Sayings of Jesus Index | Internet Sacred Text Archive Sacred Texts Bible Buy this Book at Amazon.com Christ Among the Doctors, by Alberecht Duerer [1506] (Public Domain Image) The Complete Sayings of Jesus by Arthur Hinds [1927] Contents Start Reading Page Index Text [Zipped] Like the Jefferson Bible , this is an attempt to edit the four gospels into a consistent account, in this case focusing on the words of Jesus. There is plenty of connecting narrative around the instances where Jesus speaks, so this is better than simply presenting each quote out of context. The focus on what Jesus is attributed as saying makes it easier to browse the core texts of the New Testament. All in all, a very useful reference, and a great read.
