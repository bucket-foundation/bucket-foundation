Pennsylvania Folklore This part of the site has grown organically rather than by plan, and a small number of texts about Pennsylvania have coalesced here, for no particular reason. Pow-wows; or Long Lost Friend by George Hohman . [ 1820 ] A small book of folk-magic and healing by a early nineteenth Century Pennsylvania Dutch practicioner of 'Pow-wow'.
