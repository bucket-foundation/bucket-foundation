This is a very limited on-line translation service. To use it, click on the link below, select one of the "English to..." choices in the drop down menu and click on 'translate'. You can then navigate the translated version of the site in the large frame at the bottom of the page more or less normally. Keep in mind that only the first 16Kb or so of each page is translated. Since the pages are translated automatically, they will have errors in them. Sacred-texts is not responsible for these errors, and we have no way to correct them, so please don't ask us to fix them. The babelfish seems to have trouble matching link labels with their corresponding links: the left to right links at the top of most sacred-texts pages sometimes seem to get translated in reverse order: note that conventionally the leftmost one in the list is the link to the main site index.
