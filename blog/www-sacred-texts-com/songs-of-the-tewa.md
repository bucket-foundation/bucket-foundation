Songs of the Tewa Index | Internet Sacred Text Archive Sacred Texts Native American Southwest Songs of the Tewa tr. by Herbert Joseph Spinden [1933] Contents Start Reading Page Index Text [Zipped] This is a collection of poetry of the Tewa. The Tewa are a Pueblo culture, who live on the Hopi Reservation in Arizona. This monograph includes translations of a cycle of Tewa poems. An appendix has interlinear texts for some of the Tewa songs. The book is preceeded by an essay on Native American poetry and literature, including samples from the north to the south of the American continent.
