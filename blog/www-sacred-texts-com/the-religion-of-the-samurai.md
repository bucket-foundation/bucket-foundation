The Religion of the Samurai Index | Internet Sacred Text Archive Sacred Texts Buddhism Buy this Book at Amazon.com The Religion of the Samurai A Study of Zen Philosophy and Discipline in China and Japan By Kaiten Nukariya [1913] Contents Start Reading Text [Zipped] Kaiten Nukariya's 1913 Religion of the Samurai focuses on Northern (Mahayana) Buddhism, and Zen Buddhism in particular. This short book contains a wealth of detail, as well as very lucid explanations of seemingly elusive Zen Buddhist concepts. It includes a text on the 'Origin of Man' by Kwei Fung Tsung Mih, a notable Chinese scholar who was the seventh Patriarch of the Kegon sect.
