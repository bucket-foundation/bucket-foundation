Wynschenk Dom ed. by Evelyn Underhill [1916] Contents Start Reading Text [Zipped] Jan van Ruysbroeck [b. 1293 or 1294, d. 1381] was a Flemish mystic. He led a life of asuterity, eventually founding a congregation in Groenendaal. His writings were widely circulated in his lifetime and he influenced an entire generation of Christian mystics.
