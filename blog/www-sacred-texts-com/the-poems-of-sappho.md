The Poems of Sappho Index | Internet Sacred Text Archive Sacred Texts Classics Sappho Buy this Book at Amazon.com Loves' Votaries, by Lawrence Alma-Tadema (detail) [1891] (Public Domain Image) The Poems of Sappho by John Myers O'Hara [1910] Contents Start Reading Page Index Text [Zipped] This is an early 20th century attempt to weave the fragments of Sappho into a set of complete poems. The translator is building on the consensus view of Sappho at the time, very heavily influenced by such speculations as Bilitis . The poetry is serviceable, and O'Hara is careful not to overstep his mission. This book is not very well known, and because it was printed in a small run and never reprinted, is fairly scarce and hence used copies are expensive. --J.B. Hare, June 11th, 2008.
