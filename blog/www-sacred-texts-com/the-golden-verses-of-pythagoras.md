The Golden Verses of Pythagoras Index | Internet Sacred Text Archive Sacred Texts Classics Buy this Book at Amazon.com The Golden Verses of Pythagoras by Fabre d'Olivet [1917] Contents Start Reading Fabre d'Olivet (b. 1768, d. 1825), a French esoteric writer, originally published this book in 1813. The first section, an essay on esoteric Poetics, was dedicated to the Section of Literature of the Imperial Institute of France. The second portion contains the Greek text of the Golden Verses, along with French and English translations. The final part of the book is an extended essay on the Golden Verses. The Golden Verses are also available at this site in a more recent English translation by Florence Firth .
