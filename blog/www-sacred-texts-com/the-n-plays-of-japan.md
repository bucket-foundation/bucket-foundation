The Noh Plays of Japan Index | Internet Sacred Text Archive Sacred Texts Shinto Buddhism The Nō Plays of Japan By Arthur Waley [1921] Nō (also spelled Noh ) drama incorporates folk-tales, poetry, and dance in an exquisite, very formalized setting. These works are of interest beyond their artistic appeal, because they are interwoven with Buddhist (and to a lesser extent, Shinto) traditions and beliefs, and provide deep insight into Japanese culture.
