An Introduction to Astrology by William Lilly , ( Zadkiel ed.) [ 1852 ] The first modern text on Astrology , updated in the 19th century, and still in use today. The Sand Reckoner of Archimedes translated by Thomas L. Heath [ 1881 ] Did Archimedes invent calculus two millenia before Newton and Leibnitz?
