Method

Solvability = 0.55 x resolved + 0.45 x formal, where formal is proved 1.0, partial 0.6, statement 0.35 and none 0.1. Formal counts a machine-checked proof in any assistant: Lean, Coq, Isabelle or HOL Light. Minted state follows formal status: a proved result is minted, partial is pending, a stated result is draft, and no formalization is unminted. Production status maps minted to accepted and pending to submitted.

Embeddings come from bge-small-en-v1.5 over name, branch and tokens. Angle is the rank along the first two principal directions. The network links each node to its six nearest neighbours by cosine similarity. The catalog, levels and market tags were chosen by hand and are open to review.
