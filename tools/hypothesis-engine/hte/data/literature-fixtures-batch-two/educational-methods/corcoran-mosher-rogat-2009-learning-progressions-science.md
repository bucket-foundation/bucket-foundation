# Learning Progressions in Science: An Evidence-Based Approach to Reform

Sets the evidentiary bar a claimed learning progression must clear before it counts as
more than a plausible sequence, and names local-versus-universal validity and item
quality as separate failure modes even past that bar.

## Key Claims

- A learning progression remains a hypothesis until tested against real learner data,
  not expert judgment alone.
- A progression can be locally valid for one curriculum and assessment without being
  valid past that context.
- Assessment items detecting a progression's levels require their own validation work,
  independent of the progression itself.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- What population and context scope bounds where the five-state model, once validated,
  should be treated as valid.
- Whether workspace tool logs are sufficient data for this validation, or a dedicated
  instrument is required.

## How It Bears on Research OS

Extends `LEARNER-STATE-MODEL.md` OPEN-5: the general validation framework Hambleton
and Jones's psychometric bar sits inside, naming a scope-of-validity question that
remains even after OPEN-2's Rasch or IRT test passes.
