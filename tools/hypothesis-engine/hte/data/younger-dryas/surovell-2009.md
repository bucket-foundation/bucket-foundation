abstract: >
  Based on elevated concentrations of a set of “impact markers” at the onset of the Younger Dryas
  stadial from sedimentary contexts across North America, Firestone, Kennett, West, and others have
  argued that 12.9 ka the Earth experienced an impact by an extraterrestrial body, an event that had
  devastating ecological consequences for humans, plants, and animals in the New World [Firestone RB,
  et al. (2007) Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 104:16016–16021]. Herein, we report the results of an
  independent analysis of magnetic minerals and microspherules from seven sites of similar age,
  including two examined by Firestone et al. We were unable to reproduce any results of the Firestone
  et al. study and find no support for Younger Dryas extraterrestrial impact.
