Polingual teaches a set of languages at once. Pick one language you know and the languages you want. Each word returns in every language of the set, in one mixed review queue, with shared roots shown side by side.

HOW IT WORKS
• Spaced review: words come back right before you would forget them.
• Listen: hear each word spoken in every language of your set.
• Study offline: your languages come with the app, and progress syncs when you are back online.
• Translate: type a word in any language you know and see it in every language you study, with audio.

DAILY GAMES
Daily Set, Mini, Word Groups, Same Word, Speed Match, Which Voice, Flags and Letter Sprint. A new round of each every day.

LIVE ROOMS
Start a room, share the code, and play a word game with friends on Android, iPhone or the web. Add bots when you want a bigger table.

LISTEN
Radio stations from the countries whose languages you study, playing in the background.

LANGUAGES
English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, German, Dutch, Swedish, Polish, Finnish, Russian, Greek, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Hebrew, Persian, Turkish, Czech, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai, Tamil, Latin, Ancient Greek, Sanskrit and Old English. Any of them can be the root language.

PRESETS
Romance, Germanic, Iberian and East Asian sets get you started in one tap.

ONE ACCOUNT
Sign in with an email code. Your language systems and progress follow you across Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac, the Bucket app and bucket.foundation.

FREE AND PREMIUM
Games and live rooms are free for everyone and show ads. Polingual Monthly and Polingual Yearly open daily lessons and review and remove all ads. New subscribers get 3 days free. Payment is charged to your Google Play account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews at the same price unless you cancel before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in Google Play, Payments and subscriptions.
