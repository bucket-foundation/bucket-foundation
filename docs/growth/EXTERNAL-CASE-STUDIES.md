# External Case Studies

Bead bkt-y425, external half. Fourteen brands, four groups. Each entry gives the brand move, the channel, the measured result, and the near-zero-budget copy for Bucket and Polingual. Figures marked "secondary" come from blogs or aggregators and need a primary check before they go in a deck.

## Learning Apps

### Duolingo

- **Move:** Duo the owl as an unhinged character. Trend-chasing short video, reposted across platforms, plus stunts at concerts.
- **Channel:** TikTok first, then Instagram and X.
- **Result:** 16.8M TikTok followers by April 2025, per Adweek as cited secondary. Q4 2023 DAU 26.9M, up 65% year over year. Duolingo's own filings credit most growth to word of mouth; no source ties DAU to TikTok directly.
- **Bucket copy:** Give Polingual one character with a voice and post a 15-second clip a day of it reacting to a real learner moment. The first user found it fun: film that.
- Sources: [markhub24 summary of Adweek](https://www.markhub24.com/post/why-brands-are-moving-from-campaigns-to-conversations), [Brand24](https://brand24.com/blog/duolingo-social-media-strategy/)

### Anki

- **Move:** Free, open-source desktop app with a shared-deck library. Users build the content and the reputation.
- **Channel:** AnkiWeb shared decks, r/medicalschoolanki, med-school word of mouth after reports of higher USMLE Step 1 scores.
- **Result:** The AnKing deck became the default Step 1 and Step 2 deck; the AMA now recommends Anki to students. No public revenue or community counts found.
- **Bucket copy:** Ship shareable, forkable Polingual decks and research buckets with a public URL. Seed one niche where scores matter, such as a language exam, and let one power user own the canonical deck.
- Sources: [AMA](https://www.ama-assn.org/medical-students/succeed-medical-school/what-anki), [University of Hawaii study](https://scholarspace.manoa.hawaii.edu/items/afe392ed-8555-4816-9410-a0a89b77a91e/full)

### Brilliant

- **Move:** Free daily problem as the hook, premium for the full path. The brand is "learn by doing" with interactive puzzles.
- **Channel:** Daily problems, YouTube creator sponsorships, paid social.
- **Result:** About 10M users and $73M raised, with a $50M Series C in May 2022. Revenue and MAU figures online conflict and are secondary.
- **Bucket copy:** One free daily item, a word in Polingual and a foundation in the canon, shareable as an image card. It costs nothing and gives people a reason to return each day.
- Sources: [yespress profile](https://yespress.io/brilliant-org.md), [KQED MindShift](https://www.kqed.org/mindshift/30136/online-game-challenges-gifted-students-to-compete-against-each-other)

### Khan Academy

- **Move:** A founder tutoring one cousin, recorded and posted in public. Nonprofit mission stated from day one.
- **Channel:** YouTube.
- **Result:** 35,000 views a day by December 2009, 70,000 a day in 2010. 3.5M unique users in 2011. Over 2B views, 8.17M subscribers and 155M registered users by 2023.
- **Bucket copy:** The founder teaches one real person on camera, in Polingual, each week. One learner, one lesson, posted raw. The nonprofit story does the rest.
- Sources: [Stanford GSE](https://ed.stanford.edu/news/how-sal-khan-launched-revolution-online-learning), [TechCrunch 2011](https://www.techcrunch.com/2011/10/19/khan-academy-triples-unique-users-to-3-5-million/), [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sal_Khan)

### Quizlet

- **Move:** Users make study sets for themselves; each set becomes a public page that ranks in search.
- **Channel:** Organic search plus classmates sharing sets.
- **Result:** The brand term "quizlet" draws about 14M searches a month, per Semrush. The peer Brainly grew from 1.6M to 10.1M monthly visits on the same UGC-to-SEO loop, secondary.
- **Bucket copy:** Make every public bucket and every Polingual deck an indexable page with a clean title. Sitemap it. The users write the SEO.
- Sources: [Sramana Mitra interview](https://sramanamitra.com/?p=126133), [Semrush](https://cw.semrush.com/website/quizlet.com/overview), [AIOSEO Brainly](https://aioseo.com/trends/brainly-seo-case-study/)

### Speak

- **Move:** Speaking-first AI tutor, launched in one country with a strong pain point before going global.
- **Channel:** Korea first, app stores, OpenAI partnership as credibility.
- **Result:** Over 10M users, user base doubling each year for five years, about 3M Koreans by company claim. Valued at $1B in December 2024 after $162M raised.
- **Bucket copy:** Pick one language pair and one country for Polingual and win it before adding more. Post in that community's own forums and language.
- Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/31/openai-backed-language-learning-app-speak-raises-16m-to-expand-to-the-u-s), [BNN Bloomberg](https://bnnbloomberg.ca/business/technology/2024/12/10/openai-backed-language-tutor-startup-doubles-value-to-1-billion)

## Creator and Community Platforms

### Whop

- **Move:** Content Rewards: brands fund clip bounties paid per 1,000 verified views. Whop also cut its 30% Discover fee to 0% in May 2025 to grow supply.
- **Channel:** Thousands of clippers posting on TikTok, Shorts and Reels.
- **Result:** Over $40,000 a day paid across nearly 1M videos a month, per Forbes as cited by CreatorDB. Median rate about $1 per 1,000 views.
- **Bucket copy:** Post a small bounty pool on Whop for clips of Polingual sessions, capped at a fixed budget such as $100. The 0% fee lesson applies too: keep authors' take at 100% and say so loud.
- Sources: [Whop docs](https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/growth-marketing/content-rewards), [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/whop), [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creator-news/the-rise-of-the-clip-economy/), [Builtbyfoundry](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/what-is-clipping-1-dollar-per-1000-views-50-campaigns)

### Substack

- **Move:** Writers recommend each other inside the product, so every newsletter feeds the network.
- **Channel:** In-product recommendations and the Substack app.
- **Result:** Recommendations and the app drive 50% of new subscriptions and 25% of new paid ones, February 2024. Lenny Rachitsky got 78% of new free subscribers from other newsletters.
- **Bucket copy:** Let every bucket recommend three other buckets on its page. Start a Bucket Substack and swap recommendations with ten research and language newsletters.
- Sources: [Substack](https://on.substack.com/p/substacks-recommendations-network), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/02/22/substack-now-lets-writers-curate-a-network-of-recommended-publications-for-their-subscribers/amp/), [Pubstack Success](https://pubstacksuccess.substack.com/p/recommendations-a-substack-super)

### Notion Templates

- **Move:** Creators sell or give away ready-made systems and post them on social, which markets Notion for free.
- **Channel:** X, TikTok and YouTube, Gumroad storefronts, the Notion template gallery.
- **Result:** Thomas Frank passed $2.1M in template sales in about two years. Easlo passed $500,000. Both secondary, gross figures.
- **Bucket copy:** Publish free "starter buckets" and Polingual study plans as one-click copies, then credit the author by name on each copy. Recruit two creators who already post study systems.
- Sources: [Thomas Frank on Typefully](https://typefully.com/TomFrankly/dollar1-million-in-notion-template-sales-kuFT0iD), [Builtbyfoundry case study](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/thomas-frank-notion-templates-2m-creator-business), [Kajabi](https://kajabi.com/blog/can-you-make-money-selling-notion-templates)

## Nonprofit and Open-Source Brands

### Wikipedia

- **Move:** Annual banner from the founder or the foundation asking readers for a small gift, with constant A/B testing of the ask.
- **Channel:** On-site banners and email.
- **Result:** FY 2023-24: over 8M donors, average gift about $10. FY 2022-23: $169.6M from 14.9M donations. A $3 suggested gift brought 40% more donations and 20% more money.
- **Bucket copy:** One in-app banner, written in the founder's voice, with a $3 default ask. Test two versions. A nonprofit gets to ask; use it.
- Sources: [Wikimedia 2023-24 report](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Fundraising/2023-24_Report), [2022-23 report](https://meta.wikipedia.com/wiki/Fundraising/2022-23_Report), [Chronicle of Philanthropy](https://www.philanthropy.com/article/how-wikimedia-lets-donor-research-guide-its-online-fundraising/)

### Mozilla Firefox

- **Move:** Spread Firefox asked fans to fund a New York Times ad that printed every donor's name.
- **Channel:** Community site, then print as the earned-media moment.
- **Result:** Goal was 2,500 names. About 10,000 people gave around $250,000. The two-page ad ran December 16, 2004, when Firefox had 11M downloads.
- **Bucket copy:** A "founding readers" wall on bucket.foundation listing every early user and donor by name. Turn the wall into the launch post.
- Sources: [Mozilla press release](https://blog.mozilla.org/press/?p=1346), [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2004/10/29/232156/give-me-firefox/), [Slashdot](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/04/10/30/1318239/nyt-firefox-campaign-raises-250000)

### Our World in Data

- **Move:** Free charts and data under CC BY, built in a public GitHub repo, so every outlet can embed and cite them.
- **Channel:** Embeds and citations by media, the WHO, the UN and governments.
- **Result:** COVID data reached over 130M users in about a year, per founder Max Roser. It became a primary global source within months.
- **Bucket copy:** License canon entries CC BY with a one-line citation and an embed snippet. Each citation is a backlink and a brand impression. This fits the paid-to-cite model: reading and embedding stay free.
- Sources: [80,000 Hours interview](https://80000hours.org/podcast/episodes/max-roser-our-world-in-data/), [OWID COVID history](https://ourworldindata.org/owid-covid-history), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_World_in_Data)

## Research-Led Growth

### Stripe Press

- **Move:** Beautiful reissues of important books on technology and progress, run as brand, with revenue secondary.
- **Channel:** Physical books, a design-led website, word of mouth among builders.
- **Result:** Stripe told Axios at launch it does not see the press as a major revenue source. An unsourced estimate puts sales near 1,000 books a day across sixteen titles.
- **Bucket copy:** Reissue public-domain foundations, such as primary derivations already in the canon, as well-set free web editions and cheap print-on-demand, the way the KDP book already does for AGFarms.
- Sources: [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2018/07/17/payments-company-stripe-is-getting-into-book-publishing), [Herbert Lui](https://herbertlui.net/contentions-poor-charlies-almanack-stripe-press-and-why-it-all-works/), [Steve Ruiz](https://steveruiz.substack.com/p/the-press-playbook)

### Astral Codex Ten

- **Move:** Long-form research essays, free by default, with yearly subscription drives that unlock paid posts.
- **Channel:** Substack email and reader word of mouth.
- **Result:** About 20,000 new free subscribers a year in 2024, while paid fell by about 500 a year once in-post prompts were removed. Price $10 a month with a $2.50 option.
- **Bucket copy:** Run one research newsletter from Bucket with one deep post a week. Keep a visible support ask on every post: ACX shows that removing it costs paid members.
- Sources: [ACX 2024 drive](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/subscrive-drive-2024-free-unlocked), [ACX 2023 drive](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/2023-subscription-drive-free-unlocked)

## Patterns for Bucket

1. **Public pages from user work.** Quizlet, Anki and Notion templates grow because each thing a user makes is a URL. Every bucket and deck needs a public, indexable, copyable page.
2. **A daily reason to return.** Duolingo and Brilliant both run on one small daily item. Ship a daily word and a daily foundation.
3. **Name the people.** Mozilla's donor wall, Notion creator credit and Bucket's author payments share one idea: people share what carries their name.
4. **Free to read, easy to cite.** Our World in Data and Wikipedia earned reach by letting anyone reuse the work. Bucket's paid-to-cite model keeps reading and embedding free, which is the part that spreads.

Gaps: no primary source yet for Brilliant revenue, Anki community size or Stripe Press sales. Recheck before external use.
