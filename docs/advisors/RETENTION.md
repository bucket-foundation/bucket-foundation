# Retention

Draft for the founder. Status: unsigned. This document is not legal advice. The founder sets each period. F.

| Data | Kept | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Contact rows in the store | 24 months from the date seen | Verified again or deleted |
| Derived engine builds | The last two | Older builds deleted at the next build |
| Works cache | 12 months | Deleted |
| Query files and saved results | On the asker's own disk | Under the asker's control |
| Reveal log | 24 months | Deleted |
| Do-not-contact rows and the removal log | As long as suppression is needed | Reviewed yearly |
| Removal correspondence | 12 months | Deleted |

The reveal log and the removal log hold a time, an outcome and keyed hashes. They hold no name and no address.

Deleting the derived directory rolls back every build and leaves the store unchanged.
