# Advisor Engine Purpose

Draft for the founder and counsel. Status: unsigned. This document is not legal advice. F marks a decision the founder owns. C marks a point counsel must confirm.

## Purpose

One purpose: helping a person who is applying for research training find advisors to write to, one message at a time. The applicant writes the message. Bucket sends no mail.

## Controller

bucket.foundation is held in the founder's personal capacity pending a nonprofit filing. The founder is the controller today. F.

## What Is Processed

| Step | Data | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Storage | Public scholarly records about researchers: name, institution, country, field, topics, identifiers, recorded advisor and student links, and published contact details with their source | A private store on the founder's machine, outside every repository |
| Derivation | Topic features, link counts, activity years and coverage counts for advisors whose identity match is direct or high | A derived directory on the founder's machine, mode 0700 |
| Listing | An ordered list of advisors with reasons, shown to the person who asked | The founder's machine in slice 1 |

## Compatibility of the Derived Engine

The store was collected to support advisor discovery. The derived engine is a new use of that data and gets its own assessment before any list leaves the founder's machine.

| Question | Draft answer | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Is the new use linked to the original purpose | Yes. Both serve an applicant looking for an advisor. | F |
| What would a listed person expect | A researcher who publishes and supervises expects applicants to find them through public records. A listed person may not expect a private index that orders them. | F writes, C confirms |
| What kind of data | Professional and public. No special category is collected on purpose. Research topics can hint at health, belief or politics in rare cases. | C |
| What are the consequences | A listed person may receive more applicant mail. A wrong identity match can attach one person's papers to another person's name. | F |
| What safeguards exist | Identity matches below high stay out. A removal request hides the whole identity cluster. Addresses stay hidden by default. Every result prints a coverage statement. | F |

## Uses Ruled Out

- Bulk mail of any kind.
- Sale or transfer of the store or of any list drawn from it.
- Scoring people by citation counts or h-index.
- Any use of the store unrelated to advisor discovery, without a new written purpose.
