# Legitimate Interest Balancing Test

Draft for the founder and counsel. Status: unsigned. This document is not legal advice. It assumes legitimate interest is the basis under review. Counsel confirms whether that basis holds for each step. C.

## Scale

The store holds about 21.8 million researcher records and about 460,000 recorded advisors. About 70,000 advisors can be matched to their papers at direct or high confidence. Over 17,000 of those are in EU and EEA states and close to 4,000 are in the United Kingdom.

## Storage

| Part | Draft |
|---|---|
| Interest | Applicants lack a single place to find who supervises work in a field. Public records answer that when joined. |
| Necessity | Joining lineage sources to publication records needs a local copy. A live lookup across sources per query is slower and sends each applicant's interests to third parties. |
| Impact on the person | Public professional data is copied into a private store. The person does not know unless told. |
| Safeguards | Store outside every repository at mode 0700. Each contact row keeps its source, URL and date. A do-not-contact table purges and blocks contact rows. Retention limits in `RETENTION.md`. |
| Open point | Whether individual notice is required or disproportionate at this scale. C. |

## Ranking

| Part | Draft |
|---|---|
| Interest | An applicant needs the few advisors whose work fits, out of tens of thousands. |
| Necessity | Topic overlap needs each advisor's topics. No other personal attribute enters the score. |
| Impact on the person | The person is profiled by research topic and ordered against others. A wrong identity match misstates their work. |
| Safeguards | The score is topical fit alone. Citation counts and h-index stay out of score and row. Medium and low identity matches are left out. An identity audit runs before the order is trusted. A correction route exists. |
| Open point | Whether ordering people counts as profiling that needs a full impact assessment. C. |

## Listing

| Part | Draft |
|---|---|
| Interest | The applicant acts on a list. A list with reasons and stated limits serves them better than a bare search. |
| Necessity | The row shows name, institution, country, field, topics, lineage counts and one public profile link. |
| Impact on the person | More applicant mail. Being listed after asking to leave would be a harm, so removal is honoured on every query. |
| Safeguards | Founder-only in slice 1. Addresses hidden by default. One address at a time, by explicit request, logged. A coverage statement on every result. An untested order carries a plain label and no rank number. |
| Open point | Disclosure of a list to students is a later step with its own test. F and C. |

## Address Disclosure

This step is open. `PURPOSE.md` lists options A, B and C. Each option changes this test.

| Option | Effect on the balance |
|---|---|
| A, never | No address is disclosed. The lightest duty. |
| B, one at a time | Disclosure to a third party enters the test and the notice. Community norms on bulk mail and opt-out bear on how a student uses the address. C confirms. |
| C, relay | Bucket becomes the sender and carries the unsolicited-mail rules of each country. C confirms before any build. |

The founder's own use of a stored address for his own applications is a separate item in `COUNSEL-CHECKLIST.md`.

## Draft Conclusion

Storage and ranking on the founder's machine, with the safeguards above, are proposed as proportionate. Listing to anyone other than the founder waits for counsel. F signs, C confirms.
