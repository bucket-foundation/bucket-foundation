# Notice for People Listed

Draft. Unpublished. This document is not legal advice. Counsel confirms the text before it is published, and says whether individual notice is required. C.

## Who Holds the Data

bucket.foundation, held today in its founder's personal capacity. Contact route: to be set by the founder before publication. F.

## What Is Held

Public professional records about researchers: name, institution, country, field, research topics, scholarly identifiers, recorded advisor and student links, and contact details a person or their institution published, each with its source and the date it was seen.

## Why

To help a person applying for research training find advisors whose work fits theirs. The applicant writes to the advisor. Bucket sends no mail.

## What Is Done With It

Advisors whose identity is matched with high confidence are ordered by topical fit against what an applicant describes. Citation counts and h-index play no part.

## Who Sees It

Today the founder alone, on his own machine. Whether applicants see a list, and whether any applicant sees a published address, is undecided. This notice will be updated before either happens.

## How Long

See `RETENTION.md`.

## Removal

Write to the contact route and give any one identifier: an ORCID, an OpenAlex id, a profile URL, an email address, or a name with an institution. No proof of identity is needed to be removed.

1. The request is matched to every record of the same person.
2. All of those records are hidden from every list, and stored contact details are deleted and blocked from being collected again.
3. If a name matches several people, Bucket asks for one more identifier. Until the answer arrives every candidate is hidden.
4. If no record is held, a block is stored so later collection skips the person.

Being listed again after a removal needs a request through the same channel, or proof of control of the identifier.

A file an applicant saved before a removal is out of Bucket's reach.

## Correction

| Request | What happens |
|---|---|
| These papers are someone else's | The identity match is deleted and blocked from being made again |
| Two people were merged | The records are split and stay out until rebuilt |
| An advisor or student link is wrong | The link is hidden, and the reply names the source that holds it |
| Field, topic or institution is wrong | The value is hidden, and the reply points to the correction route of the source |

## Rights

Depending on where a person lives, they may have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction and objection, and a right to complain to a regulator. C confirms the wording for each jurisdiction.
