## This Week

### 1. Open Source Collective

Due by Oct 30.

Move the repo to an org first. Drafted answers:

- **What does the project do?** Bucket is an open protocol and reference app for paying once for a research paper over x402 and citing it forever, with the citation fee routed to the author. It also ships Bucket Academy, a free spaced-repetition course of 487 science lessons, and Polingual, a 35-language word explorer built on Wiktionary data.
- **License?** MIT for code; the protocol spec is CC0 in intent (`docs/foundation/PROTOCOL.md`).
- **How will you use funds?** Hosting, storage and RPC costs, about $780 a year expected (`03-BUDGET.md` E-1 to E-3), then author payouts through the reference bucket.
- **Who maintains it?** Gianangelo Dichio, sole maintainer, 2,385 commits.

### 2. Anthropic AI for Science

Due by Oct 31.

- **Project:** A grounded Socratic tutor for Bucket Academy. It answers only from a closed set of cited lessons and abstains otherwise. The code is built and ships off until an API key exists (`src/app/api/academy/tutor`).
- **Scientific use:** The Bucket hypothesis engine (`tools/hypothesis-engine/`) generates and scores research hypotheses against a canon of foundations in mathematics, physics, chemistry and biophysics. Every quantitative claim cites a Lean-checked lemma or a logged source.
- **Who benefits:** Students and independent researchers without journal access. The tutor is free with no paywall.
- **Credits needed:** Ask for the program default; usage is unmeasured, so do not name a figure.

- **What is your company going to make?** Polingual is a language app where every word links by meaning, sound, spelling and etymology across 35 languages, and learners earn signed credentials on the CEFR scale that a visa office or employer can verify.
