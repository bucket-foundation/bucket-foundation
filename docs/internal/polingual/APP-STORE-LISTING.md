## Description

Polingual teaches a set of languages at once. Pick one language you know and the languages you want. Each word returns in every language of the set, in one mixed review queue, with shared roots shown side by side.

HOW IT WORKS
• Placement: a short test in each language finds your level so you start where you are.
• Spaced review: words come back right before you would forget them.
• Speak and listen: hear each word, say it back, and get a pronunciation check.
• Grammar in context: gender, conjugation and the past tense appear as you progress.

LANGUAGES
English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, German, Dutch, Swedish, Polish, Finnish, Russian, Greek, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Hebrew, Persian, Turkish, Czech, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai, Tamil, Latin, Ancient Greek, Sanskrit and Old English. Any of them can be the root language.

PRESETS
Romance, Germanic, Iberian and East Asian sets get you started in one tap.

ONE ACCOUNT
Sign in with the email you use for Bucket. Your language systems and progress follow you across iPhone, iPad, Mac, the Bucket app and bucket.foundation.

SUBSCRIPTION
Polingual Monthly and Polingual Yearly open the whole app. New subscribers get 3 days free. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically at the same price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in Settings, Apple Account, Subscriptions. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription.

## Privacy Nutrition Label

Tracking: No. Polingual does not track people across apps or websites and runs no ad or analytics SDK.

Data linked to the person, collected only after they sign in:

| Data type | Use | Linked | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contact info: email address | App functionality: sign-in code and account | Yes | No |
| User content: other user content, the language systems and review history | App functionality: sync across devices | Yes | No |
| Identifiers: user id | App functionality: tie progress to the account | Yes | No |

Not collected: location, contacts, health, financial info, browsing history, search history, diagnostics, audio data. Audio from the Speak button goes to Apple speech recognition and is not stored or received by Polingual. Apple handles payment; once App Store Server Notifications are wired to the backend, add Purchases: purchase history, app functionality, linked.
