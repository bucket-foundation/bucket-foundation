# Multilingual Cities, Language Communities and In-Person Events as a Polingual Market

## Most Multilingual Cities

### Takeaway
NYC leads on documented language count (700+ varieties) with about 3.1 million immigrants, over a third of residents. Toronto (46.6% immigrant), Miami-Dade (about 51%), Dubai (83% or more) and Los Angeles (39.7%) lead on foreign-born share. City comparisons mix definitions and years, so they rank cities roughly.

### Cited Findings
- ELA's NYC language map lists 700 language varieties at over 1,200 sites. The 2019 release listed 637 at nearly 1,000 sites. ELA calls it a partial snapshot focused on Indigenous, minority and endangered languages: [ELA NYC map](https://elalliance.org/our-work/maps/nyc-map); [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/newyork/news/this-interactive-map-highlights-the-700-languages-spoken-in-nyc-041921); [6sqft](https://www.6sqft.com/new-map-shows-over-600-languages-spoken-in-nyc/)
- ELA co-director Ross Perlin says 700 is about 10% of the world's languages. ELA estimates Census data is reliable for only about 60 languages: [National Geographic](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/culture/article/new-york-city-700-languages); [languagemap.nyc](https://languagemap.nyc/Info/About)
- NYC immigrant population is steady at 3.1 million. Immigrants make up more than a third of residents and 43% of the workforce (city release, June 2026): [NYC Mayor's Office](https://www.nyc.gov/ru/mayors-office/news/2026/06/-newest-new-yorkers--report-highlights-new-york-city-s-immigrant)
- China and the Dominican Republic are NYC's two largest immigrant origins, followed by Jamaica and Mexico. A CUNY analysis of 2024 data finds the China, Hong Kong and Taiwan group now larger than the Dominican one: [NextShark](https://nextshark.com/chinese-immigrants-nyc-largest-foreign-group)
- Toronto 2021 Census: 46.6% of residents are immigrants, about 1.29 million people: [City of Toronto 2021 Census backgrounder](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/8f69-2021-Census-Backgrounder-Immigration-Ethnoracial-Mobility-Migration-Religion-FINAL.pdf)
- Dubai ranked first among world cities with 83% foreign-born (World Migration Report 2015 data): [Emirates 24/7](https://www.emirates247.com/news/emirates/dubai-most-cosmopolitan-city-globally-83-population-is-foreign-born-2016-01-17-1.617596). The same report-era data puts Singapore at 38% and Melbourne at 35%: [WorldAtlas](https://www.worldatlas.com/articles/the-most-cosmopolitan-cities-in-the-world.html)
- Greater Melbourne 2021 Census: 53.9% speak a language other than English at home. In the City of Melbourne the share is 46.0%, with Mandarin most common (10,972 speakers), then Cantonese and Indonesian: [profile.id Melbourne](https://profile.id.com.au/melbourne/language)
- Los Angeles foreign-born share is 39.7%, with 64.5% of immigrants from Latin America. Miami-Dade is about 51% foreign-born, with 93% of immigrants from Latin America: [Geography Worlds, citing Census estimates](https://geographyworlds.com/blog/most-diverse-cities-world-guide/). Secondary source.
- London's foreign-born share appears as 36.4% in listicles and 200+ languages are commonly cited. I found no primary ONS figure in this pass: [EF English Live](https://englishlive.ef.com/en/blog/english-in-the-real-world/linguistically-diverse-cities-around-the-globe/)
- World Cities Culture Forum publishes a comparable foreign-born percentage table across member cities: [WCCF data 2022](https://worldcitiescultureforum.com/data-2022/?data-screen=foreign_born_population_percentage)

### Inferences
- Miami and Los Angeles hold the densest Spanish-speaking base in the US, so a Romance-language night there has a ready Spanish-native crowd to anchor the room.
- NYC fits a Romance intercomprehension night: large Dominican, Mexican and other Latin American populations, plus Brazilian, Italian, French and Francophone African communities and the cultural institutes of all four languages.
- Dubai and Singapore have high foreign-born shares, but their mixes lean South Asian and Chinese. A Romance-cluster product fits them less than a South Asian or Sinitic cluster would.

### Gaps
- Houston foreign-born share and current London ONS 2021 figure were not retrieved. ACS 2024 one-year tables would give comparable US city numbers.
- Toronto language count (claims range from 140 to 175+) lacks a primary source here.

## Existing Language Events and Communities

### Takeaway
The in-person language exchange scene runs on a free-entry, bar-funded model (Mundo Lingo, Meetup groups) with venues taking the revenue through drink sales. NYC has several Meetup groups near 20,000 members. Polyglot conferences draw a few hundred to about 800 enthusiasts. None of the large players run gamified quiz formats, which leaves an opening.

### Cited Findings
- Mundo Lingo: founded 2011 in Buenos Aires by Ben Moreira, spread by franchising, free weekly socials in bars. Attendees wear flag stickers for the languages they speak, native on top: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mundo_Lingo); [Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/?p=244230)
- Mundo Lingo funding: bar sales only, no commissions, business collaborations or advertising accepted. This rules it out as a sponsorship partner under current policy: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mundo_Lingo)
- Mundo Lingo scale conflicts by source: 30 cities at the 2019 peak, events suspended in 2020, relaunched in Rio and Buenos Aires in 2022; 39 cities in 24 countries (Sept 2024 mirror) versus 17 cities in 12 countries (Jan 2025): [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mundo_Lingo); [Mundo Lingo history](https://mundolingo.org/history). It has an NYC Meetup group: [Meetup](https://meetup.com/mundo-lingo-new-york-city)
- NYC Meetup: Language Exchange NYC lists 19,758 members and NYC Language Exchange Friends 18,422 (undated counts): [Meetup French topic](https://www.meetup.com/en-AU/topics/french/us)
- Langroops NYC: volunteer-run weekly free events across many languages: [Meetup](https://meetup.com/new-york-langroops-language-exchange). SpeakIt NYC: monthly any-language exchange, $2 suggested donation: [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/speakit-nyc/). The Language Collective: Tuesday exchange at Lulla NYC, one-drink minimum: [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/language-collective/events/315412975/)
- Polyglot Gathering 2026 in Brno, Mendel University: 827 participants from 67 countries, just over half under 45 (attendee blog): [Omniglot](https://www.omniglot.com/bloggle/?p=24618). After English, the most spoken languages were German, Spanish, French, Italian, Russian, Polish and Portuguese: [Omniglot News](https://www.omniglot.com/radio/?p=4480)
- Polyglot Conference 2026 is in Bologna, 18 to 20 November 2026, focused on Italian linguistic diversity: [Omniglot](https://www.omniglot.com/bloggle/?p=24618). Asiaglot 2026 ran 27 to 29 March in Taiwan: [Forumosa](https://tw.forumosa.com/t/mar-27-29-asiaglot-2026/256367)
- "LangFest" at UConn (15 April 2026) is an academic language-science research festival, unrelated to polyglot events: [UConn LangFest](https://www.languagefest.uconn.edu/)
- HelloTalk runs occasional company-blogged offline meetups (London; hopes for an Australia series): [HelloTalk blog](https://www.hellotalk.com/blog/hellotalk-community-offline-meetup). A Tandem, Polyglot Conference and Polyglot.tw joint meetup ran in Taipei: [Luma](https://luma.com/qz1czofc)
- ConversationExchange-style directories list NYC partners for one-to-one meetups: [MyLanguageExchange NYC](https://mylanguageexchange.com/city/New_York_City__United_States.asp)

### Inferences
- The venue-pays model of bar trivia and the attendee-free model of language exchanges are compatible: a language quiz night can be free to attend, funded by the bar or a sponsor, with the app as the hook.
- Existing NYC groups of about 20,000 members are distribution channels. Offering organizers a free hosted quiz round gives Polingual reach without building a community from zero.
- Polyglot Gathering and Polyglot Conference audiences are evangelists, small in number. They suit launches and content more than revenue.

### Gaps
- No verified attendance per event for NYC exchanges. No public data on HelloTalk or Tandem formal event programs in 2026.
- Polyglot Club in-person event volumes were not retrieved.

## Cultural Institutes and City Programs

### Takeaway
NYC's city ESOL network (We Speak NYC through MOIA and the three library systems) runs free weekly conversation classes, a channel for English learners. The cultural institutes sell paid classes and memberships and run social events, which makes them co-host candidates for a Romance-language night.

### Cited Findings
- We Speak NYC: free weekly English conversation classes using city-produced videos and covering rights and city services; no registration, walk-in. Run in person at Brooklyn Public Library branches (some designated MOIA English Learning and Support Centers) and on Zoom through Queens Library, with Summer 2026 sessions listed: [Brooklyn Public Library](https://www.bklynlibrary.org/calendar/free-english-class-crown-heights-library-20260727-1100am); [Queens Library flyer](https://www.queenslibrary.org/sites/default/files/2026-06/Summer%202026%20VIR%20WSNYC%20Flyer%20%2806.03.26%29.pdf)
- MOIA publishes an annual report under Local Law 185 of 2017; the 2024 report covers its programs: [MOIA 2024 Annual Report](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/immigrants/downloads/pdf/MOIA-2024-Annual-Report_4.4.25.pdf)
- L'Alliance New York (formerly FIAF) is an independent nonprofit dating to 1898, HQ at 22 East 60th Street, with a membership offering class discounts, members-only events and screenings. It runs open houses with $20 trial classes and a Montclair NJ campus: [L'Alliance NY](https://lallianceny.org/about/); [Fall Open House 2026](https://lallianceny.org/event/fall-open-house-2026-fete-de-la-rentree/). It sells a conversation "Café Club" class at B1: [CourseHorse](https://coursehorse.com/nyc/classes/language/french/intermediate-french/caf---club--intermediate-b1-)

### Inferences
- Cultural institutes earn from classes and memberships. A joint Romance night lets each one recruit students from the others' audiences, a reason for them to co-host or cross-promote at low cost.
- City ESOL programs are free and grant-run. They are partners for reach and goodwill, with little budget to pay a vendor.

### Gaps
- Instituto Cervantes NY, Goethe-Institut NY, Italian Cultural Institute NY and Japan Foundation NY event formats and sponsorship policies were not retrieved.
- No source found on consulate "international days", UN International Mother Language Day events in NYC (21 February), or university international nights.

## Intercomprehension

### Takeaway
Intercomprehension has 20+ years of EU-funded platforms (Galanet 2003, Galapro, Lingalog, Miriadi from 2015, EuroComRom's "Seven Sieves") and positive but mostly small or non-controlled evidence. I found no commercial consumer product built on it, which leaves room for Polingual.

### Cited Findings
- Galanet (2003) built virtual teams of Romance learners writing a joint article over a semester. Galapro trained teachers. Miriadi, from 2015, hosts group online training and a network of teachers and researchers. Platforms covered Spanish, Catalan, Italian, French, Portuguese and Romanian: [Wikipedia Intercomprehension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intercomprehension); [UniTo Miriadi paper 2016](https://iris.unito.it/retrieve/e27ce435-2c52-2581-e053-d805fe0acbaa/2016_MIRIADI_IC_branchee.pdf)
- EuroComRom's core tool is the "Seven Sieves" filtering transparent lexical, morphological and phonological features. Meißner reports "very encouraging results, with very few exceptions": [Meißner, Core Vocabulary of Romance Plurilingualism](https://epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/40520/1/Meissner_The_Core_Vocabulary_of_Romance_Plurilingualism.pdf)
- A 21-hour Romance intercomprehension module with Italian pupils aged 10 to 11 showed initial plurilingual competence and larger EFL reading gains than a comparison group (pre/post with comparison group): [Taylor & Francis, Int. J. Multilingualism](https://informahealthcare.com/doi/full/10.1080/14790718.2021.2019746)
- UNITA (EU university alliance) intercomprehension courses reached over 2,000 people; the project report says all students who took the final test exceeded the expected level (project report, no control): [UNITA D3.3](https://univ-unita.eu/Ficheiros/Sites/86/Paginas/1255/D3.3_Reports%20on%20the%20effectiveness%20of%20training%20programs.pdf); [UNITA interactive guide](https://univ-unita.eu/our-actions/romance-languages-and-intercomprenhension/interactive-guide/)
- University of Florence runs a 2026 intercomprehension programme with Euniwell Alliance partners: [UniFi IC2026](https://www.cla.unifi.it/upload/sub/IC2026-ENG.pdf)
- Parma CLA pilot reports possible positive psycho-cognitive effects and effective bimodal input; small sample: [Revistas USP](https://revistas.usp.br/italianistica/article/download/68617/73970/94963)

### Inferences
- The evidence centers on receptive skills (reading, listening). Quiz formats test recognition, so they match what the research supports. Claims about speaking gains would overreach.
- All the work sits in universities and EU projects. A consumer app with a social quiz layer would be first to package it for the public.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed controlled evaluation of EuroComRom itself was found. Miriadi current user numbers and funding status were not found.
- No commercial intercomprehension product was found in this pass; a targeted app-store search would confirm.

## Event-Led Growth and Venue Pricing

### Takeaway
Hosted bar trivia sells to venues at about $125 to $250 a night, with themed nights up to $500, on multi-week contracts. Venues pay because trivia fills slow weeknights. Kahoot sells one-time event plans sized by participant cap. This sets the price band for a Polingual venue product.

### Cited Findings
- Geeks Who Drink: venues paid $125 to $200 a week (older Wired figure via blog): [Untappd](https://lounge.untappd.com/how-to-make-money-by-hosting-trivia-night-at-your-brewery-or-bar/). 2019: $200 per night, up to $500 for themed nights, free for players: [Albuquerque Journal](https://www.pressreader.com/usa/albuquerque-journal/20190811/281492162949080). A competitor cites a 2026 sales sheet at $195 per show on 16+ week agreements with venue-supplied prizes (unverified, interested party): [Quizado](https://quizado.com/compare/geeks-who-drink-alternative)
- Geeks Who Drink offers only full-service weekly trivia for US public bars and restaurants, with a dedicated client manager: [Geeks Who Drink FAQ](https://www.geekswhodrink.com/faq/)
- King Trivia does not publish venue pricing; it sells fully hosted and in-house hosted packages: [Quizado](https://quizado.com/compare/king-trivia-alternative). Professional hosts cost $150 to $500 per event: [Untappd](https://lounge.untappd.com/how-to-make-money-by-hosting-trivia-night-at-your-brewery-or-bar/). One operator charges $150 to $250 flat per location: [Crowdpurr forum](https://community.crowdpurr.com/t/pub-restaurant-pricing/298)
- Sporcle virtual live trivia: $5 per device for one hour, $8 for two hours: [Sporcle support](https://support.sporcle.com/hc/en-us/articles/35885201093517-What-Happens-When-I-Buy-a-Ticket-to-Sporcle-s-Virtual-Live-Trivia)
- Kahoot one-time event plans: Event Standard up to 1,000 participants per session, Event Max up to 2,000, each active 30 days; Event Max with onboarding up to 5,000: [Kahoot events pricing](https://kahoot.com/register/pricing-again-events/); [Kahoot support](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/11166126591891). Kahoot Cup 2025 drew nearly 8,000 live players: [Kahoot blog](https://kahoot.com/blog/2025/11/18/kahoot-cup-2025-recap/)
- CNBC reported in 2023 that trivia nights raise bar and restaurant profit: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/04/07/trivia-night-boosts-profit-for-bars-restaurants.html) (headline only; figures not retrieved)

### Inferences
- Revenue paths: (1) venue license at $150 to $250 a night for a hosted language quiz night, matching bar trivia; (2) sponsor-funded free nights where a cultural institute, tourism board or brand pays; (3) app conversion, each attendee joining the lobby from a phone with a QR code that starts the 3-day trial.
- The big-screen lobby works like Kahoot: attendees install or open the app to play, so every event doubles as an install drive. Tracking event-code signups to paid conversions is the key metric.
- Mundo Lingo refuses sponsors; Meetup groups and cultural institutes do not, so they are the first partners.

### Gaps
- Jackbox bar or commercial licensing terms were not found.
- No data found on brand or tourism-board sponsorship of language or cultural nights (Turespaña, Visit Portugal, ENIT Italy, Atout France), or sponsor price ranges for such events.
- No published conversion rates from live events to app subscriptions for any language app.
