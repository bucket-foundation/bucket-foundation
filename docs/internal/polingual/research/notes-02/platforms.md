# Polingual Platform Expansion Beyond Phone and Web

Research date: 2026-11-15. Figures older than 2026 carry their year. Unsourced items sit under Gaps.

## VR, AR and MR: Quest, Vision Pro, Android XR, Pico

### Takeaway
Quest is the only headset base big enough for a paid language product, and its most active users are teens 12 to 16. Language VR apps there stay small: about 100K to 200K lifetime users each, with low six-figure Steam revenue. Vision Pro and Android XR have too few units to justify a native build in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Meta reports record unique Quest users in 2025 but gives no MAU figure. Store developer revenue rose "very slightly" over 2024. Premium sales still earn the most, IAP grew more than 10%, and subscription revenue grew double digits from a small base: [Meta GDC 2026 State of VR](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/blog/gdc-2026-state-of-vr/)
- Teens 12 to 16 are the most active Quest audience, and much discovery happens outside the headset: [Meta GDC 2026](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/blog/gdc-2026-state-of-vr/)
- More than 100 Quest titles passed $1M gross in 2025. Gross is counted before Quest's 30% platform fee: [Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/meta-quest-record-users-2025-100-apps-1-million/)
- Meta Horizon+ has more than 1M subscribers and paid developers nearly $20M in 2025: [Meta GDC 2026](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/blog/gdc-2026-state-of-vr/); [Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/metas-horizon-plus-1m-active-subscriber/)
- Horizon Worlds is moving its focus to mobile, and individual Worlds left the Store shelves: [Meta GDC 2026](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/blog/gdc-2026-state-of-vr/)
- Quest shipped 5.6M units and Vision Pro about 390K to 400K in 2024 (IDC, as reported second-hand): [iTiger/IDC](https://www.itiger.com/news/1111994714)
- Vision Pro shipped about 45K units in Q4 2025, about 600K lifetime, and Apple cut production. The October 2025 M5 refresh did not lift demand: [Technobezz](https://www.technobezz.com/news/apple-slashes-vision-pro-production-after-shipping-45000-hol-2026-01-11-2wu4); [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2026/01/02/apple_vision_pro_meta_quest_sales_drop/)
- Samsung Galaxy XR, the first Android XR headset, launched in October 2025 at $1,799. One low-confidence blog puts 2025 shipments near 70K: [Treeview](https://treeview.studio/blog/xr-spatial-computing-smart-glasses-market-statistics-report)
- Total XR shipments were about 14.3M to 14.5M in 2025, with growth coming from smart glasses. IDC forecasts MR headsets down 27.2% in 2026 and XR glasses up 109.8%: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2026/01/02/apple_vision_pro_meta_quest_sales_drop/); [vr.org](https://vr.org/articles/smart-glasses-outselling-vr-headsets-xr-market-2025-data)
- **Immerse:** $9M Series B in March 2022 led by Eagle Venture Fund and Mustang Creek Capital, then a $5M internal round, likely January 2024, bringing total funding to $16.5M. Sources disagree on the date of the $5M round. Meta gave it a research grant and 500 Quest 2 headsets in 2022, and it calls itself an official Meta education partner. Its model is live teacher-led classes in social VR, sold B2B and to schools: [Immerse blog](https://immerse.com/blog/immerse-raises-9-million-to-become-first-language-immersion-learning-platform-in-the-metaverse); [OCBJ](https://www.ocbj.com/technology/immerse-raises-5m-for-language-learning-app/); [PIE News](https://thepienews.com/news/immerse-partners-meta-vr-learning/); [Mixed](https://mixed-news.com/en/immerse-wants-to-teach-you-languages-in-social-vr/)
- Immerse raised its Quest Store rating from 3.7 to 4.5 and ran a study with Southern Connecticut State University in spring 2025: [Immerse about](https://immerse.com/about)
- **Noun Town:** launched on Quest at $14.99 as a one-time purchase and also sells on Steam. The developer claims more than 200,000 players across 12 languages, and it has added a mixed-reality "Noun Town Home" mode. SteamData estimates Steam gross revenue at $114.66K on 10.95K units, period unstated: [UploadVR](https://uploadvr.com/noun-town-available-now-quest/); [noun.town](https://noun.town/); [SteamData](https://steamdata.ai/en-US/game/1643100/noun-town-vr-language-learning); [UploadVR Home](https://www.uploadvr.com/noun-town-home-wants-to-teach-you-new-languages-in-mixed-reality)
- Noun Town runs a separate education pricing page for schools: [noun.town education](https://noun.town/education/pricing.php)
- **Mondly VR:** launched in 2017 on Daydream, reached Quest in 2021, and claimed 100,000 users. Pearson bought Mondly in May 2022: [Mondly blog](https://www.mondly.com/blog/?p=6211); [Campus Technology](https://campustechnology.com/articles/2022/05/02/pearson-acquires-mondly-language-learning-platform.aspx); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mondly)
- **Vision Pro:** Duolingo runs there as the compatible iPhone/iPad app, with no native visionOS build found. ARLingo was an early native language title: [App Store Vision editorial](https://apps.apple.com/gb/vision/editorial/6503710138); [ARLingo](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6476953173)

### Inferences
- A Polingual VR title should ship as a premium Quest game at $10 to $20, matching Noun Town, with an optional Polingual subscription unlock. A standalone VR subscription would sit in the smallest Store revenue segment.
- The teen-heavy Quest audience fits a party or multiplayer quiz format better than a course format. Polingual Play is the natural VR entry point.
- Immerse shows the money in language VR comes from schools and B2B classes. Consumer VR language apps cap out at tens of thousands of paying units.
- Smart glasses are the growing XR segment, so passive vocabulary overlays on glasses are worth watching over full headset builds.
- A WebXR build of an existing web game reaches Quest, Pico and Android XR browsers with no store fee. This is the cheapest test.

### Gaps
- No Quest-specific sales or download numbers for Immerse, Noun Town, Mondly VR, ImmerseMe or Language Lab. Meta does not publish them, and no tracker was found.
- No Pico user or language-app data found.
- Language Lab and ImmerseMe were not researched in this pass.
- Build tech (Unity or Unreal with the Meta XR SDK, Spatial SDK, WebXR, visionOS SwiftUI and RealityKit) and Apple's and Google's fee structures on visionOS and Android XR are prior knowledge, not verified in this session.

## Kids: Store Programs, COPPA, GDPR-K and Kids Language Apps

### Takeaway
Kids language and learning is a large paid market run on parental subscriptions. Lingokids raised $120M in 2025 at about a $480M valuation, and Novakid grew revenue 34% in 2025. Shipping to under-13s requires COPPA compliance, in full force since April 22, 2026, and no third-party ads or analytics in Apple's Kids category.

### Cited Findings
- The FTC published its COPPA amendments on April 22, 2025. They took effect June 23, 2025, with a full compliance deadline of April 22, 2026: [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/22/2025-05904/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule); [Hunton](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/coppa-rule-amendment-compliance-deadline-approaches)
- The new rules require separate verifiable parental consent before sharing children's data with third parties for targeted advertising, ban indefinite retention, and require a written data retention policy: [Davis Polk](https://www.davispolk.com/insights/client-update/ftc-prioritizes-coppa-enforcement-new-compliance-obligations-take-effect); [BBB Programs](https://bbbprograms.org/media/insights/blog/coppa-amended)
- Maximum civil penalty is $53,088 per violation, and the FTC has made COPPA an enforcement priority: [compliancehub](https://compliancehub.wiki/blog/coppa-april-22-deadline-ftc-updated-rule-compliance); [Davis Polk](https://www.davispolk.com/insights/client-update/ftc-prioritizes-coppa-enforcement-new-compliance-obligations-take-effect)
- Maryland's Kids Code adds state-level duties on top of COPPA: [Freshfields](https://blog.freshfields.us/post/102mop1/a-new-era-for-child-online-privacy-updated-coppa-rule-and-maryland-kids-code-imp)
- Apple guideline 1.3: Kids Category apps should not include third-party analytics or advertising. In limited cases they are allowed if no PII or device fingerprint goes to third parties. The rule dates from 2019: [TechCrunch 2019](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1880829); [Apple developer news 2019](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=06032019j)
- Google Play has a parallel ban on processing certain personal data in child-directed apps: [Revera](https://uae.revera.legal/info-centr/news-and-analytical-materials/83-zapusk-prilozheniya-dlya-detej-kakie-trebovaniya-predyavlyayut-app-store-i-google-play)
- **Lingokids:** raised $120M in 2025 led by Bullhound Capital and General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund, at an estimated $480M valuation. It reports 168M to 185M lifetime downloads. Revenue is undisclosed; Dealroom gives a $10M to $50M range and the last specific figure is $30M for FY2020. It licenses kids IP such as Disney (500M plays) and Blippi (200M plays in year one): [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/lingokids-raises-120m-for-interactive-learning-app-for-kids/); [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/companies/lingokids/); [Kidscreen](https://kidscreen.com/?p=221973); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/lingokids-surpasses-500m-plays-across-disney-activities/); [Blippi release](https://business.mammothtimes.com/mammothtimes/article/gnwcq-2025-10-17-lingokids-exclusive-blippi-content-surpasses-200-million-plays-in-first-year-showcasing-the-power-of-the-1-entertainment-platform-for-unders-8s)
- Lingokids also runs an Alexa skill: [Lingokids Alexa](https://lingokids.com/alexa)
- **Novakid:** live online English tutoring for kids. It reports 34% revenue growth and 49% EBITDA growth in 2025. A secondary blog says revenue passed $80M in 2025 (unverified). It launched an AI speaking app for teens: [Novakid press](https://www.novakidschool.com/press/novakid-delivers-exceptional-yoy-growth-with-ai-driven-expansion-and-strong-financial-performance); [Brand Hopper](https://thebrandhopper.com/featured-startups/novakid-founders-business-model-funding-competitors/); [BriefGlance](https://briefglance.com/articles/novakid-targets-teen-learners-with-ai-native-english-speaking-app)
- **Khan Academy Kids** is free. In Q4 2025 it had 393K to 448K US active users and peak weekly downloads of 30.3K (Sensor Tower): [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-unified-top-5-educational-games-units-us-642f2824e1714cfff1ed8590)
- **Duolingo:** 2025 revenue was $1.04B, up 38.7%. Q3 2025 DAU were 50.5M and paid subscribers 11.5M. Duolingo ABC is a free literacy app, with no download total found: [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1247833/annual-duolingo-revenue/); [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/02/16/3-key-takeaways-from-duolingos-2025); [Similarweb ABC](https://www.similarweb.com/app/google/com.duolingo.literacy)

### Inferences
- A kids Polingual should be a separate app (a "Polingual Kids" listing) with a parent-gated subscription. Mixing under-13 users into the main app pulls the whole app under COPPA and Apple 1.3.
- Lingokids' growth came from licensed kids IP and an entertainment framing. A pure vocabulary drill app competes with free Khan Academy Kids and Duolingo ABC.
- Live quiz lobbies with kids raise chat and voice moderation duties under COPPA. Kids mode should drop free text and voice chat.

### Gaps
- No current text found for Google Play Designed for Families or Teacher Approved criteria. Check Play Console policy pages.
- No GDPR-K (GDPR Article 8 age of consent, 13 to 16 by member state) or UK Age Appropriate Design Code sources pulled in this pass.
- Gus on the Go and Little Pim not researched. No revenue found for Duolingo ABC or Lingokids paid subscriber counts.

## Smart TV and Big-Screen Party Games

### Takeaway
Phone-as-controller TV games are a proven format, and AirConsole has moved it into cars (Audi, Porsche, Skoda, BMW, VW). No language app has a strong TV presence, so Polingual's big-screen quiz lobbies have an open lane.

### Cited Findings
- AirConsole: over 1M monthly active players on pay TV in 2022, about 9M lifetime players in 190+ countries. A third party estimates its Android TV app at about 49.6K MAU: [CSI Magazine 2022](https://csimagazine.com/csi/Vewd-and-AirConsole-team-on-TV-gaming.php); [Sovereign](https://www.sovereignmagazine.com/article/swiss-startup-airconsole-beats-apple-and-google-to-luxury-car-gaming); [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.airconsole.androidtv)
- AirConsole runs inside Vewd OS smart TVs and pay-TV boxes on Android and Linux: [CSI Magazine](https://csimagazine.com/csi/Vewd-and-AirConsole-team-on-TV-gaming.php)
- AirConsole launched in Audi in June 2025 with Mattel's Pictionary Car Party, came to the Porsche Macan at the end of 2025, added Tetris to Skoda EVs in February 2026, and is in BMW and VW (Atari games from October 2025): [Advanced Television](https://www.advanced-television.com/2025/06/26/265657/); [ChannelLife](https://channellife.news/story/porsche-brings-in-car-multiplayer-gaming-to-macan-with-airconsole); [CMOtech](https://cmotech.uk/story/skoda-brings-airconsole-tetris-to-parked-ev-drivers)

### Inferences
- Polingual's live lobby already matches the Jackbox and AirConsole pattern: one shared screen, phones as controllers. A thin tvOS and Android TV/Fire TV host app (or a Cast and AirPlay target from the web lobby) is a small build.
- An AirConsole listing or a licensed language party game for in-car use is a distribution deal, not a store submission. That suits Polingual Play.
- Revenue on TV should come from unlocking the Polingual subscription or a one-time party pack. TV ad models need scale Polingual lacks.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on Apple TV, Fire TV, Samsung Tizen or LG webOS app store sizes, fees or language app performance. Jackbox sales and Netflix Games party titles not researched.
- AirConsole's revenue share terms for third-party developers not found.

## Voice, Cars, Wearables, Extensions, Messaging, Roblox, Fortnite, Minecraft

### Takeaway
Most of these are retention or acquisition channels, not revenue channels. Google killed third-party voice apps in 2023. Browser extensions get bought by bigger apps (Babbel bought Toucan). Discord Activities and Roblox are the two with working in-platform payments and young audiences.

### Cited Findings
- **Google Assistant:** Conversational Actions shut down on June 13, 2023; transactions ended May 1, 2023. Google moved to App Actions on Android: [Google sunset overview](https://developers.google.com/assistant/ca-sunset); [Voicebot 2022](https://voicebot.ai/2022/06/13/google-assistant-actions-voice-apps-to-sunset-focus-shifts-to-android-apps)
- **Alexa:** Spanish skills are being migrated into Spain and Mexico locale variants. Lingokids keeps an Alexa skill: [Amazon FAQ](https://developer.amazon.com/en-US/docs/alexa/faq/spanish-migration-faq.html); [Lingokids Alexa](https://lingokids.com/alexa)
- **Browser extensions:** Babbel acquired Toucan in September 2023, terms undisclosed, and moved Toucan's paid features into the free tier. Toucan runs on Chrome and Edge with about 200K+ users (third-party count). It had raised a $4.5M seed earlier: [EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/09/berlin-based-babbel-acquires-us-based-toucan-to-further-expand-its-learning-ecosystem-with-browser-extension/); [Babbel support](https://support.babbel.com/hc/en-gb/articles/18269893246738-Toucan-browser-extension); [Chromeboard](https://chromeboard.com/extension/toucan-by-babbel-language-lokjgaehpcnlmkebpmjiofccpklbmoci); [Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/toucan-raises-4m-seed-round-language-learning-browser-extension)
- Language Reactor focuses on subtitles inside streaming sites. No acquisition found: [Lingopie](https://lingopie.com/blog/best-chrome-extensions-for-language-learning/)
- **Discord Activities:** open to all developers since September 2024. In-Activity IAP uses the Embedded App SDK (`getSkus`, `startPurchase`, `getEntitlements`). Discord launched Orbs currency in July 2025 and announced Social Commerce at GDC 2026 (second-hand). The fee is reported as 15% on the first $1M then 30%, but sources conflict: [Discord docs](https://docs.discord.com/developers/monetization/implementing-iap-for-activities); [GamesBeat 2024](https://gamesbeat.com/discord-opens-activities-in-app-games-and-features-to-all-developers/); [Cinevva](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-discord-activities); [StraySpark](https://www.strayspark.studio/blog/discord-activities-embedded-app-sdk-indie-game-distribution-2026)
- **Roblox:** on track to pay creators $1B in 2025. Creator Rewards pays 5 Robux per Active Spender who spends 10+ minutes a day in an experience. Roblox raised payouts 8.5% and added text-to-speech and real-time voice translation APIs: [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/roblox-says-on-track-to-pay-out-1b-to-creators-in-2025-thefly); [Roblox DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628); [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/roblox-hikes-creator-pay-85-as-platform-courts-premium-game-devs)
- Duolingo was the first language brand on Roblox, with a branded space fed by a game jam of about two dozen community games and a virtual merch store. It also appeared in Decentraland. This was a marketing activation, date unconfirmed: [TechRound](https://techround.co.uk/?p=67352)
- **Minecraft Education:** the Lesson Library has six subjects including Language and filters by language. Partners include BBC Earth, NASA and the Nobel Peace Center. It is sold as school licenses through Microsoft partners. Its last public user count, over 2M, is several years old: [Minecraft Education blog](https://education.minecraft.net/blog/design-personalized-lessons-with-copilot-teach); [Licensing](https://education.minecraft.net/licensing); [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/minecraft-education-edition-crosses-2-million-users)

### Inferences
- A Discord Activity version of the live quiz lobby is the closest fit of any platform here: Polingual already runs multiplayer web lobbies, Activities are web apps in an iframe, and Discord's audience skews teen and young adult.
- Roblox pays for engagement time, so a language party game there earns small Creator Rewards and works mainly as funnel and brand marketing, as Duolingo used it.
- A Chrome extension that tags words on any page or in subtitles is a cheap acquisition channel into the paid app. Toucan shows the standalone extension business is thin, and the exit is a sale to a larger app.
- A voice-only build on Google is dead. Alexa is weak for a paid product.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on Alexa in-skill purchasing status, Apple Watch app performance, CarPlay audio app rules, WhatsApp or Telegram bot language products, or Fortnite UEFN creator payouts and education islands.
- Language Reactor user counts not found.

## Duolingo and Other Majors on These Platforms

### Takeaway
Duolingo uses the extra platforms for brand reach and habit loops (Roblox activation, an iPhone app running on Vision Pro) and has built no flagship product off phone and web. Babbel went the acquisition route into browsers with Toucan, and Pearson bought Mondly with its VR app.

### Cited Findings
- Duolingo on Vision Pro is the compatible iPad/iPhone app, featured in Apple's Vision editorial picks: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/vision/editorial/6503710138)
- Duolingo's Roblox space was a game-jam marketing activation with a merch store: [TechRound](https://techround.co.uk/?p=67352)
- Duolingo launched a $249 e-piano hardware item alongside AI video calls: [CO/AI](https://getcoai.com/news/duolingo-launches-ai-video-calls-and-e-piano-for-enhanced-learning)
- Babbel bought Toucan in 2023 and made it free: [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2023/09/19/babbel-expands-language-learning-ecosystem-with-toucan-acquisition)
- Pearson bought Mondly, VR app included, in 2022: [THE Journal](https://thejournal.com/articles/2022/04/29/pearson-acquires-mondly-online-language-learning-platform.aspx)
- Lingokids runs on Alexa and YouTube alongside its apps: [Lingokids Alexa](https://lingokids.com/alexa); [Kidscreen](https://kidscreen.com/?p=221973)

### Inferences
- No major has proved a large paid business off phone and web. The open slots for Polingual are big-screen group play (TV, Discord, car) because that matches the lobby product it already has.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm the history of Duolingo's Apple Watch app, its Chrome extension, or any Duolingo Alexa skill from sources in this session. These need a direct check of Duolingo's blog and support site.
- No Sensor Tower or Appfigures data on language app revenue by platform.
