# Language Proficiency Certificates and Tests for Polingual

## English Tests: TOEFL, IELTS, DET, PTE and the Rest

### Takeaway
English proficiency testing is a mature market dominated by a few owners, and it shrank in 2025 as the US, UK, Canada and Australia restricted international students. The Duolingo English Test (DET) is the one startup-style entrant that broke in: $42.0M revenue in 2025, about 4% of Duolingo revenue, now falling.

### Cited Findings
- DET revenue: $42.0M in 2025, $45.6M in 2024, $41.2M in 2023 (10-K revenue table, line "Duolingo English Test 42,006 / 45,640 / 41,212" in thousands). About 8% decline in 2025: [TOEFL Resources on Duolingo 2025 10-K](https://www.toeflresources.com/blog/duolingo-posts-2025-financial-report/)
- Duolingo FY2025 total revenue $1,037.6M, up 39%; DET is about 4% of that: [Duolingo 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828025049514/q3fy25duolingo9-30x25share.htm); [TOEFL Resources](https://www.toeflresources.com/blog/duolingo-posts-2025-financial-report/)
- Quarterly letters do not break out DET; total bookings "include purchases of the Duolingo English Test" alongside ads and in-app purchases: [Duolingo Q3 2025 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828025049514/q3fy25duolingo9-30x25share.htm)
- Analyst estimate of DET volume: about 600,000 tests in 2025, down about 17% from about 723,000 in 2024 (assumes every test sold at $70 list, so rough). Decline attributed to US being less attractive to international students and cheaper at-home tests aimed at Canada and the UK: [TOEFL Resources](https://www.toeflresources.com/blog/duolingo-posts-2025-financial-report/)
- Q2 2025 letter flagged a DET headwind from fewer international applicants (secondary summary): [Fintool Q2 2025](https://fintool.com/app/research/companies/DUOL/earnings/Q2%202025)
- DET price $70 single test: [Cialfo guide 2026](https://www.cialfo.co/blog/the-complete-guide-to-the-duolingo-english-test-2026)
- DET acceptance: "over 6,000 accepting programs in more than 100 countries" per Duolingo's August 2025 booklet: [Duolingo Top Accepting Programs PDF](https://englishtest-static.duolingo.com/media/resources/Top%20Accepting%20Programs.pdf). 5,500+ programs at end of 2024, "double the number since 2020": [DET 2024 year in review](https://blog.englishtest.duolingo.com/the-duolingo-english-test-a-year-in-review-2024). A press item says 6,500+: [Mediainfoline](https://www.mediainfoline.com/education/duolingo-english-test-now-accepted-by-over-6500-universities-worldwide)
- All eight Ivy League schools accept DET for admission; Harvard Medical School and Yale School of Medicine do not for graduate health programs: [Times Higher Education, sponsored](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/student/blogs/duolingo-english-test-2025-accepted-countries-schools-visas); [Cialfo](https://www.cialfo.co/blog/universities-that-accept-the-duolingo-english-test-full-list-by-country-2026)
- DET is not on the UK Home Office SELT list as of 2025; visa routes needing a SELT require IELTS UKVI or PTE UKVI: [Cialfo](https://www.cialfo.co/blog/universities-that-accept-the-duolingo-english-test-full-list-by-country-2026); THE sponsored content claims universities can still issue a CAS on DET: [THE](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/student/blogs/duolingo-english-test-2025-accepted-countries-schools-visas)
- IELTS: 7,786,614 test sessions processed August 2023 to September 2025 (multi-year): [voxbooster compilation](https://voxbooster.com/blog/standardized-language-test-takers-statistics-2026). IDP English test volume fell 18%, 1,584,100 to 1,293,800, in the 12 months to 30 June 2025, India IELTS down 50%: [IDP](https://careers.idp.com/news/New-English-language-testing-data-new-opportunity-to-set-students-up-for-success); [TOEFL Resources](https://www.toeflresources.com/2025/08/)
- PTE (Pearson) volume fell 3% in H1 2026 and Pearson expects decline through 2026: [voxbooster compilation](https://voxbooster.com/blog/standardized-language-test-takers-statistics-2026); E2Language is a listed PTE prep provider on Pearson's site: [Pearson PTE](https://www.pearsonpte.com/articles/prepare-online-with-e2language/)
- Fees, third-party guides: TOEFL iBT about $195 to $270, IELTS $180 to $250, PTE $185 to $250; India: TOEFL INR 18,000 since 1 Oct 2025, IELTS about INR 18,000 to 19,000, PTE about INR 18,000 to 18,900, DET INR 6,100: [num8ers](https://num8ers.com/guides/exam-fees-2026-guide/); [Abroad Cube](https://abroadcube.com/blog/toefl-exam-fees-increased-to-rs-18000); [Galvanize](https://galvanizetestprep.com/toefl-exam-fee/). TOEIC about $120 to $200 (single aggregator): [Linguasphere](https://linguasphere.info/guides/learning/language-proficiency-exam-cost/)
- PTE includes unlimited free score reports; IELTS and TOEFL charge $20 to $30 per extra report: [testbook](https://testbook.com/news/ielts-toefl-pte-exam-fee-comparison-2025/)
- EF SET: free, 50 minutes, no registration or exam fee, CEFR aligned, certificate can be posted on LinkedIn: [EF SET](https://www.efset.org/efset/); [EF SET and CEFR](https://www.efset.org/cefr/). EF's B2B arm Efekta claims 30M+ test takers since 2016 and 50,000+ certifying exams a week (unverified on EF's own site): [Efekta](https://www.efekta.com/en/ef-set-test/)
- ETS acquired PSI (proctoring/testing): [The PIE News](https://thepienews.com/testing-organisation-ets-acquires-psi/). IXL acquired Emmersion, an AI language assessment developer: [Learning Counsel](https://thelearningcounsel.com/articles/industry-news/ixl-learning-acquires-emmersion-developer-ai-powered-language-assessments). Pearson acquired Mondly: [The PIE News](https://thepienews.com/pearson-acquires-online-language-learning-platform-mondly/)

### Inferences
- The high-stakes English test market is contracting with study-abroad policy, so a new entrant in late 2026 is fighting for a shrinking pie against DET at $70 and EF SET at $0.
- DET's 2025 revenue (about $42M) after nine years and Duolingo's brand sets a ceiling on how big a new high-stakes test can get from a consumer app base.
- EF SET is the closer model for Polingual: free, CEFR aligned, LinkedIn shareable, used as a lead magnet for EF's paid products. It has no admissions or visa standing.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2025 TOEFL volume or ETS test revenue found. Cambridge English, Michigan (MET) and iTEP volumes and fees not found.
- Official IELTS volume for 2025 alone not found; IDP figure covers all tests IDP delivers.
- PTE full-year 2025 numbers not confirmed from Pearson's report directly.

## Other Languages: Official Tests, Volumes and Fees

### Takeaway
Each major language has a state-backed certificate run by a cultural or government body; JLPT and DELF/DALF are the largest, and Asian language tests are growing. These bodies control their brand and do not license issuance to apps.

### Cited Findings
- JLPT: 1,718,943 applicants and 1,470,989 examinees in 2024 across 96 countries and areas; about 1.9M applicants in 2025, a record: [JLPT statistics](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/statistics/archive.html); [JLPT 2024](https://jlpt.jp/e/statistics/index.html)
- HSK: 719,000 global candidates in 2024, up from 32,000 in 2004: [China Services Info](https://investinchina.chinaservicesinfo.com/s/202506/16/WS68513029498eec7e1f739ff5/more-in-region-are-seeking-to-learn-chinese.html)
- TOPIK: 360,000 takers in 2022, 420,000 in 2023, 490,000 in 2024, 550,000 January to September 2025 (Korea Ministry of Education): [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/academic-misconduct-on-the-rise-as-topik-test-takers-hit-new-high/10426129); [Korea.net](https://www.korea.net/NewsFocus/Society/view?articleId=279830)
- DELF/DALF: 513,435 candidates in 2025 (+3%), driven by DELF scolaire and DELF Junior; 498,463 in 2024 at 1,182 exam centres; 9M+ candidates since 1985: [France Education international, Feb 2026](https://www.france-education-international.fr/actualites/lettre-fei/2026-02/les-certifications-de-langue-francaise-de-france-education-international-en-forte-hausse); [FEI 2024 figures](https://www.france-education-international.fr/actualites/lettre-fei/2025-03/les-chiffres-2024-des-certifications-de-france-education-international-une-dynamique-en)
- SIELE (Spanish, Instituto Cervantes with UNAM, Salamanca, UBA): SIELE Global about EUR 140 to 155; single modules EUR 55 to 100: [UC3M](https://uc3m.es/ss/Satellite/Idiomas/en/TextoDosColumnas/1371241453103); [UCM](https://www.ucm.es/ccee-en/siele); [iNMSOL](https://www.inmsol.com/siele-exam-fees-and-registration/)

### Inferences
- The combined non-English volumes found (JLPT about 1.9M, DELF/DALF about 0.5M, TOPIK about 0.6M+, HSK about 0.7M) show steady demand for certificates in the languages Polingual likely teaches. Learners preparing for these are a test-prep audience.
- DELF growth comes from school versions; school-age certificates are the growth segment.

### Gaps
- No 2025 candidate counts found for DELE, SIELE, Goethe-Zertifikat, TestDaF, CILS, CELI or CAPLE.
- Official fees for JLPT, HSK, TOPIK, DELE, Goethe not verified (they vary by country).

## Frameworks and US School Credentials: CEFR, ACTFL, Seal of Biliteracy, Credit

### Takeaway
CEFR (A1 to C2) is the global common language for levels; in the US, the Seal of Biliteracy in all 50 states plus DC runs on a short list of approved tests, led by AP, Avant STAMP 4S and ACTFL AAPPL. A new test reaches that market only through state-by-state approval.

### Cited Findings
- Council of Europe manual "Relating Language Examinations to the CEFR" sets the linking process: familiarisation, specification, standardisation, validation: [Council of Europe](https://coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/relating-examinations-to-the-cefr)
- All 50 states and DC allow high schools to award a Seal of Biliteracy: [EdWeek](https://edweek.org/teaching-learning/students-nationwide-can-earn-a-seal-of-biliteracy-how-it-can-be-more-accessible/2024/06); [WV Department of Education flyer](https://wvde.us/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/33894-Seal-of-Biliteracy-Flyer-UPDATE-v1.pdf)
- Most common tests used for the Seal: AP, STAMP 4S, AAPPL: [The Global Seal](https://theglobalseal.com/seal-of-biliteracy)
- Avant says every state with a Seal approves STAMP 4S (vendor claim): [Avant](https://avantassessment.com/?p=1338). AAPPL accepted by "most" state programs; North Dakota lists AAPPL, STAMP 4S, ALIRA, ASLPI: [ND DPI](https://www.nd.gov/dpi/education-programs/english-learnermulticultural-education/north-dakota-seal-biliteracy/ndsb). California keeps its own approved list: [CDE](https://www.cde.ca.gov/SP/ml/apprvdassesmnts.asp)
- STAMP 4S price $79.90 single test; school bulk $39.90 (2 to 19), $24.90 (20 to 99), $22.90 (100 to 499), $19.90 (500+); ACE recommends STAMP 4S for college credit: [Avant pricing](https://rebrand.avantassessment.com/pricing-2); [About STAMP 4S](https://rebrand.avantassessment.com/?p=1906)
- The Global Seal of Biliteracy is a private alternative that accepts third-party tests such as STAMP and SIELE: [The Global Seal qualifying tests](https://theglobalseal.com/qualified-tests-requirements)

### Inferences
- Avant shows the economics of the school testing channel: about $20 to $40 per test at district volume, sold B2B to schools.
- Polingual cannot issue a Seal; it can prep students for STAMP/AAPPL/AP and sell to schools as a Seal-readiness tool.

### Gaps
- ACTFL and ILR scale details, AAPPL price, and CLEP language credit amounts were not sourced in this pass. (Background, unverified here: CLEP offers Spanish, French and German exams; ILR runs 0 to 5 for US government; ACTFL runs Novice to Distinguished.)
- No single authoritative 50-state table of approved Seal tests found.

## How Duolingo Built DET and Won Acceptance

### Takeaway
DET took about four years from 2016 launch to mainstream acceptance, and the step change came from COVID closing test centres in 2020. Duolingo backed it with a research team, a public technical manual, CEFR alignment studies, AI item generation, remote proctoring and a $70 price against $200+ incumbents. Independent validity evidence stayed thin for years.

### Cited Findings
- Launched 2016; created by Burr Settles, author of the first technical manual: [DET technical manual](https://www.peb.ufrj.br/images/documentos/DET-technical_manual-V01.pdf); [THE](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/student/blogs/duolingo-english-test-admissions-myths-facts)
- By fall 2020, 2,500+ institutions accepted it and test takers grew 1,500%+ that year; acceptance was growing before the pandemic: [eLearning Inside](https://news.elearninginside.com/?p=16131)
- Design: computer-adaptive test, items made with human-in-the-loop generative AI so no two tests match, security from browser lockdown, remote proctoring and AI; technical manual dated 15 December 2023 (Cardwell, Naismith, LaFlair, Nydick): [DET technical manual](https://www.peb.ufrj.br/images/documentos/DET-technical_manual-V01.pdf); [DET blog on AI assessment](https://blog.englishtest.duolingo.com/the-duolingo-english-test-ai-driven-language-assessment/); [DET blog on ML test creation](https://blog.englishtest.duolingo.com/test-creation-machine-learning)
- Staff: Jill Burstein leads validity, efficacy and responsible AI research; Routledge handbook on the DET covers remote proctoring and AI items: [Routledge](https://www.routledge.com/The-Routledge-Handbook-of-Digital-Language-Assessment-Innovations-and-Insights-from-the-Duolingo-English-Test/Naismith-vonDavier-Burstein-LaFlair/p/book/9781041152194)
- Independent validity: Isaacs et al. 2023 in Language Testing, 1,881 test takers at a London university, predicted grades for postgraduates, not undergraduates; described itself as the only published DET predictive validity study at the time and noted lack of external validation as a common criticism: [UCL Discovery](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10164102/7/Isaacs_Examining%20the%20predictive%20validity%20of%20the%20Duolingo%20English%20Test%20Evidence%20from%20a%20major%20UK%20university_VoR.pdf); [White Rose](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/215856)
- CEFR alignment study published by Duolingo: [DET blog, CEFR alignment](https://blog.englishtest.duolingo.com/duolingo-test-aligned-with-cefr/)
- Duolingo also puts a CEFR-aligned "Duolingo Score" (out of 160, matching DET) inside its courses; the most advanced courses reach 120, end of B2; first shown in Spanish, French and English: [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-score/)

### Inferences
- Cost to Duolingo: a dedicated assessment-science team, a proctoring operation, and four-plus years of research publications before broad acceptance; COVID gave it the opening. A small app has none of those and no pandemic-scale shock to ride.
- The Duolingo Score is the low-cost analog worth copying: an in-app, CEFR-mapped level with no admissions claim.

### Gaps
- No public figure for Duolingo's total spend or headcount on DET.

## Paths In for a Small App

### Takeaway
Ranked by realism: (1) map Polingual lessons to CEFR and show an in-app level like the Duolingo Score; (2) issue a "Polingual Certificate of Completion, CEFR A2" style digital badge (Open Badges 3.0, LinkedIn shareable) that claims completion and makes no proficiency guarantee; (3) test prep for JLPT, DELF, HSK, TOPIK, SIELE, STAMP and AP; (4) partner as a prep provider listed by a test owner, as E2Language is with PTE. A high-stakes proficiency test of its own is out of reach: it needs validity studies, a psychometrics team, proctoring, and institution-by-institution acceptance.

### Cited Findings
- ALTE Q-Mark: awarded per exam after an audit against ALTE's quality standards (18 per ALTE's current page); only ALTE Full Members' exams can hold it; associate applicants can start the process; mark must be renewed: [ALTE Setting Standards](https://alte.org/Setting-Standards); [ALTE Q Mark guide](https://alte.org/resources/Documents/Guide%20to%20the%20Q%20Mark%20(1).pdf); [ALTE full members](https://alte.org/Our-Full-Members)
- ALTE released a 2026 revision of its Manual for Language Test Development and Examining covering digital assessment and AI: [ALTE](https://alte.org/); [Council of Europe test development page](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/developing-tests-examining)
- Credly supports Open Badges 3.0 and lets earners share to LinkedIn; pricing is quote-based; third-party reports put it at about $2,500 a year for 500 badges plus a $5,000 setup fee (unofficial): [Credly OB 3.0](https://info.credly.com/product/open-badge-3.0); [Credly pricing](https://info.credly.com/pricing-ppc); [Certifier on Credly pricing](https://certifier.io/blog/credly-pricing-is-credly-worth-it-in-2022). Cheaper issuers exist: [Programming Insider on free badge platforms](https://programminginsider.com/free-and-affordable-platforms-for-issuing-online-badges-to-students-in-2026/)
- Test prep market: IELTS preparation estimated at $1.65B in 2024 (low-authority market report); India IELTS and PTE coaching about $240.4M in 2025: [Growth Market Reports](https://growthmarketreports.com/report/ielts-preparation-market); [Credence Research](https://www.credenceresearch.com/report/india-ielts-and-pte-coaching-market)
- Magoosh offers IELTS among other exam prep: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magoosh). No Magoosh acquisition of E2Language found.

### Inferences
- Live quiz lobbies fit a JLPT/HSK/DELF mock-exam mode: timed CEFR-level rounds that end in a level badge.
- A self-issued certificate should say "assessed at CEFR B1 by Polingual" only after an internal standard-setting exercise following the Council of Europe manual; otherwise label it completion. Claiming equivalence to TOEFL or DELF invites trademark and consumer-protection risk.
- The B2B school channel (Seal of Biliteracy prep, per-seat pricing near Avant's $20 to $40) is the most direct route to revenue tied to certificates.
- ALTE membership is for exam boards; it is a multi-year path only if Polingual decides to become one.

### Gaps
- LinkedIn's own rules for "Licenses and certifications" entries and whether a startup can register as a LinkedIn certification partner were not found.
- No reliable size for the non-English language test prep market.
- Official prep-partner program terms (ETS, British Council, Pearson, Instituto Cervantes, Goethe) not retrieved.
