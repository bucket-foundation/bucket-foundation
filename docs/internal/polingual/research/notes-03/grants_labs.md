# Grants and Research Labs for Polingual

Scope date: 2026-11-15. Polingual is an NYC multilingual language-learning app with games and live quiz lobbies, aimed at learners of a third language and beyond, with an intercomprehension angle.

## US Federal Grants and Budget Status

### Takeaway
The two federal programs that fit a for-profit edtech startup are NSF SBIR/STTR (Learning and Cognition Technologies topic, up to $2M non-dilutive across phases, zero equity) and ED/IES SBIR ($250K Phase IA/IB, $1M Direct to Phase II). The FY2026 IES SBIR deadline (June 29, 2026) has passed and FY2027 is unannounced. SBIR was reauthorized through 2031 in April 2026, so the programs are stable even as federal language programs (Title VI/IFLE, STARTALK, FIPSE) were cut or ended.

### Cited Findings
**SBIR reauthorization**
- S. 3971 became Public Law 119-83 on April 13, 2026; one guide reports it extends SBIR/STTR through September 30, 2031. From FY2027, agencies must cap the number of proposals per small business. Source: [GrantedAI reauthorization guide](https://grantedai.com/learn/guides/sbir-reauthorization-2026-guide) (secondary; verify on sbir.gov)
- Agencies released new solicitations after reauthorization. Source: [ASBTDC](https://asbtdc.org/agencies-roll-out-new-solicitations-following-sbir-reauthorization/)

**NSF SBIR/STTR (America's Seed Fund), Learning and Cognition Technologies (LC)**
- The LC topic funds startups turning peer-reviewed learning and cognition research into products for education, workforce, training and wellbeing; NSF "will invest up to $2 million in seed funding and take zero equity." Process: Project Pitch, then Full Proposal, then review. Funded examples include game-driven learning companies (Prisms of Reality VR math, Muzology). No deadlines listed on the page; NSF uses an email notice list. Source: [NSF Seed Fund LC topic](https://seedfund.nsf.gov/topics/learning-cognition-technologies/)
- Eligibility: US small business under 500 employees, at least 50% owned by US citizens or permanent residents, all work in the US, PI at least 20 hours/week at the company. Clinical studies are not funded; limited human-subject feasibility studies may be. Source: [NSF Seed Fund LC topic](https://seedfund.nsf.gov/topics/learning-cognition-technologies/)
- Third-party listings name LC subtopics including LC4 Advanced Analytics for Learning and Cognition and LC5 Innovative Approaches to Multimodal Learning. Source: [GrantedAI LC listing](https://grantedai.com/grants/learning-and-cognition-technologies-lc-national-science-foundation-small-busine-ab9dfb4c) (not confirmed on NSF page)
- Phase I amount: sources conflict, "up to $305,000" vs. a statutory cap of $314,363. Deadline reports also conflict (one cites a November 4, 2026 full-proposal deadline). Sources: [GrantProbe](https://grantprobe.com/articles/sbir-phase-i-2026); [Grantsights](https://grantsights.com/blog/nsf-sbir-guide); [BWCO](https://www.bwcoconsulting.com/fod/nsfsbir)
- NSF FY2026 budget: Congress cut NSF 3.4% to about $8.75B, rejecting the deeper White House cut. The FY2027 request would cut STEM Education to $428M (-54%). The April 2025 terminations hit the STEM Education directorate hardest. No SBIR pause reported. Sources: [AIP FYI](https://www.aip.org/fyi/funding-cuts-hit-stem-career-pipelines); [APS News](https://www.aps.org/apsnews/2026/04/nsf-lags-trump-proposes-cuts); [Physics Today](https://physicstoday.aip.org/news/multiple-science-agencies-escape-trumps-proposed-cuts)

**ED/IES SBIR**
- FY2026: three solicitations released April 30, 2026. Phase IA (prototype of a new ed-tech product) and Phase IB (new component for an existing product) at $250,000 over 9 months; Direct to Phase II at $1,000,000 over 2 years. All due June 29, 2026. Closed now. Sources: [GrantedAI IES SBIR blog](https://grantedai.com/blog/ies-sbir-fy2026-phase-ia-ib-direct-to-phase-ii-june-29-deadline-edtech-250k-1-million-strategy); [IES solicitation info](https://ies.ed.gov/funding/research/programs/small-business-innovation-research-sbir/solicitation-information)
- FY2027 ED/IES SBIR solicitation not yet announced. Source: [Team-80 SBIR guide](https://team-80.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-for-sbir/)
- IES context: Feb 2025 cut of 160+ contracts and grants (~$900M). In August 2026 IES announced FY2027 research grants in three waves; field-initiated competitions slated for December, with fewer topics (STEM, literacy, low-incidence disabilities, workforce transitions) organized into "hubs." Sources: [AERA](https://www.aera.net/Newsroom/AERA-Highlights-E-newsletter/AERA-Highlights-February-2025/AERA-Responds-to-the-New-Administration-Efforts-to-Dismantle-Scientific-Research-and-DataNCES-and-IES-Take-Big-Hit); [EdWeek Aug 2026](https://www.edweek.org/policy-politics/after-months-of-standstill-ed-dept-announces-new-federal-research-funding/2026/08); [EdWeek Sep 2026](https://www-edweek-org.ezproxy.baylor.edu/policy-politics/ed-dept-risks-letting-180-million-for-research-expire-as-deadline-looms/2026/09)

**Title VI / IFLE, Language Resource Centers, Fulbright-Hays**
- In September 2025 ED sent all 228 Title VI grantees non-continuation notices across all seven programs, including LRCs, cutting the final year of the four-year cycle. ED requested no IFLE funding for FY2026. Sources: [Springer, IFLE shutdown article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/s41276-026-00573-2); [NFLRC LRC funding history](https://nflrc.org/language-resource-center-funding-history-1990-2026/)
- One source reports FY26 appropriations (law of Feb 3, 2026) allocated $80.7M for Title VI, $5M below FY25; spring 2026 sources say grant competitions were still in limbo. Source: [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/s41276-026-00573-2); [ASEEES](https://aseees.org/newsnet-article/title-vi-today-and-the-futures-of-seees/)
- FY2027 request proposes an $81M cut to International and Foreign Language Education. Source: [Higher Ed Dive](https://www.highereddive.com/news/how-higher-ed-would-fare-in-trumps-latest-budget-proposal/816653/)
- Campus impacts: Harvard suspended language study fellowships (Dec 2025); KU and UMich programs lost funding. Sources: [Harvard Crimson](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2025/12/2/language-study-fellowships-suspended/); [Kansan](https://www.kansan.com/news/federal-cuts-slash-ku-language-grants-leaving-programs-scrambling-for-aid/article_91c905e2-27a4-4c37-a1b1-68edb6647789.html); [Michigan Daily](https://www.michigandaily.com/news/academics/title-vi-funding-cuts-threaten-umich-foreign-language-and-area-studies/)

**FIPSE**
- Nov 10, 2025: ED restructured FIPSE, discontinued seven congressionally directed programs and redirected ~$171M to AI, civil discourse, accreditation and short-term programs. Jan 2026 awards: ~$50M each to AI, civil discourse and Workforce Pell. FY2027 request zeroes FIPSE. Sources: [Inside Higher Ed](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/government/2026/01/06/new-accreditors-civic-discourse-programs-win-fipse-grants); [Higher Ed Dive](https://www.highereddive.com/news/how-higher-ed-would-fare-in-trumps-latest-budget-proposal/816653/); [ACCT](https://perspectives.acct.org/stories/fipse-funding-under-the-second-trump-administration)

**DoD / NSA language programs**
- STARTALK (NSA-funded) was decommissioned April 30, 2025; portal taken offline. Source: [Wikipedia, STARTALK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STARTALK_(language_program)); [CAL STARTALK](https://www.cal.org/projects/startalk/)
- The Language Flagship is sponsored by NSEP and funds university Flagship programs (e.g., Russian Flagship at UGA). Source: [UGA Russian Flagship](https://russianflagship.uga.edu/about/)

**NY State / NYC**
- ESD Pre-Seed and Seed Matching Fund is equity (portfolio includes edtech Hiveclass). NYS Innovation Venture Capital Fund invests Seed to Series B. Luminate NY runs accelerator rounds. Global NY grants ($5K-$100K) target exports. Sources: [ESD Pre-Seed](https://esd.ny.gov/pre-seed-and-seed-matching-fund-program); [Instrumentl Global NY](https://www.instrumentl.com/grants/global-ny-fund-grant-program)

### Inferences
- NSF SBIR LC is the best federal fit: a game-based multilingual app with learning data maps onto "multimodal learning" and "learning analytics" subtopics, and a Project Pitch costs little to submit. Pitch can go in now.
- IES SBIR Phase IA fits a K-12 or postsecondary classroom product. Polingual would need a school-facing use case (e.g., quiz lobbies for language classrooms). Watch for the FY2027 solicitation, likely spring 2027 based on the FY2026 timeline.
- Title VI LRCs, STARTALK and FIPSE are poor bets as funders for 2027. Their cuts make LRC staff more open to partnerships with a free tool, since their own budgets shrank.
- NYS programs give equity and accelerator support, no matching grant for edtech.

### Gaps
- NSF Seed Fund's official current deadline and Phase I cap were not confirmed on nsf.gov.
- NEH: no current program found for language-learning software; NEH Digital Humanities Advancement Grants were not checked this session.
- NIH bilingualism/aging (NIA, NICHD) programs and NSEP/Flagship FY2026 budget status: not found.
- NYCEDC programs: no edtech-relevant grant surfaced.

## Non-US Funding

### Takeaway
Erasmus+ KA220 Cooperation Partnerships is the main home for intercomprehension projects, but it needs an EU-based applicant established 2+ years; Polingual would join as an associated or non-EU partner. The 2027 call is the last of the 2021-2027 programme and is expected around Nov-Dec 2026 with a deadline near early March 2027. EIC Accelerator 2026 cutoffs are all past; Innovate UK Smart grants are reported closed.

### Cited Findings
- Erasmus+ 2026 KA2 partnership deadline was 5 March 2026; a second round with a 1 October deadline exists at some National Agencies. The 2027 call is expected Nov-Dec 2026. Coordinators must be legally established 2+ years before the deadline. The new Erasmus+ programme starts 1 January 2028. Sources: [growthprojects.eu](https://growthprojects.eu/erasmus-2026-deadlines/); [erasmusportal](https://en.erasmusportal.com/haberler/2027-erasmus-cagrisi-son-basvuru-yili); [EC Cooperation partnerships](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/programme-guide/part-b/key-action-2/cooperation-partnerships)
- Past Erasmus+ intercomprehension projects: EVAL-IC (2016-2019, ref 2016-1-FR01-KA203-024155) built assessment descriptors for Romance intercomprehension; MIRIADI produced the REFIC and REFDIC competence frameworks; PEP (plurilingual education) held a Rome conference Oct 2025. Sources: [Salzburg EVAL-IC page](https://www.plus.ac.at/language-centre/research/projects/completed-projects/eval-ic/?lang=en); [Eaquals report](https://www.eaquals.org/2019/08/07/brief-report-on-participation-in-eu-funded-projects-on-intercomprehension-by-richard-rossner/); [Hamburg conference paper](https://www.conferences.uni-hamburg.de/event/536/contributions/2890/contribution.pdf)
- EIC Accelerator 2026 full-proposal cutoffs: 7 Jan, 4 Mar, 6 May, 8 Jul, 2 Sep, 4 Nov, all passed. Short proposals accepted continuously. 2027 dates pending the 2027 Work Programme. Sources: [be-funded](https://www.be-funded.de/en/blog/eic-accelerator-grant/); [ipernovation](https://ipernovation.eu/blog/eic-accelerator-2026-changes/)
- Innovate UK Smart grants: one guide says as of September 2026 they are closed to new applications; other listings conflict. Source: [Zengrants guide](https://www.zengrants.co/blog/innovate-uk-grant-guide-2026); [GrantMatch](https://www.grantmatch.co.uk/blog/guides/innovate-uk-grants)

### Inferences
- The fastest Erasmus+ path is to join a KA220 consortium led by an intercomprehension group (Grenoble LIDILEM, Aix-Marseille, Salzburg) as the technology partner, offering the app and its games as the project's learner-facing output.
- EIC and Innovate UK need an EU or UK legal entity; both are out of reach for a US-only company.

### Gaps
- Horizon Europe Cluster 2 calls on multilingualism, Canada heritage-language funding (Canadian Heritage, Official Languages), Goethe-Institut and Instituto Cervantes partnership funds: not researched this session.

## Foundations and Corporate Grants

### Takeaway
Foundation money for edtech in 2026 flows through intermediaries (Digital Promise for Gates, the Learning Engineering Tools Competition). Spencer and Duolingo fund researchers, so Polingual reaches them by partnering with an academic PI.

### Cited Findings
- Learning Engineering Tools Competition: 2026 cycle offered up to $500K in prizes with a $50K Catalyst tier for early-stage tools that support learning outcomes and research. Source: [Grantable listing](https://grantable.co/grants/learning-engineering-tools-competition-grant-e627c131edb6)
- Gates via Digital Promise: AIMS EduData R&D call (due March 2, 2026) funds projects using data from large learning platforms; a $26M Gates-backed program funds open datasets, models and benchmarks for AI in education. A Gates "AI Transforming Learning" grant listed $150K-$200K, deadline Sept 11, 2026. Sources: [Digital Promise AIMS RFP](https://digitalpromise.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/AIMS-Phase-II-Jan-RFP.pdf); [EdTech Innovation Hub](https://www.edtechinnovationhub.com/news/digital-promise-program-opens-8-million-ai-tutoring-grant-backed-by-gates-foundation); [UAlbany](https://www.albany.edu/ocfr/news/2026-gates-foundation-ai-transforming-learning-grants)
- Spencer Foundation Small Research Grants on Education: up to $50,000, two cycles a year, last full-proposal deadline April 15, 2026. Large grants $125K-$500K, deadline June 23, 2026. Applicants are researchers. Sources: [Northwestern](https://research.soc.northwestern.edu/spencer-foundations-small-research-grants-due-apr-15-2026); [UCalgary](https://research.ucalgary.ca/opportunity/2026-research-grants-education-large)
- Duolingo Dissertation Research Grants 2026: ten $5,000 awards (five in second-language learning), due May 31, 2026, closed. TIRF-Duolingo grant up to $5,000 for EFL. Duolingo English Test Competitive Research Grants: up to three awards of up to $120,000, deadline Jan 16, 2026. Sources: [Duolingo research](https://www.duolingo.com/efficacy/research); [2026 CFA](https://duolingo-papers.s3.amazonaws.com/reports/2026_Duolingo_language_dissertation_grant_cfa.pdf); [TIRF](https://www.tirfonline.org/tirf-and-duolingo-to-launch-collaborative-doctoral-dissertation-grant/)

### Inferences
- Duolingo dissertation grants are a recruiting channel: a 2027 grantee studying L3 or intercomprehension could run the study on Polingual.
- The Tools Competition Catalyst tier ($50K) is the best near-term foundation fit for a startup.

### Gaps
- No current Chan Zuckerberg, Schmidt Futures, Walton, Mozilla or Google.org call specific to language learning found. Schmidt's current role in the Tools Competition is unconfirmed.

## Research Labs and Studies Polingual Could Feed

### Takeaway
The best collaborators work on third-language acquisition, cross-linguistic influence and incidental learning through games. Polingual's data on learners who already speak two languages, and on related-language practice, matches open questions in L3 research and intercomprehension assessment.

### Cited Findings
- CMU (Dietrich College): a 2026 study led by Seth Wiener found a wordless game trained adults on Mandarin tones as well as explicit instruction; it builds on Lori Holt's incidental-learning lab and was funded by NIH and the journal Language Learning. Source: [CMU 2026 news](https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/news/news-stories/2026/wiener-game-based-learning)
- CMU and Duolingo: Duolingo set up research fellowships at CMU in second language acquisition via Alina von Davier. Source: [CMU 2022](https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/news/news-stories/2022/ml-vondavier.html)
- UMD: Naomi Feldman's NSF-funded computational models of adult speech-sound learning; UMD language science covers SLA across the lifespan; CASL (now under ARLIS) ran a joint study with Voxy on instruction by cognitive profile. Sources: [UMIACS](https://www.umiacs.umd.edu/news-events/news/clip-researchers-create-computational-models-explore-how-adults-learn-new); [UMD Language Science](https://languagescience.umd.edu/node/79); [Voxy blog](https://voxy.com/blog/index.php/tag/brain/)
- Third-language acquisition field: IAM L3 2026 (14th conference) held 3-5 Sept 2026 in Poznan. Active groups: Stockholm University Multilingualism and Third Language Acquisition group; UIC Multilingual Phonology Lab (adult L3). Recent studies use eye-tracking and artificial languages to measure cross-linguistic influence in L3 from first exposure. Sources: [IAM L3 2026](https://easychair.org/smart-program/IAML3Conference2026/room3.html); [Stockholm](https://www.su.se/english/research/research-catalogue/research-groups/9/multilingualism-and-third-language-acquisition); [UIC MultiPhon](https://hip.uic.edu/research-groups/multiphon/); [Second Language Research eye-tracking](https://metatoc.com/papers/172767-selective-cross-linguistic-influence-in-l3-acquisition-ab-initio-an-eye-tracking-study)
- Intercomprehension groups: Grenoble LIDILEM (Galatea, Galanet platform, Louise Dabene); Aix-Marseille (EuRom4/EuRom5 method, Blanche-Benveniste; current master's course by Saffi, Caddeo and colleagues); EuroComRom ("seven sieves" method, Giessen-led). Sources: [Carrasco Perea, GERFLINT](https://www.gerflint.fr/Base/Europe1/Encarnacion.pdf); [Degache et al.](https://laces.u-bordeaux.fr/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/2010-Beitrag-BonvinoCaddeoEscude_III-14-1.pdf); [Revista Studii UVVG](https://revista-studii-uvvg.ro/?p=1643)
- Salzburg University Language Centre participated in EVAL-IC and runs a current KA220-HED project (CLEVER-AI). Source: [PLUS Salzburg](https://www.plus.ac.at/language-centre/research/projects/laufende-projekte/clever-ai/?lang=en)
- University of Hawaii NFLRC tracks LRC funding and is itself an LRC affected by the cuts. Source: [NFLRC](https://nflrc.org/language-resource-center-funding-history-1990-2026/)

### Inferences
- Studies Polingual could feed: (1) L3 cross-linguistic influence, which background language transfers when a Spanish-English speaker starts Portuguese or Italian; (2) intercomprehension gains measured against EVAL-IC descriptors; (3) incidental learning in games, CMU style; (4) live quiz lobbies as a test bed for social or competitive learning effects.
- Give the app plus an anonymized data-export and consent flow to UIC MultiPhon, Stockholm L3 group, CMU Wiener/Holt, and LIDILEM or Aix-Marseille; this also seeds STTR partners for an NSF proposal.

Their current studies and 2026 status after Title VI cuts (CALPER and CARLA are LRCs) need direct lab-site checks.
