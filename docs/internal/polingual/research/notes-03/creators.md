## Creator Economics

### Takeaway
Education sponsorships price at about $15 to $45 per 1,000 views. Language-app affiliate programs pay one-time CPA of $10 to €75 per sale. A 25% recurring revenue share beats those one-time payouts if a referred subscriber stays more than a few months.

### Cited Findings
- Education sponsorship CPM 2026: $25 to $45 per 1,000 views in one guide, so a 100K-view integration pays $2,500 to $4,500 — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-sponsorship-rates/)
- Another dataset: education $15 to $35 per 1,000 views for a 60-second integration — [dev.to Spansa](https://dev.to/tryspansa/youtube-sponsorship-rates-in-2026-what-brands-pay-1dh0)
- Ad RPM context: education median RPM $10.22, Studio CPM $5.91 (ad revenue, separate from sponsorship) — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/which-youtube-niche-makes-the-most-money-in-2026-ranked-by-real-rpm-and-cpm)
- Babbel partner program on Impact: up to €75 per app sale, €80 for Babbel Live per Babbel's partner page as reported; FlexOffers lists 16% of subscriptions; Lasso lists $32 per sale; cookie 30 to 45 days — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/babbel-affiliate-program); [Lasso](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/babbel/)
- italki official: at least $10 per new student's first credit purchase; third parties list $14.40 to $18 — [italki help center](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/4405697269785-What-is-the-italki-Affiliate-Program); [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/italki-affiliate-program)
- Preply: most aggregators list $30 one-time per first lesson booking, 30-day cookie; others list $10 or percentage figures; no official page found — [Lasso](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/preply/); [Awin](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/21098)
- Lingoda: standard affiliate €25 to €55 per sale on Daisycon, €40 on FlexOffers; €100 belongs to the separate creator Ambassador program; contact affiliate@lingoda.com — [Daisycon](https://daisycon.com/campaigns/18366-lingoda-int); [Copycat Cafe](https://copycatcafe.com/blog/lingoda-affiliate-program); [Lingoda](https://www.lingoda.com/en/affiliate-program/)

### Inferences
- A 25% recurring share is competitive against the field, which pays one-time CPA. On a hypothetical $10/month subscription, 25% is $2.50 a month; it overtakes italki's $10 floor after 4 months and Preply's $30 after 12. Against Babbel's €75 it loses unless retention runs past two years. Pair the rev share with a small flat fee or a first-month bounty for large creators.
- For a 1M-subscriber education channel averaging 100K views, a paid integration costs $1,500 to $4,500 per video at the cited CPMs.

### Gaps
- No official Preply affiliate page was found. Babbel's €75 figure is reported second-hand.
- App Store commission (15% small business, 30% standard) was not sourced in this session; net-of-Apple math needs that check.

## TestFlight and Offer Code Constraints

### Takeaway
TestFlight allows 10,000 external testers via a public link, needs Beta App Review on the first external build, and each build expires after 90 days. Free access after launch goes through Offer Codes: up to 1M redemptions per app per quarter, custom codes in batches up to 25,000.

### Cited Findings
- "Invite up to 10,000 external testers"; public link for social and email; disable the link when the limit is hit; first build needs App Review for TestFlight and builds are sent for review when added to a group; up to 100 builds; testers use up to 30 devices — [Apple TestFlight](https://developer.apple.com/testflight/)
- Builds are testable for up to 90 days; each new build gets a new 90-day window — [App Store Connect Help](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/overview-of-testflight/); [Apple forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/702282)
- Developer forum reports from September 2026 describe builds expiring early, including after removing external testers — [Apple forums 848523](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/848523); [Apple forums 841920](https://origin-devforums.apple.com/forums/thread/841920)
- Offer Codes: up to 1M codes per app per quarter shared across subscriptions; up to 10 active offers per subscription; custom codes up to 25,000 redemptions at a time and reusable; one-time codes expire within six months; one redemption per customer per offer — [App Store Connect Help](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/set-up-subscription-offer-codes); [Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/apple-subscription-offers-guide)
- Older sources cite 150,000 codes per quarter; that limit is superseded — [developer-tech](https://www.developer-tech.com/news/developers-can-now-create-custom-app-store-offer-codes/)

### Inferences
- A per-creator custom code (for example LINDIE30) gives free access and attribution at once, at up to 25,000 redemptions per batch.
- For a creator beta, ship a fresh build every 60 to 80 days so the public link never points at an expired build.

### Gaps
- Whether a single public link can be split per creator for attribution was not checked; separate tester groups each get their own public link per Apple's group model, unconfirmed here.
