# Brand Partnerships, Authority Signals and Success Stories for Polingual

Scope: brand partnerships run by language apps, the logos an early edtech startup can show on its site without legal risk, and documented growth stories. Material already in docs/growth/EXTERNAL-CASE-STUDIES.md is left out here: Duolingo TikTok, Speak's Korea-first launch and $1B valuation, Anki, Brilliant, Khan, Quizlet. Searches ran November 2026. Fifteen tool calls in total, so several items stay listed as gaps.

## Brand Partnerships Language Apps Have Run

### Takeaway
The documented deals are co-marketing swaps with no money disclosed. Each side puts its mascot or content inside the other's product, and the trigger is a cultural moment: a show premiere, a game event, the Super Bowl. Duolingo runs most of them. For Babbel, Memrise and the travel brands, the deals named in the brief could not be confirmed. The closest real precedents are KAYAK x Babbel, uTalk and Rosetta Stone in airline seatback screens, and EF's Olympic contracts.

### Cited Findings
- Duolingo x Netflix, "Learn Korean or Else", announced December 10, 2024, ahead of Squid Game season 2. Duo appeared as a Pink Guard, and the course added more than 40 phrases from the series. Duolingo cited a 40% spike in learners picking Korean when season 1 came out in 2021. — [Seeking Alpha PR](https://seekingalpha.com/pr/19942562-duolingo-and-netflix-partner-launch-learn-korean-or-else-campaign-ahead-of-squid-game-season); [Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/duolingo-owl-squid-game-netflix-tiktok-campaign/735128/); [TechRadar](https://techradar.com/streaming/netflix/netflix-teams-up-with-duolingo-ahead-of-squid-game-season-2-premiere-and-i-didnt-think-that-bird-could-get-any-scarier)
- Duolingo x Brawl Stars (Supercell), September 19 to 30. Duo entered the game as a boss in a 20v1 mode set in "Duo's classroom", with language questions inside the fight, and the Brawl Stars character Spike appeared inside Duolingo. Two-way placement of each mascot in the other's product. The source does not state the year. — [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/duolingo-brawl-stars-crossover/)
- Duolingo x Eggy Party (NetEase), China, Duolingo's first game partnership there. A three-day Duolingo streak unlocked exclusive Eggy Party rewards. The quest went live August 26, and an Eggy Island crossover followed September 4. The ad won at Cannes Lions. — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/super-bowl-commercial-2024); [Cannes Lions](https://lovethework.com/work-awards/campaigns/duos-big-game-reminder-1624577); [Stylus](https://stylus.com/retail-brand-comms/super-bowl-2024-3-key-themes); [Digiday CMO interview](https://digiday.com/marketing/its-creativity-its-not-media-budget-how-duolingos-cmo-manu-orssaud-advertised-around-the-super-bowl/)

### Inferences
- Deals open to a seed-stage app follow the Eggy Party and Brawl Stars shape: a content or cosmetic swap with a mid-size game, indie game or creator, with no cash on either side. Paid sponsorships like EF's Olympic contracts need an enterprise sales team and service delivery.
- The 2026 FIFA World Cup is in North America, with matches at MetLife in the NY/NJ area. Polingual can run an unofficial "learn fan phrases" pack the way Tandem and busuu did, as long as it avoids FIFA marks.
- For a NYC app aimed at bilinguals, cultural institutes and consulates fit: Instituto Cervantes, Goethe-Institut, Alliance Française, Italian Cultural Institute, Japan Society, Korean Cultural Center. Also heritage parades and festivals such as the Puerto Rican Day Parade and the Lunar New Year festival. These are inferred targets; no language-app precedent was found for them.
- Babbel's travel pattern points to KAYAK-style deals with booking or eSIM companies, such as Airalo, Holafly or Saily. Saily already sponsors Xiaomanyc (see below).

### Gaps
- No deal values or revenue splits were disclosed for any Duolingo partnership.
- Memrise x Netflix, Duolingo x Roblox, Duolingo x Spotify, Babbel x Lufthansa and Babbel x hotels: no confirming source found within the search budget.
- No 2026 World Cup or tourism-board language-app deal surfaced.
- The Brawl Stars collaboration year is not stated in the source found.

## Authority Logos a Startup Can Use Legitimately

### Takeaway
A wall of press logos is a marketing claim. NAD has treated "As Seen In" logos as endorsements, so each logo has to match coverage that exists and that a consumer would read as favorable. Platform badges such as Apple's must follow the owner's artwork rules. Logos of customers, schools or partners need written permission.

### Cited Findings
- In a challenge to LegalZoom's "As Seen In" box, NAD applied the FTC Endorsement Guides. It found that publication logos reasonably convey that the outlets spoke favorably about the product, and noted that the Guides count an organization's name or seal as an endorsement. — [Kelley Drye Ad Law Access](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/nad-decision-considers-as-seen-in-claims); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/advertising-marketing-branding/1507904/nad-decision-considers-as-seen-in-claims)
- If the advertiser has a material connection to the outlet, such as a paid placement or sponsored post, the advertiser must disclose it on its own page. A disclosure on the outlet's site does not count. — [Kelley Drye](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/nad-decision-considers-as-seen-in-claims)
- Practitioner guidance says to get permission or a license before using a third party's logo or seal, to avoid trademark claims. — [Mondaq, "Brand Trophy Paradox"](https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/advertising-marketing-branding/1581622/the-brand-trophy-paradox-how-to-market-brand-recognition-and-avoid-legal-risk)
- The FTC publishes Endorsement Guides FAQs (16 CFR Part 255) covering endorsements and material connections. — [FTC Endorsement Guides FAQ PDF](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/documents/plain-language/pdf-0205-endorsement-guides-faqs_0.pdf)
- Apple's rules: use only Apple-provided badge artwork, one App Store badge per layout, in a subordinate position, never modified or animated. — [Apple Marketing Resources and Identity Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/)
- When an app is featured on the Today tab, for example as App of the Day, Apple notifies the developer in the App Store Connect app, which generates marketing assets to promote the feature. This is the sanctioned route to "Featured by Apple" artwork. — [Apple, Promoting your apps](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/promote)
- Speak used its OpenAI backing as a credibility signal (see the existing case study). That is investor or partner provenance, earned through the OpenAI Startup Fund. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/31/openai-backed-language-learning-app-speak-raises-16m-to-expand-to-the-u-s)

### Inferences
- Logos Polingual can earn in rough order of effort, with the evidence each needs:
  1. Platform badges: "Download on the App Store" and "Get it on Google Play" are allowed under each owner's artwork rules.
  2. "Featured on the App Store" or "App of the Day": only after Apple features the app, using the App Store Connect assets.
  3. Press: logos only for outlets that published editorial coverage, each linked to the article. Local NYC press (Gothamist, amNY, Brooklyn Paper, Patch), edtech trade press (EdSurge, The PIE News) and language podcasts are realistic. Leave out syndicated paid-release pickups such as AP or Yahoo, which NAD's reasoning makes risky.
  4. Accelerator or program badges: each program sets its own brand rules (for example Techstars, NYU Tandon Future Labs, Columbia Startup Lab, Google for Startups). Use them only after acceptance and per the program's guide.
  5. School and teacher pilots: list a school or district logo only with written permission. A named teacher quote with consent is lower risk.
  6. Research collaborations: a university lab logo needs the institution's approval. A citable outcome such as a pre/post vocabulary study is stronger.
  7. Community partners: cultural institutes, language meetups and consulate events, each with a signed one-line logo permission.
- Safer copy for early pages: "Featured in" with links, "Used at" only with permission, and "Built with" for vendors whose terms allow it.

### Gaps
- No FTC source addresses media-logo trademark permission directly. Fair use of press logos for truthful reference is unsettled, so get counsel's view.
- The search did not return any accelerator's specific badge-usage terms.

## Case Studies and Success Stories

### Takeaway
The language businesses that grew without big ad budgets had one of two engines: a founder or creator with a YouTube audience (LingQ via Steve Kaufmann, Dreaming Spanish, Xiaomanyc, Easy Languages) or a sharp product wedge (Drops' 5-minute visual game, Speak's speaking-first AI, Praktika's AI avatars). Revenue figures below come from company claims at funding time or from third-party trackers. None are audited.

### Cited Findings
- Speak: revenue reported at about $100M in 2025, up from about $15M in 2024 per Latka. A December 2024 newsletter put ARR near $50M, growing more than 100% a year. Forbes reported about 15M downloads. Total raised is $141M to $152M depending on the tracker, including a $78M Series C in 2024. The sources disagree; treat them as estimates. — [Latka](https://www.getlatka.com/companies/speak.com); [ARR Club](https://www.arr.club/speak/speak-arr-nearing-50m-growth-with-100-yoy); [Forbes AU](https://www.forbes.com.au/news/innovation/this-startup-is-racing-duolingo-to-replace-human-language-tutors-with-ai/)
- Praktika: $35.5M Series A led by Blossom Capital in May 2024, after an unannounced $2.5M seed. At the time it claimed 1.2M monthly active users in 100 countries and almost $20M in trailing-twelve-month revenue. A later profile claims 30M+ registered learners and a 2025 redesign, Praktika 4.0. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/22/praktika-raises-35-5m-to-use-ai-avatars-to-make-learning-languages-feel-more-natural); [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2024/05/22/language-app-pratika-raises-a-355m-series-a-round/); [yespress, secondary](https://yespress.io/praktika-ai)
- Drops: Kahoot bought it in December 2020 for $31M cash plus up to $19M in earnouts through 2022, about $50M in total. Drops had 2019 revenue of €6.3M, 25M users and 42 languages. Founded 2015 in Tallinn by Daniel Farkas and Mark Szulyovszky. — [Campus Technology](https://campustechnology.com/articles/2020/12/07/kahoot-buys-language-game-company-drops.aspx); [Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/kahoot-acquires-drops-for-41-9m/)
- LingQ and Steve Kaufmann: LingQ says it has served more than 3.5M learners since 2002. Steve Kaufmann passed 1M YouTube subscribers. CEO Mark Kaufmann calls Steve the company's top marketing channel, with YouTube the biggest driver, and the company grew without ad spend. Revenue estimates run from about $4M to $4.3M a year with roughly 17 staff, against a separate estimate of $200K a month from mobile. These are third-party estimates. Premium starts at $8.99 a month. — [LingQ news](https://www.lingq.com/en/news/steve-kaufmann-1m-youtube-subscribers); [App Masters summary](https://recapio.com/digest/how-lingq-scaled-to-millions-with-zero-ad-spend-by-app-masters); [Builtbyfoundry](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/steve-kaufmann-lingq-language-learning-app)
- Xiaomanyc (Arieh Smith, NYC): about 6M YouTube subscribers in November 2023 and about 7M by 2025/26 per VidIQ and SponsorRadar. Estimated earnings run $23.5K to $70K a month, which is unreliable. Sponsors include MyHeritage, BetterHelp, Saily, QuillBot and Migaku, the last a language tool. His format is approaching strangers in NYC in their own language. — [Wikipedia](https://Www.Wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiaomanyc); [SponsorRadar](https://sponsorradar.com/channels/xiaomanyc); [VidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCLNoXf8gq6vhwsrYp-l0J-Q)
- Easy Languages: a network of subtitled street-interview channels covering more than 40 languages. It earns from Patreon and memberships that offer transcripts, bonus episodes and monthly Q&As. No subscriber or revenue figures found. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Easy_Languages_(YouTube)); [Easy Languages members FAQ](https://www.easy-languages.org/podcasts/patreon)
- Dreaming Spanish: YouTube channel started 2017 by Pablo Román, turned into the Dreaming platform in 2021. It has thousands of comprehensible-input videos with 14 native hosts. The YouTube channel had about 297K subscribers in May 2025. No paid-member or revenue figures are public. — [Dreaming about page](https://www.dreaming.com/about); [Why Is This Interesting](https://whyisthisinteresting.substack.com/p/the-dreaming-spanish-edition); [VidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCouyFdE9-Lrjo3M_2idKq1A)
- Language Reactor: about 2M Chrome users and a 4.2 rating from about 4.2K reviews. It grew by riding on Netflix, YouTube and Disney+ content it does not own. — [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/language-reactor/hoombieeljmmljlkjmnheibnpciblicm)
- HelloChinese: a 4.87 rating on about 20.7K reviews, per an auto-generated publisher profile. No founder or growth data found. — [Marlvel.ai, secondary](https://marlvel.ai/api/llm/publishers/hellochinese-technology-co-ltd/overview)
- An adjacent bootstrapped app: Slopes, a ski tracker, went from an indie side project to $1M ARR and an Apple Design Award. An Apple Design Award is an authority signal an indie app can earn on craft alone. — [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/slopes-from-indie-side-hustle-to-1m-in-arr-and-an-apple-design-award)

### Inferences
- Xiaomanyc is a NYC multilingual creator whose content is the Polingual thesis on camera: speaking a third language to surprise native speakers. He already takes language-tool sponsors such as Migaku. He is the highest-fit creator partner, and also the most expensive.
- LingQ's pattern of a founder as the main channel with zero ad spend, and Easy Languages' street interviews, are copyable in NYC neighborhoods. Film live quiz lobbies and street multilingual challenges.
- Drops shows that a game-first language app with a narrow, fun loop can reach about €6M revenue and a roughly $50M exit with no AI and no tutors. That is the closest comparable to Polingual's daily-games format.
- Praktika and Speak show that investor-grade growth in 2024 to 2026 has come from AI speaking practice. Polingual's live multiplayer lobbies are a different wedge and should be pitched that way.

### Gaps
- No primary growth data found for Clozemaster, LingoDeer, HelloChinese, Pimsleur, Influent, Noun Town or Lingokids within the 15-call budget. Each needs a targeted founder-interview search.
- No revenue or member data for Dreaming Spanish or Easy Languages.
- Memrise's history, including the 2024 pivot to AI and layoffs, was not researched.
