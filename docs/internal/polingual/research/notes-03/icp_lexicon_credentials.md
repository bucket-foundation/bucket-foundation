# Polingual ICP, Segment Size, and Prior Art for Lexicon Map, Skill Tree and Credentials

## ICP Tools: Finding and Reaching Multilinguals

### Takeaway
No ad or data tool exposes a clean "speaks 3+ languages" flag. The workable stack is self-declared signals: Prolific language prescreeners for surveys, LinkedIn profile language plus listed languages for B2B, SparkToro bio-phrase search ("polyglot", "trilingual"), and community mining in r/languagelearning (about 3.4M members), HelloTalk (70M+ registered) and Tandem (35M+).

### Cited Findings
- r/languagelearning has about 3.3M to 3.4M members, growing about 120k a year: [Hive Index](https://thehiveindex.com/communities/r-languagelearning/); [Notifier.so](https://notifier.so/similar-subreddits/languagelearning) reports 3,301,786.
- HelloTalk claims 70M+ users in 200+ countries; older materials cite 50M+: [HelloTalk About](https://www.hellotalk.com/about?lang=de); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HelloTalk); [Agora case study](https://www.agora.io/en/success-stories/hellotalk).
- Tandem reports 35M+ members per a 2026 comparison citing its About page; an older comparison says 30M+: [TalkDrill](https://www.talkdrill.com/blog/compare/hellotalk-vs-tandem/).
- Prolific's "fluent languages" prescreener matches people fluent in at least one selected language (OR logic). To target multilinguals, combine "first language" with "fluent languages", and for 3+ specific languages run a short custom screening study first. Answers are self-reported: [Prolific help](https://researcher-help.prolific.com/en/articles/445169-how-do-i-prescreen-for-participants-who-are-fluent-in-multiple-languages).
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator has a "Profile language" lead filter. It reflects the profile's UI language and is no proof of proficiency; many multilinguals set English: [Findymail](https://www.findymail.com/blog/sales-navigator-search-filters); [Salesrobot](https://salesrobot.co/blogs/sales-navigator-filters).
- SparkToro Audience Research supports "frequently talks about" and "uses this phrase in their profile" searches, returning sites, podcasts, social accounts and demographics for that audience: [SparkToro guide](https://kalynamarketing.squarespace.com/blog/sparktoro-guide-reference).

### Inferences
- SparkToro bio-phrase searches for "polyglot", "trilingual", "multilingual", "speaks 4 languages" give the best map of the media this ICP follows. The Reddit and HelloTalk bases are the largest warm pools.
- For surveys and price testing, Prolific (first language X + fluent in Y + screener for a third) is the cheapest route to a verified-by-screen trilingual sample.
- LinkedIn's "Languages" section (self-listed proficiency) is a better B2B signal than the Profile language filter; Clay or Apollo enrichment would need to scrape that section.
- Tagline phrases common in polyglot flair ("PL - N, EN - C1, RU - A2/B1" seen in r/languagelearning user flair) make Reddit flair a parseable source of declared multi-language profiles.

### Gaps
- No public Meta or TikTok Ads Manager audience estimates for "polyglot"/"multilingualism" interests found; needs a logged-in Ads Manager pull.
- r/polyglot member count not found in search results.
- Similarweb, GWI, Clay, Apollo, Pollfish language targeting, and AppFollow/Appfigures review-mining specifics not verified in this pass (Apollo connector needs authorization).
- No Discord server sizes found.

## Segment Size

### Takeaway
Trilingual-plus people number in the low hundreds of millions in reachable markets: roughly 100M+ in the EU (28% of Europeans converse in 2+ foreign languages), about 85M in India (7% know 3+ languages, 2011 Census), plus Duolingo's 32M learners studying more than one language (2023). The US is mostly bilingual (22% speak a non-English language at home), with no official trilingual figure.

### Cited Findings
- 2024 Eurobarometer "Europeans and their languages": 59% of Europeans can hold a conversation in at least one foreign language and 28% in at least two: [EC Translation portal](https://translation.ec.europa.eu/languages-eu-why-multilingualism-matters/europeans-language-skills_en).
- Fieldwork Sept to Oct 2023, ~27,000 respondents aged 15+, published 21 May 2024; 4 in 5 Europeans aged 15 to 24 can converse in a foreign language; 47% speak English; 86% think everyone should speak at least one language beyond their mother tongue: [EC Education](https://education.ec.europa.eu/news/europeans-strive-toward-multilingualism-and-value-language-diversity-according-to-latest-eurobarometer).
- Top cited benefits of learning a language: job opportunities 51%, understanding other cultures 45%, better job at home 42%: [EC Education](https://education.ec.europa.eu/news/europeans-strive-toward-multilingualism-and-value-language-diversity-according-to-latest-eurobarometer).
- US: 2023 ACS puts people 5+ speaking a non-English language at home at about 22.5% (Spanish 13.7%); 2018 to 2022 5-year: 78.3% English only: [Census 2023 release](https://census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2023/language-at-home-acs-5-year.html); [Maryland ACS 2023 table](https://planning.maryland.gov/MSDC/Documents/American_Community_Survey/2023/US_ACS_2023.pdf). California is highest at 45%.
- India 2011 Census: 26% bilingual (314.9M) per Wikipedia summary; a Hindustan Times analysis says 7% know three or more languages: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multilingualism_in_India); [Hindustan Times via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/india/hindustan-times-lucknow/20181122/282196536999567).
- Global: an estimated 60% of people are multilingual (more than one language): [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/africas-linguistic-diversity-goes-largely-unnoticed-in-research-on-multilingualism-208204). Accra infants are exposed to 2 to 6 languages (121-baby study, 2025): [ScienceDaily](https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2025/02/250228113955.htm).
- Duolingo: the number of "polyglots" on Duolingo (studying more than one language) grew to over 32 million (2023 report): [CIOL](https://www.ciol.org.uk/duolingo-2023-global-trends-language-learning). Finland, Germany and the UK lead in learners studying 3+ languages (2024 report): [Duolingo 2024 Language Report](https://blog.duolingo.com/2024-duolingo-language-report/).
- Duolingo MAU estimates range from 37M to 74M depending on source and year: secondary aggregators, see [Statista](https://statista.com/statistics/1309610/duolingo-quarterly-mau).

### Inferences
- EU 15+ population is roughly 380M, so 28% implies about 105M trilingual-plus Europeans (estimate, not a published figure).
- India's 7% of 1.21B (2011) implies about 85M trilingual-plus Indians.
- Most US multilinguals are heritage bilinguals (Spanish/English); the US trilingual pool is small and best found via immigrant and international-student communities and coastal metros.
- The highest-intent slice is the overlap: already multilingual and actively learning. Duolingo's 32M multi-course learners is the closest proxy for a serviceable addressable market; Finland, Germany, UK are priority countries.
- Paid conversion in language apps runs single digits, so a realistic SOM is in the tens to hundreds of thousands of payers.

### Gaps
- Eurobarometer 2024 share for 3+ foreign languages not found on the pages read (2012 edition reported 10%, not reverified here).
- No continent-wide Africa figure for 3+ languages; Afrobarometer Round 4 language tables are the next source.
- No official US trilingual figure; ACS does not ask about third languages.
- Share of language learners who are already bilingual: no published figure found.

## Prior Art: Lexicon Map and Known-Words Tracking

### Takeaway
Known-word counts exist per language (LingQ, Migaku, WaniKani), but each app defines "known" differently and none unifies counts across languages. A cross-lexicon map with cognate linking is open territory.

### Cited Findings
- LingQ's Known Word Count includes words marked Known plus status 4 "Learned" words (which still appear in SRS review): [LingQ support](https://lingq-support.groovehq.com/help/understanding-lingq-statistics); [LingQ forum](https://www.lingq.com/en/forum/open-forum/how-exactly-does-the-known-wor/).
- Migaku tracks Unknown, Learning, Known; "Ignore" also counts toward Known words; users can import already-known words: [Migaku docs](https://magenta-dirigible-0d8.notion.site/Teach-Migaku-the-words-you-already-know-de6c68b5f05848e98f56b24203d4bf3e); [Migaku blog](https://migaku.com/blog/language-fun/language-learning-progress-tracking).
- Language Reactor has a "Marked known words" list but users request a LingQ-style known-words graph, implying none exists: [Language Reactor forum](https://forum.languagelearningwithnetflix.com/t/see-known-words-in-a-graph/22659).
- Readlang users request a LingQ-style known words count: [Readlang forum](https://forum.readlang.com/t/add-known-words-count-like-lingq/3025).
- WaniKani has 60 levels covering about 2,000 kanji and 6,000 vocabulary; learners call level 60 a milestone, not fluency: [WaniKani community](https://community.wanikani.com/t/wanikani-users-at-higher-levels-have-you-acheived-reading-fluency/58127/3); [Level 60 after 734 days](https://community.wanikani.com/t/wanikani-level-60-after-734-days/74174).
- Clozemaster uses cloze sentence practice with Fluency Fast Track collections: [Clozemaster vs Anki](https://www.clozemaster.com/blog/clozemaster-vs-anki/).
- Words With Friends ships 6 languages (English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Spanish) using Oxford word lists per language: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id321916506); [Zynga rulebook](https://zynga.my/NWWFRulebook).

### Inferences
- Polingual's lexicon map can adopt the LingQ/Migaku status ladder (unknown, learning, known, ignored) and add a cross-language layer: one node per concept, with edges for cognates and false friends.
- Users already merge data by hand (WaniKani to Anki exports), so import from Anki, LingQ and Migaku is a growth wedge for the polyglot ICP.
- Per-language word lists in Words With Friends show multi-lexicon dictionaries are licensable (Oxford); a "Scrabble across lexicons" mode can draw on the same kind of lists.

### Gaps
- Anki known-vocabulary stats, testyourvocab methodology, Wordle multi-language variants, and Scrabble's count of official-language lexicons not verified in this pass.
- No product found that unifies known words across several languages.

## Prior Art: Skill Trees and Credential Wallets

### Takeaway
The open standard stack is ready: W3C VC Data Model 2.0 (Recommendation, May 2025) and Open Badges 3.0 (final 2024) put signed badges in the learner's wallet. Credly dominates issuance (100M badges, 48M+ earners). Polingual and Bucket can issue OB 3.0 credentials that land in existing wallets.

### Cited Findings
- W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model v2.0 became a W3C Recommendation dated 15 May 2025, defining issuer, holder and verifier roles: [W3C](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model/).
- Open Badges 3.0: each badge is an issuer-signed credential compatible with VC Data Model 2.0; implementation guide v2.0 issued 7 April 2025: [1EdTech implementation guide](https://www.imsglobal.org/spec/ob/v3p0/impl).
- OB 2.0 hosted badges on the issuer's server; 3.0 moves them to the holder's wallet. The Digital Credentials Consortium's Learner Credential Wallet accepts only 3.0-compliant credentials: [POK](https://www.pok.tech/blog/posts/open-badge-3-0-complete-guide-digital-credentials).
- Open Badge Passport receives 3.0 badges from any certified issuer; wallet ecosystem policy questions remain open: [Open Badge Factory](https://openbadgefactory.com/en/your-badges-are-now-open-badges-3-0/).
- 1EdTech CLR 2.0 (March 2025) bundles many achievements into one signed credential: [Forasoft](https://www.forasoft.com/learn/elearning-video/articles-elearning/certificates-badges-verifiable-credentials).
- Credly issued its 100 millionth badge (Jan 2025): [Pearson](https://plc.pearson.com/en-GB/news-and-insights/blogs/100-million-ways-pearson-has-fuelled-learning-economy). 48M+ earners, 650K+ credentials shared monthly: [Credly](https://info.credly.com/why-choose-credly). IBM issued 1.72M and Cisco 1.77M badges on Credly in 2024: [Credly elite issuers](https://info.credly.com/top-credly-elite-issuers).
- Duolingo assigns learner personas using hours, number of courses and practice time, confirming it tracks multi-course behavior: [CIOL](https://www.ciol.org.uk/duolingo-2023-global-trends-language-learning).

### Inferences
- A Polingual skill tree maps naturally to CLR 2.0: each node an OB 3.0 achievement, the whole tree a CLR bundle the learner can share.
- Bucket's extension to medical and other credentials fits the same stack; per-node CEFR level per language gives the language credential a recognized scale.
- Credly's scale shows employers already consume badges; LinkedIn's Licenses and Certifications section is the distribution surface.

### Gaps
- Europass European Digital Credentials for Learning (EDC) details, Khan Academy mastery model, Duolingo path redesign specifics, LinkedIn certification import mechanics and medical CME tracking apps not verified in this pass.
