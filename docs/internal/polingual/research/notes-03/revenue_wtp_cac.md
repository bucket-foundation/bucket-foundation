# Revenue Models, Willingness to Pay and CAC for Language Learning Apps and Casual Educational Games

## Game Revenue Models and Benchmarks

### Takeaway
Ad-only casual games earn cents per daily user ($0.01 to $0.08 ARPDAU), and hybrid ads plus IAP reaches $0.15 to $0.50. A daily puzzle subscription like NYT Games sells for about $50 a year and has built a large paid base. For a small team with a modest DAU base, a subscription bundle beats ads and IAP for revenue per user.

### Cited Findings
- Format-level ARPDAU ranges, H1 2025: hypercasual ad-driven $0.03 to $0.08; casual ads-only $0.01 to $0.05; hybrid-casual ads plus IAP $0.15 to $0.50. The underlying data source is not named, so treat it as directional ([Playio ARPDAU benchmarks](https://blog.playio.co/arpdau-benchmarks-mobile-games)).
- GameAnalytics dropped genre-level benchmarks from its 2026 report (16,000+ games, calendar 2025). Median game: about 22% D1 retention, under 4% D7, 0.7 to 0.8% D30 ([Game Growth Advisor summary](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-03-17-mobile-game-kpis-benchmarks-2026/); [GameAnalytics GameIntel](https://gameanalytics.com/gameintel) lists Trivia and Word under Puzzle but shows no public figures).
- Puzzle and lifestyle hybrid-casual titles run about 50/50 ads to IAP; action and strategy hybrids run about 82% IAP ([Playio, citing Gamigion/AppMagic](https://blog.playio.co/arpdau-benchmarks-mobile-games)).
- 2025 global eCPM ranges cited from Playwire: rewarded $8 to $18, interstitial $2.50 to $5, banner $0.20 to $0.80. A worked example of 2 rewarded impressions per DAU at $15 eCPM gives about $0.03 ad ARPDAU ([Playio ad revenue calculator](https://blog.playio.co/mobile-game-ad-revenue-calculator)).
- Appodeal (Android, US, June 2024 to Jan 2025): puzzle games averaged 24 rewarded video views per user; interstitial figures differ between versions of the report (72.5 vs 69.8) ([Appodeal Mobile Casual Benchmarks 2025](https://gameindustrylibrary.com/documents/mobile-casual-benchmarks-report-2025)).
- Wordscapes, a top US word-puzzle game, peaked near $517K weekly revenue in late December 2025 across iOS and Android ([Sensor Tower Q4 2025 word puzzle](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-unified-top-5-word-puzzle-games-revenue-us-603697f4241bc16eb8570d37)).
- NYT Games: individual games-only subscription about $50 a year; family games plan $120 a year (Sept 2025). Core Wordle stays free; archives, stats and Wordle Bot sit behind the paywall ([Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/2025/09/the-new-york-times-launches-a-family-subscription-with-separate-wordles-for-everyone/); [AP via WTOP, Jan 2026](https://wtop.com/consumer-news/2026/01/this-just-in-from-the-new-york-times-its-first-2-player-game-and-an-evolving-business-model/)).
- Apple Ads US 2025 median for Games: CPT $2.03, CPI $4.63, CR 61.8% ([AppTweak Apple Ads benchmarks](https://apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/apple-ads-benchmarks)).
- Live quiz analog: Kahoot!+ Start for personal use lists at $7.99 per user per month, $96 billed annually; family plan price could not be confirmed ([Kahoot personal pricing](https://kahoot.com/register/pricing-personal/)). Kahoot raised Pro Plus and Max prices and added a Pro Ultra plan in Q2 2026 ([PricingSaaS diff](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/kahoot/diffs/2026Q2)).
- Duolingo's own in-app purchase revenue fell 38% YoY to $6.9M in Q3 2026 while subscription grew 18%, and ad revenue was flat at $20.8M ([Duolingo Q3 2026 shareholder letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)).

### Inferences
- At $0.03 ad ARPDAU, 10,000 DAU earns about $300 a day, roughly $110K a year. A subscription at 3% of MAU paying $50 a year earns more from the same base once MAU is several times DAU. Ads fit the free Bucket app and casual Polingual Play players; subscriptions fit Polingual.
- Duolingo's ad and IAP lines are small and shrinking next to subscription, which supports a subscription-first model for a learning product.
- Live team lobbies map to a host-pays model: the host or organizer holds the subscription and guests join free, as Kahoot does. This also turns every lobby into an acquisition event.
- Battle pass and Apple Arcade style models: no source in this pass gave benchmarks for them in word or trivia games.

### Gaps
- No public ARPDAU, payer conversion or LTV split for word, trivia or puzzle genres from GameAnalytics, Sensor Tower, Liftoff or Unity for 2025 or 2026. GameIntel holds it behind a login.
- No verified NYT Games standalone subscriber count; one figure of 5M+ came from an unreliable page.
- No payer-conversion benchmark for hybrid-casual puzzle games.

## Language App Models and Subscription Benchmarks

### Takeaway
Duolingo converts about 8.6% of MAU to paid at roughly $84 a year net per subscriber, with 13.1M payers in Q3 2026. Education subscription medians are $9.99 a month and $44.99 a year, with 6.5% download-to-trial and $0.30 revenue per install at D14. Speak shows a premium AI speaking tier at about $19 a month can reach $100M revenue.

### Cited Findings
- Duolingo Q3 2026: MAU 153.1M, DAU 64.9M (+29%), paid subscribers 13.1M (+14%), revenue $309.0M (+14%), subscription revenue $270.0M, ads $20.8M, DET $9.9M. Max tier is being sunset; Max subscribers move to Super Family at the same price; Video Call becomes unlimited for Super ([Duolingo Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm); [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069692/duol-20260930.htm)).
- Derived from the letter: paid penetration 13.1M / 153.1M = 8.6% of MAU; subscription revenue per paid subscriber about $21 a quarter, about $82 to $84 a year (my calculation).
- Duolingo FY2026 revenue guidance about $1.22B ([Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/3026122/{https:/www.zackstrade.com/2019-1-dollar}); [Quartr summary](https://quartr.com/events/duolingo-inc-duol-q3-2026_3P0xhxPR)).
- Duolingo prices from third-party sources, which conflict: Super Family $119.99 a year for up to 6 members is the most consistent figure; individual Super cited at $83.88 to $83.99 a year; Max $29.99 a month or $168 a year ([Mindomax](https://www.mindomax.com/duolingo-cost); [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/duolingo/pricing)). One report saw $143.99 Family on iPhone, so prices vary by device and region.
- Babbel: 12-month plan about $8.95 a month; lifetime all languages $299.99 as of Aug 2026, down from older $129.99 deal pricing ([Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/compare/babbel-vs-busuu); [Copycatcafe](https://copycatcafe.com/babbel); [DealNews 2025](https://dealnews.com/features/babbel/plan-pricing)).
- Speak: individual plan about $19 a month (read Sept 30, 2026) ([Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/compare/babbel-vs-speak)). Revenue $15M in mid-2024 to about $100M annualized by Nov 2025, mostly consumer ([Latka](https://www.getlatka.com/companies/speak.com); [Benzinga/Forbes](https://www.benzinga.com/news/topics/25/11/48975908/this-1-billion-ai-startup-backed-by-openai-khosla-ventures-just-hit-100m-revenue-racing-to-beat-duolingo)). Raised $78M at $1B valuation Dec 2024 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/10/openai-backed-speak-raises-78m-at-1b-valuation-to-help-users-learn-languages-by-talking-out-loud)).
- Praktika: about $8 a month per its own blog ([Praktika](https://praktika.ai/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-learn-spanish-2026-2)); almost $20M revenue in the 12 months before May 2024, 1.2M MAU ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/22/praktika-raises-35-5m-to-use-ai-avatars-to-make-learning-languages-feel-more-natural)).
- Busuu: Premium cited at $6.83 to $13.95 a month on annual billing, Premium Plus $7.49 to $19.95 ([Subger](https://subger.com/en/service/busuu)); unverified.
- Lifetime deals: Mondly all languages $79.97 to $89.99 on resellers against $299.99 MSRP (May 2026) ([Boing Boing](https://boingboing.net/2026/05/12/got-international-main-character-energy-this-lifetime-language-app-is-200-off.html); [Entrepreneur store](https://store.entrepreneur.com/sales/mondly-premium-lifetime-subscription-all-languages)). Drops lifetime $76.50, $38.25 with coupon, Dec 2023 ([Slickdeals](https://slickdeals.net/f/17189359-drops-language-learning-lifetime-subscription-for-38)).
- RevenueCat SOSA 2026, Education: download-to-trial D30 median 6.5%; 78.5% of trials start Day 0, lowest of any category; 28.5% of conversions on Day 0, lowest; 50.3% of trials are 5 to 9 days; median yearly price $44.99, highest category; median monthly $9.99; D14 revenue per install $0.30; yearly realized LTV per payer after Year 1 $22.82 ([RevenueCat Education](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-education)).
- RevenueCat SOSA 2026, all categories: trials of 17 to 32 days convert at a 42.5% median, trials under 4 days at 25.5%; Month 1 holds 35% of annual-plan cancellations overall and about 30% in Education; 95% of canceling annual customers never return ([RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/); [Heise](https://heise.de/-11310636)).
- Adapty 2026, Education: 12-month LTV $45.10; trial users have 50.4% higher LTV than direct buyers; 71.3% of trials start Day 0 and 23.5% start 31+ days after install, highest of any category; 14.3% of education apps discount. Education revenue mix 2025: weekly 52%, annual 22%, one-time 17%, monthly 9%. Top 10% of education apps take 96% of revenue ([Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/education-app-subscription-benchmarks/)).
- Adapty 2026, global: install-to-trial 10.9%; trial-to-paid 25.6%; first-renewal retention 59.2%; trial subscribers alive at Day 380: annual 19.9%, monthly 14.2%, weekly 5.5%; hard paywalls about 21% higher LTV per subscriber, soft paywalls about 50% better conversion; apps running 50+ experiments had median revenue $914,734 vs $48,848 with one ([Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/education-app-subscription-benchmarks/)).
- AppsFlyer reportedly found education apps convert over 40% of trialists; secondhand ([Pulse summary](https://www.pulse.bot/saas/news/appsflyer-new-state-of-subscriptions-for-marketers-report-reveals-shifting-consumer-behaviour-reshap-4722c45c-f92b-4127-a267-bd3ad5c3fc98/)).

### Inferences
- Education users decide late: low Day 0 trial share and the highest 31+ day trial share mean a free tier that keeps learners around, then asks, fits the category better than a hard paywall at install. The free Bucket app plus daily games can serve as that warm-up period.
- Education apps sell more lifetime (17% of revenue in 2025) than most categories. A lifetime tier fits a polyglot audience that dislikes stacking subscriptions.
- Family plans are Duolingo's direction (Max folded into Super Family). A shared plan fits multilingual households.

### Gaps
- No RevenueCat education trial-to-paid rate, refund rate or monthly churn in the visible page.
- Official 2026 list prices for Duolingo Super, Busuu, Praktika and Drops not confirmed from vendor pages.
- No disclosed Duolingo family-plan share of subscribers.

## Willingness to Pay for Language Learning

### Takeaway
Most app users will not pay: one survey found 67% content with free versions. The payers who do pay spend far more on tutors than on apps. Multilinguals learning a third language are a narrow, high-intent group, but no survey isolates their spend.

### Cited Findings
- 67% of people interested in language apps prefer a free trial or free version; those willing to pay $15+ a month were twice as likely to prefer a personal tutor ([CivicScience](https://civicscience.com/?p=37961)).
- Paddle analysis of Busuu and Duolingo data: personal-use willingness to pay about $6.31; business use cases more than twice personal ([Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/blog/busuu-duolingo-pricing)).
- Busuu user surveys, 2011 and 2012: average lifetime language-learning spend about 740 to 750 euros; over 17% spent more than 1,000 euros. Old, self-selected sample ([Busuu 2012](https://blog.busuu.com/?p=2993); [Busuu 2011](https://blog.busuu.com/?p=1723)).
- Lingoda: average American states they would pay $5,700 to learn a language; stated intent ([Lingoda](https://blog.lingoda.com/en/new-skills-americans-want)).
- Learners with employer-paid access report higher monthly spend than self-payers, N=200 ([Tyton Partners](https://tytonpartners.com/could-language-learning-be-the-hot-new-employee-benefit)).
- Tutor prices: italki community tutors $5 to $15 an hour, professional teachers $10 to $40 ([learnenglish.life](https://learnenglish.life/guides/how-much-does-italki-cost/)); English tutors $15 to over $100 an hour per italki ([italki blog](https://www.italki.com/en/blog/english-tutor-cost)). An unverified forum post says italki's single-lesson fee rose from 15% to 21% in Jan 2025 ([Chinese Forums](https://chinese-forums.com/forums/topic/63192-italki-teachers-getting-pricey/page/3)).
- Regional pricing, Adapty global: weekly price index vs US = 1.0: Italy and Spain 2.3x, France 2.0x, Germany 1.9x, UK 1.6x, Canada 0.7x, Mexico and Brazil 0.4x; European prices 29 to 39% above North America; LATAM LTV about 35% below Europe; localization tests win 62.3% of the time ([Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/education-app-subscription-benchmarks/)).
- Speak's growth to about $100M at about $19 a month shows a sizable segment pays 2x the education median for speaking practice ([Latka](https://www.getlatka.com/companies/speak.com); [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/compare/babbel-vs-speak)).

### Inferences
- A learner taking one $20 tutor hour a week spends over $1,000 a year. A $60 to $100 annual app looks cheap against that, so framing Polingual as a complement to tutoring supports a price above the $44.99 education median.
- Multilinguals in Europe can carry higher prices than US users per Adapty's index; Polingual's audience skews toward Europe, Canada and immigrant communities, so regional price tiers matter.

### Gaps
- No survey of what people who speak 2+ languages pay for a 3rd. No price-elasticity study for language apps. No verified average annual italki spend per learner.

## Customer Acquisition Cost

### Takeaway
Paid installs for education cost about $3 on US Apple Ads, and Meta education CPI data is volatile and often far higher. At a 2 to 3% install-to-paid rate, paid CAC per subscriber lands near $100 to $150, above first-year net revenue of $50 to $70. Duolingo spends about 13% of revenue on sales and marketing and leans on organic brand and social.

### Cited Findings
- Apple Ads US 2025 median, Education search results: CPT $1.52, CPI $2.91, CR 56.4%, TTR 8.3%. Global all-category: CPT $0.92, CPI $1.80 ([AppTweak](https://apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/apple-ads-benchmarks)).
- Meta education CPI per Superads: all-country average about $70.3 for Oct 2025 to Sept 2026, ranging $9.31 to $167.21 month to month; the basis is unclear and the figure sits far above general CPI ranges ([Superads Education](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cost-per-app-install/education)). Generic guidance in the same tool: iOS CPI $2 to $5+, Android $1 to $3.
- Cross-vertical CPI, Singular Q2 2026: iOS $3.03, Android $0.76 ([Singular](https://www.singular.net/quarterly-report-2026-q2/)).
- TikTok and Adjust meta-analysis claims education apps see 40% lower CPI on TikTok than other platforms; vendor-sourced, no absolute numbers ([TikTok Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/how-tiktok-powers-app-installs)).
- Duolingo GAAP sales and marketing: $42.2M in Q3 2026, about 13.6% of revenue, up from 12.9% ([Duolingo Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)). FY2025 S&M $126M vs $90M in FY2024 ([Panabee summary of 10-K](https://www.panabee.com/news/duolingo-earnings-2025-annual)). Duolingo describes lean budgets focused on organic brand-building and efficient performance marketing ([Duolingo strategy page](https://duolingo.gcs-web.com/company-strategy-overview-0)); a Q1 2025 campaign drew 1.7B organic impressions ([Duolingo IR](https://investors.duolingo.com/static-files/f75b5236-9f46-47cf-9828-4f40c69d7e27)).
- Duolingo's Q3 2026 partnerships with Brawl Stars and Eggy Party brought "millions" of new and returning users; DAU growth credited to retention, notifications and streak experiments ([Duolingo Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)).
- SplitMetrics: 92% of education app advertisers skip back-to-school Apple Ads campaigns ([SplitMetrics](https://splitmetrics.com/back-to-school-education/)).

### Inferences
- Funnel math with the medians: 6.5% download-to-trial × about 40% trial-to-paid = 2.6% install-to-paid. At $2.91 Apple Ads CPI, cost per payer is about $112. At $1.80 global CPI it is about $69.
- First-year net per payer on a $59.99 annual plan after Apple's 15% small-business fee is about $51; Adapty's education 12-month LTV is $45.10. Broad paid UA loses money in year one at median rates. Paid works only on high-intent keywords (for example "learn Portuguese for Spanish speakers") where conversion beats median, or with a higher price.
- Live lobbies and shareable daily games are the cheapest channel available: each host brings 3 to 10 players at zero media cost. Duolingo's 13% S&M on a brand-led base is the model to copy.

### Gaps
- No education-specific CPI for Meta or TikTok split by OS from AppsFlyer, Liftoff or Adjust in 2025 to 2026. Business of Apps' education benchmark page returned 403.
- No public cost per trial for education. No benchmark for creator or affiliate CAC in language apps. Duolingo's 10-K share of organic users was not found in this pass.

## Recommended Model and Unit Economics Sketch

### Takeaway
Run a freemium subscription with a free daily game layer and a host-pays live lobby. Price Polingual at about $12.99 a month or $69.99 a year, a family or group plan near $119.99, and a lifetime tier around $199 to $249. Keep Bucket free with light rewarded ads. Grow through lobbies, shareable results and creators before paying for installs.

### Cited Findings
- Education median $9.99 monthly and $44.99 yearly ([RevenueCat Education](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-education)); Speak about $19 a month ([Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/compare/babbel-vs-speak)); Duolingo Super Family $119.99 for 6 ([Mindomax](https://www.mindomax.com/duolingo-cost)); NYT Games about $50 a year ([Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/2025/09/the-new-york-times-launches-a-family-subscription-with-separate-wordles-for-everyone/)); Babbel lifetime $299.99 ([Copycatcafe](https://copycatcafe.com/babbel)).
- Longer trials convert better: 17 to 32 days at 42.5% vs under 4 days at 25.5% ([RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)); education trials cluster at 5 to 9 days ([RevenueCat Education](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-education)).
- Trial users have 50.4% higher LTV in education; annual trial subscribers retain best at Day 380 (19.9%) ([Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/education-app-subscription-benchmarks/)).
- Experimentation scales revenue: 50+ experiments correlates with 18.7x median revenue ([Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/education-app-subscription-benchmarks/)).

### Inferences
Recommended model:
- Polingual: freemium, with daily multilingual games free and the full multi-language course, unlimited lobbies, AI speaking and stats paid. 7-day trial (the education norm is 5 to 9 days; test 14). Lead with annual on the paywall.
- Prices to test: $12.99 a month, $69.99 a year, Family or Group $119.99 a year for up to 6, Lifetime $199 to $249 sold in launch windows and to the polyglot community. Regional tiers: Europe at or above US, LATAM and India at 40 to 50% of US.
- Polingual Play: free daily games with streaks and share cards; rewarded ads only on the free tier; paid removes ads and opens archives and extra languages, the NYT Games pattern. Skip battle passes and gacha IAP until DAU passes 50K.
- Live lobbies: host-pays. Free users join any lobby; hosting more than one lobby a day or more than 4 players needs a subscription. Sell a classroom or club tier later near Kahoot's $96 a year.
- Bucket: free with light ads; a funnel into the Polingual trial.

Unit economics sketch per 10,000 installs, median case with my assumptions:
- Trials: 6.5% → 650. Paid at 40% → 260 subscribers (2.6% of installs).
- Mix 70% annual at $69.99, 30% monthly at $12.99 averaging 5 months paid. Net after 15% store fee: annual $59.49, monthly about $55.21. Blended first-year net about $58 per payer → about $15,100 per 10,000 installs, $1.51 per install.
- Rewarded ads on free users: 2,000 DAU from that cohort over the first months at $0.02 ARPDAU adds roughly $40 a day while they stay active.
- Break-even CPI on year-one revenue: about $1.50. Apple Ads US education median is $2.91, so paid acquisition needs better-than-median conversion, a 2-year LTV view (annual renewal near 40 to 60%), or keyword niches.
- Path for a small team: target 70%+ organic installs. With 50,000 organic installs a year at the median funnel, about 1,300 payers and about $75K first-year net. Reaching $1M ARR needs about 15,000 paying subscribers at about $67 net, or about 575,000 installs at median conversion, so moving install-to-paid from 2.6% toward 5% through lobbies and the warm free tier matters more than any media channel.
- Creator and affiliate CAC: pay per trial or per paid conversion (for example 30 to 40% of first-year revenue, $20 to $25 per annual subscriber) so CAC stays under first-year net by construction. This is a proposal; no benchmark was found.

### Gaps
- No Polingual-specific funnel data; all conversion assumptions use category medians and should be replaced with RevenueCat data from the live app within 60 days.
- No benchmark for conversion uplift from multiplayer or live lobby features in education apps.
