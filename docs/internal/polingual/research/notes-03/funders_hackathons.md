# Funders, Competitions and Hackathons for Polingual and Bucket

Research date: 2026-11-15. Polingual: early-stage NYC language-learning app with games, live team quiz lobbies and multilingual learners. Bucket: the founder's broader learning platform.

## VC firms active in edtech and language learning, and who led the comparable rounds

### Takeaway
The firms that led recent language-app rounds are generalist growth funds (Accel on Speak, Blossom on Praktika, WestCap on Preply) plus edtech specialists at earlier stages (Owl and Goodwater on Novakid). For a pre-seed or seed Polingual round the best-fit specialists are Reach Capital (new $265M Fund V, Aug 2026, seed to Series A), Brighteye (new $72M Fund III first close, Sep 2026, Europe-focused, led Tandem) and Owl Ventures; the OpenAI Startup Fund and Y Combinator both sit on Speak's cap table and YC keeps funding language apps every batch.

### Inferences
- Polingual's multiplayer quiz format reads closer to Kahoot and Quizizz than to Speak; a pitch that frames it as "Kahoot for language" with consumer retention would fit Owl, Reach, GSV and gaming-adjacent funds.
- AI conversation tutors (Speak, Praktika, Pingo, Lucida, Eevi) are a crowded lane. Live team play and multilingual groups are a less funded angle and a cleaner story.
- Brighteye and Emerge are Europe-first; reach them only if Polingual has European users or plans.
- Firms to approach first for a NYC pre-seed: Reach Capital (fresh fund), Owl Ventures, GSV Ventures, Rethink Education (NYC-based), Learn Capital, USV (Duolingo Series A, NYC), and YC by application.

Known names from prior knowledge to check: Jennifer Carolan and Shauntel Garvey (Reach), Amit Patel and Tory Patterson (Owl), Rob Hutter (Learn Capital), Deborah Quazzo and Michael Moe (GSV), Matt Greenfield (Rethink), Benoit Wirz and Alex Latsis (Brighteye), Nic Newman (Emerge), Albert Wenger (USV, Duolingo board).
