# Polingual should sell the room first

Polingual's strongest expansion moves all use the asset it already ships: a live big-screen team quiz where every phone is a controller, across many languages, on iOS, Android and web. Scored on six dimensions, the top five opportunities after the core subscription are the **Polingual Play lobby inside the subscription (7.90 of 10)**, **paid venue language quiz nights (7.80)**, a **free-to-start Teacher Pro host plan (7.45)**, **career vocabulary built from O\*NET (7.35)** and **sponsored international nights built on Romance intercomprehension (7.25)**. Each needs weeks of work on the current stack and has no direct incumbent: Kahoot, Blooket and Gimkit run classroom quizzes with no language content, Duolingo has no product for a group around one screen, bar trivia companies sell no language nights, and no app found generates vocabulary from an arbitrary job title. New devices rank lower. Smart TV casting and a Discord Activity are cheap ports of the lobby and score near 6.9, while Quest VR, consoles, Roblox, a kids app and a home-grown proficiency test score under 5 because they need a second codebase, a compliance regime or years of validity research. Revenue per payer is set by the consumer subscription, where Duolingo earns about **$82 a year per paying subscriber**; the room products add revenue from people who host, at **$48 to $180 a year per teacher** and **$125 to $250 a night per venue**. The twelve-month plan runs lobby and venues first, teachers and career vocabulary second, CEFR badges and test prep third, and TV, Discord and B2B pilots last, with every in-person event doubling as a trial-signup drive.

## Polingual is four products sharing one lobby engine

Polingual today is a paid multi-language learning app on iOS, Android and web with a 3-day trial, fed by the free Bucket app, with games and live team quiz lobbies built for a shared screen. Polingual Play is the planned games line. The earlier games research found that the lobby belongs in Next.js with a DOM or Phaser 4 front end and Supabase Realtime, which allows **500 concurrent connections on Pro and 10,000 with the spend cap off**, enough for about 16 classrooms of 30 before the cap comes off ([Supabase Realtime limits](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/realtime/limits)). That one engine can be sold four ways: to a learner as part of a subscription, to a host such as a teacher or bar that brings thirty non-paying players, to a sponsor that pays for the room, and to an employer or school that pays for seats. Every opportunity below is one of those four buyers on one surface.

The market Polingual sits in is large and concentrated. Duolingo booked **$1.04B in 2025, up 38.7%**, with 11.5M paid subscribers in Q3 2025 ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1247833/annual-duolingo-revenue/); [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/02/16/3-key-takeaways-from-duolingos-2025)), and its Q3 2026 letter reports **64.9M daily users, 13.1M payers and $270.0M of quarterly subscription revenue** ([Duolingo Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)). Market sizing for language learning ranges from **$5.86B to $78.86B** depending on definition, and one firm expects B2B to hold **over 56% of online language learning** ([Meticulous](https://meticulousresearch.com/reports/language-learning-market-5561); [GII](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/sky1920026-language-learning-market-size-share-growth.html); [Meticulous online](https://meticulousresearch.com/reports/online-language-learning-market-5025)). The gap is format. Kahoot reached **$146M revenue and 24M active users** in 2022 with a subject-agnostic quiz, and Gimkit wins share with about seven employees ([Apps Run The World](https://www.appsruntheworld.com/apps-top-500-software-vendors/kahoot/); [Plisio](https://plisio.net/education/what-is-gimkit)). A language specialist built for the room has no direct rival in any channel researched.

## The fit chart ranks 26 opportunities on six dimensions

Each opportunity is scored 1 to 10 on market size, revenue per user, cost and time to build, fit with what Polingual ships, competitive gap and speed to first revenue. Higher is better on every axis, so a cheap, fast build scores high on the build axis. Weights are 20% for market size, 20% for fit and 15% for each of the other four, because a large market Polingual cannot reach with its current lobby and stack is worth less to a small team than a smaller one it can serve next month. The scores are analyst judgments anchored to the evidence in the sections that follow; gaps between tiers carry more meaning than the decimal places inside a tier.

## The room wins on fit and has no language incumbent

### Lobby, venues and sponsored nights rank 1, 2 and 5

The lobby inside the subscription ranks first because it costs nothing to sell: the buyer already pays. At Duolingo, games drive retention while subscriptions earn the money, and its newest play feature, real-time Chess PvP, is one-on-one on phones ([Daily Tribune](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/02/24/duolingo-chess-goes-live)). Netflix shows demand for the group format: it made cloud TV games a 2026 priority, party titles reached about 10% of eligible members, and it licensed three Jackbox games ([Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/netflix-makes-cloud-based-tv-games-a-key-priority-for-2026)). A family or friend group playing Polingual on a TV gives each subscriber a reason to bring non-payers into a trial.

Venue nights rank second on price evidence. Geeks Who Drink charged **$200 a night, up to $500 for themed nights**, with free entry for players, and a 2026 sales sheet cited by a competitor shows **$195 per show on 16-week agreements** ([Albuquerque Journal](https://www.pressreader.com/usa/albuquerque-journal/20190811/281492162949080); [Quizado](https://quizado.com/compare/geeks-who-drink-alternative)). Venues pay because trivia fills slow weeknights. The language exchange scene already gathers in bars: Mundo Lingo runs free weekly socials across cities and funds itself on bar sales, and two NYC Meetup groups list **19,758 and 18,422 members** ([Wikipedia: Mundo Lingo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mundo_Lingo); [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/en-AU/topics/french/us)). None runs a gamified quiz. A Polingual night combines the venue-pays model of trivia with the free-attendee model of exchanges, and the QR join starts a trial for every player.

The sponsored international night ranks fifth with the highest competitive-gap score. Intercomprehension, teaching speakers of one Romance language to read and understand the others, has twenty years of EU-funded platforms, from Galanet in 2003 to Miriadi from 2015 ([Wikipedia: Intercomprehension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intercomprehension)). A 21-hour module with Italian pupils aged 10 to 11 showed larger English reading gains than a comparison group, and UNITA courses reached **over 2,000 people** ([Int. J. Multilingualism](https://informahealthcare.com/doi/full/10.1080/14790718.2021.2019746); [UNITA D3.3](https://univ-unita.eu/Ficheiros/Sites/86/Paginas/1255/D3.3_Reports%20on%20the%20effectiveness%20of%20training%20programs.pdf)). No commercial consumer product was found. The evidence supports receptive skills, reading and listening, which is what a quiz tests. NYC fits a Romance night: **3.1M immigrants, over a third of residents**, with China and the Dominican Republic the largest origins, plus Brazilian, Italian, French and Francophone African communities and a cultural institute for each language ([NYC Mayor's Office](https://www.nyc.gov/ru/mayors-office/news/2026/06/-newest-new-yorkers--report-highlights-new-york-city-s-immigrant)). L'Alliance New York sells memberships and **$20 trial classes** at open houses, so a joint night lets each institute recruit from the others' audiences ([L'Alliance NY](https://lallianceny.org/event/fall-open-house-2026-fete-de-la-rentree/)). Sponsorship prices are the main unknown: no figure for tourism-board or institute sponsorship of language nights was found, so the first nights should run on a venue fee and treat sponsorship as upside. Mundo Lingo refuses sponsors and commissions, which makes Meetup organizers and institutes the first partners.

### Cities with the densest language mix come first

Miami-Dade at about **51% foreign-born** and Los Angeles at **39.7%** hold the densest Spanish-speaking bases in the US, and Toronto is **46.6% immigrant** ([Geography Worlds](https://geographyworlds.com/blog/most-diverse-cities-world-guide/); [City of Toronto](https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/8f69-2021-Census-Backgrounder-Immigration-Ethnoracial-Mobility-Migration-Religion-FINAL.pdf)). NYC leads on documented variety with **700 languages at over 1,200 sites** ([ELA](https://elalliance.org/our-work/maps/nyc-map)) and is home base, so it is the pilot city. Dubai, at **83% foreign-born**, and Singapore lean South Asian and Chinese, which suits a Sinitic or South Asian cluster night over a Romance one ([Emirates 24/7](https://www.emirates247.com/news/emirates/dubai-most-cosmopolitan-city-globally-83-population-is-foreign-born-2016-01-17-1.617596)). Polyglot events are small and evangelical: the 2026 Polyglot Gathering in Brno drew **827 participants from 67 countries**, and the Polyglot Conference runs in Bologna on 18 to 20 November 2026 ([Omniglot](https://www.omniglot.com/bloggle/?p=24618)). They suit launches and content more than revenue.

## Classrooms pay through teachers before districts

The teacher plan ranks third because every classroom game winner grew the same way. Blooket, Gimkit and Kahoot went free for teachers, spread through teacher Facebook groups, Reddit and TikTok, then sold paid teacher tiers and school plans ([Blooket history](https://blooket.it.com/who-created-blooket-founder-story/)). Duolingo for Schools is free and about **350,000 classrooms** used it at one point, so Polingual has to win on live group play and teacher control ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/introducing-duolingo-for-schools-the-ultimate-partner-for-language-teachers)). The audience is about **10.6M US K-12 world-language students** in the last national count and about **5M English learners** ([American Councils](https://www.americancouncils.org/news/new-report-world-language-study-us-k-12-schools); [Grantsights](https://grantsights.com/blog/title-iii-grants-explained)). Free class play with paid at-home practice, and a Teacher Pro tier near Gimkit's $60 a year, fits budgets that survived the end of ESSER.

District licenses rank ninth despite a large market because the money is tight and slow. Title III English learner funding sits flat at **$890M**, was withheld in summer 2025 until states sued, and faces another elimination proposal in FY27 ([EdWeek](https://www.edweek.org/policy-politics/educators-warn-flat-english-learner-funding-falls-short-of-growing-demand/2026/02)). ESSER, which equaled **30% to 40%** of district discretionary spending, is gone ([Oliver Wyman](https://www.oliverwyman.com/our-expertise/insights/2025/mar/k-12-investment-strategies-stimulus-funds-end-2025.html)). The cheapest path through privacy review is signing the National Data Privacy Agreement, which feeds a registry used by **28 state alliances** ([A4L](https://privacy.a4l.org/)). Integration order follows cost: Google sign-in and Classroom share first, then Clever Library and ClassLink SSO, since ClassLink partner integrations are free and Clever Secure Sync reportedly starts near **$10,800 a year** ([Teachfloor](https://www.teachfloor.com/blog/what-is-classlink); [Edlink](https://ed.link/community/how-to-start-a-clever-integration/)).

Colleges rank twelfth. Language enrollments fell **16.6% from 2016 to 2021**, the largest drop on record, with German down 33.6% and Korean up 38.3% ([Axios](https://axios.com/2023/11/15/college-students-foreign-languages-enrollment-french-spanish-korean)). The fast path is the clubs that already run events: language ambassador programs at MIT, Stony Brook and Santa Clara, and weekly conversation tables at schools such as Nebraska Kearney ([MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/index%2Ephp/2025/global-languages-program-empowers-student-ambassadors-0114)). A free host account per club with paid student subscriptions comes before any department license. Abroad, Korean hagwons are private businesses inside a **29.2 trillion won** private education sector, and Latin American national English programs buy through slow ministry tenders, so private academies are the faster entry ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/spending-on-private-education-jumps-60-percent-over-past-decade-data/12230869); [Inter-American Dialogue](https://thedialogue.org/blogs/2017/10/what-is-the-state-of-english-language-proficiency-in-latin-america)). ACTFL 2026 runs **November 20 to 22 in San Antonio** with about 6,000 to 7,000 attendees, too soon to exhibit; ACTFL 2027 and spring state conferences are the booth targets ([ACTFL](https://www.actfl.org/convention-and-expo)).

## Career vocabulary and CEFR badges turn learning into proof

### Job-specific vocabulary fills a gap no rival covers

Career vocabulary ranks fourth with a 9 for competitive gap. The pipeline is cheap: the learner names a job, Polingual maps it to an O\*NET-SOC code, pulls task statements and the Tools and Technology file, and an LLM ranks and translates the concrete nouns and verbs. O\*NET is **CC BY 4.0**, which permits commercial use with attribution, and covers about 900 occupations; ESCO adds about **3,039 occupations and 13,890 skills** with labels in up to 28 languages ([O\*NET licenses](https://www.onetcenter.org/license_agreements.html); [Tools and Technology readme](https://onetcenter.org/dl_files/tools_technology_readme.pdf); [Lifelong-learning.lu](https://lifelong-learning.lu/actualite/13-890-skills/en)). Job postings mostly add soft-skill and credential terms and cost money through Lightcast, while USAJOBS is free for federal roles ([Lightcast](https://lightcast.io/open-skills/faqs)). The learning case is solid: technical vocabulary makes up about **20% to 30% of specialized text** in Chung and Nation's study, about one word in three in anatomy ([Chung and Nation 2003](https://www2.hawaii.edu/~readfl/rfl/October2003/chung/chung.pdf)). Incumbents sell fixed tracks: Babbel for Business sells Construction Spanish, Command Spanish licenses occupational Spanish through community colleges for paramedics, warehouses and dental offices, and Voxy places learners in workplace scenarios ([Babbel for Business](https://www.babbelforbusiness.com/us/spanish-for-business/construction/); [Houston CC](https://hccs.edu/hcc-talent-solutions/training-solutions/spanish-esl--languages); [Voxy](https://voxy.com/?p=134850)). None found generates a set for any job on demand. No controlled study compares job-targeted vocabulary with general curricula on retention, so the feature should launch as a consumer onboarding hook and earn B2B claims with data.

Workplace seats rank tenth because demand runs in two directions with real budgets and long sales cycles. About **18 million working-age US adults** speak English below a fluent level, concentrated in agriculture, construction, manufacturing and hospitality; Oregon's natural resources sector is **25% limited English proficient** ([CCRC](https://ccrc.tc.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/community-colleges-providers-adult-education.pdf); [Oregon Employment Dept](https://qualityinfo.org/-/ability-of-oregon-workers-to-speak-english-varies-by-type-of-job-1)). English-speaking supervisors and clinicians need Spanish for workers and patients. The buyers who move fastest are state workforce boards that buy seats, as North Dakota did with 1,000 EnGen seats, and employer benefits such as Amazon Career Choice, which funds ESL and chose EnGen in 2022 ([EnGen Amazon](https://getengen.com/amazon)). Federal adult education money is a weak foundation: the FY2026 justification proposed eliminating the state grants.

### A CEFR badge is the realistic certificate

Duolingo needed about four years, a research team, a public technical manual and a pandemic to win acceptance for the Duolingo English Test, which reached **6,000+ accepting programs** and earned **$42.0M in 2025, down from $45.6M** as international student flows shrank ([DET 2024 review](https://blog.englishtest.duolingo.com/the-duolingo-english-test-a-year-in-review-2024); [TOEFL Resources](https://www.toeflresources.com/blog/duolingo-posts-2025-financial-report/)). IDP's test volume fell **18%** in the year to June 2025 and India IELTS fell 50% ([IDP](https://careers.idp.com/news/New-English-language-testing-data-new-opportunity-to-set-students-up-for-success)). A new high-stakes test from Polingual scores 3.10 and ranks 24th. The copyable model is EF SET, a free, CEFR-aligned test with a LinkedIn certificate that feeds EF's paid products, and the Duolingo Score, an in-course CEFR-mapped level with no admissions claim ([EF SET](https://www.efset.org/cefr/); [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-score/)). Polingual should map lessons to CEFR, show an in-app level, and issue an Open Badges 3.0 completion certificate; it can say "assessed at CEFR B1" only after a standard-setting exercise that follows the Council of Europe manual ([Council of Europe](https://coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/relating-examinations-to-the-cefr)). Credly costs about **$2,500 a year plus $5,000 setup** by third-party report, and cheaper issuers exist ([Certifier](https://certifier.io/blog/credly-pricing-is-credly-worth-it-in-2022)).

Test prep ranks eleventh and rides growing non-English tests. JLPT drew about **1.9M applicants in 2025**, DELF and DALF **513,435 candidates** led by school versions, TOPIK **550,000** in the first nine months of 2025 and HSK **719,000** in 2024 ([JLPT](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/statistics/archive.html); [France Education international](https://www.france-education-international.fr/actualites/lettre-fei/2026-02/les-certifications-de-langue-francaise-de-france-education-international-en-forte-hausse); [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/academic-misconduct-on-the-rise-as-topik-test-takers-hit-new-high/10426129); [China Services Info](https://investinchina.chinaservicesinfo.com/s/202506/16/WS68513029498eec7e1f739ff5/more-in-region-are-seeking-to-learn-chinese.html)). Timed mock-exam rounds in the lobby that end in a CEFR level fit the format. In US schools, the Seal of Biliteracy in all 50 states runs on AP, STAMP 4S and AAPPL, and Avant sells STAMP 4S at **$19.90 to $39.90 per test** at school volume, which prices a Seal-readiness add-on ([The Global Seal](https://theglobalseal.com/seal-of-biliteracy); [Avant](https://rebrand.avantassessment.com/pricing-2)).

## New devices pay only where a room already forms

Smart TV and Discord rank sixth and seventh because both are ports of the lobby. AirConsole proved phones-as-controllers on TV with **over 1M monthly active players** on pay TV in 2022 and has moved into Audi, Porsche, Skoda, BMW and VW cars ([CSI Magazine](https://csimagazine.com/csi/Vewd-and-AirConsole-team-on-TV-gaming.php); [Advanced Television](https://www.advanced-television.com/2025/06/26/265657/)). A Cast and AirPlay target from the web lobby, then thin tvOS and Android TV hosts, cover the living room. Discord Activities are web apps in an iframe, open to all developers since September 2024, with in-Activity purchases through the Embedded App SDK and a young audience ([Discord docs](https://docs.discord.com/developers/monetization/implementing-iap-for-activities)). Revenue there should come from upgrading players to the Polingual subscription; Discord's fee terms conflict across sources. Netflix licensing scores high on fit and gap and 2 on speed, because Netflix picks titles with a track record.

Kids, VR, Roblox and consoles fall to the bottom tier for structural reasons. A kids product sits in a funded market, with Lingokids at **168M to 185M lifetime downloads** and a $120M raise, but under-13 users bring full COPPA duties since **April 22, 2026**, penalties up to **$53,088 per violation**, and Apple's ban on third-party analytics and ads in the Kids category ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/lingokids-raises-120m-for-interactive-learning-app-for-kids/); [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/22/2025-05904/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule); [compliancehub](https://compliancehub.wiki/blog/coppa-april-22-deadline-ftc-updated-rule-compliance)). It must be a separate app with no free text or voice chat, so it waits. Quest's most active users are teens 12 to 16, but language VR titles stay small: Noun Town claims 200,000 players and grossed an estimated **$114.66K on Steam**, and Vision Pro shipped only about 600K units lifetime ([Meta GDC 2026](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/blog/gdc-2026-state-of-vr/); [SteamData](https://steamdata.ai/en-US/game/1643100/noun-town-vr-language-learning); [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2026/01/02/apple_vision_pro_meta_quest_sales_drop/)). A WebXR build of a web game is the cheap test. Roblox pays creators on engagement time and Duolingo used it as a marketing activation ([TechRound](https://techround.co.uk/?p=67352)). Browser extensions end in acquisition: Babbel bought Toucan and made it free ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2023/09/berlin-based-babbel-acquires-us-based-toucan-to-further-expand-its-learning-ecosystem-with-browser-extension/)). Google shut third-party Conversational Actions in June 2023, which removes voice as a paid channel ([Google](https://developers.google.com/assistant/ca-sunset)). Steam took about 19,000 releases in 2025 and only **8% grossed over $100K** ([GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/games/over-5-000-games-released-on-steam-this-year-didnt-make-enough-money-to-recover-the-usd100-fee-to-put-a-game-on-valves-store-research-estimates)), and consoles cost a year of kit waits.

## Conclusion

The map inverts the usual expansion logic for a language app. Majors spread to new devices and earn little there: Duolingo's Vision Pro presence is its iPhone app and its Roblox space was a marketing stunt, while Pearson and Babbel bought their way into VR and browsers and then made those products free or minor. Polingual's advantage is the social setting, and the setting decides the buyer. A living room sells subscriptions, a bar sells a weekly license, a classroom sells a teacher plan and an institute sells sponsorship, all from the same lobby code. That makes event-level attribution, a QR scan tied to a trial and then a payment, the most important piece of infrastructure in the first quarter, because it is the number every later pitch to a venue, sponsor, school, Netflix or workforce board will rest on.

The largest unknowns are prices nobody publishes: sponsor fees for cultural nights, conversion from live events to paid subscriptions, and whether teachers will pay for a language-specific room game at Gimkit's price. Each is answerable inside the first six months with a few venues, one institute partnership and a hundred teachers. Career vocabulary is the sleeper: it costs little, draws on open data, and sets up the workplace market, where buyers pay $100 a year or more per seat, without a sales team in year one.
