# Language Learning Apps and Games Market for Polingual Play

## Duolingo: Revenue, Users, Monetization and Game Features

### Takeaway
Duolingo is a $1.2B-revenue subscription business with 64.9M DAU as of Q3 2026; subscriptions are 87% of revenue and ads are under 7%. Its game push runs through Chess PvP, partner placements in Brawl Stars and Eggy Party, and collection events, and it is retiring the Max tier.

### Cited Findings
- Q3 2026 (quarter ended Sep 30, 2026, released Nov 3, 2026): revenue $309.0M, up 14%; bookings $316.1M, up 12%; subscription bookings $278.4M, up 16%: [Duolingo Q3 2026 shareholder letter, SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)
- Q3 2026 DAU 64.9M, up 29% from 50.5M; MAU 153.1M, up 13% from 135.3M; paid subscribers 13.1M, up 14% from 11.5M: [same letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)
- Q3 2026 revenue mix: subscription $270.0M, up 18%; advertising $20.8M, down 1%; in-app purchases $6.9M, down 38%: [same letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)
- FY2026 guidance: bookings $1,296M, up 12%; revenue $1,221M, up 18%; adjusted EBITDA $323M, 26.5% margin. Q4 2026 guide: revenue $322M, bookings $382M: [same letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)
- Max tier: Duolingo will stop selling new Max subscriptions and move individual Max subscribers to a Super family plan at the same price; legacy Max family plans handled in 2027. Unlimited Video Call is rolling out to all Super subscribers by year end 2026: [same letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)
- Games distribution: Duolingo partnered with Brawl Stars and Eggy Party, featuring Duo in both and rewarding players for learning on Duolingo, which brought "millions of new and returning users": [same letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)
- Monetization through collection mechanics: "Junior's Toy Hunt" was the first Avatar Collections event (collect puzzle pieces to unlock outfits), with more planned on a regular cadence: [same letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)
- FY2025 revenue about $1,037.6M, up 39%; subscriptions $873.4M, 84% of total. Secondary source citing the 10-K: [Voxbooster](https://voxbooster.com/blog/duolingo-statistics-2026); Motley Fool reports DAU passed 50M in 2025: [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/02/16/3-key-takeaways-from-duolingos-2025)
- Max was about 9% of the paying base by late 2025, up from about 5% at end of 2024. Secondary aggregator: [SQ Magazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/duolingo-statistics/)
- Chess: launched on iOS June 2025; Chess PvP announced at Duocon 2025 (Sep 2025), real-time matches against other learners: [Duolingo Duocon 2025 release](https://investors.duolingo.com/news-releases/news-release-details/duolingo-unveils-major-product-updates-turn-learning-real-world). PvP reached Android in Feb 2026 via a "Matches" tab and "Play a Person" skill-matched pairing: [Daily Tribune](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/02/24/duolingo-chess-goes-live)
- Chess daily users: Duolingo said "millions of daily learners" at Duocon 2025; third-party claims of 1M and about 7M daily users conflict and are unverified: [Duocon release](https://investors.duolingo.com/news-releases/news-release-details/duolingo-unveils-major-product-updates-turn-learning-real-world)
- Commentary on Duolingo's AI-built move into games: [Speedrun, a16z](https://speedrun.substack.com/p/duolingos-ai-powered-leap-into-games)

### Inferences
- Duolingo's games serve retention and DAU, and money comes from the subscription. IAP fell 38% and ads were flat, so selling games or consumables directly has not been a growth lever for the category leader.
- Duolingo now acquires users by placing Duo inside large consumer games. A games-first language product competes with that funnel and could also use it: partner placements in existing games are a tested acquisition path.
- Chess PvP shows Duolingo is moving into live, real-time matchmaking. Live multiplayer is no longer empty ground in solo mobile, though Duolingo has nothing for a shared big screen or group play in a room.
- Retiring Max suggests a standalone AI premium tier did not convert at scale; folding AI features into the base subscription is the market direction.

### Gaps
- Leagues, Adventures and Duolingo Score were not covered in the Q3 2026 letter. No 2026 engagement figures for these features found.
- No official Chess DAU number in filings.
- FY2025 figures come from secondary sources; the 10-K was not fetched directly.

## Other Language Apps: Revenue, Models and Game Elements

### Takeaway
AI conversation apps are the fastest growers: Speak passed $100M annual revenue in 2025 and Praktika reported $55M ARR. Babbel is a subscription business near €350M. Reliable revenue data for Busuu, Memrise, Drops, Mondly, Rosetta Stone and Lingokids was not found in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- Babbel: €352M revenue in 2024, up 6.6%. A separate $750M figure from another aggregator conflicts and looks unreliable: [Skillademia](https://skillademia.com/statistics/babbel-statistics/); [Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/?p=70303)
- Babbel is paid-only, while Memrise and Busuu offer free tiers: [Electroiq](https://electroiq.com/stats/babbel-statistics/)
- Babbel launched an AI "Speak" feature in Sep 2025: [Babbel press](https://www.babbel.com/press/en-us/releases/babbel-speak). Babbel discontinued consumer live classes in mid-2025 per a review site, unverified: [Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/duolingo-babbel-rosetta-stone-busuu-memrise-language-learning-apps-ranked-2026)
- Speak (AI tutor, OpenAI-backed): more than $100M annual revenue in 2025, up from $15M in 2024; $1B valuation at its 2024 Series C ($78M): [Latka](https://www.getlatka.com/companies/speak.com); Forbes figure relayed via [Silicon Valley Investclub tear sheet](https://siliconvalleyinvestclub.com/companies/speak/tear-sheet.pdf). CB Insights lists 2023 revenue $24M and $165.8M raised: [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/speak-2/financials)
- Praktika (AI avatar tutor): $55M ARR, profitable, AI gross margin above 90%, up from $20M ARR in May 2024. Date of report not confirmed: [ARR Club](https://www.arr.club/signal/praktika-hit-55m-arr-profitable)
- Drops: Kahoot acquired Drops in 2020 for about $50M; Drops then counted 25M users: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2077983). Drops 2025 revenue estimate $4.4M, unverified aggregator: [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/languagedrops.com)
- Busuu: owned by Chegg per a review site; no deal details in results: [Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/duolingo-babbel-rosetta-stone-busuu-memrise-language-learning-apps-ranked-2026)

### Inferences
- Revenue in 2024 to 2026 moved to AI speaking practice (Speak, Praktika, Duolingo Video Call). Game mechanics are a retention layer in all these apps, and none of them sells a game.
- Drops is the closest precedent for a "learning as a short game" brand, and its estimated revenue after eight years is small. Pure vocabulary-game mechanics alone have not built a large business.

### Gaps
- No sourced 2025 or 2026 revenue for Babbel, Busuu, Memrise, Mondly, Rosetta Stone or Lingokids.
- Ownership facts known from background but not verified in this pass: IXL owns Rosetta Stone (bought 2021), Chegg bought Busuu in 2021, Pearson bought Mondly in 2022. Verify before use.
- No Sensor Tower or Appfigures download or revenue data was retrieved for any app.

## Language Games on Steam, Consoles and Classroom Quiz Platforms

### Takeaway
Premium language games sell in the tens to hundreds of thousands of units at best. Ubisoft's DS "My Coach" line is the one case where a language-adjacent game line reached millions, and it rode a casual-console boom. Classroom live quiz tools (Kahoot, Blooket, Gimkit) reach tens of millions on free-to-teacher, paid-upgrade models.

### Cited Findings
- Influent (Three Flip Studios, Mar 2014, $19.99): SteamSpy owner estimate 200,000 to 500,000: [SteamSpy](https://www.steamspy.com/dev/Three+Flip+Studios). Studio claims it taught more than 500,000 students on Steam and later shipped a mobile version: [Capsule Computers](https://www.capsulecomputers.com.au/?p=317231)
- Learn Japanese To Survive! Hiragana Battle (Sleepy Duck, $6.99): "Very Positive", about 1,736 Steam reviews; no sales estimate: [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/438270); [Datahumble](https://datahumble.com/learn-japanese-to-survive-hiragana-battle-438270). Sequel Katakana War estimated at about 35.6k units and $120k gross, third-party estimate: [SteamData.AI](https://steamdata.ai/en-US/game/554600/learn-japanese-to-survive-katakana-war)
- Koe (Strawberry Games, Apr 2024, $19.99): JRPG that teaches Japanese; 22 Steam reviews and an all-time peak of 22 concurrent players: [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/672430); [SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/672430/charts)
- My Spanish Coach (Ubisoft, DS): VGChartz shows about 0.43M lifetime, flagged as incomplete: [VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/game/12604/my-spanish-coach/sales). My Word Coach (DS) sold 1.40M, 1.03M of it in Europe: [VGChartz DS million sellers](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/83097/million-seller-watch-nintendo-ds/). Ubisoft's "My Coach" series totals about 7M across titles, fan wiki: [Video Game Sales Wiki](https://vgsales.fandom.com/wiki/Best-selling_Ubisoft_games)
- Kahoot: taken private in 2024 in a $1.7B deal led by Goldman Sachs Alternatives, General Atlantic and Kirkbi; 2022 recognized revenue $146M, up 60%; 1.4M paid subscribers, 24M active users including 8M educators: [Apps Run The World](https://www.appsruntheworld.com/apps-top-500-software-vendors/kahoot/). Revenue estimate of $150M as of Oct 2025 is an aggregator figure: [Sig.ai](https://geo.sig.ai/brands/kahoot)
- Blooket: more than 15M users as of June 2024; 54M monthly site visits by Nov 2025; school bundles from about $550 a year: [Caplight](https://www.caplight.com/company/blooket); [AhaSlides](https://ahaslides.com/blog/blooket-review/)
- Gimkit: independent, Seattle, about seven employees per Tracxn late 2025; department license $650 a year for up to 20 teachers: [Plisio](https://plisio.net/education/what-is-gimkit); [Jotform](https://www.jotform.com/blog/blooket-vs-gimkit/)

### Inferences
- Steam is a weak channel for premium language games: the best known title (Influent) reached a few hundred thousand owners over ten years at $19.99, and recent narrative entries like Koe barely registered. A paid Steam game should be treated as marketing, not core revenue.
- Classroom live quiz is the largest proven "game plus learning" market, and its winners (Kahoot, Blooket, Gimkit) are subject-agnostic. None of them is a language specialist, which leaves room for a language-specific live team quiz with built-in content, audio and pronunciation.
- Gimkit shows a team of under ten people can win a share of the classroom live game market on teacher licenses.

### Gaps
- No verified sales for Hiragana Battle, My Spanish Coach, or Switch-era language titles.
- No Wanikani-style game data (Wanikani is a web SRS service, not a game; no game spin-off found).
- Blooket and Gimkit revenue not found.

## Market Size and Growth 2025 to 2026

### Takeaway
Analyst estimates for language learning apps cluster at $6B to $8.5B for 2026; game-based learning estimates span $6B to $36B depending on definition. Duolingo's $1.2B 2026 revenue is the best hard anchor and implies it holds a large share of consumer app spend.

### Cited Findings
- Language learning apps: $5.86B in 2025, $6.42B in 2026, 7.2% CAGR: [Meticulous Research](https://meticulousresearch.com/reports/language-learning-apps-market-5658)
- Language learning apps: $7.36B in 2025, $8.55B in 2026: [Straits Research](https://straitsresearch.com/report/language-learning-apps-market)
- Broader online language learning: $21.1B in 2025, $24.4B in 2026 (Mordor, via aggregator): [Voxbooster](https://voxbooster.com/blog/language-learning-statistics-2026)
- Game-based learning: $6.23B in 2025 to $17.82B by 2030: [MarketsandMarkets](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/ResearchInsight/game-based-learning-market.amp); $20.71B in 2025, $26.28B in 2026, 26.9% CAGR: [Business Research Company](https://www.thebusinessresearchcompany.com/report/game-based-learning-global-market-report); $24.5B in 2025: [IMARC](https://imarcgroup.com/game-based-learning-market); $29.46B in 2025, $34.90B in 2026: [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/game-based-learning-market)
- Generative AI apps projected above $10B consumer spend in 2026 (Sensor Tower, secondary): [Mobile Marketing Reads](https://mobilemarketingreads.com/?p=49718)

### Inferences
- Duolingo at about $1.2B plus Speak, Babbel and Praktika at roughly $0.5B together is consistent with a consumer language app market in the $6B to $8B range only if many small apps and offline spend are included; treat the analyst figures as loose.
- Game-based learning figures include corporate training and K-12 platforms, so they overstate the space open to a consumer language game line.

### Gaps
- No Sensor Tower or Appfigures category totals for language learning consumer spend were found.
- No HolonIQ figure retrieved.

## Flash Portal Era and Browser Game Portals Today

### Takeaway
Browser portals carry word and typing games, and a few vocabulary titles, but no language-learning brand of note was found on Poki or CrazyGames. The space is open and ad-funded.

### Cited Findings
- CrazyGames hosts Typing Rush (Night Steed Games, Apr 2026), a listen-and-type word race with elimination mode and global leaderboard: [CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/game/typing-rush)
- Lexy (vocabulary and spelling) and Plangman (hangman) are listed as CrazyGames titles by a third-party catalog: [EduGameHQ](https://edugamehq.pages.dev/games/lexy/)
- Poki's word category holds puzzles and typing games such as Word City Crossed, Hangman and Fast Typer, with no language-learning category: [Poki Word Games](https://poki.com/en/words). Word Finder lists support for English, French, Spanish, German, Italian and Portuguese: [WebCatalog](https://webcatalog.io/games/poki-word-finder)

### Inferences
- Short multilingual word games could ship to Poki and CrazyGames as ad-funded acquisition funnels into Polingual, with little direct competition from language brands.

### Gaps
- No sourced record of Flash-era language games (Newgrounds, Kongregate, Miniclip) was found in this pass.
- Poki and CrazyGames revenue-share terms for educational games were not researched.

## Trademark Check: Polingual, Polingual Play, PolyPlay

### Takeaway
No registered "Polingual", "Polingual Play", "PolyPlay" or "Poly Play" mark turned up in web searches, but a live USPTO search was not run. Several near-identical language app names exist, and the "Poly-" game names are crowded.

### Cited Findings
- PolyLingual: an iOS language-learning app by Brennan Dumm, Education category, multi-language vocabulary study: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1639800345); [support page](https://bjdumm.github.io/PolyLingo_iOS)
- Polly Lingual: a language-learning product with lessons and live tutors, on the Chrome Web Store: [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/polly-lingual/neehdmebkjkhalgkbehnklenjcaejipd?hl=en); [All Language Resources](https://www.alllanguageresources.com/polly-lingual/)
- POLLPLAY: a registered mark for gaming software, owned by Studio Bay And Sky, Inc., serial 85698145: [Justia Trademarks](https://trademark.justia.com/owners/studio-bay-and-sky-inc-2416939)
- A game titled "poly play" is listed on a game database with no owner details: [Gamebrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/poly-play)
- Other "Poly" game brands: Poly Party on Steam (board games and minigames), PolyTap developer on Google Play: [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1291110); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/developer?id=PolyTap)

### Inferences
- "Polingual" sits one letter from "PolyLingual" and close to "Polly Lingual", both in the same class (language learning software). A USPTO examiner could raise likelihood of confusion if either holder has a filing; neither appears registered from these searches.
- "Polingual Play" is safer than "PolyPlay": POLLPLAY is a live gaming-software mark and "Poly" game names are common, so a standalone "PolyPlay" brand carries more conflict risk.

### Gaps
- USPTO Trademark Search (the TESS replacement) was not queried; it needs a direct search for POLINGUAL, POLYLINGUAL, POLLY LINGUAL, POLYPLAY and POLY PLAY in classes 9, 28, 41 and 42.
- EUIPO and WIPO Madrid were not checked.
- Steam was not searched for exact "Polingual" titles.
