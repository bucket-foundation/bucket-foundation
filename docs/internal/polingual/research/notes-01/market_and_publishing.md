# Games Market Size and Publishing Paths for a Small Studio

## Market size by segment, 2025 and 2026

### Takeaway
Newzoo puts 2025 global games revenue at $201.6B, up 9.1%, and forecasts $213.9B for 2026, up 6.1%. Mobile is 57% of spend. Web games are a small, fast-growing slice with no audited size: third-party estimates run $1.6B to $6.2B, and Poki alone reports 100M monthly players.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 final, Newzoo: total $201.6B, +9.1% YoY, first year above $200B. Mobile $113.3B, +10.7%. PC $43.6B, +12%, the highest PC growth in Newzoo's dataset. Console $44.7B, +2.8%. [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/global-games-revenue-breached-200b-in-2025-newzoo/); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/global-games-revenue-surpasses-200bn-for-the-first-time-mobile-generates-113bn/)
- Newzoo's September 2025 forecast for 2025 was $188.8B. The final figure came in $12.8B higher. [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/newzoo-global-games-market-set-to-hit-1888bn-in-2025)
- Newzoo scope: physical and digital game sales, in-game purchases, subscriptions such as Game Pass, and mobile platform revenue. It excludes advertising revenue, hardware, B2B, gambling and betting. Web portal games earn mostly from ads, so Newzoo totals undercount them. [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/global-games-revenue-breached-200b-in-2025-newzoo/)
- 2026 forecast, Newzoo: $213.9B, +6.1%. Mobile $121.1B, +6.8%, about 57% of the market. Console $46.9B, +5.1%, contingent on GTA VI releasing in November 2026. PC $45.9B, +5.3%. [Players.com.ua](https://players.com.ua/en/news/newzoo-forecasts-global-video-game-market-to-reach-213-9-billion-in-2026/); [GodIsAGeek](https://godisageek.com/2026/08/global-games-market-2026/); [GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-the-gaming-market-in-2026)
- 2026 players, Newzoo: 3.10B mobile, 977M PC, 651M console. [Players.com.ua](https://players.com.ua/en/news/newzoo-forecasts-global-video-game-market-to-reach-213-9-billion-in-2026/)
- Newzoo's PC and Console Report 2026 expects PC to grow at 6.6% CAGR against console 4.4% and pass console revenue by 2028. [ChannelNews](https://www.channelnews.com.au/pc-gaming-revenue-set-to-overtake-consoles-by-2028/); [Newzoo PDF via InvestGame](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2026-03-12-newzoo_2026_pc_and_console_gaming_report_wp.pdf)
- Steam generated about $16B in 2025, per 80.lv's analysis of third-party data. [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/steam-earned-usd16b-in-2025-but-nearly-half-of-19-000-games-got-under-10-reviews)
- Web/HTML5 market size estimates from report vendors, 2025: $1.62B [MarketResearch.com](https://marketresearch.com/LP-Information-Inc-v4134/Global-H5-Games-Growth-Status-39433094); $3.45B [Verified Market Research](https://www.verifiedmarketresearch.com/product/html5-games-market/); $6.24B for the broader webgame market including ads and virtual goods [Market Research Intellect](https://www.marketresearchintellect.com/product/webgame-market/).
- Playgama counted more than 15,000 new web games released in H1 2025, 2.7x H1 2024, and says most reports undervalue web gaming at $2B to $3B because they miss ad revenue. Playgama is a web game distributor with an interest in a larger number. [GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/playgama-most-popular-engines-for); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/more-than-15000-new-web-games-were-released-in-q2-2025)
- Poki: 625M players in 2025, 100M monthly players, over 1B gameplays a month, 100+ countries, 500+ developers, top games above 5M monthly players each. Poki grew without external funding. [Poki for Developers](https://developers.poki.com/); [Poki 2025 year in review](https://poki.com/blog/2025-at-poki-a-year-in-review); [DigitalMediaNet](https://digitalmedianet.com/poki-announces-milestone-of-625-million-players-without-raising-external-funding/amp/)
- Poki revenue is not public. Similarweb estimates $100M to $200M a year. [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/company/poki.com)
- CrazyGames audience: about 35M monthly active users by Semrush estimate, over 50M per the company's developer portal. [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/crazygames-vs-poki-vs-coolmath-games-2026/); [Cinevva guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-crazygames)
- Mainstream press called websites "video games' hottest new platform" in November 2025. [The Star / Bloomberg syndication](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/11/10/video-games-hottest-new-platform-is-an-old-one-websites)

### Inferences
- Mobile is the largest pool by a factor of 2.5, but PC is the fastest-growing segment and Steam has the lowest entry cost of any storefront.
- Web portal revenue sits outside the Newzoo totals. A $2B to $6B range is the defensible band; cite it as a range with the vendor caveat.

### Gaps
- No Sensor Tower or Circana 2026 segment figures turned up in the searches run. Newzoo's full Global Games Market Report 2026, due September 10, 2026, may revise the preliminary numbers above; I did not see the revised figures.
- No audited revenue for Poki or CrazyGames.
- No segment size for educational or edutainment games.

## Steam

### Takeaway
Steam costs $100 per game, recouped after $1,000 in revenue, with a 30% platform cut. It took about 19,000 releases in 2025, and most earn little. Language-learning titles on Steam show small sales in third-party estimates.

### Cited Findings
- Steam Direct fee: $100 per product, non-refundable, recouped once the product earns $1,000 Adjusted Gross Revenue. [Steamworks: Steam Direct](https://partner.steamgames.com/steamdirect)
- Onboarding paperwork: digital agreement, bank details, tax questionnaire, identity verification. This takes 15 to 30 days. [Steamworks](https://partner.steamgames.com/steamdirect)
- Timing: 21-day wait between paying the fee and release. Store page must be public as "coming soon" for at least two weeks. Valve reviews the store page and the build, 1 to 5 days each, checking configuration and that the game runs. [Steamworks](https://partner.steamgames.com/steamdirect)
- Revenue split: 30% to Valve, 25% on a game's revenue above $10M, 20% above $50M, in force since October 2018. [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com:443/valves-new-revenue-sharing-favours-big-budget-games-and-indie-devs-arent-happy); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2018/12/01/steam-valve-developer-revenue-agreement/)
- Releases per year, SteamDB: about 19,008 in 2025 by mid-December, against 18,558 in 2024 and 14,111 in 2023. [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/12/over-19000-games-have-released-on-steam-in-2025-with-nearly-half-seeing-fewer-than-10-reviews); [Generation Amiga](https://www.generationamiga.com/2025/12/27/from-14k-to-19k-steam-game-launches-hit-all-time-high-in-2025/)
- Visibility: about 2,200 2025 titles had no reviews and another 7,100 peaked at nine or fewer. [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/steam-earned-usd16b-in-2025-but-nearly-half-of-19-000-games-got-under-10-reviews)
- Earnings: per Gamalytic-based estimates, over 5,000 2025 releases (about 40%) did not earn back the $100 fee, and only 8% grossed over $100K. [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/games/over-5-000-games-released-on-steam-this-year-didnt-make-enough-money-to-recover-the-usd100-fee-to-put-a-game-on-valves-store-research-estimates); [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/news/22399/only-8-of-steam-games-gross-over-100-thousand-40-make-less-than-steam-listing-fees)
- Language-learning games on Steam, modeled estimates: WonderLang Polyglot about $24K gross, 828 units at $39.99 [DataHumble](https://datahumble.com/wonderlang-polyglot-3599480); Mondly VR about 4K copies, about $20K gross lifetime [GameSensor](https://gamesensor.info/app/1141930); Noun Town has an early access run and a 1.0 release with sales above its publisher's prior average [SteamData](https://steamdata.ai/en-US/game/2313720/noun-town-language-learning). GameSensor states its numbers are modeled from review counts and pricing, not Valve data.

### Inferences
- Educational games on Steam sell in the low thousands of units in the examples found. Steam suits a language game as a secondary channel or wishlist funnel, with income driven by marketing outside Steam.

### Gaps
- No Valve data or analyst study on the "Education" tag as a category. Sales numbers for edu titles are third-party models.

## Xbox

### Takeaway
ID@Xbox is free to join for approved studios and supplies dev kits at no cost, but approval favors teams with shipped titles and kit delivery has lagged. The Microsoft Store pays developers 88% on PC games.

### Cited Findings
- No fees to apply to ID@Xbox, to submit to certification, or to publish and update games, aside from a one-time Partner Center account fee. [Microsoft Developer: Publish](https://developer.microsoft.com/en-AU/games/publish)
- Approved concepts get dev kits free. Commercial Xbox Series X|S kit price rose $500 to $2,000; ID@Xbox members still get kits free. [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/108399/microsoft-raises-prices-of-xbox-devkits-by-500-id-xbox-members-still-get-free-xdks/index.html)
- Studios with a shipped track record on console, PC or mobile get priority. [GamesRadar, dated](https://gamesradar.com/indies-can-self-publish-without-fees-xbox-one)
- An indie studio reported in December 2025 that kits had not arrived about a year after ID@Xbox approval. [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/indies-idxbox/indie-studio-says-xbox-is-encouraging-development-for-its-platform-yet-id-xbox-dev-kit-delays-are-leaving-approved-projects-stuck-in-limbo)
- GDC 2026: free PlayFab backend for every game shipping on Xbox, and an "Indie Selects" editorial program. [Cinevva news, secondary](https://app.cinevva.com/news/2026-03-11-xbox-playfab-free-crossplatform)
- Microsoft Store PC games split: 88% developer, 12% Microsoft, since August 2021, no volume thresholds. [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/microsoft-store-giving-88-net-sales-revenue-developers-isnt-enough-shake-steam); [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/microsofts-new-pc-revenue-share-makes-things-weird-for-cross-buy-games)

### Inferences
- Xbox is the cheapest console to start on. Certification cost is zero; the real cost is engineering time and the wait for kits.

### Gaps
- Console store split on Xbox, reported historically as 30%, was not confirmed in a 2026 source. Game Pass deal terms are negotiated case by case and are not public.

## PlayStation

### Takeaway
Registration runs through PlayStation Partners with a studio profile and short project plan. Sony lends one PS5 dev kit and one test kit free to newly licensed indies under a 2022 program. Kit prices are not public.

### Cited Findings
- Development Hardware Loan Program, July 2022: newly licensed indie publishers and developers get one PS5 dev kit and one PS5 test kit on loan at no cost. [Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/131568/playstation-development-hardware-program); [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2022/07/27/playstation-launches-new-indie-program-to-provide-developers-with-free-ps-dev-kits)
- Process: register on PlayStation Partners, get approved, sign the Global Developer and Publisher Agreement, then gain tools, docs and Help Center access. Registration asks for studio information and a short project plan. Sony publishes no review timeline or rubric. Some countries are unsupported. [MacMyths, secondary, cites Sony guidance dated April 7, 2026](https://macmyths.com/how-playstation-game-pitches-work-for-developers/)
- Feature costs can surprise indies: a report described a $25,000 charge for a PlayStation feature. [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-18851)

### Inferences
- Budget for the loan program as the default path; plan for added costs like age ratings, localization QA and any paid features.

### Gaps
- No public PS5 dev kit price. No confirmation the loan program still runs in 2026. PlayStation Store revenue split not confirmed in a current source.

## Nintendo Switch and Switch 2

### Takeaway
Developer Portal registration is free and open to anyone, but Switch access needs a second application, and Switch 2 kits have been scarce for indies. Kit pricing is under NDA; the original Switch kit was reported around $450.

### Cited Findings
- The Nintendo Developer Portal is Nintendo's single developer program, open to developers of any size and experience. [Nintendo Developer FAQ](https://developer.nintendo.com/faq); [Register](https://developer.nintendo.com/register)
- Access to Switch information requires a separate application after account creation. [Nintendo: The Process](https://developer.nintendo.com/the-process)
- Original Switch dev kit reported as low as $440 to $450. [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/nintendo-switch-dev-kits-could-run-as-low-as-440); [1d3](https://1d3.com/?p=1750)
- eShop base commission 30%. [1d3](https://1d3.com/?p=1750)
- Switch 2 kit pricing stays under NDA; one 2026 guide reports 3 to 6 weeks from purchase to delivery. [Strayspark, secondary](https://www.strayspark.studio/blog/nintendo-switch-2-unreal-engine-indie-development-2026)
- Kit access is contested: Nintendo prioritized Switch 2 kits for indies needing extra performance [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2025/06/nintendo-prioritising-switch-2-dev-kits-for-indies-needing-extra-oomph-say-devs); New Blood said access was similar to Switch 1 but Nintendo screens for shovelware [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Indie-game-studio-says-Switch-2-dev-kits-are-more-accessible-but-Nintendo-fears-shovelware-slop.1244947.0.html); a 2026 report says Nintendo steered some developers to first-gen Switch [Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2563289/nintendo-said-to-be-steering-devs-away-from-switch-2-in-favor-of-first-gen-hardware).
- New Switch 2 eShop rules aim to curb low-quality listings. [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/we-have-to-compete-for-visibility-with-bad-actors-who-are-just-running-a-grift-devs-hope-new-switch-2-eshop-rules-fight-slop-and-worry-good-games-may-get-flagged-but-steam-is-still-miles-ahead-of-nintendo)

### Inferences
- A Switch 1 build runs on Switch 2 via backward compatibility, so a small studio can target Switch 1 first.

### Gaps
- No source found on eShop education titles: no separate category, certification path, or sales data turned up.
- No confirmed Switch 2 kit price.

## Apple Arcade and Netflix Games

### Takeaway
Both are curated and invite-driven; neither takes open submissions. Apple pays an upfront fee plus a play-based bonus pool, with payouts reported as falling. Netflix closed most internal studios in 2025 and now licenses third-party party, kids and IP games for TV play, with phones as controllers.

### Cited Findings
- Apple Arcade selection: Apple pitches concepts to studios it thinks fit, and some deals began from a concept and wireframes. [Digital Trends interview with Alex Rofman](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/apple-arcade-alex-rofman-interview/)
- Deal shape: upfront fee plus a bonus pool tied to qualifying play sessions; early deals were three-year exclusives with fixed fee plus royalties. Developers keep their IP. Apple does not disclose splits. [Gadget Hacks](https://apple.gadgethacks.com/news/apple-arcade-struggles-6-years-later-bundle-success-hides-gaming-woes); [iMore](https://imore.com/gaming/apple-is-going-to-start-removing-apple-arcade-games-this-is-why); [Yahoo](https://news.yahoo.com/apple-arcade-invests-developers-while-203000373.html)
- 2024: developers described falling payouts since about October 2020 and few original greenlights. [MacRumors, 2024](https://www.macrumors.com/2024/02/26/developers-describe-apple-arcade-smell-of-death)
- 2026: Arcade reported 250+ games from 180 developers in 30+ countries. [Remio, secondary](https://www.remio.ai/post/apple-gaming-strategy-expands-beyond-arcade-but-developers-still-need-proof). Apple keeps adding licensed "+" versions of App Store hits, such as Family Feud Pocket in June 2026. [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/kw/newsroom/2026/06/mini-football-legends-family-feud-pocket-and-seven-more-hits-join-apple-arcade/)
- Netflix 2025: closed Boss Fight, Night School and Moonloot, and earlier Team Blue. Stated focus areas: Kids (Netflix Playground app), Party, Narrative, Mainstream. [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/netflix-shutters-2-game-studios-including-unhinged-maker-night-school/); [Mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/netflix-to-close-la-studio-night-school-helsinkis-moonloot-and-cut-internal-game-teams/)
- Head of games Alain Tascan moved Netflix from mobile to TV and PC streamed games with phones as controllers. [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/netflix-shutters-2-game-studios-including-unhinged-maker-night-school/)
- January 2026: co-CEO Greg Peters named cloud TV games a top priority; about one-third of members had access to TV games, and party titles reached about 10% of eligible members. [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/netflix-makes-cloud-based-tv-games-a-key-priority-for-2026)
- April 2026: Netflix licensed three Jackbox games; its TV lineup includes Pictionary, Boggle, LEGO Party, Tetris Time Warp and Overcooked. [Pulse](https://www.pulse.bot/gaming/news/netflix-adds-three-jackbox-games-to-its-tv-app-17b902be-17cb-447a-8af9-59b1676a4e6c); [etcentric](https://www.etcentric.org/netflix-will-offer-party-games-via-its-tv-service-for-first-time/)

### Inferences
- A phone-as-controller team quiz for a TV screen matches Netflix's party and kids categories. Netflix licenses established titles, so a small studio would need a proven game and traction before a pitch.

### Gaps
- No public Netflix licensing terms or submission route. No official 2026 Apple Arcade terms.

## Poki and CrazyGames

### Takeaway
Poki is a curated, application-based portal with a default five-year web exclusive and a reported 50/50 ad split on Poki-sourced traffic. CrazyGames is open submission with a QA review, a 7 to 21 day test launch, and no required exclusivity.

### Cited Findings
- Poki: developers apply through the developer site; the SDK handles ads. [Poki for Developers](https://developers.poki.com/); [Poki SDK](https://sdk.poki.com/)
- Poki terms, secondary: web-exclusive deal is the default, running five years, web only, so Steam and app stores stay open. Ad revenue reportedly splits 50/50 on Poki-sourced traffic, and developers keep a larger share of traffic they bring. Non-exclusive deals are reportedly flat-fee. [Cinevva guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-poki); [Playgama comparison](https://playgama.com/blog/business-faqs/poki-vs-crazygames-vs-gamedistribution-revenue-share/). Playgama is a competitor.
- CrazyGames process: QA review against technical and quality requirements. Basic Launch tests the game with a limited audience for 7 to 21 days with monetization off; it ends after 7 days and 500 plays. Promotion depends on average playtime, retention, conversion to gameplay, and CTR (CTR applies from November 23, 2026). Full Launch turns on ads and in-game purchases and starts revenue share. [CrazyGames Docs](https://docs.crazygames.com/)
- CrazyGames terms, secondary: no exclusivity required, extra pay offered for launch exclusivity; monthly payout above a €100 minimum; one 2026 game jam's terms gave developers 60% of ad revenue and 70% of in-game purchase revenue. [Cinevva guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/publish-game-crazygames); [Playgama](https://playgama.com/blog/business-faqs/should-i-go-exclusive-with-one-web/)

### Inferences
- CrazyGames is the low-friction test bed: free, no exclusivity, and its Basic Launch metrics give retention data before any store spend. Poki gives more reach in exchange for web exclusivity.

### Gaps
- Neither portal publishes its standard split in the pages fetched. Poki's contract terms were found only in secondary guides.

## Engine porting and console middleware

### Takeaway
Unity has built-in console export once a studio holds platform approval. Godot has no official console export, so studios use W4 Games or porting houses such as Lone Wolf Technology. Port quotes from vendor pages run about $8K to $60K.

### Cited Findings
- The Godot project does not ship console exports, citing per-platform cost and NDA licensing. [Godot: About official console ports](https://godotengine.org/article/about-official-console-ports/); [Godot and consoles](https://godotengine.org/article/godot-consoles-all-you-need-know/)
- W4 Games, founded by Godot core developers, sells console support with a Starter tier for studios under $300K annual revenue. [W4 Consoles](https://www.w4games.com/w4consoles); [W4 pricing announcement, 2023](https://w4games.com/2023/12/11/w4-games-announces-pricing-model-for-console-ports/)
- Lone Wolf Technology is listed in Godot docs for Switch and PS4 porting and publishing. [Godot docs 3.3](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/3.3/tutorials/platform/consoles.html)
- Defold supports Switch for approved developers. [Defold manual](https://www.defold.com/llms/manuals/nintendo-switch/)
- Cost ranges from vendor pages: $8K to $40K for Godot ports, $10K to $60K for Unity ports, with no stated method. [Coherent Labs](https://www.coherentlab.com/blog/game-porting-cost-guide); [NipsApp](https://nipsapp.com/game-porting-conversion-services/)
- Porting advice for indies: plan input, performance, and certification requirements early. [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/tips-best-practices-for-indie-devs-planning-a-console-port)

### Inferences
- A web-first game in a JS/HTML5 engine has no direct console path; reaching consoles means a rebuild in Unity or Godot or a contracted port.

### Gaps
- No current quotes from Virtuos, Lone Wolf, Sickhead or other porting houses. No public pricing for W4 tiers above Starter in this pass.
