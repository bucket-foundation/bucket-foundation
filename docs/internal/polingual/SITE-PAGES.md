## Privacy

Polingual Privacy Policy
Effective {launch_date}

Polingual is published by AGFarms LLC and runs on the same account system as Bucket at bucket.foundation.

What we collect

- Email address, when you sign in. We use it to send your one-time sign-in code and, if you agree, emails about your trial and subscription.
- Learning progress, when you sign in: your language systems, placement results and review history. We store it so it syncs across your devices, the Bucket app and bucket.foundation.
- A user id that ties your progress to your account.

Without sign-in, your progress stays on your device and we receive none of it.

What we do not collect

We do not track you across apps or websites. Polingual has no advertising or analytics SDK. We do not sell or share personal data.

Speech

When you tap Speak, your audio goes to Apple's speech recognition to check your pronunciation, under Apple's privacy policy. Polingual does not store or receive the audio.

Payments

Apple processes every purchase. We receive the subscription status and a transaction id from Apple, linked to your account when you are signed in. We never see your payment details.

Where data lives

Account and progress data is stored with our database host in the United States and sent over HTTPS.

Your choices

You can delete your account and all progress by emailing privacy@polingual.com from the address on the account. We delete it within 30 days. Unsubscribe from email with the link in any message.

Children

Polingual is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone who does not sign in. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without a parent's consent.

Contact: privacy@polingual.com

## Terms

Polingual Terms of Use
Effective {launch_date}

These terms cover your use of the Polingual app and polingual.com, published by AGFarms LLC. Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement also applies to the app.

Subscription

Polingual Monthly and Polingual Yearly are auto-renewable subscriptions sold through the App Store. New subscribers get a 3-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews at the same price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings, Apple Account, Subscriptions. Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your account

## Support

Polingual Support

Email support@polingual.com. We answer within two business days.

Common questions

- Start or cancel the free trial: Settings on your iPhone, your name, Subscriptions, Polingual.
- Restore a purchase on a new device: open Polingual, tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen.
- Sync with Bucket: sign in with the same email in both apps.
- Speaking does not work: allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for Polingual in Settings, Privacy and Security.
- Refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.
- Delete your account: email privacy@polingual.com from the address on the account.
