## One Line

Polingual teaches one word across up to six languages at once, with a live team quiz that runs on a classroom projector while students answer from their phones.

## Short Pitch

Most people who learn a language already speak two. Polingual is built for them: bilingual and heritage-language students learning a third language, and the teachers who run mixed classrooms. A Spanish speaker sees a new French, Italian or Portuguese word next to the Spanish word they know, with native audio, each language's own script and the roots that link them. Spaced review keeps every word alive, and a daily set of multilingual games keeps students coming back.

The live team quiz is the classroom wedge. A teacher puts the lobby on a projector, students join from their phones in whichever languages they study, and the whole class plays one round together. Kahoot, Blooket and Gimkit run live quizzes with no language content; Duolingo has language content and no shared-screen team play.

## Model

Freemium subscription at $9.99 a month or $59.99 a year with a free trial. Daily games stay free. In live lobbies the host subscribes and guests play free. Teacher Pro near $60 a year and venue language nights are the next two revenue lines. Creators earn 25% of first-year revenue from subscribers who join with their code.

## Why Now

Duolingo reports 32 million learners studying more than one language. About 105 million Europeans speak two or more foreign languages. English learners are about 10% of US K-12 students, and the Seal of Biliteracy now exists in every state. No product tracks what a learner knows across all their languages. Polingual is building that lexicon map next.
