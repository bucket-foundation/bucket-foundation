# Bucket OS

Founder question, bkt-ul9zv, 2026-10-25: should Bucket become the main product, with Research OS as one part of it?

## Current State

- Research OS is the product in the repo: canon, research workspace, hypothesis engine, Learn.
- Learn holds two surfaces. Science decks: 487 atoms. Languages: 326 items in each of 29 languages, now going to 1,000 words plus 300 phrases.
- Polingual ships as its own app for languages. The Bucket app is the beta people get.
- Languages produce the most applied learning: users can say, read and order things after a week. Science decks ask recall and self-rating.

## Proposal

Bucket OS is the shell. It holds four modules that share one account, one memory model and one credential record:

1. Learn: FSRS decks for every subject. Polingual is Learn scoped to languages, sold as its own app.
2. Read: assigned reading with notes and progress (bkt-75rks).
3. Research: the canon and the hypothesis engine, where Research OS lives today.
4. Prove: signed credentials for what a learner has mastered (bkt-any9z).

## Order

1. Ship Polingual 1.0 and get retention numbers from beta tracking (bkt-5vsj4).
2. Bring science up to the language standard: applied tasks and several question types (bkt-5ayq0, bkt-xjsyw).
3. Add Read, with the founder as the first learner.
4. Rename the Research OS shell to Bucket and move Research under it. URLs keep working through redirects.
