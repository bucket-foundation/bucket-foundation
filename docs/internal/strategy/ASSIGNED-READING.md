# Assigned Reading

Bead bkt-75rks. The founder is the first learner.

## Feature

- Save a paper by URL or DOI into a reading list.
- Assign it to a learner with a due date.
- Track progress by page or section, plus notes that link to Learn cards.
- Finishing a paper can create cards from its key claims, so the paper enters spaced review.
- Lives under Read in Bucket OS (BUCKET-OS.md). It reuses the account and the FSRS store.
