## Why he matters

Andrew A. Marino, PhD, JD — collaborator with Robert O. Becker on the
Syracuse VA bioelectricity research (1960s–1980s). Co-authored ~40 papers
with Becker on bone regeneration, EMF biology, electric currents in tissue.
Continued the work post-Becker as professor at LSU Health Shreveport.

## Key works

- *Going Somewhere: Truth About a Life in Science* (memoir)
- ~150 primary research papers (1960s–2020s)
- *Electromagnetism & Life* with Becker (1982)
