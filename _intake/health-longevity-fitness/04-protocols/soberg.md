## The recipe — the "Søberg Principle"
- **Cold:** ~**11 minutes/week TOTAL** of deliberate cold exposure (cold water ~ the temperature you "want to
  get out of," roughly 10–15 °C), **split across ~2–4 sessions** of a few minutes each.
- **"End on cold":** finish a hot↔cold session **on the cold**, not the warm, so the body re-warms itself
  (drives endogenous thermogenesis rather than passive external warming).
- **Heat (companion):** ~**57 minutes/week** of sauna/heat (e.g. ~3×/week), per the Finnish-sauna-derived band.
- Goal she states: stimulate **brown-fat thermogenesis / metabolic health**, not endurance of suffering.

## Where the efficacy is graded
- **`soberg-winter-swimmer-thermogenesis`** (NEW, J) — her own Cell Rep Med 2021 study: habitual
  winter-swimmers (cold+sauna ≥2 yr) showed **altered BAT thermoregulation and markedly higher cold-induced
  thermogenesis** vs controls. **Honest tier:** small (n≈8 vs 8), **cross-sectional / observational**,
  trained men — generates the mechanism, does not prove the 11-min dose causes metabolic-health *outcomes*.
- Cross-ref **H-domain** (NOT re-graded): `cold-norepinephrine-thermogenesis-mechanism`,
  `cold-activated-bat-adult-humans`, `shivering-vs-nonshivering-thermogenesis`,
  `cold-acclimation-insulin-sensitivity-t2d` (note: the insulin-sensitivity *outcome* came from a
  **6 h/day × 10-day** research protocol, **not** 11 min/week — keep the dose↔evidence link honest).
- **`soberg-11min-end-on-cold`** (NEW, J) — the "11 min/week + end on cold" prescription itself is a
  **practitioner synthesis** (a usable minimum threshold she distilled), **not a trial-derived dose with an
  outcome endpoint.** Reasonable, low-cost; mechanism-grade, not outcome-grade.

## ⚠️ SAFETY
- **Cold-water immersion: cardiac/vascular caution** — cold-shock gasp + BP/HR surge; risky with cardiac
  disease, arrhythmia, uncontrolled hypertension, pregnancy. Never combine with breath-holding or do alone in
  open/cold water (drowning/hypothermia/afterdrop). Enter gradually; supervise; warm up actively afterward.
- **Heat/sauna contraindications** — pregnancy, unstable cardiovascular disease, hypotension, intoxication,
  dehydration; hydrate; exit if lightheaded.

## Honest framing notes
- Søberg's contribution is **real primary BAT/cold metabolism data + a practical minimum-dose heuristic.** The
  heuristic's appeal is precisely its modesty (~11 min/week) — but "minimum dose to move a *mechanism*" is not
  "dose proven to extend healthspan." Don't upgrade it.
- **COI:** the Soeberg Institute / book / app.
