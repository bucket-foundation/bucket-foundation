## The recipe — the "Built to Move" daily vital signs / practices

Starrett frames movement health as a set of testable **"vital signs"** + daily practices, deliberately
low-tech and self-administered:

### 1. Sit-to-rise / getting-off-the-floor
- **Test:** the **sitting-rising test (SRT)** — lower to a cross-legged sit and stand back up using as few
  points of support (hands/knees) as possible; scored 0–10. Practice the descent/ascent daily.
- **Why it's load-bearing:** the SRT score is the one piece here with a hard outcome association (Brito 2014,
  all-cause mortality). → graded `sit-to-rise-mortality` (cohort). **Note the honest gap:** the SRT *predicts*
  mortality (it indexes strength + balance + body composition); **practicing the test is not proven to change
  mortality.** Don't conflate the biomarker with the intervention.

### 2. Sit on the floor ~30 min/day
- Accumulate floor-sitting (various positions) to preserve hip/ankle range and reduce chair-bound stiffness.

### 3. Daily walking volume
- A step-count "vital sign" (~8,000–10,000+/day as a target band). Cross-ref **E-domain**
  `physical-activity-dose-response-mortality`.

### 4. Reduce sedentary/sitting time — "Deskbound" / standing
- Break up prolonged sitting; standing desk + movement snacks. → graded `sedentary-time-mortality-mixed`
  (the association is real but **largely attenuated by total activity** — sitting is a proxy, not a clean
  independent killer; record the mixedness).

### 5. Mobility / "mobilizations"
- Controlled range-of-motion work, tissue work (rolling, banded distraction), breathing, position drills;
  range-of-motion tests for hips, shoulders, ankles, spine, couch-stretch, etc.
- **Honest tier:** the mobility-restores-performance / prevents-injury claims are mostly **mechanistic /
  clinical-impression / anecdotal** — not folded into a graded outcome claim this wave (flagged, not endorsed).

### 6. Breathing + balance vital signs
- A breath-hold/breathing "vital sign" and a single-leg balance test as cheap self-checks. Breath → cross-ref
  G-domain `slow-breathing-autonomic-hrv`.

## Honest framing notes
- The **only hard-outcome claim** in this system is the SRT↔mortality *association* (Brito 2014). Everything
  else is reasonable, low-risk movement hygiene at **mechanistic/anecdotal** tier — useful, not proven to
  extend life.
- Repeated caution: a predictive **biomarker** (SRT, grip, gait speed) being associated with mortality does
  **not** mean training that biomarker changes mortality — a recurring trap in this domain.
- **COI:** coaching/education/product business (The Ready State).
