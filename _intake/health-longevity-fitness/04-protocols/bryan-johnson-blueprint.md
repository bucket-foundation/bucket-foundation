## The recipe (as published)

**Frame.** A fully measured, algorithm-driven N=1: every intervention is supposed to be justified by a
biomarker and re-evaluated against dense self-measurement ("Don't Die"). The deliverable is the **open,
instrumented dataset + protocol**, not a proven outcome.

### Diet
- ~1,977 kcal/day, ~10% caloric restriction vs maintenance; nutrient-dense, largely plant-based.
- Fixed meals (historically "Super Veggie," "Nutty Pudding," a green-giant blend); last food ~11am
  (early time-restricted eating — eats within an ~6-hour morning window).
- High polyphenol / high fiber (~50+ g/day); olive oil (~30 ml/day); minimal added sugar, no fried food.

### Supplement stack (large, revised over time; representative core)
- Daily: collagen peptides, creatine, cocoa flavanols, EPA/DHA (omega-3), vitamin D3, vitamin K2,
  zinc, magnesium (glycinate/threonate), CoQ10/ubiquinol, NAC, taurine, glucosamine sulfate,
  ashwagandha, garlic, ginger, curcumin, lutein/zeaxanthin, lithium (microdose), B-complex/folate.
- Geroscience-adjacent: metformin (intermittent), rapamycin (was used weekly, **discontinued ~2024**
  after Johnson reported no net benefit + side effects — a notable self-reported negative), acarbose,
  NMN/NR (NAD+ precursor), spermidine, fisetin, Ca-AKG, glucosamine.
- (Stack changes frequently; always check the live published version for current items/doses.)

### Exercise
- ~1 hour/day, 7 days/week; mix of resistance, cardio (zone 2 + some high intensity), and ~25
  specific exercises; emphasis on muscle preservation, VO2max, grip, balance, flexibility.

### Sleep (the keystone he emphasizes most)
- Fixed schedule, ~8–8.5 h; same bedtime nightly; cool dark room; consistent wind-down; wearable
  sleep tracking; "perfect sleep" treated as the highest-leverage intervention.

### Light / circadian / skin
- Morning bright-light exposure; evening light hygiene (blue-light minimization); red-light therapy;
  aggressive photoprotection (sunscreen, hat) for skin-aging.

### Other / advanced (experimental, several discontinued)
- Therapeutic plasma exchange and a one-off father→son→grandson plasma-swap experiment (**stopped** —
  reported no benefit). Follistatin gene therapy (self-reported, experimental). Total-body MRI, dense
  blood panels, DEXA, epigenetic-age tests, organ-specific aging clocks, skin/hearing/penile/etc.
  age measurements across ~70+ organs.

### Measurement layer (the actual product)
- Dense, frequent biomarker panels; epigenetic age; DEXA; VO2max; sleep architecture; "speed of aging"
  (DunedinPACE-style pace metric, which he publicizes as <1.0 = aging slower than 1 yr/yr).

## Honest framing notes
- **Tier of the whole thing = `nequals1`** (single subject, no randomization, no control). No causal
  inference is possible about any single component.
- **Self-reported reversals matter as data:** Johnson **discontinued rapamycin and plasma exchange**
  after reporting no benefit / harm — these negatives are part of the open record and are *more*
  informative than the positive marketing.
- **COI:** Blueprint sells supplements/foods ("Blueprint Stack," olive oil, etc.) — commercial interest
  in the protocol's perceived efficacy.
- The contribution is **measurement transparency**, not validated life extension.
