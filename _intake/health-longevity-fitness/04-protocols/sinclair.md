## The recipe (as he has publicly described; changes over time — verify live)
### Supplements (his publicized personal stack)
- **NMN** (nicotinamide mononucleotide, ~1 g AM) — NAD+ precursor.
- **Resveratrol** (~1 g, with fat/yogurt) — claimed STAC/SIRT1 activator.
- **Metformin** (evening; he has discussed timing it away from exercise) — off-label.
- **Vitamin D3, vitamin K2, omega-3 (TMG/betaine** as a methyl-donor alongside NMN**), spermidine, quercetin,
  fisetin** (intermittent), a statin (personal/familial), low-dose aspirin (has varied), coenzyme Q10.

### Lifestyle
- **One meal skipped / time-restricted eating** (intermittent fasting); largely plant-forward, low sugar,
  reduced red meat (consistent with his mTOR/IGF-1-restraint view).
- Regular **exercise** (incl. high-intensity), **cold/heat hormesis**, keep **BMI/blood glucose** low.

## Where the efficacy is graded
- **`sinclair-resveratrol-sirt1-contested`** (NEW, J) — resveratrol as a *direct* SIRT1 activator is
  **disputed**: Pfizer/Amgen reported the in-vitro activation was a **fluorophore-substrate assay artifact**;
  GSK (which bought Sirtris for ~$720M in 2008) **discontinued SRT501** (2010, GI side effects + non-specific
  activity). Sinclair contests the artifact reading. **Tier: in-vitro/contested; human anti-aging outcome:
  none.** Direction: refutes (the simple "resveratrol activates SIRT1 → longevity in humans" claim).
- **NMN / NAD+** → cross-ref **B-domain** `nad-precursor-nr-human-surrogate` (precursors **raise blood NAD+ —
  a surrogate** — but human clinical-outcome benefit is unproven) and `sirtuins-nad-decline`. Not re-graded.
- **Metformin (healthy non-diabetics)** → cross-ref **B-domain** `metformin-mortality-cohort` and
  `tame-trial-design` (the outcome question is **still unresolved**; TAME unfunded/ongoing; exercise-adaptation
  interference is a real counter-concern — see Attia's softened stance).
- **TRE / IF mechanism** → cross-ref **D-domain** `if-metabolic-switching-mechanism`,
  `protein-igf1-age-dependent-mortality`.
- **`sinclair-personal-regimen-self-experimental`** (NEW, J) — the *whole stack as a longevity protocol* is
  **N=1, no control, no human outcome data, heavy COI**; its information value is near zero for causal
  inference. Direction: unfalsifiable as stated.
