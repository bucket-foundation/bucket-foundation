## The recipe (functional-breathing program)

### 1. Nasal breathing as default (day and night)
- Breathe **through the nose** at rest, during sleep (some use mouth-taping), and during easy exercise.
- **Rationale:** nasal nitric-oxide (sinus NO → vasodilation/airway), filtering/humidifying, better CO2 economy.
- Cross-ref **G-domain** `nasal-breathing-nitric-oxide`. **Honest note:** the NO mechanism is real; the
  performance/sleep **outcome** evidence is thin/anecdotal; **mouth-taping has safety caveats** (don't tape with
  nasal obstruction, reflux risk, intoxication, or in children without clinical guidance).

### 2. The BOLT score (Body Oxygen Level Test) — his signature metric
- **Measure:** after a normal nasal exhale, pinch the nose and time until the **first definite urge** to breathe
  (NOT a max breath-hold). Benchmarks he uses: **<25 s = dysfunctional, ~40 s = goal.**
- Used as a progress metric and to titrate the program.
- → graded `bolt-score-predictive-unvalidated`: it is a reproducible measure of **breath-hold tolerance / CO2
  sensitivity**, but its **predictive validity** for health/performance is **a proprietary heuristic, not
  independently validated**; treat the 25/40 s cutoffs as program targets, not clinical thresholds.

### 3. Light / reduced breathing & breath-hold drills (CO2 tolerance)
- Gentle **air-hunger** drills (reduce breathing volume; walking breath-holds; "breathe light to breathe right")
  to raise CO2 tolerance. Cross-ref **G-domain** `bohr-effect-co2-tolerance` (Bohr-effect rationale; the
  CO2-tolerance→performance claim is **contested**) and `buteyko-asthma-symptoms-rct` (Buteyko improved asthma
  *symptoms/medication use*, NOT lung function).

### 4. Slow / cadence breathing
- Slow nasal breathing toward the resonance range. Cross-ref **G-domain** `slow-breathing-autonomic-hrv`,
  `exhalation-vagal-mechanism`. Not re-graded.

### 5. Simulated altitude (advanced)
- Breath-holds to induce mild intermittent hypoxia ("high-altitude simulation"). Mechanistic rationale; hard
  outcome evidence thin.

## Honest framing notes
- The system is **functional-breathing retraining**; its best-supported piece is the shared slow-breathing
  autonomic mechanism (G). The most-marketed pieces (BOLT cutoffs, CO2-tolerance→athletic-performance) are
  **heuristic/contested**, not outcome-proven.
- A Buteyko-lineage caution: chronic **hyperventilation/over-breathing as the cause of disease** is a strong
  framing that the controlled evidence supports only narrowly (asthma symptoms), not broadly.
- **COI:** Oxygen Advantage is a paid book/certification/coaching brand.
