## The framework

**Medicine 3.0.** Reframe medicine from reactive disease-treatment ("Medicine 2.0") to proactive,
personalized, prevention-first risk reduction against the "Four Horsemen" of early death:
1. Atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD)
2. Cancer
3. Neurodegenerative disease (Alzheimer's etc.)
4. Metabolic dysfunction (type 2 diabetes / "foundation" disease that worsens the other three)

**Healthspan, not just lifespan.** Three dimensions of healthspan to defend: cognitive, physical, and
emotional. Emotional health is explicitly named as co-equal (Attia's late addition after his own
mental-health work).

**The Centenarian Decathlon.** Backcast from the specific physical tasks you want to do in your last
decade (e.g. lift a 30 lb suitcase overhead, get off the floor unassisted, hike, carry grandchildren,
rise from a chair) → reverse-engineer the strength, VO2max, stability, and mobility "reserve" you need
to bank **now**, because all of it declines with age. Train today for the 85-year-old's physiology.

## The recipe (five levers)

### 1. Exercise (Attia's highest-weighted lever)
- **VO2max** — treats cardiorespiratory fitness as the single strongest modifiable longevity predictor;
  Zone 2 (long, conversational, mitochondrial/fat-oxidation base) **plus** VO2max intervals (e.g.
  4×4 min near-max).
- **Strength** — heavy resistance training; specific markers: grip strength, leg/hip strength,
  ability to carry load.
- **Stability** — foundational; injury-prevention and force-control (DNS, breathing, foot/spine work).
- Rough weekly shape: ~3–4 h zone 2, ~1 VO2max session, 3+ strength sessions, daily stability.

### 2. Nutrition ("nutritional biochemistry")
- No single dogmatic diet; tools: caloric restriction, dietary restriction (cut quality/junk), or
  time restriction — chosen by individual metabolic data (CGM, ApoB, fasting insulin, lipids).
- **High protein** (~1.6–2.2 g/kg/day) to defend muscle — explicitly the **counterweight** to the
  Longo protein-restriction longevity position (see protein/mTOR conflict in `06-evidence/CONFLICTS.md`).
- Manage ApoB aggressively for ASCVD prevention.

### 3. Sleep
- 7.5–8.5 h; sleep hygiene, consistency, limit alcohol (which he flags as a real sleep/longevity cost).

### 4. Emotional health
- Named as a pillar; therapy, relationships, distress tolerance — not a footnote.

### 5. Pharmacology / supplements (individualized, biomarker-driven)
- Aggressive lipid management (statins/ezetimibe/PCSK9 to drive ApoB low for ASCVD).
- Rapamycin (Attia has discussed personal off-label use, cautiously dosed) — flagged experimental.
- Metformin (he has publicly **softened** on metformin for healthy non-diabetics, partly over
  exercise-adaptation interference — tracks the metformin conflict in `06-evidence/CONFLICTS.md`).
- Common supplements: omega-3 (EPA/DHA), vitamin D, magnesium, others per labs. No NAD+ hype stance.

## Honest framing notes
- The **framework** (Medicine 3.0, Centenarian Decathlon, four horsemen, healthspan dimensions) is a
  prioritization heuristic — not itself a tested intervention; tier = organizational/`anecdotal` as a
  whole, while its components inherit their own tiers.
- The **strongest-evidence component is VO2max/cardiorespiratory fitness → mortality** (large cohort
  data, e.g. Mandsager 2018 JAMA Netw Open) — graded in `J-claims.json`.
- Attia's protein-for-muscle stance and Longo's protein-restriction stance are a **live open conflict**,
  not a settled answer — recorded as such, not resolved here.
- **COI:** podcast/book/subscription business and a longevity clinical practice.
