## The recipe

### 1. Strength / conditioning programming (the "9 adaptations")
- Frames fitness as **9 trainable adaptations** (skill, speed, power, strength, hypertrophy, muscular
  endurance, anaerobic capacity, VO2max, long-duration steady state). Program to your goal, don't chase all at
  once; manage the **concurrent-training interference** effect.
- Cross-ref **E-domain** claims `resistance-training-mortality-meta`, `crf-vo2max-strongest-mortality-predictor`,
  `concurrent-training-interference`, `hiit-crf-cardiometabolic-meta`. Not re-graded here.
- Protein for hypertrophy: ~**0.7 g/lb/day** (≈1.6 g/kg), leucine-threshold per-meal dosing — cross-ref
  D-domain `protein-augments-resistance-training-gains`, `elderly-higher-protein-prevents-sarcopenia`, and the
  discovered concept *Leucine threshold / protein-per-meal dosing*.

### 2. Hydration (the "Galpin Equation" — his signature heuristic)
- **Daily baseline:** ~**0.5 oz of fluid per lb body weight per day** (≈30 mL/kg) — e.g. ~100 oz for a 200 lb
  person; front-load earlier in the day to protect sleep.
- **During hard/long exercise:** **bodyweight(lb) ÷ 30 = oz to drink every ~15 min.**
- Add electrolytes (esp. sodium) when sweat losses are high; gauge by sweat rate (weigh-in/weigh-out) and urine
  color. → graded as a **heuristic**: `galpin-hydration-heuristic` (mechanistic/anecdotal, NOT outcome-proven).

### 3. Sleep (recovery keystone)
- Consistency + duration first; he popularizes the **"QQRT" frame — Quantity, Quality, Regularity, Timing.**
- Cross-ref **I-domain** `sleep-duration-mortality-ushape`, `aasm-7h-consensus`, `hrv-autonomic-recovery-biomarker`.
  Not re-graded here.

### 4. Recovery & readiness monitoring
- **HRV** + resting HR + sleep + subjective readiness to autoregulate training load. Cross-ref I-domain
  `hrv-autonomic-recovery-biomarker`. Breathwork (nasal, slow) for down-regulation → G-domain
  `slow-breathing-autonomic-hrv`.

### 5. Testing-first philosophy
- "Test, don't guess": VO2max, DEXA, blood panels, strength/power tests to individualize. Cross-ref **L-domain**
  (biomarkers).
