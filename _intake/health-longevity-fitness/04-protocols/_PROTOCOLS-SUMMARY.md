> **Status:** v0.2 — 2026-06-27 (Wave 4 expansion: +9 practitioners). Companion to `INDEX.md` (the table) and
> `02-domains/J-claims.json` (the graded claims). This file is the prose synthesis of *what the protocols are
> and how seriously to take each one.*

## The point of Domain J
The N=1 / practitioner layer is where the field is **most popular and least rigorous**. The whole discipline
of this domain is **separating the recipe from the evidence**: a published protocol (Blueprint, Medicine 3.0,
Oxygen Advantage, the Longevity Diet) carries **zero evidentiary weight by virtue of existing or being
popular** (`protocol-not-evidence-axiom`). Each component is pulled out and graded against its **own primary
source**, never the practitioner's authority.

## How the practitioners stratify (by evidence posture, not popularity)

**Most trial-anchored.** **Longo** (FMD RCTs, large IGF-1 epi, mouse lifespan) and **McGill** (own
spine-biomechanics literature + small clinical RCTs) are the two whose signature recipes rest on real primary
data — though even Longo's is mostly **surrogate-marker / animal**, not human-longevity outcome.

**Solid framework, evidence borrowed.** **Attia** and **Galpin** translate strong exercise/metabolic science
(VO2max↔mortality, strength↔mortality, protein↔muscle). Their frameworks (Medicine 3.0, 9 adaptations) are
**organizational heuristics**; the strength is the inherited cohort/RCT evidence underneath.

**Communicators — grade the source, not the voice.** **Huberman**, **Patrick**, **McKeown** package others'
primary science. Their best items are the shared mechanisms (circadian light, slow-breathing autonomics);
their *most-branded* items are the **thinnest** — Huberman's caffeine-delay & NSDR branding, McKeown's BOLT
cutoffs — reasonable and low-risk, but heuristic/anecdotal, not outcome-proven.

**Real primary data + a modest heuristic.** **Søberg** authored genuine BAT/cold-thermogenesis data
(Cell Rep Med 2021) and distilled the **~11 min/week, end-on-cold** rule — but that dose is a *mechanism-grade
minimum*, and the only cold protocol with an actual metabolic **outcome** (insulin sensitivity) used
**6 h/day × 10 days**, not 11 min/week. The dose↔evidence gap is the honest story.

**Movement hygiene, mostly mechanistic.** **Starrett's** system is low-risk and sensible; its single
hard-outcome anchor is the **sit-to-rise↔mortality** *association* (Brito 2014) — a composite **biomarker**,
not proof that practicing the test extends life. Sedentary-time risk is **mixed** (largely attenuated by total
activity).

**Most contested / highest COI.** **Sinclair** is the cautionary tale: the resveratrol→SIRT1 mechanism is a
**disputed assay artifact** (Pfizer/Amgen; SRT501 discontinued), NMN raises only a **surrogate** (NAD+), and
the personal stack is **N=1 advocacy with dense financial COI**. The canonical "mechanism sold as outcome"
case in the whole corpus.

## The recurring traps this domain exists to catch
1. **Protocol ≠ evidence** — popularity is not data (`protocol-not-evidence-axiom`).
2. **Mechanism ≠ outcome** — "NMN raises NAD+", "sulforaphane activates Nrf2", "cold spikes norepinephrine",
   "high BOLT = healthy" are all *mechanisms* routinely upsold as *outcomes*.
3. **Biomarker ≠ intervention** — VO2max, grip, sit-to-rise, BOLT *predict* mortality; training them is not
   proven to *change* it.
4. **Dose↔evidence honesty** — the dose with the outcome (6 h/day cold) is not the dose being sold
   (11 min/week / 3-min plunge).
5. **Name = provenance, not evidence** — communicators inherit their sources' tiers; a PhD/MD on the byline
   doesn't upgrade a claim.
6. **Self-reported negatives are gold** — Johnson discontinuing rapamycin/plasma-exchange is *more* informative
   than the marketing positives.

## Open conflict touched here
- **protein ↔ mTOR / IGF-1** (`conflict-protein-mtor`, status **OPEN**): Longo (mid-life protein restriction)
  vs Attia/Galpin/Phillips (protein-for-muscle). Almost certainly **age- and context-dependent**; recorded as
  a first-class disagreement, not resolved.

## Safety (normative, not efficacy)
Wim Hof breathing **never in/near water** (drowning); cold immersion cardiac caution + never with breath-holds;
sauna/heat contraindications; mouth-taping caveats; FMD not for frail/diabetic/pregnant/ED. Full notes in each
file and consolidated in `INDEX.md`.
