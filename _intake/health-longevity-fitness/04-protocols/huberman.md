## The recipe (the canonical "optimal morning/day" stack)

### 1. Morning sunlight (his single most-emphasized item)
- Get **outside light in the eyes for ~10–30 min within ~30–60 min of waking** (longer if overcast; never
  stare at the sun; don't use sunglasses for this; through a window/windshield is much weaker).
- **Rationale:** trigger the morning cortisol pulse and set/advance the circadian clock via melanopsin ipRGC →
  SCN (the *mechanism* is well established; see I-domain). Pairs with **evening dim/red light** and
  **avoiding bright light ~10pm–4am**.
- → graded: `huberman-morning-light-circadian` (+ cross-ref I-claims `light-melatonin-action-spectrum`,
  `room-light-melatonin-suppression`).

### 2. Delay caffeine ~90–120 min after waking
- Wait **90–120 min** before the first caffeine; rationale is adenosine clearance (avoid the "afternoon crash"
  from masking residual adenosine). Also caps caffeine ~8–10 h before bed.
- **Honest tier:** the adenosine *mechanism* is real; the *benefit of delaying* is a heuristic with essentially
  no RCT. → graded `huberman-caffeine-delay` (mechanistic/anecdotal).

### 3. NSDR / Yoga Nidra (his coined umbrella term "Non-Sleep Deep Rest")
- **10–20 min** guided body-scan/long-exhale relaxation, used to recover from poor sleep, "learn to fall back
  asleep," and restore focus/dopamine. → graded `huberman-nsdr-yoga-nidra` (small/early evidence).

### 4. Physiological sigh / breathwork for acute state control
- **Double-inhale + long exhale** to down-shift arousal in real time. This is already a graded **G-domain**
  protocol → cross-ref claims `cyclic-sighing-mood-arousal-rct`, `exhalation-vagal-mechanism`
  (see `04-protocols/G-breath-protocols.md`, `proto-physio-sigh`). **Not re-graded here.**

### 5. Exercise / movement
- Endorses zone 2 + strength + the broad longevity-exercise consensus. Cross-ref **E-domain** claims
  (`crf-vo2max-strongest-mortality-predictor`, `resistance-training-mortality-meta`). Not re-graded here.

### 6. Deliberate cold / heat (hormesis)
- Cold for an alertness/NE surge; sauna for relaxation/cardio. Cross-ref **H-domain**
  (`cold-norepinephrine-thermogenesis-mechanism`, `sauna-frequency-mortality-kihd`). Not re-graded here.

### 7. Supplements (representative, frequently revised)
- Sleep stack he has publicized: **magnesium threonate, theanine, apigenin** (± glycine, GABA, inositol);
  also creatine, omega-3, vitamin D, and (disclosed) testosterone-support items. Doses change — verify live.
