## ⚠️ SAFETY (read first — this is a hazard, not an efficacy claim)
- **NEVER do the breathing in, on, or near water.** Cyclic hyperventilation + breath-holds can cause a
  **hypocapnic shallow-water blackout → drowning.** Deaths and near-drownings have occurred. Do breathwork
  **lying or sitting on dry land only.**
- **Never while driving / standing / in a pool / bath / open water.** Fainting is expected and designed-around
  on land; it is lethal in water.
- **Cold immersion: cardiac/vascular caution.** Cold-shock causes a gasp reflex + BP/HR spike — risky with
  cardiac disease, arrhythmia, uncontrolled hypertension, or pregnancy. Cold + breath-hold compounds blackout
  risk. Start gradual (cold showers), never alone in open/cold water.
- Epilepsy, prior fainting, serious cardiovascular/respiratory disease → medical clearance first.

## The recipe (the three pillars)

### 1. Breathing
- **3–4 rounds**, each: **30–40 deep, full cyclic breaths** (in fully, let out passively, no force) →
  after the last exhale, **hold the breath out** (empty-lung retention) as long as comfortable →
  **recovery breath** (inhale, hold ~15 s) → repeat. (This is `proto-whm` in `G-breath-protocols.md`.)

### 2. Cold exposure
- Progressive: cold showers → ice baths / cold-water immersion; build tolerance gradually.

### 3. Commitment / mindset
- Focus/meditation framing tying the two together; daily practice.

## Where the efficacy is graded (cross-references, NOT re-graded here)
- **`wim-hof-voluntary-sns-immune-attenuation`** (G) — trained WHM practitioners voluntarily activated the
  sympathetic axis and **attenuated the inflammatory response to IV endotoxin** (Kox et al., PNAS 2014). Real,
  but the effect is **bundled** (breath + cold + meditation) — can't isolate the cold or the breath alone.
- **`wim-hof-lactate-mediated-antiinflammatory`** (G) — proposed mechanism (epinephrine/lactate → IL-10 ↑,
  pro-inflammatory cytokines ↓).
- **`wim-hof-disease-axspa-proof-of-concept`** (G) — small add-on trial signal in axial spondyloarthritis.
- **`wim-hof-systematic-review-caution`** (G) — systematic review: **small, low-quality** evidence base;
  promising but not established for clinical outcomes.
- Cold pillar mechanism → **H-domain** `cold-norepinephrine-thermogenesis-mechanism`,
  `cold-activated-bat-adult-humans`.

## Honest framing notes
- The famous endotoxemia result is **genuine and striking** but (a) bundled, (b) acute immune challenge, not a
  chronic-disease or longevity outcome, (c) trained subjects. Don't market it as proven disease therapy.
- The **biggest real-world risk is the water-drowning hazard**, which is independent of whether the method
  "works." Safety dominates.
- **COI:** WHM is a paid course/app/brand.
