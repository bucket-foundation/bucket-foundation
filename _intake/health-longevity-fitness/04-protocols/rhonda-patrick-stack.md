## The recipe (as publicly described)

### Core supplement stack
- **Omega-3 (EPA/DHA)** — high dose (~2–4 g/day); her most-emphasized supplement; targets omega-3
  index, inflammation, brain aging.
- **Vitamin D3** — dose to a target serum 25(OH)D (~40–60 ng/mL); a long-standing FoundMyFitness theme.
- **Magnesium** (glycinate / malate) and a broad-spectrum **multivitamin** as "micronutrient insurance."
- **Vitamin K2**, **vitamin C**, **B-complex** (incl. methylfolate), **zinc**.
- **Sulforaphane** — via broccoli sprouts or stabilized supplements (Nrf2 activation, detox/antioxidant
  enzyme induction); her signature compound.
- **Ubiquinol (CoQ10)**, **PQQ**, **alpha-lipoic acid**, **lutein/zeaxanthin**.
- **Fiber / inulin** and fermented foods for the microbiome.
- Sometimes-cited: **myo-inositol**, **glycine + NAC** (or GlyNAC for glutathione), **berberine**,
  **lithium (microdose)**, **collagen + vitamin C**, **creatine** (cognitive + muscle).

### Diet pattern
- Micronutrient-dense, largely whole-food / Mediterranean-leaning; lots of vegetables (the
  "smoothie" — kale/spinach/berries/avocado is a signature); fatty fish for omega-3; fermented foods.
- Time-restricted eating (~10 h window) on most days.

### Non-supplement protocols she emphasizes (hormesis)
- **Sauna** — heat stress, citing the Finnish Laukkanen cohort data (heat-shock proteins,
  cardiovascular/mortality association); cross-ref `01-people/cards/laukkanen.md` and the H-thermal domain.
- **Exercise** — vigorous aerobic + resistance; lactate/BDNF and brain benefits.
- **Cold exposure** — discussed, more cautiously than sauna.

## Honest framing notes
- The stack is **micronutrient-optimization + hormesis**, not a longevity intervention with outcome
  proof. Whole stack tier ≈ `nequals1`/`anecdotal`; individual items inherit their own tiers (e.g.
  omega-3 and vitamin D have RCT/meta literature with **mixed** outcome results — record the mixedness).
- Patrick's strength is **translating primary micronutrient literature**; her contribution is curation
  + communication, which is provenance, not evidence (per SCHEMA hard rule).
- **Sulforaphane** has solid mechanistic/`mechanistic`-tier Nrf2 biology but limited hard human outcome
  data — a clean example of the mechanism≠outcome gap.
- **COI:** FoundMyFitness membership/business; has had supplement-brand relationships.
