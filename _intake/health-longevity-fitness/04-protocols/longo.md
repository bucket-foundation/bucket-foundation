## The recipe

### 1. The "Longevity Diet" (everyday dietary pattern)
- **Pescetarian / mostly plant-based** (vegetables, legumes, whole grains, nuts, olive oil; fish ~2–3×/week;
  little/no red or processed meat).
- **Lower protein** in mid-life (~**0.31–0.36 g/lb/day**, ≈0.7–0.8 g/kg), **mostly plant protein** — to keep
  **IGF-1 / mTOR** restrained; he advises **raising protein after ~65–70** to protect against frailty
  (age-dependent, explicitly).
- **~12-hour eating window** daily (e.g. finish ~3–4 h before bed); avoid long-term very-low-carb/high-animal-
  protein keto.
- Higher carbohydrate from complex sources; meals timed to chronobiology.

### 2. Periodic Fasting-Mimicking Diet (FMD / ProLon)
- A **5-day, low-calorie, low-protein, low-sugar, plant-based** cycle (~day-1 ~1,100 kcal → ~day-2–5 ~700 kcal),
  repeated **monthly to a few times/year** depending on risk, then resume normal eating.
- Goal: trigger fasting-state programs (↓IGF-1, ↑autophagy, stem-cell/regenerative signaling) **without a
  full water fast.**

## Where the efficacy is graded (cross-references — NOT re-graded here)
- **`fmd-human-risk-markers`** (D) — 3 monthly FMD cycles improved cardiometabolic **risk markers** in a human
  RCT (surrogate endpoints, not hard outcomes).
- **`fmd-mouse-healthspan-lifespan`** (D) — periodic FMD improved healthspan/lifespan markers in **mice**.
- **`protein-igf1-age-dependent-mortality`** (D) — the **age-dependent** protein↔IGF-1↔mortality association
  (high protein worse at 50–65, *reversing* after 65) — the empirical spine of his protein advice.
- **`protein-carb-ratio-mouse-lifespan`**, **`laron-ghr-deficiency-cancer-diabetes-protection`** (D) — the
  low-protein/low-IGF-1 longevity rationale (mouse + Laron human-genetic).
- Mediterranean-pattern support → **`mediterranean-diet-predimed-cv-events`** (D).

### NEW composite claim
- **`longo-longevity-diet-pattern`** (J) — the Longevity Diet *as a packaged pattern* is a **synthesis of

  evidence-aligned; **not** proven to extend human lifespan.
