### Edward Jenner

| | |
|---|---|
| **id** | edward-jenner |
| **lifespan** | 1749-1823 |
| **era** | 18th-19th c. |
| **region / tradition** | Berkeley, Gloucestershire, England - natural philosophy / medicine |
| **domains** | Infectious disease, vaccinology |
| **evidence_posture** | `mainstream-rigorous` |
| **primary works** | *An Inquiry into the Causes and Effects of the Variolae Vaccinae* (1798) |

**Field contribution.** Demonstrated that inoculation with cowpox protects against smallpox, founding the practice of vaccination that would eventually eradicate smallpox - the deadliest pathogen in human history.

**Why indexed.** Vaccination is one of the highest-leverage interventions ever devised; Jenner's cowpox work is its origin and the conceptual root of all immunization.

**Built on.** folk knowledge of cowpox-dairymaid immunity; variolation practice

**Influenced / downstream.** Pasteur (attenuated vaccines), Donald Henderson (eradication), all vaccinology

**Tags.** `vaccination` `smallpox` `cowpox` `immunization`
