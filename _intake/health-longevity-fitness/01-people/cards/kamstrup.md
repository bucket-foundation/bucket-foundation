**Field contribution.** Provided Mendelian-randomization evidence from the Copenhagen general-population cohorts that lipoprotein(a) [Lp(a)], an LDL-like particle whose level is ~90% genetically determined by the LPA gene, is a causal - not merely correlated - risk factor for myocardial infarction.

**Why indexed.** The genetic proof that Lp(a) causes cardiovascular disease, converting a long-neglected biomarker into a causal target now pursued by drug developers.

**Built on.** nordestgaard

**Influenced / downstream.** Lp(a) as causal target, emerging Lp(a)-lowering therapeutics
