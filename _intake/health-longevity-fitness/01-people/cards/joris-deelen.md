**Field contribution.** Led the largest longevity meta-GWAS, confirming APOE and the 5q33.3/CDKN2B-AS locus as the reproducible human longevity signals, and built metabolomic mortality-risk scores. A consistent voice on the reproducibility limits of longevity GWAS.

**Why indexed.** Anchors what human genetics actually supports about lifespan: the empirical check on overstated longevity-gene claims.
