**Field contribution.** Identified longevity-associated gene variants (CETP, APOC3, growth-hormone/IGF-1 axis) in a cohort of centenarians, and designed the TAME (Targeting Aging with Metformin) trial — the first attempt to get a regulator (FDA) to accept aging itself as a trial endpoint.

**Why indexed.** Created the regulatory template for treating aging as an indication, and grounds it in human centenarian genetics rather than model organisms.

**Built on.** centenarian cohort studies

**Influenced / downstream.** TAME, geroscience regulatory strategy
