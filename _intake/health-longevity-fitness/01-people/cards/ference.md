**Field contribution.** Used Mendelian randomization - naturally randomized lifelong genetic exposure to lower LDL/apoB - to triangulate genetic, epidemiological, and randomized-trial evidence into the claim that LDL/apoB is causal for atherosclerosis and that risk depends on cumulative exposure (LDL x time), not just current level.

**Why indexed.** Lead author of the consensus that reframed LDL/apoB from 'risk marker' to 'causal cumulative exposure', the strongest formal synthesis of the lipid-causality argument.

**Built on.** sniderman, Mendelian randomization methodology

**Influenced / downstream.** lipid-lowering guidelines, early-intervention lipid argument

**Cross-branch.** links to causal-inference (Mendelian randomization) method shared with genetics branch
