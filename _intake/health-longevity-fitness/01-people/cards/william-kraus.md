**Field contribution.** Principal investigator of CALERIE, the only long-term randomized controlled trial of sustained caloric restriction in non-obese humans, which showed cardiometabolic improvements without harm, and led the STRRIDE exercise-dose-response trials.

**Why indexed.** CALERIE is the anchor of human caloric-restriction evidence; every CR-in-humans claim routes through it.

**Influenced / downstream.** Daniel Belsky (CALERIE re-analysis), CR field
