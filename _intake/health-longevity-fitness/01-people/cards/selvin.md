**Field contribution.** Showed in the ARIC cohort that glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c) - a time-integrated measure of glycemia - predicts cardiovascular disease and death even within the non-diabetic range, helping move HbA1c from a diabetes-monitoring tool to a continuous risk biomarker.

**Why indexed.** Established HbA1c as a graded mortality/CVD predictor across the glycemic spectrum, the empirical basis for using it as a longevity-relevant metric in non-diabetics.

**Built on.** ARIC cohort methodology

**Influenced / downstream.** HbA1c-as-risk-marker practice
