**Field contribution.** Founded TwinsUK (a major heritability/epigenetics cohort), then led the PREDICT studies showing large person-to-person variation in metabolic responses to identical foods, and championed gut-microbiome diversity and dietary-fiber/plant variety ('30 plants a week') as health levers, commercialized via ZOE.

**Why indexed.** A primary node linking the microbiome and personalized-nutrition response to metabolic health and aging.

**Built on.** twin-study methodology

**Influenced / downstream.** personalized-nutrition field, Segal (PREDICT)

**Disputed / known unknowns.** ZOE commercial COI; individualized-response claims are strong but their longevity payoff is unproven.
