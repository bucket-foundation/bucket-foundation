**Field contribution.** Showed physiologically that meditation (initially TM) elicits a reproducible **"relaxation response"** — reduced oxygen consumption, heart rate, respiratory rate and sympathetic arousal ("a wakeful hypometabolic state"). Then **de-religioned** it into a generic technique (a repeated word/phrase + passive attitude), arguing the physiology is tradition-independent. Founder of Harvard's Mind/Body Medical Institute.

**Why indexed.** Provides the *mechanism* layer beneath "meditation relaxes you" — a measurable autonomic/metabolic shift — and the bridge between TM (a commercialized lineage) and secular clinical practice. A mechanism finding, NOT an outcome: relaxation physiology ≠ disease or longevity benefit.

**Built on.** Transcendental Meditation (Maharishi); autonomic/cardiovascular physiology

**Influenced / downstream.** kabat-zinn (secular mindfulness); mind-body medicine; relaxation-training literature
