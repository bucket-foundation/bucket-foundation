**Field contribution.** A leading translational advocate for rapamycin (mTOR inhibition) as the best-evidenced pharmacological geroprotector, and co-founder of the Dog Aging Project, which runs a randomized rapamycin trial in companion dogs as a bridge between mouse ITP data and human aging.

**Why indexed.** Bridges the strongest animal longevity signal (rapamycin) toward translation, and is an outspoken critic of weakly-evidenced longevity hype.
