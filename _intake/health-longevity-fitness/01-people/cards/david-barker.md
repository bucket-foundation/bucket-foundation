**Field contribution.** Originated the fetal-origins (Barker) hypothesis - that the in-utero and early-life environment programs adult cardiometabolic disease decades later - founding the field of Developmental Origins of Health and Disease.

**Why indexed.** Moves the highest-leverage cardiometabolic intervention point a full generation upstream; a foundational reframing of where chronic-disease risk is set.

**Built on.** early-life-nutrition epidemiology
