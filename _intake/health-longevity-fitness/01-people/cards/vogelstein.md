### Bert Vogelstein

| | |
|---|---|

| **primary works** | *A genetic model for colorectal tumorigenesis (Fearon & Vogelstein, Cell)* (1990); *Cancer Genome Landscapes (Science)* (2013) |

**Field contribution.** Defined the multi-hit, clonal-evolution model of cancer at the molecular level — the colorectal adenoma-to-carcinoma sequence showing tumors accumulate driver mutations in an ordered progression — and later led the genome-era synthesis distinguishing "driver" from "passenger" mutations across cancer types.

**Why indexed.** The most-cited cancer geneticist; established cancer as a stepwise somatic-genomic disease, the empirical backbone connecting mutation accumulation, age, and tumorigenesis.

**Built on.** Knudson's two-hit hypothesis; oncogene/tumor-suppressor discovery

**Influenced / downstream.** liquid-biopsy / circulating-tumor-DNA diagnostics; driver-mutation cancer genomics; the somatic-mutation-and-aging literature

**Canon bridge.** disease-mechanism / somatic genomics

**Disputed / known unknowns.** The "bad luck" (stem-cell-division) cancer-risk paper drew controversy over how much cancer is attributable to intrinsic replication error vs modifiable exposures.
