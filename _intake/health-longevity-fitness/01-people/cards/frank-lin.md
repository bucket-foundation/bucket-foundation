**Field contribution.** Built the hearing-loss-to-dementia research program from observational (BLSA) evidence to the ACHIEVE randomized trial - the best recent causal evidence that treating a single modifiable factor (hearing loss) slows cognitive decline in higher-risk older adults.

**Why indexed.** Turns a correlation into a near-causal, actionable lever; one of the cleaner 'treat the body to protect the brain' results.

**Built on.** age-related hearing and cognition epidemiology

**Influenced / downstream.** the Lancet dementia-prevention factor list
