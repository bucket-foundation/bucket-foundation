**Field contribution.** Revived and established serial endosymbiotic theory: mitochondria and chloroplasts originated as free-living bacteria taken up by host cells, making organelles the descendants of ancient symbionts. Upstream of every mitochondrial-origin and mitochondrial-aging argument.

**Why indexed.** The endosymbiotic origin of mitochondria is the biological foundation under all bioenergetic-aging claims; canon-adjacent (origin of complexity).

**Built on.** Konstantin Mereschkowski, Ivan Wallin

**Influenced / downstream.** Nick Lane, William Martin, mitochondrial biology

**Canon bridge.** bridges to bucket-canon 05-biophysics

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Also championed the Gaia hypothesis and broader symbiogenesis-drives-speciation claims that remain contested.
