**Field contribution.** Led the Finnish KIHD cohort analyses showing frequent sauna use (4–7×/week) is associated with substantially lower cardiovascular and all-cause mortality and lower dementia incidence, establishing the dose-response sauna-longevity association in a large prospective population.

**Why indexed.** The empirical backbone of the heat-stress / sauna longevity literature.

**Built on.** KIHD cohort

**Influenced / downstream.** sauna/heat-shock-protein discourse (Patrick, Attia)

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Observational; healthy-user bias and reverse causation not fully excluded; no RCT.
