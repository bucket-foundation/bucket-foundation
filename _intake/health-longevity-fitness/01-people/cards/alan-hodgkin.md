### Alan L. Hodgkin

|---|---|

**Field contribution.** With Huxley, built the quantitative voltage-clamp model of the action potential, deriving the time- and voltage-dependent Na+ and K+ conductances of the squid giant axon. Shared the 1963 Nobel.

**Why indexed.** The Hodgkin-Huxley equations are the canonical mathematical foundation of neuronal excitability - a real, predictive biophysical law, canon-tier.

**Built on.** Bernstein membrane theory; Cole/Curtis voltage clamp

**Influenced / downstream.** Andrew Huxley, computational and cellular neuroscience

**Canon bridge.** bridges to bucket-canon 05-biophysics (membrane bioelectricity)
