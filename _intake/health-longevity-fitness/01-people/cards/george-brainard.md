**Field contribution.** Determined the human melatonin action spectrum, showing peak circadian sensitivity to short-wavelength (~460-480 nm blue) light, direct evidence for a non-rod/non-cone (melanopsin/ipRGC) circadian photoreceptor.

**Why indexed.** The empirical basis for blue-light circadian effects and melanopsin photoreception in humans.
