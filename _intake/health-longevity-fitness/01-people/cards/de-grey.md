**Field contribution.** Proposed SENS (Strategies for Engineered Negligible Senescence) — recasting aging as seven categories of accumulated molecular/cellular damage to be repaired rather than metabolism to be slowed — and 'longevity escape velocity.' Now runs the LEV Foundation's combination-therapy mouse rejuvenation trials.

**Why indexed.** Reframed the aging problem as a damage-repair engineering target, shaping how a generation of the longevity movement thinks; indexed for influence, claims contested.

**Built on.** free-radical/damage theories

**Influenced / downstream.** SENS Research Foundation, longevity-biotech movement

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Timeline predictions ('1000-year-olds') widely regarded as over-optimistic; SENS's seven-category sufficiency is unproven; non-traditional credentials and 2021 misconduct allegations (later partially addressed) are part of the record.
