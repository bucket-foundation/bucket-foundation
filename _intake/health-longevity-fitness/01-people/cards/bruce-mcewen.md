**Field contribution.** Introduced 'allostatic load', the cumulative physiological wear from chronic stress and repeated activation of stress-response systems, and mapped how stress hormones remodel the brain and body, providing the framework linking chronic stress to disease.

**Why indexed.** Allostatic load is the organizing concept for chronic-stress damage and recovery science.

**Built on.** Hans Selye
