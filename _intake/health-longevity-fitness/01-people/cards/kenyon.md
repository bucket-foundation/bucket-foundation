**Field contribution.** Showed that a single-gene mutation (daf-2, the worm insulin/IGF-1 receptor) doubles lifespan in C. elegans and that the effect requires the FOXO-family transcription factor daf-16. This converted aging from an unregulated wear-and-tear process into a genetically tractable, hormonally controlled program.

**Why indexed.** The first demonstration that aging rate is set by a discrete, conserved signalling pathway — the founding result of modern molecular gerontology.
