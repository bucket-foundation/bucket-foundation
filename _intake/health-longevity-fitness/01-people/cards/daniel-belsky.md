**Field contribution.** Built the Dunedin pace-of-aging epigenetic measures (DunedinPoAm, DunedinPACE), trained on longitudinal physiological decline rather than chronological age, and applied them to the CALERIE caloric-restriction trial to show measurable slowing of aging pace.

**Why indexed.** Reframed aging biomarkers from 'how old' to 'how fast you are aging' and tied them to a randomized human intervention.
