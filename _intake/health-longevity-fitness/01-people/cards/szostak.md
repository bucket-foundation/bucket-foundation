**Field contribution.** With Blackburn, showed that telomeric DNA sequences are a general, transplantable chromosome-cap function — a Tetrahymena telomere sequence protected a linear plasmid in yeast — proving telomere maintenance is a universal eukaryotic mechanism, not a ciliate quirk. Shared the 2009 Nobel for telomere/telomerase biology.

**Why indexed.** The third pillar (with Blackburn and Greider) under the telomere-attrition hallmark of aging; established that chromosome-end protection is a conserved, mechanistic problem rather than a curiosity.

**Built on.** Blackburn's telomere-sequence work; Olovnikov's/Watson's end-replication problem

**Influenced / downstream.** all telomere/telomerase biology; the replicative-senescence hallmark; later origins-of-life protocell research

**Canon bridge.** canon-adjacent 05-biophysics / aging-mechanisms

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Telomere length as a tractable anti-aging *lever* (vs marker) is contested — Mendelian-randomization evidence ties longer telomeres to higher cancer risk (see Haycock).
