**Field contribution.** Ran the Seven Countries Study linking saturated-fat intake and serum cholesterol to cardiovascular disease (the diet-heart hypothesis), conducted the Minnesota Starvation Experiment, and developed the K-ration and Body Mass Index usage — shaping 20th-century diet-disease epidemiology.

**Why indexed.** The origin of the diet-heart hypothesis that still structures nutrition debates; indexed frontier-contested because the saturated-fat conclusions remain disputed (the central nutrition conflict).

**Built on.** nutritional physiology

**Influenced / downstream.** dietary-guidelines era, saturated-fat / seed-oil debates

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Seven Countries country-selection and the saturated-fat conclusions are heavily contested (Teicholz et al.); a live, unresolved nutrition conflict.
