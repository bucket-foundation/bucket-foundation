**Field contribution.** Created the POLG 'mutator' mouse: a proofreading-deficient mtDNA polymerase that accumulates mtDNA mutations and produces a premature-aging phenotype, providing direct in-vivo evidence linking mtDNA mutation load to aging.

**Why indexed.** The mutator mouse is the foundational causal test of the mitochondrial theory of aging.
