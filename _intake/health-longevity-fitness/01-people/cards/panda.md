**Field contribution.** Helped identify melanopsin as the non-visual photoreceptor that entrains the circadian clock to light, then showed that time-restricted feeding (eating within a confined daily window) protects mice from diet-induced metabolic disease independent of calories — founding the time-restricted-eating field.

**Why indexed.** Connects the light-entrainment substrate to a concrete metabolic intervention (TRE); a key circadian-to-metabolism node.

**Built on.** circadian clock genetics
