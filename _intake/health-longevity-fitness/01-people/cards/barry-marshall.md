### Barry J. Marshall

|---|---|

**Field contribution.** Co-discovered that Helicobacter pylori causes most peptic ulcer disease and gastric cancer, and drank a culture of the bacterium himself to prove causation - overturning the stress-and-acid dogma. Shared the 2005 Nobel with Warren.

**Why indexed.** The definitive 'find the infectious cause, cure the disease' overturn of a chronic-disease paradigm; the model case for re-examining 'lifestyle' diseases for microbial causes.

**Built on.** Robin Warren's histopathology observations

**Influenced / downstream.** H. pylori eradication therapy, infection-cancer causation
