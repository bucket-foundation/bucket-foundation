**Field contribution.** Systematized and popularized **Ashtanga Vinyasa yoga**: fixed, demanding sequences of postures linked breath-to-movement (vinyasa) with ujjayi breath, bandhas (locks) and drishti (gaze). The athletic, sweat-driven parent style of Western "power yoga" and much of modern vinyasa flow.

**Why indexed.** Codifier of the most physically intense mainstream yoga lineage — relevant to cardiorespiratory/strength load and to higher injury exposure than gentle/restorative styles. Index the method separately from the man.

**Built on.** krishnamacharya (his teacher)

**Influenced / downstream.** power yoga; modern vinyasa flow; Western studio yoga

**Tags.** `ashtanga` `vinyasa` `mysore` `power-yoga` `ujjayi` `drishti`
