**Field contribution.** Leads the Emerging Risk Factors Collaboration, which pools individual-participant data from scores of prospective cohorts to estimate biomarker-mortality associations at a scale and rigor (adjustment, regression-dilution correction) unavailable to single studies - producing the highest-tier evidence on CRP, fibrinogen, glucose, and lipids as risk predictors.

**Why indexed.** The consortium and methodology behind the most authoritative biomarker-versus-mortality effect estimates; the standard against which single-cohort biomarker claims are checked.

**Built on.** prospective-cohort meta-analysis methods

**Influenced / downstream.** ridker, biomarker risk-prediction guidelines

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Indexed as a consortium-anchored figure; ERFC findings often temper enthusiastic single-study biomarker claims by showing modest incremental predictive value.
