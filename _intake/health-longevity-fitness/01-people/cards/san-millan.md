**Field contribution.** Popularized and clinically framed 'Zone 2' training — exercise at the intensity that maximizes fat oxidation and mitochondrial function just below lactate accumulation — as a longevity and metabolic-health lever, linking lactate-clearance capacity to mitochondrial health and insulin sensitivity.

**Why indexed.** The principal translator of mitochondrial/lactate exercise physiology into the widely-adopted Zone-2 longevity protocol.

**Built on.** lactate-threshold physiology, Brooks lactate-shuttle

**Influenced / downstream.** Attia, Zone-2 training movement
