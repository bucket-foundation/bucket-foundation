**Field contribution.** Characterized how NAD+ decline with age impairs sirtuin and mitochondrial function and links metabolism to immune aging; as Buck president steers a major geroscience institute. Work centers on ketone bodies (beta-hydroxybutyrate) and NAD+ as signalling metabolites, not just energy substrates.

**Why indexed.** A leading voice on NAD+/sirtuin biology grounded in rigorous metabolism rather than supplement marketing — useful counterweight in the NAD+ conflict.
