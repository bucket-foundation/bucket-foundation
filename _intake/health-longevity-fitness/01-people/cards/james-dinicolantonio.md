**Field contribution.** A principal popularizer of the hypothesis that omega-6 'seed oils' and their oxidized linoleic-acid metabolites (OXLAMs) drive coronary disease, and of contrarian salt and saturated-fat positions.

**Why indexed.** The most visible node of the seed-oils-cause-disease narrative; indexed at hypothesis tier (mechanism marketed as established outcome). INCLUSION does not equal ENDORSEMENT.

**Influenced / downstream.** seed-oil-skeptic movement

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Presents a mechanistic hypothesis (OXLAM to CHD) as settled; the outcome claim is not supported by RCT evidence (cf. Mozaffarian).
