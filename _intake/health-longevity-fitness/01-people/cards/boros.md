**Field contribution.** Argues that the deuterium/hydrogen ratio of metabolic water matters biologically — that mitochondrial nanomotors discriminate against deuterium, and that deuterium load affects cell proliferation and metabolism. Uses stable-isotope-resolved metabolomics (SIRM) as the analytic method.

**Why indexed.** A primary scientific voice for the deuterium-metabolism thesis that bridges to the biophysics water substrate; indexed frontier-contested.

**Built on.** stable-isotope metabolomics

**Influenced / downstream.** Somlyai (DDW clinical), deuterium-depletion community

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Clinical relevance of deuterium depletion in humans is unproven; effect sizes and disease claims are contested.
