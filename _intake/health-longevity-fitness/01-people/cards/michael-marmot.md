**Field contribution.** Established the social-gradient-in-health program: the Whitehall studies of British civil servants showed a stepwise mortality gradient by employment grade - not a poverty threshold - with job control and autonomy as candidate mediators. Chaired the WHO Commission on Social Determinants of Health.

**Why indexed.** Social position is itself a graded, dose-dependent determinant of mortality independent of access to care - the empirical foundation under the 'upstream determinants dwarf clinical optimization' argument.

**Built on.** Geoffrey Rose, John Snow (population thinking)

**Influenced / downstream.** determinants-of-health frameworks, health-equity policy
