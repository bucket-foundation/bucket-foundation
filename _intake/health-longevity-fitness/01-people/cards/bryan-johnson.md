**Field contribution.** Runs the most extensively measured and fully open single-subject longevity experiment — a fixed diet, ~100-supplement stack, exercise, sleep, and dense biomarker/imaging panel ('Don't Die'), publishing all protocols and results. The contribution is the open, instrumented N=1 dataset itself, not validated efficacy.

**Why indexed.** The reference example of the practitioner-N=1 tier — maximal measurement, zero randomization; indexed as data, graded nequals1.

**Built on.** quantified-self movement

**Influenced / downstream.** measurement-driven biohacking

**Disputed / known unknowns.** No causal inference possible from N=1; many interventions lack RCT support; commercial Blueprint products are a COI.
