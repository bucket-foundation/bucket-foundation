### Alexey M. Olovnikov

| **primary works** | *Principle of marginotomy in template synthesis of polynucleotides (Dokl Akad Nauk SSSR)* (1971); *A theory of marginotomy (J Theor Biol)* (1973) |

**Field contribution.** Independently deduced the end-replication problem — that conventional DNA polymerase cannot fully copy the ends of linear chromosomes, so they shorten with each division — and was first to explicitly tie this terminal underreplication to the finite (Hayflick) division capacity of cells, in his "theory of marginotomy."

**Why indexed.** The theoretical root of telomere-driven replicative senescence: Olovnikov supplied the *prediction* (chromosome ends must shorten and limit division) that telomere/telomerase biology later confirmed mechanistically.

**Built on.** Watson's 1972 note on replicating chromosome ends; Hayflick's replicative-limit data

**Influenced / downstream.** Blackburn, Greider, Szostak; the telomere-attrition hallmark

**Canon bridge.** canon-adjacent aging-mechanisms / 05-biophysics

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Olovnikov's later "redumeres/redusomes" and lunar-gravitation aging theories are fringe and not accepted; only the marginotomy/end-replication insight is canon-relevant.
