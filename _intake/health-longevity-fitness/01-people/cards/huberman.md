The protocols repackage existing literature; his role is communication and protocolization.

**Why indexed.** The most-consumed translator of circadian/light/breath/cold protocols to the public; indexed strictly as communicator — verify each protocol's primary source and effect size.

**Built on.** Satchin Panda (circadian light), Wim Hof / Buteyko (breath)

**Influenced / downstream.** mass adoption of light/cold/breath protocols

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Protocol effect sizes are often overstated relative to the underlying evidence tier; mechanism-to-outcome leaps are common in the format.
