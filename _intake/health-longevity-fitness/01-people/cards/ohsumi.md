**Field contribution.** Used yeast vacuoles to visualize autophagy directly and then identified the core ATG genes that execute it, mapping the molecular machinery of how cells recycle their own components under starvation. Won the 2016 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.

**Why indexed.** Defined the genetic machinery of autophagy, now one of the twelve hallmarks of aging and the mechanistic basis of fasting/mTOR-inhibition longevity claims.
