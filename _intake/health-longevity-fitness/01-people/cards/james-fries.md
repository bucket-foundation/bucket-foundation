### James F. Fries

**Field contribution.** Authored the compression-of-morbidity hypothesis - that if disease onset can be postponed faster than death, lifetime morbidity compresses into a shorter terminal window ('square the curve').

**Why indexed.** The rigorous statement of healthspan-over-lifespan and the organizing goal of the entire geriatric-prevention enterprise.

**Built on.** actuarial and survival-curve demography

**Influenced / downstream.** healthspan science, the longevity-medicine framing
