**Field contribution.** A Soviet physician who developed the Buteyko method — deliberate reduced-volume, nasal breathing to raise arterial CO2 tolerance — claiming chronic over-breathing (hyperventilation) underlies asthma and many disorders. The method's asthma-symptom benefit has modest RCT support; its broader disease theory does not.

**Why indexed.** The origin of the modern CO2-tolerance / nasal-breathing breathwork lineage that McKeown and Nestor transmit.

**Built on.** respiratory physiology (Bohr effect)

**Influenced / downstream.** McKeown, Nestor, breathwork movement

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Asthma symptom/medication-reduction has some RCT support; the broad 'hyperventilation causes most disease' theory is not validated.
