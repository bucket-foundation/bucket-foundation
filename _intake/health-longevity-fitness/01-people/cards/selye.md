|---|---|

**Field contribution.** Defined biological 'stress' and the General Adaptation Syndrome (alarm → resistance → exhaustion), and distinguished beneficial 'eustress' from harmful distress — the conceptual foundation under hormesis, allostatic load, and the entire stress-physiology basis of cold/heat/exercise adaptation.

**Why indexed.** Originated the scientific concept of physiological stress and adaptation that underlies all hormesis and recovery science.

**Built on.** Cannon (homeostasis)

**Influenced / downstream.** hormesis field, Mattson, allostasis (McEwen), Picard
