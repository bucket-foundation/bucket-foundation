**Field contribution.** Defined the "immune risk phenotype" — an inverted CD4/CD8 T-cell ratio, cytomegalovirus (CMV) seropositivity, and poor T-cell proliferation — that predicts mortality in the very old, and made the case that lifelong CMV infection is a major driver of T-cell immunosenescence ("memory inflation").

**Why indexed.** A central, measurable framework for immune aging: connects a chronic latent infection to the decline in infection defense and vaccine response that shapes late-life mortality.

**Built on.** T-cell immunology; longitudinal aging cohorts

**Influenced / downstream.** immunosenescence-and-vaccines research (Goronzy/Weyand); CMV-and-aging field

**Canon bridge.** immune-aging mechanism

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Whether CMV is net-harmful across all ages is debated (some "immune-fitness" arguments); the IRP's generalizability beyond the original Swedish cohorts is an open question.
