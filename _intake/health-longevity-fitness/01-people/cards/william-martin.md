**Field contribution.** Proposed the hydrogen hypothesis for eukaryogenesis and, with Michael Russell, the alkaline-hydrothermal-vent theory in which natural proton gradients across mineral membranes drive the first metabolism, grounding the origin of life in chemiosmotic geochemistry. Nick Lane's principal scientific collaborator.

**Why indexed.** Carries chemiosmosis back to the origin of life itself; the origin-of-life foundation under the corpus's bioenergetics thread.
