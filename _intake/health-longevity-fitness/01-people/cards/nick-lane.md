**Field contribution.** Synthesized the case that membrane-bound proton gradients (chemiosmosis) set the energetic constraints on the origin of life and the leap to eukaryotic complexity, arguing that mitochondrial bioenergetics, not genes alone, gate the size and complexity a cell can reach. The bridge author connecting mainstream mitochondrial biology to deep bioenergetic theory.

**Why indexed.** The principal contemporary synthesizer linking chemiosmotic theory, endosymbiosis, and origin-of-life to mitochondrial aging; the readable spine under the corpus's bioenergetics vertical.
