**Field contribution.** Quantified how resistance exercise and dietary protein (leucine, dose, distribution, timing) drive muscle protein synthesis, building the evidence that higher-than-RDA protein and resistance training protect against sarcopenia — the empirical core of the strength/protein side of the longevity diet conflict.

**Why indexed.** The primary muscle-protein-synthesis evidence base; the rigorous counterweight to protein-restriction longevity claims.

**Built on.** protein-turnover methodology

**Influenced / downstream.** Galpin, Attia, sarcopenia-prevention guidance

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Higher-protein-for-longevity stance opposes the Longo/IGF-1 restriction camp; likely age/context dependent.
