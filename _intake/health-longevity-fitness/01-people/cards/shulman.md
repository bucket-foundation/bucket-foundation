**Field contribution.** Used in-vivo magnetic-resonance spectroscopy to define the ectopic-fat mechanism of insulin resistance: lipid accumulation inside muscle and liver cells (diacylglycerol-driven kinase activation) blunts insulin signaling, independent of total adiposity — and showed it is reversible with modest fat loss.

**Why indexed.** Pins insulin resistance — the central, reversible cardiometabolic-aging node — to a specific, measurable, modifiable mechanism rather than weight or "metabolic syndrome" as a black box.

**Built on.** the glucose-fatty-acid (Randle) cycle; NMR metabolic methods

**Influenced / downstream.** NAFLD/MASLD pathophysiology; diabetes-remission-by-weight-loss work (Taylor's twin-cycle)

**Canon bridge.** canon-adjacent 05-biophysics (cellular bioenergetics)

**Disputed / known unknowns.** The precise lipid species and signaling steps (DAG/PKCε vs ceramide) remain an active mechanistic debate.
