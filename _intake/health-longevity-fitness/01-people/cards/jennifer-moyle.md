**Field contribution.** Co-developed and experimentally proved chemiosmosis alongside Mitchell, providing the quantitative proton-translocation measurements (H+/O and H+/ATP stoichiometries) that turned the theory from proposal into validated mechanism.

**Why indexed.** The experimental half of the chemiosmotic proof; indexed both for the science and as a documented case of an under-credited collaborator.

**Built on.** Peter Mitchell

**Influenced / downstream.** bioenergetics

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Substantially under-credited relative to Mitchell despite co-authoring the decisive experimental papers; not included in the 1978 Nobel.
