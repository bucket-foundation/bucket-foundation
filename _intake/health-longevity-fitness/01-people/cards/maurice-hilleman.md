### Maurice Hilleman

| | |
|---|---|

**Field contribution.** The most prolific vaccine developer in history (~40 vaccines), including measles, mumps, rubella, hepatitis A and B, varicella, and pneumococcus; the hepatitis B vaccine was the first to prevent a human cancer.

**Why indexed.** Hilleman's vaccines prevent millions of deaths annually - arguably more life-years saved than any other single 20th-century scientist; hepatitis B vaccine is also a foundation case for infection-driven cancer prevention.

**Built on.** Jenner-Pasteur vaccinology tradition

**Influenced / downstream.** modern childhood immunization schedules
