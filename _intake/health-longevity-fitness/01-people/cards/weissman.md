**Field contribution.** With Katalin Karikó, discovered that substituting modified nucleosides (e.g. pseudouridine) into synthetic mRNA prevents it from triggering destructive innate-immune inflammation while preserving translation — the modification that made mRNA usable as a therapeutic and vaccine platform.

**Why indexed.** The enabling chemistry behind the BNT162b2/mRNA-1273 COVID vaccines and an entire new class of programmable mRNA medicines; a foundational platform shift in vaccinology and protein therapeutics.

**Built on.** in-vitro-transcribed mRNA technology; innate-immune RNA-sensing (TLR) biology

**Influenced / downstream.** mRNA vaccine/therapeutic industry; personalized cancer mRNA vaccines

**Canon bridge.** canon-adjacent 04-information / molecular biology

**Disputed / known unknowns.** None on the core discovery; long-term and broader-indication efficacy of mRNA platforms is still being established.
