**Field contribution.** Proposed and defended chemiosmotic theory: ATP synthesis is powered by a transmembrane electrochemical proton gradient (the proton-motive force) generated by the respiratory chain, not by a chemical high-energy intermediate. Won the 1978 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for the theory.

**Why indexed.** Chemiosmosis is the actual physical law under mitochondrial ATP production: canon-tier (05-biophysics), the foundation beneath every downstream mitochondrial-aging claim.

**Built on.** David Keilin, Hans Lundegardh

**Influenced / downstream.** Nick Lane, all of bioenergetics

**Canon bridge.** canon-tier 05-biophysics

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Chemiosmotic theory was fiercely resisted for ~15 years (the 'ox-phos wars') before experimental validation.
