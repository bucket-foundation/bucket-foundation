### Leonard Guarente

| | |
|---|---|
| **id** | guarente |

| **primary works** | *SIR2 extends replicative lifespan in yeast (Cell/Genes Dev)* (1999–2000); *Sir2/NAD+ links metabolism to aging and caloric restriction* (2000) |

**Field contribution.** Showed that the SIR2 gene (a NAD+-dependent deacetylase) regulates replicative lifespan in yeast and links caloric restriction to longevity through NAD+, founding the sirtuin field of aging research. Mentor to David Sinclair.

**Why indexed.** Originated the sirtuin/NAD+ theory of aging — one of the two or three dominant molecular frameworks in the field.

**Built on.** yeast aging genetics

**Influenced / downstream.** Sinclair, Imai, Verdin, NAD+ field

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Some sirtuin lifespan claims (incl. resveratrol activation) were later challenged; the core NAD+/Sir2 biology stands.

**Tags.** `sirtuins` `sir2` `nad` `caloric-restriction` `yeast`
