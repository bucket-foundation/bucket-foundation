**Field contribution.** Showed in the Insulin Resistance Atherosclerosis Study (IRAS) that HOMA-IR, a fasting-insulin-and-glucose index of insulin resistance, predicts incident cardiovascular disease, supporting fasting insulin resistance as an early-warning metabolic biomarker before overt dysglycemia.

**Why indexed.** Provided cohort evidence that a simple fasting-derived insulin-resistance index forecasts cardiovascular disease, anchoring the 'measure insulin resistance early' argument.

**Built on.** IRAS cohort

**Influenced / downstream.** fasting-insulin / HOMA-IR screening advocacy
