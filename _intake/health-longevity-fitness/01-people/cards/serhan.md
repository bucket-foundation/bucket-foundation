**Field contribution.** Discovered specialized pro-resolving mediators (SPMs) — lipoxins, resolvins, protectins, maresins — derived from omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids, and showed that the *resolution* of inflammation is not passive decay but an actively driven, programmed biochemical process.

**Why indexed.** Reframes "anti-inflammatory" from suppression to active resolution — a foundational mechanism for chronic inflammation/inflammaging, omega-3 biology, and why failed resolution (not just excess inflammation) drives age-related disease.

**Built on.** eicosanoid/prostaglandin biochemistry (Bergström, Samuelsson)

**Influenced / downstream.** inflammaging research; omega-3 mechanism studies; resolution-pharmacology drug development

**Canon bridge.** canon-adjacent 05-biophysics (lipid-mediator signaling)

**Disputed / known unknowns.** SPM measurement methods and the magnitude of dietary-omega-3-to-SPM translation in humans are actively debated.
