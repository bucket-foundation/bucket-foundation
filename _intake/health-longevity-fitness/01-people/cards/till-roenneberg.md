|---|---|

**Field contribution.** Coined 'social jetlag' (the mismatch between biological and social clocks) and operationalized human chronotype via the Munich ChronoType Questionnaire, turning circadian misalignment into a measurable population variable.

**Why indexed.** Created the standard human constructs, chronotype and social jetlag, for studying circadian misalignment and health.

**Influenced / downstream.** circadian-health epidemiology
