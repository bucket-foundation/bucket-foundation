**Field contribution.** Discovered the PD-1 immune checkpoint, the basis of the anti-PD-1/PD-L1 therapies (pembrolizumab, nivolumab) that became the broader, better-tolerated arm of cancer immunotherapy. Shared the 2018 Nobel with Allison.

**Why indexed.** PD-1 blockade is a foundation-tier cancer treatment mechanism and the most widely deployed checkpoint pathway.

**Built on.** tumor and lymphocyte immunology

**Influenced / downstream.** James Allison (CTLA-4), anti-PD-1/PD-L1 oncology
