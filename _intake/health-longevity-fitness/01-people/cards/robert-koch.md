### Robert Koch

| | |
|---|---|
| **id** | robert-koch |

| **primary works** | *Die Atiologie der Milzbrand-Krankheit: anthrax* (1876); *Die Atiologie der Tuberkulose: tubercle bacillus* (1882) |

**Field contribution.** Formalized Koch's postulates - the logical criteria for proving a specific microbe causes a specific disease - and isolated the anthrax, tuberculosis, and cholera bacilli using pure-culture and staining methods he largely invented. Won the 1905 Nobel.

**Why indexed.** Koch's postulates are the methodological backbone of germ theory and of all causal reasoning about infectious disease - a foundation-tier epistemic rule, not just a discovery.

**Built on.** Louis Pasteur, Ferdinand Cohn

**Influenced / downstream.** Paul Ehrlich, modern medical microbiology, causal-inference criteria
