### Joseph Pilates

| | |
|---|---|
| **id** | pilates |
| **lifespan** | 1883–1967 |

**Field contribution.** Created 'Contrology' (now Pilates) — a mat- and apparatus-based system emphasizing core control, alignment, breath, and controlled whole-body movement — that became a foundational modern method for core stability, rehabilitation, and mobility.

**Why indexed.** Originated one of the two or three dominant Western movement/core-conditioning systems still in clinical and fitness use.

**Built on.** gymnastics, boxing, self-defense, yoga influences

**Influenced / downstream.** rehabilitation, mobility/core training
