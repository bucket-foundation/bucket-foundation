**Field contribution.** Showed (via continuous glucose monitoring + microbiome + a machine-learning model) that postprandial glucose responses to identical meals vary enormously between people and can be predicted and personalized — founding data-driven personalized nutrition and validating CGM-guided N=1 dietary tailoring.

**Why indexed.** Provided the rigorous proof-of-concept for personalized, measurement-driven nutrition — the data backbone under CGM biohacking.

**Built on.** microbiome science, machine learning

**Influenced / downstream.** Spector/PREDICT, CGM nutrition movement
