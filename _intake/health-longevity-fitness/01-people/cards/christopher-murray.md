**Field contribution.** Architect of the Global Burden of Disease enterprise and founder of IHME - the standardized comparative risk-assessment machinery (DALYs, attributable-risk modeling) that ranks PM2.5, tobacco, and alcohol among top-tier global mortality risks and underpins the 'no safe level' alcohol framing.

**Why indexed.** GBD is the canonical quantitative substrate for 'what actually kills people at scale' - the calibration layer against which any individual longevity intervention should be sized.

**Built on.** WHO burden-of-disease tradition

**Influenced / downstream.** national health-policy prioritization, the 'no safe level' alcohol literature
