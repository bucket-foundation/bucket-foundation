**Field contribution.** Lead author of the 2013 'Hallmarks of Aging' framework, which organized the aging literature into nine (later twelve) categorical hallmarks — genomic instability, telomere attrition, epigenetic alteration, loss of proteostasis, deregulated nutrient sensing, mitochondrial dysfunction, cellular senescence, stem-cell exhaustion, altered intercellular communication, plus disabled autophagy, dysbiosis, chronic inflammation.

**Why indexed.** Provides the consensus organizing ontology of mainstream geroscience — the spine onto which nearly every other figure in this map is hung.
