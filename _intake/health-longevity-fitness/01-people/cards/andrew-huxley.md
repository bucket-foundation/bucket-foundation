**Field contribution.** Co-author of the Hodgkin-Huxley action-potential model and the numerical methods used to solve it; independently co-founded the sliding-filament theory of muscle contraction. Shared the 1963 Nobel.

**Why indexed.** The HH model is the foundational law of neuronal excitability; sliding-filament is the foundational mechanism of muscle force - two canon-tier biophysical foundations.

**Built on.** Hodgkin collaboration; muscle physiology

**Influenced / downstream.** computational neuroscience, muscle biophysics
