**Field contribution.** Tested evolutionary theories of aging in the wild (island opossums with relaxed predation evolve slower aging), championed long-lived species (naked mole-rats, bats, clams) as windows into aging mechanisms, and famously bet (with Olshansky) on human lifespan limits.

**Why indexed.** A founder of comparative biogerontology — using species that defy the aging 'rules' to find what governs lifespan.

**Built on.** evolutionary senescence theory

**Influenced / downstream.** Gladyshev, comparative-aging field
