**Field contribution.** Led the Duke group (with Pericak-Vance, Strittmatter, Corder) that established the APOE-ε4 allele as the major common genetic risk factor for late-onset Alzheimer's disease, with a clear gene-dose effect (risk and earlier onset rising with each ε4 copy).

**Why indexed.** The strongest common-variant risk finding in neurodegeneration and the genetic variable most relevant to consumer genetics and dementia-risk discussion — a true risk allele, not deterministic, and the canonical case for counseling-before-disclosure.

**Built on.** Alzheimer's linkage studies; apolipoprotein biology

**Influenced / downstream.** APOE-disclosure research (REVEAL); amyloid/lipid-transport Alzheimer's biology; risk-stratified prevention trials

**Canon bridge.** practical-genetics / neuro risk

**Disputed / known unknowns.** ε4's effect is modified by sex, age, and ancestry (smaller in African ancestry); it is probabilistic, and its mechanism (lipid handling vs amyloid) is still worked out.
