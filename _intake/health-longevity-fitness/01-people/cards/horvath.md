**Field contribution.** Built the first multi-tissue epigenetic clock — a weighted set of ~353 CpG methylation sites whose state predicts chronological age across nearly all human tissues — establishing DNAm age as a measurable biomarker of biological aging, later extended to mortality-predicting clocks (GrimAge).

**Why indexed.** Made 'biological age' a quantitative, reproducible measurement, the dependent variable nearly every intervention now tries to move.

**Built on.** epigenetics of aging

**Influenced / downstream.** Morgan Levine, DunedinPACE, clock industry
