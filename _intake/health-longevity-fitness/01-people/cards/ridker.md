**Field contribution.** Championed high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hsCRP) as a measurable index of vascular inflammation predicting cardiovascular events, ran JUPITER (a statin trial that enrolled on CRP rather than LDL) and CANTOS (which lowered events with an anti-inflammatory antibody at unchanged LDL), supplying trial evidence for the inflammation hypothesis of atherosclerosis.

**Why indexed.** The reference figure linking a measurable inflammatory biomarker (hsCRP) to cardiovascular risk and testing it in randomized trials - the empirical anchor of the residual-inflammatory-risk concept.

**Built on.** inflammation hypothesis of atherosclerosis

**Influenced / downstream.** danesh, residual-inflammatory-risk paradigm

**Cross-branch.** links to Domain B inflammaging (Franceschi) as a measurable vascular endpoint

**Disputed / known unknowns.** The clinical primacy of CRP-guided therapy is debated; JUPITER's effect size and CRP's incremental value over standard risk factors have been contested.
