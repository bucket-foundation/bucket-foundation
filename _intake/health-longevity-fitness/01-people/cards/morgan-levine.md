**Field contribution.** Developed PhenoAge — a clock trained on clinical mortality rather than chronological age — and contributed to DunedinPACE, which measures the *rate* of aging from a single blood draw. Shifted clock-building from 'how old are your cells' to 'how fast are you aging and how likely to die'.

**Why indexed.** Reframed biological-age measurement around outcomes (mortality, healthspan), giving interventions a clinically meaningful target.
