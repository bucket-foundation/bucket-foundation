**Field contribution.** First-authored the Framingham analyses linking reduced heart-rate variability (HRV) - a measure of autonomic/parasympathetic tone - to elevated mortality and cardiac-event risk in a community cohort, establishing HRV as a population-level prognostic biomarker.

**Why indexed.** The foundational cohort evidence that HRV predicts mortality, underpinning the now-ubiquitous use of HRV in wearables and recovery tracking.

**Built on.** Framingham Heart Study

**Influenced / downstream.** fred-shaffer, HRV recovery-tracking practice
