**Field contribution.** Defined the operational frailty phenotype (>=3 of 5: unintentional weight loss, exhaustion, weakness, slow gait, low activity) - the cohort-validated construct that separates frailty from age and comorbidity and predicts falls, disability, and death.

**Why indexed.** A foundational, measurable construct that made frailty a research and clinical object distinct from chronological age.

**Built on.** geriatric epidemiology (Cardiovascular Health Study)

**Influenced / downstream.** the frailty-index literature, geriatric-assessment practice
