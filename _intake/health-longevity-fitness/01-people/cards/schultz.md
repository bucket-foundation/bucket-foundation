**Field contribution.** Recorded midbrain dopamine neurons and showed they fire to reward-PREDICTION ERROR — the discrepancy between expected and received reward — not to pleasure itself. Dopamine signals "better/worse than expected," matching the temporal-difference teaching signal of reinforcement learning.

**Why indexed.** The empirical foundation that debunks "dopamine = pleasure" and the consumer "dopamine detox / dopamine fasting" framing; load-bearing for any honest account of motivation, craving, and reward in addiction and behavior change.

**Built on.** reinforcement-learning theory (Sutton & Barto); Olds/Milner reward circuitry

**Influenced / downstream.** computational neuroscience, addiction neuroscience (Berridge wanting-vs-liking), reinforcement learning in AI

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Dopamine's full functional repertoire (movement, salience, aversion coding) is broader than RPE alone; debate continues on heterogeneity of dopamine subpopulations.
