**Field contribution.** Mined a traditional Chinese remedy (sweet wormwood) for antimalarial leads, then used a low-temperature extraction to isolate the active compound artemisinin — now the backbone of artemisinin-combination therapy, the most effective treatment for falciparum malaria and a major driver of the global malaria-mortality decline.

**Why indexed.** The canonical example that traditional pharmacopeias can be real sources of drug leads — validated NOT by the traditional theory but by isolation, purification, structure, and controlled trial; the disciplined counter-case in the CAM-grading corpus.

**Built on.** the *Zhouhou Beiji Fang* extraction hint; the Project 523 antimalarial screen

**Influenced / downstream.** artemisinin-combination therapy (ACT); natural-product drug discovery

**Canon bridge.** evidence-grading / drug-discovery method

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Attribution of the broader team effort (Project 523) is historically complex; emerging artemisinin resistance is an active threat to the therapy.
