**Field contribution.** Reported, with colleagues at Pfizer (and separately Amgen), that the apparent SIRT1 'activation' by resveratrol and the Sirtris STAC compounds was a fluorophore-substrate assay artifact - activation appeared only with a non-physiological fluorogenic peptide and vanished with native substrate - supplying the empirical biochemical check on the resveratrol-longevity claims.

**Why indexed.** Indexed as the group behind the in-vitro result that directly contested the mechanism underlying a major longevity-supplement narrative - an example of an empirical refutation entering the canon's evidence layer.

**Built on.** sirtuin enzymology

**Influenced / downstream.** resveratrol-mechanism debate, sirtuin-activator skepticism

**Disputed / known unknowns.** The assay-artifact finding is itself contested; Sinclair and Sirtris/GSK argued allosteric activation is real with native substrates bearing hydrophobic residues. Indexed as a documented scientific disagreement, not a settled verdict. INCLUSION != ENDORSEMENT.
