**Field contribution.** Showed that progressive high-intensity resistance training builds large strength and functional gains even in frail people in their 80s and 90s - establishing that the anabolic response to loading persists at the extreme end of age.

**Why indexed.** The proof that resistance training works at the oldest, frailest extreme - the anchor result under 'lift weights for healthspan at any age'.
