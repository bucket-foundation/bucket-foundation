**Field contribution.** Discovered the glymphatic system — a glia-dependent perivascular network that clears metabolic waste (including amyloid-beta) from the brain — and showed it is markedly more active during sleep, providing a mechanistic link between sleep and neurodegeneration risk.

**Why indexed.** Gave sleep a concrete brain-clearance mechanism, central to sleep-aging-dementia claims.

**Built on.** CSF/astrocyte physiology

**Influenced / downstream.** sleep-and-Alzheimer's research

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Magnitude and some mechanics of glymphatic flow (and anesthesia effects) are debated in the literature.
