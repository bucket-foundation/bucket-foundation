**Field contribution.** Created the first gene-edited human babies by disabling CCR5 in embryos - a scientifically dubious, medically unjustified, and consent-violating act condemned worldwide; convicted and imprisoned in China (2019).

**Why indexed.** Indexed as the field's defining ethical-violation cautionary tale, not for a scientific contribution - inclusion documents the boundary case for germline editing. Inclusion is not endorsement.

**Built on.** CRISPR-Cas9 (Doudna/Charpentier)

**Influenced / downstream.** the global moratorium debate on heritable human genome editing

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Widely condemned as unethical and scientifically unsound; the edits were mosaic and did not cleanly reproduce the protective CCR5-delta32 allele. Inclusion documents the ethics boundary, not a validated result.
