**Field contribution.** Ran the NIA rhesus-monkey caloric-restriction study and helped reconcile it with the divergent Wisconsin result (diet composition and feeding controls explain the difference), and showed that fasting interval/meal timing improves rodent healthspan independent of calorie or diet content.

**Why indexed.** Provided the primate-level CR evidence and disentangled calories from timing — key to interpreting the human CR/TRE literature.
