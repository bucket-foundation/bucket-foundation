**Field contribution.** Proposed the 'disposable soma' theory — organisms allocate limited energy between reproduction and somatic maintenance, so aging results from evolutionarily optimal under-investment in repair — providing the dominant evolutionary explanation for why aging exists and why nutrient-sensing/repair pathways govern it.

**Why indexed.** The reigning evolutionary-theory foundation that explains *why* the cellular hallmarks of aging are tolerated.

**Built on.** Medawar, Williams (antagonistic pleiotropy)

**Influenced / downstream.** Partridge, evolutionary biogerontology
