**Field contribution.** Proposed the 'triage theory', that under micronutrient scarcity the body allocates vitamins/minerals to immediate survival at the expense of long-term maintenance, accelerating age-related disease, and earlier the mitochondrial-decay theory of aging and the Ames mutagenicity test.

**Why indexed.** Connects nutrition to mitochondria to aging via a falsifiable allocation principle; indexed for the triage/mitochondrial-decay framework.

**Influenced / downstream.** nutritional-geroscience, mitochondrial-aging field
