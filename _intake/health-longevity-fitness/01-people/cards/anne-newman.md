**Field contribution.** Showed in the Health ABC cohort that DXA-measured lean muscle mass does not independently predict mortality once muscle strength is accounted for, the empirical root of the field's shift from 'muscle mass' to 'muscle strength/function' as the protective variable.

**Why indexed.** Provided the data that reframed sarcopenia outcomes around strength rather than mass - a correction repeatedly cited against mass-centric body-composition claims.

**Built on.** Cardiovascular Health Study, Health ABC

**Influenced / downstream.** strength-over-mass reframe, sarcopenia definitions
