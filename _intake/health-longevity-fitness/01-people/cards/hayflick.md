**Field contribution.** Demonstrated that normal human cells divide only a finite number of times in culture (the 'Hayflick limit') before entering senescence, overturning the dogma of cellular immortality and founding the concept of replicative senescence — later mechanistically tied to telomere shortening.

**Why indexed.** Established replicative senescence as a real, intrinsic cellular aging clock — the empirical bedrock under the senescence hallmark.

**Built on.** tissue-culture biology

**Influenced / downstream.** Campisi, Blackburn/Greider (telomere mechanism)
