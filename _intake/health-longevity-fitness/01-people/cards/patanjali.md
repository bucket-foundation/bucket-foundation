**Field contribution.** Compiled the *Yoga Sutras*, the foundational text of classical yoga, codifying the "eight limbs" (ashtanga): ethical restraints, posture (asana — described only as "a steady, comfortable seat"), breath control (pranayama), sense-withdrawal, concentration, meditation (dhyana) and absorption (samadhi). The text is a **meditative/philosophical** system, not a manual of physical postures.

**Why indexed.** The textual/historical root invoked by nearly all modern yoga, and the source most often *mis-cited* to lend ancient authority to postural ("asana") practice — a conflation the corpus keeps explicit. Cultural provenance, not biological evidence.

**Built on.** Samkhya philosophy; pre-existing yogic oral tradition

**Influenced / downstream.** Hatha yoga; Krishnamacharya lineage; all modern postural & meditative yoga
