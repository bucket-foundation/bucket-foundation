**Field contribution.** Discovered prions — infectious agents composed of protein alone, with no nucleic acid genome — which propagate by templating misfolding of the normal host protein. Overturned the central assumption that all transmissible pathogens must carry DNA or RNA.

**Why indexed.** Foundational reconceptualization of "infection" and a bridge to the protein-misfolding/aggregation diseases of aging (Alzheimer's, Parkinson's), where prion-like seeded propagation is now a leading mechanistic frame.

**Built on.** Alper's radiation-resistance scrapie data; Griffith's protein-only hypothesis

**Influenced / downstream.** protein-misfolding neurodegeneration research; the "prion-like spread" model of tau/alpha-synuclein

**Canon bridge.** canon-adjacent 05-biophysics (protein conformation as heritable information)

**Disputed / known unknowns.** The protein-only hypothesis was fiercely resisted for years; some strain/transmissibility details remain debated, but the core is established.
