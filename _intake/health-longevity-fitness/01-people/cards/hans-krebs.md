**Field contribution.** Discovered the citric-acid (Krebs/TCA) cycle, the central hub of aerobic energy metabolism that oxidizes acetyl-CoA and feeds reducing equivalents to the respiratory chain, and earlier the urea cycle. Won the 1953 Nobel Prize in Medicine.

**Why indexed.** The TCA cycle is a central metabolic law: the input side of the chemiosmotic machine; foundational to bioenergetics and nutrition alike.

**Built on.** Albert Szent-Gyorgyi, Otto Warburg

**Influenced / downstream.** Peter Mitchell, Nick Lane, metabolic biochemistry
