**Field contribution.** Created **Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR)** in 1979 — an 8-week secularized clinical program (body scan, sitting meditation, gentle Hatha yoga) that stripped meditation of religious framing so it could enter medicine and be studied in RCTs. The single intervention that made "mindfulness" a research and clinical object; parent of MBCT and the broader mindfulness movement.

**Why indexed.** MBSR is the most-studied meditation intervention; it anchors the meditation evidence base (Davidson 2003 immune/brain study; the Goyal 2014 meta is largely MBSR/MBCT trials). The honest read: *moderate* evidence for anxiety/depression/pain, weak for most else, expectancy-/active-control-limited.

**Built on.** patanjali (classical yoga); Theravada/Zen Buddhist meditation; Soen Sa Nim, Thich Nhat Hanh

**Influenced / downstream.** MBCT (Segal/Williams/Teasdale); richard-davidson; clinical mindfulness field
