**Field contribution.** Founder of the Harvard Program in Placebo Studies; demonstrated that open-label placebo (knowingly taking an inert pill) still produces symptom benefit in IBS - reframing placebo as real, ritual- and context-driven endogenous physiology rather than mere deception.

**Why indexed.** Establishes placebo as a genuine, mechanism-bearing modulator of subjective experience - the bounding principle for reading every unblindable wellness intervention.

**Built on.** Beecher placebo tradition; Benedetti/Levine placebo neurobiology

**Influenced / downstream.** open-label-placebo trials, the context-and-ritual literature
