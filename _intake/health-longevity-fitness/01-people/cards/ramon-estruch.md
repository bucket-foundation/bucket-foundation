**Field contribution.** Led PREDIMED, the largest randomized trial of the Mediterranean diet for cardiovascular prevention, which reported reduced major cardiovascular events. The trial was retracted and republished in 2018 after randomization irregularities were found and corrected.

**Why indexed.** PREDIMED is the strongest Mediterranean-diet outcome RCT; its retraction/republication is a trial-integrity case study indexed for both reasons.

**Influenced / downstream.** Mediterranean-diet field

**Disputed / known unknowns.** 2018 retraction/republication after discovery that part of the cohort was not individually randomized; corrected analysis upheld the main finding.
