**Field contribution.** Performed the original caloric-restriction experiments (1935) showing that rats fed a calorie-restricted but nutritionally adequate diet lived substantially longer than freely-fed rats — the first demonstration that diet can extend maximum lifespan, founding the entire field of dietary longevity intervention.

**Why indexed.** The origin experiment of caloric restriction — the most robust, most-replicated longevity intervention in biology.

**Built on.** nutrition science

**Influenced / downstream.** Walford, Fontana, Longo, all CR research
