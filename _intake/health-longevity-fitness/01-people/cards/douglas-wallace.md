**Field contribution.** Founded human mitochondrial genetics: established that mitochondrial DNA is maternally inherited in humans, defined the heteroplasmy threshold effect (a tissue tolerates mutant mtDNA until a critical fraction is exceeded), and identified the first pathogenic mtDNA mutation — building the framework linking bioenergetic genotype to disease and aging.

**Why indexed.** The genetic substrate under all mitochondrial-aging and bioenergetic-decline claims; reframes much of disease and aging through inherited and accumulated energy-system variation rather than nuclear genes alone.

**Built on.** chemiosmotic bioenergetics (Mitchell); cytoplasmic-inheritance genetics

**Influenced / downstream.** mitochondrial-medicine field; Larsson/Trifunovic mtDNA-mutator-mouse work; Nick Lane's bioenergetic synthesis

**Canon bridge.** canon-adjacent 05-biophysics (bioenergetics genetics)

**Disputed / known unknowns.** The quantitative contribution of somatic mtDNA mutation to normal aging (vs disease) remains debated; the "mitochondrial theory of aging" has been substantially revised.
