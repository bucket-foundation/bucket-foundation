**Field contribution.** Founder of **contemplative neuroscience** — the rigorous neuroscience of meditation. Showed an 8-week MBSR course shifted prefrontal EEG asymmetry toward a positive-affect pattern AND improved antibody response to influenza vaccine (2003), and that long-term Tibetan adepts can self-generate high-amplitude gamma EEG synchrony (2004). Founded the Center for Healthy Minds.

**Why indexed.** The lab that converted meditation from anecdote into a brain/immune measurement program — the credible end of the contemplative-science literature. Caveat retained: his foundational studies are small (n in the tens) and the field's wider claims (telomeres, longevity) far outrun this rigorous core.
