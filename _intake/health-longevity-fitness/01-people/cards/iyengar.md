**Field contribution.** Systematized Hatha yoga asana into a precise, alignment-based, prop-assisted method (Iyengar Yoga), codifying hundreds of postures with detailed technique in 'Light on Yoga' — the most influential transmission of yoga asana to the modern world and the basis for most clinical yoga research.

**Why indexed.** The principal codifier of modern postural yoga, the movement/flexibility tradition most studied for measurable health effects.

**Built on.** Krishnamacharya (his teacher)

**Influenced / downstream.** global modern yoga, clinical yoga research
