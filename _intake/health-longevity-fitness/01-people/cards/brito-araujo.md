**Field contribution.** Developed and validated the sitting-rising test (SRT) - scoring how few supports a person needs to lower to and rise from the floor - and showed in a middle-aged-and-older cohort that lower SRT scores predict higher all-cause mortality, converting a musculoskeletal-fitness movement into a hard-outcome biomarker.

**Why indexed.** The outcome-anchored evidence beneath floor-mobility and 'sit-to-rise' advice; a rare case where a simple movement test is tied directly to mortality data.
