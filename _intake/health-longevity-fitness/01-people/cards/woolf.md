**Field contribution.** Defined central sensitization — an injury-triggered increase in the gain/excitability of central nociceptive neurons that produces allodynia and hyperalgesia independent of ongoing tissue damage. Established that pain can be a state of the nervous system, not merely a readout of peripheral injury.

**Why indexed.** The mechanistic spine of "nociplastic" chronic pain (fibromyalgia, much chronic low-back pain): explains how pain becomes real and central without proportional tissue damage — the science under modern pain-neuroscience education.

**Built on.** nociceptor physiology; spinal-cord plasticity

**Influenced / downstream.** Moseley/Butler pain-neuroscience education; nociplastic-pain classification; neuropathic-pain pharmacology

**Canon bridge.** canon-adjacent (neural plasticity / pain)

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Translating the mechanism into reliably effective treatments remains hard; the construct is sometimes over-applied clinically.
