**Field contribution.** Co-leads the NIA Interventions Testing Program (ITP), the rigorous multi-site replicated mouse-lifespan testing platform that produced the landmark finding that rapamycin extends lifespan even when started in old age, and validated/refuted many candidate geroprotectors (acarbose, 17-α-estradiol vs. resveratrol).

**Why indexed.** Built the gold-standard replicated testbed that separates real geroprotectors from hype — the field's quality filter.

**Built on.** caloric-restriction rodent studies

**Influenced / downstream.** rapamycin translation, geroprotector vetting
