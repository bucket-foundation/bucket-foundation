**Field contribution.** Pooled-analysis lead establishing that usual gait speed predicts survival in older adults about as well as multi-factor clinical models, turning a simple walking-speed measurement into a validated functional aging biomarker.

**Why indexed.** Made gait speed a credentialed survival predictor - the canonical example of a cheap functional metric rivaling complex panels.
