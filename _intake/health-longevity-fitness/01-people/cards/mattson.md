**Field contribution.** Pioneered the neuroscience of intermittent fasting and hormesis — that intermittent energetic stress (fasting, exercise) triggers adaptive, protective responses (BDNF, ketones, autophagy, mitochondrial biogenesis) that benefit the brain and slow aging ('intermittent metabolic switching').

**Why indexed.** A founder of the fasting/hormesis-for-brain-aging framework, grounding it in adaptive-stress neurobiology.

**Built on.** hormesis (Selye lineage), autophagy

**Influenced / downstream.** intermittent-fasting field, Longo, Huberman
