**Field contribution.** Proposed that biological systems can sustain long-range quantum coherence — 'Fröhlich condensation' — where metabolic energy pumps a macroscopic fraction of molecules into a single coherent vibrational mode, a candidate mechanism for non-thermal collective order in living matter.

**Why indexed.** A founding theoretical claim of quantum biology; bridges UP to canon-05-biophysics (coherence). Indexed frontier-contested — theoretical tier, experimentally unconfirmed in vivo.

**Built on.** BCS superconductivity, Szent-Györgyi (electronic biology)

**Canon bridge.** bridges UP to bucket-canon/05-biophysics (coherence); sibling to the carded Popp

**Disputed / known unknowns.** In-vivo Fröhlich condensation has never been experimentally demonstrated; remains a theoretical proposal.
