**Field contribution.** Led the American Heart Association statement arguing cardiorespiratory fitness (CRF/VO2max) should be measured as a routine clinical vital sign, and produced imaging-based work on exercise effects on visceral fat, joining the 'fitness as the strongest modifiable mortality predictor' argument to a measurement recommendation.

**Why indexed.** The figure who pushed cardiorespiratory fitness from a research variable toward a recommended clinical vital sign.
