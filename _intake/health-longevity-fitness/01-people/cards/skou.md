**Field contribution.** Discovered the sodium-potassium ATPase — the membrane enzyme that hydrolyzes ATP to pump Na+ out and K+ into the cell against their gradients. This is the pump that builds and maintains the transmembrane ion gradients storing the electrical potential energy every nerve and muscle cell spends to fire.

**Why indexed.** Canon-tier bioenergetics: the Na/K pump is the literal energy-storing machine under all bioelectricity and a large fraction of the body's resting metabolic spend — foundation beneath neuronal excitability, membrane transport, and cellular ion homeostasis.

**Built on.** membrane physiology; the ATP-as-energy-currency framework

**Influenced / downstream.** Hodgkin-Huxley excitability, all of ion-transport physiology, neuronal bioenergetics

**Canon bridge.** canon-tier 05-biophysics

**Disputed / known unknowns.** None on the core discovery; later structural details (E1/E2 conformations, stoichiometry) refined over decades.
