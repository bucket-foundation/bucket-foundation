**Field contribution.** Coined 'aerobics,' created the 12-minute and 1.5-mile fitness tests, and built the Cooper Center Longitudinal Study whose data established cardiorespiratory fitness (VO2max-proxy) as a powerful independent predictor of all-cause and cardiovascular mortality.

**Why indexed.** Founded the quantification of aerobic fitness and the cohort evidence that cardiorespiratory fitness is among the strongest longevity predictors.

**Built on.** exercise physiology

**Influenced / downstream.** VO2max-mortality literature, Attia, Benjamin Levine
