**Field contribution.** Built organ-specific plasma proteomic aging clocks showing that different organs age at different rates within the same person and that an 'aged' organ predicts disease and mortality risk for that system.

**Why indexed.** Operationalized organ-specific biological age from a single blood draw, a frontier of personalized aging measurement.
