| | |
|---|---|

**Field contribution.** Developed a method combining cyclic hyperventilation breathing, cold exposure, and focus; subject of the Kox 2014 study showing trained practitioners can voluntarily blunt the inflammatory response to endotoxin — a rare case where a practitioner method generated a controlled mechanistic result.

**Why indexed.** The central practitioner node linking breath + cold + autonomic control; one method with one notable mechanistic study, otherwise anecdotal.

**Built on.** Tummo / pranayama traditions

**Influenced / downstream.** mass cold-exposure/breathwork adoption

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Outcome claims far exceed the single small mechanistic study; hyperventilation+water carries real drowning/blackout risk.
