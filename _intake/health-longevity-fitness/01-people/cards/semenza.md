**Field contribution.** Co-discovered HIF-1, the transcription-factor master switch by which cells sense and respond to oxygen availability — driving erythropoietin, angiogenesis (VEGF), and glycolytic metabolism when oxygen is low. Defined the oxygen-sensing axis at the molecular level.

**Why indexed.** Foundation-grade mechanism: oxygen sensing underlies altitude adaptation, ischemia, tumor biology, and exercise/hypoxia-conditioning claims; a canon-promotion candidate as the physical law of cellular oxygen homeostasis.

**Built on.** erythropoietin regulation; cellular oxygen physiology

**Influenced / downstream.** Kaelin (VHL), Ratcliffe (PHD/prolyl-hydroxylase), Beall/Simonson (Tibetan EPAS1 selection), HIF-stabilizer anemia drugs

**Canon bridge.** canon-adjacent 05-biophysics (cellular oxygen homeostasis)

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Therapeutic HIF modulation carries a cancer/angiogenesis double-edge; "intermittent hypoxia training" claims extrapolate beyond the validated mechanism.
