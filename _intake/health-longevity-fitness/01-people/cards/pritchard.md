**Field contribution.** Co-authored the omnigenic model — the argument that complex traits are influenced by essentially all genes expressed in relevant cells, with effects propagating through dense regulatory networks, so individual variants carry tiny effects. Earlier built foundational population-structure inference (STRUCTURE).

**Why indexed.** The theoretical reason single-gene "fitness gene / longevity gene / trait gene" claims have negligible individual predictive power — the spine of the argument against direct-to-consumer genetic over-interpretation.

**Built on.** GWAS-era heritability findings; quantitative-genetics theory (Fisher's infinitesimal model)

**Influenced / downstream.** polygenic-score interpretation and portability debates (Martin); critiques of DTC genetics

**Canon bridge.** canon-adjacent (genetic-architecture theory)

**Disputed / known unknowns.** "Omnigenic" is a model under active debate — the boundary between core and peripheral genes, and its mechanistic literalness, are contested.
