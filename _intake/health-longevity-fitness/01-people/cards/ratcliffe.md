**Field contribution.** Established the oxygen-sensing arm of the HIF pathway: the EGLN/PHD prolyl-hydroxylase enzymes use molecular oxygen as a substrate to tag HIF-alpha for VHL-mediated degradation in normoxia. When oxygen falls, hydroxylation stops and HIF accumulates — the direct chemical link between O2 concentration and the cell's transcriptional response.

**Why indexed.** Completes the molecular logic of oxygen sensing; the PHD/EGLN mechanism is the exact target of high-altitude natural selection in Tibetans and of HIF-stabilizer drugs for anemia.

**Built on.** Semenza's HIF discovery; Kaelin's VHL tumor-suppressor work

**Influenced / downstream.** HIF-prolyl-hydroxylase-inhibitor drug class; altitude-adaptation genetics

**Canon bridge.** canon-adjacent 05-biophysics (cellular oxygen homeostasis)

**Disputed / known unknowns.** As with Semenza, clinical HIF modulation is a balancing act between benefit (anemia, ischemia) and risk (tumor angiogenesis).
