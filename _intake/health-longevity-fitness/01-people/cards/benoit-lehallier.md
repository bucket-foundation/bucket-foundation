**Field contribution.** Showed the human plasma proteome changes in non-linear 'waves' across the lifespan (clustering near ages ~34, 60, 78) and built a proteomic aging clock from circulating proteins.

**Why indexed.** Established crests of proteomic aging and the plasma-proteome clock, a foundation of organ/blood-based aging measurement.
