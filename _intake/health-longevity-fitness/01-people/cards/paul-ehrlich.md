### Paul Ehrlich

| | |
|---|---|

**Field contribution.** Originated the principle of selective toxicity - the 'magic bullet' that harms the pathogen but not the host - and delivered the first targeted antimicrobial, Salvarsan for syphilis. Also founded receptor-based thinking in pharmacology. Won the 1908 Nobel.

**Why indexed.** Selective toxicity is the conceptual foundation under all antimicrobial and targeted-drug design - a foundation-tier idea, not a single compound.

**Built on.** Robert Koch (microbiology), dye chemistry

**Influenced / downstream.** all rational chemotherapy and targeted drug design
