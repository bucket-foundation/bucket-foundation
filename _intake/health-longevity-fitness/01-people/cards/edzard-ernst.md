**Field contribution.** The world's first professor of complementary medicine; spent his career grading CAM modalities (acupuncture, homeopathy, chiropractic, herbalism) honestly on the same evidence ladder as mainstream medicine.

**Why indexed.** The model for the corpus's 'the grade is the neutrality' doctrine - the definitive non-dismissive, non-credulous voice on alternative medicine.

**Built on.** evidence-based-medicine methodology

**Influenced / downstream.** evidence-graded CAM reviews; the skeptic-clinician tradition
