**Field contribution.** Discovered the TOR genes in yeast (1991) as the molecular targets of rapamycin, establishing TOR as the conserved master regulator of cell growth in response to nutrients — the discovery that mTOR/Sabatini work later extended to mammals. Lasker Award 2017.

**Why indexed.** Original discovery of the TOR pathway, the most-targeted longevity node; the yeast foundation under all mTOR geroscience.

**Built on.** rapamycin (Easter Island Streptomyces)

**Influenced / downstream.** Sabatini, entire mTOR field
