**Field contribution.** Observed that hay fever and eczema were inversely related to family size and birth order, and proposed the "hygiene hypothesis" — that reduced early-life infection/microbial exposure predisposes to allergic disease. The foundational (and later substantially revised) observation linking the modern microbial environment to immune dysregulation.

**Why indexed.** The origin of the entire immune-environment-mismatch literature in allergy and autoimmunity; the seed observation that Rook's "old friends" model and microbiome immunology later corrected and extended.

**Built on.** allergy epidemiology; family-cohort survey data

**Influenced / downstream.** Rook's old-friends/biodiversity reframing; microbiome-immune-education research

**Canon bridge.** immune-environment hypothesis lineage

**Disputed / known unknowns.** The original "infections protect" framing is now seen as too simple — protective exposure is to commensal/environmental microbiota, not childhood pathogens (Rook's correction).
