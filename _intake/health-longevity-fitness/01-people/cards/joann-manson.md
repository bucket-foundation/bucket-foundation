**Field contribution.** A principal investigator of the Women's Health Initiative and PI of VITAL - the large RCTs that established the age-dependent risk/benefit of hormone therapy and showed that vitamin D and fish-oil supplements do not prevent cancer, CVD, or fractures in replete populations.

**Why indexed.** The field's reality-check trialist: separates predictor from lever for two of the most-marketed supplement categories and for HRT.

**Built on.** large-cohort and large-RCT epidemiology

**Influenced / downstream.** vitamin-D/omega-3 deprescribing, the HRT-timing literature
