**Field contribution.** Proposed 'Anatomy Trains' — that muscles are functionally linked into continuous myofascial 'meridian' lines transmitting tension across the body — reframing movement and bodywork around fascial continuity rather than isolated muscles. A practitioner framework with partial anatomical support.

**Why indexed.** The dominant popular framework for fascia/myofascial-chain thinking in movement and manual therapy; indexed practitioner-n1.

**Built on.** Rolfing / structural integration, fascia anatomy

**Influenced / downstream.** movement/fascia training, Starrett milieu

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Specific 'meridian line' continuity and force-transmission claims are only partly supported by dissection/biomechanics evidence.
