### John B. Gurdon

| **primary works** | *The developmental capacity of nuclei taken from intestinal epithelium cells of feeding tadpoles (J Embryol Exp Morphol)* (1962) |

**Field contribution.** Showed by somatic-cell nuclear transfer in frogs that a differentiated cell's nucleus retains all the genetic information to make a whole organism - i.e. differentiation is reversible. Shared the 2012 Nobel with Yamanaka.

**Why indexed.** Reversibility of cell fate is the conceptual ancestor of iPSCs and the entire reprogramming/partial-reprogramming longevity bet.

**Built on.** embryology of nuclear potency

**Influenced / downstream.** Shinya Yamanaka (iPSCs), cloning, partial reprogramming
