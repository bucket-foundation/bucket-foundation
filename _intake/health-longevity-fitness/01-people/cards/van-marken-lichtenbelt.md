**Field contribution.** Co-authored the landmark demonstration that functional brown adipose tissue is present and cold-activatable in adult humans (PET-CT), reopening BAT as a metabolic target, and developed the concept of 'healthy thermal variability' against constant thermoneutral indoor living.

**Why indexed.** Provided the human evidence that adult brown fat exists and responds to cold — the mechanistic foundation under cold-exposure metabolic claims.

**Built on.** rodent BAT physiology

**Influenced / downstream.** Søberg, cold-thermogenesis field
