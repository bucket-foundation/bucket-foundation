### Joseph Lister

**Field contribution.** Applied germ theory to the operating room, introducing carbolic-acid antisepsis that turned frequently-lethal operations into survivable ones and collapsed post-surgical sepsis rates.

**Why indexed.** The translation of germ theory into surgical practice - a pillar of the sanitation revolution that drove the early-modern mortality decline.

**Built on.** Louis Pasteur (fermentation/germ theory)

**Influenced / downstream.** aseptic surgery, hospital infection control
