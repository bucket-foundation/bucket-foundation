**Field contribution.** Co-discovered telomerase, the enzyme that extends telomeres (chromosome-end caps), and characterized telomere structure — establishing telomere attrition as a hallmark of aging and a link between cellular replication limits and organismal aging. Nobel Prize 2009.

**Why indexed.** Telomere attrition is a canonical hallmark of aging; Blackburn discovered the enzyme that governs it.

**Built on.** McClintock (chromosome ends)

**Influenced / downstream.** telomere/aging field, Hayflick-limit mechanism

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Telomere-length-as-biomarker and psychosocial-stress→telomere claims (Telomere Effect) are weaker than the core enzymology.
