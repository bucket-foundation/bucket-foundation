**Field contribution.** Ran the foundational human sleep-restriction experiments showing that curtailed sleep impairs glucose tolerance, raises evening cortisol, and disrupts appetite hormones, establishing the causal sleep-to-metabolic-dysfunction link.

**Why indexed.** The origin of experimental evidence that sleep loss directly damages metabolic and endocrine function.

**Influenced / downstream.** sleep-metabolism field
