**Field contribution.** Quantified lumbar-spine loading and injury mechanics in the lab, defining spine-sparing movement principles, the role of core stiffness/bracing, and rehabilitation built on the 'McGill Big Three' (curl-up, side bridge, bird-dog) rather than spinal flexion.

**Why indexed.** The empirical foundation of modern spine biomechanics and evidence-based back-pain management.
