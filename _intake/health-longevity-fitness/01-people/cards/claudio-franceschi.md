**Field contribution.** Coined 'inflammaging', the chronic, low-grade, sterile inflammation that accompanies aging, and the related 'garb-aging' (accumulated molecular debris) concept, and built centenarian-biology cohorts to test them.

**Why indexed.** Inflammaging is one of the organizing concepts of mechanistic geroscience; indexed at the framework level.

**Influenced / downstream.** Elena Biagi, hallmarks-of-aging authors, microbiome-aging field
