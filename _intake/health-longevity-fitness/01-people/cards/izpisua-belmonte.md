**Field contribution.** Demonstrated that cyclic, partial expression of Yamanaka factors in living mice reverses signs of aging in tissues without erasing cell identity or causing teratomas — establishing 'partial reprogramming' as an in-vivo rejuvenation strategy. A scientific lead at Altos Labs.

**Why indexed.** Translated reprogramming from a dish to a whole organism, the proof-of-concept for in-vivo age reversal.

**Built on.** Yamanaka

**Influenced / downstream.** Altos Labs, Sinclair reprogramming work

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Durability, safety, and cancer risk of in-vivo reprogramming remain open; some Salk-era papers drew scrutiny.
