**Field contribution.** Ran head-to-head validations showing that consumer sleep-tracking devices estimate total sleep time reasonably but stage sleep (light/deep/REM) poorly against polysomnography, quantifying the gap between marketed and measured sleep metrics.

**Why indexed.** The reference validity check on consumer sleep wearables - the empirical caveat against over-trusting sleep-stage numbers from rings and watches.

**Built on.** polysomnography, actigraphy validation
