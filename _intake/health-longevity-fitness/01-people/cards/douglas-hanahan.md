**Field contribution.** Co-authored the Hallmarks of Cancer framework, the canonical organising scaffold that reduces cancer's complexity to a set of acquired capabilities (sustained proliferation, evasion of growth suppressors, replicative immortality, angiogenesis, invasion/metastasis, and later additions).

**Why indexed.** The Hallmarks framework is the conceptual periodic table of oncology and the explicit template on which the Hallmarks of Aging was modelled - canon-adjacent organizing theory.

**Built on.** decades of molecular oncogene/tumor-suppressor biology

**Influenced / downstream.** Hallmarks of Aging (Lopez-Otin), all hallmarks-style syntheses

**Tags.** `hallmarks-of-cancer` `oncology` `framework`
