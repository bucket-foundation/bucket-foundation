**Field contribution.** Co-architect of the Geometric Framework of Nutrition, which models how animals balance multiple nutrients, and co-led the mouse work showing lifespan is governed by the protein:carbohydrate ratio rather than total calories.

**Why indexed.** Reframed the diet-longevity question from 'how many calories' to 'what macronutrient balance', a foundational shift.

**Influenced / downstream.** Samantha Solon-Biet, David Le Couteur, macronutrient-geometry field
