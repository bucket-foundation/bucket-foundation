**Field contribution.** "Father of modern yoga." Fused medieval Hatha asana with gymnastic/physical-culture movement, breath-synchronized flow (vinyasa) and therapeutic individualization, then taught the students who exported yoga globally — B.K.S. Iyengar, K. Pattabhi Jois, T.K.V. Desikachar and Indra Devi. Nearly every globally practiced postural style traces to his Mysore school.

**Why indexed.** The single lineage node from which the two most-studied modern styles (Iyengar, Ashtanga) descend — the historical root under almost all yoga-as-medicine research interventions.

**Built on.** Hatha yoga tradition; (claimed) Himalayan teacher Ramamohana Brahmachari

**Influenced / downstream.** iyengar; pattabhi-jois; Desikachar; Indra Devi; global modern postural yoga
