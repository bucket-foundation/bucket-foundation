**Field contribution.** Dissected how rapamycin's benefits (mTORC1) separate from its side effects (mTORC2/insulin resistance), and showed that restricting specific dietary amino acids (branched-chain amino acids, especially isoleucine) — not just total protein — improves metabolic health and extends lifespan in mice, refining the protein-longevity debate.

**Why indexed.** Moved the protein/mTOR longevity question from 'how much protein' to 'which amino acids,' and clarified rapamycin's dual signalling.

**Built on.** Sabatini (mTOR), Longo/Bartke (protein-IGF)

**Influenced / downstream.** amino-acid-restriction field, rapalog dosing
