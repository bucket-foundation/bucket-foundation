**Field contribution.** Demonstrated heterochronic parabiosis — joining old and young mouse circulations rejuvenates old muscle/liver stem cells via systemic factors (Notch, TGF-beta) — and later argued the effect is mostly *dilution of old pro-aging factors* rather than youthful factors, via neutral blood-exchange experiments.

**Why indexed.** The founding modern parabiosis result and the source of the 'old blood is the problem' reframing that drives plasma-dilution therapeutics.

**Built on.** 19th-c. parabiosis, Thomas Rando

**Influenced / downstream.** Wyss-Coray, Amy Wagers, plasma dilution trials

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Young-factors vs. old-factor-dilution interpretation is unresolved; GDF11 rejuvenation claims (Wagers) are contested.
