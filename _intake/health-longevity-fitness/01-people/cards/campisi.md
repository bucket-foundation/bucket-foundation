**Field contribution.** Established that cellular senescence is antagonistically pleiotropic — tumor-suppressive early in life but pro-aging late — and characterized the senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP), the cocktail of inflammatory factors senescent cells secrete that drives tissue dysfunction and 'inflammaging'.

**Why indexed.** Turned senescence from a tissue-culture curiosity into a causal driver of aging and the rationale for the entire senolytics field.
