**Field contribution.** Showed that four transcription factors (Oct4, Sox2, Klf4, c-Myc — 'Yamanaka factors') can reprogram an adult somatic cell back to an embryonic-like pluripotent state, resetting its epigenetic and apparent biological age. Nobel Prize 2012.

**Why indexed.** Proved cellular age is reversible by epigenetic reprogramming — the mechanistic basis of the entire partial-reprogramming rejuvenation field (Altos, NewLimit).
