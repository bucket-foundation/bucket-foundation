### Moshe Feldenkrais

| | |
|---|---|

**Field contribution.** A physicist and judoka who created the Feldenkrais Method — slow, attention-based movement sequences that use the nervous system's capacity to relearn movement patterns to improve mobility, reduce pain, and reorganize motor control ('awareness through movement', 'functional integration').

**Why indexed.** A founding somatic-education method linking movement, attention, and neuroplasticity — distinct from strength/stretch paradigms.

**Built on.** judo, neurophysiology, motor learning

**Influenced / downstream.** somatics, movement-rehabilitation, Portal milieu
