**Field contribution.** Developed the euglycemic clamp (the gold-standard insulin-sensitivity measure) and articulated the 'ominous octet' model of type 2 diabetes - insulin resistance plus progressive multi-organ beta-cell failure.

**Why indexed.** The mechanistic spine of why type 2 diabetes is progressive and multi-organ; the measurement and the model under modern metabolic medicine.

**Built on.** insulin-action physiology

**Influenced / downstream.** SGLT2/GLP-1 era diabetes therapy, metabolic-aging research
