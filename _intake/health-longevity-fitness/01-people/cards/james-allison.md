### James P. Allison

| | |
|---|---|

| **primary works** | *Enhancement of antitumor immunity by CTLA-4 blockade (Science)* (1996) |

**Field contribution.** Showed that blocking the CTLA-4 inhibitory checkpoint releases T-cells to attack tumors - the founding insight of checkpoint-inhibitor immunotherapy (ipilimumab). Shared the 2018 Nobel with Tasuku Honjo.

**Why indexed.** Checkpoint blockade is a foundational, durable cancer therapy and a new mechanistic class (remove the brakes on immunity rather than poison the tumor).

**Built on.** tumor-immunology tradition

**Influenced / downstream.** Tasuku Honjo (PD-1), the checkpoint-inhibitor field
