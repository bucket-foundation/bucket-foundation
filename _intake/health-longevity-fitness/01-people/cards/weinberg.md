### Robert A. Weinberg

| | |
|---|---|

**Field contribution.** Co-authored the Hallmarks of Cancer framework — the organizing scaffold that reduced cancer's complexity to a set of acquired capabilities (sustained proliferation, evasion of growth suppressors and apoptosis, replicative immortality, angiogenesis, invasion/metastasis, and later metabolic and immune hallmarks). Earlier, isolated the first human oncogene (RAS) and the first tumor-suppressor gene (RB).

**Why indexed.** The conceptual periodic table of cancer, on which the Hallmarks of Aging was explicitly modeled; foundational to the genome-disease model of cancer and to how aging biology now frames its own hallmarks.

**Built on.** somatic-mutation theory of cancer; oncogene/tumor-suppressor genetics

**Influenced / downstream.** López-Otín's Hallmarks of Aging; all of modern molecular oncology

**Canon bridge.** canon-adjacent (framework-tier, parallels aging hallmarks)

**Disputed / known unknowns.** The hallmarks are a heuristic, not a law; critics note they can over-tidy a heterogeneous set of diseases.
