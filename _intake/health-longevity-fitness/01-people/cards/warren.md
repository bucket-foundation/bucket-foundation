**Field contribution.** Pathologist who first observed spiral bacteria (Helicobacter pylori) colonizing inflamed human gastric mucosa and linked them to chronic gastritis — the observation that, with Barry Marshall's culture and self-experiment, overturned the "stress and acid" dogma and reclassified peptic ulcer disease and much gastric cancer as infectious and curable with antibiotics.

**Why indexed.** A canonical case of a careful observation defeating an entrenched paradigm; reframed a major chronic disease as an infection and a downstream cancer cause.

**Built on.** gastric histopathology

**Influenced / downstream.** Marshall (causation proof); H. pylori eradication therapy; the infection-causes-cancer paradigm

**Canon bridge.** disease-atlas / infectious-causation

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Initial reception was deeply skeptical; the association was doubted until Marshall's self-infection and antibiotic-cure data settled causation.
