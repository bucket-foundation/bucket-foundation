| **primary works** | *Risks and benefits of estrogen plus progestin in healthy postmenopausal women (WHI, JAMA)* (2002) |

**Field contribution.** Led the Women's Health Initiative Writing Group whose primary result — that combined estrogen+progestin increased breast cancer, stroke, and venous thromboembolism without net cardiovascular benefit — halted that arm of the trial and abruptly collapsed worldwide hormone-replacement prescribing.

**Why indexed.** The single trial that overturned a generation of observational "HRT protects the heart" practice and became the field's reference case in observational-vs-randomized discordance and healthy-user bias.

**Built on.** observational HRT cohorts (Nurses' Health Study); the WHI randomized platform

**Influenced / downstream.** the menopausal-HRT "timing hypothesis" reappraisal (Manson, Hodis/ELITE, KEEPS); evidence-based menopause medicine

**Canon bridge.** women's-health / trial-vs-cohort method

**Disputed / known unknowns.** The original WHI framing under-weighted age/timing — later analyses show a more favorable risk-benefit for women starting HRT near menopause onset; the initial blanket interpretation is now seen as overcorrected.
