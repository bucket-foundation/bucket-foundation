**Field contribution.** Showed that periodic fasting and a calorie/protein-restricted 'fasting-mimicking diet' (FMD) lower IGF-1, trigger autophagy and stem-cell regeneration, and improve metabolic markers; commercialized the FMD as ProLon. A principal champion of the high-protein-shortens-lifespan side of the protein/mTOR conflict.

**Why indexed.** Translated fasting biology from yeast/mouse into a defined, tested human dietary protocol — and is a named party in the field's central protein conflict.

**Built on.** Clive McCay (CR), Cynthia Kenyon

**Influenced / downstream.** fasting-mimicking diet field

**Disputed / known unknowns.** High-protein-is-harmful position is contested by Attia/Galpin (muscle, elderly mortality); likely age-dependent. Commercial ProLon stake is a COI.
