**Field contribution.** Champion of the sirtuin/NAD+ axis of aging — resveratrol and NMN as sirtuin/NAD+ modulators — and of the 'information theory of aging,' which holds that aging is loss of epigenetic information, reversible by partial reprogramming. A prolific communicator and biotech founder.

**Why indexed.** The most prominent popularizer of the NAD+/sirtuin and epigenetic-reprogramming theses; indexed for influence, with claims flagged as contested.

**Built on.** Leonard Guarente, Yamanaka

**Influenced / downstream.** NMN/NAD+ supplement market, public longevity discourse

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Resveratrol/sirtuin mechanism (Sirtris, sold to GSK) heavily disputed; NMN human benefit unproven; 2024 'biological age reversal' and supplement claims drew strong criticism from peers (incl. Brenner, Kaeberlein).
