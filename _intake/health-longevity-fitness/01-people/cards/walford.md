**Field contribution.** A leading 20th-century caloric-restriction researcher who advanced the immunologic theory of aging and famously practiced and documented severe CR himself, including during the Biosphere 2 enclosure — bringing rodent CR longevity findings toward human self-experiment and public attention.

**Why indexed.** A pivotal bridge figure carrying caloric restriction from rodent labs toward human practice; indexed frontier-contested (his own severe-CR advocacy outran the human evidence).

**Built on.** McCay (CR)

**Influenced / downstream.** Fontana, CRONies/CR Society, Longo

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Severe-CR human longevity benefit never demonstrated; Walford himself died at 79 of ALS; Biosphere CR was confounded.
