**Field contribution.** Architect of the Copenhagen cohort Mendelian-randomization program, which used large genetically-characterized population samples to test causality for Lp(a), remnant cholesterol, triglycerides, and other lipid biomarkers, establishing a reusable template for distinguishing causal lipid exposures from confounded correlations.

**Why indexed.** Built the population-genetics machinery behind much of modern causal lipid epidemiology; senior author across the Copenhagen causal-biomarker literature.

**Built on.** genetic epidemiology methods
