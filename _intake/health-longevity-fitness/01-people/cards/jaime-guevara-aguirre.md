**Field contribution.** Followed an Ecuadorian cohort with Laron syndrome (growth-hormone-receptor deficiency, very low IGF-1) for decades, documenting near-absence of cancer and diabetes despite obesity, the human natural experiment in the IGF-1/protein-longevity debate.

**Why indexed.** The principal human evidence that low GH/IGF-1 signaling protects against age-related disease.
