### Alexander Fleming

**Field contribution.** Discovered penicillin (1928) when a Penicillium contaminant lysed staphylococci, opening the antibiotic era that made modern surgery, chemotherapy, and transplantation possible. Presciently warned that misuse would breed resistance. Shared the 1945 Nobel with Florey and Chain.

**Why indexed.** Penicillin is the origin point of the antibiotic era; Fleming's resistance warning is the founding statement of antimicrobial stewardship.

**Built on.** Paul Ehrlich (selective toxicity)

**Influenced / downstream.** Howard Florey, Ernst Chain, the antibiotic industry, AMR science
