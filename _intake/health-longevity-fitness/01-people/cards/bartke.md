**Field contribution.** Showed that growth-hormone-deficient (Ames, Snell) and GH-resistant (Laron) dwarf mice live dramatically longer than normal mice — the strongest single-gene mammalian longevity effect — establishing that reduced GH/IGF-1 signalling extends mammalian lifespan, mirroring human Laron-syndrome cancer/diabetes protection.

**Why indexed.** The mammalian proof that the GH/IGF-1 axis sets lifespan, anchoring the protein/IGF-1 longevity debate in mouse genetics.
