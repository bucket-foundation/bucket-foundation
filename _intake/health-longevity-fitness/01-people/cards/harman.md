**Field contribution.** Proposed the free-radical theory of aging (1956) and its mitochondrial variant (1972) — that accumulated oxidative damage from reactive oxygen species drives aging — the single most influential mechanistic aging theory of the 20th century and the rationale for the entire antioxidant field.

**Why indexed.** The foundational oxidative-damage theory of aging; indexed frontier-contested because the simple version is now substantially revised.

**Built on.** radiation chemistry

**Influenced / downstream.** antioxidant research, mitochondrial-aging field

**Disputed / known unknowns.** Antioxidant supplementation largely failed to extend lifespan; ROS now seen as also adaptive (mitohormesis); the simple 'oxidative damage = aging' version is considered insufficient.
