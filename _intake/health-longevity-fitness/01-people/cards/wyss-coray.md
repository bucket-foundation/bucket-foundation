**Field contribution.** Showed that young-mouse plasma improves old-brain function via heterochronic parabiosis, and built plasma-proteomic 'organ clocks' that estimate the biological age of individual organs from blood proteins, revealing that organs age at different rates within one person.

**Why indexed.** Bridged young-blood rejuvenation biology to a measurable, organ-specific aging readout in humans.
