### Claude Bouchard

| | |
|---|---|

| **primary works** | *Familial aggregation of VO2max response to training: HERITAGE (J Appl Physiol)* (1999); *Adverse metabolic response to regular exercise (PLoS ONE)* (2012) |

**Field contribution.** Founder of exercise-response-heritability science; the HERITAGE Family Study proved that the gains from an identical training program vary enormously between people (from ~0 to +40%+ VO2max) and are substantially heritable (~47%).

**Why indexed.** The empirical core of the responder/non-responder reframe - the reason 'exercise dose' must be individualized rather than prescribed uniformly.

**Built on.** exercise physiology, quantitative genetics

**Influenced / downstream.** precision-exercise-medicine, the non-response debate (Montero/Lundby)
