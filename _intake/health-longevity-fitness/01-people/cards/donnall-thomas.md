**Field contribution.** Pioneered allogeneic bone-marrow / hematopoietic stem-cell transplantation — establishing conditioning, donor matching (HLA), and management of graft-versus-host disease — turning a uniformly fatal set of leukemias and marrow failures into curable conditions.

**Why indexed.** The original, established, genuinely curative stem-cell therapy — the rigorous gold-standard contrast against which predatory "stem-cell clinic" marketing in the longevity space should be graded.

**Built on.** radiation hematology; HLA tissue-typing

**Influenced / downstream.** all of hematopoietic-cell transplantation; modern cellular and gene therapies (incl. ex-vivo CRISPR sickle-cell cures)

**Canon bridge.** disease-atlas / cell-therapy foundation

**Disputed / known unknowns.** None on the contribution; transplant carries real mortality risk, the honest boundary against casual "stem-cell" claims.
