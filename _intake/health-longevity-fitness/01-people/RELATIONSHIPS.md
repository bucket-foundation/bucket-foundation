## Key lineage clusters

- **Bioenergetics spine:** Krebs (TCA) -> Mitchell+Moyle (chemiosmosis) -> Margulis/Martin/Woese (endosymbiosis & origin of life) -> Nick Lane (synthesis) -> mitochondrial geroscience (Wallace, Trifunovic/Larsson). Kruse hangs heterodox claims off this spine.
- **NAD+/sirtuin tree:** Guarente -> Sinclair, Imai (sirtuins/NAD+); Brenner & (partly) Verdin = the empirical counterweight (NAD+ biochemistry debate).
- **mTOR tree:** Michael Hall (TOR, yeast) -> Sabatini (mTOR) -> Lamming; intersects nutrition geometry (Simpson/Le Couteur/Solon-Biet) and the protein conflict (Phillips/Layman/Bauer vs protein-restriction).
- **Insulin/IGF worm tree:** Kenyon (daf-2) -> Ruvkun (molecular identity), Murphy (DAF-16 targets); mammalian parallel Bartke; human parallel Guevara-Aguirre (Laron).
- **Senescence/telomere tree:** Hayflick (replicative senescence) -> Campisi (SASP) -> Kirkland (senolytics); Blackburn -> Greider (telomerase).
- **Parabiosis/young-blood tree:** Rando -> Conboy; Wyss-Coray -> Villeda, Lehallier, Oh; Wagers (GDF11 dispute = the field's open rivalry).
- **Epigenetic-clock tree:** Horvath -> Lu, Levine -> Higgins-Chen (PC-clocks); Belsky (DunedinPACE); Gladyshev/Ferrucci/Moqri (Biomarkers of Aging Consortium).
- **CR/fasting lineage:** McCay -> Walford -> Longo/Fontana/de Cabo; Kraus (CALERIE) -> Belsky; Varady (the within-field null).
- **Circadian/sleep tree:** Brainard & Czeisler (photoreception) -> Gooley; Roenneberg (chronotype/social jetlag); Nedergaard -> Iliff (glymphatic); Van Cauter (sleep-metabolism); Walker vs Guzey (claims critique).
- **Deuterium circle:** Somlyai -> Boros -> Que Collins / Roberts; popularized by Asprey, Greenfield, Kruse (all frontier-contested / practitioner tier).
