# Domain H — Thermal Stress & Hormesis

> **Status:** v0.1 (Wave 1) — 2026-06-27. Graded claim set; companion data in `H-claims.json` (10 claims).
> **Discipline:** heat/cold physiology + hormesis. The **outcome/application layer** — mechanisms resting
> on foundations (mitochondrial uncoupling/UCP1, proteostasis, redox) carry `canon_link` UP to
> `bucket-canon/05-biophysics/`.
>
> **The governing rule, and this domain is where it matters most:** *cold raises norepinephrine* is a
> **mechanism**, not the **outcome** "cold improves mood / focus / longevity." *Heat induces HSPs* is a
> **mechanism**, not "sauna extends lifespan." Almost all thermal-stress media collapses exactly these
> gaps. Tiers below keep them apart.

## How to read the tiers (descending rigor)
`cohort` > `rct` > `mechanistic` > `theoretical`. Structural fact: the **sauna-mortality data is the
strongest thermal-longevity evidence and it is `cohort` (one Finnish men's cohort), with no RCT.** The
cold-exposure literature is the inverse — rich `mechanistic` data (BAT, norepinephrine, thermogenesis) but
very thin on hard human OUTCOMES. Read that asymmetry honestly.

---

## 1. Heat / sauna — the mortality cohorts (Laukkanen / KIHD)

- **Laukkanen et al., JAMA Intern Med 2015** (`10.1001/jamainternmed.2014.8187`, **n=2,315 Finnish men,
  ~20y**): dose-dependent — **4-7 sauna sessions/week vs 1/week → all-cause mortality HR ~0.60, fatal-CVD
  HR ~0.50, sudden cardiac death HR ~0.37**. Longer sessions also lower risk. `cohort` / outcome.
- **Laukkanen et al., Age & Ageing 2016** (`10.1093/ageing/afw212`): same cohort — **dementia HR ~0.34,
  Alzheimer's HR ~0.35** (4-7x/week vs 1x). `cohort` / outcome.
- **Laukkanen & Kunutsor, Mayo Clin Proc 2018** (`10.1016/j.mayocp.2018.04.008`): mechanism review —
  sauna acutely raises HR/cardiac output, lowers BP, improves arterial compliance ("mimics moderate
  exercise"). `mechanistic`.

**Watch the gap (`conflict-sauna-healthy-user`):** all observational, one male cohort, no RCT.
**Healthy-user bias and reverse causation are not excluded** — sick men sauna less. The dose-response is
striking but causality is unproven. "Sauna 4x/week cuts your mortality 40%" is a *cohort association*, not
a demonstrated effect of doing more sauna.

## 2. Heat-shock proteins — the proteostasis mechanism

- **Périard, Racinais & Sawka, Scand J Med Sci Sports 2015** (`10.1111/sms.12408`): heat induces
  **HSPs (HSP70/90)** — molecular chaperones supporting proteostasis — and drives integrated heat
  acclimation (plasma-volume expansion, earlier sweating, lower thermal strain). `mechanistic`.
  UP-link to canon (**proteostasis**) and SIDEWAYS to Domain B's "loss of proteostasis" hallmark.

**The HSP→longevity leap is the canonical mechanism-laundering move in this domain.** HSP induction is
real; that it extends human healthspan is unproven. Tagged `mechanistic`, not `outcome`.

## 3. Cold — brown fat, norepinephrine, shivering vs non-shivering thermogenesis

The substrate (three independent 2009 NEJM papers — `mechanistic`):
- **van Marken Lichtenbelt et al.** (`10.1056/NEJMoa0808718`), **Cypess et al.** (`10.1056/NEJMoa0810780`),
  **Virtanen et al.** (`10.1056/NEJMoa0808949`): **functional cold-activated brown adipose tissue (BAT)
  exists in adult humans**, recruited by cold, inversely related to BMI/age. UP-link to canon (UCP1,
  mitochondrial uncoupling).

The acute mechanism (`mechanistic`):
- **Šrámek et al., Eur J Appl Physiol 2000** (`10.1007/s004210050065`): cold (14 °C) immersion raised
  **norepinephrine ~+530%**, dopamine ~+250%, and metabolic rate. **This is the single most-laundered
  claim in cold media** — it is a *mechanism*, NOT the *outcome* "cold makes you happier/sharper/live
  longer." Acute thermogenesis is dominated by **shivering**; non-shivering/BAT grows with acclimation.

Adaptive remodeling (`mechanistic`):
- **Søberg et al., Cell Rep Med 2021** (`10.1016/j.xcrm.2021.100408`): winter swimmers show enhanced
  cold-induced (non-shivering/BAT) thermogenesis — genuine adaptation. *Caveat:* small n, and swimmers
  also sauna (cold+heat bundled), complicating attribution.

The rare human OUTCOMES (`rct`, but small/surrogate):
- **Hanssen et al., Nat Med 2015** (`10.1038/nm.3891`): **10 days of mild cold acclimation improved
  insulin sensitivity ~43% in type-2 diabetics** (via muscle GLUT4, largely BAT-independent). Real
  outcome — but **prolonged mild cold (hours/day at ~14-15 °C), NOT the brief 'cold plunge'** that's
  popularly marketed. `rct` (surrogate).
- **Buijze et al., PLoS ONE 2016** (`10.1371/journal.pone.0161749`): **cold-shower RCT (n=3,018)** →
  ~29% less sickness-absence, but *no* change in sick-days-per-illness; self-reported, unblinded.
  Rare actual RCT; hard to interpret. `rct` (mixed).

**The honest cold summary:** strong `mechanistic` story (BAT, NE, thermogenesis), genuinely thin OUTCOME
evidence, and a systematic mismatch between the *protocol that has data* (long mild cold) and the
*protocol that's sold* (short intense plunges).

## 4. Hormesis — the unifying frame (and its hazard)

- Thermal stress, like exercise and fasting, is framed as a **hormetic stressor**: a sub-damaging dose
  triggers adaptive stress-response programs (HSPs, antioxidant/redox defenses, thermogenic remodeling)
  with net benefit; excess harms. `theoretical` / hypothesis (Calabrese; mitohormesis — Ristow).
  UP-link to canon (mitohormesis, redox signaling); SIDEWAYS to Domain B (free-radical/mitohormesis
  conflict) and Domain E (exercise as hormesis).

**The hazard:** hormesis risks becoming **unfalsifiable** if used to retro-explain any result. The human
beneficial-dose window for cold and heat is genuinely *unknown* — tagged `theoretical`, not `outcome`.
Contrast therapy (alternating hot/cold) inherits both the heat and cold evidence states; it has little
independent hard-outcome data and is not given its own outcome claim this wave.
