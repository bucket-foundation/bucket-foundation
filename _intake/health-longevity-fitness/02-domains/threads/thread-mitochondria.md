## What connects
The mitochondrion is the single object every other domain reaches up to. The chain:

**A (foundation, canon):** chemiosmosis (Mitchell, Nobel 1978) → proton-motive force → ATP synthase
as rotary motor → endosymbiotic origin (Margulis) → proton-gradient origin of life (Lane/Martin) →
mtDNA as a second genome (Wallace). These are *laws/derivations*, in
`canon-figures/05-biophysics.md` already (Mitchell, Margulis, Lane, Wallace, Szent-Györgyi).
→ **down to** →

**B (aging mechanisms):** "mitochondrial dysfunction" is a named Hallmark of Aging
(`mito-dysfunction-hallmark`); bioenergetic decline + ROS leak + impaired mitophagy. The
free-radical theory and its mitohormetic correction sit here.

**C (genetics):** mtDNA heteroplasmy + tissue-specific threshold (`wallace-2013-heteroplasmy-threshold`),
the POLG mutator mouse (`trifunovic-2004-mutator-mouse`, `kujoth-2005-mutator-apoptosis`), and somatic
mtDNA accumulation (`somatic-mtdna-heteroplasmy-accumulates-with-age`) — the genetic readout of
bioenergetic aging.

**D (metabolism):** ketone-body signaling (`bhb-signaling-metabolite`), metabolic flexibility /
substrate switching, AMPK energy sensing — all are mitochondrial-load phenomena. NAD+ (the ETC's
central redox cofactor) ties this to `thread-nad-redox.md`.

**E (exercise):** the cleanest *interventional* mitochondrial claim in the whole corpus —
endurance training ~doubles mitochondrial enzyme content (`exercise-mitochondrial-biogenesis-holloszy`,
Holloszy 1967); lactate shuttle + metabolic flexibility (`lactate-threshold-metabolic-flexibility-zone2`);
VO2max (`crf-vo2max-strongest-mortality-predictor`) is, physiologically, integrated mitochondrial
capacity — and it is the strongest longevity predictor in preventive medicine.

So: **the foundation that exercise, fasting, cold, and aging all act through is one and the same —
mitochondrial bioenergetics.** That convergence is *why* the bridge memo argues the bioenergetics
lineage is canon-tier.

## Where it's SOLID
- **Chemiosmosis** is settled, Nobel-tier law (1978). ATP synthase as a proton-driven rotary motor
  is structurally confirmed. This is bedrock, not hypothesis.
- **Exercise → mitochondrial biogenesis** (Holloszy) is mechanistically certain and the one place
  the foundation has a clean human-relevant interventional handle.
- **mtDNA is a distinct, maternally inherited, metabolism-specific genome** with real disease
  consequences (Wallace) — uncontested.
- **Mitochondrial dysfunction is a genuine correlate of aging** across tissues — the *phenomenon*
  is robust.

## Where it's HYPE / contested
- **Causality of mitochondrial damage in *normal* aging is unproven.** `conflict-mtdna-mutation-causality`:
  POLG mutator mice age prematurely, but at mutation loads far above what humans accumulate, and the
  phenotype runs through **apoptosis, not ROS** (Kujoth) — which undercuts the naive picture.
- **The free-radical / oxidative-damage theory is mostly resolved AGAINST its naive form**
  (`conflict-free-radical-theory`): antioxidant trials are null/harmful; low ROS are signals
  (mitohormesis), not just damage. "Mop up free radicals to slow aging" is the canonical wrong turn.
- **"Boost your mitochondria" supplements** (CoQ10, PQQ, urolithin-A, NAD precursors) ride this
  foundation's prestige far past the evidence — see `thread-nad-redox.md`.
- **Zone-2-is-uniquely-optimal-for-mitochondria** is an over-extrapolation
  (`conflict-zone2-optimal-mito`); HIIT also drives strong biogenesis.
