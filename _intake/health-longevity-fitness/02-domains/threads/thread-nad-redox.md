# Thread — NAD+ / Redox (the supplement-hype reality check)

> **Status:** v0.1 (Wave 1 cross-cut) — 2026-06-27. Weaves A/B/J claims by reference. This is the
> thread where a genuine FOUNDATION (redox biochemistry) is most aggressively laundered into an
> unproven OUTCOME (NAD-boosting supplements extend life). The gap is the whole story.

## What connects
NAD+ is simultaneously (1) the central redox cofactor of the electron-transport chain — pure
FOUNDATION — and (2) the substrate of sirtuins and the marketing hook of a supplement industry —
pure OUTCOME-layer hype. The thread spans:

- **A (biophysics, canon):** NAD+/NADH is the redox currency of oxidative phosphorylation — under
  Mitchell's chemiosmosis and the whole ETC (`canon-figures/05-biophysics.md`: Mitchell,
  Szent-Györgyi bioenergetics). Redox signaling is a foundation-candidate also touching
  Pollack/Ling structured-water and Szent-Györgyi's submolecular-biology programme.
- **B (aging):** NAD+ declines with age, lowering sirtuin activity (`sirtuins-nad-decline`); NAD
  precursors (NR) raise blood NAD+ ~60% in humans but move **no hard endpoint**
  (`nad-precursor-nr-human-surrogate`). The free-radical/mitohormesis question
  (`mito-dysfunction-hallmark`) is a redox question.
- **J (practitioners):** the N=1/supplement layer — Sinclair-style NAD-precursor + resveratrol
  stacks, sulforaphane/Nrf2 antioxidant induction (`rp-sulforaphane-mechanism`), omega-3
  (`rp-omega3-mixed`). This is where redox biochemistry becomes a product.

The shared root with `thread-mitochondria.md` (NAD+ runs the ETC) and `thread-hormesis.md`
(mitohormesis = redox signaling) is exact: **all three are facets of one redox-bioenergetics core.**

## The reality check (the point of this thread)
Three named conflicts converge on the same verdict — **the foundation is real, the supplement
outcome is not shown:**

1. **`conflict-nad-precursor-efficacy`** — NR/NMN reliably *raise NAD+* (surrogate moved) but **no
   powered hard-endpoint human RCT** shows a health/longevity benefit. Mechanism real, outcome
   unproven. The single most important claim in this thread.
2. **`conflict-resveratrol-sirtuin`** — the in-vitro "direct SIRT1 activator" finding was a
   **fluorophore assay artifact**; lifespan extension does **not** replicate in lean mammals. A
   textbook case of foundation-prestige laundering a failed outcome.
3. **`conflict-free-radical-theory`** — *mostly resolved against the naive version*: antioxidant
   RCT meta-analyses are null/harmful because **low ROS are beneficial signals (mitohormesis)**.
   "Take antioxidants to slow aging" is the wrong lesson from redox biology. Sulforaphane/Nrf2
   (`rp-sulforaphane-mechanism`) is robust *mechanism*, unproven *clinical outcome* — note it
   works by *inducing endogenous* defense (hormetic), not by direct radical-scavenging.

## Where it's SOLID
- **NAD+ as ETC redox cofactor** and its **age-related decline** — settled biochemistry.
- **NAD precursors raise blood NAD+** in humans — reproducible (it's the *surrogate* that's real).
- **Redox as signaling, not just damage** — the resolved, correct frame (mitohormesis).

## Where it's HYPE
- **NAD-boosting supplements extend healthspan/lifespan in humans** — **unproven**; only the
  surrogate moves. The flagship hype claim of the longevity-supplement market.
- **Resveratrol as a CR-mimetic sirtuin activator** — does not survive replication.
- **Antioxidant supplementation slows aging** — refuted/harmful at the meta level.
- These are exactly the claims `protocol-not-evidence-axiom` (J) exists to fence off: a
  practitioner's stack is provenance, not evidence.
