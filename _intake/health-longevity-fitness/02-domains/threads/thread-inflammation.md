# Thread — Inflammation / Inflammaging

> **Status:** v0.1 (Wave 1 cross-cut) — 2026-06-27. Weaves graded claims across B/C/D/E/I by
> reference. Inflammaging is the corpus's most-shared *integrative* mechanism — but also the most
> chronically ambiguous on cause-vs-consequence. Keep that tension front of mind.

## What connects
Chronic, sterile, low-grade inflammation — **"inflammaging"** (Franceschi) — is a 2023 Hallmark of
Aging and a hub that nearly every other domain feeds into and reads out of:

- **B (aging):** `inflammaging-franceschi` — IL-6 / CRP / TNF predict frailty and mortality
  (`cohort`); a named integrative hallmark. Cellular senescence drives it via the pro-inflammatory
  **SASP** (`senescence-accumulation-mechanism`) — the mechanistic source of much sterile
  inflammation; senolytics aim to cut it (`senolytics-extend-function-mouse`).
- **C (omics):** inflammaging is now *measurable* — the **iAge** deep-learning immune clock flags
  **CXCL9** as a dominant driver (`sayed-2021-iage-inflammatory-clock`), operationalizing the
  hallmark as a clock. The **dysbiosis → inflammaging** bridge runs through SCFA loss
  (`scfa-gut-barrier-anti-inflammatory-mechanism`, `biagi-2010-centenarian-gut-remodel`).
- **D (metabolism):** CR/FMD lower CRP and inflammatory markers (`calerie-cr-cardiometabolic-humans`,
  `fmd-human-risk-markers`); the seed-oil debate is fundamentally an inflammation argument
  (`omega6-oxlam-chd-hypothesis` vs `linoleic-biomarker-lower-cvd-mortality`); BHB blocks the NLRP3
  inflammasome (`bhb-signaling-metabolite`).
- **E (exercise):** contracting muscle is anti-inflammatory via **myokines**
  (`muscle-endocrine-organ-myokines`) — the mechanistic basis of exercise's systemic
  anti-inflammatory effect; this is the cleanest *interventional* lever on inflammaging.
- **I (sleep/stress):** sleep loss and chronic stress raise inflammatory tone via cortisol /
  allostatic load (`sleep-debt-metabolic-endocrine`, `allostatic-load-stress-mediators`).

## Where it's SOLID
- **Inflammatory markers (IL-6, CRP, TNF) predict frailty and mortality** — robust, replicated
  `cohort` evidence. The *association* is among the strongest in geroscience.
- **The SASP is a genuine mechanistic source** of sterile inflammation; senescent-cell clearance
  reduces it in mice.
- **Exercise lowers systemic inflammation** via myokine signaling — mechanistically grounded and
  the most actionable handle.
- **CXCL9-anchored iAge** turns a fuzzy concept into a measurable clock — a real methodological step.

## Where it's HYPE / contested
- **Cause vs consequence is unresolved across the board.** Is inflammaging a *driver* of aging or a
  *readout* of accumulated damage? `inflammaging-franceschi` itself flags this; the microbiome
  version is an explicit open conflict (`conflict-microbiome-cause-or-consequence` — dysbiosis
  drives inflammaging vs merely reflects host health/diet/drugs).
- **"Anti-inflammatory" supplements and diets** are sold on marker-moving (CRP down) without
  hard-endpoint proof. Lowering a biomarker ≠ extending healthspan.
- **Seed oils as a uniquely inflammatory driver** is not supported by higher-tier evidence
  (`conflict-seed-oils-linoleic-acid`); the OXLAM mechanism is narrative, the biomarker cohorts run
  the other way.
- **Senolytics' anti-inflammatory longevity story is `animal` + one tiny open-label human pilot**
  (`dq-ipf-first-in-human-pilot`) — strong mouse data, near-absent human outcomes.

## Open questions
1. Is inflammaging upstream (driver) or downstream (readout)? The whole thread hinges on this.
2. Does lowering an inflammatory clock (iAge/CXCL9) with an intervention move a hard endpoint —
   i.e. is it a *validated surrogate*? (ties to `biomarkers-of-aging-consortium-validation-gap`)
3. Which lever is causal: senescent-cell burden, gut barrier/SCFA, myokine tone, or sleep/stress —
   and do they share a final common inflammatory pathway?
