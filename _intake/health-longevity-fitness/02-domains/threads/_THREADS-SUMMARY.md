## The meta-pattern (why these six and not others)
Every thread is the same shape: a **real FOUNDATION mechanism** (redox/proton-motive bioenergetics,
melanopsin photoreception, vagal control, biphasic stress-response) that the outcome layer reaches
up to — and a **laundering gap** where that mechanism gets sold as a hard human outcome it hasn't
earned. The threads are where the corpus's governing rule (mechanism ≠ outcome; association ≠
causation) does the most work, because cross-domain claims are where conflation is easiest to hide.

Three of the six (`mitochondria`, `hormesis`, `nad-redox`) share a single root: **redox /
proton-motive bioenergetics.** That convergence is the empirical basis of `CANON-BRIDGE-PROPOSAL.md`,
which argues the bioenergetics lineage is the bridge from the inherited Kruse/biophysics foundations
to mainstream geroscience.
