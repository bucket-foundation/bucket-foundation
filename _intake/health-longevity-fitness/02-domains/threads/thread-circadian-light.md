## What connects
Light is the entry point through which a single mechanism — **non-visual photoreception via
melanopsin/ipRGCs entraining the SCN master clock** — propagates into sleep, metabolism, hormones,
and the corpus's heavy Kruse layer:

- **Sleep (I):** the SCN is entrained primarily by short-wavelength light
  (`light-melatonin-action-spectrum`, Brainard 2001); evening light suppresses melatonin and
  delays phase (`room-light-melatonin-suppression`, `evening-screen-light-circadian-delay`).
- **Metabolism (D):** meal timing is a *peripheral-clock* zeitgeber; early TRE improves insulin
  sensitivity weight-independently (`etrf-insulin-sensitivity-weight-independent`,
  `tre-mice-metabolic-protection`); circadian misalignment associates with obesity
  (`social-jetlag-obesity`). Circadian/light is the mechanism under "when you eat matters."
- **Hormones / stress (I→B):** cortisol rhythm, melatonin, and HPA-axis tone are clock outputs;
  allostatic load (`allostatic-load-stress-mediators`) and sleep-debt endocrine disruption
  (`sleep-debt-metabolic-endocrine`) are circadian-adjacent.
- **Biophysics foundation (A, canon-target):** melanopsin non-visual photoreception is a
  FOUNDATION-layer phenomenon (`I-claims.json` UP-link to `bucket-canon/05-biophysics/`,
  non-visual photoreception). This is the **shared mechanism that mainstream chronobiology and
  Jack Kruse's circadian-light thesis both rest on** — the most important agreement in the corpus.

## Mainstream ↔ Kruse: agree vs diverge
(Full version in `I-sleep-circadian.md §7`; summarized here as the thread's payload.)

**AGREE (mainstream evidence backs the Kruse-adjacent claim):**
- Light is the dominant zeitgeber for the SCN — photic entrainment via melanopsin/ipRGCs. Strong.
- Blue/short-wavelength light at night suppresses melatonin and disrupts timing. `mechanistic`/`rct`.
- Morning/daytime outdoor light aids entrainment + mood; circadian misalignment harms metabolism.

**DIVERGE (Kruse's claims exceed the evidence tier):**
- *Causal primacy of sunlight over food* — Kruse subordinates food timing to light far beyond the
  evidence; mainstream holds light = master for the *clock*, food = a *separate* peripheral
  zeitgeber. → `speculative`.
- *UV/IR/red light as broad systemic therapy* — narrow mainstream support only (Jeffery 670 nm
  retinal-mitochondria, `mechanistic`); systemic claims outrun it. → `speculative`.
- *"Non-native EMF" disrupting circadian/mitochondrial biology* — no mainstream support. → `speculative`.
- *Deuterium / structured-(EZ)-water coupling to circadian biology* — `speculative`; sits in canon
  `05-biophysics` as a foundation-candidate *under review*, not an established outcome.

## Where it's SOLID
- The **melanopsin/ipRGC → SCN → melatonin** mechanism is settled human science. The *spine* of the
  Kruse circadian thesis is independently validated — a genuinely citeable result for the canon.
- Evening-light melatonin suppression is reproducible (`rct`).

## Where it's HYPE
- **Blue-blocking glasses (the product)** are not validated — Cochrane 2023 found no clear benefit
  (`blue-blocking-lenses-no-clear-benefit`, `conflict-blue-blocking-glasses`). The *mechanism*
  (evening blue light is disruptive) is real; the *lens product* is the laundering. Behavioral
  light avoidance / dimming is the supported lever, not the eyewear.
- **TRE's circadian benefit beyond calories** is small and weight-independent at best
  (`conflict-tre-efficacy-vs-cr`); the popular late-16:8 is largely null.
- **UV/IR/melanin/water/nnEMF** Kruse extensions: `speculative` pending primary derivation — the
  exact set of claims the bridge memo and `LANDING-PROPOSAL` must decide canon-promotion vs parking.
