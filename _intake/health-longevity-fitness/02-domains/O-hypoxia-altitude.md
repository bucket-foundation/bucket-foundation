# Domain O — Hypoxia, Altitude & the HIF Stress Axis

> **Status:** v0.1 (Wave 5) — 2026-06-27. Graded claim set; companion data in `O-claims.json` (10 claims).
> **Discipline:** oxygen-sensing molecular biology + high-altitude physiology/genetics + hormesis.
> **Why this domain exists (the unbiased-completeness argument):** Domain G (breath) flagged it — hypoxia
> is the **adjacent hormetic stressor** to cold (Domain H), exercise (Domain E), and fasting (Domain D),
> sitting on the same hormesis cross-cutting thread, yet the longevity world barely indexes it. It is also
> the domain with the **deepest foundation**: oxygen-sensing via HIF won the **2019 Nobel Prize in
> Physiology/Medicine** (Kaelin, Ratcliffe, Semenza), and high-altitude human adaptation (EPAS1/HIF-2α in
> Tibetans) is one of the cleanest examples of recent natural selection in our species. The discipline this
> domain demands is **mechanism-vs-outcome**: HIF biology and altitude genetics are rock-solid `mechanistic`/
> `genetic` foundations; the leap to "hypoxia training extends human lifespan" is almost entirely unproven.

## How to read the tiers
The asymmetry is even sharper than thermal (Domain H): **the foundation is `meta`/`genetic`-strong, the
human longevity OUTCOME is near-absent.** HIF molecular biology = settled `mechanistic`. Tibetan EPAS1
selection = settled `genetic`/`cross-sectional`. Altitude-longevity epidemiology = `cohort` but heavily
confounded. Intermittent-hypoxia *clinical* outcomes = small/early `rct` on surrogates. "Hold your breath
to live longer" has essentially **no hard-outcome human evidence** — it is `mechanistic` at best.

## 1. HIF — the oxygen-sensing foundation (canon-grade)

- **Semenza & Wang, Mol Cell Biol 1992** (`10.1128/mcb.12.12.5447`): discovery of **HIF-1**, the
  hypoxia-inducible transcription factor — the master switch that, under low O2, drives **erythropoietin
  (EPO), VEGF/angiogenesis, glycolytic enzymes, and metabolic reprogramming.** The molecular basis of how
  every cell senses and responds to oxygen. `mechanistic` (foundation).
- The oxygen-sensing pathway (HIF stabilization vs prolyl-hydroxylase/VHL-mediated degradation in normoxia)
  earned the **2019 Nobel Prize** (Kaelin, Ratcliffe, Semenza). This is **canon-promotion candidate
  material** — an axiom-level mechanism, not an outcome claim. UP-link to `bucket-canon` (molecular biology
  of oxygen homeostasis).

**The honest framing:** HIF biology is *foundation*. Everything else in this domain is an **application**
that may or may not inherit a real human benefit from it. The mechanism being Nobel-grade does **not**
license any longevity protocol downstream.

## 2. High-altitude human adaptation — EPAS1/HIF-2α (the natural experiment)

- **Yi et al., Science 2010** (`10.1126/science.1190371`, 50 Tibetan exomes): **EPAS1 (HIF-2α) is the
  strongest signal of natural selection** distinguishing Tibetans from lowland Han — the most extreme
  allele-frequency difference yet found between human populations. `genetic` / mechanism.
- **Simonson et al., Science 2010** (`10.1126/science.1189406`): independent confirmation — **EPAS1 and
  EGLN1** under selection in Tibetans (EGLN1 = the prolyl-hydroxylase that degrades HIF). `genetic`.
- **Beall et al., PNAS 2010** (`10.1073/pnas.1002443107`): the **adaptive phenotype** — EPAS1 variants
  associate with **lower hemoglobin** at altitude. The Tibetan adaptation is to **avoid maladaptive
  polycythemia** (the Andean/chronic-mountain-sickness response), not to over-produce red cells. `genetic`/
  `cross-sectional`.

**The crucial nuance (anti-laundering):** Tibetan and Andean (EPAS1 vs different routes) and Ethiopian
high-altitude populations adapted *differently* — there is **no single "altitude gene = longevity" story.**
The Tibetan adaptation is about **tolerating chronic hypoxia without harm**, which is a different thing from
hypoxia *causing* longevity. These are `genetic` mechanism facts, not outcome claims about lifespan.

## 3. Altitude ↔ longevity epidemiology (the confounded cohort layer)

- Population studies report **lower cardiovascular/some-cause mortality at higher residential altitude**
  (e.g., Faeh et al., Circulation 2009 — Swiss National Cohort: lower CHD and stroke mortality per 1,000 m
  of altitude). `cohort` / outcome — **but heavily confounded.**

**Watch the gap (`conflict-altitude-longevity-confounding`):** residential altitude correlates with
**lower obesity, more physical activity (terrain), lower temperature, higher UV, different diet, migration/
healthy-mover selection, and air quality** — any of which could carry the mortality signal. Attributing it
to hypoxia per se is unwarranted. This is the most confounded `cohort` claim in the domain; the direction is
real, the *cause* is genuinely unknown. Mendelian-randomization on altitude is not feasible. Open conflict.

## 4. Intermittent hypoxia — the dose discipline (where it lives or dies)

- **Navarrete-Opazo & Mitchell, Am J Physiol Regul Integr Comp Physiol 2014** (`10.1152/ajpregu.00208.2014`):
  the governing principle — **intermittent hypoxia is dose-dependent and bidirectional.** *Low-dose* IH
  (mild, few brief cycles/day) drives beneficial plasticity (respiratory/motor neuroplasticity, vascular
  and metabolic adaptation); *high-dose* IH (severe, many cycles — as in **obstructive sleep apnea**) is
  pathological (hypertension, oxidative stress, cognitive/cardiovascular harm). `mechanistic`/review.

**This is the single most important claim in the domain.** The *same stimulus* (intermittent hypoxia) is
**therapeutic at low dose and the mechanism of OSA harm at high dose** — the purest hormesis illustration in
the corpus. Any "hypoxic training" claim that ignores dose is laundering. Cross-link Domain H (hormesis) and
Domain I (sleep — OSA is chronic pathological IH).

- **Intermittent hypoxia–hyperoxia training (IHHT)** in older adults: small early trials report improved
  exercise tolerance, body composition, and cognitive measures. `rct` but **small, surrogate, short** —
  promising signal, not established outcome. Tagged accordingly; the hard-endpoint trials don't exist yet.

## 5. Breath-hold / apnea training — real adaptation, no longevity outcome

- **Baković et al., J Appl Physiol 2003** (`10.1152/japplphysiol.00221.2003`): repeated maximal breath-holds
  trigger **splenic contraction**, transiently raising circulating hematocrit/O2-carrying capacity — a real,
  acute hematological adaptation (elite freedivers show enhanced splenic and apnea responses). `mechanistic`.
  Chronic apnea training also raises EPO transiently and improves CO2/hypoxia tolerance.

**The discipline:** these are genuine `mechanistic` adaptations (spleen, EPO, chemoreflex), **not** evidence
that breath-holding extends healthspan. And there's a **hard safety boundary** — hypoxic breath-holds,
especially near water (cross-link Domain G / Wim Hof shallow-water-blackout hazard), can be lethal. The
mechanism is real; the outcome is unproven; the risk is concrete.

## 6. "Live high, train low" and exercise-in-hypoxia — performance, not lifespan

- Altitude/hypoxic exposure for athletes (altitude tents, LHTL) improves erythropoietic and some endurance
  markers — a **performance** literature, not a longevity one. `mechanistic`/`rct` (performance surrogates).
  Indexed here for completeness with the explicit note that **performance ≠ healthspan**, and that the
  longevity transfer is unestablished. Cross-link Domain E (exercise).

## The honest summary of this domain
1. **The foundation is exceptional, the outcome is nearly empty.** HIF oxygen-sensing (Nobel 2019) and
   Tibetan EPAS1 selection are among the strongest `mechanistic`/`genetic` results in the entire corpus —
   and they say **nothing directly about extending human life via hypoxia.**
2. **Dose is everything (the hormesis core).** Identical intermittent hypoxia is **therapeutic at low dose
   and the pathological engine of sleep apnea at high dose.** Any protocol claim that omits dose is invalid.
3. **Altitude-longevity epidemiology is real but maximally confounded** — lower obesity, more movement, UV,
   migration selection all ride along; hypoxia-as-cause is unproven and probably unprovable observationally.
4. **Breath-hold/apnea adaptations are genuine (spleen, EPO, chemoreflex) but carry a hard safety ceiling
   and zero longevity-outcome evidence.** Mechanism real, outcome absent, risk concrete.

## Cross-links
- **UP to canon:** HIF/oxygen-sensing molecular biology (Nobel 2019), EPO/VEGF/glycolytic reprogramming → `bucket-canon/05-biophysics/` (or a molecular-biology branch) — **canon-promotion candidate**.
- **SIDEWAYS:** hormesis ↔ Domain H (thermal) & Domain D (fasting) & Domain E (exercise); pathological IH / OSA ↔ Domain I (sleep); CO2/chemoreflex & breath-hold safety ↔ Domain G (breath); altitude athletic performance ↔ Domain E.
- **CONFLICTS:** `conflict-altitude-longevity-confounding` (does altitude itself extend life, or is it confounding?).

## Gaps flagged for Wave 6
A hard-endpoint RCT of low-dose intermittent hypoxia in aging (cognition, cardiometabolic, function) — the
field's missing trial; whether any altitude-longevity signal survives full confounder adjustment / sibling
or migration designs; dose–response curves for "safe beneficial" vs "OSA-like harmful" IH in humans;
breath-hold training's chronic (not acute) physiology and its safety envelope; and the canon-promotion
decision for HIF oxygen-sensing as a foundation axiom (vs leaving it indexed as outcome-layer mechanism).
