# Canon Bridge Proposal — The Bioenergetics Lineage

> **Status:** v0.1 (Wave 1 finding) — 2026-06-27. Feeds `LANDING-PROPOSAL.md`.
> **Scope:** a *promotion* recommendation, not a decision. It argues which figures/concepts in the
> health-longevity-fitness corpus are FOUNDATION-tier (axioms, laws, primary derivations) and
> therefore candidates to land UP in `bucket-canon/05-biophysics` / `canon-figures/05-biophysics.md`,
> versus which are OUTCOME-tier and stay in this corpus. Final landing is the founder's call.
> **Companion:** `02-domains/threads/thread-mitochondria.md` (the through-line this memo formalizes).

---

## 1. The finding

The Wave-1 random walk surfaced a clean, load-bearing result: **a single lineage —
bioenergetics — is the bridge between the Kruse/biophysics foundations the corpus inherited and
mainstream geroscience.** Every actionable longevity domain reaches up to the same place:

- Exercise (E) → mitochondrial biogenesis, VO2max = integrated mitochondrial capacity.
- Metabolism (D) → ketone signaling, AMPK, metabolic flexibility = mitochondrial substrate load.
- Aging mechanisms (B) → "mitochondrial dysfunction" is a named Hallmark; the free-radical theory
  and its mitohormetic correction are redox stories.
- Genetics (C) → mtDNA heteroplasmy, the mutator mouse = the bioenergetic genome.
- Thermal (H) → UCP1 / mitochondrial uncoupling (BAT).

The point where they all land is **the proton-motive force across the mitochondrial membrane** —
Peter Mitchell's chemiosmosis. That is not an outcome claim. It is a *law*. So the corpus's own
structure forces the question the canon exists to answer: **which of the bioenergetics figures are
foundations, and therefore belong in canon, not in the outcome layer?**

Crucially, this is the bridge the corpus has been missing. The inherited biophysics layer is heavy
on the *contested* end (Kruse, structured water, biophotons, melanin, nnEMF). The bioenergetics
lineage is the *uncontested* spine that connects that layer to textbook mitochondrial medicine —
it lets Bucket hold a 05-biophysics branch that is rigorous at its core and explicit about its
frontier, rather than one that reads as fringe.

## 4. The principle this memo applies

> A contribution is **foundation-tier** if it is an axiom, law, primary derivation, or a structural
> identification that becomes the substrate of further work — and is **outcome-tier** if it is an
> application, association, biomarker, protocol, or clinical claim that *consumes* such a foundation.

By that test, chemiosmosis (Mitchell+Moyle), the Krebs cycle (Krebs), endosymbiosis (Margulis), the
vent origin of life (Lane+Martin), and the mtDNA genome (Wallace) are **laws and identifications** —
canon. VO2max-predicts-mortality, NAD-supplements, epigenetic clocks, and the free-radical theory
are **what the laws imply or fail to imply** — outcome layer. The bioenergetics lineage is the bridge
precisely because it sits on the *foundation* side of that line while every health domain sits on the
*outcome* side and reaches up to it.
