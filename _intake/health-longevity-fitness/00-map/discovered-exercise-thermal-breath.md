## Concepts / threads to expand (Wave 2 candidates)
- **PGC-1α** — master regulator of mitochondrial biogenesis (the molecular layer under Holloszy). Canon-bridge to biophysics.
- **Polarized training** — ~80% easy / 20% hard; the likely resolver of the Zone-2-vs-HIIT conflict (Seiler).
- **VO2max trainability / responder variance** — HERITAGE Family Study (Bouchard) — why fitness gains differ; bridges Domain C.
- **Rate of force development / power as a distinct mortality predictor** — Attia emphasis; separate from grip/strength.
- **Blood-flow-restriction (BFR) training** — hypertrophy at low load; surfaced incidentally (Patterson 2019).
- **Irisin controversy** — antibody/quantification disputes; the cautionary myokine.
- **UCP1 / mitochondrial uncoupling** — the thermogenesis molecular layer; canon-bridge.
- **Beige/brite adipocytes** — browning of white fat; the cold-adaptation cellular mechanism.
- **Tummo (g-tummo) meditation** — Tibetan heat-generating practice; predecessor/parallel to Wim Hof (Benson 1982; Kozhevnikov 2013).
- **Respiratory sinus arrhythmia (RSA) / resonance frequency (~0.1 Hz) / baroreflex** — the HRV-breathing coupling mechanism.
- **HRV biofeedback / resonance-frequency training** (Lehrer) — distinct from "just breathe slow"; has its own RCT base.
- **Hypoxia / intermittent hypoxic training / altitude** — adjacent hormetic stressor (breath-hold → hypoxia overlap).
- **Pre-Bötzinger complex + sigh reflex (Feldman/Yackle)** — neural basis of why sighing resets arousal.
- **Heat as exercise-mimetic / passive heating for cardiovascular health** (Brunt, Minson) — sauna↔exercise crossover trials. Wave-2.
- **Contrast therapy** — needs independent evidence; currently inherits heat+cold claims only.
- **Cold + hypertrophy timing** — resolved conflict; broader "when does cold help vs hurt recovery" question open.

## Cross-domain bridges noticed
- **Hormesis** explicitly links E (exercise) ↔ H (thermal) ↔ D (fasting) ↔ breath-holds (G hypoxia). The unifying frame.
- **Mitochondria/PGC-1α/UCP1** links E (biogenesis) ↔ H (thermogenesis) ↔ canon biophysics. Single molecular spine.
- **Autonomic balance / HRV** links G (breath) ↔ H (cold) ↔ Domain I (sleep/recovery) ↔ Domain L (wearables).
- **Adrenaline/norepinephrine** links H (cold) ↔ G (Wim Hof) — the shared acute-stress mediator (and the shared laundering risk).
