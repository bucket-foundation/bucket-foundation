## Concepts / threads to expand
- **SASP** (senescence-associated secretory phenotype) — the paracrine damage mechanism; link to inflammaging.
- **Mitohormesis** — low-ROS-as-signal; directly refutes naive free-radical theory → CONFLICTS.
- **"Information theory of aging"** (Sinclair) — aging = loss of epigenetic information, recoverable by reprogramming. Theoretical/animal.
- **Hyperfunction theory** (Blagosklonny) — aging = continued developmental programs (mTOR), not damage accumulation. Alternative to damage paradigm.
- **STACs** (sirtuin-activating compounds) — resveratrol → synthetic STACs; the assay-artifact controversy.
- **Geroscience hypothesis** — targeting aging itself prevents multiple diseases at once (TAME's premise).
- **Spermidine** (Eisenberg 2009) — autophagy inducer, cohort mortality signal; Wave-2 candidate claim.
- **Urolithin A** — mitophagy inducer, has human muscle RCTs; Wave-2 candidate (links mito + autophagy).
- **NMN vs NR** bioavailability debate; oral NMN tissue delivery question.
- **Parabiosis / young plasma** (Conboy, Wyss-Coray) — altered intercellular communication hallmark; GDF11 controversy. Wave-2.
- **Klotho** — anti-aging hormone; cognition/longevity associations. Wave-2.
- **Cellular reprogramming companies** — Altos Labs, NewLimit, Retro Biosciences, Turn Bio — industry node for Domain K.
- **PEARL trial** (rapamycin in humans) + **Dog Aging Project / TRIAD** (rapamycin in companion dogs) — trials to watch, Domain K.
- **Lifespan vs healthspan vs the "longevity dividend"** — framing concepts.
- **Antagonistic pleiotropy / disposable soma** (Williams, Kirkwood) — evolutionary theories of aging; foundational, belongs near canon.
