# Health · Longevity · Fitness — Ideal-State Research Map

> **Status:** v0.1 — written 2026-06-27, BEFORE the random walks begin.
> **Purpose:** Define the unbiased target shape of the field so research has a frontier to fill.
> This map is a *working draft, not a contract.* Branches may split, merge, or rename as
> material is absorbed. Where this corpus ultimately *lands* in Bucket is a deliverable
> (`LANDING-PROPOSAL.md`), not a decision made here.
>
> **Relationship to Bucket canon:** This is the **OUTCOME layer**. Bucket's `05-biophysics`
> branch holds the *foundations* (axioms, laws, primary derivations). Health/longevity/fitness
> are downstream *applications* of those foundations. Every claim here that rests on a canon
> axiom should cross-link up to `bucket-canon/05-biophysics/`.

## Operating principles (how "unbiased" is enforced)

1. **Index all, exclude nothing — but grade everything.** Mainstream RCT science and fringe
   N=1 biohacking both get indexed. The difference is the evidence tier attached, never
   omission. See `06-evidence/SCHEMA.md`.
2. **Provenance on every claim.** `[source + date + author]`. No floating assertions.
3. **Separate mechanism from outcome from protocol.** "Cold raises norepinephrine" (mechanism)
   ≠ "cold extends lifespan" (outcome claim) ≠ "11 min/week cold @ 14°C" (protocol). These
   get tagged differently and must not be silently conflated.
4. **Conflict is a feature.** Where experts disagree (protein & mTOR, NAD+ boosting, rapamycin
   dosing, seed oils), record the *disagreement* as a first-class object, not a resolved answer.
5. **Random-walk expansion is allowed and expected.** This map is a seed. Following citations,
   "people also mention," reference-mining transcripts, and lab→paper→author chains will surface
   nodes not listed here. New nodes get added to `FRONTIER.md` and the map is versioned up.

## The 12 domains (decomposition of the field)

Numbering is provisional. Each links down to `02-domains/<id>.md` once populated.

### A. Biophysical foundations *(bridge UP to bucket-canon/05-biophysics)*
The substrate. Already partially in the canon. Health track *consumes* this, doesn't re-derive it.
- Mitochondria, redox, electron transport, ATP, reactive oxygen species
- Structured/EZ water — fourth phase (Pollack) — *already a canon concept*
- Light & circadian biology — melanopsin, retinal, UV/IR/red light, non-visual photoreception
- Deuterium / deuterium-depleted water metabolism (Boros, Somlyai)
- Bioelectricity & morphogenetic fields (Becker, Levin)
- Quantum biology — coherence, tunneling, spin (Frohlich, Al-Khalili)
- Melanin as semiconductor / energy transduction
- Membrane & cell-water physics (Ling association-induction)

### B. Hallmarks & mechanisms of aging *(mainstream geroscience spine)*
- The Hallmarks of Aging (López-Otín 2013 → 12 hallmarks 2023): genomic instability,
  telomere attrition, epigenetic alterations, loss of proteostasis, disabled macroautophagy,
  deregulated nutrient sensing, mitochondrial dysfunction, cellular senescence, stem cell
  exhaustion, altered intercellular communication, chronic inflammation, dysbiosis
- Nutrient-sensing pathways: mTOR, AMPK, sirtuins/NAD+, IGF-1/insulin, FOXO
- Cellular senescence & senolytics (dasatinib+quercetin, fisetin)
- Autophagy (Ohsumi, Nobel 2016)
- Epigenetic reprogramming / partial Yamanaka factors
- Inflammaging & immunosenescence

### C. Genetics, epigenetics & -omics
- Longevity genetics: FOXO3, APOE, centenarian GWAS, daf-2 (C. elegans)
- Epigenetic clocks: Horvath, GrimAge, DunedinPACE, PhenoAge, methylation
- Metabolomics / proteomics / lipidomics aging signatures
- Microbiome & gut–everything axis
- Heteroplasmy & mitochondrial genetics (Wallace)

### D. Metabolic health & nutrition
- Insulin sensitivity, glucose regulation, CGM-driven N=1
- Protein intake ↔ mTOR ↔ longevity tradeoff *(major open conflict)*
- Caloric restriction, fasting, time-restricted eating, fasting-mimicking (Longo)
- Ketosis, metabolic flexibility
- Macro/micronutrient debates (seed oils, saturated fat, fiber) — record as conflicts

### E. Exercise physiology & strength
- Resistance training, hypertrophy, sarcopenia prevention, muscle as endocrine organ
- Cardiorespiratory fitness — VO2max as the single strongest longevity predictor
- Zone 2 training, lactate threshold, mitochondrial biogenesis from exercise
- HIIT, concurrent training, dose-response
- Power, grip strength, rate-of-force as mortality biomarkers

### F. Movement, mobility & flexibility *(media-heavy — see 03-movement-library)*
- Joint mobility, range of motion, controlled articular rotations (CARs / FRC)
- Flexibility vs mobility distinction, PNF, static/dynamic stretching
- Spine mechanics (McGill), hip/shoulder/ankle complexes
- Locomotion patterns, ground movement, gait, fascia
- Yoga — asana, lineages (Iyengar, Ashtanga, Hatha), measurable effects

### G. Breath
- Respiratory physiology, CO2 tolerance, Bohr effect, nasal vs mouth breathing
- Breathwork systems: Wim Hof, Buteyko, pranayama, box breathing, coherent breathing
- Effects on autonomic balance, HRV, blood pH, performance

### H. Thermal stress & hormesis
- Cold exposure / cold thermogenesis — brown fat, norepinephrine, shivering vs non-shivering
  thermogenesis (Kruse, Søberg, van Marken Lichtenbergh)
- Heat / sauna — Finnish cohort mortality data (Laukkanen), heat-shock proteins
- Hormesis as the unifying frame — what dose of stress is beneficial
- Contrast therapy, deliberate discomfort

### I. Sleep, circadian & recovery
- Sleep architecture, stages, glymphatic clearance (Walker, Nedergaard)
- Circadian alignment, light hygiene, meal timing, chronotype
- HRV, recovery, autonomic nervous system, parasympathetic tone
- Stress, cortisol, allostatic load

### J. Practitioners, protocols & N=1 systems
- Bryan Johnson — Blueprint (most-measured N=1; open data)
- Peter Attia — Medicine 3.0, "centenarian decathlon"
- Rhonda Patrick — micronutrients, sulforaphane, FoundMyFitness
- Andrew Huberman — protocol communication (note: communicator, grade primary sources)
- Jack Kruse — circadian/cold/light biophysics *(already in corpus)*
- Each: extract the protocol, separate it from the evidence, grade it

### K. Labs, institutions & funding
- Academic: Buck Institute, Salk, Harvard (Sinclair/Gladyshev), Stanford (Wyss-Coray),
  UW (Kaeberlein, Pollack), Berkeley (Conboy), Tufts (Levin), Albert Einstein (Barzilai), USC (Longo)
- Industry: Altos Labs, Calico, Retro Bio, NewLimit, BioAge, Unity, Loyal (dog aging)
- Nonprofit/advocacy: SENS/LEV Foundation, Methuselah, Hevolution, Astera
- Trials to watch: TAME (metformin), rapamycin trials, dog aging project

### L. Measurement & biomarkers
- VO2max, DEXA (body comp + bone), grip strength, gait speed
- Blood panels (ApoB, LDL-P, hsCRP, HbA1c, fasting insulin, IGF-1)
- Epigenetic age tests, glycan age, inflammatory age
- Wearables — HRV, sleep, CGM, the quantified-self data layer
- The "what to actually track" synthesis

## Cross-cutting threads (the connective tissue)

- **Hormesis** unifies thermal, exercise, fasting, hypoxia
- **Mitochondria** unifies biophysics, exercise, metabolism, aging
- **Circadian/light** unifies sleep, metabolism, Kruse-biophysics, hormones
- **Inflammation** unifies metabolic, aging, exercise, nutrition
- **NAD+/redox** unifies biophysics, aging mechanisms, supplements
- **Autonomic balance (HRV)** unifies breath, cold, sleep, stress
