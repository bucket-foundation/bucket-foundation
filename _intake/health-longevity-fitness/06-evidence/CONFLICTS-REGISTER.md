# Conflicts Register — the 29 open fights

These are the 29 fights scientists are still having about your health — places where confident advice on both sides outruns the evidence. Learn to spot them and you stop getting whiplash every time a headline flips. Each row is one dispute: the question, what each camp claims with its best evidence, and how settled it is.

**Total: 29 conflicts** — **15 still open**, **14 partly settled** (none fully closed; for most, no human longevity trial exists to settle them). Status legend is below the table.

A conflict only counts as settled when a top-tier evidence review resolves it — a meta-analysis (the strongest kind, pooling many trials at once) that qualifies or overturns one side. A practitioner's name is provenance, not evidence; both sides are recorded with their best evidence. The short tag after each side is how strong that evidence is, in plain words — "monkey studies", "human trial", "no trial yet", and so on.

## Status legend
- **open** (15): no top-tier review has resolved it; both sides stand. Usually because no human longevity trial exists or is even feasible.
- **partially-resolved** / **mostly-resolved** (14): a higher-quality source qualifies one side (dose, timing, context, definition) without fully closing it. The qualifier is in the status cell.
- A status is never "closed" — by design, conflicts stay first-class objects; resolution is recorded, not deleted.
