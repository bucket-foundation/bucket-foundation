# Heat / Sauna — Protocol Inventory

> Deliberate heat exposure (Domain H — thermal hormesis). Best cohort evidence in the thermal domain:
> Laukkanen KIHD Finnish cohort — sauna 4-7x/week associated with lower all-cause & cardiovascular
> mortality (`cohort` tier, healthy-user caveat). Mechanisms: heat-shock proteins, cardiovascular load
> mimicking exercise, growth hormone, eNOS. Lead figures: Jari Laukkanen, Rhonda Patrick (FoundMyFitness
> sauna review), Finnish sauna tradition.

| # | Protocol | What it trains / triggers | Key cues | Source / system | Candidate demo video |
|---|----------|---------------------------|----------|-----------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Finnish dry sauna session** | Heat-shock proteins, CV conditioning, relaxation | ~80-100°C, 15-20 min, hydrate, exit if dizzy, cool-down between rounds | Finnish tradition / Laukkanen | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ksACDxZezdU |
| 2 | **Laukkanen frequency protocol (4-7x/week)** | The dose tied to the mortality-reduction cohort signal | Longer sessions (>19 min) & higher frequency = stronger association; not causal | Laukkanen JAMA Intern Med 2015 `cohort` | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=explainer |
| 3 | **Sauna + cold contrast rounds** | Vascular pumping, hormetic stacking | Hot round → cold plunge/shower → rest; repeat 2-4 rounds; end per goal | Nordic tradition | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Iz0w7K0jZ8E |
| 4 | **Hydration + electrolyte protocol around heat** | Safety / performance of repeated heat exposure | Pre-hydrate, replace sodium/electrolytes, don't sauna fasted+dehydrated | Rhonda Patrick / FoundMyFitness | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jH8eXBKKD9k |

**High-value targets to download (3-4):** Finnish sauna session, sauna+cold contrast, hydration protocol. (Several sauna explainers are talking-head; prefer short demonstration/walk-through clips.)

**Gaps for Wave 3:** Laukkanen 2015 + 2018 primaries; Rhonda Patrick sauna meta-review; infrared vs traditional sauna distinction; heat-shock-protein mechanism papers; heat-acclimation for athletes. **Safety:** heat + alcohol, pregnancy, cardiac conditions, and sauna-after-intense-exercise dehydration risks must be flagged. Healthy-user/reverse-causation confound is the main critique of the cohort data.
