# Breath — Movement Inventory

> Breathwork systems & their autonomic/physiological targets (Domain G). Separate mechanism
> ("slow breathing raises HRV / vagal tone") from outcome ("breathwork extends healthspan").
> Lead figures: Wim Hof, Konstantin Buteyko, James Nestor (*Breath*), Patrick McKeown (Oxygen Advantage),
> Andrew Huberman (physiological sigh, Balban 2023 Cell Reports RCT — `rct` tier). CO2 tolerance / Bohr effect is the unifying mechanism.

| # | Technique | What it trains | Key cues | Source / system | Candidate demo video |
|---|-----------|----------------|----------|-----------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Box breathing (4-4-4-4)** | Autonomic balance, calm, focus; CO2 tolerance | Inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4; nasal; smooth | Navy SEAL / Mark Divine | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEmt1Znux58 |
| 2 | **Physiological sigh** | Fastest acute down-regulation; offloads CO2 | Double inhale (top-up) through nose, long full exhale through mouth | Huberman / Balban 2023 (Cell Reports) `rct` | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kSZKIupBUuc |
| 3 | **Wim Hof rounds** | Controlled hyperventilation + breath-hold; adrenaline/pH shift | 30-40 deep breaths, exhale-hold to urge, recovery hold 15s; **never in water/driving** | Wim Hof Method | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tybOi4hjZFQ |

**High-value targets to download (3-6):** Box breathing, physiological sigh, Wim Hof rounds, Buteyko, coherent breathing.

**Gaps for Wave 3:** Balban 2023 RCT primary (cyclic sighing > meditation for mood/HRV); Wim Hof immune-response study (Kox 2014 PNAS); pranayama overlap with yoga; nasal-breathing performance RCTs; CO2-tolerance training dosing. **Safety:** Wim Hof / breath-holds carry shallow-water-blackout risk — always flag.
