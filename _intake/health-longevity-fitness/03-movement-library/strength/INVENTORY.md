# Strength — Movement Inventory

> The fundamental human strength patterns: **squat, hinge, push, pull, carry** (+ lunge/single-leg, core/anti-rotation).
> Strength training is the best-evidenced movement domain for longevity: sarcopenia prevention,
> grip strength & muscle mass as mortality biomarkers (Domain E). Demonstration teachers: Mark Rippetoe
> (Starting Strength), Alan Thrall, Squat University (Aaron Horschig DPT), Jeff Nippard. Outcome claims here
> can reach `cohort`/`rct` tier — cross-link to 02-domains/E.

| # | Pattern | Movement | What it trains | Key cues | Source / teacher | Candidate demo video |
|---|---------|----------|----------------|----------|------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | Squat | **Back squat** | Knee-dominant lower body, quads/glutes, whole-body brace | Brace, knees track toes, hip crease below knee, neutral spine, drive through midfoot | Starting Strength / Squat University | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ultWZbUMPL8 |
| 2 | Hinge | **Conventional deadlift** | Hip-dominant posterior chain, hamstrings/glutes/back | Bar over midfoot, neutral spine, take slack out, push floor away, hips+shoulders rise together | Alan Thrall / Starting Strength | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wYREQkVtvEc |
| 3 | Push | **Push-up / overhead press** | Horizontal/vertical push, chest/shoulders/triceps | Push-up: rigid plank, elbows ~45°, full ROM. Press: braced, bar over midfoot, head through | Calisthenics / Starting Strength | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IODxDxX7oi4 |
| 4 | Pull | **Pull-up / row** | Vertical/horizontal pull, lats/upper back/biceps | Full hang, pull elbows to ribs, chest to bar, controlled lower; row: brace, retract scap | Calisthenics standard | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eGo4IYlbE5g |
| 5 | Carry | **Farmer's carry / loaded carry** | Grip, trunk stability, total-body work capacity | Tall posture, ribs down, brace, walk controlled, don't lean | Dan John / Stuart McGill | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Apz_required |
| 6 | Single-leg | **Split squat / lunge** | Unilateral leg strength, balance, hip stability | Torso tall, front knee tracks toe, drop back knee, drive through front heel | Squat University | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2C-uNgKwPLE |
| 7 | Core | **Plank / anti-rotation (Pallof)** | Trunk anti-extension/anti-rotation stiffness | Brace, neutral spine, glutes on; Pallof: resist the twist, press straight out | Stuart McGill (big-3) | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pSHjTRCQxIw |
| 8 | Hinge (KB) | **Kettlebell swing** | Explosive hip hinge, posterior chain power | Hike pass, snap hips, float arms, neutral spine, plank at top | Pavel / StrongFirst | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YSIQscxeweY |

**High-value targets to download (3-6):** Back squat, conventional deadlift, push-up, pull-up, farmer's carry, kettlebell swing.

**Gaps for Wave 3:** grip-strength→mortality primary studies; resistance-training dose-response RCTs; hypertrophy vs strength rep ranges (Domain E); McGill big-3 full series.
