# Yoga — Movement Inventory

> Asana practice across lineages (Hatha, Iyengar = alignment/props, Ashtanga = vinyasa sequence).
> Measurable effects: flexibility, balance, parasympathetic tone/HRV, stress (Domain F/I bridge).
> Grade physiological claims separately from tradition. Demonstration via Creative-Commons / open yoga channels
> (Yoga With Adriene, Fightmaster, DoYogaWithMe). Sanskrit + English names given.
