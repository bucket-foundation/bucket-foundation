# Mobility — Movement Inventory

> Mobility = active, controlled range of motion. Distinct from flexibility (passive ROM).
> Lead frameworks: Functional Range Conditioning (FRC, Andreo Spina), Kelly Starrett (The Ready State),
> GMB Fitness. Provenance on each = teacher/system; grade demonstration only as `anecdotal` unless a primary study is attached.

| # | Movement | What it trains | Key cues | Primary source / teacher | Candidate demo video |
|---|----------|----------------|----------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Shoulder CARs** (Controlled Articular Rotations) | Active rotational capacity of glenohumeral joint; joint health/articular tissue | Stand tall, brace core, move shoulder through largest pain-free circle, irradiate/keep tension, slow tempo | FRC / Andreo Spina | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XGsbHFV4xtg |
| 2 | **Hip CARs** | Active rotational capacity of hip | Stand on one leg, knee drives up→out→back→down, keep pelvis/spine still, max tension | FRC / Andreo Spina | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Hm4uemSGUk |
| 3 | **Spinal CARs / segmental cat-cow** | Segmental spinal control flexion↔extension | Move one segment at a time, breathe, no momentum | FRC / GMB | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K9bK0BwKFjs |
| 4 | **90/90 hip switch** | Internal + external hip rotation, hip transitions | Both knees 90°, tall spine, rotate from hips not lean, control the switch | Kelly Starrett / FRC | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jBdQg6QQYpw |
| 5 | **Shoulder dislocates (pass-throughs)** | Shoulder girdle flexibility + control | Wide grip on band/stick, straight arms, slow pass over head, narrow grip as you improve | GMB / gymnastics warmup | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mSCsbWCYg18 |
| 6 | **Deep squat (Asian) hold / prying** | Ankle dorsiflexion, hip flexion, end-range tissue tolerance | Feet flat, elbows pry knees out, chest tall, relax into bottom | Ido Portal / Kelly Starrett | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lhSZBNVMq0Q |
| 7 | **Ankle dorsiflexion (knee-to-wall)** | Ankle ROM for squat/gait | Knee tracks over toes to wall, heel down, find max distance | The Ready State | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8WSj0Ng2qY |
| 8 | **Thoracic spine rotation (open book)** | T-spine rotational mobility | Side-lying, top arm opens to floor behind, exhale into rotation, hips stacked | physio standard | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Et3o-z6mGw |
| 9 | **Wrist CARs / prep** | Wrist articular health for loading | Circle wrist under control, then loaded floor flexion/extension rocks | GMB wrist series | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uH_F2j2hN9Y |

**High-value targets to download (3-6):** Shoulder CARs, Hip CARs, 90/90 hip switch, deep squat hold, shoulder dislocates, ankle knee-to-wall.

**Gaps for Wave 3:** FRC PAILs/RAILs end-range isometrics; neck CARs; loaded-progression mobility; any primary-study evidence that CARs change passive ROM (currently `anecdotal`).
