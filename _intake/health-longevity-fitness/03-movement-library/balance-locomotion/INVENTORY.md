# Balance & Locomotion — Movement Inventory

> Ground movement, crawling, get-ups, gait, and balance drills (Domain F). Gait speed & balance
> (single-leg stand, sit-to-rise) are validated mortality/aging biomarkers — cross-link Domain L.
> Lead figures: Ido Portal (movement culture), GMB Fitness (locomotion), Original Strength (resets),
> Tim Anderson, get-up via StrongFirst (Turkish get-up). Frailty/fall-prevention is the longevity payoff.

| # | Movement | What it trains | Key cues | Source / teacher | Candidate demo video |
|---|----------|----------------|----------|------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Baby/leopard crawl** | Contralateral coordination, shoulder+hip stability, core | Knees hover, opposite hand/foot, slow, hips low, breathe; head neutral | GMB / Original Strength | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fa0pf1Hd5cw |
| 2 | **Turkish get-up** | Ground-to-stand strength, shoulder stability, full-body integration | Roll to elbow→hand→bridge→sweep leg→lunge→stand; eyes on weight; reverse same | StrongFirst / Pavel | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0bWRPC49-KI |
| 3 | **Sit-to-rise (floor get-up, no hands)** | Lower-body strength + balance + mobility composite (mortality biomarker) | Sit cross-legged to floor and back without hands/knees; score out of 10 | Brito 2012 (sit-rise test) `cohort` | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I7VGZUd9w3Y |
| 4 | **Single-leg balance / stand** | Proprioception, ankle/hip stability, fall prevention | Stand on one leg, hips level, soft knee, progress eyes-closed; 10s test biomarker | Araujo 2022 (10-s balance) `cohort` | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W9ASBp_Wfd4 |
| 5 | **Gait / walking drills (posture, arm swing, cadence)** | Efficient locomotion, gait-speed health marker | Tall posture, contralateral arm swing, midfoot strike, relaxed cadence | gait standard | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=breb2L5Gn-4 |

**High-value targets to download (3-6):** Baby crawl, Turkish get-up, sit-to-rise test, single-leg balance, bear crawl.

**Gaps for Wave 3:** Brito 2012 sit-rise & Araujo 2022 10-s balance primaries (both predict mortality — strong cohort tier); Ido Portal locomotion full system; vestibular/proprioception training; fall-prevention RCTs in elderly (Otago program).
