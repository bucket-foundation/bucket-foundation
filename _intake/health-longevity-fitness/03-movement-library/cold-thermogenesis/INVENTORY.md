# Cold Thermogenesis — Movement/Protocol Inventory

> Deliberate cold exposure (Domain H — thermal hormesis). Mechanisms: norepinephrine spike,
> brown-fat / non-shivering thermogenesis, cold-shock proteins, mood/dopamine. Outcome claims
> (fat loss, longevity, immunity) are weaker-tier than the mechanism. Lead figures: Wim Hof,
> Susanna Søberg (Søberg Principle: end on cold, ~11 min/week total), van Marken Lichtenbergh (brown fat),
> Jack Kruse (cold-EMF — `speculative`), Huberman (DCE protocols).

| # | Protocol / entry | What it trains / triggers | Key cues | Source / system | Candidate demo video |
|---|------------------|---------------------------|----------|-----------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Cold shower entry** | Acute NE spike, cold-shock adaptation, willpower | Start warm→cold last 1-3 min, control breath (no gasping), exhale into it | Wim Hof / Huberman | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pq6WHJzOkno |
| 2 | **Cold plunge / ice bath entry** | Brown-fat activation, NE, mood; non-shivering thermogenesis | Calm controlled breathing, submerge to shoulders, ~50-59°F, **never alone, time-capped** | Søberg / Huberman | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b3qTGHwQTGk |
| 3 | **Søberg Principle (end on cold)** | Maximize brown-fat adaptation, avoid blunting | ~11 min/week total cold, do NOT warm up actively after (let body rewarm) | Susanna Søberg | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4dG6Wbf3aQk |
| 4 | **Breath control during cold (no-gasp)** | Override cold-shock gasp reflex; vagal control | Long slow exhales, relax shoulders/face, don't hyperventilate in water | Wim Hof Method | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lFNNqd2RR_o |
| 5 | **Contrast therapy (hot↔cold)** | Vascular pumping, recovery (mechanism contested) | Alternate sauna/hot and cold plunge; end on cold for adaptation, hot for relaxation | hormesis / recovery | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Iz0w7K0jZ8E |

**High-value targets to download (3-5):** Cold shower entry, cold plunge entry, Søberg principle explainer, breath control in cold.

**Gaps for Wave 3:** Søberg 2021 (Cell Reports Medicine) primary; brown-fat PET-CT studies (van Marken Lichtenbergh 2009 NEJM); the cold-after-resistance-training blunts-hypertrophy conflict (record as conflict); Kruse cold-EMF claims tagged `speculative`. **Safety:** cold-water immersion carries cardiac + drowning + afterdrop risk — never alone, never combined with breath-hold hyperventilation.
