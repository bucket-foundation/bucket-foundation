# Flexibility — Movement Inventory

> Flexibility = passive range of motion / tissue extensibility. Methods: static, PNF
> (proprioceptive neuromuscular facilitation: contract-relax), dynamic, loaded stretching,
> FRC PAILs/RAILs. Lead sources: Kit Laughlin (Stretch Therapy), Tom Merrick, Emmet Louis,
> Kelly Starrett. Most claims `anecdotal`/`mechanistic`; flexibility→performance/longevity links are weak-tier.

| # | Movement | What it trains | Key cues | Primary source / teacher | Candidate demo video |
|---|----------|----------------|----------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Couch stretch** | Hip flexor + quad / rectus femoris length | Rear shin up wall, posterior pelvic tilt, squeeze glute, ribs down, breathe | Kelly Starrett (The Ready State) | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2K2zV8h3aE |
| 2 | **Pancake (seated wide-leg fold)** | Hip flexion + adductor length | Legs wide, hinge from hips anteriorly tilt pelvis, flat back first then round | Tom Merrick / Emmet Louis | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6dXp8a2YlVU |
| 3 | **PNF contract-relax hamstring** | Hamstring extensibility via autogenic inhibition | Stretch to end range, isometric push 5-10s, relax, gain new range, repeat | Kit Laughlin / standard PNF | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6n2Hn4qf0Q0 |
| 4 | **Front split progression (hip flexor + hamstring)** | Sagittal split ROM | Square hips, back-leg hip flexor lengthen, front hamstring, support on blocks | Emmet Louis / Tom Merrick | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q9d9p9q5b0M |
| 5 | **Pike / standing forward fold (jefferson curl loaded)** | Posterior chain length under load | Slow segmental spinal flexion with light weight, reverse segment by segment | Ido Portal / Christopher Sommer (GST) | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q9F-l8nO8gA |
| 6 | **Butterfly / seated groin** | Adductor + hip external rotation | Soles together, hinge from hips, elbows can guide knees, no bounce | Kit Laughlin | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y3aZ8C7zDfY |
| 7 | **Shoulder/chest doorway stretch** | Pec major/minor + anterior shoulder | Forearm on frame, step through, rotate torso away | physio standard | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SV7w8s8w8wA |

**High-value targets to download (3-6):** Couch stretch, pancake, PNF hamstring, butterfly/groin, Jefferson curl.

**Gaps for Wave 3:** loaded-stretching evidence (eccentric ROM); time-under-tension dosing; the static-stretch-before-lifting performance debate (record as conflict); bridge/backbend progressions.
