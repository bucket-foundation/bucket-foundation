# Movement Library — Safety Flags

> Built 2026-06-27 (Wave 3). First-class safety object for the movement library. Every
> demonstration in a flagged category MUST surface the relevant warning when exported to Bucket
> canon or any downstream product. These are hard contraindications and risk-of-death scenarios,
> not soft "consult your doctor" boilerplate. Severity: 🔴 can kill · 🟠 serious harm · 🟡 caution.

## 🔴 Breath-holds + water — shallow-water blackout
- **Movements:** `breath/wim-hof-rounds`, `breath/box-breathing` (long holds), `cold-thermogenesis/breath-control-cold`, any breath-hold practice.
- **Rule:** **NEVER do Wim Hof breathing, hyperventilation, or breath-holds in or near water** (pool, bath, ocean, plunge) or while driving.
- **Why:** Hyperventilation lowers CO2, suppressing the urge-to-breathe trigger. You can black out from hypoxia with no warning and drown silently. Deaths are documented.
- **Source basis:** standard freediving/shallow-water-blackout physiology; Kox 2014 (`10.1073/pnas.1322174111`) studied WHM in a controlled clinical setting, NOT in water.
- **Demonstration requirement:** the video and its caption must show the practice done seated/lying on dry land.

## 🔴 Cold-water immersion — cardiac, cold-shock, afterdrop, drowning
- **Movements:** `cold-thermogenesis/cold-plunge-entry`, `cold-shower-entry`, `contrast-therapy`.
- **Rules:**
  - **Never plunge alone.** Time-cap exposure (the Soberg dose is ~11 min/week *total*, not per session).
  - **Never combine cold immersion with prior hyperventilation/breath-holds** (compounds blackout + cardiac risk).
  - **Cardiac caution:** the cold-shock response spikes heart rate, blood pressure, and triggers gasp + arrhythmia risk. Contraindicated/clear-with-clinician for known cardiac disease, arrhythmia, uncontrolled hypertension, Raynaud's.
  - **Afterdrop:** core temperature keeps falling after you exit; rewarm passively, do not re-enter cold, watch for confusion/shivering cessation (hypothermia signs).
- **Why:** cold-shock gasp reflex → aspiration/drowning; sudden sympathetic surge → cardiac events; afterdrop → hypothermia.
- **Source basis:** Sramek 2000 (`10.1007/s004210050065`) cold-shock physiology; Buijze 2016 cold-shower RCT (`10.1371/journal.pone.0161749`) used *brief* showers, not prolonged immersion.

## 🟠 Heat / sauna — dehydration, cardiac, pregnancy, alcohol
- **Movements:** `heat-sauna/finnish-sauna`, `laukkanen-frequency`, `sauna-cold-contrast`, `hydration-protocol`.
- **Rules:**
  - **No alcohol before/during sauna** — combines vasodilation + dehydration + arrhythmia + impaired thermoregulation; implicated in sauna deaths.
  - **Pregnancy:** avoid high-heat sauna/hot tubs, especially first trimester (neural-tube/hyperthermia risk).
  - **Cardiac:** unstable angina, recent MI, severe aortic stenosis → avoid; otherwise clear with clinician.
  - **Hydration:** pre-hydrate, replace electrolytes; do **not** sauna fasted + dehydrated. Exit immediately if dizzy/nauseous.
  - **Sauna immediately after intense exercise** compounds dehydration/orthostatic risk — cool and rehydrate first.
- **Source basis:** Laukkanen cohort (`10.1001/jamainternmed.2014.8187`) is observational and on a healthy Finnish population accustomed to sauna; healthy-user confound means the safety envelope is not transferable to deconditioned/cardiac individuals without caution.

## 🟠 Loaded-spine movements — flexion under load, bracing
- **Movements:** `strength/deadlift`, `flexibility/jefferson-curl`, `strength/kettlebell-swing`, `mobility/spinal-cars`.
- **Rules:**
  - Maintain a braced, neutral spine under heavy load (deadlift, swing). Loaded *end-range flexion* (Jefferson curl) must start with **very light** load and progress slowly — it is a mobility tool, not a max-strength lift.
  - Contraindicated/modify for acute disc pathology, spondylolysis, osteoporosis (fracture risk on loaded flexion).
  - The McGill big-3 (`mcgill-big3-back-stability`) exist precisely to build spinal endurance *before* loading — sequence accordingly.
- **Why:** loaded flexion + shear is the classic disc-herniation mechanism; bracing failure under heavy hinge load is the common injury path.

## 🟡 Hyperventilation-based breathwork — fainting / falls
- **Movements:** `breath/wim-hof-rounds`, any controlled-hyperventilation round.
- **Rule:** always seated or lying down on land; never standing, never driving, never in water (see 🔴 above). Light-headedness/tingling is expected; loss of consciousness is a fall/aspiration risk.

## 🟡 Aggressive stretching / PNF / splits — strain, joint instability
- **Movements:** `flexibility/pnf-hamstring`, `front-split`, `pancake-stretch`, `mobility/shoulder-dislocates`.
- **Rules:** no ballistic bouncing; PNF contractions sub-maximal; warm tissue first; hypermobile individuals should build *strength* at end-range (FRC PAILs/RAILs), not chase passive range. Stop at sharp/nerve pain (tingling/numbness = nerve, not stretch).

## Recorded conflicts (first-class, unresolved — not yet adjudicated)
These are *evidence conflicts*, distinct from safety flags. Carry them as objects, do not silently pick a side.
1. **Static stretching before lifting** — may transiently reduce force/power output (perf debate). Demonstration tier; record both positions.
2. **Cold-water immersion after resistance training** — repeated post-workout cold may blunt hypertrophy/strength adaptation. Mechanistic + small RCTs; relevant to `cold-thermogenesis` ↔ `strength` sequencing.
3. **Infrared vs traditional (convective) sauna** — the Laukkanen mortality cohort is on *traditional* Finnish sauna; infrared evidence does not inherit it.
4. **Contrast therapy (hot↔cold) for recovery** — popular but recovery benefit is contested in the literature.

## Export contract
When any movement from a flagged category is surfaced in Bucket canon, a product, or an agent
response, the matching safety flag above must travel with it. Cold + breath-hold combinations are
