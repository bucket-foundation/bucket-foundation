## Prose synthesis

**The pattern is "right answer, oversold confidence," not error.** Where Patrick and Galpin diverge
from the corpus, they trip exactly the two honesty rules `01-STATE-OF-THE-FIELD.md` is built around:

- **Predictor → lever.** The omega-3 index (#1) and vitamin D (#5) are the textbook cases. Both are
  genuine *risk predictors* (low index / low 25-OH-D track higher mortality) that the corpus grades
  `cohort`/`rct` as **CONTEXT-ONLY** because the RCTs that *raised* the marker in replete people
  (VITAL) came back null. Patrick presents the predictor as if raising it were a proven lever — the
  single most common laundering gap in the whole field. Her vitamin-D dose (5,000 IU/d, blanket)
  also sits above the corpus's treat-to-level 800-2,000 IU maintenance.

- **Mechanism → outcome.** Sulforaphane (#7) is graded `rp-sulforaphane-mechanism` — Nrf2 induction
  is robust, human hard-endpoint cancer data are thin; she cites observational % cancer reductions as
  if established. The DO-HEALTH "omega-3 slows biological aging" claim (#4) is the subtlest: the
  *falls/infection* RCT signal is real, but the **epigenetic-clock slowing she leans on is not a
  validated surrogate** (`biological-age-tests-not-validated-surrogate`; `conflict-which-clock-is-valid`,
  open). A clock moving is not an outcome.

**Sauna is the cleanest "data agrees, causality oversold" case (#9-11).** Patrick's numbers reproduce
the KIHD cohort *exactly* (4-7×/wk → HR ~0.60 all-cause, ~0.50 fatal-CVD, ~0.34 dementia). The corpus
carries those same numbers — and three open conflicts she rarely flags: healthy-user bias in a single
male cohort with no RCT (`conflict-sauna-healthy-user`), "sauna mimics exercise" overreach
(`sauna-cardiovascular-physiology`), and infrared ≠ traditional (`conflict-infrared-vs-traditional-sauna`).

**Galpin is the most corpus-aligned voice in this wave — arguably in any wave so far.** Every
strength/creatine/protein/hydration/recovery claim he makes maps to a `REAL`-graded or
already-named corpus entry, including his own hydration equation (graded `anecdotal` but "usable").
His recovery teaching (don't ice straight after hypertrophy work; #23) lands squarely on
`conflict-cold-after-resistance`. The only place he edges into corpus open-conflict territory is
"Zone 2 uniquely optimal for mitochondria" (`conflict-zone2-optimal-mito`) and the high end of his
protein range vs the 1.6 g/kg plateau — both minor.

**Net for canon:** this cluster *strengthens existing grades rather than challenging them.* No claim
forces a regrade or a new conflict object. The corpus already anticipated both speakers (dedicated
`rp-` claims; Galpin named in two). Recommended intake actions are additive only: (a) a graded entry
for **Bruce Ames' triage theory / "longevity vitamins"** (#16) — currently absent; (b) optionally a
`rp-vitamin-d-dose` claim capturing the 5,000 IU blanket-dose overshoot vs treat-to-level; (c) note
the omega-3/DO-HEALTH epigenetic-aging framing under `conflict-which-clock-is-valid` as a
contemporary example of clock-as-outcome marketing.
