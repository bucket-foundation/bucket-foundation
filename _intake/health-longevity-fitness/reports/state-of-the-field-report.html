Abstract & reading instructions

This report distills a 247-claim research corpus on human healthspan into a single intellectually honest map. Its central finding is structural, not a discovery: the field's evidence is lopsidedly distributed against its attention. The interventions with the largest, most replicated effects are behavioral and dull (don't smoke; build fitness and strength; move; sleep; keep apoB low; stay connected). The interventions that command the market and the podcast hours (NAD⁺, senolytics, cold plunges, glucose monitors, biological-age clocks) are supported mostly by mechanism and mice. The report's job is to keep those two categories from being confused.

The three epistemic rules that govern every claim below (1) Predictor ≠ lever. A biomarker that predicts death is not automatically something that, when changed, prevents it. (2) Cohort ≠ RCT. You cannot randomize fitness, sleep, or smoking across decades; healthy-user and reverse-causation bias inflate the strongest-looking numbers. (3) "Something beats nothing" is the most robust signal in the corpus — the steepest gains sit at the low end of every dose-response curve; optimization past that is real but smaller and noisier than it is marketed.

Every assertion resolves to a graded entry of the form claim-id → 02-domains/*-claims.json (effect size, population, tier, DOI, confidence note). Disagreements are first-class objects in 06-evidence/CONFLICTS.md. Tiering follows a ten-rung evidence ladder from meta (systematic review of RCTs) down through rct · cohort · mechanistic · animal · in-vitro · n=1 · anecdotal to speculative. The corpus is the substrate; this document is the read-out.

The asymmetry is the whole story: strong interventions, weak evidence tier; weak interventions, strong marketing. Longevity is a field where the best things to do are proven by observation and the most-sold things are proven by mechanism.

I. What the evidence actually supports — the high-confidence levers

The honest ranking is dull on purpose. These eight individually outrank every exotic intervention in the corpus, and they are mutually reinforcing. A program that does not start here is optimizing noise.

  1. Don't smoke. The single largest modifiable mortality factor. A quietly devastating corollary from longevity genetics: the celebrated "longevity genes" of lifespan GWAS are largely smoking-behavior and cardiometabolic genes — the heritability of long life is substantially the heritability of not doing the obviously harmful thing. timmers-2019-parental-lifespan-gwas
  2. Build and keep cardiorespiratory fitness (VO₂max). The strongest exercise–mortality relationship in preventive medicine: roughly 5× lower all-cause mortality elite-vs-low fitness, ~13% per MET, with no observed upper limit of benefit — a magnitude rivaling or exceeding smoking and diabetes. Treat CRF as a clinical vital sign. (Cohort-tier — unrandomizable — but so large and dose-graded it is as close to certain as the field offers.) crf-vo2max-strongest-mortality-predictor
  3. Resistance-train for strength. It is strength and grip — not muscle mass — that independently predicts mortality; resistance activity ~10–17% lower mortality, on a J-curve where more is not better (benefit peaks ~30–60 min/week). The sarcopenia field itself reframed from mass to strength on exactly this evidence. grip-strength-mortality-pure dexa-strength-not-mass-predicts-mortality
  4. Move more; break up sitting. The steepest dose-response is at the sedentary→active transition, and because it is device-measured it is the least-confounded signal in the entire domain. physical-activity-dose-response-mortality
  5. Lower lifetime apoB / LDL. One of the only causal blood levers in the corpus — Mendelian genetics, epidemiology and RCTs converge, and because cumulative exposure is what matters, earlier is structurally better than later. apoB outperforms LDL-C as the measure. ldl-apob-causal-ascvd apob-superior-to-ldlc
  6. Sleep ~7 hours, regularly. U-shaped mortality with a ~7h nadir — both short and long sleep worse — with regularity and timing carrying independent weight. The causal direction is partly contested (illness drives both tails). sleep-duration-mortality-ushape
  7. Keep a healthy metabolic profile. Diabetes ~2× vascular risk; HbA1c predicts within the non-diabetic range; visceral fat predicts beyond BMI. Metabolic health is upstream of most of the others. hba1c-predicts-cvd-nondiabetic visceral-fat-independent-mortality-predictor
  8. Protect social connection. Among the largest psychosocial mortality associations in the literature — an effect size that rivals smoking cessation (Holt-Lunstad meta-analyses) — and routinely omitted from optimization stacks precisely because nothing is sold for it. Its omission is itself a bias the corpus is built to correct. social-connection-mortality-meta
The shape of the truth The high-confidence levers are functional and behavioral (fitness, strength, movement, sleep, connection) plus two causal blood levers (apoB, glucose). The most useful measurements mirror this: they are functional (VO₂max, grip, gait speed, chair-rise, the 10-second balance test) plus a few causal/early blood markers (apoB, Lp(a) measured once, HbA1c, fasting insulin) — not the commercial age-clocks and continuous monitors.

III. Hype & overclaim — the laundering gap

Each item below is sold harder than its evidence. The corpus's single most common failure mode is the laundering gap: a real mechanism, or a mouse result, is marketed as a hard human outcome it has not earned. Naming the gap is the antidote.

NAD⁺ boosting (NR / NMN)

NAD⁺ decline with age is real; human RCTs move only surrogates. No demonstrated longevity or hard-endpoint benefit. Mechanism real, outcome unproven. nad-precursor-nr-human-surrogate

Resveratrol / sirtuin activation

The in-vitro SIRT1 activation was substantially a fluorophore assay artifact; lifespan extension fails in lean animals. conflict-resveratrol-sirtuin

Senolytics (D+Q, fisetin)

Striking in mice; human evidence is tiny first-in-human pilots. Not established for healthy people. senolytics-extend-function-mouse

Metformin / rapamycin off-label

Metformin = a confounded cohort signal plus a trial (TAME) designed but not yet run; rapamycin = mouse lifespan plus one immune RCT, human geroprotective dose unknown. conflict-rapamycin-dosing

Partial epigenetic reprogramming

Mouse only. Genuinely exciting biology; not a human intervention. partial-reprogramming-ocampo-2016

Cold plunge "for longevity"

Rich BAT/norepinephrine mechanism, but the one human metabolic outcome used prolonged mild cold (hours), not the brief plunge sold — a dose↔evidence mismatch. cold-acclimation-insulin-sensitivity-t2d

Wim Hof method

One RCT shows trainable immune suppression — but acute-adrenaline-driven, bundled, small, short, healthy subjects. ⚠ Lethal in/near water. conflict-wim-hof-mechanism

CGM / "glucose spikes" for the healthy

No outcome RCT in non-diabetics; glucose variability has no proven outcome meaning; sensors disagree with each other. conflict-cgm-healthy-utility

Seed-oil panic

Polarized, low-rigor; higher-tier evidence runs the other way (PUFA replacing saturated fat lowers CHD). Certainty exceeds evidence on both sides. conflict-seed-oils-linoleic-acid

Biological-age clocks as a scorecard

Predict at the population level but are not a validated surrogate; first-gen clocks are noisy, clocks disagree, one number can't tell you if a protocol "worked." conflict-which-clock-is-valid

Consumer HRV / sleep-stage numbers

Wearables measure sleep stages poorly and in a flattering direction; overnight HRV is a within-person trend only. "Orthosomnia" is a documented harm.

Blue Zones as proof of a lifestyle

Extreme-age records partly track clerical error and pension fraud; verified demography collapses the rates. The strong claim is contested. conflict-blue-zones-data-quality

The 10,000-step example, in one line The step target is a 1960s Japanese marketing figure; the mortality benefit plateaus at ~7,000–8,000 steps, and intensity adds nothing over volume. A perfect miniature of the whole field — a sold number that the evidence does not support, doing real but smaller good than advertised.

V. The structural finding — bioenergetics as the bridge to the canon

Above the individual debates sits a structural result that emerged from the research itself. Every actionable longevity domain — exercise (mitochondrial biogenesis, VO₂max as integrated mitochondrial capacity), metabolism (substrate load, ketone signaling), aging (the "mitochondrial dysfunction" hallmark), genetics (mtDNA heteroplasmy), thermal (UCP1 uncoupling) — reaches up to the same place: the proton-motive force across the mitochondrial membrane.

That place is not an outcome claim. It is a law — Peter Mitchell's chemiosmosis. The corpus's own structure therefore forced a question the canon exists to answer: which findings are foundations (axioms, laws, primary derivations) and which are outcomes (applications that consume a foundation)? The bioenergetics lineage is the bridge precisely because it sits on the foundation side of that line while every health domain sits on the outcome side and reaches up to it. Critically, it is the uncontested spine that connects the inherited — and more speculative — biophysics layer (structured water, biophotons, non-native EMF) to textbook mitochondrial medicine, letting the canon hold a 05-biophysics branch that is rigorous at its core and explicit about its frontier.

Acted on this report cycle — canon promotions Three foundation-tier figures were promoted into bucket-canon/05-biophysics: Hans Krebs (citric-acid cycle), Jennifer Moyle (co-proved chemiosmosis; an attribution-parity correction), and William Martin (alkaline-vent origin of life, with Lane) — joining Mitchell, Margulis, Lane, Wallace and Szent-Györgyi already carded. A new concept node, chemiosmosis / proton-motive force / redox bioenergetics, now anchors the foundation that the outcome layer cites upward to.

The inherited Kruse-tier biophysics is handled by the same discipline rather than by dismissal: the circadian-light spine is validated by mainstream chronobiology (melanopsin → SCN; evening blue light disruptive), while its extensions remain speculative (sunlight's metabolic primacy over food, UV/IR systemic therapy, deuterium/water coupling). Foundation and frontier, labeled — not merged, not excluded.

VI. The human & institutional landscape

The corpus carded 176 figures into a 180-node, 155-edge relationship graph spanning mentor lineages (Guarente→Sinclair/Imai; Kenyon→Ruvkun; Mitchell→Lane), institutional affiliations, and documented rivalries. They are indexed without endorsement — mainstream and fringe sit on the same list, separated by an evidence_posture tag (mainstream-rigorous, clinical-translator, mainstream-communicator, frontier-contested, fringe-to-canon, practitioner-n1), never by exclusion.

Institutions & the trial reality

24 labs are mapped across academia (Buck, Salk, Harvard, Stanford, UW, Berkeley, Tufts, Einstein, USC), industry (Altos, Calico, Retro, NewLimit, BioAge, Unity, Loyal) and funders (NIA, Hevolution, SENS/LEV, Methuselah). The single most important institutional finding is sobering: across the 15 mapped human aging trials, every active trial uses a surrogate endpoint (VO₂max, DXA fat, gait speed, an epigenetic clock). The one trial proposing a hard morbidity/mortality endpoint — TAME (metformin) — remains unfunded and unregistered. There is, as of this writing, no hard-endpoint human longevity trial running anywhere. This is why so many claims in Section III are structurally stuck at animal or surrogate: the experiment that would promote them has not been run.

VII. The capstone — read this if nothing else

The evidence is lopsided toward a short list of boring, powerful, mostly-functional levers: don't smoke; build and keep cardiorespiratory fitness and strength; move more; sleep ~7 hours regularly; keep apoB/LDL low across life; keep a healthy metabolic profile; protect social connection. These individually outrank every exotic biohack in the corpus, and they compound.

The one causal, modifiable blood lever is apoB/LDL (measure Lp(a) once). The highest-signal measurements are functional (VO₂max, grip, gait, chair-rise, the 10-second one-leg stand — each an independent mortality predictor) plus a few causal/early blood markers (apoB, Lp(a), HbA1c, fasting insulin). Almost everything that gets sold — biological-age clocks, CGM for the healthy, consumer HRV and sleep-stage numbers, senolytics / NAD⁺ / rapamycin for healthy people, cold plunges, seed-oil panic — is either a correlate dressed as a scorecard, a mouse result not yet in humans, or a dose that does not match the studied dose.

The operating instruction: spend attention on the Tier-I levers; treat the Tier-II set as trends worth following; enjoy the Tier-III items as experiments, not protocols. And above the practice, hold the discipline that produced this report — keep foundation-tier laws separate from outcome-tier applications, grade every claim by what it has actually earned, and let the disagreements stand as objects rather than collapsing them into false certainty.
