## PATTERNS (cross-chapter, ranked by leverage)

1. **"genuinely" is a house-style violation and it is everywhere (~45 hits across the six files).**
   The global style rule bans "genuinely / truly / really" as intensifiers outright. It has become
   this author's verbal tic — it appears in headings (`23` A2 "what genuinely helps"), verdicts
   ("genuinely disease-modifying," "genuinely saves lives," "genuinely effective"), and prose. The
   fix is almost always **deletion** (the sentence is stronger without it) or one concrete substitute.
   This is the single highest-yield pass across the whole slice. Every located instance is flagged
   per-chapter below; the top-level instruction is: **delete "genuinely" on sight unless it is doing
   real contrastive work**, and even then prefer a specific word.

2. **"X, not Y" antithesis — mostly PROTECT, selectively cut.** The slice leans hard on comma-splice
   contrast ("predictor, not lever"; "controlled, not cured"; "symptoms, not mortality"). Unlike
   generic AI antithesis, most of these are *load-bearing technical distinctions* the book has
   defined as recurring frames (`predictor ≠ lever` is an explicit device). **Do not flatten these** —
   rewriting "stents save lives in acute occlusion, not stable disease" into a positive-only sentence
   would lose the correction that is the whole point. I flag only the handful that are rhetorical
   padding rather than a real two-way distinction. Treat this as the one place the house antithesis
   rule yields to meaning.

3. **"the honest…" / "honestly" as a section-tag is overused** and starting to read as a verbal
   crutch ("the honest take," "honestly graded," "honest grade," "the honest part," "told honestly").
   It is part of the book's voice and mostly earns its place, but 2–3 per chapter would land harder
   than 8–10. Thin it where it's decorative (flagged per-chapter).

4. **Throat-clearing openers in a few tables/sections** ("It is worth holding them apart, because…",
   "This table is reference-grade"). Minor; tighten in place.

5. **Best-reading chapter: `24-disease-neuro-rheum.md`** — strongest hooks, best rhythm variation,
   cleanest verdicts. **Most in need of the "genuinely" pass: `42` and `23`** (highest density).
