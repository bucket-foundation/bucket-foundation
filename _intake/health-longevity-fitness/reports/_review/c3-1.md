Across chapters this is correct anchoring; **within** a chapter it is often stated 3× (foundations, mechanism-bridge, mito). The within-chapter repeats are the cut target — never touch the cross-chapter anchor copies, which are load-bearing findability.
- **`37-mitochondrial-health` is the biggest length win in the slice.** Its own marquee thesis sentence is printed near-verbatim twice within 45 lines (open + §37.1 blockquote), and its conclusion ("exercise is the mitochondrial drug; the bottle is an adjunct") is printed 3×. The §37.8 verdict table and the "honest detail" prose below it cover the same supplements twice — but the prose carries the PMIDs/n/effect-sizes, so this is a **tighten-the-table-verdict-column, not cut** situation.
- **`00-start-here` is the model the rest should imitate** — a true bottom-line-up-front page, every DO item carries a `§` pointer, MEASURE/SKIP sections are skimmable. Flag as GOOD/keep essentially untouched.
- **Findability is already strong** — the slice has explicit skim cues ("You can skim this table", "In a hurry? skim to §37.7", "How to use this map"). The gap is not missing signposts; it is redundant restatement. Length reduction here = deleting duplicate prose, not adding boxes.
