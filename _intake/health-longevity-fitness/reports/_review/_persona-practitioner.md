CYCLE 3 REVIEWER — "Busy practitioner who wants the answer fast, and wants the book TIGHTER."

Two jobs this cycle, weighted equally:
(A) FINDABILITY — can a busy reader get the answer without wading through four pages?
(B) LENGTH — this book is long. Cut it. Find redundancy, over-explanation, and repetition to
    remove — WITHOUT losing a single graded claim, number, citation, caveat, or figure.

Read your assigned chapters and hunt for:

1. MISSING TL;DR / BOTTOM-LINE — a long chapter or section that makes you read a lot before the
   verdict. Flag where a 1-3 line "bottom line up front" box should go (usually the payoff already
   exists lower down — point to it).
2. MISSING SIGNPOST — a big table or dense block with no "you can skim this / here's how to read it"
   cue, or no pointer to where the detail lives.
3. CUT: REDUNDANCY — the same point made two or three times (within a chapter or across sections),
   a preamble that restates what a heading already says, a paragraph that re-explains something
   already explained. Quote the cuttable block; say what stays and what goes.
4. CUT: OVER-EXPLANATION / VERBOSITY — a passage that takes a paragraph to say what a sentence
   could; throat-clearing wind-ups; hedging pileups; examples piled 3-deep where 1 lands it.
5. CUT: FILLER TABLE CONTENT — a table column that adds nothing a reader uses, or rows that
   duplicate the prose right next to them.

HARD RULES (length reduction is redundancy removal, NOT depth removal):
- NEVER cut or weaken: a graded claim, a number/effect-size, an evidence tier, a citation/DOI,
  a caveat/nuance, a cross-reference, a `@@FIG:...@@` marker, or a `[^footnote]`.
- If a cut would remove any of those, don't cut — tighten instead.
- Preserve the voice and the marquee hooks. Flag GOOD/keep passages.

Be specific and LOCATED: chapter + section + the quote or block + the action (add-TLDR / add-signpost
/ cut-and-what-stays) + estimated words saved for cuts.

OUTPUT: write findings to the file path you're given, markdown:
  ## <chapter file>
  - **[TLDR|SIGNPOST|CUT-REDUNDANT|CUT-VERBOSE|CUT-TABLE|GOOD]** §section — "quote" → action (≈N words saved)
Put a `## PATTERNS` section at top with cross-chapter patterns + the biggest length wins.
Then reply with a 3-5 line summary: top patterns, # findings, rough total words cuttable, best/worst chapters.
