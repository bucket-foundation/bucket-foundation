# Cycle 2 line-edit findings — slice 7 (training / modalities / sports / nutrition / fasting / recovery)

## PATTERNS

- **"genuinely" is the dominant AI-tell across the slice** (banned intensifier). It appears in every
  chapter and is almost always deletable with zero loss — the sentence is stronger without it. Same for
  the two "truly" cases in §44. This is the single highest-yield sweep: search each file for
  `genuinely`/`truly` used as an intensifier and cut the word.
- **"robust" and "boost"** (house-kill words) each appear a couple of times in a lifestyle/marketing
  sense — swap for "consistent/strong" and "effect/lift".
- **Antithesis is load-bearing here, so handle with care.** The book's core honesty device is
  `predictor ≠ lever` / "mechanism ≠ outcome" / "X is associated, not proven." Most "X, not Y"
  constructions ARE the argument and must be preserved. I flag only the few that are rhetorical
  comma-splices (not carrying a distinction), and I explicitly mark the honesty-motif ones as GOOD/protect.
- **The recurring motifs read as voice, not repetition** — "something beats nothing," "adherence beats
  optimality," "the dose sold ≠ the dose studied," "boring, free, unmonetizable." Protect these; they are
  the spine that makes six long chapters feel like one book.
- **Readability ranking:** §45 (sports) reads best — strong cold-open, varied rhythm, a real narrative
  thread. §36 (fasting) and §05 (recovery) also strong. §44 (modalities) is the heaviest — necessary
  card-after-card structure, but the intro clause-piles and the sheer uniformity are where a reader
  coasts; that's the one chapter that most rewards a rhythm pass.
- **Top overall fixes:** (1) delete every intensifier "genuinely"/"truly"; (2) kill "boost"/"robust";
  (3) vary the §44 intro paragraphs; (4) leave the honesty-antithesis alone.
