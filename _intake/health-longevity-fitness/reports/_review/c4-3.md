## PATTERNS

**Accuracy (systemic):** low risk. No footnote/claim mismatches, no orphaned "(Name, Year)",
no broken § cross-refs detected across the slice. The two errors below are isolated, not a
pattern. The one thing worth a global grep before print: **unit hygiene in the summary
tables** — the FEV₁/FVC slip (17) is the kind of thing a table-condensing pass introduces.

**Structure (biggest lever): the per-chapter "Go deeper" lists duplicate the footnote DOIs.**
Chapters 17, 23, and 24 carry BOTH a full `[^footnote]` apparatus (every DOI) AND a "Go
deeper" section that re-lists most of the same DOIs with annotations. That double-carry is
the largest page-dropper available in this slice. Collapsing each "Go deeper" to ~5 curated
annotated picks — while every DOI stays in the footnotes — loses **no citation**. Estimated
save across the three chapters: ~700–800 words. **Guardrail:** do NOT apply to ch. 42 (it has
no footnotes — its "Go deeper" is the ONLY citation record) or ch. 07 (its "Go deeper" table
holds unique, non-footnoted sources: USPSTF, ACC/AHA guideline, Attia *Outlive*, WCRF).
