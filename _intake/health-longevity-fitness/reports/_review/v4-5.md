## 27-derm-dental-ent-eye §A (dermatology) / §B.1 (fluoride) / §C.1 (hearing) / §D (eye)

### §D.3 Glaucoma — EMGT
- claim: "randomly lowering IOP ... slowed progression vs no initial treatment (progression 45% treated vs 62% control)" | cited: Heijl 2002 EMGT, doi:10.1001/archopht.120.10.1268
- **CONFIRMED** — EMGT reported disease progression in 45% of treated vs 62% of controls; median progression also delayed ~18 months. Source: EMGT (Arch Ophthalmol 2002), multiple secondary confirmations.

### §D.4 AMD — MARINA
- claim: "monthly ranibizumab ... ~95% lost <15 letters; ~34% gained ≥15 letters vs sham" | cited: Rosenfeld 2006 MARINA, doi:10.1056/NEJMoa054481
- **CONFIRMED** — At 12 mo, ~95% of ranibizumab patients lost <15 letters (vs 62% sham); 33.8% of the 0.5 mg arm gained ≥15 letters (vs 5% sham). Source: MARINA (NEJM 2006).

### §D.5 Diabetic retinopathy — DCCT
- claim: "intensive glucose control ... reduced the development and progression of retinopathy by ~76%/~54%" | cited: DCCT 1993, doi:10.1056/NEJM199309303291401
- **CONFIRMED** — Intensive therapy reduced incidence of retinopathy by 76% and progression by 54% over ~6.5 yr. Source: DCCT (NEJM 1993).

### §B.1 Caries — fluoride toothpaste (Marinho)
- claim: "prevented-fraction ~24% on the DMFS index" | cited: Marinho 2003 Cochrane, doi:10.1002/14651858.CD002278
- **CONFIRMED** — Marinho 2003 (70 trials): average DMFS reduction of 24% for fluoride vs placebo dentifrice in the permanent dentition.

### §D.1 Myopia — outdoor time (He)
- claim: "added 40 minutes of outdoor class time per school day; over 3 years it reduced incidence of new myopia" | cited: He 2015 JAMA, doi:10.1001/jama.2015.10803
- **CONFIRMED** — Guangzhou cluster-RCT, first-graders, +40 min/day outdoor, reduced 3-yr myopia incidence. Source: He 2015 (JAMA).

### §C.1 Hearing — ACHIEVE subgroup
- claim: "hearing aids slowed decline ~48% in the higher-risk subgroup" | cited: Lin 2023 ACHIEVE, doi:10.1016/S0140-6736(23)01406-X
- **CONFIRMED** — Null in the total population; 48% slower cognitive decline over 3 yr in the pre-specified higher-risk (ARIC) subgroup. Source: ACHIEVE (Lancet 2023).

## 34-emergency-acute §2 (CPR/AED) / §3.2 (stroke)

### §2.1 Bystander CPR — Hasselqvist-Ax
- claim: "30-day survival of 10.5% with bystander CPR vs 4.0% without" (~30,000 arrests, Sweden) | cited: Hasselqvist-Ax 2015 NEJM, doi:10.1056/NEJMoa1405796
- **CONFIRMED** — 30,381 witnessed OHCAs; 30-day survival 10.5% (CPR before EMS) vs 4.0% (no CPR before EMS); adjusted OR 2.15. Source: NEJM 2015.

### §2.2 AED — PAD trial
- claim: "training community volunteers to use public-access AEDs roughly doubled survivors of OHCA versus CPR alone" | cited: Hallstrom 2004 PAD, doi:10.1056/NEJMoa040566
- **CONFIRMED** — Survivors doubled (30/128 in CPR+AED units vs 15/107 in CPR-only units, p=0.03). Source: PAD trial (NEJM 2004).

### §3.2 Stroke — neuron loss rate
- claim: "a large-vessel stroke destroys on the order of ~1.9 million neurons per minute" | cited: (owned by §24; Saver "Time Is Brain")
- **CONFIRMED** — Saver 2006 (Stroke): 1.9 million neurons, 14 billion synapses lost per minute in typical large-vessel ischemic stroke.

## 43-developmental-congenital-pediatric §1.5 (folate) / §5.1 (vaccine) / §3.7 (SIDS)

### §1.5 Neural tube defects — MRC folate
- claim: "periconceptional folic acid cut NTD recurrence by ~72% in the MRC Vitamin Study (1991)" | cited: MRC Vitamin Study 1991 Lancet, PMID 1677062
- **CONFIRMED** — 6 NTD cases/593 supplemented vs 21/692 placebo = 72% risk reduction. Source: MRC Vitamin Study (Lancet 1991).

### §5.1 Vaccine–autism — Taylor meta-analysis
- claim: "a meta-analysis of >1.2 million children found no relationship" | cited: Taylor 2014 Vaccine, doi:10.1016/j.vaccine.2014.04.085
- **CONFIRMED** — 5 cohort studies totaling 1,256,407 children (plus 5 case-control, 9,920); no association with vaccination/MMR/thimerosal/mercury. Source: Taylor 2014.

### §3.7 SIDS — prone sleeping risk (Gilbert)
- claim: "prone (front) sleeping carried a roughly 4-fold higher SIDS risk" | cited: Gilbert 2005 Int J Epidemiol, doi:10.1093/ije/dyi088
- **MINOR** — Gilbert 2005's *pooled* odds ratio is ~3.0 (OR 2.93 front-vs-back by 1970; OR 3.00 front-vs-other by 1986), not ~4-fold. The direction and the companion claim ("tens of thousands of deaths preventable" — >10,000 UK, ≥50,000 Europe/USA/Australasia) are confirmed. Fix: "roughly 3-fold" matches the cited paper (individual studies and later meta-analyses range higher, but the Gilbert pooled estimate is ~3).

## 38-surgery-perioperative §3 (bariatric)

### §3.6 Bariatric — STAMPEDE
- claim: "STAMPEDE randomised patients with T2D to intensive medical therapy vs surgery and found surgery produced far higher rates of glycaemic control and diabetes remission at 5 years" | cited: Schauer 2017 NEJM, doi:10.1056/NEJMoa1600869
- **CONFIRMED** — 150 patients, HbA1c ≤6.0% and remission far higher with surgery vs medical therapy at 5 yr (~1/3 of surgical patients in durable remission off meds). Source: STAMPEDE 5-yr (NEJM 2017). (ORBITA n=200, Down life-expectancy 25→60, childhood ALL ~90% cure not separately re-queried — all standard, well-supported figures.)

## 39-anesthesia-critical-care §2.5–2.6 / §5.1 / §5.4

### §5.1 ARDS — ARDSNet low tidal volume
- claim: "low tidal volumes (~6 mL/kg) reduced mortality by about 9 percentage points (~22% relative)" | cited: ARDSNet 2000 NEJM, doi:10.1056/NEJM200005043421801
- **CONFIRMED** — Mortality 31.0% vs 39.8% (8.8 pp absolute ≈ 22% relative), P=0.007. Source: ARDSNet/ARMA (NEJM 2000).

### §2.5 Anesthesia mortality — Bainbridge
- claim: "declined from roughly 357 per million before the 1970s to about 34 per million (~1 in 29,000) in the 1990s–2000s" | cited: Bainbridge 2012 Lancet, doi:10.1016/S0140-6736(12)60990-8
- **CONFIRMED** — 357/million (pre-1970s) → 52/million (1970s–80s) → 34/million (1990s–2000s). 34/million = 1 in ~29,400. Source: Bainbridge 2012.

### §5.1 COVID — RECOVERY dexamethasone
- claim: "dexamethasone ... cut mortality in ventilated patients by about a third" | cited: RECOVERY 2021 NEJM, doi:10.1056/NEJMoa2021436
- **CONFIRMED** — Rate ratio 0.65 in ventilated patients (~1/3 reduction); 0.83 overall. Source: RECOVERY (NEJM 2021).

### §2.6 POCD — ISPOCD1
- claim: "cognitive dysfunction in ~26% of older patients at one week and ~10% at three months" | cited: Moller 1998 ISPOCD1 Lancet, doi:10.1016/S0140-6736(97)07382-0
- **CONFIRMED** — 25.8% at 1 week, 9.9% at 3 months (vs 3.4% / 2.8% controls). Source: ISPOCD1 (Lancet 1998, PMID 9525362).

### §2.6 Awareness — Sebel / NAP5
- claim: "explicit awareness with recall at roughly 0.13% (about 1–2 per 1,000) (Sebel 2004) ... NAP5 much lower reported rate (~1 in 19,000)" | cited: Sebel 2004 Anesth Analg; Pandit 2014 NAP5 Br J Anaesth
- **CONFIRMED** — Sebel 2004: 0.1–0.2% (1–2/1,000). NAP5: ~1:19,600 patient-reported. Both match.

## 40-imaging-radiology §40.3 (radiation) / §40.4 (incidentaloma) / §40.6 (IR) / §40.7 (AI)

### §40.6 Stroke thrombectomy — HERMES
- claim: "thrombectomy roughly halves disability ... number-needed-to-treat of ~2.6 to reduce disability by one level" | cited: HERMES 2016 Lancet, doi:10.1016/S0140-6736(16)00163-X
- **CONFIRMED** — Pooled 5-RCT IPD meta-analysis; NNT 2.6 to reduce disability ≥1 mRS level. Source: HERMES (Lancet 2016).

### §40.3.2 CT cancer projection — Smith-Bindman
- claim: "~93 million CT examinations in the US in 2023 and projected ~103,000 future radiation-induced cancers ... ~5% of all new cancer diagnoses" | cited: Smith-Bindman 2025 JAMA Intern Med, doi:10.1001/jamainternmed.2025.0505
- **CONFIRMED** — 93M CT exams (2023), ~103,000 projected lifetime cancers, ~5% if utilization persists. Source: Smith-Bindman 2025.

### §40.3.2 Childhood CT — Mathews
- claim: "linking CT exposure to cancer in 680,000 young Australians, found a ~24% higher overall cancer incidence" | cited: Mathews 2013 BMJ, doi:10.1136/bmj.f2360
- **CONFIRMED** — 680,211 exposed; overall cancer incidence 24% greater (IRR 1.24). Source: Mathews 2013 (BMJ).

### §40.3.2 Childhood CT — EPI-CT
- claim: "948,174 European individuals scanned before age 22 ... excess relative risk of ~1.96 per 100 mGy ... roughly 1–2 extra blood cancers per 10,000 children scanned" | cited: Bosch de Basea 2023 Nat Med, doi:10.1038/s41591-023-02620-0
- **CONFIRMED** — 948,174 individuals; ERR 1.96 per 100 mGy for hematological malignancies; 1–2 per 10,000 attributable. Source: EPI-CT (Nat Med 2023).

### §40.7 AI mammography — MASAI
- claim: "AI support increased cancer detection ~29% (6.4 vs 5.0 per 1,000) without a significant rise in false positives, and cut radiologist reading workload ~44%" | cited: Lång 2023 MASAI, Lancet Oncol (24)00298-X
- **MINOR** — Workload reduction (44.3%) and "no FP rise" are correct. But the detection figures are off: the published cancer-detection rate was **6.1 vs 5.1 per 1,000** (not 6.4 vs 5.0), a **~20% increase**, not ~29% — recall rate 2.2% vs 2.0%. Source: MASAI interim (Lancet Oncol 2023). Fix: "increased cancer detection ~20% (6.1 vs 5.1 per 1,000)." (Some press releases quote "29% more cancers," but the trial's own detection-rate numbers give ~20%; the manual's specific per-1,000 values don't match the paper either.)
