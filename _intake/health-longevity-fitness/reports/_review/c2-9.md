# Cycle 2 line-edit — slice 9 (state-of-field / conflicts register / go-deeper)

## PATTERNS

- **These three files are already very clean.** Almost no throat-clearing ("it is important to
  note," "in order to"), no `delve`/`leverage`/`seamless`/`boost`. The prose is tight, the voice is
  dry and confident, and most sentences earn their place. The line-edit surface is small — the wins
  are a handful of AI-tell words and a few incidental antitheses.
- **`genuinely` as an intensifier appears twice and must go** (house hard-rule): 46 §Calibration-
  Spectrum ("genuinely dangerous"), go-deeper §1.5 Nestor ("genuinely engaging"). Both read fine
  with the adverb deleted. Note: plain `genuine` as an *adjective* ("genuine mechanism," "genuine
  corrective," "genuine RCT") is fine and should be left alone.
- **`robust` (kill-list word) appears 3× but every use is technical** (= well-replicated / strong).
  Leave the scientific ones; the only true deadweight is "robust replicated negative" (46 §hard-
  contradictions #8), where `robust` + `replicated` say the same thing twice.
- **Two kinds of `X, not Y` antithesis, and they need opposite treatment.**
  (1) The book's *coined honesty refrains* — "provenance, not evidence" and "predictor ≠ lever" —
  are load-bearing motifs repeated on purpose. **Protect these; do NOT flatten them.** (`≠` isn't
  even a comma-splice.)
  (2) A few *incidental* antitheses in argument prose ("calibration, not direction"; "not lying, it
  is rounding up"; "not a failure column — it is…") are the accidental kind the house style targets.
  Rewrite those as a clean positive statement + a separate short sentence, per the CLAUDE.md rule.
- **Top overall fixes:** (a) delete both `genuinely`s; (b) recast the three incidental antitheses in
  ch.46; (c) trim "robust replicated" → "replicated." Everything else is optional polish.
