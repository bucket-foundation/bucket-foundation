## PATTERNS

- **Systemic accuracy risk = cross-chapter count/id drift.** The only real
  accuracy defects are *numbers and claim-ids that disagree between chapters that
  should agree*: the conflict-count (atlas vs State-of-the-Field), one claim-id
  that differs between two chapters (CALERIE), and one claim-id that differs
  *within* State-of-the-Field (microbiome). None is a wrong scientific fact; each
  is a traceability/consistency defect a skeptic would catch and that undermines
  the manual's "every claim graded and traceable" promise. Recommend a global
  pass that treats claim-ids and headline counts as a single source of truth.
- **Biggest structural fat = third-pass restatement inside the two big
  chapters.** §12.4 (mechanism-bridge) and §37.8's closing recap paragraph each
  re-summarize graded content already stated in the same chapter's table + narrative.
  These can be compressed while keeping every footnote/claim-id/tier — the largest
  safe page-drops in the slice (~200–240 words). Cross-*chapter* repetition of the
  ROS/mitohormesis story (01 §2.6/§6, 12 rows 14–15/§12.3.8, 37 §37.5) is
  deliberate self-containment and is left intact.
