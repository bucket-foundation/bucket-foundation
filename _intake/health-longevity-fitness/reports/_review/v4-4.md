## 25-oncology §25.5.2 (checkpoint immunotherapy)
- claim: "CheckMate 067 … combination nivolumab + ipilimumab in advanced melanoma produced **~52% overall survival at 5 years**" | cited: Larkin et al., *NEJM* 2019, 10.1056/NEJMoa1910836
- **CONFIRMED** — 5-year OS was 52% for nivo+ipi (44% nivo alone, 26% ipi alone), CheckMate 067, n=945, previously untreated advanced melanoma. Matches exactly. (https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1910836)

## 25-oncology §25.2.4 (heritable cancer / BRCA)
- claim: "BRCA1 carriers ~55–72% lifetime breast-cancer risk, ~39–44% ovarian; BRCA2 ~45–69% breast, ~11–17% ovarian — Kuchenbaecker 2017" | cited: Kuchenbaecker et al., *JAMA* 2017
- **CONFIRMED** — Kuchenbaecker (JAMA 2017, prospective cohort) reports breast-cancer risk to age 80 of 72% (BRCA1) and 69% (BRCA2), ovarian 44% (BRCA1) and 17% (BRCA2). The manual's upper bounds match the paper exactly; the ranges span other estimates. (https://www.healio.com/news/hematology-oncology/20170620/researchers-define-cancer-risk-in-brca1brca2-carriers)

## 25-oncology §25.7 (laetrile debunk)
- claim: "the definitive NCI-sponsored clinical trial (Moertel et al., *NEJM* 1982) found **no benefit** and documented **cyanide toxicity**" | cited: Moertel et al., *NEJM* 1982, 10.1056/NEJM198201283060403
- **CONFIRMED** — 178 cancer patients on amygdalin + metabolic therapy; "no substantive benefit … in terms of cure, improvement, or stabilization"; several patients showed cyanide toxicity / near-lethal blood cyanide. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7033783/)

## 26-infectious-disease §5.2 / footnote (HIV treatment-as-prevention)

- **CONFIRMED** — HPTN 052 early-release: 96% reduction in genetically-linked HIV-1 transmission with early ART in serodiscordant couples. Matches. (https://www.hptn.org/research/studies/hptn-052)

## 08-brain-cognitive §2 (hearing → cognition, ACHIEVE)
- claim: "In the prespecified higher-risk subgroup … the hearing intervention **slowed cognitive decline by ~48% over 3 years**" (primary endpoint null overall) | cited: Lin et al. (ACHIEVE), *Lancet* 2023, 10.1016/S0140-6736(23)01406-X
- **CONFIRMED** — n=977, ages 70–84; primary endpoint null in full cohort; in the higher-risk ARIC subgroup the intervention reduced 3-year cognitive change by 48%. Direction and nuance (subgroup, not primary) correctly stated. (https://aaic.alz.org/releases_2023/hearing-aids-slow-cognitive-decline.asp)

## 08-brain-cognitive §6.2 (lecanemab, CLARITY-AD)
- claim: "decline was **1.21 with lecanemab vs 1.66 with placebo — a difference of −0.45 points (~27% slower)**; **ARIA-E ~12.6%** … **ARIA-H ~17.3%**" | cited: van Dyck et al., *NEJM* 2023, 10.1056/NEJMoa2212948
- **CONFIRMED** — CLARITY-AD (n=1795): CDR-SB change 1.21 vs 1.66, difference −0.45 (27% slower decline); ARIA-E 12.6%, ARIA-H 17.3%. All four numbers match. (https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2212948)

## 08-brain-cognitive §6.1 (shingles vaccine → dementia)
- claim: "being vaccinated **reduced new dementia diagnoses over 7 years by ~3.5 percentage points (a ~20% relative reduction)**, with a stronger effect in women" | cited: Eyting, Geldsetzer et al., *Nature* 2025, 10.1038/s41586-025-08800-x
- **CONFIRMED** — Welsh regression-discontinuity natural experiment; vaccinated ~20% less likely to develop dementia, effect stronger in women. Relative-reduction figure and sex effect match. (https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08800-x)

## 20-mental-health §1.3 (antidepressants, Cipriani)
- claim: "a network meta-analysis of **522 trials, ~116,000 patients, all 21 antidepressants** … all 21 beat placebo … pooled SMD ~**0.30**" | cited: Cipriani et al., *Lancet* 2018, 10.1016/S0140-6736(17)32802-7
- **CONFIRMED** — 522 double-blind trials, 116,477 participants, all 21 drugs more efficacious than placebo, average SMD 0.30. Matches exactly. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30264701/)

## 20-mental-health §7 (anorexia mortality)
- claim: "**standardized mortality ratio (SMR) of ~5–6 for anorexia nervosa** … meta-analysis of 36 studies" | cited: Arcelus et al., *Arch Gen Psychiatry* 2011, 10.1001/archgenpsychiatry.2011.74
- **CONFIRMED** — Arcelus meta-analysis of 36 studies; AN crude mortality 5.1 per 1000 person-years, weighted SMR ~5.86 (i.e. ~5–6×). Matches. (https://www.scirp.org/reference/referencespapers?referenceid=2877912)

## 35-addiction §3.4 / §0 (MOUD mortality, Sordo)
- claim: "methadone and buprenorphine **roughly halve all-cause and overdose mortality** versus being out of treatment" | cited: Sordo et al., *BMJ* 2017, 10.1136/bmj.j1550
- **CONFIRMED** (conservative) — Sordo: methadone all-cause mortality 11.3 (in) vs 36.1 (out) per 1000 py (out-to-in RR 3.2, ~69% lower in treatment); buprenorphine 4.3 vs 9.5 (RR 2.2, ~55% lower); methadone overdose RR 4.8. "Roughly halve" understates the effect (it is more than halved) but is directionally correct and a common conservative shorthand. No error. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28446428/)

## 35-addiction §0 (transition-to-dependence, Lopez-Quintero)
- claim: table: nicotine "~68%", alcohol "~22%", cocaine "~21%", cannabis "~9%" of ever-users → dependence | cited: Lopez-Quintero et al., *Drug Alcohol Depend* 2011, 10.1016/j.drugalcdep.2010.11.004
- **CONFIRMED** — NESARC: nicotine 67.5%, alcohol 22.7%, cocaine 20.9%, cannabis 8.9%. All match the rounded figures. (The manual correctly footnotes that the ~23% opioid figure is Anthony 1994, not Lopez-Quintero, since NESARC did not cover opioids.) (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21145178/)

## 21-pain-injury-rehab §5.4 (opioids, SPACE)
- claim: "SPACE … found **no benefit of opioids on pain-related function — and if anything slightly worse pain in the opioid group**" | cited: Krebs et al., *JAMA* 2018, 10.1001/jama.2018.0899
- **CONFIRMED** — n=240, 12 months; BPI interference 3.4 (opioid) vs 3.3 (non-opioid), P=.58 (no difference); pain intensity significantly better in the non-opioid group (BPI severity 4.0 vs 3.5, P=.03). Direction matches. (https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2673971)

## 21-pain-injury-rehab §6.1 (knee arthroscopy sham, Moseley)
- claim: "arthroscopic surgery for knee OA is no better than a *sham (placebo) operation*" | cited: Moseley et al., *NEJM* 2002, 10.1056/NEJMoa013259
- **CONFIRMED** — n=180 randomized to débridement, lavage, or placebo surgery; outcomes after arthroscopy "no better than … a placebo procedure." Matches. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12110735/)

## 21-pain-injury-rehab §2.2 (asymptomatic MRI, Brinjikji)
- claim: "disc degeneration in ~52% of asymptomatic 30-year-olds and ~88% of 60-year-olds" | cited: Brinjikji et al., *AJNR* 2015, 10.3174/ajnr.A4173
- **CONFIRMED** — Brinjikji age-specific asymptomatic prevalence table (by decade 20–80) reports disc degeneration 52% at age 30 and 88% at age 60. Matches the paper's published table. (https://www.ajnr.org/content/36/4/811)
