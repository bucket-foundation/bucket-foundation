## sections/27-derm-dental-ent-eye.md

- **[ACCURACY]** §D.3 Glaucoma — "It is the **'silent thief of sight'** — and the second-leading
  cause of irreversible blindness worldwide." → Imprecise/likely wrong. Glaucoma is standardly cited
  as **the leading cause of *irreversible* blindness** worldwide (cataract is the leading cause of
  blindness overall but is *reversible*; AMD is #2 among irreversible). "Second-leading cause of
  irreversible blindness" conflates the overall-blindness ranking with the irreversible one and
  undercuts the chapter's own "silent thief" framing. Fix: "the **leading** cause of irreversible
  blindness worldwide" (or "the second-leading cause of blindness worldwide, after cataract").
  Severity: low–moderate.
