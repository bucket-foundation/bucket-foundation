## 24-disease-neuro-rheum §3 — HERMES thrombectomy NNT
- claim: "thrombectomy roughly doubled the odds of functional independence — NNT ≈ 2.6, meaning you treat about three people for one to walk away independent" | cited: Goyal 2016, Lancet (HERMES)
- **MINOR** — The NNT of 2.6 in HERMES is for a reduction of ≥1 level on the modified Rankin Scale (a one-category disability shift), NOT for achieving functional independence (mRS 0–2). Functional independence rose from 26.5% to 46.0% (aOR ~2.35–2.71) → NNT ≈ 5 for one extra independent patient. So the 2.6 figure is real and from HERMES but is attached to the wrong endpoint ("walk away independent"). Fix: state NNT 2.6 = one-level-less-disability; NNT ~5 for functional independence. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26898852/
