# Cycle 3 (Practitioner) — Findability + Length findings — slice-3

## PATTERNS

- **Findability is already strong across this slice.** Every chapter opens with a bolded 1-2 line
  hook, most have an explicit "read this first" / "skim it now" at-a-glance table with an
  evidence-tier key, and 17/23 have an explicit "One-line verdict up front" box. This is the model
  the rest of the book should copy. The gaps are narrow: a few long chapters (07, 22, 24) bury their
  bottom-line under 400+ lines and never front-load a verdict box the way 17/23 do — the payoff
  already exists in their synthesis/summary sections and just needs lifting to the top.
- **Biggest length win = the repeated honesty-rules preamble.** The "Predictor ≠ lever / Cohort ≠ RCT
  / Something beats nothing" trio is re-explained *in full, with parenthetical glosses,* at the top of
  17, 22, 24, and 42 (and re-stated again in each chapter's synthesis). Defining it once (a shared
  callout the chapters cross-ref) and shrinking each chapter's copy to a one-line pointer + the
  chapter-specific example saves ~30-60 words per chapter without losing a single graded claim. This
  is the cleanest cross-chapter cut. (Keep the chapter-specific *example* — cut the generic re-teach.)
- **Second win = synthesis sections that restate the opening table.** 17 §8, 22 §8, 23 Synthesis, and
  24 §18 each re-narrate points already made in the at-a-glance table + body. They carry real value
  (the "movement is the universal lever" throughline, the oversold/contested tables) but each has 1-2
  bullets that are pure recap. Tighten, don't delete.
- **Third win = cross-chapter duplicated debunks/markers.** The detox/cleanse debunk is written out
  three times (17 §3.5 liver, 17 §7.3 lymph, 23 §D4 — 23 at least cross-refs). ED-as-vascular-warning
  is fully written in 17 §6.3, 17 table, *and* 42 §6.2 + 42 honesty-preamble. The protein-kidney myth
  is fully written in 17 §2.5 and 22 §6.1. One canonical treatment + pointers would save ~150+ words
  total. Preserve every citation and number; collapse the prose.
- Rough total cuttable across the slice: **~1,300-1,600 words**, almost all redundancy/verbosity, zero
  graded-claim loss. Best-built chapters for findability: **17** and **23** (verdict box + skim cue +
  tier key — keep as template). Loosest/longest-before-verdict: **07** and **24**.
