# Cycle 2 line-edit — slice 8

## PATTERNS

- **"genuinely" / "truly" / "really" as intensifiers (house-style violation — highest priority).**
  Slice-wide the word "genuinely" is used as a throwaway intensifier five times and "truly"/"really"
  once each. Global rule bans all three. Every one is deletable with zero meaning loss. Located below.
- **"robust" (AI-tell kill-word).** Appears as an evidence descriptor several times ("most robust
  finding," "robust, generalizable," "robust across every method"). Swap for *well-replicated /
  consistent / holds up*. **One exception must stay:** `19 §7.1` "0 = robust" is the Fried frailty
  *term of art* (robust vs. pre-frail vs. frail) — do NOT touch that one.
- **"boost" (AI-tell kill-word).** One live prose instance (`29 §4.3`, "can boost short-term
  engagement"). The `19 §4.3` "sperm-boost" is inside a quoted marketing product name — leave it.
- **Intentional antithesis motifs — protect, don't flatten.** The manual's spine is two deliberate
  contrasts: **predictor ≠ lever** and **healthspan, not lifespan**. These read as "X, not Y" but are
  load-bearing defined framings repeated on purpose; treat them as content, not as house-style slips.
  Do not "fix" them. Flag only *incidental* antithesis splices (a couple noted below).
- **Overall quality:** the strongest-reading chapters are `33-public-health-systems` and
  `WHAT-TO-TRACK-SYNTHESIS` (tight, varied, funny where it earns it). `29-behavior-change` is close
  behind. The weakest for pure prose mechanics is `19-life-stages` — its opener is a single dense
  run-on and it carries most of the intensifier hits.
- **Top overall fixes:** (1) delete every "genuinely/truly/really" intensifier; (2) fix the garbled
  "They're not smoking..." list in `09` §opener and §7 (momentarily reads as a negation); (3) break
  the `19` opening run-on paragraph.
