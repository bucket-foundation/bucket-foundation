# Cycle 3 — Slice 6 findings (practitioner: findability + length)

## PATTERNS

- **The manuals ALREADY contain their own bottom-line — it just lives at the *end*, not the top.** Every
  chapter closes with a strong "honest synthesis" / "honesty footer" / master-verdict paragraph that would
  serve perfectly as an up-front TL;DR box. The single highest-leverage move across all six files is to lift a
  1–3 line pointer (or a "Bottom line up front:" line) to the top of each chapter and each long Part. No new
  writing needed — the payoff exists, it's just buried under 3–5 pages of setup. Highest-value targets: §41
  (Part A/B/C are 8+ pages before the "Honesty footer" verdict), §28 (5 Parts, verdict at the very bottom),
  §32 (already has a "read this first" table — the best-in-class model the others should copy).
- **The big verdict tables need a "how to read / skim this" signpost AND several have a redundant column.**
  §31, §32, §30, §41 all open a load-bearing matrix with grade-code legends, but only §32 tells the reader
  "read this first." Add that cue to each. Two tables (§31 §8 recap matrix; §41 B.1 caveat column partly)
  duplicate prose that immediately surrounds them.
- **The "three/five honesty rules" preamble is restated 2–4× per chapter** (intro → rules block → per-section
  → synthesis → footer). The rules are the manual's spine and must stay *once*, stated well — but the 3rd and
  4th restatements are pure length. Biggest single length win in the slice: trimming the repeated
  mechanism-≠-outcome / predictor-≠-lever / natural-≠-safe restatements, especially where §28, §32, §30, §10
  each say it four times.
- **Cross-chapter redundancy:** the "natural ≠ safe," St John's Wort CYP3A4 inducer, DSHEA regulation gap,
  and placebo-is-real-but-bounded passages are written out in *full* in §28, §30, AND §32. One canonical
  treatment + cross-refs (which already exist) would remove ~400–600 words across the slice without losing a
  claim.
- **Verbosity tic:** long throat-clearing wind-ups ("This is where the section earns its keep", "If you read
  only one part of this section", "Read this before anything else") and em-dash hedging pileups. Tighten, don't cut.
- **Rough total cuttable across slice: ~2,600–3,300 words** (of ~32,000), almost all redundancy/verbosity —
  zero graded claims, numbers, tiers, citations, caveats, figures, or footnotes touched.
- **Best-organized (least work): §31** (four-beat pattern up front is a real TL;DR) and **§32** (verdict table
  first). **Worst for findability: §41** (longest wind-up before payoff) and **§28** (verdict buried under 5 Parts).
