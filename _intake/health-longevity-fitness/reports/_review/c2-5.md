# Cycle 2 line-edit findings — slice 5

## PATTERNS (cross-chapter + top fixes)

1. **"genuine / genuinely" is the crutch word of this slice.** It appears dozens of times across all six
   chapters as an intensifier ("genuinely bleaches," "genuinely preserves sight," "genuinely transformed,"
   "genuinely evidence-based," "genuinely less pain," "genuinely effective," "genuine modern win," "a genuine
   staging advance"). The house style bans "genuinely" outright and it reads as a tic. **Top fix: delete
   every "genuinely" and thin "genuine" to one use per section max.** The sentence is almost always stronger
   without it (a fact stated flat is more convincing than a fact insisted on).
2. **"real / really" as a filler adjective** ("a real option," "a real lever," "real progress," "real but
   supportive," "a real, RCT-supported advance"). Same tic, lower frequency. Cut where the noun already
   carries the weight.
3. **Antithesis ("X, not Y") is load-bearing here — mostly leave it.** The manual's entire method is
   `predictor ≠ lever`, `cohort ≠ RCT`, `mechanism ≠ outcome`, "managed, not cured," "diagnosis, not
   epidemic." These are the book's spine and should be preserved. I flag only the few places where a plain
   positive restatement is cleaner, and I flag the strongest ones as GOOD so they're protected. Do **not** do
   a blanket antithesis purge on this slice — it would gut the argument.
4. **Near-verbatim self-repeats inside a single chapter** (39 repeats its consciousness thesis twice in 30
   lines; 40 states its §07 cross-ref three times in 40 lines). Worth a dedup pass.
5. **The strongest writing is the cold-open first sentence of each chapter and the one-line hammers.** Protect
   34's "The failure is the arrest no one touched," 38's "Both things are true. Read it straight," 40's "An
   image is a measurement, not a verdict." These are the voice; don't sand them.

Best-reading chapters: **34** (emergency) and **38** (surgery) — tight, propulsive, earned voice.
Densest/most-in-need: **40 §40.3.2** (LNT) reads like a data dump, and **39** has the most repetition.
