# Biophysics Grading Framework — how to canon a claim physics can't yet fund

*Reference for the biophysics-branch work (health manual + Bucket canon). Built from four sourced research passes. The point: grade biophysics by physical reality + all the evidence, honestly — not by whether the Western guild funded an RCT — while refusing to give heterodoxy a free pass.*

## Why the evidence ladder alone is not neutral

The EBM hierarchy (meta > RCT > cohort > … > mechanism) grades **provenance**, and the provenance pipeline was selected. The Flexner Report (1910, Carnegie/Rockefeller-funded) standardized US medicine onto one lab-pharmacological paradigm and defunded the rest; within ~25 years medical schools roughly halved and homeopathic/eclectic/most women's and Black schools closed (Flexner "100 Years Later," PMC3178858; Rockefeller Archive). Inside EBM, three biases are documented by mainstream methodology, not cranks:

- **Funding bias** — industry-sponsored trials favor the sponsor, RR ~1.34, by a route risk-of-bias tools miss (Lundh/Bero, Cochrane MR000033). RCTs get run on what's **patentable and fundable**; light, cold, fields, minerals, behavior rarely get one — so their base looks thin for economic, not biological, reasons.
- **Language / index bias** — PubMed/Embase index mostly English journals; excluding non-English work biases results, worst in exactly the fields where Russian/Japanese/German primary work lives (Egger, *Int J Epidemiol* 2002; *J Clin Epidemiol* 2021). **"No evidence" often means "no English-indexed evidence."**
- **Base-rate** — the RCT tier is not automatically clean (Ioannidis, *PLoS Med* 2005).

Conclusion: the ladder is **one axis, not the ranking.**

## The framework — two axes, never collapsed, plus one gate

**Axis 1 — Mechanistic / first-principles plausibility.** Does it follow from (or at least not violate) known physics/chemistry?
`M0` contradicts conservation laws · `M1` no mechanism proposed yet (Semmelweis-1847) · `M2` plausible hand-wave · `M3` specific mechanism, falsifiable quantitative predictions · `M4` derived from first principles and quantitatively confirmed.

**Axis 2 — Evidence state, reported per literature tradition, never merged into one tier.** Show the disagreement:
`W` Western RCT/meta · `R` Soviet/Russian & other non-English · `C` clinical-observational/practitioner · `X` mechanistic/bench. Each cell tagged `strong / mixed / weak / absent / contested`. **`absent-W` is not evidence against** — it is usually a funding/patent/index artifact. A claim can legitimately read `X:strong, R:mixed, W:absent, C:strong`.

**The Gate — replicability & falsifiability, applied evenly.** Has the core effect reproduced in hands with no stake, ideally blinded? Does it make a prediction that could fail? This is what separates suppressed truth from pathological science — and it is applied identically to a pharma RCT and a Soviet clinic series.

**Reporting rule.** An entry shows `M-score | per-tradition evidence cells | gate status | one-line note`. `M3, W:absent, X:strong, gate:partial` is labeled *"mechanistically sound, under-tested"* — kept in view, not promoted to fact, not dismissed. **Axis 1 alone never promotes a claim to true** (Wegener's mechanism was wrong; cold fusion had a confident theory). Plausibility buys the right to be *tested and shelved in view*; only the gate promotes toward fact. That asymmetry is the safeguard against laundering wishful biophysics.

## The discernment rule (suppressed truth vs pathological science)

A heterodox claim earns "plausibly-suppressed-truth" by passing **all three**, regardless of who attacks or blesses it: (1) **independent replication** by a disinterested group, ideally blinded; (2) **mechanistic coherence** yielding falsifiable predictions (or a stable empirical regularity awaiting one — the Semmelweis case); (3) **falsifiability it has survived** — sharpens tests under criticism rather than reaching for ad hoc rescue or authority.

It is **pathological/ideological science** when the effect lives at the detection limit and shrinks under better controls, has no coherent mechanism, meets criticism with excuses, and survives on authority (state power → Lysenko; journal prestige → Benveniste; media → cold fusion) instead of surviving falsification. *Establishment status does not predict truth:* Blondlot was inside the French Academy and wrong; Semmelweis was an outsider and right; Lysenko was anti-establishment (against Mendel) and wrong. "Which establishment blessed it" is worthless as a discriminator.

| Case | Right? | Mechanism (then) | What decided it |

Semmelweis and Wegener are load-bearing: they show the axes come apart (right evidence, missing/wrong mechanism), which is why you never collapse them.

## The biophysics claim-map (graded, from the research passes)

**Real and neglected — earns honest shelf space (mechanism solid, evidence real if narrow):**
- **Ultraweak photon emission (UPE)** from cells via ROS chemiexcitation — `M-solid / X:established`. Legit oxidative-stress readout. (The *emission*, not any "field.")
- **Long-range biological electron transfer** (Marcus theory, Nobel 1992; respiratory/photosynthetic chains; Geobacter cytochrome EET exists) — `M4 / established`. Szent-Györgyi's *instinct*, correctly reframed.
- **Bioelectricity as an instructive signal** (Becker injury currents; Levin Vmem prepatterning in planaria/*Xenopus*) — `M-A / established in models, frontier in mammals`. The strongest-grounded member of the cluster.
- **EZ / exclusion-zone water phenomenon near Nafion** — `M-B+ / replicated by ≥10 labs`. A real phenomenon (with a *wrong* headline theory — see below).
- **Air ions → depression (esp. SAD)** — `M-moderate / W:mixed-positive` (Perez meta, *BMC Psychiatry* 2013; Terman trials). Single-group-heavy.
- **Geomagnetic storms → small CV/HRV effects** — `M-moderate / W:mixed` (Zilli-Vieira, *Sci Rep* 2019). Chizhevsky's defensible successor claim.
- **MM-wave (EHF) skin-mediated analgesia** — `M-moderate / W:weak-moderate` (Usichenko RCT, *Eur J Pain* 2008). Surface effect, opioid-linked.
- **Frey microwave-auditory effect** — `M-high / established` (thermoelastic; the real non-thermal-*seeming* RF effect the Soviets stressed).
- **Cytochrome-c-oxidase red/NIR absorption** (photobiomodulation mechanism) — `M-established / narrow outcomes`.

**Frontier — mechanism plausible, evidence thin or model-only, keep in view labeled as such:**
- Developmental/therapeutic bioelectricity **in mammals**; UPE as brain-activity marker; enzyme quantum tunneling *harnessed for rate* (the *occurrence* is established); radical-pair magnetoreception full in-vivo chain (birds); deuterium-depleted water anticancer (real isotope physics, thin single-group clinical).

**Overreach / did not hold — name it, and grade it that way:**
- **Mitogenetic *effect*** (light induces mitosis) — Langmuir's pathological-science exemplar; `failed`.
- **Popp coherent biophoton field** — "no reliable evidence for coherence/nonclassicality" (Cifra, *J Lumin* 2015); `M-implausible / failed`.
- **Kaznacheyev mirror cytopathic effect** — unreplicated outside Novosibirsk.
- **Pollack "fourth phase" structure + water health products** — neutron radiography finds no density difference; diffusiophoresis (Schurr) explains the real exclusion effect without a new phase; `M-D / health-claims F`. Same failure lineage as polywater (Elton & Spencer 2020).
- **Ling "no pumps"** — refuted; Na/K-ATPase is Nobel-established (Skou 1997).
- **Fröhlich coherent GHz resonances** underpinning EHF therapy — directly falsified (Lundholm, *Struct Dyn* 2015); the sharp "resonant frequencies" don't replicate.
- **Presman's universal "EM as primary information carrier"**, **Chizhevsky solar-cycle → wars/history** (numerology), **broad "microwave sickness"** at very low intensity, **air-ion serotonin mechanism** — all `failed/null`.

**Cautionary anchors (cite these so the reader sees the discipline):** polywater (Soviet physical chemistry produced both a wrong polywater *and* correct DLVO colloid theory — origin is orthogonal to correctness); Lysenko; N-rays; water memory. Grade on mechanism + replication, not the accent or the aesthetics.

## What this means for the book

1. The biophysics sections grade on **both axes, per-literature, with the gate** — never a single PubMed tier. "Not tested in a Western RCT" is stated as exactly that, distinct from "false" and from "true in the body."
2. The **real-and-neglected** list above earns honest shelf space with its mechanism stated and its thin/absent Western evidence stated plainly.
3. The **overreach** list is named as overreach with the specific killing result — that honesty is what makes including the neglected material credible.
4. The framework itself (two axes + the calibration table) is worth a short **teaching passage** in the foundations chapter: it shows the reader *how to weigh a biophysics claim*, which is more durable than any single verdict.

## Appendix — Jack Kruse as a source (graded)

Kruse is a recurring name in the biophysics/longevity space and one partial input to the 05-biophysics branch. He is **a generator of mechanism-first hypotheses at the frontier**, not a source of outcome evidence. This appendix fixes how to use him.

**Sourcing tier.** His quantum-health corpus is **entirely self-published** (jackkruse.com, Patreon, podcasts, his book *Epi-Paleo Rx*) — zero PubMed-indexed case reports on any light/EMF/water/mitochondria thesis; his peer-reviewed publications are from his neurosurgery career. His "CPC" (Clinical Practice Case) series reads as theoretical essays and member Q&A, not documented patient cases with baseline/outcome/follow-up data, and much is paywalled. His strongest "case" is his own **n=1** (~130 lb lost via cold thermogenesis + a "leptin reset"). Member outcomes are self-reported, advocate-collected forum testimonials. So every *clinical* Kruse claim sits at **`n=1 / anecdotal / C:advocate-collected`** — real reported experience, no controlled evidence, no independent replication.

**His signature theses, graded on the two axes (verbatim quotes):**

| Kruse claim (his words) | Axis 1 (mechanism) | Axis 2 (evidence) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| DHA essentiality for the brain (obligate seafood, perinatal DHA) | M3 | X/W:established | **Established** — largely textbook nutrition |
| "DHA … the first lipid on Earth to become a fully assembled semiconductor in one molecular piece … alternating holes (P) and electron rich (N) areas" | M2 (metaphor from the polyene π-cloud) | X:absent | **Speculative** — Szent-Györgyi *bulk-semiconductor* lineage, the superseded framing; long-range electron transfer is real, "DHA generates photocurrent in cells" is unshown |

| EZ water dielectric "rises to 160 from 78" (Pollack) | M2 | contested | **Contested** — Pollack lineage; the EZ phenomenon is real, the sweeping physiological scope is not |
| "the 'on and off switch' Darwin was looking for … is an ELF, called the Schumann resonance" controlling gene expression | M1 | absent | **Speculative overreach** — Schumann resonance is real EM physics; "it is the epigenetic master switch" is unsupported |

**The rule for using Kruse.** Mine his corpus for **sharp mechanistic questions** — some are good (the tunneling-distance anchor, DHA essentiality, morning-light/circadian entrainment, cold physiology) — grade each on the framework, and cite `jackkruse.com` with its `n=1/anecdotal` tier. Do not import his n=1 or member anecdotes as outcome evidence, and name the overreach (DHA-photocurrent, Schumann-as-switch, 80%-of-disease) as overreach on the same rule that credits the parts he gets right. Circadian core validated; extensions speculative — the split holds claim by claim.
