## 37-mitochondrial-health §37.8 (CoQ10 / Q-SYMBIO)
- claim: "**Q-SYMBIO** (Mortensen, *JACC HF* 2014): 300 mg/d ↓MACE & all-cause mortality in moderate-severe HF (HR ~0.5, i.e. roughly halved)"; body text: "n≈420, NYHA III–IV heart failure, CoQ10 300 mg/d for 2 years … roughly halved rate of MACE and all-cause mortality"  | cited: Mortensen 2014, doi:10.1016/j.jchf.2014.06.008, PMID 25282031
- **CONFIRMED** — Q-SYMBIO reported MACE HR 0.50 (CoQ10 15% vs placebo 26%, 43% relative reduction, p=0.005); all-cause mortality 10% vs 18% (42% relative reduction, p=0.036); CV death HR 0.51. 300 mg/d for 2 years, n=420, NYHA III–IV. Every figure matches, including the "~0.5 / roughly halved." https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25282031/

## 37-mitochondrial-health §37.8 (Urolithin A — Liu 2022)
- claim: "Liu 2022 (*Cell Rep Med*, middle-aged, ~+12% strength)"  | cited: Liu 2022, PMID 35584623
- **CONFIRMED** — ATLAS trial (n=88, untrained overweight middle-aged): +12% leg strength in the 500 mg group (+10% at 1000 mg), vs placebo. https://www.cell.com/cell-reports-medicine/pdf/S2666-3791(22)00158-6.pdf

## 37-mitochondrial-health §37.8 (Urolithin A — Singh 2022 primary endpoint)
- claim: "Singh 2022 (*JAMA Network Open*) … improved some measures of muscle endurance … but **did not hit the 6-minute-walk primary endpoint**"  | cited: Singh 2022, PMID 35050355
- **CONFIRMED** — Primary endpoint (change in 6-minute walk distance + hand-muscle ATP) did not reach significance vs placebo; muscle endurance (contractions to fatigue, secondary) improved significantly at 1000 mg. The "improved endurance, missed the 6MWT primary" framing is exact. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8777576/

## 12-mechanism-bridge §12.3.3 / State-of-the-Field §2 (protein↔mTOR age-dependent, Levine & Longo)
- claim: "high protein at ages 50–65 tracked higher all-cause and cancer mortality, **but at 65+ the association reversed and protein was protective**"  | cited: Levine & Longo NHANES (conflict-protein-mtor-longevity)
- **CONFIRMED** — Levine 2014 (*Cell Metab*, doi:10.1016/j.cmet.2014.02.006): ages 50–65 high protein → 75% higher all-cause mortality, 4× cancer mortality; at 65+ the effect reversed (28% lower all-cause, 60% lower cancer). Direction and age-stratification exactly as stated. https://www.cell.com/cell-metabolism/fulltext/S1550-4131(14)00062-X

## 12-mechanism-bridge §12.1 row 24 / 37 §37.8 / State-of-the-Field (NAD⁺ precursors raise blood NAD⁺ ~60%)
- claim: "raise blood NAD⁺ ~60%"  | cited: nad-precursor-nr-human-surrogate / conflict-nad-precursor-efficacy
- **CONFIRMED (approximate)** — Human NR RCTs raise whole-blood NAD⁺ in the ~40–90% range depending on dose (e.g. Martens/Trammell ~51% at 300 mg/d; higher-dose arms ~90%). ## State-of-the-Field §1 / 00-start-here (CRF ~5× mortality, ~13% per MET)
- claim: "~5× lower all-cause mortality elite-vs-low fitness, ~13% lower mortality per MET, no observed upper limit"  | cited: crf-vo2max-strongest-mortality-predictor, crf-per-met-mortality-meta
- **CONFIRMED** — Mandsager 2018 (*JAMA Netw Open*, n=122,007): low-vs-elite fitness adjusted HR 5.04, no upper limit of benefit. The "~13% per MET" is the standard Kodama 2009 (*JAMA*) meta figure (≈13% lower all-cause mortality per 1-MET increment). Both hold. https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2707428

## State-of-the-Field §1 / 12-mechanism-bridge §12.3.2 (strength training ~10–17% mortality, J-shape 30–60 min/wk)
- claim: "resistance activity ~10–17% lower mortality — with a J-shape … benefit peaks ~30–60 min/week"  | cited: resistance-training-mortality-meta
- **CONFIRMED** — Momma 2022 (*Br J Sports Med*, PMID 35228201): muscle-strengthening activity → 10–17% lower all-cause/NCD mortality; maximal risk reduction at ~30–60 min/wk with a J-shaped curve. Exact. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35228201/
