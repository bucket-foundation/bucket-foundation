# Cycle 2 line-edit — slice 2

## PATTERNS

**1. "genuinely" is the dominant AI-tell across the slice (top fix).** It appears as a bare intensifier
15+ times (heaviest in 14-nervous, 15-immune, 18-genetics). Global house style bans the word outright.
In almost every case it can be deleted with zero loss ("genuinely indexes" → "indexes", "genuinely
required" → "required", "genuinely useful" → "useful"). One global pass removing it does most of the AI-tell
cleanup for the slice. Same treatment for the two `robust`s in 15-immune and the `robustly` in 18.

**2. The "X, not Y" antithesis is this book's signature AND its most frequent house-style violation.**
The whole pedagogy runs on contrast pairs — `predictor ≠ lever`, `regulate, not boost`, `readout, not a
dial`. Those thesis-anchor pairs are load-bearing voice and should mostly stay. The problem is the
*incidental* comma-splice antitheses that pile up around them ("it is information, not a lever"; "a bystander,
not a reason"; "a high-risk-population lever, not a population supplement"). Recommendation: keep the named
thesis pairs; convert the throwaway ones to plain positive statements so the device keeps its punch. Flagged
representative instances per file below rather than every occurrence (there are dozens).

**3. Voice is strong and should be protected.** Every chapter opens with a real hook and keeps a dry, funny,
honest register ("moisturizer with a marketing budget", "expensive urine", "the uninvited tenant that ages
your T cells"). Do not sand these down. GOOD sentences flagged per file.

**4. A few long bullet-paragraphs need a break for rhythm** — the worst offenders are 18 §A.1 (the
"Polygenicity" mega-bullet) and 15 §2.4 (the CMV run-on). Everything else reads at a good varied pace.

**Best-reading chapters:** 13-endocrine and 11-body-systems (nearly free of the "genuinely" tic; tight,
well-bridged). **Most AI-tell cleanup needed:** 14-nervous and 15-immune.
