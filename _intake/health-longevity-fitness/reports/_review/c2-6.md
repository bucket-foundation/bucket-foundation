# Cycle-2 line edit — slice 6

## PATTERNS

Cross-chapter observations and the highest-leverage fixes across all six files.

- **`genuinely` / `genuine` overload is the dominant house-style violation in this slice.** The word
  "genuinely" appears as an intensifier ~20+ times across the six chapters (and "genuine/genuinely" together
  far more), and the global rule bans "genuinely/truly/really" outright. It has become a verbal tic:
  "genuinely counter-intuitive," "genuinely valuable," "genuinely hard," "genuinely different,"
  "genuinely revolutionary," "a genuinely dangerous failure mode," "genuinely promising." **Top fix: delete
  it almost everywhere.** In most cases the sentence is stronger with the word gone (the fact carries the
  emphasis). Located instances are listed per-file below; treat the file lists as representative, not
  exhaustive — a global pass for `\bgenuine(ly)?\b` is warranted.
- **Doctrinal antithesis is protected; prose antithesis is fair game.** These chapters run on defined-term
  contrasts — `predictor ≠ lever`, `mechanism ≠ outcome`, `cohort ≠ RCT`, `dose-sold ≠ dose-studied`. Those
  use the `≠` symbol, are load-bearing caveats/claims, and must NOT be touched (HARD RULE). I did not flag
  them. I *did* flag incidental "X, not Y" comma-splices in ordinary prose where a positive restatement
  preserves the meaning. These are far rarer than the doctrinal ones — the book mostly earns its contrasts.
- **The "The honest X:" / "honest" label is itself a tic.** "The honest framing," "the honest status," "the
  honest evidence," "the honest caveat," "the honest reading," "honest debunks," "the honest one-liner"
  recur dozens of times, sometimes 4–6 times in one chapter (esp. §10, §31, §32). Each instance is fine; the
  *cumulative* repetition dulls the word. Not a per-line rewrite — a suggestion to vary the framing lead-ins
  ("The evidence:", "In practice:", "Where it actually lands:") in a few spots so "honest" keeps its punch.
- **Voice is strong and should be protected.** The dry, blunt register ("say it straight," "the microscope is
  the gold standard *and* a source of real disagreement — both are true," "expensive urine," "the bathtub is
  honest; the chamber is the upsell") is the best thing about this slice. Flagged GOODs below so they're not
  sanded down.
- **Best-reading chapters: §41 (pathology) and §32 (biohacking)** — tight, well-varied, hooks land. **Most
  in need of a tightening pass: §10 (medical/pharmacology)** — a few genuinely-heavy paragraphs and the
  longest run-on sentences in the slice. **§28** is clean but has the densest "genuinely" cluster.
