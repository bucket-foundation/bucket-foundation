# IUPAC Gold Book

Source: https://goldbook.iupac.org/ (direct fetch 403; summary from Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IUPAC_books)
Fetched: 2026-05-01

Formal title: *Compendium of Chemical Terminology*. The "Gold Book" name honors Victor Gold, who initiated the first edition (1987). Second edition: A. D. McNaught and A. Wilkinson, eds., Blackwell Scientific, Oxford, 1997 (ISBN 0-86542-684-8). Online version maintained by IUPAC with continuing updates; entries are individually citable with DOIs. Function: the discipline-standard normative reference for chemical terms (definitions of "acid", "bond order", "transition state", "Gibbs energy", etc.). Translations: French, Spanish, Polish.

Canon relevance: discipline-standard reference work. Direct candidate for `03-chemistry/reference/`.
