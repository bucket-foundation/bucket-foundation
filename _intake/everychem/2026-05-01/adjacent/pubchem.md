# PubChem

Source: https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/about (direct fetch returned an empty stub; summary from Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PubChem)
Fetched: 2026-05-01

Operator: National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI), part of the National Library of Medicine (NIH). Launched September 2004. Contents (as of 2020): ~293M depositor-provided substance descriptions and ~111M unique chemical structures, plus bioassay data. License: U.S. Government work, public domain. Access: web UI, FTP bulk download, REST/PUG-REST API. Already a live x402 endpoint via the x402-research-gateway (see AGFarms Strategic Priority #8).

Canon relevance: not canon (it is a database, not a primary text). Operationally critical as a research substrate; relevant to feed402 supply-side strategy.
