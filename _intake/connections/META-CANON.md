# Meta-Canon — what the bridges actually mean

*Written 2026-05-10 from `BRIDGES.md` data + the corpus state at FTS=5,327 docs.*

The branch grid (mathematics · physics · chemistry · information · biophysics · cosmology · mind · deep-history · art · sacred-texts) is a **classification convenience**, not a partition of reality. The corpus shows it: every candidate bridge concept tested spans 10 of 10 branches with hundreds of hits each. **Canon is not seven pillars. Canon is a single weave with seven entry points.**

This document is the working interpretation: which bridges matter most, what they connect, and where the load-bearing fusions sit.

## The four primary bridges (load-bearing axes)

These are concepts that *most of the canon corpus is actually about*, regardless of which branch entry-point you came in through. Total FTS hit count is the rough mass of the bridge:

| Bridge | Mass | Where it shows up |
|---|---:|---|
| **time** | 1,224 | physics (relativity, arrow-of-time, entropy) · cosmology (cosmological time, Big Bang) · mind (Bergson, Husserl, James, phenomenology of duration) · sacred-texts (kairos vs chronos, eternity, eschatology) · biophysics (circadian, ageing, mitochondrial decay) |
| **light** | 1,033 | physics (Maxwell EM, photon, photoelectric effect) · biophysics (melanin/photosynthesis/biophoton/cytochrome NIR) · sacred-texts (Genesis fiat lux, Buddhist clear-light, Sufi lights) · art (illumination, chiaroscuro) |
| **information** | 1,019 | information (Shannon/Turing/Chaitin) · physics (Wheeler "it from bit", quantum measurement) · biophysics (DNA, signaling, neural codes) · cosmology (holographic principle, black-hole entropy) · mind (mental content, symbol grounding) |
| **energy** | 833 | physics (mechanics, thermodynamics, mass-energy) · chemistry (bond energy, reactions) · biophysics (mitochondrial ATP, bioenergetics) · cosmology (dark energy, vacuum energy) |

These four are the **canonical primitives** Bucket should treat as first-class. Every entry into canon should be tagged with which of these axes it touches.

## What the data tells us

1. **The seven branches are pedagogical, not ontological.** The canon doesn't actually live in branches. It lives in concept-bridges and author-connectors. The branch numbering is useful for navigation, dangerous if treated as a real partition.

2. **Light, time, information, energy are the four canonical axes.** Any new ingestion should ask: which of these four does this primarily feed? Most things feed ≥2.

3. **The Pollack-Becker-Ling-Marino-Mitchell line *is* the spine of branch 05-biophysics**, and it's the line Kruse repeatedly invokes. That's not coincidence — that's the structural reason Kruse is in canon.

4. **Penrose and Wheeler are the two living philosopher-physicists Bucket should treat as exemplar.** Each spans 4+ branches with technical-canonical work. They're the *shape* of what a canon contributor looks like.

5. **William James is the under-appreciated bridge author.** 389 hits, spans mind ↔ sacred ↔ physics-of-experience. Bucket should explicitly elevate him in 07-mind.

6. **Symmetry has low mass (125) but the cleanest fusion** — Noether's theorem literally maps math symmetries to physics conservation laws, 1:1, derivable. This is what *all* canon bridges should aspire to: derivable, not just thematic.
