## 🧭 Process

1. Pick a row, copy slug into a new `_intake/<slug>/` folder.
2. Stub `README.md` with target list + license/copyright check.
3. Use the right `agf-*` tool to ingest.
4. Once canon-tier and license-clean, promote to `bucket-canon/0X-branch/`.
5. Lens / evidence / gov-records material stays in `_intake/` or sibling track.

## ⚠️ Copyright reality check

Bucket Foundation is a nonprofit research index. We can ingest:
- Anything in the public domain (pre-1929 reliably; many older scientific
  works post-1929 also PD via expiration / non-renewal).
- Anything with explicit OA / CC license.
- Anything we have permission for (Kruse corpus = pending permission).

For copyrighted-but-canonical works (Becker, Pollack, Russell post-1953, etc.):
- We can hold **citations + extracts under fair use** in our canon index.
- We cannot host full PDFs publicly.
- Storage strategy: gdrive private mirror for internal research; canon
  entries point to original sources + held excerpt.
