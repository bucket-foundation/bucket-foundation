## Licensing landmines

- **OpenStax is currently CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, not plain CC BY 4.0.** Confirmed twice independently on openstax.org/license (site-wide footer) and on a book's own colophon. This reverses the widely-held assumption that OpenStax is fully open/commercial-safe; the license page also references a "recent update" to their licensing approach whose original blog post is gone (404). Verify per-book before treating any OpenStax title as unrestricted, and re-check this before any Bucket monetization touches OpenStax-derived content.
- **Khan Academy is CC BY-NC-SA**, non-commercial only, with an explicit ban on framing and paid-login walls. Bucket's free-to-read model is likely fine for link/embed use, but full caching into Bucket's own citeable graph, especially once Bucket's x402 citation-fee model or any sibling AGFarms revenue touches that content, crosses into "commercial advantage," which the license forbids.
- **CK-12 is no longer Creative Commons at all.** It moved from CC BY to CC BY-NC to a proprietary "CK-12 Curriculum Materials License" hosted at ck12info.org, a page this session could not read (blocked every fetch). Do not assume NC-style permissiveness; get eyes on the actual license text before ingesting anything CK-12.
- **MIT OCW and PhET are both CC-NC variants** (BY-NC-SA and BY-NC respectively). PhET's license goes further and defines "commercial use" to explicitly include "non-profits using simulations in paid services" — a nonprofit status alone does not exempt Bucket from PhET's commercial clause if any paid tier exists anywhere downstream.
- **Britannica has no public self-serve API and its ToS bans scraping/AI-training outright** ("data mining, robots, screen scraping... such as artificial intelligence... except with our express written consent"). The only lawful path is an enterprise syndication license, quote-based, and nothing on their site suggests education-nonprofit-friendly pricing. Treat as OUTSOURCE-or-skip, never as an ingest target.
- **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy explicitly discourages third-party hosting.** Authors grant Stanford exclusive worldwide publication rights; SEP's own guidance is to link out, not distribute copies. This is a link-only source, not a caching candidate, regardless of attribution.
- **HathiTrust: only about a third of the corpus (6.7M of ~18M volumes) is US public domain.** The rest is access-restricted and geographically gated by publication country/date. Bulk ingestion must filter tightly to the public-domain subset via their formal Data API/HTRC channel, not general scraping.
- **Wolfram Alpha is a paid computation vendor, not a content source** — budget it as an OUTSOURCE line item for any "show the computed answer" feature, not as free graph-ingestible data. Exact 2026 pricing figures could not be verified in this session (pricing page didn't render through available fetch tools) and should be confirmed directly with Wolfram before budgeting.
- **Library of Congress and Europeana rights are per-item, not blanket.** Both aggregate content from many contributing institutions with mixed rights statements (LOC) or standardized-but-varied rights tags (Europeana: CC0/CC BY/CC BY-SA/Public Domain Mark/"Copyright Not Evaluated"). Treat both as INGEST-with-a-rights-filter, never as blanket-open corpora.
