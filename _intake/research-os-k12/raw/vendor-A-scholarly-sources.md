## Licensing landmines

- **Elsevier ScienceDirect**: full text pulled via the API cannot be shared with third parties under any current terms; only a DOI list may be shared so others rebuild the corpus with their own key. Full access requires the requesting institution already hold a subscription. No minors carve-out, the ban is universal.
- **Wiley**: could not verify current terms directly, both TDM pages 403'd. Treat as non-commercial-research-only, no redistribution, until confirmed against Wiley's own site.
- **Springer Nature**: the Meta API and Full Text/TDM API are subscription-gated under a "TDM Reservation Policy." Only the separate Open Access API (CC-BY content) is safe to cache; do not conflate it with the paid APIs.
- **Clarivate Web of Science**: no free tier exists at all. A paid license is required before any call succeeds, and redistribution terms are not public.
- **JSTOR**: Constellate/TDM output must not be redistributed and in some cases must be deleted after the project ends, noncommercial research only. The free-reading allowance drops to 10 articles per 30 days on September 1, 2026, ruling JSTOR out as a bulk source regardless of TDM terms.
- **Semantic Scholar**: a meaningful share of "S2 Data" is tagged CC-BY-NC or ODC-BY per dataset. CC-BY-NC content cannot legally enter a paid, x402-metered citation flow; it has to be filtered out or handled outside the monetized graph.
- **Dimensions DSL API**: its own terms state the API is "not intended for bulk data or to power dashboards or other derivative products," which is a direct, self-declared bar against the kind of ingestion a knowledge graph needs.
- **CORE**: aggregates from open-access repositories, but license is inherited per source record, not granted blanket by CORE. Some harvested items remain all-rights-reserved despite repository hosting. Check the license field on every record before caching or showing to minors.
- **bioRxiv/medRxiv**: license is author-chosen per preprint (CC-BY, CC-BY-NC, CC-BY-ND, or full opt-out). A nontrivial share carries NC or ND terms that block redistribution even though the preprint itself reads as "open."
