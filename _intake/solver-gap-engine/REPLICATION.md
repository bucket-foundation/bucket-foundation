# Replication Funding

## Unit Of Work
A claim: one theorem or empirical result, a Lean statement when one exists, and a sample protocol that states inputs, range and pass rule.

## Flow
1. A group claims a topic from the gap candidate list or the canon.
2. The group runs the sample protocol and posts results with raw data and code.
3. Independent groups replicate the protocol. Each replication is its own citeable record.
4. A result counts as replicated after two independent groups match it.

## Payment
Citations pay through x402 as today. A cited claim splits its fee between the original author and each replicating group. A replication pool funds replications of claims with the highest citation counts.

## Checks
Lean claims are checked by the kernel. Empirical claims are checked by rerunning the posted code on the posted samples.
