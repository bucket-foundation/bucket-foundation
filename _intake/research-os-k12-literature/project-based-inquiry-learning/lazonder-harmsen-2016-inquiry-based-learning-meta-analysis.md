# Meta-Analysis of Inquiry-Based Learning

A meta-analysis of 72 studies comparing the effectiveness of different types of guidance in
inquiry-based learning across age groups.

## Key Claims

- Across 72 studies, guidance showed facilitative overall effects on learning activities
  (d = 0.66), performance success (d = 0.71), and learning outcomes (d = 0.50), each with a
  confidence interval excluding zero.
- Type of guidance moderated the effect on performance success but not on learning
  activities or learning outcomes.
- Considerable variation appeared in guidance effects on learning activities, and the
  available studies did not permit a definitive conclusion on age-related differences.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Which of this meta-analysis's guidance-type categories the four separate tools (Locate,
  Quote, Check, Organize) each correspond to, since one workspace mixes several guidance
  types.
- Whether the age-related guidance question this meta-analysis leaves unresolved is
  answerable from a narrow-grade-band pilot, or needs district-scale data.

## How It Bears on Research OS

The most direct, quantified precedent for the four-tool workspace's own design: guidance
helps inquiry-based learning overall, but which type of guidance helps which outcome is not
uniform, an argument against assuming the shared "no tool writes the answer" constraint is
enough specification on its own. Extends overlap-map questions 1 and 10 with a quantified
guidance effect size; a second external anchor for `PREREGISTRATION-DRAFT.md`'s Sampling Plan
alongside Chen and Yang (2019).
