# Designing the Knowledge Integration Environment

Proposes knowledge integration, adding, distinguishing among, and connecting ideas
into a coherent view, as a continuous measure of understanding, and shows learners
routinely hold contradictory ideas side by side without noticing.

## Key Claims

- Learners routinely hold contradictory ideas about the same phenomenon without
  noticing the contradiction.
- Prompting learners to generate, compare, and reconcile competing ideas produces more
  durable integration than presenting one correct idea for memorization.
- Knowledge integration scores predicted transfer performance better than a
  single correct-answer post-test.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether a knowledge integration score transfers to the shorter claim-evidence
  productions the workspace produces.
- How a continuous integration score would combine with the current binary
  progression.

## How It Bears on Research OS

Extends `LEARNER-STATE-MODEL.md` OPEN-3 with a continuous alternative to the binary
knowledge-type question: score how many ideas a learner connected and how well
contradictions were reconciled, rather than which knowledge type was reached.
