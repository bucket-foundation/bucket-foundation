# The Use of Scoring Rubrics: Reliability, Validity and Educational Consequences

Reviews inter-rater agreement studies on rubric-scored open-ended student work and
finds topic-specific rubrics with benchmark examples and rater training raise
agreement, though a residual disagreement rate persists across rubric quality levels.

## Key Claims

- Topic-specific analytic rubrics produced higher inter-rater agreement than generic
  rubrics reused across tasks.
- Benchmark examples and rater training sessions raised agreement above a rubric's text
  description used alone.
- A residual disagreement rate persisted even for well-constructed, trained, and
  benchmarked rubrics.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- What agreement rate a production rubric would need to clear before contest
  disagreement reads as measurement error versus a real difference in judgment.
- Whether benchmarks and training would raise agreement between a human reviewer and
  Check's own verdict.
