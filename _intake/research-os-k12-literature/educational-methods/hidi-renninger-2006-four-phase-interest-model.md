# The Four-Phase Model of Interest Development

Proposes four ordered phases of interest development, from situationally triggered to
individually sustained, each requiring a different kind of support, and argues
interest and learning are reciprocally causal.

## Key Claims

- Interest development moves through four phases distinguished by situational-versus-
  individual origin and passing-versus-self-sustained status.
- Each phase responds to a different support type, novelty for triggered interest,
  self-set goals for well-developed individual interest.
- Interest and learning are reciprocally causal rather than interest being a
  one-directional predictor.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether the four interest phases correlate with, are independent of, or overlap
  Bucket's five states.
- What scaffold-fading schedule the model implies as interest phase, alongside state,
  advances.

## How It Bears on Research OS

whether the five states track triggered-through-individual interest or measure
