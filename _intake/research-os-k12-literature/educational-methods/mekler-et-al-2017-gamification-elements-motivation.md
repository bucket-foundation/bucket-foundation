# Towards Understanding the Effects of Individual Gamification Elements

An experiment isolating gamification elements on an image-tagging task: points and
levels raised performance without a measured drop in intrinsic motivation; no element
tested produced a significant motivational cost.

## Key Claims

- Points and levels raised task output relative to a no-element control, with no
  significant drop in self-reported intrinsic motivation.
- A leaderboard and a narrative-story element showed no significant change in either
  performance or motivation relative to control.
- No element tested produced a significant decrease in intrinsic motivation, a null
  result for the crowd-out prediction in this task.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether the null crowd-out result generalizes from a short, low-stakes task to a
  recurring school task.
- Whether recognition elements are a safer performance lever than tangible payment.

## How It Bears on Research OS
