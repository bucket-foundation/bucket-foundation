# The ICAP Framework: Linking Cognitive Engagement to Active Learning Outcomes

Names the framework `learning/research-os/PLAN.md` section 2 and `learning/research-os/LEARNER-STATE-MODEL.md` section 2 map the five Research OS learner states against first, four ordered engagement modes, Passive, Active, Constructive, Interactive, each argued to produce more durable learning than the one before it.

## Key Claims

- Overt behaviors during learning can be sorted into four modes by what the learner does with the material: Passive (attending with no added output), Active (physically manipulating the material with no new content generated), Constructive (generating output beyond what was given), Interactive (a joint construction with a partner where each utterance builds on the other's).
- The four modes are predicted to be ordered in the learning they produce, Interactive at or above Constructive, Constructive above Active, Active above Passive, because each successive mode requires the deeper cognitive processing the one before it does not.
- The ordering held across a review of the empirical literature spanning many domains and age groups, with the clearest evidence for Constructive beating Active and Active beating Passive; the Interactive-over-Constructive step has fewer controlled studies isolating it cleanly.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether a construct with more than four levels, such as Bucket's own five-state model, adds predictive power over ICAP mode alone, or only re-describes the same four-way ordering under new labels.
- How to classify an activity that mixes modes within one session, such as a learner who reads passively then writes a constructive explanation, for the purpose of a single engagement-mode label.

## How It Bears on Research OS

`LEARNER-STATE-MODEL.md` section 2 maps Awareness to Passive, Understanding to Constructive, and Production to Interactive, with Internalization landing on Constructive today and moving toward Interactive once the scripted peer step `learning/research-os/PLAN.md` section 3 plans ships. `RESEARCH-QUESTIONS.md` item 1 is written to test the open question above directly: whether Bucket's five states predict delayed transfer beyond ICAP mode, or restate it.
