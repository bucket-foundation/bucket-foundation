# Worked-Out Examples: Instructional Explanations Support Learning by Self-Explanations

Shows that brief, rationale-focused instructional explanations added to a worked
example scaffold self-explanation rather than replace it, improving near-transfer
outcomes over both unexplained examples and unscaffolded self-explanation prompts.

## Key Claims

- Short instructional explanations increased the frequency of learners' own
  self-explanations rather than substituting for them.
- Scaffolded examples outperformed unexplained examples and unprompted self-explanation
  on near-transfer post-tests.
- The benefit required explanations to target a step's rationale rather than restate
  its content.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether a Check verdict can pair with a brief targeted hint without collapsing into
  explanation-replaces-self-explanation.
- What counts as rationale-focused versus content-restating for an automatically
  generated hint.

## How It Bears on Research OS

rationale-focused hint scaffolds rather than replaces inference generation, a design
