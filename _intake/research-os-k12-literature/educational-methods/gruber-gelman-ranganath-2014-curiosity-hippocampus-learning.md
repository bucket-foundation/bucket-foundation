# States of Curiosity Modulate Hippocampus-Dependent Learning via the Dopaminergic Circuit

Shows high-curiosity states raise memory for the curiosity-satisfying answer and, with
it, for unrelated incidental material encountered nearby, linking the effect to
hippocampal and dopaminergic activity.

## Key Claims

- High-curiosity trivia answers were remembered better than low-curiosity answers at a
  one-day delay.
- High curiosity also raised memory for unrelated incidental material shown during the
  waiting period, a spillover effect.
- fMRI data linked high curiosity to increased hippocampal and dopaminergic-circuit
  activity.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether the spillover effect appears for material adjacent to a high-curiosity task
  in a classroom workspace.
- How long a curiosity-induced encoding gain lasts in a realistic multi-task session.
