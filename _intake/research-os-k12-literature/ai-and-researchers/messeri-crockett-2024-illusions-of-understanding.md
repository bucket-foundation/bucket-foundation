# Artificial Intelligence and Illusions of Understanding in Scientific Research

A framework naming four illusions AI tools can create in research: explanatory depth,
exploratory breadth, objectivity, and universal applicability, each a case of progress
that looks real without a matching gain in understanding.

## Key Claims

- An illusion of explanatory depth arises when a researcher accepts an AI explanation
  as their own understanding without being able to reconstruct it independently.
- An illusion of exploratory breadth arises when AI tools make a narrow slice of a
  possible space feel like the whole space.
- These illusions compound most where a field lacks cheap ground-truth checking,
  a condition the paper notes is less true in domains with fast, cheap verification.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- What practices reliably catch an illusion of understanding before it enters a
  field's literature.
- Whether cheap verification, available in a K-12 classroom, is enough to prevent the
  illusion or only its surface form.

## How It Bears on Research OS

Names the exact risk behind the overlap map's question 12: Production should be graded
against the illusion-of-explanatory-depth case this paper describes, not against
whether a claim merely passes the Check tool. A learner unable to reconstruct their own
