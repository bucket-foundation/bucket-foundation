# The Ideation-Execution Gap: Execution Outcomes of LLM-Generated versus Human Research Ideas

The direct execution-focused follow-up to Si, Yang, and Hashimoto (2024): the novelty
advantage LLM-generated ideas showed at the ideation stage shrinks, and can flip, once the
idea is actually executed.

## Key Claims

- 43 expert researchers were recruited to each execute one of a set of randomly assigned
  research ideas, either written by human experts or generated by an LLM, spending over
  100 hours per idea and writing a short paper documenting the resulting experiments.
- Blind expert review of the executed projects found LLM-generated ideas' scores
  (novelty, excitement, effectiveness, and overall) dropped significantly more after
  execution than human-written ideas' scores did, closing most of the ideation-stage gap
  Si, Yang, and Hashimoto (2024) found in favor of LLM-generated ideas.
- On several review metrics the ranking even flips: human-written ideas score higher
  than LLM-generated ideas once execution outcomes, not just the idea itself, are judged,
  evidence current LLMs are better at producing ideas that read as novel than at
  producing ideas that execute into strong results.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether the same ideation-execution gap this paper measures for open-ended NLP
  research ideas applies to the narrower, more scaffolded hypothesis space the hypothesis
  engine searches, where a candidate hypothesis is built from slot-filled evidence rather
  than generated freely.
- Whether a K-12 Production, authored by a learner using AI-scoped tools rather than
  generated wholesale by an LLM, would show the same execution-stage score drop, a
  different division of labor than either arm this study tests.

## How It Bears on Research OS

Extends `docs/RESEARCH-OS-INTEGRATION.md` question 21's own reporting-quality caution
with an execution-stage measurement the earlier ideation-only study could not provide: an
AI-generated hypothesis that looks good before it is checked is exactly the case the
engine's own holdout ledger is built to catch, and this paper's own finding, that novelty
judged at ideation time overstates novelty judged after execution, is direct evidence for
withholding a validated ranking label until enough holdout cases clear
`MIN_VERIFIED_FOR_LABEL = 20`, rather than trusting an unexecuted hypothesis's own
apparent quality.
