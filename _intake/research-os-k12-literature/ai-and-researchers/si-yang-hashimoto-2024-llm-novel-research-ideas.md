# Can LLMs Generate Novel Research Ideas? A Large-Scale Human Study with 100+ NLP Researchers

The most direct empirical test available of whether an LLM can generate research ideas that expert researchers rate as more novel than ideas from other expert researchers, the exact question underlying whether the hypothesis engine's own generated hypotheses could count as a real contribution rather than a re-ranking of existing evidence.

## Key Claims

- In a blind, controlled comparison, expert NLP researchers rated LLM-generated research ideas as more novel, on average, than ideas generated by other expert human researchers on the same prompts, though not rated as more feasible.
- The same study found LLM idea generators showed less diversity across their own outputs than the human researchers did, repeatedly proposing similar ideas even when prompted to generate many.
- The authors caution that novelty and feasibility, as rated by experts reading a written idea, are a proxy for research value rather than a direct test of whether the idea would produce a successful, publishable finding if pursued.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether novelty ratings on a written idea predict eventual research success if the idea were pursued through to a finished study, a follow-up the paper's own authors flag as unresolved.
- How an LLM idea generator's own low output diversity interacts with a system, like the hypothesis engine, that already generates a large combinatorial hypothesis population and relies on a tournament to surface the best ones from that population.

## How It Bears on Research OS

Is the closest existing evidence for whether the hypothesis engine's own combinatorial generation step could, in principle, surface something an expert would call novel: the same low-diversity risk this study documents for LLM idea generation is a direct concern for a generator drawing repeatedly from the same fixed slot vocabulary, and argues for treating the engine's own self-report's coverage-rate checks as a diversity audit, beyond a calibration one.
