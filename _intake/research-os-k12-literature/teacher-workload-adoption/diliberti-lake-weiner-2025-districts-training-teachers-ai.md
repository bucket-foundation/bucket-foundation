# More Districts Are Training Teachers on Artificial Intelligence: Findings from the American School District Panel

Reports rising district-level AI training for teachers concentrated on general
literacy and acceptable-use policy rather than subject-specific integration, with
unclear data-privacy guidance named as a leading barrier to expansion.

## Key Claims

- District-reported AI training rose across survey waves, with the largest gains among
  districts that had offered none previously.
- Training content concentrated on general AI literacy and acceptable-use policy over
  tool-specific or subject-specific integration.
- Unclear data-privacy guidance and a lack of vetted, subject-aligned tools were the
  most common named barriers to expansion.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether a pilot's onboarding would read as the subject-specific guidance this survey
  finds largely missing.
- How a constrained workspace's data-privacy posture compares against districts'
  vetted-tool bar.
