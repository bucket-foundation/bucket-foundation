# Towards Hybrid Human-AI Learning Technologies

Proposes a six-level scale of AI control over classroom learning and reports deployed
systems cluster at intermediate, shared-regulation levels, with teacher trust and
workload lagging behind system accuracy at higher control levels.

## Key Claims

- AI control over learning spans six levels, from full teacher control through shared
  regulation to full AI adaptation with no teacher checkpoint.
- Deployed systems cluster at intermediate levels where AI proposes and a teacher or
  learner disposes.
- A system granted too much control can outrun teacher trust even when more accurate,
  a barrier independent of correctness.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Where Research OS's own workspace sits on this scale, given Check and Organize run
  without a checkpoint while Production requires one.
- Whether lowering oversight below the current Production gate would outrun teacher
  trust the way reviewed systems did.
