# High school science fair: Student opinions regarding whether participation should be required or optional and why

Surveys high school and post-high-school STEM-track students on whether science fair
participation should be required, finding little support for requiring the competitive
version while support for a non-competitive version rises with maturity and experience.

## Key Claims

- Few students in either surveyed group thought competitive science fair should be required,
  citing lack of enjoyment and interest in competing as the leading reasons.
- Support for requiring a non-competitive version ranged as high as 91 percent and rose with
  maturation, experience, and STEM-track enrollment, citing scientific-thinking and
  research-skill learning as the leading reasons in favor.
- Students opposed to even the non-competitive requirement cited lack of enjoyment and
  interest, and some questioned the fairness of how science fair is run.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether the same required-versus-optional split would hold for a Production submission
  carrying a citation payment rather than a competitive ranking.
- What design features separate an endorsable non-competitive deliverable from a
  disendorsed competitive one, past ranking alone.

## How It Bears on Research OS

Bears on H3 and on whether Production submission should ever be mandatory: requiring a
competitive deliverable draws the least support, while a non-competitive one draws broad
support conditional on maturity and experience, an argument for Research OS's existing
non-competitive Production design and a flag for fairness-of-process risk in the teacher-
review ladder.
