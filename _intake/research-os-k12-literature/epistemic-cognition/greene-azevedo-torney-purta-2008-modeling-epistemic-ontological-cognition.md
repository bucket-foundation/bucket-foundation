# Modeling Epistemic and Ontological Cognition: Philosophical Perspectives and Methodological Directions

Proposes an integrated model across four qualitatively different epistemic positions,
scored along three dimensions, with justification for a knowledge claim as a central
construct.

## Key Claims

- Integrates aspects of several developmental and belief-system models of personal
  epistemology into a single model characterizing four qualitatively different epistemic
  positions, realism, dogmatism, skepticism, and rationalism, by how a person's beliefs
  pattern across three dimensions.
- The model incorporates philosophical epistemology directly, in particular a focus on a
  person's multiple means of justification for a knowledge claim, rather than treating
  justification as a single undifferentiated belief.
- Proposes a statistical approach for testing the model's own construct and predictive
  validity by examining relationships among the four positions, the three dimensions, and
  outside covariates, addressing psychometric concerns Hofer and Pintrich (1997) raise
  about earlier quantitative measures.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether the four-position model's own emphasis on multiple means of justification maps
  onto what Check's own citation-validity verification checks, a single-source
  justification standard, or whether Check's own design implicitly assumes a rationalist
  position without naming it.
- Whether the model's own proposed statistical validation approach has ever been applied
  to a K-12 sample specifically, a gap this paper, aimed at a general audience of
  researchers, does not close.

## How It Bears on Research OS

Gives `PLAN-REVISION-3.md` section 2d's own harder, understanding-specific bar a richer
candidate structure than a single certainty dimension: if a Production's own
justification field were scored against this model's own multiple-means-of-justification
criterion, rather than against whether a citation merely exists, the rubric would be
targeting a specific philosophical position, rationalism, the model names as one of four,
rather than assuming justification is a single undifferentiated behavior every learner
either does or does not perform.
