# Leveraging Khanmigo for Personalized Tutoring

A mixed-methods study comparing Khan Academy's Khanmigo AI tutor against unassisted
web search and paper-based instruction for undergraduate physics learning, finding
Khanmigo users scored higher than web-search users and reported higher engagement.

## Key Claims

- Khanmigo users showed higher post-test physics scores than unassisted web-search
  users, and scores comparable to paper-based instruction.
- Khanmigo users reported higher engagement and motivation than the other two
  conditions.
- The authors attribute the result partly to Khanmigo's Socratic, prompting-rather-
  than-answering design, though this was not isolated experimentally.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether the gain over web search reflects Socratic questioning specifically or
  structured tutoring generally.
- Whether the result generalizes from undergraduate physics to K-12 students or less
  structured subjects.

## How It Bears on Research OS

Is the closest published comparator to the Check tool: Khanmigo's prompting-rather-
than-answering design is the same constraint Research OS's tools enforce by
construction, and its outperformance of unassisted search is a real-world data point
for the three-arm testbed in `RESEARCH-QUESTIONS.md` Q8, though it lacks the
testbed's own full-chatbot arm.
