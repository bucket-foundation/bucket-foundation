# Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence: Reliable, Safe & Trustworthy

Argues human control and system automation are separable design axes rather than a
single tradeoff, so a system can raise automation while a governance structure, audit
trails, oversight, keeps a human accountable for consequential outputs.

## Key Claims

- Human control and automation are separable dimensions rather than opposite ends of
  one scale.
- A reliable, safe, trustworthy system pairs automated components with governance
  structures that keep a human accountable.
- Full automation without oversight and full manual control without automated support
  are both named design failure modes.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Where Research OS's four tools sit on the separable control-and-automation grid.
- What audit trail this framework's governance requirement would call for on a
  Production claim.

## How It Bears on Research OS

Extends overlap map questions 1 and 10: gives a vocabulary for what the workspace
already does structurally, high-automation retrieval tools paired with a
teacher-review gate that functions as this paper's own governance structure.
