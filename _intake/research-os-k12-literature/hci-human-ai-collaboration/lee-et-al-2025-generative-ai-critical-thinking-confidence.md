# The Impact of Generative AI on Critical Thinking

A survey of 319 knowledge workers and 936 real generative-AI use examples: higher
confidence in AI output predicted less critical thinking; higher self-confidence
predicted more, with critical thinking shifting toward verification and integration
rather than disappearing.

## Key Claims

- Higher trust or confidence in AI output was associated with less critical thinking
  during the task, across real use examples.
- Higher self-confidence in unassisted task ability was associated with more critical
  thinking, the opposite direction from AI confidence.
- Generative AI use shifted the nature of critical thinking toward verification,
  integration, and stewardship, away from original generation.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether the qualitative shift toward verification represents skill loss or
  redeployed effort.
- Whether the confidence-based pattern holds for K-12 students, whose confidence
  calibration differs from surveyed adults.
