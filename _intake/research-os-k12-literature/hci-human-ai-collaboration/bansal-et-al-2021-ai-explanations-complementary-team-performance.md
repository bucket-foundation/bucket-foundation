# Does the Whole Exceed its Parts? The Effect of AI Explanations on Complementary Team Performance

> **Promoted to outcome tier, 2026-09-10.** This record lives in

> size, RUBRIC E7, and depends on the foundation record named by

> the canon entry is the citeable record.

Tests whether a human paired with an explainable AI outperforms either alone and finds
complementary performance was rare, with convincing explanations sometimes raising
agreement with a wrong AI answer rather than raising team accuracy.

## Key Claims

- Complementary team performance, exceeding both human alone and AI alone, was rare
  even when explanations raised stated trust in the AI.
- More convincing explanations sometimes increased agreement with a wrong AI answer,
  an over-reliance effect distinct from a trust effect.
- A person's own independent accuracy predicted complementary performance better than
  any explanation-design variable tested.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether retrieval-only tools sidestep the over-reliance risk this paper documents
  for explanation-carrying AI.
- Whether a learner's own independent skill predicts complementary performance better
  than any workspace feature.

## How It Bears on Research OS

Extends overlap map questions 1 and 10 with a direct empirical test: complementary
performance was rare, and convincing explanations could raise over-reliance rather
than accuracy, a caution against assuming Check's verdict produces complementary
performance by design.
