# Generative AI Enhances Individual Creativity but Reduces Collective Diversity

A randomized experiment: generative-AI-assisted writers produced more individually
creative stories, but the AI-assisted population of stories was less diverse from
each other than an unassisted population, a cost concentrated in writers who adopted
the AI's suggestion directly.

## Key Claims

- AI-assisted writers produced stories judged more creative individually than
  unassisted writers.
- Stories in the AI-assisted group were more similar to each other than stories in
  the unassisted group, a collective diversity loss.
- The diversity loss was larger for writers who used the AI suggestion directly than
  for writers who substantially altered it.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether the individual-gain, collective-loss trade holds at the same rate across
  different task types.
- Whether prompting divergence from the AI's first suggestion recovers diversity
  without losing the individual gain.

## How It Bears on Research OS
