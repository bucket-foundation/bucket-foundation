# Does Using AI Assistance Accelerate Skill Decay and Hinder Skill Development Without Performers' Awareness?

A laparoscopic surgical-skill training study finding that AI-assisted trainees showed
faster unassisted skill decay after training than unassisted trainees, with no
difference in either group's confidence about their own skill.

## Key Claims

- AI-assisted training produced faster decline in unassisted performance over a
  retention interval than unassisted training.
- Confidence ratings did not track the decay: assisted trainees were not aware their
  skill had decayed more.
- AI-assisted trainees performed as well or better during the assisted phase itself,
  masking the later decay.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether the same decay pattern holds for cognitive and reading-comprehension tasks
  rather than psychomotor skill.
- Whether informing trainees of the decay risk changes offloading behavior.

## How It Bears on Research OS

Argues Research OS's Production state should be graded on an unassisted,
unannounced check scheduled after a retention interval, not immediately after the
assisted session, since same-day testing would miss the decay this study documents.
Read alongside Bastani and colleagues (2025) for the exam-performance parallel.
