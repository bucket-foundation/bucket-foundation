# Adaptive Learning Using Artificial Intelligence in e-Learning

A systematic review of 63 papers on AI-driven adaptive learning, classifying
approaches into content personalization, path optimization, and performance
prediction, and naming a field-wide gap in long-term retention and transfer
measurement.

## Key Claims

- Adaptive-learning approaches split into content-based personalization, path
  optimization, and performance prediction, with path optimization least represented.
- Knowledge-graph and prerequisite-based methods recur across the reviewed path-
  optimization literature, alongside reinforcement-learning and collaborative-
  filtering approaches.
- Most reviewed systems are evaluated on short-term or simulated gains rather than
  long-term retention or transfer.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Which path-optimization technique family performs best in a head-to-head comparison
  rather than separate studies.
- Whether the field-wide transfer-measurement gap is closing in more recent work.

## How It Bears on Research OS

Places the prerequisite-graph papers in this branch inside the wider field of
adaptive path optimization, confirming frontier-backward routing is one instance of a
technique family. Its named gap, short-term rather than transfer-tested evaluation, is
the same gap `RESEARCH-QUESTIONS.md` Q5 and the overlap map's question 3 already flag
for Research OS's own routing evaluation.
