# Concept Mapping: A Useful Tool for Science Education

Sets out validity criteria for a concept map's own claimed relations, expert review
against a domain's accepted structure, and documents that novice maps commonly lack
the cross-links connecting otherwise separate branches that expert maps show.

## Key Claims

- A concept map's propositions require checking against the domain's own accepted
  structure or expert judgment, a bar past internal consistency alone.
- Novice-built maps commonly show flat, list-like hierarchies missing cross-links
  present in expert-built maps.
- Repeated mapping across a course revealed restructuring a single end-of-unit
  snapshot would have missed.

## Research Questions It Leaves Open

- Whether the novice-flat, expert-cross-linked pattern also distinguishes a
  low-confidence `inferred` edge from a high-confidence curator-authored one.
- Whether tracking a graph's edge set over multiple snapshots would reveal similar
  restructuring.

## How It Bears on Research OS

Extends `LEARNER-STATE-MODEL.md` OPEN-2 by analogy: a concept map earns validity
through expert review against accepted structure, the same standard `ROUTING.md`'s
teacher-flag path applies to a reviewed `inferred` edge.
