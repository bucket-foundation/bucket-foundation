## Residual issues

Flagged, left as they are; the reasoning for each follows.

- **`app:beads` has no incoming `\Cref`.** The Bead-list appendix section
  itself is never cross-referenced from the body (its own `tab:beads`
  table is). Left alone: it is a self-contained reference appendix, the
  same role the Glossary appendix plays, and forcing an artificial
  in-body citation would not make either more useful.
- **Overfull-hbox warnings** on eight `\texttt{}` bead names and four
  glossary spec-path URLs (pre-existing, unrelated to this pass's edits;
  confirmed present before and after by rerunning `make pdf` against the
  original committed `main.tex`). These are LaTeX line-breaking warnings,
  not errors, and fixing them means either shortening the bead/spec
  identifiers themselves (a naming decision outside this review) or
  hand-tuning column widths in the `longtable`/`tabular` definitions;
  left as a known toolchain wrinkle, same class as the missing
  `cleveref`/`glossaries` packages `PAPER-STANDARDS.md` already tracks.
- **`figures/*.pdf` are not byte-reproducible across runs**, confirmed
  this pass: two back-to-back `make figures` invocations produced
  images identical in every respect except one embedded PDF
  `/CreationDate` timestamp. This contradicts `PAPER-STANDARDS.md`'s
  Figures rule ("two runs of `make figures` on the same source produce
  the same bytes") at the byte level while honoring it in substance: the
  rendered content stays identical, and only matplotlib's PDF backend
  timestamp differs. Fixing it needs
  `savefig(..., metadata={'CreationDate': None})` or a pinned
  `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH` in every `fig_*.py`; left alone as a toolchain-wide
  pattern shared across every figure script in this repository, and
  touching seven committed binaries sat outside the requested scope.
- **`Bucket.Belief.fuse` is now uncited.** It remains a correct,
  compiling Lean implementation of Jøsang cumulative fusion, but no
  current paper claim depends on it after the `def:stemma` fix. Left in
  place (never delete or weaken working Lean) as forward-looking
  infrastructure for a future run that fuses two independent generation
  passes' opinions; the paper does not claim it does today.
