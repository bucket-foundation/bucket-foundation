# Monetarism

ID `M-monetarism`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: monetary theory.

## What It Is
Milton Friedman's school: policy should follow a rule for money growth. The 1968 AEA address, "The Role of Monetary Policy", is the founding text for its policy view. Grade A.

## Claims
<!-- voice-ignore-next 1 -->
- Monetary policy "cannot peg interest rates for more than very limited periods" and "cannot peg the rate of unemployment for more than very limited periods."
- What it can do: "prevent money itself from being a" major source of disturbance, and "provide a stable background for the economy."
- Prescription: "adopting publicly the policy of achieving a steady rate of growth in a specified monetary total."

## Test Against 2026
M2 grew 5.89% in the year to Oct 2026 with CPI at 4.08%; see [money-supply-m2](money-supply-m2.md). The Fed targets an interest rate and sets no money growth path, per [fed-funds-target-range](fed-funds-target-range.md). The BoE 2014 article treats the money stock as an outcome of lending; see [boe-2014-money-creation](boe-2014-money-creation.md).
