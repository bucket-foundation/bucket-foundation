# Federal Deficit

ID `M-federal-deficit`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: fiscal.

## What It Is
Outlays minus receipts of the federal government, reported monthly in the Monthly Treasury Statement on about the eighth business day.

## Market Reaction
Larger deficits raise borrowing needs; see [treasury-issuance-quarterly-refunding](treasury-issuance-quarterly-refunding.md). The bond market reacts to issuance surprises more than to the monthly deficit print.

## Release Schedule
MTS, Bureau of the Fiscal Service, 2:00 p.m. ET, about the eighth business day of the month.
