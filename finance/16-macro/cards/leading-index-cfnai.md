# Chicago Fed National Activity Index

ID `M-leading-index-cfnai`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: indicators.

## What It Is
A weighted average of monthly activity indicators, scaled so zero is trend growth. The three-month average, CFNAI-MA3, carries the signals.

## Thresholds
From the Chicago Fed. Grade A.
- Recession: "Following a period of economic expansion, an increasing likelihood of a recession has historically been associated with a CFNAI-MA3 value below -0.70."
- Inflation: MA3 above +0.70 more than two years into an expansion raises the likelihood of sustained rising inflation; above +1.00 makes it substantial.
