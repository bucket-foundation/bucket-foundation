# Wages: ECI and Average Hourly Earnings

ID `M-wages-eci-ahe`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: inflation.

## What It Is
Average hourly earnings come monthly with payrolls and shift with job mix. The Employment Cost Index is quarterly and holds job mix fixed.

## Current State
- Average hourly earnings, private, $38.00 for Oct 2026, up 3.12% year on year, FRED CES0500000003. Grade A.
- ECI civilian total compensation 178.574 for 2026 Q3, up 3.37% year on year, FRED ECIALLCIV. Grade A.
Wage growth near 3.1% to 3.4% runs below headline CPI at 4.1%, so real wages fell over the year.

## Market Reaction
Wage growth above productivity plus 2% is the cost-push channel the Fed watches; current readings do not show a wage-price spiral.
