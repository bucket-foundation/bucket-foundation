# Yield Curve as a Recession Signal

ID `M-yield-curve-recession`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: business cycles.

## What It Is
The New York Fed model maps the 10-year minus 3-month Treasury spread to the probability of a recession twelve months ahead.

## Current State
- 10y minus 3m spread 0.51 points on 25 Nov 2026; 10y minus 2y 0.24. Grade A.
- NY Fed model probability for Oct 2027: 12.4%, from its allmonth file last saved 5 Nov 2026. Grade A.
- The lab crisis synthesis records inversions in Jun 1973, Dec 1978, Nov 1980, Aug 2000, Aug 2006, Jun 2019 and Nov 2022; the 2022 inversion lasted into 2025 with stocks rising. See [CRISIS-PATTERNS](../../01-money-history/CRISIS-PATTERNS.md).

## Market Reaction
Re-steepening after a long inversion, driven by the short end falling, has come near recession starts; driven by the long end rising, it reflects term premium; see [term-premium-acm](term-premium-acm.md).
