# Median CPI and Trimmed Mean PCE

ID `M-median-trimmed-mean`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: inflation.

## What It Is
Cleveland Fed median CPI takes the median component price change each month. Dallas Fed trimmed mean PCE drops the extreme tails of component changes. Both filter one-off shocks.

## Current State
- Median CPI 3.66% for Oct 2026, FRED MEDCPIM158SFRBCLE, an annualized monthly rate. Grade A.
- Trimmed mean PCE 12-month rate 2.26% for Sept 2026, FRED PCETRIM12M159SFRBDAL. Grade A.
The two point in different directions: the monthly median runs hot, the 12-month trimmed mean near target. The window differs, one month against twelve.

## Release Schedule
Median CPI on CPI day; trimmed mean on PCE day.
