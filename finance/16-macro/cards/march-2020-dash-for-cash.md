# March 2020 Dash for Cash

ID `M-march-2020-dash-for-cash`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: money market plumbing.

## What Happened
<!-- voice-ignore-next 1 -->
- FSB holistic review, Nov 2020: "Market dysfunction was exacerbated by the substantial sales of US Treasuries" by leveraged non-banks and foreign holders; "Dealers also faced difficulties absorbing large sales of assets"; "Absent central bank intervention, it is highly likely that the stress in the financial system would have worsened significantly." Grade A.
- Lab synthesis: SPY, TLT, GLD and BTC fell together and the 10-year yield rose from 0.54% to 1.18% during the equity crash; see [CRISIS-PATTERNS](../../01-money-history/CRISIS-PATTERNS.md). Grade A.

## Response
- 15 Mar 2020: target range cut to 0 to 1/4 percent; Treasury holdings up "by at least $500 billion" and agency MBS "by at least $200 billion"; coordinated swap line action. Grade A.
- 23 Mar 2020: purchases "in the amounts needed to support smooth market functioning"; PMCCF, SMCCF and TALF set up; MMLF and CPFF expanded. Grade A.
- 31 Mar 2020: temporary FIMA repo facility, made standing 28 Jul 2021; see [fed-swap-lines](fed-swap-lines.md). Grade A.

## Lessons
Treasuries failed as a hedge when leveraged holders sold; only cash and an unlimited central bank backstop stopped it. See [treasury-basis-trade](treasury-basis-trade.md).
