# Bank of England

ID `M-boe`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: central bank operations.

## Policy Rate
- Bank Rate 4%, raised on 5 Nov 2026. The Bank: energy prices "have stayed high and volatile due to conflict in the Middle East"; "Inflation is expected to reach about 5% by mid-2027"; the rise is meant "to reduce how much rising energy costs affect other prices." Grade A.
- UK inflation 3.6% against a 2% target. Next decision 17 Dec 2026. The MPC meets eight times a year. Grade A.
