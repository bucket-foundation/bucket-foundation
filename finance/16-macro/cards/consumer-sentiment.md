# Consumer Sentiment

ID `M-consumer-sentiment`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: indicators.

## What It Is
University of Michigan Surveys of Consumers index, preliminary mid-month and final at month end, with 1-year and 5-year inflation expectations.

## Current State
UMCSENT 48.0 for Oct 2026; 1-year inflation expectation 4.7%. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
Sentiment tracks gas prices and inflation more than spending; markets react to the expectations questions more than the index.
