# Treasury General Account

ID `M-treasury-general-account`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: money market plumbing.

## What It Is
The Treasury's operating cash account at the New York Fed. Taxes and debt sales flow in; spending flows out. It is a Fed liability, so every dollar parked there is a dollar removed from bank reserves or ON RRP.
