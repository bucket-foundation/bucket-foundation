# Money Supply, M2

ID `M-money-supply-m2`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: indicators.

## What It Is
Federal Reserve H.6 broad money: currency, checking, savings, small time deposits and retail money funds. Velocity M2V divides nominal GDP by M2.

## Current State
M2SL $23,532.8B for Oct 2026, up 5.89% year on year, above the 4.08% CPI rate. Grade A, computed.

## Market Reaction
Monetarists read M2 growth as a lead for inflation; see [monetarism](monetarism.md).
