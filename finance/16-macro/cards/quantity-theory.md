# Quantity Theory of Money

ID `M-quantity-theory`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: monetary theory.

## What It Is
Irving Fisher, The Purchasing Power of Money, 1911, 2nd edition 1922, Chapter II. The equation of exchange: "MV = S pQ", simplified "in the form PT", where P is a weighted average of prices and T the volume of trade. Grade A.

## Claim
"the price level varies (1) directly as the quantity of money in circulation (M), (2) directly as the velocity of its circulation (V), (3) inversely as the volume of trade done by it (T)." Fisher: "The first of these three relations is worth emphasis. It constitutes the 'quantity theory of money.'" Grade A.

## Test Against Data
MV = PY is an identity; the theory needs V and T to be stable or independent of M. FRED M2V lets the lab test velocity stability; see [money-supply-m2](money-supply-m2.md). The BoE 2014 article treats M as created by lending in response to demand, which reverses the causal arrow; see [boe-2014-money-creation](boe-2014-money-creation.md).

## Current State
M2 up 5.89% and CPI up 4.08% in the year to Oct 2026; real GDP up 2.29% in the year to 2026 Q3. With velocity flat, the identity fits those three numbers within a point.
