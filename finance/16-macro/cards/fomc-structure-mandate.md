# FOMC Structure and Mandate

ID `M-fomc-structure-mandate`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: central bank operations.

## Mandate
<!-- voice-ignore-next 1 -->
Federal Reserve Act Section 2A: the Board and the FOMC "shall maintain long run growth of the monetary and credit aggregates commensurate with the economy's long run potential to increase production, so as to promote effectively the goals of maximum employment, stable prices, and moderate long-term interest rates." Grade A.

## How It Works Now
- The FOMC states its stance as a target range and directs the NY Fed Desk; the Board sets IORB and the primary credit rate. Both appear in the 28 Oct 2026 implementation note. Grade A.
- The 2 percent inflation goal is named in the 28 Oct 2026 statement. Grade A.
- Votes are public: 10 to 2 on 28 Oct 2026, two dissents for a hike. Grade A.
- Eight scheduled meetings a year; in 2026 the March, June, September and December meetings carried projections; see [fomc-calendar-dot-plot](fomc-calendar-dot-plot.md).

## Market Reaction
Dissents and shifts in statement language move rate expectations between meetings.
