# Money Market Funds

ID `M-money-market-funds`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: money market plumbing.

## What It Is
Funds holding bills, repo and short paper. Government funds sit at the center of the dollar plumbing: they lend in tri-party repo, buy bills and, when rates allow, park cash at the ON RRP.
