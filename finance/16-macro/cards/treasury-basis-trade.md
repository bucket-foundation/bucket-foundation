# Treasury Cash-Futures Basis Trade

ID `M-treasury-basis-trade`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: money market plumbing.

## What It Is
When Treasury futures trade rich to cash bonds, leveraged funds sell futures, buy the bonds and finance them in overnight repo, earning the convergence at expiry. The spread is narrow, so the trade runs at high leverage from both repo and futures initial margin.

## Evidence
BIS Quarterly Review, 18 Sept 2023, "Margin leverage and vulnerabilities in US Treasury futures". Grade A.
- Leveraged funds held net short Treasury futures of about $600 billion, over 40% in two-year contracts, comparable to levels before Sept 2019 and March 2020.
- Margin leverage was about 175x on five-year and 120x on ten-year futures before the pandemic, down to about 70x and 50x after 2021.
- In August 2019 an initial margin increase preceded an unwind; March 2020 showed similar dynamics in the dash for cash.
- "Margin deleveraging, if disorderly, has the potential to dislocate core fixed income markets."
