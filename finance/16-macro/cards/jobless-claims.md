# Jobless Claims

ID `M-jobless-claims`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: indicators.

## What It Is
Weekly Department of Labor count of new state unemployment insurance claims, initial, and of people still claiming, continuing. The highest-frequency official labor data.

## Current State
Initial claims 189,000 for the week of 21 Nov 2026; continuing claims 1,697,000 for the week of 14 Nov. Grade A.

## Release Schedule
Department of Labor, Thursdays 8:30 a.m. ET, moved a day around holidays.

## Market Reaction
Small single-week moves; a sustained rise in the four-week average leads payroll weakness and gets priced.

## Data
