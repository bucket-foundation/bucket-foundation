# Endogenous Money

ID `M-endogenous-money`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: monetary theory.

## What It Is
The view that the money supply is set by loan demand, bank behavior and the policy rate, with the central bank supplying reserves on demand at its chosen price. "Loans create deposits", so "banks do not need to increase deposits prior to extending a loan." Lineage: Wicksell's natural and money rates of interest; Schumpeter; Basil Moore's horizontalism in the 1970s and 1980s. Grade B.

## Central Bank Statement
The Bank of England, 2014: "bank lending creates deposits" and central bank money is not "multiplied up" into loans; the central bank sets "the price of reserves." Grade A. See [boe-2014-money-creation](boe-2014-money-creation.md).

## Fed Practice
Under the [ample reserves framework](ample-reserves-framework.md) the Fed adds reserves through bill purchases when repo rates rise and pays [IORB](iorb.md): supply follows demand at a set price, the horizontalist picture.

## Test Against 2026
Bank loans and M2: M2 rose 5.89% in the year to Oct 2026 while reserves rose $91B on the year to 18 Nov; lending drives deposits, and reserves follow the Fed's reserve management.
