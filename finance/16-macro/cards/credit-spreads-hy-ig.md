# Credit Spreads: High Yield and Investment Grade

ID `M-credit-spreads-hy-ig`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: indicators.

## What It Is
ICE BofA option-adjusted spreads: "the calculated spreads between a computed OAS index of all bonds in a given rating category and a spot Treasury curve." Grade A.

## Current State
High yield OAS 3.72% and investment grade 0.98% on 24 Nov 2026. FRED notes that starting April 2026 these series carry only 3 years of observations. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
Spreads widen with equity drawdowns and lead defaults; tight spreads with high VIX, as now, is a divergence to watch.
