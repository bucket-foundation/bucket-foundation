# Interest on Reserve Balances

ID `M-iorb`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: central bank operations.

## What It Is
The rate the Fed pays banks on reserve balances held at Reserve Banks. Under the ample reserves regime it is the main administered rate: no bank lends reserves below what the Fed pays to hold them, so IORB anchors the fed funds rate from inside the range.

## Mechanism
The January 2019 FOMC statement set the regime: "control over the level of the federal funds rate and other short-term interest rates is exercised primarily through the setting of the Federal Reserve's administered rates, and in which active management of the supply of reserves is not required." IORB is set by the Board of Governors; the ON RRP rate sets a floor for non-banks.

## Current State
- IORB 3.90%, effective 29 Oct 2026, voted unanimously by the Board. Grade A.
- EFFR 3.88% and SOFR 3.95% on 25 Nov 2026. SOFR above IORB signals reserves near the lower edge of ample. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
Changes in IORB move with the target range. Technical adjustments to IORB inside the range, without a range change, signal the Fed is fine-tuning money market control.
