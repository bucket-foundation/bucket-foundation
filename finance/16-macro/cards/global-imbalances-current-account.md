# Global Imbalances and the US Trade Deficit

ID `M-global-imbalances-current-account`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: global imbalances.

## What It Is
A current account deficit means a country spends more than it earns abroad and finances the gap by selling assets to foreigners. The US deficit is the counterpart to surpluses in exporters that recycle dollars into US assets; see [tic-foreign-holdings](tic-foreign-holdings.md).
