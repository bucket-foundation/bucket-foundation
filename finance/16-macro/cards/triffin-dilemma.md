# Triffin Dilemma

ID `M-triffin-dilemma`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: global imbalances.

## What It Is
Identified in the 1960s by Robert Triffin: "the conflict of economic interests that arises between short-term domestic and long-term international objectives" for a reserve currency issuer. The issuer must supply its currency to meet world demand for reserves, which requires running deficits, while confidence in the currency needs surpluses. Grade B.

## Evidence
- By 1959 "the number of U.S. dollars in circulation exceeded the amount of gold that was backing them." The gold link ended with the 1971 Nixon shock; see [Bretton Woods](../../01-money-history/crises/X-bretton-woods.md). Grade B.
- 2009: PBoC governor Zhou Xiaochuan named the dilemma as the root of the crisis and proposed a gradual move toward IMF SDRs. Grade B.

The dollar holds 56.70% of world reserves in 2026 Q2, see [dollar-dominance-cofer](dollar-dominance-cofer.md), while the US goods and services gap widened to $105.0B in Sept 2026, see [global-imbalances-current-account](global-imbalances-current-account.md).
