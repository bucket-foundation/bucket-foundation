# Dollar Index

ID `M-dollar-index`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: indicators.

## What It Is
The Fed's nominal broad dollar index weighs the dollar against major trading partners. The ICE DXY is a narrower basket of major currencies.

## Current State
DTWEXBGS 123.60 for 20 Nov 2026. Grade A. DXY is tracked live by the lab brief as `DX-Y.NYB`.

## Market Reaction
A rising dollar tightens global dollar funding and hits emerging market debtors; see [eurodollar-system](eurodollar-system.md).
