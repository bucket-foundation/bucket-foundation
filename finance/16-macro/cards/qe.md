# Quantitative Easing

ID `M-qe`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: central bank operations.

## What It Is
<!-- voice-ignore-next 1 -->
Central bank purchases of bonds paid for with new reserves. The Bank of England: QE "is intended to boost the amount of money in the economy directly by purchasing assets, mainly from non-bank financial companies," first raising those sellers' bank deposits. Grade A.

## The 2020 Program
- 15 Mar 2020: Treasury holdings up "by at least $500 billion" and agency MBS "by at least $200 billion", with the target range cut to 0 to 1/4 percent. Grade A.
- 23 Mar 2020: purchases "in the amounts needed to support smooth market functioning." Grade A.
- Holdings fell by more than $2.2 trillion in the runoff from June 2022 to December 2025; see [qt](qt.md). Grade A.

## Distinguish From Reserve Management
Bill purchases since Dec 2025 aim at reserve levels. The Fed called the 2019 version "purely technical measures" that "do not represent a change in the stance of monetary policy"; see [ample-reserves-framework](ample-reserves-framework.md). QE buys duration to lower long yields; bill purchases leave duration with the market.

## Market Reaction
The March 2020 open-ended commitment marked the equity bottom within days; see [CRISIS-PATTERNS](../../01-money-history/CRISIS-PATTERNS.md).
