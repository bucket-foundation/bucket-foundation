# GDP and GDPNow

ID `M-gdp-gdpnow`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: indicators.

## What It Is
BEA real GDP, quarterly, in advance, second and third estimates. The Atlanta Fed GDPNow model nowcasts the current quarter from incoming data.

## Current State
- Real GDP $24,665.2B chained 2017 dollars for 2026 Q3, up 2.29% year on year. Grade A.
- GDPNow 2.90% annualized for 2026 Q4, FRED GDPNOW. Grade A.

## Release Schedule
BEA 8:30 a.m. ET. Q3 2026 third estimate on 23 Dec 2026. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
The advance estimate moves markets; later estimates rarely do. GDPNow shifts after each data release and is priced continuously.
