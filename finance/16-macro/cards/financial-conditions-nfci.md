# Financial Conditions Indexes

ID `M-financial-conditions-nfci`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: indicators.

## What It Is
- Chicago Fed NFCI: weekly index of money, debt and equity markets and the banking system. Built to average zero with standard deviation one since 1971. "Positive values of the NFCI have been historically associated with tighter-than-average financial conditions." Grade A.
- St. Louis Fed STLFSI4: weekly stress index. "zero is viewed as representing normal financial market conditions"; values above zero mean above-average stress. Grade A.

## Current State
