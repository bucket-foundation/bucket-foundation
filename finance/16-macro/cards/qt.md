# Quantitative Tightening

ID `M-qt`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: central bank operations.

## What It Is
Shrinking the Fed balance sheet by letting securities mature without reinvestment, up to monthly caps. Each dollar of runoff drains a dollar of reserves or ON RRP.

## 2022 to 2025 Program
- Plans of 4 May 2022: runoff from 1 Jun 2022; Treasury cap "initially be set at $30 billion per month" rising to "$60 billion per month" after three months; agency debt and MBS cap $17.5 billion rising to $35 billion. Grade A.
- 29 Oct 2025: the FOMC "would cease the runoff of its securities holdings" starting 1 Dec 2025, as money market conditions suggested reserves "were approaching the ample level." Holdings fell by "more than $2.2 trillion" over the program. Grade A.
- 10 Dec 2025: reserve management purchases of about $40 billion of bills a month from 12 Dec 2025, per the NY Fed operating policy. Grade B, from search summary of the NY Fed page.

## Current State
The 28 Oct 2026 directive rolls over all Treasury principal, reinvests all agency principal into bills and buys bills "to maintain an ample level of reserves." Runoff is over; the Fed is growing Treasury bill holdings. Total assets $6,741.5B on 18 Nov 2026. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
The end of runoff came as SOFR began printing above IORB in 2025; see [sofr-iorb-spread](sofr-iorb-spread.md). Same pattern as 2019: runoff stops when repo stress appears.
