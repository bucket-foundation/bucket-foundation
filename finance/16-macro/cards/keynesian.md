# Keynesian Economics

ID `M-keynesian`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: monetary theory.

## What It Is
John Maynard Keynes, The General Theory of Employment, Interest and Money, 1936. Output and employment are set by aggregate demand, and a market economy can rest below full employment. Grade A.

## Claims
From Chapter 3, The Principle of Effective Demand:
- "The value of D at the point of the aggregate demand function, where it is intersected by the aggregate supply function, will be called the effective demand."
- "The propensity to consume and the rate of new investment determine between them the volume of employment."
- "the economic system may find itself in stable equilibrium with N at a level below full employment."

## Policy Line
When private demand falls short, fiscal spending and lower rates fill the gap. The 2020 fiscal response and the 2008 to 2014 rate cuts are the modern cases; see [qe](qe.md) and [federal-deficit](federal-deficit.md).

## Current Read
Unemployment at 4.1% and a 2026 deficit near $2.0 trillion: demand support runs while the economy sits near full employment, which Keynesian theory itself predicts adds to inflation.
