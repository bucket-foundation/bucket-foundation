# Kindleberger: Manias and the Stabilizer

ID `M-kindleberger-manias`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: business cycles.

## Works
- Manias, Panics, and Crashes: A History of Financial Crises, Macmillan 1978, reprinted in 2000 after the dot-com bubble. A history of speculative bubbles. Grade B.
- The World in Depression, 1929 to 1939, 1973, revised 1986. Grade B.

## Claims
- The Depression ran long and deep because the US hesitated to take over leadership after Britain could no longer provide it. "for the world economy to be stabilized, there has to be a stabilizer, one stabilizer." Grade B.
- The stabilizer's five tasks: keep markets open for distress goods; provide countercyclical or stable long-term lending; police exchange rate stability; coordinate macro policies; act as lender of last resort "by discounting, or otherwise providing liquidity, in a financial crisis." Grade B.
