# Term Premium

ID `M-term-premium-acm`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: indicators.

## What It Is
The extra yield investors demand to hold a long bond instead of rolling short bills. Not observed; estimated by models such as Kim-Wright, carried on FRED as THREEFYTP10, and Adrian-Crump-Moench at the New York Fed.

## Current State
Kim-Wright 10-year term premium 0.89% for the week of 20 Nov 2026, FRED THREEFYTP10. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
Rising term premium with stable policy expectations reflects supply, fiscal and inflation-risk worries; see [treasury-issuance-quarterly-refunding](treasury-issuance-quarterly-refunding.md).
