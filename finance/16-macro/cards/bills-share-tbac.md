# Bills Share of Marketable Debt

ID `M-bills-share-tbac`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: fiscal.

## What It Is
The fraction of marketable Treasury debt in bills, maturing in a year or less. A higher share lowers average duration and ties the Treasury's interest bill to the policy rate.

## Market Reaction
A rising bills share keeps term premium lower than coupon issuance would, and loads money funds and stablecoins with bills; see [stablecoins-tbill-buyers](stablecoins-tbill-buyers.md).
