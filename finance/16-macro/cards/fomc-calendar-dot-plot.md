## Read
A 2026 median of 4.1 against a 3.875 midpoint implies one more 25 bp hike in December. The longer-run 3.2% less 2.0% inflation gives a 1.2% neutral real rate, under the Laubach-Williams 1.65%; see [r-star](r-star.md). The 2-year at 4.63% prices above the dots.

## Market Reaction
Statement, dots and press conference move 2-year yields and the dollar within minutes; a shift in the median path for the next year carries the most weight.
