# Durable Goods Orders

ID `M-durable-goods`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: indicators.

## What It Is
Census advance report on new orders for goods meant to last three years or more. Core capital goods orders, nondefense ex aircraft, proxy business equipment investment.

## Current State
Lab file `DGORDER` holds the series; pull before use. AI capex runs at about $725B for the four hyperscalers in 2026 per [2026-STATE](../../02-assets/2026-STATE.md), which shows up in equipment orders.

## Release Schedule
Census, 8:30 a.m. ET. Dec 2026 data on 27 Jan 2027. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
Aircraft orders swing the headline; markets read core capital goods.
