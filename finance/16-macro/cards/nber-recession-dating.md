# NBER Recession Dating

ID `M-nber-recession-dating`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: business cycles.

## What It Is
The NBER Business Cycle Dating Committee names monthly peaks and troughs of US activity after the fact. Its dates are the reference for recession studies and for FRED `USREC`.

## Evidence
NBER chronology, last updated 14 Mar 2023. Grade A.
| Period | Mean contraction, months | Mean expansion, months |
|---|---|---|
| 1854 to 2020 | 17.0 | 41.4 |
| 1854 to 1919 | 21.6 | 26.6 |
| 1919 to 1945 | 18.2 | 35.0 |
| 1945 to 2020 | 10.3 | 64.2 |

Recent cycles: Jul 1990 to Mar 1991, 8 months; Mar 2001 to Nov 2001, 8; Dec 2007 to Jun 2009, 18; Feb 2020 to Apr 2020, 2. The longest expansion ran 128 months, Jun 2009 to Feb 2020. The current expansion began after the April 2020 trough.

## Current State
No peak announced after April 2020. Real-time recession probability from the Chauvet-Piger smoothed model is 0.40% for Sept 2026, FRED RECPROUSM156N. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
Announcements come too late to trade. Equity bottoms have come before NBER trough announcements in the lab crisis data; see [CRISIS-PATTERNS](../../01-money-history/CRISIS-PATTERNS.md).
