# Fed Swap Lines and FIMA Repo

ID `M-fed-swap-lines`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: money market plumbing.

## What It Is
- Standing swap lines: "The Bank of Canada, the Bank of England, the Bank of Japan, the European Central Bank, the Federal Reserve, and the Swiss National Bank maintain standing U.S. dollar liquidity swap line arrangements." The Fed lends dollars against foreign currency; the foreign central bank lends the dollars on to its banks. Grade A.
- FIMA Repo Facility: lets "central banks and other international monetary authorities with accounts at the Federal Reserve Bank of New York" repo their Treasuries with the Fed for dollars, "an alternative temporary source of U.S. dollars for foreign official holders of Treasury securities other than sales of the securities in the open market." Temporary from 31 Mar 2020, standing from 28 Jul 2021. Grade A.

## Current State
Swaps outstanding $146 million on the 18 Nov 2026 H.4.1, FRED SWPT: near zero, no offshore dollar stress. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
A jump in SWPT marks offshore dollar shortage, as in March 2020. The FIMA facility keeps foreign central banks from dumping Treasuries in a squeeze; see [march-2020-dash-for-cash](march-2020-dash-for-cash.md) and [eurodollar-system](eurodollar-system.md).
