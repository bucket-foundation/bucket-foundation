# Minsky Financial Instability Hypothesis

ID `M-minsky-financial-instability`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: business cycles.

## What It Is
Hyman Minsky, "The Financial Instability Hypothesis", Levy Economics Institute Working Paper 74, 1992. Three income-debt relations: hedge, speculative and Ponzi finance. Grade A.

## Claims
- Hedge units "can fulfill all of their contractual payment obligations by their cash flows."
- Speculative units meet payments "on income account" but "cannot repay the principle out of income cash flows" and must roll over liabilities.
- Ponzi units have cash flows insufficient for "either the repayment of principle or the interest due" and must sell assets or borrow.
- First theorem: "the economy has financing regimes under which it is stable, and financing regimes in which it is unstable."
- Second theorem: "over periods of prolonged prosperity, the economy transits from financial relations that make for a stable system to financial relations that make for an unstable system."
