# Breakevens and TIPS

ID `M-breakevens-tips`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: inflation.

## What It Is
The breakeven is the nominal Treasury yield minus the TIPS real yield of the same maturity: the inflation rate at which the two pay the same, plus risk premia.
