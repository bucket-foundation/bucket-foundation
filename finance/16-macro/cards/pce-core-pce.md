# PCE and Core PCE

ID `M-pce-core-pce`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: inflation.

## What It Is
BEA's price index for personal consumption expenditures. The FOMC's 2 percent goal is stated in PCE terms. PCE weights shift with spending.

## Current State
- PCEPI 132.703 for Oct 2026, up 3.84% year on year. Grade A.
- Core PCEPILFE 131.312, up 3.26% year on year, 0.29% on the month. Grade A.
- Core PCE above core CPI, 3.26% against 2.87%, reverses the usual order.

## Release Schedule
BEA Personal Income and Outlays, 8:30 a.m. ET. November 2026 data on 23 Dec 2026. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
Mostly known after CPI and PPI, since forecasters map those into PCE; surprises are small.
