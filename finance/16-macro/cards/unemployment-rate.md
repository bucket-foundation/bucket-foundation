# Unemployment Rate

ID `M-unemployment-rate`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: indicators.

## What It Is
Household survey share of the labor force without a job and looking. U-6 adds part-time for economic reasons and marginally attached workers.

## Current State

## Market Reaction
