# Nonfarm Payrolls

ID `M-nonfarm-payrolls`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: indicators.

## What It Is
BLS establishment survey count of payroll jobs, released in the Employment Situation report on the first Friday of most months.

## Current State
PAYEMS 159,293 thousand for Oct 2026: +165k on the month after +60k in Sept; up 0.56% year on year, about 890k jobs. Grade A, computed from FRED.

## Release Schedule
8:30 a.m. ET: Nov 2026 data on 4 Dec 2026; Dec on 8 Jan 2027; Jan on 5 Feb 2027; Feb on 5 Mar 2027. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
Payrolls and unemployment land together and move front-end yields and the dollar at 8:30. Revisions to the prior two months can matter as much as the headline.
