## Transmission Protection Instrument
Approved 21 Jul 2022 to support "effective transmission of monetary policy" against unwarranted, disorderly market moves. Eligibility: no excessive deficit procedure, no excessive imbalance procedure, sustainable public debt per Commission, ESM, IMF and ECB analysis, and compliance with recovery plan and Semester commitments. Purchases should have no lasting effect on the balance sheet or stance. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
The Fed to ECB rate gap, 3.88% against 2.50%, moves EUR/USD; Italian spreads test the TPI.
