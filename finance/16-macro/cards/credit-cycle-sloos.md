# Credit Cycle and SLOOS

ID `M-credit-cycle-sloos`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: business cycles.

## What It Is
The Fed's Senior Loan Officer Opinion Survey asks banks quarterly whether they tightened or eased lending standards. The net percentage tightening for commercial and industrial loans to large and mid-sized firms is FRED DRTSCILM.

## Current State
Net 1.7% of banks tightening C&I standards for the Oct 2026 survey: close to neutral. Grade A.

## Evidence
The JST tests in [CRISIS-PATTERNS](../../01-money-history/CRISIS-PATTERNS.md) show crises follow fast credit growth: crisis odds within two years rise from 5.1% to 10.9% when 5-year credit growth is in a country's top fifth.
