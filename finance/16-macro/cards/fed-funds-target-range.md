# Fed Funds Target Range

ID `M-fed-funds-target-range`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: central bank operations.

## What It Is
The FOMC sets a 25 bp target range for the overnight rate banks charge each other on unsecured reserves. It steers the range with administered rates: interest on reserve balances, the ON RRP offering rate and the standing repo rate.

## Current State
- Target range 3.75% to 4.00% since the 16 Sept 2026 hike; held on 28 Oct 2026 by a 10 to 2 vote. Cook and Hammack preferred a further 25 bp hike. Grade A.
- Statement of 28 Oct 2026: "Economic activity is expanding at a strong pace", "Inflation remains elevated", "The Committee will deliver price stability." Grade A.
- Effective fed funds rate 3.88% on 25 Nov 2026, FRED EFFR. Grade A.
- Next meeting 8 and 9 Dec 2026.

## Market Reaction
Two-year yields price the path of the range; DGS2 was 4.63% on 24 Nov 2026, 63 bp above the top of the range, so the curve prices further hikes or a higher-for-longer path. A surprise in the decision moves 2-year yields and the dollar first. See [fomc-calendar-dot-plot](fomc-calendar-dot-plot.md).
