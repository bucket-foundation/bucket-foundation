# Foreign Holdings of Treasuries, TIC

ID `M-tic-foreign-holdings`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: global imbalances.

## What It Is
Treasury International Capital data, published monthly about six weeks after the reference month, lists Treasury holdings by country based on custody location.

Japan fell $126B and China $135B over the year; the UK, a custody center, and Cayman, a hedge fund domicile, rose. Foreign official bill holdings fell from $371.3B to $288.5B.

## Market Reaction
Official selling is slow and mostly visible after the fact. Custody shifts to London, Brussels and Cayman blur who owns what.
