# Natural Rate of Interest, r-star

ID `M-r-star`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: monetary theory.

## What It Is
The New York Fed defines r-star as "the real short-term interest rate expected to prevail when an economy is at full strength and inflation is stable." The Laubach-Williams model, 2003, extracts it from real GDP, inflation and the fed funds rate; Holston, Laubach and Williams, 2023, adapts it after COVID. Grade A.
