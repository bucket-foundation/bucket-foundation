# September 2019 Repo Spike

ID `M-sept-2019-repo-spike`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: money market plumbing.

## What Happened
From Anbil, Anderson and Senyuz, FEDS Notes, 27 Feb 2020. Grade A.
- 16 Sept 2019: SOFR 2.43%, up 13 bp; EFFR 2.25%, at the top of the target range.
- 17 Sept: SOFR above 5%; EFFR 2.30%, above the range. SOFR volume $1.18 trillion.
- Causes: quarterly corporate taxes moved cash into the TGA on 16 Sept, and $54B of coupon Treasuries settled the same day, raising dealer repo needs. Reserves fell about $120B over two business days to $1.34 trillion on 13 and 16 Sept, a multi-year low.
- The authors: "the extent of the increase in both the level and volatility of rates in secured and unsecured markets was surprising."

## Response
- 17 Sept: first overnight repo operation, up to $75B, added $53B of reserves; rates fell at once. Operations each morning that week were fully subscribed.
- 19 Sept: IOER cut to 20 bp below the top of the range; ON RRP to 5 bp below the bottom.
- 11 Oct: bill purchases of about $60B a month through 2020 Q2 and repo through at least January 2020, called "purely technical measures"; see [ample-reserves-framework](ample-reserves-framework.md).

## Lessons
Reserves near $1.4 trillion were below ample in 2019. The [standing repo facility](standing-repo-facility.md) exists to cap this. Watch tax dates that coincide with coupon settlement and a TGA rebuild.
