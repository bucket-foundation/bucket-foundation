# Bank of Japan

ID `M-boj`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: central bank operations.

## Policy Rate
- 18 Sept 2026: by a 7 to 2 vote the Board moved the guideline so the uncollateralized overnight call rate stays "at around 1.25 percent", effective 24 Sept 2026. Interest on current account balances, the complementary deposit facility, set at 1.25%; basic loan rate 1.5%. Dissenters Asada and Sato wanted to keep the prior guideline. Grade A.
- 30 Oct 2026: held at around 1.25% by 8 to 1. Grade A.
- Next statement after the December meeting; the BoJ 2026 list shows statements in Jan, Mar, Apr, Jun, Jul, Sept and Oct. Grade A.

## Balance Sheet
- ETF sales started January 2026 at about ¥330 billion a year, book value. On 30 Oct 2026 the pace was raised to about 0.1% of market trading value, about ¥860 billion a year at book value. Grade A.
- A JGB outright purchase plan was issued 16 Jun 2026. Grade A.

## Risks Named
The Sept 2026 statement flags "the impact of the situation in the Middle East on financial and foreign exchange markets" and "developments in global AI-related demand." Grade A.

## Market Reaction
BoJ hikes narrow the US-Japan rate gap and can force yen carry trade unwinds; Japan's US Treasury holdings fell $126B in the year to Sept 2026; see [tic-foreign-holdings](tic-foreign-holdings.md).
