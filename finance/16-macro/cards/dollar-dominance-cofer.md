# Dollar Dominance and COFER

ID `M-dollar-dominance-cofer`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: global imbalances.

## What It Is
The IMF Currency Composition of Official Foreign Exchange Reserves, quarterly. From 2025 Q3 the IMF dropped the unallocated portion and reports shares of total reserves, with the imputed share published apart. Grade A.

## Current State
IMF data brief, 30 Sept 2026, for 2026 Q2. Grade A.
| Currency | 2026 Q1 | 2026 Q2 |
|---|---|---|
| US dollar | 57.18% | 56.70% |
| Euro | 20.04% | 20.60% |
| Yen | 5.43% | 4.96% |
| Renminbi | 1.98% | 2.11% |
Total reserves $13.22 trillion in Q2 against $13.10 trillion in Q1. The IMF wrote that dollar claims "were largely unchanged" in Q2; the share fell on valuation as other currencies gained. Sterling rose 0.16 points.

## Market Reaction
Share moves are slow and valuation-driven; official selling shows up first in [TIC](tic-foreign-holdings.md). Central bank gold buying, the PBoC for 24 straight months, is the other side; see [pboc](pboc.md).
