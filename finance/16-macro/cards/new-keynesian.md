# New Keynesian Economics

ID `M-new-keynesian`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: monetary theory.

## What It Is
The framework central banks use for policy models: optimizing households and firms, sticky prices, and an interest rate rule. The survey reference is Clarida, Galí and Gertler, "The Science of Monetary Policy: A New Keynesian Perspective", NBER WP 7147, May 1999, Journal of Economic Literature December 1999. Grade A.

## Claims
From the abstract. Grade A.
- The authors study "the monetary policy design problem within a simple baseline theoretical framework."
- "Among other things, we show that the optimal policy implicitly incorporates inflation targeting."
- Gains from commitment appear even when the central bank does not try to push output above its natural level.

## Policy Tools It Produced
Inflation targets, interest rate rules such as the [Taylor rule](taylor-rule.md), forward guidance, and a natural rate of interest estimated by models such as [r-star](r-star.md). The FOMC dots and 2% target are its practice; see [fomc-calendar-dot-plot](fomc-calendar-dot-plot.md).

## Critiques In This Atlas
The [endogenous money](endogenous-money.md) and [BoE 2014](boe-2014-money-creation.md) cards put bank lending at the center. [Minsky](minsky-financial-instability.md) adds a financial cycle.
