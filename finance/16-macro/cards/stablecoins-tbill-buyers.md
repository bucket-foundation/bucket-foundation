# Stablecoins as T-Bill Buyers

ID `M-stablecoins-tbill-buyers`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: global imbalances.

## Law
GENIUS Act, S. 1582 enrolled, Sec. 4(a)(1)(A): reserves "on an at least 1 to 1 basis" in currency or Fed balances; insured deposits; Treasuries "with a remaining maturity of 93 days or less"; overnight repo backed by such bills; overnight reverse repo on Treasuries, tri-party, centrally cleared or bilateral with a creditworthy counterparty; government money funds holding only those assets; other federally issued liquid assets a regulator approves; and tokenized forms. Monthly public reserve reports, examined monthly by a registered accounting firm, certified by the CEO and CFO. Grade A.

## Evidence
- BIS Working Paper 1270, Ahmed and Aldasoro, "Stablecoins and safe asset prices", 28 May 2025, daily data January 2021 to March 2026: a $3.5 billion inflow, 2 standard deviations, "lowers 3-month Treasury bill yields by 0.71 basis points on impact, and up to 4 basis points within 10 days, with limited spillovers to longer tenors." Effects grow under Treasury market stress and with the sector's scale. Grade A.
- Tether Q3 2026 BDO attestation, 30 Sept 2026: $122.84B of directly held bills, $9.52B overnight and $7.18B term reverse repo; assets $191.45B against liabilities $183.50B. Grade B, press reports of the attestation.

## Current State
DefiLlama total stablecoin supply in the lab: $283.7B on 27 Nov 2026, down from a $314.4B peak on 17 May 2026 and $301.2B on 28 Oct. Grade A for the lab file. Bills outstanding are $7,381.7B, so stablecoins hold a few percent of the bill stock; see [bills-share-tbac](bills-share-tbac.md).

## Market Reaction
The 93-day cap makes issuers front-end buyers only. A $30B drawdown, as from May to November 2026, is a bill seller; by the BIS estimate, scaled linearly, outflows of that size push 3-month yields up by single basis points. The linear scaling is a house extrapolation.
