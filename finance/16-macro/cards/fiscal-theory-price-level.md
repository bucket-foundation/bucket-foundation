# Fiscal Theory of the Price Level

ID `M-fiscal-theory-price-level`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: monetary theory.

## What It Is
John H. Cochrane, The Fiscal Theory of the Price Level, Princeton University Press, published 17 Jan 2023. The publisher's summary: "Prices adjust so that the real value of government debt equals the present value of taxes less spending." Inflation arises when people doubt the government will repay its debts in full. Cochrane's related note adds liquidity: "Debt is the present value of surpluses plus the liquidity services of debt." Grade A.

## Mechanism
If expected future primary surpluses fall, the real value of nominal debt must fall, through a higher price level or lower bond prices. Monetary policy sets the nominal rate; fiscal expectations pin the price level. It extends the [fiscal dominance](fiscal-dominance.md) logic of Sargent and Wallace.

## Test Against 2026
CBO projects debt rising toward 175% of GDP by 2056 under current law, which implies no planned surpluses; see [debt-dynamics-r-minus-g](debt-dynamics-r-minus-g.md). The fiscal theory reads a high term premium and inflation above target as markets discounting surpluses. Long-run breakevens near 2.1% say markets still expect the gap to close.
