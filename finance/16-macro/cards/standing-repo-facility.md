# Standing Repo Facility

ID `M-standing-repo-facility`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: central bank operations.

## What It Is
Standing overnight repo operations in which the Fed lends cash against Treasury and agency collateral at a fixed rate, capping repo rates near the top of the target range.

## Current State
- Rate 4.0%, the top of the range, per the 28 Oct 2026 directive. Primary credit rate also 4.0%. Grade A.
- Usage $0.0 billion on 25 Nov 2026, FRED RPONTSYD. SOFR at 3.95% sits 5 bp under the facility rate. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
Daily take-up above zero, outside quarter and year ends, marks reserve scarcity. September 2019 had no such facility and repo printed far above the range; see [sept-2019-repo-spike](sept-2019-repo-spike.md).
