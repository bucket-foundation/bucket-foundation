# Treasury Issuance and the Quarterly Refunding

ID `M-treasury-issuance-quarterly-refunding`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: fiscal.

## What It Is
Each quarter Treasury publishes borrowing estimates on Monday and a refunding statement with coupon auction sizes on Wednesday morning, after advice from the Treasury Borrowing Advisory Committee.

## Current State
- 2 Nov 2026 estimates: $618B privately held net marketable borrowing for Oct to Dec 2026 with an $850B end of December cash balance; $800B for Jan to Mar 2027 with $1.000 trillion cash. Jul to Sep 2026 borrowing was $742B. Grade A.
- 4 Nov 2026 refunding: $125B of 3-year, 10-year and 30-year securities to refund $97B maturing, raising $28B new cash. Treasury "anticipates maintaining nominal coupon and FRN auction sizes for the next several quarters" and "is monitoring SOMA purchases of Treasury bills and growing demand for Treasury bills from the private sector." Grade A.
- Monthly sizes for Nov 2026: 2-year $69B, 3-year $58B, 5-year $70B, 7-year $44B, 10-year $42B, 20-year $16B, 30-year $25B, FRN $28B. Grade A.
- Next estimates 1 Feb 2027. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
Coupon size increases raise term premium; 1 Nov 2023, when Treasury raised coupons less than expected, is the cited example of a rally on refunding day. Holding sizes and leaning on bills keeps duration supply flat and pushes the burden to the front end.
