# Supply Shocks and Oil

ID `M-supply-shocks-oil`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: inflation.

## What It Is
Price shocks from the supply side, energy above all, raise headline inflation and cut output at once.

## Current State
- WTI $82.24 on 24 Nov 2026. Grade A.
- Headline CPI 4.08% against core 2.87% for Oct 2026: energy and food carry the gap. Grade A.
- The Bank of England raised Bank Rate to 4% on 5 Nov 2026, citing energy prices "high and volatile due to conflict in the Middle East" and expected UK inflation near 5% by mid-2027. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
Central banks split on whether to look through: the Fed hiked in Sept 2026, and the 28 Oct dissenters wanted more.
