# People's Bank of China

ID `M-pboc`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: central bank operations.

## Loan Prime Rate
- Mechanism since 20 Aug 2019: 20 quoting banks submit quotes "by adding a few basis points to the interest rate of open market operations" before 9:00 a.m. on the 20th of each month; NIFC drops the highest and lowest, averages and rounds to the nearest 0.05%. Two tenors: one year and over five years. Grade A.
- 20 Nov 2026: 1-year LPR 3.0%, 5-year 3.5%, held for the 18th month since May 2025. Grade B.

## Reserves
FX reserves $3.405 trillion in Oct 2026, up from $3.400 trillion. Gold 78.30 million ounces, the 24th straight monthly addition. Grade B. See [china-reserves-and-rmb](china-reserves-and-rmb.md).

## Market Reaction
LPR holds while US rates rose widen the US-China rate gap, which shows in USD/CNY and in China's Treasury holdings, down $135B in the year to Sept 2026; see [tic-foreign-holdings](tic-foreign-holdings.md).
