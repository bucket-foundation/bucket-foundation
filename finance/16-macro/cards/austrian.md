# Austrian Business Cycle Theory

ID `M-austrian`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: monetary theory.

## What It Is
Credit expansion pushes the market rate of interest below the natural rate; firms start longer, more roundabout production that saving cannot sustain; the bust liquidates the malinvestment. Mises set out an early version in The Theory of Money and Credit, 1912; Hayek developed it in Prices and Production, 1931. Grade B.

## Claims
- Mises: "There is no means of avoiding the final collapse of a boom brought about by credit expansion." Grade B, as quoted on Wikipedia.
- Policy view: bailouts and deficit stimulus prolong the misallocation.

## Evidence Against
- Friedman, 1969 and in his 1993 plucking model paper, found the theory inconsistent with US data. Grade B.
- Hummel: investment spending stayed positive in recessions with data, except the Great Depression. Grade B.
- Garrison replied that "Friedman's empirical findings are broadly consistent with both Monetarist and Austrian views." Grade B.

## Overlap
Its core, cheap credit breeds booms that end in busts, matches Signal 1 of [CRISIS-PATTERNS](../../01-money-history/CRISIS-PATTERNS.md) and [Minsky](minsky-financial-instability.md), with a different remedy.
