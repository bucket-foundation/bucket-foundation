# JOLTS

ID `M-jolts`. As of 2026-11-27. Section: indicators.

## What It Is
BLS Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey: openings, hires, quits and layoffs, monthly with a one-month extra lag.

## Current State
Job openings 7,523 thousand for Sept 2026, FRED JTSJOL. Grade A.

## Market Reaction
Quits track wage pressure; openings falling with low layoffs is the soft-landing pattern.
