# Cash and Treasury Bills

ID `A-cash`. As of 2026-11-27.

## How It Returns
Short-term interest; the risk-free baseline.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
US bills real return 0.5% a year 1900 to 2025 ([UBS GIRY 2026 summary](https://www.ubs.com/content/dam/assets/wm/static/cio/documents/giry2026-summary-public.pdf)). Grade A.

## Access Routes
High-yield savings, money market funds, T-bills on TreasuryDirect, tokenized Treasury funds.

## Regulation
FDIC insurance to $250k; SEC Rule 2a-7 for money funds. Grade A.

## Liquidity
Same day.

## Fees
Money funds 0.1% to 0.4%.

## 2026 Situation
Reserves pay 3.90% (IORB) after the Oct 2026 FOMC ([Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20261028a.htm)); stablecoin issuers hold large T-bill books.
