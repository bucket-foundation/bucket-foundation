# Farmland

ID `A-farmland`. As of 2026-11-27.

## How It Returns
Cash rent or crop income plus land appreciation.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
US farmland averaged $4,500 an acre in 2026, up 3.4% nominal and 0.4% real; 2020 to 2025 growth 6.8% a year nominal, 2.6% real. Cropland $6,020, pasture $2,000 an acre. Grade A. [USDA ERS](https://www.ers.usda.gov/topics/farm-economy/land-use-land-value-tenure/farmland-value). The NCREIF Farmland Index needs a member login; no return figure quoted.

## Access Routes
Direct purchase, farmland REITs (FPI, LAND), platforms such as AcreTrader and FarmTogether.

## Regulation
AFIDA reporting for foreign owners; over 20 states restrict foreign ownership. Grade B.

## Liquidity
Low; REITs trade daily.

## Fees
Platform fees about 0.75% to 1% a year; closing costs on direct buys.

## 2026 Situation
Real values flat in 2026 per USDA ERS. The Fed puts US farmland at $3.66T, growth 3.3% ([Fed FSR Nov 2026, Table 1](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/files/financial-stability-report-20261113.pdf)).
