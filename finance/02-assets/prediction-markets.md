# Prediction Markets

ID `A-predict`. As of 2026-11-27.

Deep dive: [deep/prediction-markets.md](deep/prediction-markets.md).

## How It Returns
Binary contracts pay $1 if an event happens. Edge comes from better probability estimates than the market price; Kelly sizing governs bet size.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
No long-run index. Contracts pay $1 or $0, so trader returns sum to zero before fees.

## Access Routes
Kalshi (CFTC-registered DCM), Polymarket US, Robinhood, Coinbase, Webull event contracts.

## Regulation
CFTC asserts exclusive jurisdiction; it sued Arizona, Connecticut and Illinois in April 2026 and Kentucky, the ninth state sued, on 2026-06-23 in E.D. Ky. (The Block and Bloomberg Law dated 2026-06-23; Front Office Sports published 2026-06-23). Maryland (4th Cir., 2026-11-17) and Ohio federal courts and Nevada, Massachusetts and Michigan state courts ruled for the states. More than 20 suits pending ([Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/trump-administration-backs-kalshi-polymarket-with-lawsuit-against-kentucky/); [gambling.com](https://www.gambling.com/us/news/cftc-vs-states-inside-the-fight-over-who-regulates-prediction-markets)). Grade B.

## Liquidity
Good on major events, thin on long tail.

## Fees
Exchange fees on trades; spreads.

## 2026 Situation
The federal versus state jurisdiction fight is unresolved as of 2026-11-27; a circuit split points toward the Supreme Court.
