# GPUs and Compute

ID `A-compute`. As of 2026-11-27.

Deep dive: [deep/gpus-compute.md](deep/gpus-compute.md).

## How It Returns
Rental income from GPU hours less power and operations, over a 4 to 6 year depreciation life; debt investors earn a spread over SOFR.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
CoreWeave's $8.5B delayed-draw loan closed 31 Mar 2026, rated A3 by Moody's, the first investment-grade GPU-backed financing, priced near SOFR+225 versus about 14% private credit in 2023 ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/coreweave-closes-8-5-billion-155028785.html); [Wing VC](https://www.wing.vc/content/how-the-gpu-became-collateral)). Grade B.

## Access Routes
Neocloud stocks (CoreWeave, Nebius), GPU-backed private credit funds, BDCs, direct rental via marketplaces.

## Regulation
No asset-specific law; lending under UCC Article 9 liens. Grade A.

## Liquidity
Equity daily; loans illiquid; GPU resale value falls as new generations ship.

## Fees
Private credit funds charge 1% to 1.5% plus incentive fees.

## 2026 Situation
CoreWeave debt about $35B at 30 June 2026, with $6.1B principal due rest of 2026 and $5.7B in 2027 ([Capacity](https://capacityglobal.com/news/coreweaves-debt-hits-35bn/)). Nvidia agreed to rent back unused neocloud capacity, making it collateral source, guarantor and customer ([Capacity](https://capacityglobal.com/news/nvidia-now-agrees-to-rent-back-unused-gpu-capacity-from-neocloud-operators-if-customer-demand-falls-short/)).
