# NVIDIA H100

ID `I-gpu-h100`. As of 2026-11-27.

Parent: [GPUs and Compute](../../gpus-compute.md).

## Specs
H100 SXM: 80 GB memory, 3.35 TB/s bandwidth, FP8 3,958 TFLOPS and FP16 1,979 TFLOPS with sparsity, up to 700 W configurable TDP, NVLink 900 GB/s. Grade A.

## Purchase Price
NVIDIA publishes no list price. Jarvislabs puts one unit near $25,000 in 2026; CloudZero gives about $31,000 for a new 80 GB card as of August 2026 and $250,000 to $320,000 for an 8-GPU HGX server. Grade C.
