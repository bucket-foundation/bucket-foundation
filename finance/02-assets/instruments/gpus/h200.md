## Specs
H200 SXM: 141 GB HBM3e, 4.8 TB/s bandwidth, FP8 3,958 TFLOPS and FP16 1,979 TFLOPS with sparsity, up to 700 W configurable TDP, NVLink 900 GB/s. NVIDIA labels these preliminary. Grade A.
