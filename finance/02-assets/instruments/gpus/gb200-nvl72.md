# NVIDIA GB200 NVL72

ID `I-gpu-gb200-nvl72`. As of 2026-11-27.

Parent: [GPUs and Compute](../../gpus-compute.md).

## Specs
One rack holds 36 Grace CPUs and 72 Blackwell GPUs: 13.4 TB HBM3E, 576 TB/s memory bandwidth, NVLink 130 TB/s, FP8 720 PFLOPS sparse and 360 dense, FP16 360 PFLOPS sparse and 180 dense. Per superchip with 2 GPUs: 372 GB, 16 TB/s, 3.6 TB/s NVLink. Rack power: not fetched. Grade A for listed figures.

## Purchase Price
About $3 million per rack, per a teardown cited by GPUSmith via IntuitionLabs, undated, and per Wolfe Research on 30 Jan 2026 as seen in search results. Implied superchip price $60,000 to $70,000. Grade C.
