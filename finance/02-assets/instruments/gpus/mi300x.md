# AMD Instinct MI300X

ID `I-gpu-mi300x`. As of 2026-11-27.

Parent: [GPUs and Compute](../../gpus-compute.md).

## Specs
192 GB HBM3, 5.3 TB/s bandwidth, FP8 2,614.9 TFLOPS dense and 5,229.8 sparse, FP16 1,307.4 dense and 2,614.9 sparse, 750 W board power, 7 x16 Infinity Fabric links. The AMD datasheet PDF and product page timed out; figures come from search excerpts of the AMD datasheet and from Glenn Lockwood's notes citing the AMD CDNA 3 white paper. Grade B.
