# NVIDIA B200

ID `I-gpu-b200`. As of 2026-11-27.

Parent: [GPUs and Compute](../../gpus-compute.md).

## Specs
HGX B200 with 8 GPUs: 1.4 TB total memory, FP8 72 PFLOPS and FP16 36 PFLOPS sparse, dense half of that, NVLink 5 at 1.8 TB/s per GPU and 14.4 TB/s total. Per-GPU memory bandwidth and TDP: not fetched, the HGX page omits them. Grade A for listed figures, C for the gaps.

## Purchase Price
No NVIDIA list price. Mercatus, 2026: $30,000 to $40,000 per card in 8-GPU volume, street quotes $45,000 to $50,000, and $400,000 to $500,000 per HGX B200 server. Grade C.
