# Protective Put

ID `I-opt-protective-put`. As of 2026-11-27.

Parent: [stocks](../../stocks.md). Index: [options](README.md).

## Structure
Own 100 shares and buy one put. OCC also calls it a married put.

## Payoff Formulas
Per OCC. Grade A.
- Max gain: unlimited.
- Max loss: stock purchase price minus strike plus premium paid.
- Breakeven: stock purchase price plus premium paid.

## Worked Example
Premiums in the example are illustrative inputs chosen to show the arithmetic. They are not market quotes. One equity option covers 100 shares.
Buy stock at 100, buy 95 put at 2.50. Max loss 100 minus 95 plus 2.50 is 7.50, or $750. Breakeven 102.50.
