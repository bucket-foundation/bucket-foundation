# Agency Pass-Through MBS

ID `I-mbs-passthrough`. As of 2026-11-27.

Parent: [mbs](../../mbs.md). Index: [mbs instruments](README.md).

## Structure
FINRA defines an agency pass-through as a security issued under an Agency or GSE program, with timely principal and interest guaranteed by that Agency or GSE, that passes borrower payments to holders pro rata. Grade A.

## Cash Flow
Each month the holder gets scheduled principal, prepaid principal, and interest at the pass-through coupon, which sits below the loan rate by the servicing and guarantee fee. Prepayments rise when rates fall, which shortens duration and caps price gains.

## Payment Delay
Freddie Mac UMBS and Freddie Mac MBS pay on a 55-day delay; legacy fixed-rate Gold PCs used 45 days and ARM PCs 75 days. Grade A.

## Market Size
Single-family agency MBS outstanding was $9.23T at end of March 2026: 29.1% Ginnie Mae, 37.8% Fannie Mae, 33.1% Freddie Mac. Grade A. The Fed held $1.99T of agency MBS at the same date. Grade A.

## Worked Example
Balances and rates in the example are illustrative inputs chosen to show the arithmetic. A $1,000,000 pool with a 6.0% pass-through coupon pays $5,000 of interest in month one. If scheduled principal is $1,000 and borrowers prepay $9,000, the holder receives $15,000 and the balance falls to $990,000.
