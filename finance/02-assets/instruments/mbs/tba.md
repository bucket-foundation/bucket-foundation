# TBA Market

ID `I-mbs-tba`. As of 2026-11-27.

Parent: [mbs](../../mbs.md). Index: [mbs instruments](README.md).

## Definition
FINRA defines a TBA as a trade in an agency pass-through or SBA-backed ABS where the seller agrees to deliver a specified face amount meeting set criteria, and the exact pools are not named at execution. Grade A. Agreed terms are agency, coupon, maturity, face, price and settlement month.

## Eligibility
Freddie Mac UMBS are TBA-eligible single-class securities; 55-day Freddie Mac MBS are not. Grade A.

## Liquidity
Agency MBS trading averaged $369.0B a day YTD through October 2026, up 4.0% year on year; non-agency averaged $1.88B a day. Issuance YTD was $1,752.7B, up 13.9%. SIFMA published these on 4 Nov 2026. Grade A. The TBA-only split sits in the SIFMA spreadsheet; status: not fetched.

## Reporting
TBA trades report at original face value under FINRA Rule 6730, with separate paths for good-delivery and non-good-delivery trades. TRACE began disseminating TBA trades on 18 Nov 2011 and specified pools on 22 Jul 2013. Grade A for dates, Grade C for the rule text.

## Worked Example
Balances and rates in the example are illustrative inputs chosen to show the arithmetic. A lender locks a 6.5% loan and sells $10M UMBS 30-year 6.0 TBA for next-month settlement at 99. Two days before settlement it allocates pools that meet good-delivery criteria and receives about $9.9M plus accrued interest.
