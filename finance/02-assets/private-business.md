# Private Businesses and Private Equity

ID `A-private`. As of 2026-11-27.

Deep dive on buying a small business with SBA 7(a) debt: [deep/small-business-acquisition.md](deep/small-business-acquisition.md).

## How It Returns
Owner earnings and exit multiple; PE adds leverage and operational changes.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
Cambridge Associates US Private Equity Index, net pooled horizon IRR to 30 June 2026: 6.60% 1-year, 7.16% 5-year, 15.01% 10-year, 12.77% 25-year, across 1,838 funds. Marks lag public prices. Grade A.

## Access Routes
Buy or start a small business, SBA 7(a) loans, Reg CF and Reg A crowdfunding, interval funds, evergreen PE funds.

## Regulation
Reg D accredited investor rules; Reg CF caps; 2025 executive order opened 401(k)s to alternatives. Grade B.

## Liquidity
Low; fund lockups of 7 to 12 years.

## Fees
PE: 2% management and 20% carry typical. Business brokers about 10% of sale price.

## 2026 Situation
Exits remain slow; secondaries and continuation vehicles fill the gap. Retail access widens through evergreen funds.
