# Government and Corporate Bonds

ID `A-bonds`. As of 2026-11-27.

## How It Returns
Coupon income plus price change as yields move; credit spread for default risk.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
US real bond return 1.6% a year 1900 to 2025 ([UBS GIRY 2026 summary](https://www.ubs.com/content/dam/assets/wm/static/cio/documents/giry2026-summary-public.pdf)); 16-country real mean 2.50%, sd 10.74% ([JST, FRBSF WP 2017-25, Table 3](https://www.frbsf.org/wp-content/uploads/wp2017-25.pdf)). Grade A.

## Access Routes
TreasuryDirect, Treasury ETFs, corporate bond funds, I-bonds, brokered CDs.

## Regulation
Treasuries exempt from SEC registration; corporates registered with SEC; FINRA TRACE reports trades. Grade A.

## Liquidity
Treasuries are the most liquid market on earth; corporates trade OTC with wider spreads.

## Fees
ETF fees 0.03% to 0.20%; OTC markups on single corporates.

## 2026 Situation
10-year Treasury yield peaked at 5.31% on 5 Oct 2026 and stood at 4.91% on 24 Nov 2026 ([FRED DGS10](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS10)). Fed raised rates in Sept 2026 and held 3.75% to 4.00% on 28 Oct 2026 ([Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20261028a.htm)).
