# Mortgages and Mortgage-Backed Securities

ID `A-mbs`. As of 2026-11-27.

Deep dive: [deep/mbs.md](deep/mbs.md).

## How It Returns
Homeowner interest payments, passed through to MBS holders; prepayment risk shortens duration when rates fall and extends it when rates rise.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
The Fed held $1.883T of agency MBS on 18 Nov 2026, down $186B in a year as QT runoff continues (H.4.1). Grade A.

## Access Routes
Agency MBS ETFs (MBB, VMBS), mortgage REITs, private notes.

## Regulation
Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac in federal conservatorship since Sept 2008; Ginnie Mae carries full faith and credit; Dodd-Frank risk retention rules for private label. Grade A.

## Liquidity
Agency TBA market trades daily; private label thin.

## Fees
ETF fees about 0.04%; mortgage REITs carry leverage and internal costs.

## 2026 Situation
Freddie Mac 30-year rate peaked at 7.40% on 8 and 15 Oct 2026 and was 7.21% on 25 Nov 2026 ([FRED MORTGAGE30US](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/MORTGAGE30US)). Grade A. The 7.45% figure in earlier drafts is not in PMMS and is dropped. Low turnover locks prepayments, extending MBS duration.
