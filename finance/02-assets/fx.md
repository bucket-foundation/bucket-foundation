# Foreign Exchange

ID `A-fx`. As of 2026-11-27.

## How It Returns
Interest differential (carry) plus exchange rate change.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
FX turnover $9.6T a day in April 2025, up from $7.5T in 2022 (BIS Triennial Survey 2025). Grade A.

## Access Routes
Multi-currency accounts, FX ETFs, retail forex brokers, foreign bonds, stablecoins for dollar access abroad.

## Regulation
CFTC and NFA regulate US retail forex; leverage capped at 50:1 on majors. Grade A.

## Liquidity
Deepest market by volume.

## Fees
Retail spreads plus overnight financing; remittance fees average about 6% globally (World Bank Remittance Prices Worldwide).

## 2026 Situation
Dollar-denominated stablecoins act as an FX product for emerging-market savers (book research, *Geopolitical and Macroeconomic Impacts*).
