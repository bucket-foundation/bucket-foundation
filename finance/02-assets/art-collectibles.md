# Art and Collectibles

ID `A-art`. As of 2026-11-27.

## How It Returns
Price appreciation only; no income; costs of storage and insurance.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
A repeat-sales index back to the 18th century shows art prices move with same-year and lagged equity returns and with top incomes (Goetzmann, Renneboog, Spaenjers, NBER w15502). Grade A. Repeat-sales indices carry selection bias.

## Access Routes
Auctions, galleries, fractional platforms such as Masterworks, trading card and watch marketplaces.

## Regulation
Fractional shares registered under Reg A; anti-money-laundering rules apply to dealers. Grade B.

## Liquidity
Low; auctions run on seasons.

## Fees
Buyer premiums 20% to 28% at major houses; seller fees.

## 2026 Situation
Global art sales rose 4% to $59.6B in 2025, the first gain after two down years, led by public auctions ([Art Basel and UBS Art Market Report 2026](https://www.artbasel.com/stories/the-art-basel-and-ubs-global-art-market-report-2026)). Grade A.
