# Sports Trading

ID `A-sports`. As of 2026-11-27.

Deep dive: [deep/sports-trading.md](deep/sports-trading.md).

## How It Returns
Sports event contracts and sportsbook bets; positive return only with an edge over the vig or the market price.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
US commercial sportsbooks held 9.4% of $38.15B handle in Q3 2026, revenue $3.58B; September hold 8.3% (AGA tally of state reports). Grade B.

## Access Routes
Licensed state sportsbooks in about 39 states plus DC; Kalshi and Polymarket sports contracts where not blocked.

## Regulation
PASPA struck down in Murphy v. NCAA (2018); states license sportsbooks; event contracts in litigation (see prediction markets). Grade A.

## Liquidity
Instant on liquid games.

## Fees
Vig of about 4.5% on standard -110 lines.

## 2026 Situation
Sports contracts drive most prediction market volume and most state litigation; Illinois imposed a licensing and tax framework from 1 July 2026, which Kalshi is challenging ([Gambling Insider](https://www.gamblinginsider.com/?p=168300)).
