# Private Credit

ID `A-privcredit`. As of 2026-11-27.

## How It Returns
Floating-rate interest on loans to middle-market and larger firms, plus fees and original issue discount, less defaults.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
US private credit stood at $1.50T, with 13.8% growth in the latest year and 20.1% a year average growth from 2012:Q4 to 2026:Q2 (Fed FSR Nov 2026, Table 1). Private credit vehicles held about $1.5T of loans to US businesses at end 2025, about 6% of nonfinancial business debt, up from about 1% in 2012. Grade A. Direct lending has earned higher returns than comparable asset classes over the past decade (FEDS Notes, Feb 2024). Grade A.

## Access Routes
Business development companies, interval funds, private debt funds, insurer and pension mandates.

## Regulation
Fund advisers file Form PF with the SEC; BDCs register under the Investment Company Act of 1940. The Fed added private credit to the Financial Accounts (Z.1) in Sept 2026. Grade A.

## Liquidity
Low; loans are bilateral and held to maturity. Non-traded BDCs cap redemptions.

## Fees
Management fees and carried interest on funds; BDC expense ratios run several percent of assets.

## 2026 Situation
New-issue spreads widened modestly since May 2026 and stayed low by historical standards (Fed FSR Nov 2026). GPU-backed lending is a growing slice: see [GPUs and Compute](gpus-compute.md).
