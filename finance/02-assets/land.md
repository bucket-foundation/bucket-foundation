# Land

ID `A-land`. As of 2026-11-27.

## How It Returns
Appreciation from scarcity and development option; lease or timber income.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
FHFA experimental land price indexes show land prices rising faster than house prices in large metros over 2012 to 2019, raising the land share of house value, while the share fell in smaller metros. Grade A. Raw land outside metros has no national return index.

## Access Routes
Direct purchase, timber REITs, land banking, ground leases.

## Regulation
State property and zoning law; foreign ownership restrictions in a growing list of states. Grade B.

## Liquidity
Low; sales take months.

## Fees
Closing costs, property tax, no income on raw land.

## 2026 Situation
Land near data center and power sites prices off AI build-out demand; rate rise slows residential subdivision.
