# Commodities and Gold

ID `A-commodities`. As of 2026-11-27.

## How It Returns
Spot price change plus roll yield and collateral yield in futures.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
Real USD gold return 1.3% a year since 1900, and 4.7% a year in the 54 years since Bretton Woods; gold fell in 13 of 28 years with inflation above 3% ([UBS GIRY 2026 summary](https://www.ubs.com/content/dam/assets/wm/static/cio/documents/giry2026-summary-public.pdf)). Grade A.

## Access Routes
Gold ETFs, physical bullion, futures via CME, broad commodity ETFs.

## Regulation
CFTC regulates futures under the Commodity Exchange Act. Grade A.

## Liquidity
Gold and oil futures trade near 24 hours.

## Fees
ETF fees 0.15% to 0.85%; physical gold dealer spreads 1% to 5%.

## 2026 Situation
Q3 2026 gold demand 1,369t, down 1% year over year, worth $188B, up 22%; central banks added 321t, up 42% ([World Gold Council](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-demand-trends)). Grade A. COMEX December 2026 gold settled $3,670.70 on 27 Nov 2026, against a 52-week high of $5,586.20 (CME quote via Yahoo Finance). Grade B.
