# Residential and Commercial Real Estate

ID `A-realestate`. As of 2026-11-27.

Deep dive: [deep/real-estate.md](deep/real-estate.md).

## How It Returns
Rent net of costs plus price appreciation, with leverage.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
Housing real mean 7.05%, sd 9.98%, 1870 to 2015, 16 countries ([JST, FRBSF WP 2017-25, Table 3](https://www.frbsf.org/wp-content/uploads/wp2017-25.pdf)). Grade A. Case-Shiller national index fell 27.4% from July 2006 to Feb 2012 in the unadjusted series; latest reading 335.75 for Sept 2026. Grade A.

## Access Routes
Owner-occupied home, rental property, public REITs, crowdfunding platforms, Opportunity Zone funds.

## Regulation
State property law, local zoning, Fair Housing Act, 1031 exchanges, REIT rules in IRC 856. Grade A.

## Liquidity
Months to sell directly; REITs trade daily.

## Fees
Buy and sell costs 6% to 10% round trip; maintenance 1% to 2% of value a year.

## 2026 Situation
October 2026 existing-home sales fell 5.5% to a 3.75M annual rate, slowest since 2010; median price $416,700, up 0.4% year over year ([NAR](https://www.nar.realtor/newsroom/existing-home-sales-decreased-5-5-percent-october-nar-report)). Office remains distressed; data center real estate is the growth segment.
