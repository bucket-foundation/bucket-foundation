# Stablecoin Yield

ID `A-stableyield`. As of 2026-11-27.

Deep dive: [deep/stablecoin-yield.md](deep/stablecoin-yield.md).

## How It Returns
Issuers earn T-bill yield on reserves. Holders earn yield only through lending (DeFi or CeFi), exchange rewards, or tokenized Treasury funds.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
DeFi lending rates track crypto leverage demand, in a 3% to 10% band in most years; Terra's Anchor paid 19.5% until it collapsed in May 2022 (book research). Grade B.

## Access Routes
Aave and Compound lending, exchange rewards programs, tokenized money funds such as BUIDL.

## Regulation
GENIUS Act (P.L. 119-27) bars issuers from paying interest; exchange rewards remain contested. Grade A/B.

## Liquidity
Instant onchain; redemption to bank in 1 to 2 days.

## Fees
Gas fees, protocol spreads, smart contract risk.
