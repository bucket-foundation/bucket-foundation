# Public Equities

ID `A-stocks`. As of 2026-11-27.

## How It Returns
Dividends plus earnings growth plus change in valuation multiple.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
US real return 6.6% a year 1900 to 2025 ([UBS GIRY 2026 summary](https://www.ubs.com/content/dam/assets/wm/static/cio/documents/giry2026-summary-public.pdf)). 16-country real mean 6.89%, sd 21.94%, 1870 to 2015 ([JST, FRBSF WP 2017-25, Table 3](https://www.frbsf.org/wp-content/uploads/wp2017-25.pdf)). Grade A. Worst US drawdown: S&P composite fell 84.8% nominal and 80.6% real from Sept 1929 to June 1932; real total return 6.9% a year from Jan 1871 to Sept 2023 and 6.6% from Jan 1900; CAPE peaked at 44.2 in Dec 1999 against a long-run median of 16.5 (Shiller data). Grade A. The UBS summary PDF returned 403 on 2026-11-27.

## Access Routes
Brokerage account, index ETFs, 401(k) and IRA wrappers, direct stock plans.

## Regulation
Securities Act 1933, Exchange Act 1934, SEC oversight; T+1 settlement since 28 May 2024. Grade A.

## Liquidity
Daily, deep for large caps.

## Fees
Broad index ETFs charge 0.03% to 0.10% a year; commissions near zero at major brokers.

## 2026 Situation
Market leadership sits with AI-exposed megacaps whose 2026 capex guidance totals about $725B (FT tally via [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/magnificent-7-earnings-rush-reveals-ai-spending-surge-with-hyperscaler-capex-set-to-reach-725-billion-in-2026-224901707.html)). Higher long yields near 5% raise the discount rate on long-duration growth stocks.
