# Insurance-Linked Securities and Annuities

ID `A-insurance`. As of 2026-11-27.

## How It Returns
Annuities pay a contracted rate or income stream backed by insurer general accounts. Catastrophe bonds pay a floating collateral yield plus a risk spread, and lose principal when a defined catastrophe occurs.

## Historical Returns and Volatility
US life insurers earned a 4.5% net investment yield in 2025, on $10.0T of net admitted assets. Annuity premiums rose 4.2% to $546.4B; capital and surplus $540.7B (NAIC 2025 annual results). Grade A. Cat bond returns are low-correlated with stocks and fall in large hurricane years.

## Access Routes
Fixed, indexed and variable annuities through insurers and brokers; cat bond funds, including UCITS funds; Rule 144A cat bonds for qualified buyers.

## Regulation
State insurance regulators coordinate through the NAIC; variable annuities are also SEC-registered. Cat bonds sell under Rule 144A. Grade A.

## Liquidity
Annuities carry surrender charges for several years. Cat bonds trade over the counter among institutions.

## Fees
Variable and indexed annuities carry rider and mortality charges; cat bond funds charge management fees.

## 2026 Situation
Settled Rule 144A cat bond issuance reached about $20.66B by 23 Nov 2026, with a $4.44B pipeline; a full-year total near $25.1B would top the 2025 record of about $24.98B (Artemis). Grade B.
