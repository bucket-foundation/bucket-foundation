## SBA 7a Rules
- The current SOP is 50 10 8.1 with Technical Updates, effective 1 Oct 2026; it replaced 50 10 8, effective 1 June 2025. It governs 7(a) and 504 origination. Grade A. [SBA SOP 50 10 page](https://www.sba.gov/document/sop-50-10-lender-development-company-loan-programs).
- The SOP .docx returned 404 from the legacy SBA site on three tries, so the rule text below comes from lender and law-firm summaries.
- Maximum 7(a) loan: $5 million. Complete or partial changes of ownership are an eligible use. Grade A. [SBA 7(a) loans](https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/loans/7a-loans).
- Complete change of ownership needs equity of at least 10% of total project cost. A seller note counts toward it only on full standby for the life of the SBA loan, and only up to half the required injection. Grade B. [Live Oak Bank](https://www.liveoak.bank/blog/navigating-the-sbas-new-sop-50-10-8), [Starfield and Smith](https://starfieldsmith.com/2025/05/best-practices-a-review-of-equity-injection-requirements-under-sop-50-10-8/).
- Under 8.1, total transaction debt including any seller note off standby is capped at the supported business valuation. Grade C, single secondary source. [Beancount summary](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/09/23/sba-sop-50-10-8-1-change-of-ownership-equity-dscr-qoe-guide).

## Worked Structure
A $1,000,000 total project cost needs \( 0.10 \times 1{,}000{,}000 = \$100{,}000 \) of equity. Up to \( \$50{,}000 \) may be a full-standby seller note; at least \( \$50{,}000 \) is buyer cash. [empirical: SOP 50 10 8 rule via Live Oak Bank summary, 2026-11-27, arithmetic]

## Multiples
- BizBuySell Q3 2026 Insight Report, released 20 Oct 2026: 2,132 businesses sold, flat from Q2 and down 7% year over year; median sale price $350,000, up 17%; the average cash flow multiple rose 2.6%. Grade B: bizbuysell.com returned 403 to direct fetch; figures come from the release as carried by Finviz. [Finviz release](https://finviz.com/news/404656/bizbuysell-releases-q3-2026-insight-report).
- BizBuySell cash flow is seller's discretionary earnings, so the multiple is \( \text{price}/\text{SDE} \). The Q2 2026 average multiple was 2.7 and full-year 2025 was 2.61. Grade C, secondary summaries. [Small Business Trends](https://smallbiztrends.com/small-business-sales-fall-7-as-median-sale-prices-jump-17/).
- At a 2.7 multiple, a $350,000 price implies about \( 350{,}000/2.7 = \$129{,}630 \) of SDE. [empirical: BizBuySell via Finviz and Small Business Trends, 2026-11-27, arithmetic]
