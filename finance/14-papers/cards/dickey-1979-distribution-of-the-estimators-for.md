## Abstract

<!-- voice-ignore-next 1 -->
> Let n observations Y 1 , Y 2 , ..., Y n be generated by the model Y t = ρ Y t - 1 + e t , where Y 0 is a fixed constant and {e t } t = 1 n is a sequence of independent normal random variables with mean 0 and variance σ 2 . Properties of the regression estimator of ρ are obtained under the assumption that ρ = ± 1. Representations for the limit distributions of the estimator of ρ and of the regression t test are derived. The estimator of ρ and the regression t test furnish methods of testing the hypothesis that ρ = 1.
