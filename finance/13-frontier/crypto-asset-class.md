# Crypto as an Asset Class

## Question
Does bitcoin and the wider crypto market carry a distinct, persistent risk premium that justifies a portfolio allocation?

## Case For
- Liu and Tsyvinski show crypto returns have low exposure to stock, currency and commodity factors and are predicted by crypto-specific momentum and investor attention. DOI 10.1093/rfs/hhaa113, Review of Financial Studies, 2021, online 2020-09-26. [empirical: Crossref, 2026-11-27, https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1093/rfs/hhaa113]
- Bitcoin market cap stood at about $1.21 trillion on 2026-11-27. [empirical: CoinGecko API, 2026-11-27, https://api.coingecko.com/api/v3/coins/bitcoin]

## Case Against
- ECB staff Bindseil and Schaaf argue bitcoin has no cash flow, so its fair value is zero, and that ETF approval and MiCA give retail buyers a false sense of safety. [empirical: ECB Blog, 2024-02-22, fetched 2026-11-27, https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/blog/date/2024/html/ecb.blog20240222~0929f86e23.en.html]
- On 2026-11-27 bitcoin traded at $60,058, 52.4% below its $126,080 high of 2025-10-06. [empirical: CoinGecko API, 2026-11-27, https://api.coingecko.com/api/v3/coins/bitcoin]
- November 2026 spot ETF net outflows near $1.6 billion month to date per SoSoValue, as quoted by Cointelegraph. [empirical: Cointelegraph, Nov 2026, fetched 2026-11-27, https://cointelegraph.com/markets/bitcoin-etf-outflows-persist-while-equity-investors-buy-the-dip]

## What Would Settle It
- A positive risk-adjusted return across a full rate cycle with ETF access, net of drawdowns.
- Stable, low correlation to equities during equity stress.
- A cash-flow or demand model that prices bitcoin out of sample.
