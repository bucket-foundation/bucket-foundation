# Private Credit Risk

## Question
Has private credit grown into a source of systemic risk through opacity, leverage and links to banks and insurers?

## Case For Systemic Risk
- Private credit vehicles grew from about $350 billion in assets in 2012 to more than $2.6 trillion in 2025, held about $1.5 trillion of loans to US businesses at end-2025, and now make up about 6% of nonfinancial business debt, up from 1% in 2012. [empirical: Federal Reserve FSR, 2026-11-13, fetched 2026-11-27, https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/files/financial-stability-report-20261113.pdf]
- The ECB links the First Brands and Tricolor defaults since autumn 2025 to weak underwriting and opacity, with possible fraud. [empirical: ECB FSR special feature, May 2026, fetched 2026-11-27, https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/financial-stability-publications/fsr/special/html/ecb.fsrart202605_04~3f2135af91.en.html]
- The IMF October 2026 GFSR calls for tighter oversight of private credit and of links among nonbanks. [empirical: IMF GFSR Oct 2026 executive summary, fetched 2026-11-27, https://www.imf.org/en/publications/gfsr/issues/2026/10/13/global-financial-stability-report-october-2026]
- BIS Quarterly Review, September 2026, studies private credit lending to software and AI firms, a sector exposed to AI disruption. [empirical: BIS, Sep 2026, fetched 2026-11-27, https://www.bis.org/publications/financing-digital-economy-role-private-credit.pdf]

## Case Against Systemic Risk
- Euro area bank exposure is about €62.5 billion, 0.2% of assets and 2.5% of equity. [empirical: ECB, May 2026, url above]
- The Fed called private credit fund redemption risk manageable in May 2026 and reported new-issue spreads only modestly wider in November. [empirical: Federal Reserve FSR, 2026-11-13, url above; Bloomberg 2026-05-08]
- Most funds carry gates and long lockups, which the IMF said contained redemption risk as of April 2026. [empirical: IMF press briefing, 2026-04-14, fetched 2026-11-27, https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/04/15/tr-04142026-press-briefing-transcript-global-financial-stability-report-spring-meetings-2026]

## What Would Settle It
- A default cycle in which private credit loss rates and recoveries are observed against leveraged loans.
- Z.1 private credit sector data, first published September 2026, tracking bank credit lines drawn under stress.
