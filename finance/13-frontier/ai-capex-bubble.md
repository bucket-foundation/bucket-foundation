# AI Capex Bubble

## Question
Will the four largest hyperscalers earn a return on their AI capital spending, or is the buildout a bubble that ends in write-downs and an equity correction?

## Case For a Bubble
- Third-quarter 2026 capex across Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon and Meta summed to about $202 billion, an annual run rate near $808 billion. Sum of the four rows below.
- Bain, cited by Takshashila, estimates the industry needs about $2 trillion of annual revenue by 2030 and stays $800 billion short. The same paper cites about $560 billion of hyperscaler AI infrastructure spend over 2024 and 2025 against about $35 billion of AI-specific revenue. [empirical: Goswami, Takshashila Discussion Document 2026-21, 2026-11-27, https://takshashila.org.in/content/publications/20260708-ai-investment-cycle.html]
- Goldman Sachs, cited in the same paper, puts circular revenue arrangements at up to 15% of Nvidia forward revenue by 2027. CreditSights projects hyperscaler capex at 94% of operating cash flow after buybacks and dividends. [empirical: same source, 2026-11-27, same url]
- The Fed November 2026 Financial Stability Report survey lists AI as the top-cited risk, naming stretched valuations, circular financing and debt-funded buildout. [empirical: Federal Reserve FSR, 2026-11-13, fetched 2026-11-27, https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/files/financial-stability-report-20261113.pdf]

## Case Against a Bubble
- Goldman Sachs Research published "Why we are not in a bubble... yet" in October 2025, reported by Fortune on 2025-10-09. Arguments reported there: corporate AI demand grows, spending is funded from operating cash flow, and valuations sit below dot-com extremes. [empirical: Fortune, 2025-10-09, fetched 2026-11-27, https://fortune.com/2025/10/09/ai-isnt-in-a-bubble-analysts-say/]
- Takshashila argues the cycle matches other capital-heavy buildouts and that vendor financing is standard in sectors such as aircraft leasing. [empirical: Goswami 2026, 2026-11-27, https://takshashila.org.in/content/publications/20260708-ai-investment-cycle.html]
- The Fed FSR notes forward earnings growth pushed the forward P/E down over the past year, though it remains above its historical median. [empirical: Federal Reserve FSR, 2026-11-13, fetched 2026-11-27, url above]

## What Would Settle It
- AI-attributed revenue at the hyperscalers growing faster than depreciation on the new assets for several years.
- Capex guidance cuts, GPU impairments, or useful-life reductions in 10-K filings.
- Spreads on data-center and GPU-backed debt.
