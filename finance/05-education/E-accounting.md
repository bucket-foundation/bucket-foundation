## What It Is

Double-entry bookkeeping records every transaction twice, debit and credit, so the balance sheet balances. Public companies report a balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement and statement of shareholders' equity. US GAAP is set by FASB; IFRS by the IASB.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- SEC beginners guide names four statements: balance sheet, income statement, cash flow, shareholders' equity. **A**
- IFRS 18, Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements, is on the IASB issued standards list; effective 2027-01-01 (date from memory). **C**
