## What It Is

The base sequence: budget, emergency fund, pay off high-rate debt, capture employer match, fill tax-advantaged accounts, hold low-cost index funds.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- 2026 401(k) employee deferral limit $24,500; IRA limit $7,500 (IRS IR-2025-111, 2025-11-13). **A**
- 2026 401(k) catch-up for age 50+ $8,000, up from $7,500; IRA catch-up $1,100. **A**
- CFPB defines an emergency fund as a cash reserve set aside for unplanned expenses. **A**
