Nine graduate courses and a capstone, one at a time in a set sequence, with a two-week break between courses. Students earn a Foundations of Financial Engineering certificate after the first two.

## Topics

- 1 Financial Markets, 7 weeks
- 2 Financial Data, 7 weeks
- 3 Financial Econometrics, 7 weeks
- 4 Derivative Pricing, 7 weeks
- 5 Stochastic Modeling, 7 weeks
- 6 Machine Learning in Finance, 7 weeks
- 7 Deep Learning for Finance, 7 weeks
- 8 Portfolio Management, 8 weeks
- 9 Risk Management, 8 weeks
- 10 Capstone Project
