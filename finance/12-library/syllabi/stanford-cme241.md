The course site lists Markov decision processes, dynamic programming, approximate DP and reinforcement learning algorithms with Python implementations, applied to five finance problems.

## Topics

- Dynamic asset allocation to maximize utility of consumption
- Pricing and hedging derivatives in an incomplete market
- Optimal exercise of path-dependent American options
- Optimal trade order execution
- Market making
