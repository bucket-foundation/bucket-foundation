The OCW page states the purpose of the class: "to expose undergraduate and graduate students to the mathematical concepts and techniques used in the financial industry." The page now points readers to its successor, 18.642, from Fall 2024.

## Topics

- Linear algebra, probability and statistics in finance
- Time series, regression, stochastic processes
- Ito calculus, Black-Scholes, portfolio theory
- Guest lectures from practitioners
