Open Yale describes the course as "an overview of the ideas, methods, and institutions that permit human society to manage risks and foster enterprise," with an introduction to risk management and behavioral finance across securities, insurance and banking.

## Topics

- Risk management and behavioral finance
- Securities, insurance and banking industries
- Practices today and prospects for the future
