## Description

Still cleaning the same data by hand every month? Watch Excel MVPs Giles Male and Fay Bordbar turn a messy ERP export into a clean, refreshable dataset in minutes with Power Query, then build a pivot table and reconcile it to the source.

Sign up to receive the next lesson: https://cfainst.is/3WroJmJ

Whether you’re a finance professional, student or analyst, you can practice along with the supporting workbook: https://cfainst.is/4za9nBC

In this video, you’ll learn:
- What Power Query is, how the ETL (extract, transform, load) process works, and when to use it for Excel problem solving
- How to clean a messy CSV export, from removing blank rows and splitting columns to fixing text and US/UK date formats
- How to load the cleaned data into Excel, build a pivot table and reconcile it to the source
- Why repeatable, refreshable steps are among the best Excel functions for finance workflows

Presented by:
- Giles Male – Chartered accountant, Microsoft MVP and one of eight master financial modellers in the world.
- Fay Bordbar – Chartered accountant and Microsoft MVP in Excel and Copilot.

Why are Excel skills important for finance professionals? Excel is at the heart of modern finance. Advanced Excel skills can help you improve your modelling, analysis, reporting and decision-making, and support your career development.
