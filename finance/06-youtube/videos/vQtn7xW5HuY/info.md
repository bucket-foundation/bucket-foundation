## Description

Is private equity really outperforming public markets, or are flawed benchmarks, infrequent valuations and unrealized gains creating an illusion of superior returns?

In this episode of the Enterprising Investor podcast, host Kevin Max, Editor Enterprising Investor, talks with Sofia Gertsberg, Managing Director and Head of Quantitative Investment Science at HarbourVest, about why private equity performance is so difficult to measure and compare.

In this episode, you'll learn:
- Why conflicting benchmarks make private equity performance hard to compare with public markets.
- How stale valuations and infrequent pricing can distort reported returns.
- The growing divide between paper returns and actual cash distributions.
- How investment-level benchmarking could help separate genuine manager skill from leverage, market exposure and valuation effects.
- What institutional allocators and financial advisers should demand as private equity expands into wealth management and retirement portfolios.
