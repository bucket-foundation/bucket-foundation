## Description

In this episode of Invest Like The Best, Patrick O'Shaughnessy sits down with Scott Wu, co-founder and CEO of Cognition, the company behind the AI software engineer Devin, two years after his first appearance on the show. Recorded the day Cognition upgraded Devin Security, Scott explains why he calls this a dark age for cybersecurity and expects major breaches at Fortune 100 companies and government institutions within a year. They discuss why swarms of agents change the AI safety debate, why compute behaves like oil, and the gap in how AI companies and their financiers price capital. Scott shares Cognition's numbers: zero to a $1 billion run rate in under two years, a bank merging 250,000 pull requests a month with Devin, and new engineers who never download the code. He closes on why Cognition stays independent, how he learned to decide faster, and the odds he puts on taking his to-do list from Devin. A clear-eyed tour of the messy middle of the AI transition.
