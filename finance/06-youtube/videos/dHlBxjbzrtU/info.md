In this episode of Quantopian Book Conversations, Prof. Esfan Haghverdi interviews Jiri Pik, Ernest P. Chan, and Jared Broad, co-authors of Hands-On AI Trading with Python, QuantConnect, and AWS.

In this session, the authors break down the AI and ML techniques covered in the book—ranging from classical models to deep learning, LLMs, and reinforcement learning—and show how each method can be used to build real, testable trading strategies.

Perfect for quants, developers, and traders looking for practical, applied approaches to algorithmic trading with modern AI tools.

About the Book - https://www.hands-on-ai-trading.com/

Hands-On AI Trading with Python, QuantConnect, and AWS explores real-world applications of AI technologies in algorithmic trading. It provides practical examples with complete code, allowing readers to understand and expand their AI toolbelt.

Unlike other books, this one focuses on designing actual trading strategies rather than setting up backtesting infrastructure. It utilizes QuantConnect, providing access to key market data from Algoseek and others. Examples are available on the book's GitHub repository, written in Python, and include performance tearsheets or research Jupyter notebooks.

About the Authors:

Jiri Pik

Founder and Chief Executive Officer of RocketEdge

Jiri Pik is the founder and CEO of RocketEdge.com, a creative consultancy focused on cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and ultra-low-frequency financial trading. With over 20 years in financial technology, he has collaborated with leading institutions like Citi and Goldman Sachs, and holds all 12 AWS Certifications alongside several Microsoft Azure Certifications.

Ernest Chan

Founder and Chief Scientific Officer of Predictnow.ai

Ernest Chan is the founder and chief scientific officer of Predictnow.ai, specializing in machine learning solutions for risk management and optimization. With a Ph.D. in physics from Cornell University, he is a pioneer in AI-driven finance and the acclaimed author of three Wiley-published books on quantitative trading.

Jared Broad

Founder and Chief Executive Officer of QuantConnect

Jared Broad is the founder and CEO of QuantConnect, an open-source algorithmic trading platform serving thousands of quantitative funds since 2012 He holds a Biomedical Engineering degree from the University of Auckland and has dedicated his work to advancing accessible algorithmic trading tools. QuantConnect provided the full support of their team, including Derek Melchin, a senior quant at QuantConnect, to create the suite of examples.

Philip Sun

Chief Executive Officer of Adaptive Investments

Philip Sun is the CEO of Adaptive Investment Solutions, where he leads the automation of options overlay strategies using ML/AI innovations. As an adjunct faculty member at Boston University, he teaches algorithmic trading, drawing from his experience as a senior quant at top firms like Fidelity and Sentinel Investments.

Vivek Singh

Senior Product Manager at AWS AI

Vivek Singh is a senior product manager at AWS, spearheading the development of generative AI services, including data annotation and fine-tuning to ensure safe and responsible AI use. Previously, he worked in fundamental stock analysis at a large hedge fund, showcasing his diverse expertise in finance and technology.
