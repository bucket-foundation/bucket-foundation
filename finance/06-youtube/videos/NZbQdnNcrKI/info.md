Tom Hougaard and David Capablanca join Chat With Traders live in Los Angeles for a raw, unfiltered conversation on what it truly takes to survive and scale at the highest levels of trading. Despite trading vastly different markets and styles, both men share an uncompromising mindset built on extreme discipline, personal sacrifice, and total commitment.

After Tom walked away from corporate stability at J.P. Morgan and after David overcame massive student and medical debt, They forged their edges through pain, reinvention, and relentless self-analysis. Today, Tom is renowned for his fearless approach to adding to winning positions in trending markets, while David has mastered small-cap short selling by ruthlessly eliminating career-ending setups and treating trading like a high-performance sport.

In this live panel conversation, Tom and David pull back the curtain on the psychology of losing, sharing the mental frameworks that separate elite performers from the 90% who fail. They break down why losers add to losers while winners add to winners, how to embrace the discomfort of holding open profits, and why "burning the boats" might be the ultimate catalyst for success.

In this episode, we explore:
• What 90% of traders fundamentally misunderstand about risk and trading for a living
• David Capablanca’s "Burning the Boats" Story
• Tom’s journey from quitting J.P. Morgan to Day Trading
• Why human instinct avoids pain, causing traders to hope in losers and fear in winners
• Why Tom abandoned technical indicators and fixed profit targets to press winning trend days
• How David grades setups (A+ vs. B) and scales in using "bullets" across different market seasons
• Why never settling for your current lot size is the key to reaching the next level

About Tom Hougaard:
Tom Hougaard holds a BSc and MSc in Economics and Finance and began his career at JP Morgan Chase before serving as Chief Market Strategist at City Index in London. After nearly a decade on the trading floor, he left to trade his own funds full-time, achieving public triple-digit annual gains in his WhichWayToday live trading room, and is the author of Best Loser Wins.

About David Capablanca:
David Capablanca is a verified 7-figure short seller with a 90% win ratio, author of Short Selling Master, host of The Friendly Bear podcast, and founder of Friendly Bear University. He has trained countless developing day traders, expanding his educational insights globally while championing an autodidact, highly disciplined framework to achieve true financial independence.
