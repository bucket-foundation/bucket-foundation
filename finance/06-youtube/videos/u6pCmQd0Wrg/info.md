## Description

In this episode of Invest Like The Best, Patrick O'Shaughnessy sits down with Brad Gerstner, founder and CEO of Altimeter Capital and the force behind Invest America, and Geoffrey Canada, founder of Harlem Children's Zone, to unpack the children's investment accounts now known as Trump accounts. Geoffrey explains why decades of getting kids to college convinced him education isn't enough without capital, and why he worries about a country where parents sacrifice and their kids still fall behind. Brad walks through how the accounts work: $1,000 in the S&P 500 at birth, 70 million accounts already open, and philanthropists like Michael and Susan Dell and Elon Musk giving directly into them. They cover why free money sounds like a scam, the fight over the name, what the accounts mean in the age of AI, Keynes's 1930 forecast and what Brad calls Giving Pledge 2.0. A rare bipartisan conversation about giving every American kid a stake in capitalism.
