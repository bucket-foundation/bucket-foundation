## Description

Venture capital is moving faster than ever, but is today's AI boom just another 2021? Patrick sits down with Ramtin Naimi, founder and Managing Partner of Abstract Ventures, to explore the extraordinary pace of Silicon Valley, why investors are paying 3–5x to get into coveted deals, and how the potential for trillion-dollar companies is reshaping startup valuations. They discuss the liquidity crunch facing venture funds, the massive capital demands of AI infrastructure, and why today's explosive growth may be more justified than the last tech boom. Ramtin also explains why backing exceptional founders in overlooked markets is the key to outsized returns, shares lessons from investments in Conduit and Etched, and discusses the evolution of Abstract Ventures and the next generation of companies he's watching.
