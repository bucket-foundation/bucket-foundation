## Description

Christopher “Topher” Merrill is the Co-Founder and CEO of Harrison Street, a $110 billion alternative real assets manager spanning real estate, infrastructure, and credit. Topher joined Ted Seides to trace his path from a summer internship at Heitman, to raising his first funds dedicated to Central Europe after the wall came down, to founding Harrison Street in partnership with former Motorola chairman and CEO Chris Galvin.

They discuss why need-based sectors like student housing, senior living, medical office, and self-storage held up through the Global Financial Crisis, what it takes to pick the right schools and operating partners (and why he expects 15–20% of schools to go out of business), how Harrison Street approaches data centers only with strong offtake, and why evergreen structures matter to university and hospital partners. Topher also explains how the Colliers partnership and the combination of several platforms built today’s firm, why he believes DPI should be the new IRR, and why he wants his team to be open about its mistakes.
