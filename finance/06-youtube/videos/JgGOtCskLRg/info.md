Join Professor Esfan for an insightful conversation with Dr. Hamlet Medina and Dr. Ernest Chan, co-authors of the groundbreaking new book Generative AI for Trading and Asset Management (Wiley, May 2025).

In this discussion, the authors explore how generative AI is revolutionizing trading and asset management by enabling powerful applications such as:

- Leveraging large language models (LLMs) for real-time market insights
- Developing synthetic data for training and backtesting strategies
- Improving risk management and portfolio optimization
- Building advanced algorithms to react instantly to Federal Reserve announcements
- Comparing tree-based models vs. neural networks for financial applications
- Deploying large models efficiently with techniques like model pruning and quantization

They also share the journey behind writing the book, the challenges of integrating AI into financial systems, and the skills future quants will need in this new AI-driven era.

Whether you’re a professional trader, asset manager, data scientist, or simply curious about how AI is shaping the financial markets, this conversation offers practical insights and forward-looking perspectives.

📘 About the Book:
Generative AI for Trading and Asset Management serves as a comprehensive guide to using unsupervised learning, supervised learning, reinforcement learning, and LLMs to design innovative trading strategies, analyze market data, and manage risks.
