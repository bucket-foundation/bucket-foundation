## Description

The model builds the strategy. It writes the backtest. Then it declares the results brilliant. But who is checking the work? Niels Kaastrup-Larsen and Rob Carver confront the seductive promise of AI-powered investing, where faster research can also mean faster mistakes. From the dangers of overfitting and crowded trades to the growing ambition of self-driving investment portfolios, they explore what happens when technology begins to replace human judgement. Along the way, they challenge conventional thinking about trend following, portfolio protection and the pursuit of ever more sophisticated models. As AI reshapes the investment landscape, one question becomes increasingly urgent: how do we distinguish genuine trading edges from convincing illusions?
