I just built a bot that automatically copies trades from the best Polymarket traders, and I didn’t write a single line of code.

• finds top traders from the leaderboard
• pulls their recent trades in real time
• mirrors those trades to your wallet
• tracks your PnL and win rate
• runs every few minutes on a loop
• shows everything in a live dashboard
