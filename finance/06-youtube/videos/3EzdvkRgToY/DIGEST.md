Teaches: The Efficient Markets Hypothesis as a half-truth. Shiller covers its history (Gibson 1889, Conant 1904, Roberts, Fama 1969, the CRSP tape), the Sharpe-ratio manipulation that follows from selling tails, technical analysis, and random walk versus first-order autoregressive (AR-1) price models, with simulations.
