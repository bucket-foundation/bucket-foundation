Teaches: A QuantInsti webinar segment that builds modern portfolio theory in Excel on five simulated assets, then derives the tangent portfolio, CAPM and factor investing with alpha, beta and factor timing.

Claims:

- Applied to every stock in the universe, the tangent point becomes the market portfolio and the choice reduces to the market portfolio plus the risk-free asset, "a market portfolio". [0:08:13]-[0:09:14]
- Under CAPM an asset's risk is its contribution to the market portfolio, its beta, and expected return is the risk-free rate plus beta times the market risk premium, "contribution to the market portfolio". [0:10:20]-[0:13:23]
- Factor models replace the single risk premium with a sum over k factors, and the speaker credits Fama and French with a three-factor model, "a series of risk premium". [0:15:28]-[0:17:28]
- If factor premium and beta are both constant, the return is pure alpha; stable premium with varying beta calls for factor allocation, and stable beta with varying premium calls for factor timing, "that is pure alpha". [0:18:29]-[0:19:30]
