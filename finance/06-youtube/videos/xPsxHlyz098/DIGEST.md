Claims:
- Algorithmic trading means a strategy encoded in a program so decisions follow rules instead of human discretion, and hand-entered execution still counts. "you have a strategy that is that can be encoded in a program" [0:01:05]
- With generative AI the code is the easy part, and the scarce skill is deep understanding of the market and its nuances rather than math genius or superb programming. "you do not need to be a math genius" [0:06:08]

- A year earlier ChatGPT could not follow an exact strategy specification, and now it can turn one into code. "now they understand it" [0:10:08]

- A basic PC is enough to test most strategies, since nobody is training a language model with hundreds of billions of parameters. "rudimentary computing resource is quite sufficient" [0:15:32]
- Chan advises against quitting a job to trade full time: it violates the rules of risk management and automation, and a good strategy should run on its own. "The two are a contradiction" [0:19:20]
