## Description

New to QuantConnect? This is a full beginner tour of the platform in 2026, recorded on the real site: every panel and button that matters, plus an honest look at what's free and what costs money. Video 2 of our free course "Algorithmic Trading with Python & QuantConnect".

In this video:
• What QuantConnect is, and how it relates to LEAN, the open-source engine underneath
• Cloud vs local development (LEAN CLI / Local Platform)
• The Algorithm Lab: creating a project from a template
• The editor: files, outline, Mia (AI assistant), Cloud Terminal, and the 5 buttons that matter
• main.py: the initialize() and on_data() entry points of every algorithm
• Running a backtest of the default template (buy & hold SPY, 2020) and reading the results: banner, charts, statistics, orders, logs, report
• The Net Profit trap: why it shows almost nothing on a buy-and-hold test
• The research notebook (QuantBook, Jupyter) and why I don't show raw price data on camera
• The Dataset Market: categories, the US Equity Security Master (27,500 US stocks since 1998), survivorship bias, free vs paid resolutions
• The optimizer, paper trading vs live trading, and why both need a paid live node (we don't deploy anything)
• The docs (Writing Algorithms, search, Ask AI) and the community strategy leaderboard

What's free and what's paid (checked 17 Oct 2026 on quantconnect.com/pricing):
✔ Free: cloud backtests (1 node, about 20 s launch delay, 200 per day), minute/hour/daily data for all asset classes, 1 research notebook node, the web editor, the backtest report.
✘ Paid: second and tick data, the optimizer, paper and live trading (needs a live node), the LEAN CLI / Local Platform, and the API.
I'm on a paid plan. Anything that needs one is labelled in the video.
