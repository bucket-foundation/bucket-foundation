## Description

Tian Yang, CIO and manager of the VPX fund at Variant Perception, recently published a report called “The Sovereign Impossible Trinity” that is especially salient right now. Yang's thesis is that the US-China rivalry is exposing three main policy trade-offs: The necessity of sovereignty (which both of the great powers insist on) means that neither state can assure they can maintain the trio of guns (or the AI 'arms race'), butter (social spending), and sound money because of physical constraints, political conditions, and inflation. Given that the US-China AI arms race forms the backdrop of the upheaval at Nvidia, you can't understand Jensen Huang's resignation without considering geopolitical and global economic conditions. Yang talks to us today about why Jensen might be our era's Oppenheimer and what it means that the biggest powers in the world can't maintain sound monetary policy while also upholding defense and entitlements.
