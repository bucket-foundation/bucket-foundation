Four months ago, tokenized equities didn't exist on Base. Now they have crossed $1 billion in weekly volume, and by Jesse Pollak's count, Base has passed Solana to become the second-biggest onchain financing market after Ethereum.

Pollak, creator and head of Base, joins Laura Shin at Base Batches Demo Day in New York City to make the case for shipping products over flashy TradFi partnership announcements. He points to Citi business clients accepting USDC on Base and a Broadridge collaboration on onchain voting and dividends.

He also walks through 200-millisecond blocks, the B20 token standard, plans to bring tokenized stocks to the US under the SEC's innovation exemption, and why a Base token is still being explored but not rushed. His biggest bet comes last: that teams of agents built on open models could outpace frontier models, and that the market for them will emerge onchain.
