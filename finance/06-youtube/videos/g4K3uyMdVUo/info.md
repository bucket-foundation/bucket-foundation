## Description

This is the second installment of the Sunday evening lecture series, with a rigorous introduction to MBS. With pass-through notes short convexity and strips and derivatives fit for any investor profile, MBSs are a fascinating product. This video explains why prepayment risk leads to short convexity around small shifts in the parallel yield curve, how CPR (using PSA) and SMM measures are quoted, differences between TBA and Spec agency pools, differences between GSEs (Fannie and Freddie) and Ginnie, characteristics of private vs agency MBS and how repo funding using MBS paper differs from dollar rolling.
