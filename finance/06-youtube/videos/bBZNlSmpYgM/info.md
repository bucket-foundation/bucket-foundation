In this episode, we're joined by Tiah Tarumoto, Associate Wealth Advisor at PWL Capital, for a wide-ranging conversation about the evolving financial planning profession, the different ways Canadians can access financial advice, and what it means to be a true financial planning professional. Drawing on her experience across different advisory models, her involvement with the Financial Planning Association of Canada, and her perspective as an emerging professional, Tiah explores why financial planning is much broader than investment management and why the right advice depends on each person's circumstances.

We discuss why financial planning should be viewed as a collection of specialized services rather than a one-size-fits-all solution, how consumers can evaluate the qualifications, responsibilities, and compensation structures of financial professionals, and why professional designations alone don't necessarily guarantee expertise. Tiah also shares research on Canadians' financial stress, access to advice, and understanding of financial plans, while Ben Felix and Dan Bortolotti add their perspectives on DIY investing, financial literacy, professional collaboration, and the importance of knowing when to refer clients to specialists.

The conversation also begins with an important update at PWL Capital: Ben Felix's appointment as President and what it means for the firm's leadership, growth, investment philosophy, technology, and future. It's a thoughtful discussion about building a stronger financial planning profession—one that puts the right advice, accessibility, collaboration, and client outcomes ahead of traditional industry boundaries.
