## Description

Peter Mintzberg is CEO of Grayscale, one of the world's largest digital asset managers, founded in 2013. He joined the firm two years ago following a career in strategy and M&A at Goldman Sachs, Apollo, and BlackRock, drawn by what he sees as digital assets representing the next step in the evolution of traditional finance.

Ted and Peter trace the rapid mainstream adoption of digital assets, the institutional sentiment shift that has reshaped the landscape, and Grayscale's own transition from its pioneering trust structures to a broad ETF platform. They discuss the product and fee trade-offs across tactical and buy-and-hold strategies, the selection framework Grayscale applies across more than 40 tokens, and why Peter views diversification across digital assets as analogous to sector diversification in public markets.

The conversation then turns to active management in digital assets, the evolving regulatory environment, and the longer-term convergence of AI and tokenization. Peter shares his view of a future in which personalized portfolios become standard and the distinction between liquid and illiquid assets gradually fades.
