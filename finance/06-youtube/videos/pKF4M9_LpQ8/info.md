## Description

What does it take to build a company through markets you cannot predict?

Wintermute founder Evgeny Gaevoy joins Richard Muirhead to tell the story from zero to one.

Evgeny Gaevoy founded Wintermute in 2017.

The company began cautiously, pursued niches larger competitors ignored, came close to running out of runway and eventually built a trading business spanning multiple markets and venues.

In Episode 1 of Ex Machina, Evgeny sits down with Richard Muirhead to explain how.

They discuss moving faster, competition, early hiring, founder-led companies, risk, liquidity and Wintermute's strategy of preparing for several possible futures rather than relying on one.

Evgeny also walks through the $160m Wintermute hack, what happened during FTX, and why the company had designed its risk limits around surviving major failures before they happened.

They finish on the convergence of crypto and traditional finance, market structure and a radical view on investor token vesting.

Ex Machina explores the human stories behind the Machine Economy: the founders building models, agents, robots, compute and new financial infrastructure and how each went from zero to one.
