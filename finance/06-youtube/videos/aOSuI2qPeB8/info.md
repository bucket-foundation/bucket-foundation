## Description

In this episode of Invest Like The Best, Patrick O'Shaughnessy sits down with Zach Reitano, co-founder and CEO of Ro, for his first long-form podcast in nine years. Zach argues the central flaw in American healthcare is that the patient isn't the customer, and that where patients pay directly, costs fall and care improves: once drugmakers sold GLP-1s direct, prices dropped from $1,300 to $149. They cover why healthcare should be a far larger share of GDP, why he expects half of Americans to take a GLP-1, his case for judging technology by outcomes rather than inputs, from driverless cars to medicine, and how his father, a doctor, shaped his own aggressive health regimen. Zach also explains how Ro markets into stigma with Serena Williams and Megan Fox, and walks through the 2022 refounding that took a stalled company to a $3 billion run rate with fewer people. It's a candid look at where healthcare is headed and who it should serve.
