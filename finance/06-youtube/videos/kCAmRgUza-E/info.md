## Description

Neil Howe on protecting wealth, preparing your family and navigating the Fourth Turning. As economic and geopolitical uncertainty grows, Neil explains why preparation starts well before a crisis forces you to act.

The historian and author of The Fourth Turning Is Here joins Matt to discuss what his framework means for the decisions investors and households face today. Drawing on his forthcoming book, How to Prepare for the End of the World (As We Know It), Neil connects portfolio resilience with practical choices about where to live, who to rely on and how to plan across generations.

On investing, we explore limiting drawdowns, protecting purchasing power and combining cash, trend following, tail-risk hedges and real assets. Neil also shares his concerns about cybersecurity, critical infrastructure and military preparedness, then examines the AI investment cycle, the K-shaped economy and the growing concentration of corporate and political power.

The conversation closes with a family-centered approach to preparation and why Neil remains optimistic about America's future despite the risks he sees ahead.

Topics covered:
- What the Fourth Turning framework suggests about preparing for a crisis
- Why avoiding large drawdowns matters for long-term compounding
- Cash, trend following and tail-risk hedges in portfolio construction
- Inflation risk, long-duration bonds and real assets
- Cyber threats and the vulnerability of critical infrastructure
- How drones and changing military technology affect preparedness
- Why a transformative AI technology may not guarantee strong investor returns
- The K-shaped economy and the appeal of powerful institutions
- Multigenerational planning, extended families and community ties
- Neil Howe's reasons for optimism about America's future
