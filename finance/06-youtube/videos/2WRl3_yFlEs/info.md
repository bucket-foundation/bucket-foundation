HOW I VALIDATE AN AI TRADING BOT

An equity curve is the beginning of the process.

• Backtest the strategy’s rules.
• Check for repainting, overfitting and look-ahead bias.
• Test performance across other assets.
• Freeze the strategy and track it on new data.
• Review the results and risk before approving live execution.

The AI agents help manage the work. I still decide which strategies are ready to move forward.
