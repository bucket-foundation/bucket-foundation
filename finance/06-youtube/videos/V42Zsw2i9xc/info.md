## Description

For decades, China's economic rise seemed unstoppable. But beneath the surface of its extraordinary growth, something has been quietly breaking down. Kevin Coldiron is joined by Logan Wright, author of Broken China, to uncover how the world's second-largest economy became trapped in a financial system that can no longer sustain its own ambitions. From the extraordinary credit expansion that followed the 2008 financial crisis to mounting debt, questionable growth figures and an increasingly desperate dependence on exports, Logan traces the unraveling of China's economic miracle. And as the consequences begin to spill across international borders, a troubling question emerges: what happens when the economic engine that helped transform the world begins to fail?
