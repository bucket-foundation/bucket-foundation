## Description

Aydin Senkut is the Founder of Felicis, a venture capital firm he founded 20 years ago that invests in seed and Series A companies, now investing out of a billion-dollar fund.

Aydin joined Ted five years ago for his first appearance on Capital Allocators, and this conversation picks up where that one left off — covering what has changed for Felicis, for high-growth companies, and for the venture industry at large. Topics include how Aydin thinks about demand as a leading signal ahead of revenue, why valuation can lag demonstrated growth by years, how software moats are being complicated by AI, and the case for infrastructure-layer companies over application-layer bets.

The conversation also covers Felicis's portfolio construction discipline, including how the firm benchmarks each company against the rest of the portfolio to give founders an honest signal, why Felicis pledges never to vote against its founders in exchange for candor, how the firm approaches exit engineering as a deliberate practice consuming 10 to 20 percent of team time, and why international sourcing — including companies like Shopify, Canva, and Tines — represents a non-consensus edge rather than a geographic concession.
