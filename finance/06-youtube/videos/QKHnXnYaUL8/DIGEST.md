# Forwards: Pricing & Arbitrage | IIT Roorkee July 2018 | QKHnXnYaUL8 | 0.59 h

Teaches: NPTEL IIT Roorkee lecture on forward pricing by arbitrage: storage and insurance costs for commodities, dominance arguments between assets, and cash-and-carry versus reverse cash-and-carry arbitrage.

Claims:
- Holding commodities for forward delivery adds storage costs (rent for wheat, insurance premium for gold) that enter the forward price. "you have to keep it in the godown, for which you have to pay a certain amount of rent" [0:07:13]
- If asset A pays more than asset B in every state, A's price plus the present value of the difference must exceed B's price. "the price of asset A should be more than price of asset B" [0:14:47]
- Arbitrage between assets with different risk and return is not clean, because it needs a risk-adjusted equivalent return. "it involves a trade off between risk and return" [0:21:13]
- When the actual forward price exceeds theoretical, cash and carry arbitrage profits; when it is below, reverse cash and carry with net cash flow S0 e to the rT minus F0 profits. "This process is called reverse cash and carry arbitrage" [0:28:14]
