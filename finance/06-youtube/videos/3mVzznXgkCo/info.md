## Description

Scott Pulcini started on the Chicago Board of Trade floor before transitioning to screen-based trading, learning to process information under extreme pressure to become one of the biggest E-mini S&P scalpers in the world. At his peak, he traded massive size at Kingstree Trading, turning his intuitive edge into $10 million in a single year.

As algorithmic trading took over and market dynamics shifted, Scott had to evolve. Today, his edge is built on 25+ years of experience and order-flow tools, using CME MBO data and Bookmap to examine activity beyond a conventional price chart to guide his decisions while volume and volatility give him the confidence to take the trade.

In this conversation, Scott pulls back the curtain on his incredible resilience and career arc, sharing the specific realities of trading massive size and adapting to an algorithmic world. He breaks down the fundamentals of his volume-based strategy, the harsh lessons of trading on tilt, and how he transitioned from pure intuition to teaching retail traders through his Order Flow Room.
