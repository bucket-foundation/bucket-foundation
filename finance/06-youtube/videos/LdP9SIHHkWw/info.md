Netflix's The Altruists, the new miniseries about the FTX saga, has been panned across Crypto Twitter. One of the people it depicts says she and her former colleagues could barely sit through it.

Constance Wang, co-founder of Pacifica and former COO of FTX, joins Laura Shin to separate the show from what she saw firsthand. She says the series builds its plot around a Sam Bankman-Fried and Caroline Ellison romance she didn't know about until after the bankruptcy, and turns Caroline into a mastermind who looks nothing like the shy, soft-spoken colleague she knew.

They also cover the show's claim that Caroline was behind the seven versions of Alameda's balance sheet for Genesis, its portrayal of CZ, which both call racist, its stereotyped Gary Wang, and the accent the show gave Constance herself.

Constance then recalls her last conversation with Bankman-Fried before his extradition, and why it convinced her the human cost never entered his expected-value math.
