In this video, Dr. Paul Bilokon introduces reinforcement learning (RL), from its origins in the Industrial and Digital Revolutions to modern AI. He covers RL fundamentals—agents, actions, rewards—and its multidisciplinary scope across Engineering, Neuroscience, and Economics, distinguishing it from supervised and unsupervised learning. Case studies in games like Checkers and Go are mentioned by key concepts such as policies, value functions, and trial-and-error learning that drive reinforcement learning.
