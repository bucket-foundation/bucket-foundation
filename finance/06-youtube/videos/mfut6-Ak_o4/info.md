## Description

In this episode, I speak with Richard Falk-Wallace, founder of Arcana.

Richard began his career in restructuring at JPMorgan and distressed credit at Silver Point before moving to equities, covering natural resources at Viking and later as a portfolio manager at Citadel. In the seat, he noticed a gap. Factor risk models had been built for the back office, and as Richard put it to me, “the risk tooling doesn’t work for the PMs.” Arcana is his answer.

We discuss the convergence of quant and discretionary investing, and why the portfolio construction lessons quants learned long ago are finally landing on the fundamental PM’s desk. Richard explains how a PM can get a factor bet right and still drag down their book’s Sharpe ratio, and what breaking a track record into sizing, selection, and timing reveals about skill.

We close by exploring the second-order effects: crowding when everyone neutralizes the same factors, fleeting themes like AI power demand, and when more information starts to make investors worse.

Please enjoy my conversation with Richard Falk-Wallace.
