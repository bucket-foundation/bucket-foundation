## Description

In this episode of The Flow, Konstantin Shulga, CEO and Co-Founder of Finery Markets, sits down with Xin Song, CEO of GSR, one of the most established liquidity providers in digital assets. Together, they explore how GSR evolved from its early roots in market making to become a full-scale crypto capital markets partner, offering the industry everything from venture investing and OTC trading to asset management and treasury solutions.

Xin reflects on his journey from BlackRock to GSR, shares lessons from scaling a 20-person team to over 200 employees, and explains how the firm combines TradFi discipline with crypto innovation. The conversation also touches on topics like stablecoin fragmentation, the rise of electronic OTC trading, and the convergence of crypto and traditional markets.

Whether you’re an institutional investor, a trading professional, or just curious about the evolution of market structure in crypto, this episode offers a masterclass in how firms like GSR are shaping the future of liquidity.
