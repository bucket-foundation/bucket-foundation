Teaches: Flirting with Models interview with Moritz Heiden and Moritz Seibert (Estlander and Partners style trend following) on adding calendar spreads and niche commodity markets to a trend-following portfolio, hard capacity limits and fee structure.

Claims:
- The firm dropped management fees and charges only performance fees, arguing a management fee is a servicing fee paid regardless of results. "a management fee, which is essentially a servicing fee" [0:06:33]
- The fund has a hard 500 million capacity in its legal terms so it can trade small markets such as sunflower seeds, oats, canola and carbon credits. "we do have this hard cap" [0:12:35]
