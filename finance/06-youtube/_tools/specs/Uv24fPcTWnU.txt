Claim: An option gives the holder a right and the writer an obligation, so the holder pays an upfront premium to compensate the writer for the weaker position. || the writer is in an obligatory position they have to be compensated
Claim: A call is the right to buy the underlying and a put is the right to sell it at strike K. || the right to sell the stock only at time capital T for the price capital K
Claim: A European option can be exercised only at maturity T, an American option at any time up to and including T. || at any time between small t and capital T included
Claim: The call payoff at maturity is the larger of S(T) minus K and zero, so there is no loss beyond the premium if the option is not exercised. || the larger of this capital S of capital T minus capital K comma 0
