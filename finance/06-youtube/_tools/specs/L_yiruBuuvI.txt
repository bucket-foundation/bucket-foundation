Claim: Walk-forward backtesting has a clear historical reading and little leakage between training and test sets, but it tests one scenario and is prone to overfit it. || you're still testing a single scenario
Claim: Cross-validation drops the time ordering, so test sets near training data can leak information and overfit the model. || leakage can occur
Claim: Combinatorial purged cross-validation splits the data into many train and test combinations so one strategy yields many backtest paths and a distribution of forecasts. || combinatorial burst cross validation method
Claim: Because CPCV uses more observations and many paths, it lowers variance, reduces overfitting and exposes Sharpe ratios that look high only because of leakage. || there could be high sharp ratios due to these problems of leakage
