Teaches: NPTEL IIT Roorkee financial derivatives course opener: derivatives as a risk tool, hedging, and the differences between customized forwards and standardized exchange-traded futures with marking to market and margins.
Claim: The course keeps the mathematics at senior-school level, starting advanced topics from the grassroots, since most derivatives teaching is either only trading practice or only heavy math. || Mathematics will be used but at a level which is assimilable to a senior school student
Claim: Hedging is the most important use of derivatives, with examples of a portfolio tied to an index or a student needing US dollars in three months. || Hedging is the concept of trying to reduce your financial risk
Claim: Forwards are customized over-the-counter contracts that cannot be freely traded, unlike futures. || They are customized, they are tailor made
Claim: Futures are standardized, marked to market and margined, and can be closed out before maturity. || they carry the process of marking to market
Architecture: none (lecture).
