Claim: A stock buyer knows returns can be good but all money can be lost, and the issuer has no legal obligation to return it, unlike a bond issuer. || there is no sort of legal binding on the part of the firm
Claim: Selling a stock ends the right to later dividends, which pass to the new owner. || any subsequent dividend will be passed on to the new owner
Claim: A forward is a legally binding agreement made now to transact at a predetermined future date and price. || legally binding contract
Claim: The long forward position pays F at time T and sells the asset at S(T), so the payoff is S(T) minus F, with the short position the mirror image. || they pay the agreed price of the forward
