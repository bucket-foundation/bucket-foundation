Teaches: NPTEL IIT Roorkee lecture on forward pricing by arbitrage: storage and insurance costs for commodities, dominance arguments between assets, and cash-and-carry versus reverse cash-and-carry arbitrage.
Claim: Holding commodities for forward delivery adds storage costs (rent for wheat, insurance premium for gold) that enter the forward price. || you have to keep it in the godown, for which you have to pay a certain amount of rent
Claim: If asset A pays more than asset B in every state, A's price plus the present value of the difference must exceed B's price. || the price of asset A should be more than price of asset B
Claim: Arbitrage between assets with different risk and return is not clean, because it needs a risk-adjusted equivalent return. || it involves a trade off between risk and return
Claim: When the actual forward price exceeds theoretical, cash and carry arbitrage profits; when it is below, reverse cash and carry with net cash flow S0 e to the rT minus F0 profits. || This process is called reverse cash and carry arbitrage
Architecture: none (lecture).
