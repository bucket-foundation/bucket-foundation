Claim: With a risk-free asset, the efficient frontier is the half line through (0, R) and the tangency point. || the half line passing through the exclusively risk-free portfolio
Claim: The market portfolio is chosen as the portfolio whose line from (0, R) has the highest slope, found by maximizing (mean minus R) over the standard deviation. || the market portfolio will be given by that particular line which has the highest slope
