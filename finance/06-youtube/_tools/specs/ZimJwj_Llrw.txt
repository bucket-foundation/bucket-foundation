Teaches: NPTEL IIT Roorkee lecture on forward contract terminology and pricing by cash-and-carry: borrow S0, buy the asset, short a forward, and the adjustment when the asset pays income.
Claim: The forward delivery rate for a currency changes as the market's perception of the exchange rate moves between the contract date and delivery. || the rates as on 1st April for delivery of US dollars will be different
Claim: Cash-and-carry pricing borrows the current price S0, buys the asset and takes a short forward position for delivery at T, locking in a riskless position. || I take a short position under a forward contract for delivery with maturity T
Claim: A short seller who borrows an asset and sees the price fall to zero simply returns it, so the payoff is bounded by the sale price. || you will simply take the asset and give it to the person from whom you have borrowed
Claim: The base pricing ignores market frictions and any dividend income, and the next step relaxes this by allowing a dividend during the contract life. || Let us now relax this assumption
