Claim: Under continuous compounding each cash flow compounds only for the time remaining after it arrives, so P1 grows by e to the r times (T minus t1). || the remaining time left is only T minus t
Claim: Projects are compared by discounting cash flows at a stated rate, such as 6 percent per year, and the one with the higher present value wins. || the interest rate r is going to be 6 percent
Claim: A loan of V0 repaid with equal installments P over m periods satisfies V0 equals P over r times (1 minus (1 plus r) to the minus m). || you are making an EMI payment or installments over
Claim: The yield of a bond is the interest rate at which the present value of its payments equals its price, shortened to YTM. || yield to maturity
