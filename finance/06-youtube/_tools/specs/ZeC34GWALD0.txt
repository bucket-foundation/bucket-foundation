Teaches: A 2012 interview with Robert Shiller on his book Finance and the Good Society: financial capitalism as an invention still being built, democratization of finance, risk management by diversification, and benefit corporations.
Claim: Shiller says financial capitalism is an invention whose process is far from over, and that securitized mortgages need creative design even though the benefit is less visible. || designing something like a securitized mortgage takes a lot of work and creative inventiveness
Claim: He credits financialization in China and India with bringing people health care and clean homes, and describes centuries of democratization from few stock owners in the 18th century to broad ownership. || the financialization of the economies of the world
Claim: Uncertainty is managed through a mathematical theory of risk management and a behavioral theory of how people err, with diversification the main practical tool. || we have a theory of risk management
Claim: A benefit corporation has a charter with a second stated objective beside shareholder profit, and Shiller calls it an experiment that may prove at least as profitable as a conventional company. || it has two objectives
