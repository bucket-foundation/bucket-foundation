# Buy-and-Build Multiple Arbitrage

ID: `pb-roll-up`
Family: Private Business Acquisition
Retail feasibility: **C**
As of: 2026-11-27

## Logic

Buy small firms at low multiples, combine them, sell the platform at a higher multiple.
