## Logic

From the abstract: The findings suggest that the practical value of predictive systems in financial markets may depend less on generating strong unconditional return forecasts and more on transforming weak state-dependent signals into economically stable portfolio allocation rules. In the first stage, realized volatility is modeled using regime-augmented HARQ specifications combined with Markov-switching GJR-GARCH filtering to capture long-memory dynamics, asymmetry, and structural market regimes.
