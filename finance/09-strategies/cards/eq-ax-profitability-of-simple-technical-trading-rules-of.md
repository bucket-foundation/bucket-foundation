## Logic

From the abstract: The $t$-test is adopted to check whether the mean returns which are conditioned on the trading signals are by a wide margin different from unconditioned returns and whether the mean returns conditioned on the buy signals are by a wide margin different from the mean returns conditioned on the sell signals. We find that TRB rules outperform MA rules and short-term variable moving average (VMA) rules outperform long-term VMA rules.

## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: Although technical trading rules have been used by practitioners in financial markets, their profitability still remains controversial. We here investigate the profitability of moving average (MA) and trading range break (TRB) rules by using the Shanghai Stock Exchange Composite Index (SHCI) from May 21, 1992 through December 31, 2013 and Shenzhen Stock Exchange Composite Index (SZCI) from April 3, 1991 through December 31, 2013.
