## Logic

From the abstract: The splitting was done using a 80:20 split.Johanssen Cointegration tests were done on training data for pairs of commodities to check for long-run relationship and the cointegrated commodities were selected for formation of the trading process. Also parameters for the trading rule were optimized by backtesting on the training period and assumed for the testing period.
