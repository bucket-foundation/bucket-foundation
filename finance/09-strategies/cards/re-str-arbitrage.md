# Short-Term Rental Arbitrage

ID: `re-str-arbitrage`
Family: Real Estate
Retail feasibility: **C**
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.5`

## Logic

Lease units long term and sublet on Airbnb at nightly rates.

## Source

1. The Effect of Home-Sharing on House Prices and Rents: Evidence from Airbnb. Barron, Kung, Proserpio, 2021, Marketing Science. https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2020.1227 (Crossref DOI, fetched 2026-11-27)
2. Takeaways From NYC's Near-Ban on Airbnb. Skift, 2024. https://skift.com/2024/12/27/takeaways-from-nycs-near-ban-on-airbnb (fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

Barron, Kung and Proserpio show home-sharing raises rents, evidence of a rent gap.

## Post-Publication Decay

After New York's 2023 short-term rental law, Airbnb short-term listings there fell 83% by July 2024, with many hosts shifting to stays of 30 days or longer.

Decay source: The Effect of Home-Sharing on House Prices and Rents: Evidence from Airbnb. Barron, Kung, Proserpio, 2021, Marketing Science. https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2020.1227 (Crossref DOI, fetched 2026-11-27)
