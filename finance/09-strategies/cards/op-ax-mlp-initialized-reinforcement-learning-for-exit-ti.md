## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: Out-of-sample evaluation over 2024-2026 shows that the supervised MLP by a wide margin outperforms passive holding and representative fixed-time and profit-and-loss-based exit rules. Across seven independent seeds, MLP-initialized Actor-Critic averages 37.54% cumulative return and a Sharpe ratio of 1.705, compared with 35.01% and 1.651 for the supervised MLP.
