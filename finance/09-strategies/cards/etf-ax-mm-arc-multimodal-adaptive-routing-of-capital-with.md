## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: Under an all-in one-way cost of 10 basis points per unit of executed turnover, MM-ARC attains an equal-market Sharpe ratio of 1.33 and maximum drawdown of -13.7, versus 0.53 and -18.3 for the LLMoE-style routing baseline.
