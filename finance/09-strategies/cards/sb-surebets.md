## Logic

Back all outcomes across books when implied probabilities sum below one.

## Source

## Claimed Edge

Franck, Verbeek and Nüesch find arbitrage between bookmakers and exchanges in football.

## Post-Publication Decay

Casino sportsbook managers call bettors who take both sides and target off-market lines undesirable, and Westgate SuperBook adjusts limits for sharp players.
