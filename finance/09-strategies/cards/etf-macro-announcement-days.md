# Macro Announcement Days

ID: `etf-macro-announcement-days`
Family: ETFs and Macro
Retail feasibility: **A**
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.15`
Origin: Kakushadze-Serur

## Logic

Hold equities on days of scheduled macro releases such as CPI, employment and FOMC, and stay in cash otherwise.

## Source

1. Kakushadze and Serur, 151 Trading Strategies, section 19.5. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-02792-6 (Crossref DOI, fetched 2026-11-27); Spanish edition arXiv 1912.04492 (arXiv API, fetched 2026-11-27)
2. How Much Do Investors Care About Macroeconomic Risk? Evidence from Scheduled Economic Announcements. Savor, Wilson, 2011, SSRN Electronic Journal. https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.1312091 (Crossref search, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

The book cites Savor and Wilson 2013, who found excess returns concentrated on announcement days from 1958 to 2009.
