## Logic

From the abstract: We conclude that forecast-to-fill engineering-linking transparent signals to executable trades with explicit risk, cost, and impact control-can transform modest predictability into allocator-grade, billion-dollar-scalable alpha. Using a rolling 10-year training and 6-month testing walk-forward from 2015 to 2025 (2,793 trading days), we convert a smoothed trend-momentum regime signal into volatility-targeted, friction-aware positions through fractional, impact-adjusted Kelly sizing and ATR-based exits.

## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: We test whether simple, interpretable state variables-trend and momentum-can generate durable out-of-sample alpha in one of the world's most liquid assets, gold. Out of sample, the strategy delivers a Sharpe ratio of 2.88 and a maximum drawdown of 0.52 percent, net of 0.7 basis-point linear cost and a square-root impact term (gamma = 0.02).
