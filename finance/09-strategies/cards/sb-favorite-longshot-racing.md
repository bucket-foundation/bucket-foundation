# Favorite-Longshot Bias in Racing

ID: `sb-favorite-longshot-racing`
Family: Sports Betting
Retail feasibility: **C**

## Logic

Avoid longshots; favorites lose less per dollar.

## Source

1. Explaining the Favorite-Long Shot Bias: Is it Risk-Love or Misperceptions?. Snowberg, Wolfers, 2010, Journal of Political Economy. https://doi.org/10.1086/655844 (Crossref DOI, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

Snowberg and Wolfers attribute the bias to probability misperception.

## Post-Publication Decay

Bias persists, but track takeout exceeds it, so betting favorites still loses.
