## Logic

From the abstract: We call exposure to this shared direction a signal collision. A linear-impact game shows the basic externality: each allocator chooses too much common exposure because it does not bear the full price effect imposed on others.

## Source

1. Penalize the Common Trade: A Collision-Aware Layer for Learned Portfolios. Eric Benhamou and co-authors, 2026, arXiv 2611.03453. https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03453 (arXiv API, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: In a frozen panel of ten liquid cross-asset ETFs, 760 completed weeks from January 2012 through July 2026, and exact self-financing costs of 10 basis points per dollar traded, this layer changes maximum drawdown from 23.6% to 14.1%, volatility from 12.6% to 10.7%, and excess-return Sharpe from 0.664 to 0.667.
