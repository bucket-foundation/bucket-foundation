## Logic

Buy small content, SaaS or e-commerce sites on marketplaces at 2 to 4 times annual profit.

## Claimed Edge

Low multiples reflect platform and key-person risk.

## Post-Publication Decay

In March 2025 browsing data, U.S. users clicked a result link in 8% of Google visits showing an AI summary against 15% of visits without one.
