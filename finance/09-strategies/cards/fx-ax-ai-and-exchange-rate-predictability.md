## Logic

From the abstract: A simple trading strategy that goes long currencies with strong fundamentals and short currencies with weak fundamentals generates a Sharpe ratio exceeding 0.7 per annum. Finally, I explore the potential sources of predictability and find evidence that the Taylor rule framework, used by central banks to set interest rates, is a key mechanism connecting exchange rates to economic fundamentals.
