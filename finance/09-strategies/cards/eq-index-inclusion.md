# Index Inclusion Effect

ID: `eq-index-inclusion`
Family: Equities
Retail feasibility: **B**
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.5`

## Logic

Buy stocks after an S&P 500 addition announcement, sell at the effective date.

## Source

1. Do Demand Curves for Stocks Slope Down?. Shleifer, 1986, The Journal of Finance. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1986.tb04518.x (Crossref search, fetched 2026-11-27)
2. Kakushadze and Serur, 151 Trading Strategies, section 3.17. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-02792-6 (Crossref DOI, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

New S&P 500 members earned significant abnormal returns at announcement, related to index fund buying.

## Post-Publication Decay

Greenwood and Sammon document a disappearing index effect.

Decay source: The Disappearing Index Effect. Greenwood, Sammon, 2022. https://doi.org/10.3386/w30748 (Crossref search, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Capacity

Small. Index funds front-run by banks and arbitrageurs.
