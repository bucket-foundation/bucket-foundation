# House Flipping

ID: `re-flipping`
Family: Real Estate
Retail feasibility: **C**
As of: 2026-11-27

## Logic

Buy below market, renovate or resell fast.

## Source

1. Speculators and Middlemen: The Strategy and Performance of Investors in the Housing Market. Bayer, Geissler, Mangum, Roberts, 2020, The Review of Financial Studies. https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa042 (Crossref DOI, fetched 2026-11-27)
2. Zillow Offers' Volatile Error Rate. Lichtendahl et al., 2022. https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4121706 (fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

Middlemen who buy at discounts earn returns; speculators who bet on prices do not.

## Post-Publication Decay

Zillow announced in November 2021 that it would shut down Zillow Offers after roughly $500 million in losses from overpaid inventory.
