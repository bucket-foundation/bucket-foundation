# Treasury Auction Cycle

ID: `rt-auction-cycle`
Family: Rates
Retail feasibility: **B**
As of: 2026-11-27

## Logic

Short Treasuries in the days before auctions, buy after.

## Source

1. Anticipated and Repeated Shocks in Liquid Markets. Lou, Yan, Zhang, 2013, Review of Financial Studies. https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hht034 (Crossref DOI, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

Treasury yields rise before auctions and fall after in Lou, Yan and Zhang.
