# Century of Trend Following

ID: `etf-century-of-trend`
Family: ETFs and Macro
Retail feasibility: **A**
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.5`

## Logic

Diversified 1, 3 and 12-month trend signals across 67 markets since 1880.

## Source

1. A Century of Evidence on Trend-Following Investing. Hurst, Ooi, Pedersen, 2017, The Journal of Portfolio Management. https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2017.44.1.015 (Crossref DOI, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

Trend following earned positive returns across a long history with low correlation to stocks.

## Post-Publication Decay

Pre-1985 data is reconstructed. Post-publication returns positive in 2022.

Decay source: Time series momentum: Is it there?. Huang, Li, Wang, Zhou, 2020, Journal of Financial Economics. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2019.08.004 (Crossref DOI, fetched 2026-11-27)
