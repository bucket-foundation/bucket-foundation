## Logic

From the abstract: In this paper, we propose a new approach called MRC-LSTM, which combines a Multi-scale Residual Convolutional neural network (MRC) and a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) to implement Bitcoin closing price prediction. LSTM has the ability to learn long-term dependencies in series, which is used in financial time series forecasting.
