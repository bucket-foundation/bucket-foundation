# Matched Betting on Promotions

ID: `sb-matched-betting`
Family: Sports Betting
Retail feasibility: **A**
As of: 2026-11-27

## Logic

Use bookmaker free bets and lay the same outcome on an exchange to lock the bonus value.

## Source

1. Beating the bookies with their own numbers - and how the online sports betting market is rigged. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.02824 (arXiv abstract page, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

Profit equals the promotion value minus exchange commission.
