## Logic

From the abstract: Using monthly delivery-avoidance returns for 15 liquid commodity futures, the models are estimated on rolling ten-year windows and evaluated from 2016 to 2024 with transaction costs, an equal-weight long benchmark, and Maximum Entropy Bootstrap (MEB) assessment. Volatility normalisation reduces the average DVR short loss but weakens SSA-based portfolios.

## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: Commodity futures are shaped by harvest cycles, weather shocks, storage conditions, and seasonal demand, but it remains unclear whether recurring patterns yield stable out-of-sample trading profits. Across 500 MEB paths, the benchmark has the strongest average full-period profile, with a cumulative return of 16.81%, a Sharpe ratio of 0.191, and a maximum drawdown of -0.414.
