## Logic

From the abstract: This paper decomposes the return sources of a hybrid portfolio combining a fundamentalist screen - based on P/E, ROE, Net Margin, Current Ratio, and P/BV - with a moving-average crossover system (SMA 9/46), applied to the Brazilian stock exchange (B3) from January 2000 to December 2025 (300 monthly observations).

## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: CAPM and Fama-French three- and four-factor models estimated by OLS with heteroskedasticity-stable standard errors identify a positive and statistically large Jensen alpha of 1.11% per month (p = 0.005) in the three-factor model. In the four-factor model with momentum, alpha remains large (0.98% p.m.; p = 0.009), while momentum is not large (p = 0.409).
