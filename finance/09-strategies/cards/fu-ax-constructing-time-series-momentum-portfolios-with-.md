## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: A diversified risk-adjusted time-series momentum (TSMOM) portfolio can deliver large abnormal returns and offer some degree of tail risk protection during extreme market events. Through backtesting from January 2000 to December 2020 on a diversified portfolio of continuous futures contracts, we demonstrate that even after accounting for transaction costs of up to 3 basis points, our approach outperforms existing TSMOM strategies.
