# Buy-and-Hold Rental Housing

ID: `re-rental-yield`
Family: Real Estate
Retail feasibility: **B**
As of: 2026-11-27

## Logic

Buy residential property, rent it, collect net rent plus appreciation.

## Source

1. The Rate of Return on Everything, 1870-2015*. Jordà, Knoll, Kuvshinov, Schularick, 2019, The Quarterly Journal of Economics. https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjz012 (Crossref DOI, fetched 2026-11-27)
2. Kakushadze and Serur, 151 Trading Strategies, section 15.1. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-02792-6 (Crossref DOI, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

Jordà and co-authors find housing returns comparable to equities with lower volatility across 16 economies 1870-2015.

## Post-Publication Decay

Yields depend on entry price; high price-to-rent markets give low yields.

Decay source: The Rate of Return on Everything, 1870-2015*. Jordà, Knoll, Kuvshinov, Schularick, 2019, The Quarterly Journal of Economics. https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjz012 (Crossref DOI, fetched 2026-11-27)
