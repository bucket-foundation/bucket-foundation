# Poisson Goal Models

ID: `sb-dixon-coles`

Retail feasibility: **B**

## Logic

Fit team attack and defense rates with a Poisson model; bet where model probability beats odds.

## Source

1. Modelling Association Football Scores and Inefficiencies in the Football Betting Market. Dixon, Coles, 1997, Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series C: Applied Statistics. https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9876.00065 (Crossref DOI, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

Dixon and Coles found positive returns against 1990s UK bookmaker odds.

## Post-Publication Decay

Bookmakers now use the same models.

Decay source: Beating the bookies with their own numbers - and how the online sports betting market is rigged. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.02824 (arXiv abstract page, fetched 2026-11-27)
