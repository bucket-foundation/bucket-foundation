## Logic

From the abstract: In this paper, we take a step towards developing fully end-to-end global trading strategies that use systematic trends to produce superior market-specific trading strategies. In spite of the availability of data from multiple markets, current approaches in trading rely mainly on learning trading strategies per individual market.
