## Logic

From the abstract: Such trades are enabled by the assets deposited by liquidity providers (LPs). Traders arrive sequentially and submit a price bid that can be interpreted as their estimate of the risky asset price; the AMM responds to this submitted bid with an allocation of the risky asset to the trader, a payment that the trader must pay, and a revised internal estimate for the true asset price.

## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: The goal of this paper is to characterize and interpret the optimal (i.e., profit-maximizing) strategy of a monopolist liquidity provider, as a function of that LP's beliefs about asset prices and trader behavior. We generalize Myerson's virtual values, and characterize the profit-maximizing IC AMM.
