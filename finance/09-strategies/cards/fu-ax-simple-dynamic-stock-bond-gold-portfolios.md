## Logic

From the abstract: We restrict ourselves to long-only dynamic portfolios of stocks, bonds, and gold, plus cash, rebalancing monthly, using only publicly available data. We use gold as the alternative and as an inflation hedge.

## Source

1. Simple Dynamic Stock/Bond/Gold Portfolios. Nikhil Devanathan and co-authors, 2026, arXiv 2609.07946. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07946 (arXiv API, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: We evaluate portfolios on the conventional metrics: return, volatility, Sharpe ratio (computed in excess of the federal funds rate), drawdown, and turnover, in addition to consistency of performance over time, judged by the consistency of the realized annual volatility. These portfolios also outperform a suite of standard risk-based allocation methods, such as risk parity and minimum variance, evaluated on the same assets and data.
