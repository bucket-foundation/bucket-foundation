## Logic

From the abstract: We compare a passive buy-and-hold portfolio with rolling-window optimized portfolios formed under mean--variance and conditional value-at-risk (CVaR) criteria, considering both long-only and restricted long--short strategies. Overall, the analysis showed that commodity ETF allocation benefited most from conservative and downside-risk-aware optimization, while optimized portfolios continued to require explicit tail-risk and implementation diagnostics.
