# Convertible Option-Adjusted Spread

ID: `cr-convertible-oas`
Family: Credit
Retail feasibility: **D**
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.15`
Origin: Kakushadze-Serur

## Logic

Value the convertible as a bond plus an option and buy issues whose option-adjusted spread is wide against peers.
