## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: Financial metrics like the Sharpe ratio are pivotal in evaluating investment performance by balancing risk and return. This paper introduces AlphaSharpe, a new framework leveraging large language models (LLMs) to iteratively evolve and optimize financial metrics to discover enhanced risk-return metrics that outperform traditional approaches in robustness and correlation with future performance metrics by employing iterative crossover, mutation, and evaluation. <!-- voice-ignore-line -->
