## Logic

From the abstract: The first agent, an inverse portfolio optimization agent, infers an investor's risk preference and expected return directly from historical allocation data using online inverse optimization. The proposed investment pipeline is applied on real market data from April 1, 2016 to February 1, 2021 and has shown to outperform the S&P 500 benchmark portfolio that represents the aggregate market optimal allocation.
