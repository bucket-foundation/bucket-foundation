## Logic

From the abstract: A long position of a risky asset and a short position of a "replicate portfolio" of its peers represent that asset's factor residual risk. We first prove a general theoretical result: even if the exact set of factors cannot be identified, any risky asset can use some portfolio of similar peer assets to hedge against its own factor exposures.

## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: The key finding is that "unique" stocks have both a higher SARP and higher excess returns than "ubiquitous" stocks: in the cross-section, high SAR stocks have a monthly SARP (monthly excess returns) that is 1.101% (0.710%) greater than low SAR stocks.
