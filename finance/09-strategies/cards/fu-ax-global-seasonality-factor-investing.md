## Logic

From the abstract: An equal-weight ensemble of seasonality models produces an out-of-sample Sharpe ratio that is competitive with, and uncorrelated to, established global time series factor portfolios.

## Source

1. Global Seasonality Factor Investing. Andrew Butler and co-authors, 2026, arXiv 2611.15657. https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15657 (arXiv API, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: We demonstrate that each seasonality factor produces excess returns that are not reasonably explained by established global time series carry, momentum and skewness factors. Walk-forward out-of-sample testing demonstrates that fast-moving seasonal patterns, such as day-of-week and day-of-month, produce strong gross performance but with high turnover, resulting in negative performance net of transaction costs.
