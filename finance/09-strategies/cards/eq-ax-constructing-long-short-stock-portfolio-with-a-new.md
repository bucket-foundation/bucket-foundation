## Logic

From the abstract:, multi-factors are fed to an algorithm for some cross-sectional return predictions, which are further used to construct a long-short portfolio. Our loss function, motivated by the long-short strategy, is endogenously shift-invariant and can be viewed as a direct generalization of ListMLE.
