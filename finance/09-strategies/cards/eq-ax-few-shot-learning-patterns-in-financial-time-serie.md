## Logic

From the abstract: Forecasting models for systematic trading strategies do not adapt when financial market conditions rapidly change, as was seen in the advent of the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, causing many forecasting models to take loss-making positions.

## Source

1. Few-Shot Learning Patterns in Financial Time-Series for Trend-Following Strategies. Kieran Wood and co-authors, 2023, arXiv 2310.10500. https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.10500 (arXiv API, fetched 2026-11-27)
2. Published version: https://doi.org/10.3905/jfds.2024.1.157 (DOI listed by the arXiv API, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: By adapting to new financial regimes, X-Trend increases Sharpe ratio by 18.9% over a neural forecaster and 10-fold over a conventional Time-series Momentum strategy during the turbulent market period from 2018 to 2023. X-Trend can also take zero-shot positions on new unseen financial assets obtaining a 5-fold Sharpe ratio increase versus a neural time-series trend forecaster over the same period.
