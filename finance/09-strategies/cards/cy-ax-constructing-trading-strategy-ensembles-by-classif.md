## Logic

From the abstract: Each classifier gives a probability to buy or to sell and combining all their recommendations (here only done in a linear way) results in what we call a trading strategy. Only top score-performance-wise trading strategies end up in final ensembles.

## Source

1. Constructing trading strategy ensembles by classifying market states. Michal Balcerak and co-authors, 2020, arXiv 2012.03078. https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.03078 (arXiv API, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: Using the Bitcoin market we show that the strategy ensembles outperform both in returns and risk-adjusted returns in the out-of-sample period.
