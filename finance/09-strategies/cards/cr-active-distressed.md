# Active Distressed Investing

ID: `cr-active-distressed`
Family: Credit
Retail feasibility: **D**

## Logic

Buy enough of a distressed issuer debt to steer the reorganization, plan the restructuring, or lend to own the firm, sections 15.2.1 to 15.2.3.
