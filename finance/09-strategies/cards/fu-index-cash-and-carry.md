# Index Cash-and-Carry Arbitrage

ID: `fu-index-cash-and-carry`
Family: Futures and CTA
Retail feasibility: **D**
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.15`
Origin: Kakushadze-Serur

## Logic

When the future trades above spot plus financing minus dividends, buy the stock basket and sell the future; reverse when below.
