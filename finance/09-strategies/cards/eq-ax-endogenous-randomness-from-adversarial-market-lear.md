## Logic

From the abstract: The market is represented by a deterministic binary return path, while traders learn predictive strategies from observed in-sample history and trade on an out-of-sample continuation. These results support the hypothesis that market randomness can arise as the endogenous residue of arbitrage pressure rather than from exogenous stochastic shocks.
