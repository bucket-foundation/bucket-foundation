# Synthetic Forward

ID: `op-synthetic-forward`
Family: Options and Volatility
Retail feasibility: **B**
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.15`
Origin: Kakushadze-Serur

## Logic

Buy a call and sell a put at the same strike to replicate a long forward; reverse both legs for a short forward, section 2.11.

## Source

1. Kakushadze and Serur, 151 Trading Strategies, section 2.10. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-02792-6 (Crossref DOI, fetched 2026-11-27); Spanish edition arXiv 1912.04492 (arXiv API, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

Put-call parity makes the package equal to a forward. No return claim.

## Post-Publication Decay

No decay study fetched for this structure. Index option sellers earned the variance premium after 2010 with deep losses in February 2018 and March 2020. Grade C.
