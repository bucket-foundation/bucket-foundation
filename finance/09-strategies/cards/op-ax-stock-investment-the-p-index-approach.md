## Logic

From the abstract: This paper has used European put option to construct the p-index risk measure to evaluate the performance of different investment strategies in China's SSE 50 index and the US SP500 index during 2018-2023.

## Source

1. Stock Investment: The p-index Approach. Xinzhao Xie and co-authors, 2026, arXiv 2606.08569. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08569 (arXiv API, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: It is found that with the fair price strategy, one-week and one-month holding periods can earn more, and among seven economic sectors, materials sector stocks generated highest annualized rates of return: 11.04% (one-week period), 11.93% (two-week period) and 10.18% (one-month period). It is also found that the p-index-efficient-contrarian strategy outperformed in low-sentiment (low-volume) regimes, while the p-index-inefficient-momentum strategy outperformed during high-sentiment (high-volume) periods.
