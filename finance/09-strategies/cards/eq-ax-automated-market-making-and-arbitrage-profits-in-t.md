## Logic

From the abstract: In our setting, we are able to compute the expected instantaneous rate of arbitrage profit in closed form. When the fees are low, in the fast block asymptotic regime, the impact of fees takes a simple form: fees scale down arbitrage profits by the fraction of blocks which present profitable trading opportunities to arbitrageurs.
