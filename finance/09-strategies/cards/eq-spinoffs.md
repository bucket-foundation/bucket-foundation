ID: `eq-spinoffs`
Family: Equities
Retail feasibility: **A**

## Logic

Buy spun-off subsidiaries after index funds and parent holders dump them.

## Source

1. Restructuring through spinoffs. Cusatis, Miles, Woolridge, 1993, Journal of Financial Economics. https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-405x(93)90009-z (Crossref search, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

Spin-offs and parents earned abnormal returns for up to three years in Cusatis, Miles and Woolridge.
