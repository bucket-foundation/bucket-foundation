## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: Our comparison includes annualized portfolio returns, maximal drawdowns, transaction costs, portfolio concentration, and asset diversity in the portfolio. Our results show that the ERI strategy by a wide margin outperforms both the minimum-variance portfolio and the equally weighted portfolio on assets with heavy tails.
