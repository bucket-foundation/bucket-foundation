## Logic

From the abstract: Using Bitcoin as a proof of concept, we treat corresponding Kalshi markets on the asset's future price as option contracts, and through construction of different portfolio allocations present a framework for which event contracts can be utilized by retail investors in order to control risk framework without reducing underlying exposure to spot volatility. Results show that a dynamic Kalshi/Bitcoin hedging strategy outperforms more simplistic portfolios across all regimes, supporting our initial hypothesis.
