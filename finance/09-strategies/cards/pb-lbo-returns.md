# Leveraged Buyouts

ID: `pb-lbo-returns`
Family: Private Business Acquisition
Retail feasibility: **D**
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.5`

## Logic

Buy firms with debt, improve operations, sell in 3 to 7 years.

## Source

1. Private Equity Performance: Returns, Persistence and Capital Flows. Kaplan, Schoar, 2003. https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.473341 (Crossref search, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

Kaplan and Schoar find average buyout returns near the S&P 500 net of fees, with persistence.

## Post-Publication Decay

Stafford shows levered small value stocks match PE returns at lower fees.

Decay source: Replicating Private Equity with Value Investing, Homemade Leverage, and Hold-to-Maturity Accounting. Stafford, 2016. https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.2720479 (Crossref search, fetched 2026-11-27)
