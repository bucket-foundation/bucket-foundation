# Soft Book Value Betting

ID: `sb-soft-book-value`
Family: Sports Betting
Retail feasibility: **B**
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.5`

## Logic

Use sharp book odds as fair value; bet soft book odds that exceed it.

## Source

1. Beating the bookies with their own numbers - and how the online sports betting market is rigged. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.02824 (arXiv abstract page, fetched 2026-11-27)

## Claimed Edge

Kaunitz, Zhong and Kreiner earned positive returns in football until bookmakers limited them.

## Post-Publication Decay
