## Logic

From the abstract: The system employs a Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) agent with a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) layer to govern execution decisions within strict deterministic risk management boundaries. A stationary circular block bootstrap test of the Agent's risk-adjusted outperformance yields p-values of approximately 0.06, which fall just short of the conventional 5% significance threshold.

## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: Evaluated on 1-hour interval data from the Binance USD-M Futures market, the selected RL policy achieved an Out-Of-Sample performance that by a wide margin outperformed the heuristic baseline, although the results varied across five random seeds, with four of the five seeds exceeding the baseline in both CAGR and Sortino Ratio.
