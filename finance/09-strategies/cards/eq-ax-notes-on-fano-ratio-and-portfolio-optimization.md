## Logic

From the abstract: We also discuss (Fano-ratio-inspired) long-short strategies that outperform those based on optimizing the Sharpe ratio in our backtests. Thus, for long-only portfolios optimizing the Fano ratio results in a more diversified and less skewed portfolio (compared with optimizing the Sharpe ratio).
