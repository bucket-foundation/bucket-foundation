## Logic

From the abstract: Benchmark-adjusted dependence $\mathcal{X}(A) = \sum_{i,j} (A_{ij} - p_i s_j)^2 / (p_i s_j)$ admits two exact decompositions: it is a size-weighted average of investor-level deviations from the market portfolio and, symmetrically, of stock-level deviations from the investor base.
