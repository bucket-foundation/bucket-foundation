## Logic

From the abstract: This paper conducts a systematic empirical analysis of algorithmic arbitrage within Polymarket's NBA game markets. By reconstructing continuous market states from over 75 million limit order book snapshots across 173 games, we evaluate the frequency, duration, and profitability of both single-market and combinatorial arbitrage opportunities.
