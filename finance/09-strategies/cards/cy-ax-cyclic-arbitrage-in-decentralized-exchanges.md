## Logic

From the abstract: The direct exchange rate of two tokens may not match the cross-exchange rate in the market, and such price discrepancies open up arbitrage possibilities with trading through different cryptocurrencies cyclically. We propose a theoretical framework for studying cyclic arbitrage.
