## Logic

Buy municipal tax lien certificates that pay statutory interest until the owner redeems.

## Claimed Edge

Interest rates set by state statute, bid down at auction.

## Post-Publication Decay

In Illinois lien auctions, zero interest-rate bids are common and ten or more investors can bid the same rate.
