# Commodity Value

ID: `cm-value`
Family: Commodities
Retail feasibility: **B**
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.15`
Origin: Kakushadze-Serur

## Logic

Buy commodities whose price is low against their five-year average, sell those far above it.
