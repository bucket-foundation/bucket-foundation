## Logic

From the abstract: We find that our one size fits all approach delivers large outperformance in both equity and bond markets while meeting the ex-ante risk requirements, nearly doubling yearly returns vs. the MSCI World and Bloomberg Barclays Euro Aggregate Corporate Bond benchmarks in a long-only backtest. Furthermore, we demonstrate that our model delivers a benefit versus a static portfolio with fixed mean weights, showing that timing of excess return momentum has a sizeable benefit vs. static allocations.

## Claimed Edge

The abstract reports: We present a simple model that uses time series momentum in order to construct strategies that systematically outperform their benchmark. We then combine both approaches into an absolute return strategy by benchmarking vs. the Eonia Total Return Index and find large outperformance at a sharpe ratio of 1.8.
