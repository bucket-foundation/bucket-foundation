## Logic

From the abstract: Using financial and market data from WRDS, we calculate cash returns as a proxy for operational efficiency and evaluate a long-only strategy applied to Nasdaq-listed non-financial firms. Our results suggest that cash productivity, when combined with other complementary signals and careful universe selection, can be a valuable tool for generating excess returns.
