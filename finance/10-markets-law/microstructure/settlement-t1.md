# Settlement T+1

ID: `MS-t1`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

US broker-dealer trades in stocks, ETFs, corporate and muni bonds settle one business day after the trade. Cash accounts can buy with unsettled funds but must not sell before paying (free-riding).

## Key Numbers and Dates

- SEC adopted T+1 on 2023-02-15 (Release 34-96930). **A**
- Compliance date 2024-05-28. **A**
- NSCC went live for 24x5 clearing on 2026-06-28/29. **B**
- A regulated futures contract is one whose deposits and withdrawals depend on marking to market (26 U.S.C. 1256(g)(1)). **A**

## 2026 State

T+1 is the rule. Clearing now runs 24x5 to support overnight exchange sessions.
