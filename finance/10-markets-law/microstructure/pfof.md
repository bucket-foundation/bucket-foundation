# Payment for Order Flow

ID: `MS-pfof`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

A wholesaler pays a retail broker to route customer orders to it. The broker earns revenue; the customer gets price improvement inside the NBBO. Brokers disclose terms in quarterly Rule 606 reports.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Robinhood Securities Q1 2026 Rule 606: payment equals 12.35% of the NBBO spread in regular hours, 9% in extended hours. **A**
- US options PFOF reached $1.004 billion in Q2 2026. **B**
- SEC withdrew the order competition proposal (Rule 615) and 13 other proposals on 2025-06-12. **B**
- Amended Rule 605 execution-quality reports: compliance date extended to 2026-08-01. **B**

## 2026 State

PFOF stays legal for US equities and options. The 2025 withdrawal ended the push to auction retail orders. Rule 605 and 606 reports are the tools a trader has to judge routing.
