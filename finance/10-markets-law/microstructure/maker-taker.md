# Maker-Taker

ID: `MS-makertaker`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

Exchanges charge a fee to orders that take liquidity and pay a rebate to orders that post it. The access fee cap in Reg NMS Rule 610 bounds the take fee and so the rebate.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- SEC adopted Rule 610 and 612 amendments 2024-09-18: access fee cap cut to $0.001 per share (10 mils) for stocks at $1.00 or more; 0.1% of price below $1.00. **A**
- Original compliance date: first business day of November 2025. **A**
- In 2026 the SEC extended the tick size and access fee compliance date by one year, to November 2027. **B**
- June 11, 2026: SEC proposed rescinding Rule 611 (trade-through) and Rule 610(e) (locked and crossed markets). **B**

## 2026 State

The fee cap cut and half-penny ticks are pushed to November 2027. Reg NMS is under a rollback proposal. A retail trader pays no exchange fee directly; the broker absorbs or passes it.
