# Trading Hours

ID: `MS-hours`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

Crypto spot trades 24/7 on every venue. CME crypto futures went 24/7 in 2026. US equities move from a 16-hour day to 23x5 on exchanges.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- CME launched 24/7 crypto futures and options, release dated 2026-06-01 (target was 2026-05-29); weekly maintenance window about two hours on weekends. **B**
- SEC approved Nasdaq 23-hour proposal 2026-04-10. **B**
- Nasdaq Night Session 9 p.m. to 4 a.m. ET; industry 23x5 start expected Sunday 2026-12-06, pending SIP readiness and a go-live notice. **B**
- SIPs filed a plan amendment 2025-12-19 to extend hours. **B**

## 2026 State

On 2026-11-27 the equity night session is nine days from target launch with no final go-live notice found. Crypto spot and CME crypto derivatives trade through weekends.
