# Business-for-Sale Marketplaces

ID: `V-biz-sale`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is
Listing sites for buying small and online businesses: BizBuySell (main street, broker-listed), Acquire.com (startups and SaaS), Flippa (websites, apps, e-commerce).

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Open. Buy as an individual or through an LLC. A self-directed IRA can buy a business only if no disqualified person works in it; the prohibited-transaction rules sit in the law cards. SBA 7(a) loans fund many main-street deals. **B**

## Fees
Acquire.com buyer: free basic; membership from $390; Premium for deals up to $250k, Platinum all sizes; seller pricing tailored. Flippa: third-party sources cite success fees of 3% to 10% scaled by price and listing fees from $29; pricing page blocked (HTTP 403). **A** Acquire, **C** Flippa.

## Regulator
State business-broker licensing where it exists; asset sales fall under contract law. Sales of securities (stock of a company) can fall under SEC rules.

## 2026 State
BizBuySell Q3 2026: 2,132 businesses sold, down 7% year over year; median sale price $350,000, up 17%. Service firms 992 deals at $350,000 median; retail 417 at $272,500; restaurants 358 at $235,000. Buyer and seller confidence fell. **B**
