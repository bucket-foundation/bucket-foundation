## What It Is
US crypto exchange and broker; since December 2025 also US stock trading (Coinbase Capital Markets Corp) and Kalshi-based prediction markets.

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Available in NJ for spot crypto. Perpetual futures for US users through Coinbase's CFTC-registered derivatives arm; reported as first US centralized exchange approved for crypto perps in May 2026. Taxable and LLC accounts; crypto IRAs need a self-directed IRA custodian. Crypto sales report on Form 1099-DA. **B**

## Fees
After the 2026-09-16 restructure, US Advanced entry tier is reported as 0.50% maker and 0.90% taker; earlier 2026 schedule was 0.40% and 0.60%. Check the live fee page before use. **B**

## Regulator
State money transmitter licenses (NJ Department of Banking and Insurance); CFTC and NFA for derivatives; SEC and FINRA for the stock broker.

## 2026 State
"Everything exchange" push: stocks, prediction markets, US perps, pre-IPO perps outside the US, planned tokenized stocks for non-US users. **B**
