# Hyperliquid

ID: `V-hyperliquid`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is
Onchain perpetual and spot order book running on its own L1.

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Closed. The Terms of Use define Restricted Persons to cover anyone residing, located or incorporated in the US and US citizens wherever they are, and ban VPNs, proxies and location masking, per Datawallet's paraphrase; the terms page itself returned HTTP 403. Not available in any US account type. **B**

## Fees
Tier 0 perps: 0.045% taker, 0.015% maker. Tier 0 spot: 0.070% taker, 0.040% maker. **A**

## Regulator
None in the US for the protocol. CFTC would treat US-facing perps as futures.

## 2026 State
In August 2026 President Trump said his administration is working to bring Hyperliquid into the US in a fully compliant and legal fashion. Around 2026-08-31 Payward said it is working with the CFTC to give registered US users access to perps linked to Hyperliquid markets through Bitnomial; no launch is confirmed. **B**
