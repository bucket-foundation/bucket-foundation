# Kraken

ID: `V-kraken`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is
Crypto exchange (Payward). Spot, margin, CME-listed crypto futures, and US perpetuals.

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Spot available to US clients. Perpetuals for "eligible US clients" on Kraken Pro since 2026-06-15, offered through NinjaTrader Clearing LLC dba Kraken Derivatives US (CFTC-registered FCM) and listed on Bitnomial Exchange (CFTC DCM, bought by Payward). Assets: BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, ADA, LINK, DOGE, LTC, AVAX. Eight-hour funding. Perps are futures for tax purposes; whether they qualify as Section 1256 contracts is open. **A** for structure, **B** for launch date.

## Fees
Kraken's announcement lists no trading fee figures.

## Regulator
CFTC and NFA for perps; state money transmitter licenses for spot.

## 2026 State
Spot, margin (US launch June 2026), futures and perps share one account and collateral. **B**
