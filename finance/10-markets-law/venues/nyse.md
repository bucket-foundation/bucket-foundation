## What It Is
New York Stock Exchange, owned by ICE. NYSE Arca lists most US ETFs.

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Through any SEC-registered broker. Members of NYSE Arca are US broker-dealers; individuals reach the book only through a broker. Works in taxable, IRA, Roth, solo 401k and LLC accounts. **A**

## Fees
Exchange fees are paid by the broker; retail sees commission and spread. Most retail brokers charge $0 commission.

## Regulator
SEC; FINRA for member broker-dealers.

## 2026 State
SEC approved NYSE Arca 23-hours-a-day, 5-days-a-week trading in May 2026 (Release 34-106419 follows it up). NYSE Arca and Nasdaq both target 2026-12-06 for launch, pending SIP and DTCC readiness. **B**
