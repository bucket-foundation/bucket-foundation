# Pinnacle and Sharp Books

ID: `V-pinnacle`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is
Low-margin books that take sharp action and move lines on it. Pinnacle is the reference price for the market.

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Closed. Pinnacle stopped taking US customers on 2007-01-11 after UIGEA. Offshore books that take US bettors are unlicensed in NJ. Lawful US routes to sharp-style pricing: CFTC exchanges with sports contracts (Kalshi, Novig, ProphetX). Novig got DCM status 2026-06-16 and launched nationwide 2026-08-04 with a 21+ rule; ProphetX got DCM and DCO status in June 2026. **B** 

## Fees
Pinnacle prices are not reachable from the US. Exchange fees per venue card.

## Regulator
Pinnacle: Curacao and others. Novig and ProphetX: CFTC.

## 2026 State
Novig exited NJ in August 2025 after the NJ dual-currency sweepstakes ban; now back as a federal exchange. **B**
