# Betfair Exchange

ID: `V-betfair`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is
Peer-to-peer betting exchange owned by Flutter, parent of FanDuel. Bettors back and lay; Betfair takes commission on net winnings.

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Closed. Betfair ran a NJ horse-racing-only exchange with Monmouth Park from 2016 and shut it on October 1 of the closure year (announced Friday, September 11, which points to 2020). NJ handle in 2018 was $12,371,257. Commission charged to NJ customers was 12%. The UK and other non-US exchange is not open to US residents. **B** (closure year inferred)

## Fees
Commission on net market winnings.

## Regulator
NJ Racing Commission for the old NJ exchange; UK Gambling Commission for Betfair.

## 2026 State
No US Betfair exchange. CFTC event contract exchanges fill the exchange role in the US. **B**
