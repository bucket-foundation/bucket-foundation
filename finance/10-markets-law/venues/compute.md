# GPU Compute Marketplaces and Compute Futures

ID: `V-compute`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is
Spot and reserved GPU rental markets (Vast.ai, SF Compute) and new exchange-listed futures on GPU rental price indexes.

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Vast.ai: anyone can rent; on-demand per-second billing, interruptible at 50%+ discount, reserved 1, 3 or 6 months; hosts list their own GPUs and prices are set by the market. SF Compute: reserve capacity and resell unused hours; example on its page shows $3.00 per GPU-hour reserved. CME compute futures through an FCM like any NYMEX contract; ICE and Ornn contracts pending approval; Architect's AX lists GPU rental perps on Ornn data. Renting GPUs out is business income for an individual or LLC. **B**

## Fees
Vast.ai pricing page lists no platform fee. CME: exchange plus FCM fees.

## Regulator
Rental markets: none specific. Compute futures: CFTC.

## 2026 State
CME and Silicon Data H100 and B200 Rental Index futures, 730 GPU-hours per contract, $0.01 tick, announced for 2026-10-05; an article dated 2026-10-04 still described CFTC approval as pending; no first trading day or volume appears in any fetched source. Ornn raised $33M in June 2026 (a16z led). **B**
