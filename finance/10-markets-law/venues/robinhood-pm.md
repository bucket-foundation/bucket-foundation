## What It Is
Event contracts inside Robinhood Derivatives LLC (CFTC FCM, NFA member), routed to KalshiEX, ForecastEX, Rothera Exchange and Clearing, or Crypto.com Derivatives North America. Hub launched March 2025.

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Requires 18+, US residency, an individual brokerage account and an approved Robinhood Derivatives account. IRAs not listed as eligible. Official state limits: Maryland, Michigan, Nevada no sports; Ohio and Louisiana no new sports; Utah no new sports, culture, elections or politics. NJ is not on the official list, and the page says the app shows the current list. DeFi Rate, updated 2026-11-13, reports Robinhood has pulled sports contracts in Maryland, New Jersey and Nevada while economic contracts stay available. Robinhood restored NJ sports contracts in August 2025 and sued New Jersey and Nevada regulators. Not FDIC or SIPC protected. **A** for official list, **B** for NJ sports.

## Fees
Since 2026-06-01: commission = k x p x (1 - p) x contracts, k = 5% with Gold, 10% without, capped at $0.01 per contract. Exchanges may add up to $0.01 per contract each side. **A**

## Regulator
CFTC; NFA.

## 2026 State
From 2026-09-08 some football contracts route to Crypto.com. Connecticut cease-and-desist in September 2026 per DeFi Rate. **B**
