# Polymarket International

ID: `V-polymarket-intl`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is
Onchain order book on Polygon settling in USDC; the original Polymarket.

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Closed. Geoblocking is by physical location; the perps docs list the United States among places where order placement is not permitted. US persons use Polymarket US instead. **A**

## Fees
Takers pay, makers pay zero. Fee = C x feeRate x p x (1 - p). Taker rate 0.05 for sports, finance, tech, election politics; 0.01 for most crypto, economics, culture, weather; 0 for geopolitics. **A**

## Regulator
None in the US for the offshore venue.

## 2026 State
Tightened VPN rules around 2026-06-01 per Covers. **B**
