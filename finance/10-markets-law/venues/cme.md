# CME Group

ID: `V-cme`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is
CME, CBOT, NYMEX and COMEX futures and options: rates, equity index, energy, metals, ags, FX, crypto, and since 2026 compute.

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Through a futures commission merchant (FCM). Futures fit taxable and LLC accounts; IRAs need a broker that permits futures in IRAs. Section 1256 tax treatment applies to regulated futures. FanDuel Predicts, built with CME, offers event contracts; sports contracts only in states without legal mobile sports betting, so NJ residents get non-sports contracts only. **A**

## Fees
Exchange plus clearing plus FCM commission per contract.

## Regulator
CFTC; NFA for FCMs.

## 2026 State
24/7 crypto futures and options launched, CME release dated 2026-06-01. Crypto ADV was 407,200 contracts year to date at the February announcement, up 46%. Compute futures on Silicon Data H100 and B200 rental indexes announced for 2026-10-05, 730 GPU-hours per contract, ticker GPU1 for H100; launch volume not confirmed in fetched sources. **A** for 24/7, **B** for compute.
