## What It Is
Cboe Options (SPX, VIX options), Cboe equity exchanges, Cboe Futures Exchange (CFE).

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Options through a broker with options approval; IRAs allow limited options levels. SPX and VIX options are Section 1256 contracts. CFE futures through an FCM. **A**

## Fees
Per-contract exchange fees passed through by brokers.

## Regulator
SEC for options and equities; CFTC for CFE.

## 2026 State
CFE listed bitcoin and ether continuous futures, cash settled, cleared by Cboe Clear U.S., trading 23x5; launch moved from 2025-11-10 to 2025-12-15. Cboe also targets December 2026 for near 24x5 US equities trading. **B**
