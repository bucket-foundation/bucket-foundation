# DEXs

ID: `V-dex`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is
Decentralized exchanges: smart-contract pools (Uniswap, others) traded from a self-custody wallet.

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Open to anyone with a wallet; no KYC at the protocol. Taxable or LLC accounts; IRAs only through a self-directed IRA LLC that holds its own wallet. Front ends may block tokens. Tax and broker reporting for DeFi: see the law cards. **B**

## Fees
Pool fee per swap (set per pool) plus gas. Uniswap Labs charges no interface fee since 2025-12-27. Uniswap protocol fee switch turned on after the UNIfication vote; 2026 proposal extends it to eight L2 chains. **A** for interface fee.

## Regulator
No dedicated US regulator; SEC and CFTC enforcement theories apply case by case.

## 2026 State
Fee switch live; protocol revenue burns UNI. **B**
