# NJ Sportsbooks

ID: `V-sportsbooks`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is
State-licensed online and retail sportsbooks in New Jersey: DraftKings, FanDuel, Fanatics, theScore and others under the Division of Gaming Enforcement (DGE).

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Legal for bettors 21+ physically in NJ. Cash accounts only; no IRA or brokerage wrapper. Winnings are gambling income; losses deductible only as an itemized deduction (see law cards). Books limit winning and arbitrage accounts; a DraftKings co-founder defended limits as needed for viability. Massachusetts requires books to tell bettors why they are limited; no fetched NJ equivalent. **B**

## Fees
Vig built into prices. Operators pay NJ 19.75% on online sports, iGaming and fantasy revenue, up from 13% for sports, signed 2025-07-01. **B**

## Regulator
NJ DGE under the Attorney General.

## 2026 State
October 2026: NJ added data-driven responsible-gaming requirements to its rules. DGE fined Fanatics, DraftKings and theScore in 2026. A DGE official said the books were not offering prediction markets inside NJ. **B**
