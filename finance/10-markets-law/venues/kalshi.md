# Kalshi

ID: `V-kalshi`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is
KalshiEX LLC, CFTC-designated contract market for event contracts: economics, politics, weather, sports, crypto.

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Available in NJ, sports included. The Third Circuit on 2026-04-06 (opinion dated 2026-04-04 in Skadden's note) affirmed 2-1 a preliminary injunction that bars New Jersey from enforcing its gaming law against Kalshi sports contracts: they are swaps under the CEA and state law is field and conflict preempted. Judge Porter for the majority, Judge Roth dissenting. New Jersey officials filed a second extension application at the Supreme Court on 2026-07-22 (No. 25A1465). Taxable and LLC accounts; no IRA route in fetched sources. Tax character of event contract gains is unsettled. **A**

## Fees
Fee on expected earnings per contract; maker fees on some markets, charged on execution only. Fee PDF blocked (HTTP 429) on 2026-11-27. **A**

## Regulator
CFTC.

## 2026 State
CFTC approved the Kalshi BTCPERP contract on 2026-05-29. **B**
