# Real Estate Crowdfunding

ID: `V-re-crowd`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is
Online sale of real estate fund or deal interests under Regulation Crowdfunding (Reg CF), Regulation A (A+) or Reg D. Fundrise runs Reg A funds.

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Reg CF: issuer cap $5M per 12 months. Non-accredited investors with income or net worth under $124,000 may invest the greater of $2,500 or 5% of the lesser of the two across all Reg CF offerings in 12 months; above $124,000 the cap is 10%, maximum $124,000. Reg A Tier 2: up to $75M per issuer; non-accredited purchases capped at 10% of the greater of income or net worth (excluding primary residence), unless the security lists on an exchange. Tier 1 has no investor cap. Reg D 506(c) deals need accredited status. Fundrise: $10 taxable minimum, $1,000 IRA minimum via Inspira Financial. **A** for rules, **B** for Fundrise.

## Fees
Fundrise: 0.15% advisory plus 0.85% management on real estate funds; Innovation Fund 1.85%. IRA fee waived at $3,000 in Fundrise assets in the IRA or $25,000 total. **B**

## Regulator
SEC; FINRA for funding portals; NJ Bureau of Securities for Tier 1 and Reg CF notice filings.

## 2026 State
Thresholds from the 2022 inflation adjustment still apply; next adjustment due 2027. **B**
