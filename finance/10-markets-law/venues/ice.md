## What It Is
Intercontinental Exchange: owns NYSE, ICE Futures US and Europe (Brent, softs, rates), clearing houses, and mortgage technology.

## Access for a NJ US Individual
ICE futures through an FCM that carries them; equities through NYSE. **A**

## Fees
Per-contract exchange and clearing fees.

## Regulator
CFTC for ICE Futures US; SEC for NYSE; FCA for ICE Futures Europe.

## 2026 State
ICE completed its Polymarket investment with $600M in March 2026 after a $1B first tranche in October 2025; reports put the total near $1.6B. ICE and Ornn plan cash-settled GPU compute futures on the Ornn Compute Price Index (H100, H200, B200, RTX 5090); not yet approved in fetched coverage. **B**
