# Agency MBS TBA

ID: `V-mbs-tba`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is
To-be-announced forward market for Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac UMBS (and Ginnie Mae). UMBS forward trading began 2019-03-12, first settlement 2019-06-03, merging the two GSE markets.

## Access for a NJ US Individual
Direct TBA trading is a dealer market. FINRA Rule 4210(e)(2)(H) sets margin for covered agency transactions; a 2021 FINRA proposal would drop the 2% maintenance margin for non-exempt accounts (approval not confirmed in fetched sources). Individuals reach MBS through agency MBS ETFs and mutual funds in any brokerage or retirement account, or through MBS futures where listed. **B**

## Fees
Dealer bid-ask spread.

## Regulator
FHFA (conservator of the GSEs), SEC, FINRA; TMPG sets practice guidelines.

## 2026 State
FHFA announced $200B of GSE agency MBS purchases on 2026-01-08. CRS report of 2026-08-25 covers conservatorship status; no completed release found. TMPG warns that a release could split UMBS back into two markets. **B**
