# Self-Directed IRA

## What It Is

An IRA at a custodian that holds alternative assets: real estate, private notes, private companies, crypto. Same contribution limits as traditional and Roth [1].

## Rules

- Disqualified persons: the owner, fiduciaries, spouse, ancestors, lineal descendants and their spouses [3]. **A**
- Banned: borrowing from the IRA, selling property to it, pledging it, buying property for personal use [3]. **A**
- Penalty: the account stops being an IRA on January 1 of that year and all assets are deemed distributed [3]. **A**
- No collectibles or life insurance [4]. **A**
- UBTI: IRAs owe tax on unrelated business income; Form 990-T at $1,000 gross; the debt-financed income exclusion does not apply, so mortgaged real estate creates UDFI [5]. **A**

## Real Estate

No owner sweat equity, no personal use, no family tenants. A mortgage must be nonrecourse, since a personal guarantee pledges the owner's credit. Leverage triggers UDFI [3][5]. Nonrecourse point is a grade B inference from the pledge rule.

## Crypto

Crypto is property [10]; the IRS lists no ban on it in IRAs [4]. Custodian access sets the venue list.
