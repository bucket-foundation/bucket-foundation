# LLC Trading Entity

ID: `X-llc`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

A single-member LLC is disregarded for tax and changes nothing about trader status, wash sales or 475(f). A multi-member LLC files as a partnership and can make its own 475(f) election as the trading business [6]. **B**

## Use

Separates trading from other activity and documents the trade or business that Topic 429 asks for. Brokerage and futures accounts open in the LLC name. Expenses land on the entity return; for a disregarded LLC on Schedule C [6].

## State Costs

NJ Division of Revenue fees: certificate of formation $100, annual report $75 [7]. A multi-member LLC with New Jersey-source income and more than two owners pays a $150 partnership filing fee per owner, capped at $250,000 [8]. **A**
