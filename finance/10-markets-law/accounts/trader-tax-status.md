## Test

Trading to profit from daily price swings. Substantial, continuous and regular. IRS weighs holding period, frequency, dollar volume, reliance for income and time spent. Calling yourself a day trader does nothing [6]. **A**

## Mark-to-Market Election

- Elect by the unextended due date of the prior year's return, with a statement naming the first year and the business. New taxpayers: in books within 2 months 15 days of year start [6]. **A**
- Late elections generally not allowed [6]. **A**
- Effect: gains and losses ordinary on Form 4797 Part II; no wash sale, no $3,000 capital loss cap. Investment holdings must be segregated [6]. **A**
- Without 475(f): capital treatment and wash sales still apply [6]. **A**

## Expenses

Schedule C. Commissions go into basis [6]. **A**
