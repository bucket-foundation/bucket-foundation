## What It Is

Any asset any venue allows. No contribution cap. Gains are capital on Schedule D and Form 8949; wash sale and the $3,000 capital loss limit apply unless the owner has trader status with a 475(f) election [6]. Crypto is property under Notice 2014-21 [10].

## New Jersey

NJ taxes gains as ordinary gross income. Contributions to IRAs get no NJ deduction either, so the NJ gap between taxable and IRA accounts is smaller than the federal gap [2].
