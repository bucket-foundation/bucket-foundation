## Key Numbers 2026

- Contribution $7,500, catch-up $1,100 at 50 and over [1]. **A**
- Deduction phase-out when covered at work: single $81,000 to $91,000; MFJ $129,000 to $149,000 [1]. **A**

## Limits

No collectibles, no life insurance [4]. No margin borrowing: pledging the IRA as security is a prohibited transaction [3]. Futures and event contracts depend on custodian.

## New Jersey

No NJ deduction for IRA contributions; contributions are taxed by NJ in the year made and recovered tax-free on withdrawal [2]. **A**
