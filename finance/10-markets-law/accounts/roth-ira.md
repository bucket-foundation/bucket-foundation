## Key Numbers 2026

- Same $7,500 and $1,100 limits as traditional, shared [1]. **A**
- Phase-out: single $153,000 to $168,000; MFJ $242,000 to $252,000 [1]. **A**

## Limits

Same asset and prohibited-transaction rules as a traditional IRA [3][4]. Best home for high-turnover, high-gain strategies since qualified distributions escape federal tax.

## New Jersey

Qualified distributions excluded from NJ gross income; contributions not deductible [2]. **A**
