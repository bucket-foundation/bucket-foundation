# Solo 401k

ID: `X-solo401k`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

Owner with no employees, or owner and spouse. Owner contributes as employee and employer [7]. **A**

## Key Numbers 2026

- Elective deferral $24,500; catch-up $8,000 at 50; $11,250 at 60 to 63 [1]. **A**
- Employer nonelective up to 25% of plan compensation; self-employed use net SE earnings less half SE tax [7]. **A**
- Section 415(c) total $72,000; compensation cap $360,000 [8]. **B**
- Form 5500-EZ once assets reach $250,000 at year end [7]. **A**

## Use

Needs self-employment income. A trading business with trader status produces no earned income from gains, so funding comes from other self-employment [6]. Grade B inference.
