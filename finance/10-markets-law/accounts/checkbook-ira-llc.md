# Checkbook IRA LLC

ID: `X-checkbook`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

The IRA owns a new LLC and the owner manages it, writing checks without custodian approval per trade.

## Case Law

- Swanson v. Commissioner, 106 T.C. 76 (1996): investing in a newly formed entity with no owners is not a prohibited transaction. Relied on by the Tax Court in Ellis [9]. **A**
- Ellis v. Commissioner, 8th Cir. 2015: salary paid to the IRA owner by the IRA-owned LLC was a prohibited transaction; IRA disqualified for the year, over $320,000 taxed plus 10% [9]. **A**

## Rule

Owner may manage, never pay himself. All prohibited-transaction and UBTI rules of a self-directed IRA apply [3][5].
