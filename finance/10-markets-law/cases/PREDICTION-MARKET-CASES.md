## New Jersey

Kalshi keeps a preliminary injunction against New Jersey Division of Gaming Enforcement action under the 3rd Circuit ruling. It names Kalshi only. Other event contract venues in New Jersey carry state enforcement risk until the Supreme Court acts on No. 26-299. Grade B.
