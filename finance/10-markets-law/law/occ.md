## What It Is

Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, a Treasury bureau. Charters and supervises national banks, federal savings associations and federal branches of foreign banks; now also federal stablecoin issuers under GENIUS.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- 989 banks supervised per the OCC site. **A**
- Final GENIUS Act rule 2026-10-21 (NR 2026-92), effective 2027-01-18; covers reserves, redemption, risk management, custody, capital; BSA, AML and OFAC rules left to a joint Treasury rulemaking. **A**

## 2026 State

First agency with a final GENIUS rule. Application intake date to be announced separately.
