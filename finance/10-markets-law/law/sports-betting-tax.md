# Sports Betting Tax

ID: `L-bettax`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

Gambling winnings are ordinary income. Losses deduct only as an itemized deduction and only up to winnings.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- IRC 165(d) as amended by OBBBA (2025): wagering loss deduction equals 90% of losses, capped at gains; taxable years after 2025-12-31. **A**
- Professional gamblers: business expenses count as wagering losses, so the 90% cap reaches them too. **A**
- Repeal bills (Cortez Masto, Titus) introduced in 2025; no enactment found. **B**
- NJ Gross Income Tax: net gambling winnings taxable, floor at zero; 3% withholding on gambling payouts; lottery prizes $10,000 or less exempt. **A**

## 2026 State

First tax year under the 90% rule closes 2026-12-31. A bettor who breaks even on $100,000 of action owes federal tax on $10,000 of phantom income. The IRS has not addressed prediction market contracts as of 2026-06-16, so whether event contract gains fall inside 165(d) or under 1256 is unsettled. **B**
