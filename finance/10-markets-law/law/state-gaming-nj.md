# NJ DGE and State Gaming

ID: `L-stategaming`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

New Jersey Division of Gaming Enforcement enforces the Casino Control Act, licenses casino and online operators, tests internet gaming platforms and oversees sports wagering. Every legal state has its own commission; sportsbooks need a license per state.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- NJ online sports wagering tax on operators rose from 13% to 19.75% of gross revenue in the FY2026 budget (2025); online casino from 15%. **B**
- Third Circuit, Kalshi v. NJ DGE, 2026-04: 2-1 affirmance of a preliminary injunction; CEA preempts NJ gambling law for sports event contracts on a CFTC DCM. **B**
- A cert-stage extension filing appears on the Supreme Court docket 25A1465, 2026-07-22. **A**

## 2026 State

NJ cannot enforce gaming law against Kalshi while the injunction stands. Licensed NJ sportsbooks remain under DGE.
