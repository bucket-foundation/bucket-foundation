# PDT Rule Change

ID: `L-pdt`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

FINRA replaced the pattern day trader rules in Rule 4210 with intraday margin standards. The $25,000 minimum equity is gone.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- SEC approved SR-FINRA-2025-017 on 2026-04-14 (Release 34-105226). **A**
- Effective 2026-06-04; firms may phase in to 2027-10-20. **A**
- Firm computes an intraday margin deficit after margin-reducing trades; one calculation allowed. Deficit cleared by deposit or by end of a later day, outstanding no later than the 15th business day. **A**
- 90-day restriction for habitual failure past the fifth business day; deficits up to the lesser of 5% of equity or $1,000 excluded. **A**

## 2026 State

Small accounts can day trade on margin at brokers that have switched. Broker rollout varies until October 2027.
