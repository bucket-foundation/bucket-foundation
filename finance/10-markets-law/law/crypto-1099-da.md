# Crypto Tax and 1099-DA

ID: `L-1099da`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

Crypto is property. Each sale, swap or spend is a disposition with capital gain or loss. Custodial brokers report on Form 1099-DA.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- 2025 sales: gross proceeds reported, basis voluntary. **B**
- 2026 sales: gross proceeds required; basis required for covered assets, acquired after 2025 and held at the same broker. **A**
- De minimis: qualifying stablecoins optional method $10,000 per year; specified NFTs $600; payment processor sales $600. **A**
- Capital gains: 0/15/20% long-term; short-term at ordinary rates; $3,000 net loss against ordinary income. **A**

## 2026 State

First 1099-DA season (tax year 2025) produced blank-basis forms; keep own lot records. New Jersey Gross Income Tax follows the federal treatment of gain or loss from the sale or exchange of property, and the Division applies that to convertible virtual currency (TAM-2015-1(R), 2022-03-21). **A**
