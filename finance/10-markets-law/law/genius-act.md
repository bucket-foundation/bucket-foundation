# GENIUS Act

ID: `L-genius`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

Federal payment stablecoin law. Public Law 119-27, S. 1582, signed 2025-07-18. Permitted issuers hold one-to-one reserves and redeem at par.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Effective on the earlier of 2027-01-18 or 120 days after final rules. **A**
- OCC final rule 2026-10-21, effective 2027-01-18; dropped the affiliate-yield presumption and the insured-deposit reserve share. **A/B**
- FDIC and NCUA: proposals only found. Treasury state-regime similarity proposal, comments due 2026-06-02; no final found. **B**

## 2026 State

Seven weeks to effective date. OCC done; FDIC, NCUA, Treasury AML pending.
