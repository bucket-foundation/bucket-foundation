## What It Is

Financial Industry Regulatory Authority. Private self-regulatory organization for broker-dealers under SEC oversight, funded by member fees. Writes and enforces member rules, examines firms, runs trade reporting and arbitration, and administers licensing exams.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Rule 4210 intraday margin standards replaced the pattern day trader rules, effective 2026-06-04; see `pdt-intraday-margin.md`. **A**
- Hosts broker Rule 606 reports (cdn.finra.org/606). **A**

## 2026 State

The arbitration forum is where a NJ retail customer brings a claim against a broker.
