# Wash Sale

ID: `L-washsale`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

IRC 1091 disallows a loss when the taxpayer buys substantially identical stock or securities within 30 days before or after the sale. The disallowed loss adds to the replacement basis. <!-- voice-ignore-line -->

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Window: 30 days before and 30 days after. Covers contracts and options on stock or securities. **A**
- Crypto is property under Notice 2014-21, outside 1091. **A**
- H.R. 9172, introduced 2026-06-08 by Rep. Arrington, would extend 1091 to digital assets; PARITY Act and Lummis bills similar. Not enacted as of the latest reports found. **B**
- Form 1099-DA box 1i reports wash sale losses for tokenized securities treated as stock. **A**

## 2026 State

Crypto loss harvesting with instant rebuy stays legal for native tokens as of 2026-11-27. Tokenized stocks are caught.
