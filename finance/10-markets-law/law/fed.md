# Federal Reserve

ID: `L-fed`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

Central bank. Sets the policy rate, supervises bank holding companies and state member banks, and writes Regulation T, which sets initial margin for broker credit.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Kevin Warsh sworn in as Fed chair 2026-05-22, succeeding Jerome Powell. **A**
- Reg T initial margin: 50% for margin equity securities; 150% for short sales of nonexempted securities. **A**

## 2026 State

New chair, inflation running hot per May coverage. Reg T unchanged; FINRA moved day trading rules instead.
