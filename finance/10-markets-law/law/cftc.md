# CFTC

ID: `L-cftc`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

Commodity Futures Trading Commission. Regulates futures, options on futures, swaps and event contracts on DCMs such as Kalshi, CME and Polymarket US.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Michael Selig sworn in as 16th chairman 2025-12-22. **A**
- Selig sat as the sole commissioner as of mid-May 2026; House Agriculture leaders asked for nominees 2026-05-15. **B**
- CFTC sent Regulation Crypto Asset Transactions and Markets (RIN 3038-AF80) to OIRA 2026-09-17. **B**
- Final rule on event contract data reporting, Release 9330-26, 2026-11-23 (see `02-assets/prediction-markets.md`). **A**

## 2026 State

Single-member commission writing crypto and event contract rules and suing states that police prediction markets.
