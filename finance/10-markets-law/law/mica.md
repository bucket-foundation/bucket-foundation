# MiCA

ID: `L-mica`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

EU Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation. Licenses crypto-asset service providers (CASPs) and stablecoin issuers across the EU.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Transitional period ended 2026-07-01 EU-wide. **A**
- Unauthorised CASPs must wind down: no new EU clients, only exits to fiat, to an authorised platform or to self-custody. **A**
- Third-country firms serving EU clients need EU authorisation; reverse solicitation is narrow. **A**

## 2026 State

Relevant to a NJ resident only for venues that geo-split US and EU entities (Binance, Polymarket international, Hyperliquid front ends).
