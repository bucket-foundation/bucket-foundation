# Reg T Margin

ID: `L-regt`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

Federal Reserve Regulation T (12 CFR 220) limits broker credit for buying securities. FINRA Rule 4210 adds maintenance margin.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Initial margin on margin equity securities: 50% of market value or the exchange rate, whichever is greater. **A**
- Short sale of nonexempted securities: 150% (100% when a convertible is held). **A**
- Short sale of exempted or non-equity securities: 100% plus good-faith margin. **A**

## 2026 State

Unchanged in 2026. An IRA owner who uses the account as security for a loan is treated as receiving a distribution of the pledged portion under 26 U.S.C. 408(e)(4), so an IRA cannot back a margin loan. **A**
