# Section 1256

ID: `L-1256`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

IRC 1256 taxes regulated futures, foreign currency contracts, nonequity options (index options such as SPX), dealer equity options and dealer securities futures at 60% long-term, 40% short-term, marked to market at year end.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Open contracts treated as sold at fair market value on the last business day of the year. **A**
- 60/40 split regardless of holding period. **A**
- Excludes most securities futures, options on them, and swaps. **A**
- Section 1256 crypto futures (CME) report on 1099-B, not 1099-DA. **A**
- Event contracts on DCMs: a regulated futures contract must be marked to market and traded on or subject to the rules of a qualified board or exchange, and swaps are excluded. **A** The IRS has not addressed prediction market contracts as of 2026-06-16, and the CFTC calls them binary options that are swaps, which could trigger the 1256(b)(2)(B) exclusion. Treatment is unsettled. **B**

## 2026 State

Unchanged. CME micro bitcoin and ether futures get 60/40; spot crypto and ETFs do not.
