# CLARITY Act

ID: `L-clarity`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.6`

## What It Is

H.R. 3633, Digital Asset Market Clarity Act. Splits crypto oversight between SEC and CFTC and defines when a token is a digital commodity.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- House passed 294-134 on 2025-07-17. **A/B**
- Senate Banking Committee advanced 15-9 on 2026-05-14. **B**
- Senate cloture on motion to proceed failed 49-50 on 2026-09-15; Tillis voted no to keep a motion to reconsider. **B**
- Sticking points: ethics rules on officials' crypto holdings, stablecoin yield. **B**

## 2026 State

Stalled. Passage this Congress viewed as unlikely. Agencies proceed by rule: SEC Regulation Crypto Assets proposal, CFTC RIN 3038-AF80 at OIRA. No later Senate action found through 2026-11-27.
