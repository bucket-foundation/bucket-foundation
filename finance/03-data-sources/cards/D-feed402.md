## What It Is

feed402 is Gian's open protocol for selling data to AI agents over x402: the agent hits an endpoint, gets HTTP 402 with payment terms, pays in USDC on Base, and receives a citeable envelope (data, metadata, receipt). Tiers: raw, query, insight.
