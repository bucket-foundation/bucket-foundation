## What It Is

Regulatory filings as data: SEC EDGAR (submissions and XBRL facts), CFTC Commitments of Traders (weekly positions), FINRA TRACE (bond trades).

## Key Numbers and Dates

- data.sec.gov serves JSON APIs with no key; bulk ZIP republished nightly about 3:00 a.m. ET. **A**
- COT reports break down Tuesday open interest for markets with 20+ reportable traders; released Friday in a normal week. **A**
- TRACE historical and enhanced data are sold through FINRA data licensing. **A**
