## What It Is

Onchain data comes from blockchain nodes direct or from indexers: DefiLlama (TVL, DEX volume, stablecoins, free hosts plus Pro key), Dune (SQL over decoded chain tables, REST execution API), Glassnode and Artemis (paid metrics).

## Key Numbers and Dates

- DefiLlama free hosts need no key and are rate limited; Pro uses pro-api.llama.fi with key. **A**
- DefiLlama stablecoin supply on 2026-11-27: about $283.6B total; USDT $173.5B, USDC $69.1B, USDe $4.9B. **B**
- Dune API exposes execute SQL, execute query, status and result endpoints. **A**
