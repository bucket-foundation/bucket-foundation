# Long-Run Return Datasets

ID: `D-longrun`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.1`

## What It Is

Datasets that run returns over a century or more: Jorda-Schularick-Taylor Macrohistory, Shiller's US stock data, and the Dimson-Marsh-Staunton yearbook (paid, via UBS).

## Key Numbers and Dates

- JST Macrohistory Release 6 covers 18 advanced economies from 1870, annual, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. **A**
- Jorda et al., The Rate of Return on Everything, 1870-2015, QJE 134(3):1225-1298, 2019. **A**
- Shiller data: monthly S&P price, dividends, earnings, rates and CAPE since 1871. **A**
- UBS Global Investment Returns Yearbook 2026, by Marsh, Staunton and Dimson: annual returns on stocks, bonds, bills, inflation, currencies and gold for 35 markets and five composite indices since 1900. **A**
