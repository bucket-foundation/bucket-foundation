## What It Is

Research-grade price history: CRSP for US stocks with delisting returns, Ken French library for factors and sorted portfolios, exchange feeds and vendors for intraday. See `03-data-sources/DATA-SOURCES.md` for vendor rows.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- CRSP US Stock Databases now sold under Morningstar Indexes branding. **A**
- CRSP discontinued legacy FIZ format files; French library notes the change and a December 2023 methods paper. **A**
- CRSP stocks history from 1925 per DATA-SOURCES row 'Stocks 1923'; start date differs by product. Check before citing. **C**
