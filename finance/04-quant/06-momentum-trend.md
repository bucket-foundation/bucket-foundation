# Momentum and Trend

Cross-sectional momentum buys past 3 to 12 month winners and sells losers. Time-series momentum goes long assets with positive past return and short negative. Both show long samples across asset classes and both crash on sharp reversals.

## Papers

- Jegadeesh and Titman 1993, Returns to Buying Winners and Selling Losers, JF. doi:10.1111/j.1540-6261.1993.tb04702.x
- Moskowitz, Ooi, Pedersen 2012, Time series momentum, JFE (SSRN 2089463).
- Hurst, Ooi, Pedersen 2017, A Century of Evidence on Trend-Following Investing, SSRN 2993026.
- Asness, Moskowitz, Pedersen 2013, Value and Momentum Everywhere, JF (SSRN 2174501).
- Jiang, Kelly, Xiu 2023, (Re-)Imag(in)ing Price Trends, JF (SSRN 3756587). CNN on price charts.
