# Kelly and Position Sizing

Kelly maximizes expected log wealth. For a continuous strategy f = mu / sigma^2; for many strategies F = C^-1 M. Estimation error and fat tails make full Kelly too aggressive; practitioners run half Kelly or less and treat Kelly as a ceiling.
