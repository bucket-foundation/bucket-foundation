# Factor Models

Expected return equals exposure to priced risk factors plus alpha. Market, size, value, profitability, investment and momentum carry most of the published evidence. Hundreds of other factors fail replication.

## Papers

- Fama and French 1993, Common risk factors in the returns on stocks and bonds, JFE. doi:10.1016/0304-405x(93)90023-5
- Carhart 1997, On Persistence in Mutual Fund Performance, JF. doi:10.1111/j.1540-6261.1997.tb03808.x
- Fama and French 2015, A five-factor asset pricing model, JFE (SSRN 2287202, doi:10.2139/ssrn.2287202).
- Hou, Xue, Zhang 2020, Replicating Anomalies, RFS. doi:10.1093/rfs/hhy131. Most of 452 anomalies fail with microcaps controlled.
- McLean and Pontiff 2016, Does Academic Research Destroy Stock Return Predictability?, JF (SSRN 2080900).
- Gu, Kelly, Xiu 2020, Empirical Asset Pricing via Machine Learning, RFS (NBER w25398).
