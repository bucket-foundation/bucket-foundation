## What It Is

A dollar today is worth more than a dollar later. Price equals the sum of cash flows discounted at the yield. NPV and IRR follow. Macaulay duration is the present-value-weighted average time to cash flows; modified duration is price sensitivity to yield.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Macaulay, Some Theoretical Problems Suggested by the Movements of Interest Rates, Bond Yields and Stock Prices in the United States since 1856, NBER, 1938. Introduced duration. **A**
- US Treasury fits its daily par yield curve with a published methodology (monotone convex since December 2021; method date from memory). **C**
- 10-year Treasury 4.91% and 30-year above 5% on 2026-11-24 per H.15. **A**

## 2026 State

Long yields near 5% raise discount rates for every long-duration asset.
