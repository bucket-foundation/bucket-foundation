# Market Microstructure

ID: `Q-micro`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.1`

## What It Is

Microstructure studies how trading sets prices: bid-ask spreads, order flow, inventory and information. Kyle models an informed trader hiding in noise flow, with price impact lambda. Glosten-Milgrom derives the spread from adverse selection.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Kyle, Continuous Auctions and Insider Trading, Econometrica 53(6):1315, November 1985. **A**
- Glosten and Milgrom, Bid, ask and transaction prices in a specialist market with heterogeneously informed traders, JFE 14(1):71-100, March 1985. **A**
- SEC 2024-137 adds a $0.005 tick for qualifying NMS stocks, compliance November 2025. **A**
