## What It Is

Risk measures summarize loss: volatility, value at risk (VaR), expected shortfall (ES), drawdown, liquidity horizon. Returns have fat tails, so normal-based VaR understates extremes. Basel's market risk framework moved banks from VaR to ES.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Mandelbrot, The Variation of Certain Speculative Prices, Journal of Business 36(4):394, 1963. Fat tails in cotton prices. **A**
- Basel Committee, Minimum capital requirements for market risk (FRTB), January 2019, replacing the January 2016 version. Uses expected shortfall at 97.5%. **A**
- US implementation of Basel III endgame including FRTB: status not checked in this pass. **C**
