# Mean Reversion

Prices overshoot and partially revert over days (short-term reversal) and years (long-horizon reversal). Trade it with an OU model: z-score entry, half-life for lookback and holding period. The payoff profile resembles short volatility.

## Papers

- Poterba and Summers 1988, Mean Reversion in Stock Prices, JFE (NBER w2343).
- Lo and MacKinlay 1988, variance-ratio tests (NBER w2168).
- Uhlenbeck and Ornstein 1930. doi:10.1103/physrev.36.823
- Chan, Quantitative Trading (Wiley 2009) and Machine Trading (Wiley 2016).
