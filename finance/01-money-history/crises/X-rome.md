# X-rome: Roman Debasement and the Third-Century Crisis

Event card, 64 to 300 CE. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
Rome cut the silver in its coins for two centuries. The denarius went from about 94% silver under Nero to about 5% by 300 CE, and prices rose with it.

## Causes and Mechanics
Emperors raised army pay and paid it in coins struck from less silver. Good coin was hoarded, bad coin circulated (Gresham), and prices rose. From 235 to 284 civil war and invasions raised spending while tax receipts fell, so the mint carried the gap.

## Systematic Trader View
Measure the money by its metal. Each cut in fineness was a public fact once assayed, and the cut led prices. A trader would have held metal and goods against coin, the same trade as gold against fiat after 1971.
