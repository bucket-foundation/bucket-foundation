# X-1973-oil: 1973 Oil Shock and Bear Market

Event card, 1973 to 1974. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
OAPEC embargoed oil from 19 October 1973. Oil went from $2.90 a barrel to $11.65 by January 1974, inflation surged, and US stocks fell about half from January 1973 to late 1974.

## Causes and Mechanics
US producers had no spare capacity to replace lost barrels. The cost shock hit output and prices at once; policy could not offset both, as Bernanke later put it. Valuations compressed as rates and inflation rose.

## Lab Data
Shiller S&P composite: peak 118.40 in Jan 1973, trough 67.07 in Dec 1974, down 43.4% nominal and 53.5% real. CAPE 18.7 at the peak. 10y minus 3m spread inverted Jun 1973. CPI inflation 12.1% in Dec 1974. Grade A, lab.

## Systematic Trader View
The yield curve inverted in June 1973, four months before the embargo. Real losses beat nominal ones by ten points, so inflation-adjusted series are the right lens. Commodity exposure was the hedge.
