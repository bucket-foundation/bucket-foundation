# X-1792: Panic of 1792

Event card, March to April 1792. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
William Duer's leveraged bet on US government debt and bank stock failed in March 1792. Securities fell more than 20% in weeks, and Treasury Secretary Hamilton bought debt in the market to stop the slide.

## Causes and Mechanics
Duer borrowed short from banks and individuals to corner government securities. When credit tightened he stopped paying, his creditors sold, and prices fell. Hamilton used the Sinking Fund to buy securities, the first US lender-of-last-resort action.

## Systematic Trader View
A single leveraged holder controlled a thin market. The trader's signal was Duer's default; the bottom came with public buying. Hamilton's rule, lend and buy freely against good securities, recurs in 1907, 1987, 2008 and 2020.
