# X-2000-dotcom: Dot-com Bust

Event card, 2000 to 2002. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
Technology stocks peaked in March 2000. The Nasdaq-100 tracker QQQ fell 83% to October 2002 and the S&P 500 fell about half. The Shiller CAPE peaked at 44.2 in December 1999, the highest in its record.

## Causes and Mechanics
Internet firms raised equity on revenue growth with little profit, and valuations priced decades of growth. The Fed raised the funds rate to 6.5% in 2000 and the curve inverted in August 2000. Capital spending on telecom and IT collapsed and profits fell.

## Systematic Trader View
Valuation was extreme for two years before the top, so CAPE alone gave no timing. The curve inversion and the Fed hikes of 2000 did. Trend rules caught most of the 83% Nasdaq decline. Equal-weight and value stocks fell far less than the cap-weighted tech index.
