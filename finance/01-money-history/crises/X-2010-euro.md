# X-2010-euro: Eurozone Debt Crisis

Event card, 2010 to 2012. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
Greece approved an adjustment programme on 2 May 2010 as bond markets shut it out. Spreads widened across Ireland, Portugal, Spain and Italy until Mario Draghi said on 26 July 2012 that the ECB would do "whatever it takes to preserve the euro."

## Causes and Mechanics
Banks held their own governments' bonds and governments stood behind their banks, so losses ran both ways. Member states shared a currency without a lender of last resort for sovereign debt; the ECB's pledge supplied one, and spreads fell without the ECB buying under OMT.

## Systematic Trader View
A credible backstop ends a sovereign spread crisis faster than fiscal plans do. Long periphery bonds after 26 July 2012 was the trade. The sovereign-bank loop is the pattern to watch whenever banks hold large home-government bond books.
