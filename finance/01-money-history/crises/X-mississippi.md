# X-mississippi: Mississippi Bubble

Event card, 1719 to 1720. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
John Law's Mississippi Company swapped French state debt for shares while his Banque Royale printed notes to buy them. Shares rose from 500 livres to 10,000 and fell back to 500.

## Causes and Mechanics
Holders of state debt paid for shares with that debt at par. The bank lent new notes against shares, so rising prices created money that bought more shares. When note holders turned to gold and goods, inflation hit 23% a month and the price support failed.

## Lab Data
No lab series. Share path above: 95% drawdown from peak to Sep 1721.

## Systematic Trader View
Money printed to hold up an asset is the signal. Once the central bank and the issuer merged (Feb 1720), the share price and the currency became one bet, and inflation at 23% a month was the exit sign.
