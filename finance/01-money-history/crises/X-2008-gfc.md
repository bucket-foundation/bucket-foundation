# X-2008-gfc: Global Financial Crisis

Event card, 2007 to 2009. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
US house prices fell from mid-2006. Mortgage losses hit banks and dealers funded short-term; Lehman Brothers filed on 15 September 2008. TARP authorized $700 billion on 3 October 2008. The S&P 500 fell 57% to March 2009. See [M-2008](../eras/M-2008.md).

## Causes and Mechanics
Subprime loans were packaged into securities rated AAA and funded in repo and commercial paper. Falling house prices cut collateral values, lenders demanded more margin, and fire sales spread losses. Response: Fed facilities, rates near zero, QE and bank capital through TARP.

## Systematic Trader View
House prices topped 15 months before stocks and the curve inverted 14 months before them. Credit spreads and bank stocks broke in 2007. A 10-month moving-average rule exited the S&P in late 2007 or early 2008 and re-entered in mid-2009. Leverage at the funding layer (repo) turned a housing loss into a system loss.
