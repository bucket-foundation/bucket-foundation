# X-1837: Panic of 1837

Event card, 1837 to 1844. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
New York banks suspended gold and silver payments on 10 May 1837 after a land and cotton boom. Over 40% of US banks failed and a deflationary depression lasted about seven years.

## Causes and Mechanics
State banks lent freely on land and cotton. The 1836 Specie Circular required gold or silver for public land, drawing coin west, and British tightening cut credit. Cotton fell, reserves ran out and banks suspended convertibility.
