# X-2023-svb: Silicon Valley Bank and Credit Suisse

Event card, March 2023. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
SVB lost over $40 billion of deposits on 9 March 2023 and was closed on 10 March. Signature Bank failed two days later. On 19 March FINMA approved UBS's takeover of Credit Suisse and wrote off about CHF 16 billion of AT1 bonds.

## Causes and Mechanics
SVB held long bonds bought at low rates, funded by uninsured deposits from a concentrated client base. Rate hikes cut the bonds' value; clients coordinated withdrawals online. Credit Suisse had lost confidence over years; the state-brokered merger wiped AT1 holders ahead of shareholders.

## Systematic Trader View
The loss sat in public data: long-bond prices fell by half and bank call reports showed securities and uninsured deposit shares. A screen on unrealized losses against capital and uninsured deposits flagged SVB. Small-cap and regional bank exposure fell first.
