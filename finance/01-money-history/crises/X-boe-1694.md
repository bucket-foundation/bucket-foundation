# X-boe-1694: Bank of England Founding

Event card, 1694. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
Private subscribers lent £1.2 million to William III for the war with France and received a charter as the Bank of England. The Crown's debt became the bank's asset and the base for its notes.

## Causes and Mechanics
England lost the naval battle of Beachy Head in 1690 and could not borrow £1.5 million for a fleet. The Tonnage Act paid lenders 8% from new duties plus a £4,000 yearly fee. Note issue backed by state debt joined public credit to private banking, the template for central banks.

## Systematic Trader View
A monetary break: war finance created the lender of last resort. For a trader, the yield on state debt at 8% set the hurdle rate that every 1690s joint-stock venture had to beat.
