# X-1929: 1929 Crash and the Great Depression

Event card, 1929 to 1933. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
The Dow peaked at 381.17 on 3 Sep 1929, fell about 25% on 28 and 29 October, and bottomed at 41.22 in July 1932, 89% lower. Bank runs cut the money supply by nearly 30% and the banking system collapsed in March 1933.

## Causes and Mechanics
Stocks were bought on margin at about 10% down while the Fed tightened. After the crash, waves of bank failures shrank the money stock and prices fell about 30%. The Fed's 1931 and 1932 expansions were too small or reversed. The gold standard tied policy until the 1933 bank holiday and devaluation.

## Lab Data
Shiller S&P composite: peak 31.30 in Sep 1929, trough 4.77 in Jun 1932, down 84.8% nominal and 80.6% real. CAPE 32.6 at the peak against a 1871 to 2024 median of 16.5. JST: 8 country crises start in 1931. Grade A, lab.

## Systematic Trader View
CAPE at twice its median plus a 6% discount rate gave the setup. The second leg (1930 to 1932) did the damage, driven by bank failures and a falling money stock. A trend rule (price under its 10-month average) would have been out by late 1929 and stayed out through most of the decline.
