# X-1987: Black Monday

Event card, 19 October 1987. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
The Dow fell 22.6% on 19 October 1987, the largest one-day fall on record. The Fed affirmed it would supply liquidity the next morning and the market regained over half the loss within two sessions.

## Causes and Mechanics
Portfolio insurance sold futures as prices fell, which pushed prices lower and triggered more selling. Stocks settled in three days while futures settled in one, forcing liquidations. Reforms added circuit breakers.

## Systematic Trader View
A 44% rally in seven months with 10-year yields rising toward 9.4% set up the fall. The crash came from mechanical selling, so the economy held and the drawdown recovered in under two years. A rule that buys sharp mechanical drops when the central bank commits liquidity paid here, in 1998 and in 2020.
