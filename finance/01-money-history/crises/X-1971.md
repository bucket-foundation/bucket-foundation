# X-1971: Nixon Shock

Event card, 15 August 1971. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
Nixon closed the gold window on 15 August 1971, with a 90-day wage and price freeze and a 10% import surcharge. The dollar became fiat and major currencies floated by March 1973.

## Causes and Mechanics
Foreign-held dollars exceeded US gold reserves, which invited a run on gold. Closing convertibility removed the anchor; the Smithsonian agreement patched parities for a short time.

## Lab Data
FRED CPIAUCSL: CPI inflation reached 12.1% by Dec 1974 and 14.6% by Mar 1980. 10y minus 3m Treasury spread inverted in Jun 1973, Aug 1974, Dec 1978 and Nov 1980. Grade A, lab.

## Systematic Trader View
The break started the long inflation trade: gold, commodities and short-duration assets over bonds through 1980. The leading data point was the gold cover ratio, published by the Treasury.
