# X-1907: Panic of 1907

Event card, October 1907. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
A failed corner of United Copper exposed New York trust companies. Knickerbocker Trust suspended, call money hit 100%, and J.P. Morgan organized private rescues. The panic led to the Federal Reserve Act of 1913.

## Causes and Mechanics
Trusts held about 5% cash reserves against 25% at national banks and lent their deposits overnight to stock buyers. Runs on trusts pulled that call money, rates spiked and stocks fell.

## Lab Data
Shiller S&P composite: peak 10.03 in Sep 1906, trough 6.25 in Nov 1907, down 37.7% nominal, 40.3% real. CAPE 19.2 at the peak. JST counts 5 country crises starting 1907. Grade A, lab.

## Systematic Trader View
Stocks topped 13 months before the panic. The tell was funding: lightly reserved lenders funding equities overnight. Call-money rates were the price of liquidity, and their jump from 9.5% to 100% marked the climax.
