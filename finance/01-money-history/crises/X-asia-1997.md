# X-asia-1997: Asian Financial Crisis

Event card, 1997 to 1998. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
Thailand devalued the baht on 2 July 1997. The crisis spread to Indonesia and Korea, which needed $118 billion of international support. US banks agreed on 24 December 1997 to roll over loans to Korean banks.

## Causes and Mechanics
A decade of growth above 5% hid fast credit growth, weak supervision and current-account deficits. Firms borrowed short-term in dollars behind currency pegs; when the pegs broke, their debts grew in local terms and runs followed.

## Lab Data
Yahoo SPY daily: 27 Oct 1997 down 7.25%, the 8th worst SPY day since 1993. Grade A, lab.

## Systematic Trader View
Short-term foreign-currency debt against reserves was the tell, plus a current-account deficit under a peg. The US equity hit lasted days; the local equity and currency losses lasted a year. Dollar strength is the transmission channel.
