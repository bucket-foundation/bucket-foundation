## What Happened
An energy shock in March 2026 tied to war with Iran sent Brent above $100 and global equities to their worst month since 2022. Private credit defaults hit a record 5.8%. Bitcoin fell 53% from October 2025 to June 2026. In October 2026 the IMF named an AI-asset correction and a sovereign yield spike as near-term risks. The S&P 500 fell 11.5% from 6 October to 20 November.

## Causes and Mechanics
Oil supply risk lifted inflation while yields were already high. The 10-year at 5.13% in October 2026 sits above the 3-month bill, so the curve is positive again after the longest inversion on record. Leverage sits in private credit and crypto perpetuals.
