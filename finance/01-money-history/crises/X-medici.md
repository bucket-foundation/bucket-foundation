# X-medici: Medici Bank

Event card, 1397 to 1494. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
The Medici Bank grew from 10,000 florins in 1397 into the largest bank in Europe, banker to the Papacy. It shrank through bad loans to princes and closed when the Medici were expelled from Florence in 1494.

## Causes and Mechanics
Deposits ran many times capital and reserves were thin. Branch managers lent to rulers, the English crown among them, who could default at will. Losses in London and Bruges, gold losing value against silver from 1475 to 1485, and family withdrawals drained capital before the political end.

## Systematic Trader View
Concentration was the tell: one client, the Papacy, supplied most profit and most deposits, and sovereign borrowers paid when they chose. A creditor would have watched loans to rulers against capital.
