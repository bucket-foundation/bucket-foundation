# X-2025: Market Stress in 2025

Event card, 2025. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
On 2 April 2025 US tariffs set off an equity crash and a Treasury selloff at once. A 90-day pause on 9 April sent the S&P 500 up 9.5% in a day. On 10 October 2025 a tariff threat triggered more than $19 billion of crypto liquidations. In September Tricolor and First Brands failed, raising questions on private credit.

## Causes and Mechanics
April: tariff risk hit growth and the dollar's safe-asset role together, so bonds failed to hedge. October: perpetual futures run at 20 to 50 times leverage with venue-local collateral pricing; when depth vanished, auto-deleveraging closed hedged shorts too.

## Systematic Trader View
