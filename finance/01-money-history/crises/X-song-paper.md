# X-song-paper: Song Paper Money

Event card, 1023 to 1279. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
The Song state took over private *jiaozi* notes in 1023, the first government paper money. Later *huizi* notes were issued in rising amounts to fund wars with the Jin and the Mongols, and their value collapsed.

## Causes and Mechanics
Notes started as receipts against deposited coin with fixed series and a two-year expiry. War spending broke the limits; issue rose without new copper backing, people hoarded coin, and forgery spread.

No lab series. The issue count is the signal: circulation rose eightfold from the early 1200s to 1231.

## Systematic Trader View
Note issue grew faster than the copper and the economy behind it. The discount of notes to coin was the market price of that gap. Watch issuance against reserves; the Jiaozi page is flagged as needing citations, so treat its numbers as grade B at best.
