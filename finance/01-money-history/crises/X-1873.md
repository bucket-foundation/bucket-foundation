# X-1873: Panic of 1873

Event card, 1873 to 1879. Grades: A primary or lab-computed, B secondary, C snippet or single unchecked source.

## What Happened
The Vienna exchange crashed in May 1873 and Jay Cooke & Co. failed on 18 September 1873 over Northern Pacific bonds. The NYSE closed for ten days and a long depression followed.

## Causes and Mechanics
Railroads were overbuilt on bond finance. After Vienna, European capital pulled back and new bonds could not be sold. Cooke's failure set off runs; New York bank reserves fell from $50 million to $17 million.

## Systematic Trader View
Stocks peaked in April 1872, 17 months before Cooke failed. A foreign crash (Vienna, May) arrived four months before the US panic. Capital-spending booms financed by bonds end when the bond buyers leave.
