# Money Timeline

As of 2026-11-27. Grades: A primary, B secondary, C single source pending check. Book research = `~/Documents/Historical Ledger Evolution From Clay to Stablecoins.pdf` and `Your Money Is Broken` draft 1, the founder's own prior research.

## Credit Before Coins
c. 3000 BCE. Sumerian temple scribes record grain and silver debts on clay tablets; the shekel starts as a weight of barley and silver. Credit ledgers precede coin. Grade B. Sources: David Graeber, *Debt: The First 5,000 Years* (2011), ch. 3; book research, *Historical Ledger Evolution*.
Card: [M-ledger](eras/M-ledger.md).

## Commodity Money
Cattle, grain, cowrie shells, salt and weighed metal serve as money where they are scarce, divisible and accepted in trade. Cowries circulated from China to West Africa for over two millennia. Grade B. Source: Glyn Davies, *A History of Money* (3rd ed., 2002).
Card: [M-commodity](eras/M-commodity.md).

## Coinage
c. 630 BCE. Lydia stamps electrum lumps, the first state-guaranteed coins. Rome later debases the denarius from near-pure silver under Augustus to under 5% by the 270s CE. Grade B. Sources: British Museum, Lydian electrum collection notes; Davies 2002.
Card: [M-coin](eras/M-coin.md).

## Paper Money and Banking
11th century: Song China issues *jiaozi* paper notes. 1400s: Medici bank uses bills of exchange across Europe. 1600s: London goldsmith receipts become bank notes and fractional reserve lending. Grade B. Sources: Davies 2002; Raymond de Roover, *The Rise and Decline of the Medici Bank* (1963).
Card: [M-bank](eras/M-bank.md).

## Central Banks
1668 Sveriges Riksbank. 1694 Bank of England, founded to lend to the Crown. 1913 Federal Reserve Act after the Panic of 1907. Grade A. Sources: riksbank.se history; bankofengland.co.uk history; Federal Reserve Act, 38 Stat. 251.
Card: [M-cb](eras/M-cb.md).

## Gold Standard
Britain adopts gold de facto in 1717 and by law in 1821; most industrial economies join by the 1870s. Suspended in 1914, partially restored in the 1920s, abandoned by Britain in 1931 and by the US for domestic holders in 1933 (Executive Order 6102). Grade A/B. Sources: Barry Eichengreen, *Golden Fetters* (1992); EO 6102.
Card: [M-gold](eras/M-gold.md).

## Bretton Woods
July 1944. 44 nations peg to the dollar, the dollar pegs to gold at $35 an ounce; IMF and World Bank are created; IMF Articles signed 22 July 1944. Grade A. Source: Articles of Agreement of the IMF, 1944.
Card: [M-bw](eras/M-bw.md).

## Fiat Era
15 August 1971. Nixon closes the gold window. By March 1973 major currencies float. US CPI-U rises from 40.5 in 1971 to 304.7 in 2023 and 313.7 in 2024 (BLS CPI-U, 1982-84=100). Grade A. Sources: Nixon address, 15 Aug 1971; BLS CPI-U series CUUR0000SA0.
Card: [M-fiat](eras/M-fiat.md).

## Eurodollar
1950s onward. Dollar deposits held in banks outside the US, beyond Fed reserve rules. The market funds global trade and carry and becomes the channel the Fed backstops with swap lines in 2008 and 2020. Grade B. Sources: BIS Quarterly Review, "The global dollar system" (2022); book research, *Geopolitical and Macroeconomic Impacts*.
Card: [M-euro](eras/M-euro.md).

## 2008 MBS Crisis
Subprime mortgages packaged into MBS and CDOs, rated AAA, funded through repo and commercial paper. House prices fall from 2006; Bear Stearns fails March 2008, Lehman files 15 Sept 2008. TARP authorizes $700B. Case-Shiller national index falls about 27% peak to trough. Grade A/B. Sources: Financial Crisis Inquiry Commission Report (2011); S&P CoreLogic Case-Shiller US National Index (FRED CSUSHPINSA).
Card: [M-2008](eras/M-2008.md).

## Bitcoin and Crypto
31 Oct 2008 white paper; genesis block 3 Jan 2009. Ethereum launches 2015, enabling programmable tokens. US spot bitcoin ETFs approved 10 Jan 2024. Grade A. Sources: Nakamoto, "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System"; SEC approval order 34-99306.
Card: [M-crypto](eras/M-crypto.md).

## Stablecoins
2014 Tether launches. 2018 USDC launches. May 2022 TerraUSD collapses: price $0.409 by 12 May and market cap down about 95%. March 2023 USDC breaks peg to $0.87 for a weekend when $3.3B of reserves are stuck at Silicon Valley Bank. Total supply reaches about $293B in May 2026, then falls to just under $280B by 9 Nov 2026 after a $14.2B 30-day contraction (Glassnode). Grade B. Sources: [FRBNY Staff Report 1073](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr1073.pdf); book draft; Circle statement 11 Mar 2023; [coinotag/Glassnode, 2026-11-11](https://en.coinotag.com/bitcoin-30-day-stablecoin-drain-14-2b-glassnode); [dextools, July 2026](https://www.dextools.io/news/genius-act-deadline-missed-stablecoin-market-onchain-july-2026).
Card: [M-stable](eras/M-stable.md).

## GENIUS Act
Signed 18 July 2025 as P.L. 119-27, the first US federal law for payment stablecoins: 1:1 reserves in cash, deposits and short Treasuries, monthly attestations, issuers licensed federally or by qualifying states, no interest paid by issuers to holders. Final rules were due 18 July 2026; regulators missed the date. The OCC finalized its framework in November 2026, with license applications opening after OMB form approval and a filing window before 18 Jan 2027. Grade A/B. Sources: [CRS R49351, 2026-09-17](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/R49351.html); [Gibson Dunn, 2026-11-09](https://www.gibsondunn.com/the-genius-act-occ-finalizes-its-payment-stablecoin-framework/); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/genius-act-missed-deadline-regulators-writing-rules/).
Card: [M-stable](eras/M-stable.md).

## CBDCs
China's e-CNY in pilot since 2020. ECB in preparation phase for a digital euro since Nov 2023, with issue possible during 2029. US Executive Order 14178 (23 Jan 2025) bars federal agencies from creating a CBDC. Atlantic Council tracker counts 146 countries and currency unions exploring a CBDC as of May 2026, three launched. Grade A/B. Sources: EO 14178; ECB digital euro page; Atlantic Council CBDC Tracker.
