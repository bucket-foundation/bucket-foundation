# C-18: Eighteenth Century

Century overview, 1700 to 1799.

## Money and Markets
John Law and the South Sea Company swap state debt for shares in 1719 and 1720; both bubbles collapse. Britain adopts gold de facto in 1717. The young United States has its first panic in 1792 and Hamilton buys debt to stop it.

## Recurring Pattern
Debt-for-equity swaps whose promoters profit from their own share price; money printed to hold up an asset.
