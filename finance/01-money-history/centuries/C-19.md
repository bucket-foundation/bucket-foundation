# C-19: Nineteenth Century

Century overview, 1800 to 1899.

## Money and Markets
Gold standard spreads; US banking runs without a central bank. Panics in 1837 and 1873 follow land, cotton and railroad booms. JST records 36 systemic banking crises in 18 countries from 1870 to 1913.

## Recurring Pattern
Commodity or capital-spending boom on bank credit, then a reserve drain at the money center, then suspension and deflation.
