# C-ancient: Ancient World

Century overview, to 500 CE.

## Money and Markets
Money starts as credit on temple ledgers, then becomes weighed metal and, from Lydia around 630 BCE, stamped coin. Kings reset debts by decree. Rome finances its army by cutting the silver in its coins until the denarius is about 5% silver by 300 CE.

## Recurring Pattern
Debt grows faster than the harvest that services it; rulers cancel it. States short of revenue debase the coin; prices follow the metal.
