# C-20: Twentieth Century

Century overview, 1900 to 1999.

## Money and Markets
1907 leads to the Fed. 1929 to 1933 is the worst equity and banking collapse in the data. Bretton Woods pegs to gold until 1971; fiat follows, then the Great Inflation, Volcker, and a run of crises in currencies and leverage: 1987, Japan, the ERM, Asia, Russia and LTCM. JST counts 27 crises from 1914 to 1945, none from 1946 to 1972, and 25 from 1973 to 2020.

## Recurring Pattern
Funding runs on lightly reserved lenders; pegs that need rates the economy cannot bear; leverage on spreads that widen together.
