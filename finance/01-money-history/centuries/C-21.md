# C-21: Twenty-First Century

Century overview, 2000 to date.

## Money and Markets
Dot-com bust, then the 2008 mortgage and funding crisis, the eurozone sovereign-bank loop, China in 2015, the COVID dash for cash, meme stocks, the crypto failures of 2022, SVB in 2023, the 2025 tariff shock and the 2026 energy shock with a crypto bear. Central banks now backstop dealers, foreign dollar borrowers and stablecoin reserves in crises.

## Recurring Pattern
Duration and liquidity mismatch outside regulated banks; dash-for-cash episodes where every asset but cash falls.
