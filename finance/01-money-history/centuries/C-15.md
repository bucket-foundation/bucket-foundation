# C-15: Fifteenth Century

Century overview, 1400 to 1499.

## Money and Markets
The Medici Bank becomes the largest in Europe on papal deposits and loans to princes, then shrinks through sovereign defaults and closes in 1494.

## Recurring Pattern
Lending to rulers who can refuse to pay, with thin reserves and one dominant client.
