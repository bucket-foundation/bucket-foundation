# C-medieval: Medieval Period

Century overview, 500 to 1400.

## Money and Markets
Song China issues the first state paper money in 1024 and overissues it to fund wars; notes lose most of their value by the 1200s. In Europe, Italian merchant banks build bills of exchange across trade fairs.

## Recurring Pattern
Paper without a limit on issue loses value in proportion to war spending.
