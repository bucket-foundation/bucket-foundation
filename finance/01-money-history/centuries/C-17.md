# C-17: Seventeenth Century

Century overview, 1600 to 1699.

## Money and Markets
Tulip contracts boom and bust in 1637. Sweden's Riksbank opens in 1668 and the Bank of England in 1694 to lend to the Crown.

## Recurring Pattern
Unmargined forwards and the birth of central banking out of war finance.
