# M-cbdc: Central Bank Digital Currencies

Layer 0 node `M-cbdc`. Grades: A primary, B secondary, C single source or unchecked.

## What Happened
A CBDC is digital money that is a direct liability of the central bank. China runs the largest pilot. The ECB is preparing a digital euro for possible issue in 2029. The US went the other way: the Fed said in 2022 it would not issue one without law from Congress, and EO 14178 of January 2025 bars federal agencies from creating one.
