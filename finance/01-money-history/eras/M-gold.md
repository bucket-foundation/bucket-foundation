# M-gold: Gold Standard

Layer 0 node `M-gold`. Grades: A primary, B secondary, C single source or unchecked.

## What Happened
Currencies were defined as weights of gold and redeemable on demand. The US held a de facto gold standard from the 1830s and a legal one from 1900 at $20.67 an ounce. In 1933 Executive Order 6102 required private holders to deliver gold to the Federal Reserve, and the Gold Reserve Act of 1934 revalued gold to $35.
