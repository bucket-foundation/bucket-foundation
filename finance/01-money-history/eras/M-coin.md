# M-coin: Coinage

Layer 0 node `M-coin`. Grades: A primary, B secondary, C single source or unchecked.

## What Happened
Lydia in western Anatolia struck the first coins, stamped lumps of electrum and later gold and silver under Croesus. A stamp turned weighed metal into a counted, state-guaranteed unit.
