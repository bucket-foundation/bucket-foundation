# M-inflation: Inflation and Purchasing Power

Layer 0 node `M-inflation`. Grades: A primary, B secondary, C single source or unchecked.

## What Happened
Under fiat money the price level rises every year the central bank targets positive inflation. US CPI-U was 40.5 in 1971, the year the gold window closed, and 313.7 in 2024. A 1971 dollar buys about 13% of what it did. Coin debasement did the same thing before paper.
