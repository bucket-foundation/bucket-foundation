# M-euro: Eurodollar

Layer 0 node `M-euro`. Grades: A primary, B secondary, C single source or unchecked.

## What Happened
Banks outside the US take dollar deposits and make dollar loans free of US bank rules. The market grew from about $20 billion in 1964 to trillions, and dollar debt hidden in FX swaps now rivals on-balance-sheet debt. In 2008 and 2020 Fed swap lines sent dollars to non-US banks offshore.
