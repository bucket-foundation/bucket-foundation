# M-cb: Central Banking

Layer 0 node `M-cb`. Grades: A primary, B secondary, C single source or unchecked.

## What Happened
States chartered banks to manage the currency and lend to the government. Sweden's parliament founded the Riksbank after Stockholms Banco failed. The Bank of England began as the government's banker. The US created the Federal Reserve in 1913.
