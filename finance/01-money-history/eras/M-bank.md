# M-bank: Banking and Bills of Exchange

Layer 0 node `M-bank`. Grades: A primary, B secondary, C single source or unchecked.

## What Happened
Deposit banks turned receipts for coin into paper that circulated as money. Stockholms Banco issued Europe's first banknotes in 1661 and failed. Italian bankers moved credit across Europe with bills of exchange, and London goldsmiths lent against deposits.
