# M-fiat: Fiat Money

Layer 0 node `M-fiat`. Grades: A primary, B secondary, C single source or unchecked.

## What Happened
On 15 August 1971 Nixon suspended dollar convertibility into gold, froze wages and prices and added a 10% import tax. The Smithsonian agreement tried to hold pegged rates; the system ended soon after, and major currencies floated by March 1973. Money since then is backed by law and central bank policy.
