# M-commodity: Commodity Money

Layer 0 node `M-commodity`. Grades: A primary, B secondary, C single source or unchecked.

## What Happened
Before coins, value was counted in goods: cattle, grain and weighed silver. Homer prices armour in head of cattle. Babylonian law sets fines and debts in grain and in shekels and minas of silver, both units of weight.
