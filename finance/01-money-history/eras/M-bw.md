Layer 0 node `M-bw`. Grades: A primary, B secondary, C single source or unchecked.

## What Happened
In July 1944 delegates from 44 nations met at Bretton Woods, New Hampshire, and created the IMF. Each member set a par value in gold or in the 1944 gold dollar and held spot rates within 1% of parity. The dollar stood convertible into gold for foreign official holders at $35 an ounce until August 1971.
