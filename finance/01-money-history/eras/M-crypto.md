# M-crypto: Bitcoin and Crypto

Layer 0 node `M-crypto`. Grades: A primary, B secondary, C single source or unchecked.

## What Happened
Satoshi Nakamoto proposed "a purely peer-to-peer version of electronic cash" secured by hash-based proof-of-work. The genesis block was mined on 3 January 2009. Bitcoin is a bearer digital asset with no issuer.
