# M-2008: Shadow Banking and 2008

Layer 0 node `M-2008`. Grades: A primary, B secondary, C single source or unchecked.

## What Happened
Subprime mortgages were packaged into securities, rated high, and funded with short-term repo and commercial paper. House prices fell from 2006, funding ran, Bear Stearns was rescued in March 2008 and Lehman Brothers filed for bankruptcy in September 2008. The FCIC concluded the crisis was avoidable.
