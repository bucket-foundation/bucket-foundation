# M-stable: Stablecoins

Layer 0 node `M-stable`. Grades: A primary, B secondary, C single source or unchecked.

## What Happened
Stablecoins are tokens that promise one dollar on redemption. Algorithmic TerraUSD collapsed in May 2022. USDC ran in March 2023 when Silicon Valley Bank, which held its deposits, failed. The GENIUS Act, P.L. 119-27, signed 18 July 2025, set federal rules: 1:1 reserves, no interest to holders, federal or state licensing.
