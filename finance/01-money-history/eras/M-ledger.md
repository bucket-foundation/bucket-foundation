# M-ledger: Credit Ledgers

Layer 0 node `M-ledger`. Grades: A primary, B secondary, C single source or unchecked.

## What Happened
Mesopotamian scribes wrote debts in grain and silver on clay. Written record keeping starts with cuneiform around 3350 BCE, and law codes treat the debt tablet as the instrument of credit. Credit recorded on clay predates coinage by over two thousand years.
