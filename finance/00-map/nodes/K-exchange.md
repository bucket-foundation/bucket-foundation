## What It Is

US equities trade across registered exchanges, alternative trading systems and wholesalers under Regulation NMS. NMS links venues through the order protection rule, the access rule and the sub-penny rule. Retail flow mostly routes to wholesalers who pay for it (PFOF).

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Regulation NMS adopted in Release 34-51808, File S7-10-04, June 2005. Three pillars: Order Protection Rule, Access Rule with access fee caps, Sub-Penny Rule. **A**
- SEC release 2024-137, 2024-09-18: new $0.005 tick for NMS stocks priced at or above $1.00 whose time-weighted quoted spread qualifies; lower access fee caps; odd-lot data in the consolidated feed. **A**
- Compliance dates in 2024-137: first business day of November 2025 for Rule 612, Rule 610 and round lots; first business day of May 2026 for odd-lot information. **A**
- Later SEC actions may have moved these compliance dates. Not checked in this pass. **C**

## 2026 State

Half-penny tick and reduced access fee caps are in their first year of effect, per the 2024 compliance schedule. Confirm no later exemptive order delayed them before citing.
