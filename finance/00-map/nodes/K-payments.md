## What It Is

Payment rails move money between accounts: ACH batch credits and debits run by Nacha rules, Fedwire for large value, FedNow for instant settlement, card networks, SWIFT messaging for cross-border, and stablecoin rails. x402 is an open standard that revives HTTP status 402 for machine payments.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- FedNow went live 2023-07-20. **A**
- ACH Network 2025: 35.2B payments, up 4.9%, $93T value, up 7.9%; 141M a day on average. **A**
- Same Day ACH 2025: 1.4B payments, $3.9T value. **A**
- x402 repo moved under the x402 Foundation; coinbase/x402 is now a development fork. SDKs cover EVM, Solana, Aptos, Stellar, Hedera and more. **A**

## 2026 State

x402 has a foundation home and multi-chain SDKs as of late 2026. Bucket's paid-to-cite flow depends on it.
