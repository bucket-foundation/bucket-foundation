## What It Is

US finance splits oversight. SEC: securities, exchanges, broker-dealers, advisers. CFTC: futures, swaps, event contracts. OCC, Fed, FDIC: banks. State regulators and gaming boards: money transmitters, insurance, sports betting. The GENIUS Act adds a federal stablecoin regime.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- SEC mission: protect investors, maintain fair, orderly and efficient markets, facilitate capital formation. **A**
- CFTC regulates US derivatives markets, including futures, options and swaps. **A**
- GENIUS Act, Public Law 119-27, S. 1582, approved 2025-07-18. **A**
- GENIUS effective on the earlier of 18 months after enactment (2027-01-18) or 120 days after final implementing regulations. **A**

## 2026 State

GENIUS takes effect no later than 2027-01-18, seven weeks out. Rulemaking by OCC, Fed and FDIC is the 2026 workstream.
