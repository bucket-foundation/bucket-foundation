## What It Is

Hyperscalers are spending on data centers, GPUs and power at a pace without precedent. The spend shows up in cash flow statements as purchases of property and equipment.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Microsoft PP&E purchases: FY2024 $44.5B, FY2025 $64.6B, FY2026 (to 2026-06-30) $115.9B; quarter to 2026-09-30 $39.4B. **A**
- Alphabet: 2024 $52.5B, 2025 $91.4B; 2026 Jan to Sep $132.0B. **A**
- Meta: 2024 $37.3B, 2025 $69.7B; 2026 Jan to Sep $83.1B. **A**
- Amazon productive asset purchases: 2024 $83.0B, 2025 $131.8B; quarter to 2026-09-30 $77.3B. **A**
- IEA Energy and AI report blocked automated fetch (403); no power figures used. **C**

## 2026 State

Alphabet alone spent $132B in nine months of 2026, more than its full 2025. Annualizing the latest quarters puts the four at about $750B a year (derived).
