## What It Is

The FOMC sets a target range for fed funds and steers it with interest on reserves and the ON RRP rate. Changes pass to repo, bills, bank loans, mortgages and asset prices with lags.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- FOMC held the target range at 3-3/4 to 4 percent on 2026-10-28, vote 10 to 2. **A**
- Effective fed funds 3.88% on 2026-11-18 through 2026-11-24. **A**
