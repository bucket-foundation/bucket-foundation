## What It Is

The foreign exchange market is the largest market by turnover. It trades OTC through dealers in spot, forwards, FX swaps, currency swaps and options. The BIS Triennial Central Bank Survey measures it every three years in April.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Global FX turnover $9.6T a day in April 2025, up 28% from April 2022. This supersedes the 2022 figure of $7.5T a day. **A**
- USD on one side of 89% of trades; EUR 28.9%, JPY 16.8%, GBP 10.2%. CHF rose to sixth. **A**
- FX swaps $4T a day, up 5%. Spot up 42%, outright forwards up 60%; shares 31% and 19%. **A**
- 2022 survey: more than $7.5T a day in April 2022. **A**
