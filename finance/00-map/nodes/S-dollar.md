## What It Is

The dollar is the main reserve, funding, invoicing and FX vehicle currency. Sanctions use that reach. Stablecoins export dollar claims onchain; GENIUS requires reserve backing in dollars and Treasuries.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- USD was 58% of disclosed global official FX reserves in 2024 (Fed, 2025 edition of the international role of the dollar). **A**
- USD on one side of 89% of FX trades in April 2025 (BIS Triennial). **A**
- Dollar stablecoin float about $284B on 2026-11-27 (DefiLlama). **B**
