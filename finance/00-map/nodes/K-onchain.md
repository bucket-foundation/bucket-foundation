## What It Is

Onchain markets settle trades on public blockchains. Automated market makers (AMMs) price against a pool formula; Uniswap v3 added concentrated liquidity; v4 adds hooks and a singleton pool contract. Onchain order books and perpetual futures venues run alongside.
