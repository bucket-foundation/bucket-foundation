# Clearing and Settlement

ID: `K-clearing`
As of: 2026-11-27
Bead: `bkt-q5moa.1`

## What It Is

Clearing nets and guarantees trades through a central counterparty; settlement moves cash and securities. In US equities DTCC runs NSCC (clearing) and DTC (depository). FICC clears Treasuries.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- SEC adopted T+1 on 2023-02-15 (release 2023-29); compliance date 2024-05-28. **A**
- SEC adopted Treasury central clearing mandate on 2023-12-13 (release 2023-247), first set for 2025-12-31 cash and 2026-06-30 repo. **A**
- SEC extended those dates by one year on 2025-03-04 (Federal Register 2025-03351): cash Treasury trades 2026-12-31, repo 2027-06-30. **A**
- SEC staff issued DTC a no-action letter on its tokenization services pilot, letter dated after DTCC's 2025-12-11 request. **A**

## 2026 State

US cash Treasury clearing mandate hits on 2026-12-31, five weeks out. Repo follows 2027-06-30. The map line 'T+1 since May 2024' stands.
