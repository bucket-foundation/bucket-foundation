## What It Is

Futures, options, swaps and credit default swaps transfer price, rate and credit risk. Exchange-traded contracts clear at CCPs; OTC contracts trade bilaterally or clear centrally under post-2008 mandates. The CFTC regulates futures and swaps; the SEC regulates security-based swaps.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- OTC derivatives notional outstanding $846T at end-June 2025, up 16% year on year. BIS calls this an acceleration from a 5% annual trend since end-2016. **A**
- Gross market value of OTC derivatives $21.8T at end-June 2025, up $5T (+29%). **A**
- OTC interest rate derivatives turnover $7.9T a day in April 2025, up 59% from 2022 (Triennial). **A**
- CFTC publishes a Weekly Swaps Report from swap data repository data. **A**

## 2026 State

Euro rate derivatives drove 2025 growth; euro IRD turnover nearly doubled to $3.0T a day and passed USD contracts at $2.4T.
