## What It Is

Kindleberger and Minsky describe the cycle: displacement, credit boom, euphoria, distress, panic. Bank runs happen when short-term funding flees long or impaired assets. 2023 showed the run can move at phone speed.

## Key Numbers and Dates

- Fed review of SVB, 2023-04-28: failure from mismanaged interest rate and liquidity risk, weak board oversight, and supervisors who did not act forcefully enough. **A**
- FDIC failed bank list shows Nano Banc, Irvine CA, closed 2026-09-25; Tioga-Franklin Savings Bank, Philadelphia, closed 2026-08-21. **A**

## 2026 State

Small-bank failures continue in 2026 at a low rate; no systemic event as of the fetch.
