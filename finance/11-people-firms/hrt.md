## Profile

<!-- voice-ignore-next 3 -->
> Hudson River Trading LLC is an American electronic market making company headquartered in New York City, New York, United States.
> As of 2021, it accounted for more than 15% of all daily equities trading in the United States.
> Hudson River Trading employs over 1,000 people in 14 offices around the world, including New York, Chicago, Austin, Boulder, London, Singapore, Shanghai, Mumbai, and Dublin.

## Documented Methods

<!-- voice-ignore-next 6 -->
> The firm focuses on research and development of automated trading algorithms using mathematical techniques, and trades on over 200 markets worldwide.
> The company is a member of the Principal Traders Group, an advisory group formed by the Futures Industry Association (FIA).
> In January 2014, Hudson River Trading and three other quantitative trading firms formed the Modern Markets Initiative, a trade lobbying group.
> On January 16, 2018, Hudson River Trading acquired its rival firm Sun Trading, a global market maker that traded on over 115 exchanges.
> The firm hires programmers, software engineers, and mathematicians to develop and improve its trading strategies.
> In March 2014, New York Attorney General Eric Schneiderman announced a probe into high-frequency traders, including Hudson River Trading, getting early access to raw stock market feeds at an annual price of $180,000.
