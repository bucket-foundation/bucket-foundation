## Profile

<!-- voice-ignore-next 3 -->
> Citadel Securities LLC is an American market making firm headquartered in Miami, Florida.
> It is one of the largest market making firms in the United States, along with Virtu and Jane Street, and is involved in one out of every four stock trades in the United States as of 2026.
> It is also the largest designated market maker on the New York Stock Exchange.

## Documented Methods

<!-- voice-ignore-next 6 -->
> Citadel Securities provides liquidity and trade execution to retail and institutional clients.
> The firm also trades futures, equities, credit, options, currencies, and Treasury bonds.
> Citadel Securities is a separate entity from the hedge fund Citadel LLC, although both were founded and are majority owned by American hedge fund manager Kenneth C.
> Citadel Securities was formed in 2002 as a market maker, providing liquidity and trade execution to retail and institutional clients.
> By August 2011, the company ended its foray into investment banking to instead focus on electronic trading and market making.
> In 2014, the firm expanded its market-making offering to interest rate swaps.

## Published Figures

<!-- voice-ignore-next 4 -->
> In October 2018, Bloomberg reported that 40% of Robinhood's revenue came from selling customer orders to firms such as Citadel Securities and Two Sigma Securities.
> During the first half of 2020, Citadel Securities doubled its profit while generating $4 billion ($4.98 billion in 2025) in revenue due to increased volatility and retail trading caused by the coronavirus pandemic.
> The firm ended the year with $7.5 billion ($8.25 billion in 2025) in revenue, which topped the company's previous record set the year prior.
> According to the Wall Street Journal, about one-third of stock orders from individual investors in 2015 was completed through Citadel, which accounts for about 10 percent of the firm's revenue.
