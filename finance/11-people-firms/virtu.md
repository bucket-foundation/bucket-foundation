## Profile

<!-- voice-ignore-next 3 -->
> Virtu Financial, Inc. is an American electronic market making company.
> The company is headquartered in Manhattan, New York City.
> As of 2026, Virtu is one of the largest market making firms in the United States along with Citadel Securities and Jane Street.

## Documented Methods

<!-- voice-ignore-next 6 -->
> The company suggested standards for electronic firms that call themselves market maker.
> Virtu believes market makers should be obligated to quote at or near the inside of the national best bid and offer throughout the day and quote at various price points in a number of different securities.
> Virtu was ranked as one of the five largest high-frequency traders of equities in Europe in 2011.
> In November 2014, Reuters reported that Chris Concannon, president and chief operating officer at Virtu Financial, will succeed William O'Brien as president of BATS Global Markets, a trading venue that was founded by high-frequency traders.
> Vincent Viola co-founded Madison Tyler with David Salomon, a former arbitrage trader at Goldman Sachs.
> Virtu acquired a market-making unit that handles NYSE Amex stocks from Cohen Capital Group LLC in December 2011.
