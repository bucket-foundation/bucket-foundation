## Profile

<!-- voice-ignore-next 3 -->
> Jane Street Capital is an American electronic market making company headquartered in Manhattan, New York City.
> It is one of the largest market making firms in the United States along with Virtu and Citadel Securities.
> It employs approximately 3,000 people across its global offices, and trades across more than twenty countries.
