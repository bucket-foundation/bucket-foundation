## Profile

<!-- voice-ignore-next 3 -->
> Paul Tudor Jones II (born September 28, 1954) is an American billionaire hedge fund manager.
> In 1980, he founded Tudor Investment Corporation, an asset management firm headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut.
> Eight years later, he co-founded the Robin Hood Foundation, which focuses on poverty reduction.
