## Profile

<!-- voice-ignore-next 3 -->
> Fischer Sheffey Black, Jr. (January 11, 1938 – August 30, 1995) was an American economist, best known as one of the co-authors of the Black–Scholes option pricing model.
> He held academic positions at the University of Chicago and the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, later working at Goldman Sachs.
> In addition to his work on option pricing, Black made important contributions to the development of the capital asset pricing model (CAPM).
