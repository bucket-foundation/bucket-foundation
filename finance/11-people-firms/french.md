## Profile

<!-- voice-ignore-next 3 -->
> Kenneth Ronald "Ken" French (born March 10, 1954) is the Roth Family Distinguished Professor of Finance at the Tuck School of Business, Dartmouth College.
> He has previously been a faculty member at MIT, the Yale School of Management, and the University of Chicago Booth School of Business.
> He has worked on asset pricing with Eugene Fama.

## Documented Methods

<!-- voice-ignore-next 3 -->
> They wrote a series of papers that cast doubt on the validity of the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM), which posits that a stock's beta alone should explain its average return.
> These papers describe two factors above and beyond a stock's market beta which can explain differences in stock returns: market capitalization and "value".
> They also offer evidence that a variety of patterns in average returns, often labeled as "anomalies" in past work, can be explained with their Fama–French three-factor model.
