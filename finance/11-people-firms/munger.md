## Profile

<!-- voice-ignore-next 3 -->
> Charles Thomas Munger (January 1, 1924 – November 28, 2023) was an American businessman, investor, and philanthropist.
> He was the vice chairman of Berkshire Hathaway, the conglomerate controlled by Warren Buffett, from 1978 until his death in 2023.
> Buffett described Munger as his closest partner and right-hand man, and credited him with being the "architect" of modern Berkshire Hathaway's business philosophy.
