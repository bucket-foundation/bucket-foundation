## Profile

<!-- voice-ignore-next 3 -->
> CoreWeave, Inc. is an American cloud-computing company based in Livingston, New Jersey.
> As of 2026, the company operates 51 AI data centers, specialized data centers designed for higher parallel computing performance via heavy use of graphics processing units (GPUs).
> Founded as Atlantic Crypto in 2017 the company focused on high-performance computing, CoreWeave has its own data centers operating in the United States and Europe Its $1.6 billion supercomputer data center for Nvidia in Plano, Texas, has been described by Nvidia as the fastest AI supercomputer in the world.

## Documented Methods

<!-- voice-ignore-next 6 -->
> It had nine portfolio companies at launch.
> At this point, CoreWeave had signed deals with nine of the ten top AI model providers.
> As of June 30, 2026, CoreWeave reported total debt of approximately $35.1 billion in its Q2 2026 quarterly filing, split between recourse (approximately $31.4 billion) and non-recourse (approximately $3.7 billion) facilities structured against hyperscaler contract commitments.
> Michael Intrator is the company's chief executive and founder, alongside his other cofounders Brian Venturo as chief strategy officer, Brannin McBee as chief development officer, and Peter Salanki as CTO.
> Through its 2025 acquisition of Weights & Biases, Coreweave brought model development, evaluation, and monitoring natively onto its platform.
> In October 2025, CoreWeave launched AI Object storage, an object storage platform to facilitate teams training large language models.
