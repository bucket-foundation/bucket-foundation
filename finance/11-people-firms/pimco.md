## Profile

<!-- voice-ignore-next 3 -->
> Pacific Investment Management Company LLC (PIMCO) is an American investment management firm.
> While it has a specific focus on active fixed income management worldwide, it manages investments in many asset classes, including fixed income, equities and other financial assets across public and private markets.
> PIMCO is one of the largest investment managers, actively managing more than $2 trillion in assets for central banks, sovereign wealth funds, pension funds, corporations, foundations and endowments, as well as individual investors around the world.
