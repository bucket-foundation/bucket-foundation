## Profile

<!-- voice-ignore-next 3 -->
> Citadel LLC (formerly known as Citadel Investment Group, LLC) is an American multinational hedge fund and financial services company.
> Founded in 1990 by Kenneth Griffin, it has more than $77 billion in assets under management as of January 2026.
> The company has over 3,100 employees, with corporate headquarters in Miami, Florida, and offices throughout North America, Asia, and Europe.

## Published Figures

<!-- voice-ignore-next 6 -->
> As of December 2025, Citadel has posted $90.4 billion in net gains since its inception in 1990, making one of the "most successful hedge funds in history", according to LCH Investments, and CNBC.
> Within eight years, the firm had more than $2 billion in assets under management.
> In 2006, Citadel and JPMorgan Chase took over the energy portfolio and division of failed hedge fund Amaranth Advisors, which had suffered a 65% ($6 billion) loss in assets.
> However, they rebounded with a 62% return in 2009.
> On January 17, 2012, Citadel's flagship funds, Citadel Kensington Global Strategies Fund Ltd and Citadel Wellington LLC, crossed their respective high watermarks, earning back the 50% of assets lost during the 2008 financial crisis.
> Starting at the beginning of 2014, over an 18 months period the hedge fund's assets under management increased $10 billion; from $16 billion to $26 billion as a result of "a 29% rise for its main hedge funds and a flow of new cash".
