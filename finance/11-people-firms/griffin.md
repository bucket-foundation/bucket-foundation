## Profile

<!-- voice-ignore-next 3 -->
> Kenneth Cordele Griffin (born 1968) is an American hedge fund manager, entrepreneur and investor.
> He is the founder, chief executive officer, co-chief investment officer, and 80% owner of Citadel LLC, a multinational hedge fund.
> He also owns Citadel Securities, one of the largest market makers in the United States.
