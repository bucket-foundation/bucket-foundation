## Architecture
Five parts: `data_pipeline/` (providers, fetcher, processor, `run_pipeline.py`), `model_core/` (`alphagpt.py` model, `engine.py` trainer, `vm.py` stack VM, `factors.py`, `ops.py`, `vocab.py`, `backtest.py`), `strategy_manager/` (runner, portfolio, risk), `execution/` (Jupiter swap, RPC handler, trader) and `dashboard/`. `lord/` holds a regularization experiment.

## Data Layer
Birdeye and DexScreener providers feed TimescaleDB or plain Postgres through `asyncpg`.

## Event Loop
Async: `StrategyRunner.run_loop` monitors open positions, scans for entries, runs inference per token and executes buys or sells.

## Execution
Jupiter aggregator swaps on Solana with RPC handler; `_execute_buy` and `_execute_sell` take a score or exit ratio.

## Risk
`RiskEngine` rejects pools under 5,000 USD liquidity and tokens with no sell quote (honeypot check). Config sets a 5 percent stop loss and a 10 percent first take profit that sells half.

## Strategies Shipped
Formula tokens generated by the model are evaluated by `StackVM` over feature tensors and backtested; the best formula scores new tokens.

## AI Components
`AlphaEngine` trains a generative model that emits factor formulas, with a regularization option.

## Connectors
Birdeye, DexScreener, Jupiter, Solana RPC.

## Fit
Compact pipeline for formula search on Solana tokens, with the honeypot check as its one safety control.
