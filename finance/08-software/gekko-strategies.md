# Gekko Strategies

https://github.com/xFFFFF/Gekko-Strategies. License none. Stars 1,447. Last push 2020-01-09. JavaScript. Checked 2026-11-27.

## Architecture
Collection of Gekko trading-bot strategy files, one folder each, plus `backtest_database.csv` of results.

## Data Layer
Gekko candle feeds.

## Event Loop
Gekko strategy `update` and `check` callbacks per candle.

## Execution
Gekko advice long or short.

## Risk
None.

## Strategies Shipped
BBRSI, DEMA, EMADIV, MACD, MK_RSI_BULL_BEAR, Neapticator, CCI, buy-at-sell-at and neural-net variants.

## AI Components
Some strategies use neural nets (Luke_NN, ManuNet, Neapticator).

## Connectors
Gekko only.

## Fit
Strategy zoo for a retired engine. Mine for signal ideas.
