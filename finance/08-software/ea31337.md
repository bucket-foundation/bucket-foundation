## Architecture
MetaTrader expert advisor. `src/EA31337.mq4` and `.mq5` include `include/ea.h`, which defines the `EA31337` class on top of the EA31337-classes library. Strategies, indicators and meta strategies live in git submodules (`strategies`, `strategies-meta`, `classes`) that are empty in this clone. `include/common/` holds input sets for the Lite, Advanced, Rider and Elite editions.

## Data Layer
MetaTrader terminal price feed and tester history. Meta News strategy embeds yearly news CSVs as resources (2018 to 2024).

## Event Loop
MetaTrader callbacks: `OnInit`, `OnTick` (calls `ea.OnTick`), `OnTimer`, `OnTradeTransaction`. Strategies are attached per timeframe from M1 to H12 through inputs such as `Strategy_M15`.

## Execution
Orders go through the terminal's trade API. Advanced mode maps conditions (new day, equity above or below 1, 2 or 5 percent) to actions (close all, close in profit, close most loss).

## Risk
`EA_Risk_MarginMax`, per-strategy stop strategies attached by `StrategyAddStops`, and the equity conditions above.

## Strategies Shipped
Lite defaults: Meta Scalper (M15), Meta Trend (M30), Meta RSI (H1, H6, H8), Meta Volatility (H2), Momentum (H3), Meta Conditions (H4), SAR (H12). Strategy sources sit in the unfetched submodule.

## AI Components
Genetic optimization of input parameters through the tester. No model inference.

## Connectors
MetaTrader 4 and 5.

## Fit
Forex multi-strategy reference with a full MetaTrader build chain and Docker. Strategy logic requires fetching the submodules. GPL-3.0 limits reuse.
