bucket.foundation exists to make primary research **paid-for-once** and **citeable-forever** — and to route citation fees to authors, not publishers.

It is built for the small number of people who can take a model and an axiom and reach a layer of reality nobody has reached before. AI + foundations + a small number of brilliant humans = the next layer of reality.

Canon holds only *foundations*: axioms, real math, rules, laws, principles, primary derivations. Outcomes — longevity, disease, cognition — are downstream applications, cross-mirrored into the branches that derive them.

The work is open-source under [MIT code + CC0 intent](/governance). The protocol is [feed402](/protocol) over x402 on Base. Code, canon entries, claim cards, figures, bridges, all live in one repo: [bucket-foundation](https://github.com/bucket-foundation/bucket-foundation).

## Why education — the founding research.

Bucket exists to **reform education**, and reform that is not grounded in evidence is opinion. The founding research is the [education-atlas](https://github.com/bucket-foundation/education-atlas): 78,326 observations finding that for five thousand years every knowledge technology widened access to *consume* knowledge and none widened access to *produce* it — leaving 99.86% of humanity only ever consuming, and just ~0.14% reaching the frontier where it is produced.

[Read the mission](/mission) for the full evidence — the three crises, the 270× access cliff, the consume-vs-produce gap — and an honest map of what Bucket reforms (the knowledge layer) and what it explicitly does not (schooling logistics, K-12 funding, the floor-access crisis).

## History, briefly.

**2022.** bucket 1.0 shipped as a four-verb prototype, *build · discuss · discover · publish*, a social network for collaboratively building and debating theories of history with evidence. It never shipped publicly. The slogan: *build the past.*

**2023-2025.** The prototype slept. The slogan widened: *build history.* A hackathon build landed the reference site on Vercel.

**2026.** Reactivated as an open-source nonprofit. Two verbs deleted, canon added. The thesis turned: *bucket is the new renaissance.* Read the [manifesto](/manifesto).

**2026-06.** Story Protocol minting was removed on 2026-06-14; the citation fee rail over x402 on Base is specified and does not settle yet. Walrus storage and Dynamic sign-in went with it. Credentials are Open Badges 3.0 verifiable credentials. Research happens in [Research OS](/research-os), and the [desktop app](/download) runs it on your machine.
