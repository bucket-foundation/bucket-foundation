## Description

A village in today's Amasya, Turkey. According to Mehmed Neşrî (Vol. 1, pp. 374-9 (1949)), Kara Devletşah, a commander in the Timurid Sultanate, stayed in this village and was defeated here or nearby by Bayezid I.
