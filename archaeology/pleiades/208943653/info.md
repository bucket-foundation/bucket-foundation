## Description

Remains of an ancient settlement of Roman date, recorded by Gsell (AAA, whence BAtlas). The location should lie within a kilometer of the BAtlas coordinates: a point near modern Bir el Kerma, which is located in the modern municipal territory of Tixter (Bordj Bou Arreridj willaya).
