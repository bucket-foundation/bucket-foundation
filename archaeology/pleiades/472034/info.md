## Description

An ancient port settlement in the north of Sardinia, taken by the author of the Antonine Itineraries as the starting point for the description of all the routes on the island. It is possibly to be identified with modern Lungoni/Santa Teresa Gallura (thus, tentatively, BAtlas).
