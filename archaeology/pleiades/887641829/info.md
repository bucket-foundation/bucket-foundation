## Description

Masseria Muro (comune di Mesagne, province of Brindisi, south Italy) is the findspot of a hoard of ancient coins. In 1976, a deposit of coins originating from a number of Greek cities of south Italy was discovered. The coins range in date from the Archaic to the Hellenistic periods.
