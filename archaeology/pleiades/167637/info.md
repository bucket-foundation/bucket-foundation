## Description

The mountainous area around the Little St Bernard Pass, conquered by the Romans and organized by Augustus into the province that took its name (q.v.). The name may reference a people that once inhabited the region; classical theorizing that the name relates to Greeks (e.g., Pliny) has been discounted by modern scholars.
