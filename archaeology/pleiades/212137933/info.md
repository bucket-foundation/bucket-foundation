## Description

A sanctuary of Asklepios at Eleusis, active from the late 5th century BC until the 3rd century AD, known from inscriptions found in a field 1.5 km north of the sanctuary of Demeter and Kore on the bank of the Kephisos River in the 1890s. The site has never been archaeologically explored and the exact location is now unknown.
