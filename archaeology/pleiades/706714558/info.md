## Description

This unlocated, fortified settlement was founded and built by Ninurta-kudurrī-uṣur of Suhu, which he named after himself: Dūr-Ninurta-kudurrī-uṣur (“Fort Ninurta-kudurrī-uṣur”). The city is presumably located in the kingdom of Suhu.
