## Description

Azotos Paralios, also referred to as Ashdod-Yam is a multi-period archaeological site located on the Mediterranean coast of Israel. It exists as early as the time of the Assyrian empire. The toponym "Azotos Paralios" belongs to its Hellenistic phase.
