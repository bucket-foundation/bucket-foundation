## Description

An ancient, spring-fed lake (modern Çapalı Göl/Çapalı Bataklık/Karakuyu Gölü in Turkey's Afyonkarahisar province) next to which the ancient settlement of Aporidos Come and the Roman castellum known as "Alutrena" were located.
