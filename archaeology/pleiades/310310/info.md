## Description

Remains of an ancient fortification recorded by Gsell (AAA, whence BAtlas). The location should lie within a kilometer of the BAtlas coordinates: a point east-northeast of modern Bousâada in the municipal territory of modern At Muḥli/Beni Mouhli in Algeria's Setif province.
