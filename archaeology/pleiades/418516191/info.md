## Description

The eleventh region of Rome was created under the Augustan administrative division of 7 BCE. According to the Regionary catalogues, this region contained 19 shrines (aediculae), 89 townhouses (domūs), 16 warehouses (horrea), 15 bath houses, and 20 fountains.
