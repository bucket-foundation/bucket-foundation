## Description

Remains of an ancient settlement of Roman date, recorded by Gsell (AAA, whence BAtlas). The location should lie within a kilometer of the BAtlas coordinates: a point near the modern CW 43 road just to the northeast of Teniet Ennasr in the Algerian wilaya of Bordj Bou Arreridj.
