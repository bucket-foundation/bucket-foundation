## Description

An ancient place, mentioned in the maritime portion of the Antonine Itineraries as between Centum Cellae (modern Civitavecchia) and Rapinium (modern Bagni Sant'Agostino). BAtlas identifies Algae with modern Torre Valdaliga; whereas NP considers it unlocated.
