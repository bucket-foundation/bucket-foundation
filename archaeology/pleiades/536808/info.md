## Description

An ancient literary tradition, seemingly based on a single vague passage in Alcman and subsequently embroidered with mythological implications, places a people (Erisychaioi) and/or a polis (Erisyche) in Acarnania. Freitag, whence BAtlDir, rejects this tradition as fictional.
