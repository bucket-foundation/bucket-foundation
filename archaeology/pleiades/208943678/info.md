## Description

Remains of an ancient settlement of Roman date, recorded by Gsell (AAA, whence BAtlas). The location should lie within a kilometer of the BAtlas coordinates: a point north of the modern CW15 roadway in the municipal territory of modern At Muḥli/Beni Mouhli in Algeria's Setif province.
