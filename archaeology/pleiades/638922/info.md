## Description

An ancient place, mentioned in the so-called Stadiasmus Maris Magni, that was probably a promontory (cape) and can likely be identified with the modern İblis Burnu (Cape İblis/Cape Angistro on the Turkish coast of the Gulf of Fethiye).
