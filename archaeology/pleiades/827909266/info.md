## Description

A site located on what is now an island in Tahtalı Barajı near Izmir. The site shows signs of habitation from the Chalcolithic period to the Late Bronze Age, as well as a later Romano-Byzantine settlement. The plain surrounding the site was flooded by the Tahtalı Dam in 1999.
