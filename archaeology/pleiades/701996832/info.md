## Description

Rogmorum is a toponym included on the Peutinger map, and it is connected to roadways radiating from Tavium toward the east. Wilson discusses whether or not Rogmorum is a corruption of Trocmorum, an epithet connected with Tavium. The compilers of the Barrington Atlas did not include this place on map 63.
