## Description

This unlocated, fortified settlement was founded and built by Šamaš-rēša-uṣur of Suhu, which he named after himself: Dūr-Šamaš-rēša-uṣur (“Fort Šamaš-rēša-uṣur”). The city is presumably located in the kingdom of Suhu.
