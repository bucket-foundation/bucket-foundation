## Description

An ancient settlement of Pisidia, located at modern Korkuteli in Turkey's Antalya province. The acropolis hill was heavily damaged by the construction of the modern dam in the 20th century. The majority of the ancient settlement probably lay to the east of the acropolis and is now covered by the neighborhood of Alaaddin (Alaeddin Mahalessi).
