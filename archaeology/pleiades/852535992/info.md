## Description

An archaeological site in modern Turkey's Tokat province that now lies in the eastern portion of the modern town of Erbaa. Excavations were conducted there in the mid-20th century. It is best known for having produced artifacts of the Hatti culture, indicating intensive human activity between 4,000 and 2,000 BCE. Due to agricultural activity and development, the site has been significantly damaged and no archaeology is visible on publicly available aerial/satellite imagery.
