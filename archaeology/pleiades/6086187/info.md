## Description

Archaeological site at the interfleuve of the Yenisei and Khemchik Rivers in Tuva. The site, which flooded by the construction of the Sayano-Shushenskaya power plant in the mid-1980s, contained artifacts from the Neolithic through the Medieval periods.
