## Description

Leuke Akte, the ‘White cape’ located on the Libyan coast of Egypt, was the site of an Isis cult wherein the Egyptian goddess was worshipped as Aphrodite, Muchis, and Eseremphis. An oracular shrine of Apollo was also found there.
