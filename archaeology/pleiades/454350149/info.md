## Description

The Grotta del Tasso di Sofignano (also referred to as Buca del Tasso) is a cave located near Camaiore in the province of Lucca in Italy. The cave is an archaeological site where the presence of Homo neanderthalensis is attested ca. 40,000 years ago.
