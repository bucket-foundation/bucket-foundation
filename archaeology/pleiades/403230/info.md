## Description

The archaeological site of Lago dell’Accesa, in the locality known as "La Pesta", is situated near the lake of Lago dell'Accesa. The site was situated so as to exploit local mineral resources, and the archaeological remains provide extensive evidence for habitation from the ninth through sixth centuries B.C.
