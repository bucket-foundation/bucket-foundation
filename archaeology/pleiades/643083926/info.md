## Description

This sanctuary is located in the Kerameikos of Athens, south of the street of the tombs and was established in the early Hellenistic Age. It has often been identified as a sanctuary of Hekate, but more recent research suggests that it was dedicated to Artemis Soteira. It also contained a prophetic well associated with Paian.
