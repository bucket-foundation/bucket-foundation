## Description

An archaeological site with evidence of three settlement mounds. Phases of identifiable human activity include the Hellenistic period at Mound B (3rd-1st century B.C.) and Hepthalo-Turk (5th-9th century A.D.) at Mounds A and C. Traces of Islamic activity also were detected.
