## Description

Remains of a quarried ancient settlement of Roman date, recorded by Gsell (AAA, whence BAtlas). Gsell places it approximately 2 kilometers southeast of the village of "Medjana". The location should lie within a kilometer of the BAtlas coordinates: a point west of the modern RN 106 highway between Meğana and Bordj Bou Arreridj in the Algerian wilaya of Bordj Bou Arreridj.
