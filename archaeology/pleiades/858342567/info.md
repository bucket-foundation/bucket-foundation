## Description

Remains of an ancient settlement of Roman date, recorded by Gsell (AAA, whence BAtlas). The location should lie within a kilometer of the BAtlas coordinates: a point in the immediate vicinity of modern Timechtaouine (Draa Kebila municipal territory, Setif province, Algeria).
