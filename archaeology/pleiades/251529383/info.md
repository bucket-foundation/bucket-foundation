## Description

Settlement on the Yarmuk River in the southern Golan, approximately 5 km south of the Sea of Galilee, consisting of a round tell and a lower terrace covering approximately two hectares. The site contains signs of habitation from the Late Bronze Age through the Iron Age (ca. 15th-8th centuries BCE).
