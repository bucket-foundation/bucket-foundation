## Description

Noted by Gsell (AAA, whence BAtlas), modern Akbou des Beni Chénacha was the site of a large establishment of the Roman period, probably a villa. The location was approximately 2 kilometers to the southwest of colonial Camp-du-Maréchal (now Tadmaït in Algeria's Tizi Ouzou province.
