## Description

Stephanus, in the context of his entry for the well-attested Psophis in Arcadia, places a separate "polis Psophis he palaia" in Acarnania. Oberhummer (whence RE and BAtlDir) is inclined to reject this assertion, which presumably follows a lost literary tradition accessible to Stephanus as a "mythographer's error".
