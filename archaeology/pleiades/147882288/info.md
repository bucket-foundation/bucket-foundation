## Description

The Via Nova Traiana is a Roman road built along the Limes Arabicus during the reigns of the emperors Trajan and Hadrian. The route may supersede an earlier one known as the "Via Regia" or "King's Highway", a route of communication and transhumance in use since the Neolithic period.
