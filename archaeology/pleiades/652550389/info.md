## Description

An early Bronze Age site in modern Jordan, located about 12 km southwest of Madaba. The site has been interpreted as a ceremonial meeting place for regional groups, featuring a central area with monumental structures and standing stones surrounded by dolmen fields, which may mark burial sites.
