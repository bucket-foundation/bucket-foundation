## Description

Remains of an ancient fortification of Roman date, recorded by Gsell (AAA, whence BAtlas). The location should lie within a kilometer of the BAtlas coordinates: a point along the modern CW 43 road to the east of Djaafra and just past the intersection with the road linking Ouchanene Kebira and Tiâroussine (Bordj Bou Arreridj willaya, Algeria).
