## Description

An ancient river of north Africa, placed by the Ravenna Geographer between the Isaris (modern Oued Isser) and the Ligar (perhaps the Siga). Modern identification of the Nigrensis is uncertain: the modern Oued Amieur, Salsaf, Sikkak, and Tafna are all candidates.
