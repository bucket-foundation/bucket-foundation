## Description

Aithalidai/Hybadai? was a deme of ancient Attica. Following the Cleisthenic reforms, Aethalidae was assigned to the phyle of Leontis. After 307/6 BCE, it belonged to the phyle of Antigonis. The deme's location is uncertain.
