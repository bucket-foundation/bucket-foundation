## Description

Attested on the Peutinger Map, ancient Rapis lay on a route from Augusta Vindelicum (modern Augsburg, Germany) to Navoa and thence to Cambodunum (modern Kempten). BAtlas follows Römer Bayern in tentatively placing Rapis at modern Schwabmünchen.
