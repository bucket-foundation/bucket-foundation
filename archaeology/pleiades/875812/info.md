## Description

A reconstructed road stretch from a station named Barbare (possibly modern Mohammed Khan in Turkey's Şanlıurfa Province) through Nisibis (modern Nusaybin at the Turkish/Syrian border) to a road station named Ad fl(umen) Tigrim (modern Cizre in Turkey's Şırnak Province).
