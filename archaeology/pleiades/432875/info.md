## Description

A large fanum sacred to ‘Herculis Rani’? at Campochiaro, the name of which is then preserved in the station mentioned on the Peutinger Table. Scholars like Adriano La Regina support linking this fanum to a place mentioned by Livy, although see the discussion by Oakley.
