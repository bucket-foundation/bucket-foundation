## Description

A name included on the Peutinger map. The "Graaei" were a Paeonian tribe whose territory was located on the Strymon River. Scholars debate whether this name should be correlated with the archaeological site of Terpni/Nigrita, a place located 21 kilometers southeast of Kalokastro.
