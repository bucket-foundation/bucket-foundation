## Description

Hadrumetum/Iustinianopolis was an important ancient port in North Africa. Founded by the Phoenicians, the site pre-dated Carthage. Under Roman administration, the site prospered, and Trajan assigned it colonial status as Colonia Concordia Ulpia Trajana Augusta Frugifera Hadrumetina.
