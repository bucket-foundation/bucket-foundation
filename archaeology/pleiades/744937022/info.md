## Description

The location of the Late Roman and Visigothic suburbium beyond the Roman circus. Transition from suburban villas to a large-scale Visigothic expansion, which included various churches and the Praetorium, or Royal Palace. Destroyed during the ninth century. Excavations carried out in the early 2000s.
