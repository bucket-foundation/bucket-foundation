## Description

Mentioned in Ptolemy and in a Roman-era itinerary document, Octaviolca has long been thought to have been located at or near modern Camesa (Valdeola) in Spain. More recent excavations have identified Roman structures immediately south of Camesa that are now widely regarded as the proper site.
