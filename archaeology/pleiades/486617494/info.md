## Description

A sanctuary north of the Acropolis of Athens, consisting of a platform of the Archaic period, and 200 miniature <i>skyphoi</i> from the 3rd century BC. It is disputed whether it was an independent sanctuary or part of the sanctuary of Eros and Aphrodite.
