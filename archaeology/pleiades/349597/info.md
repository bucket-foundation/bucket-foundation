## Description

An ancient place in north Africa for which a number of name variants are attested. Its ancient location is uncertain; it may perhaps be identifiable with one or another ancient sites on the island of Djerba: Henchir el Khaway (thus BAtlDir) or Henchir Bourgou (Tahar 2022).
