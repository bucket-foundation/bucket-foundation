## Description

Olshausen describes at Horoztepe (Berg) the remains of a fortified settlement (Burg-ruinen) with Hellenistic stairways, cisterns, and rock tombs, but walls of a "later date". Horoztepe, located at modern Bergaa in Turkey's Tokat province, is best known as the site of a Bronze Age settlement and associated cemetery of the Hatti culture. Only cursory publications of the mid-20th-century excavations have appeared; none mention a later fortification, and none are visible in modern, publicly available aerial/satellite imagery.
