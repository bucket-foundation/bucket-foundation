## Description

The attested Hellenistic settlement of "Taulara" may be identified with a now-invisible castle site at modern Horoztepe in Turkey's Tokat province (BAtlas, following Olshausen who argues for a location in the area between Tokat and Horoztepe, but does not commit to the latter).
