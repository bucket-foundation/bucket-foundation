## Description

This vicus is mentioned on Roman inscriptions discovered in the area of modern Lecce dei Marsi in Italy. The precise location of the town is not known, but it is probably not to be found at the medieval foundation known now as "Lecce Vecchio", which was abandoned after an earthquake in the early 20th century.
