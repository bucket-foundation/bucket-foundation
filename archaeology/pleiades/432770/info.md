## Description

Castro dei Volsci is an Italian comune located in the province of Frosinone. Its name - Castro dei Volsci - dates only from 1816. There is evidence for pre-Roman and Roman settlement in the area, including the polygonal masonry circuit of fortifications on nearby Montenero.
