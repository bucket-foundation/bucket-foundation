## Description

A Vespasianic domus in Ostia Antica, built ca. 70-75 CE, situated outside the Porta Marina, and north of the continuation of the Decumanus Maximus. It had a slightly concave façade, comprising six shops and an entrance corridor leading to an interior peristyle garden.
