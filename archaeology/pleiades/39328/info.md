## Description

A region on the Red Sea coast of the Arabian peninsula, mentioned in a north-to-south list of toponyms by Pliny the Elder, and by other Greco-Roman sources. BAtlas identified it as lying in the area of the modern al-Lith governorate in Saudi Arabia.
