## Description

An extensive (50+ hectares) ancient settlement located in the modern French commune of Pierrefonds, lying between Pierrefonds itself and Mont Berny to the northeast. Variously referred to in the modern literature as "Pierrefonds", "La Ville des Gaules", "Mont Berny", or "Saint-Étienne-Roilaye" (another nearby modern town), the ancient name of the vicus is unknown.
