## Description

An ancient river mentioned only by Pausanias, who describes it, along with the Elektra, as lying between Andania and Kyparissia in Messenia. RE declines to identify it with a specific modern river other than to assert that it must have been a tributary of the ancient Balyra. BAtlas 58 labels a river stretch corresponding to the upper course of the modern Mavrozoumena river as mapped and named in OpenStreetMap. In BAtlDir, the corresponding modern name / location is given as "Malthis ", a name that does not appear in GeoNames, OpenStreetMap, or Wikidata, but that is clearly related to Malthi, a modern village near this river stretch. The BAtlas arrangement makes the Koios a tributary of the Elektra, which is then a tributary of the Balyra.
