## Description

Remains of an ancient settlement of Roman date, recorded by Gsell (AAA, whence BAtlas). The location should lie within a kilometer of the BAtlas coordinates: a point to the east of Bouchiba, between it and the modern road connecting Golea and Tassamert el Foucani (Tassamert municipal territory, Bordj Bou Arreridj province, Algeria).
