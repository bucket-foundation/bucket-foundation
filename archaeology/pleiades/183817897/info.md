## Description

Laoues 2015 identifies this location as the source of the aqueduct that supplied Tubusuctu from the northwest. It is located between modern Tighilt Messaoud and Takhlicht in the municipal territory of Fenaia Ilmaten in Algeria's Kabylia province.
