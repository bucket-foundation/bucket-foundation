## Description

A sanctuary -- with remains of at least two successive temples (6th C BCE and 4th C BCE), a great altar, and votive deposits -- located on the modern Gremoulias saddle, 3.5km SE of Kalavryta in the northern Peloponnese. The site is not known from ancient literature. Scholars speculate that the deity worshiped may have been Artemis and the site may have been an extra-urban sanctuary of nearby Kynaitha.
