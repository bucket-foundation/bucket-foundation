## Description

The Kragos denoted a coastal massif in modern Turkey's Seydikemer district (Muğla Province). It seems to have comprised the peaks known today as Avdancık, Sandak Dağ, and their contiguous neighbors. It was seen as distinct from the larger Antikragos massif, which lay immediately north of the Kragos on the other side of the modern Avlan gorge.
