## Description

Fowler discusses a fragmentary Roman milestone found along the road from Hippo to Calama that mentions a "Via per Alpes Numidicas". The Clauss / Slaby epigraphic database (EDCS-04001742) lists the findspot as Ain Berda in the province of Africa proconsularis and dates the inscription to ca. 150 CE.
