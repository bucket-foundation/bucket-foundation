New in this build:
- Games: every round ends with 1 to 3 stars and your personal best. Flags plays 10-word rounds with a combo. Words you miss come back in your next study session.
- Content stays in the app: videos and links open inside it. Radio keeps playing across tabs with a mini player and lock screen controls, and Listen starts with stations near you.
- Family and friends: Account > Family and friends. Invite family or friends by link, see each other's streak, languages and strength chart, cheer or nudge, set a weekly goal.
- Push notifications for cheers and nudges. Allow notifications when asked.
- Translate reads in dark mode and has a cleaner layout.
- Letter sounds game: each new letter bounces in with its example word, a big play button, a slow voice and a mic to say it back. The quiz plays the word, hides the hint until you ask, answers on one tap and fills in the missing letter.
- Daily reminders turn on when notifications are already allowed.

Please try: play a Flags round, miss a word and see it in your next session; play a radio station near you and lock the phone.
