Screenshot sharing: take a screenshot on any screen. A share sheet opens with a bucket.foundation link for that screen (a word in Study or Translate, a language set, a course or a lesson). Send it to yourself in Messages and check the preview card.

Open a shared link on your phone: it should open the same word, language set, course or lesson in Bucket.

App Clip: in TestFlight, open the App Clip links for a word, a language set and a lesson. Each one shows the shared content, tap a word to hear it, and offers Get Bucket.

Also in this build: one Sign in button with Apple or email, Stop while speaking, live text while you speak, the placement picker for the languages you know, and Redo placement.
