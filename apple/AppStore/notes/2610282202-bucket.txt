New in this build:
- Five tabs: Today, Learn, Games, Content, Account. Learn switches between Languages and Subjects.
- Games: Match, Memory and Flags with the words you have studied.
- Content: read a Wikipedia article in each of your languages, listen to radio from those countries, find shows to watch.
- Family and friends: Account > Family and friends. Invite family or friends by link, see each other's streak, languages and strength chart, cheer or nudge, set a weekly goal.
- Push notifications for cheers and nudges. Allow notifications when asked.
- Translate reads in dark mode and has a cleaner layout.
- New letters: each new letter or character gets a card that shows it and plays its sound before the quiz asks for it.
- Science cards render math formulas.

Please try: invite someone to a family group, cheer them, and open Translate in dark mode.
