What to test
- Sign in: one Sign in button under Account opens Apple or email. Sign in with Apple now works.
- Speaking: your words appear as you talk. Tap Stop to finish early, or it stops about a second after you go quiet.
- Translate on Today: say a whole sentence and tap Stop to translate it.
- Placement: pick the languages you already know. Only those get a level test; the rest start from the first words.
- Redo placement: open a language set, tap Progress, then Redo placement next to a language.
- Match card: the word changes between sessions instead of repeating the same one.
