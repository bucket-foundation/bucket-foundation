Shot 1 comes from `-polingual.onboarded NO -onboardingStep 0`. Shots 2 to 5 come from `-polingualOpen -polingualDemo 60 -openLang en:fr,es,it:<home|study|place|system>` on a fresh install. Shot 3 adds `-forceKind choose`. Shot 6 is the paywall on a fresh install with `-polingual.onboarded YES`.
