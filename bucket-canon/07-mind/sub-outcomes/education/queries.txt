# Bloom 1984, The 2 Sigma Problem: one-to-one tutoring plus mastery
# feedback moves the average tutored student two standard deviations above
# a conventionally taught control; names the ceiling group instruction
# chases. Depends on the retrieval-practice/testing-effect mechanism.
10.3102/0013189x013006004

# Kulik, Kulik & Bangert-Drowns 1990, 108-study meta-analysis tempering
# Bloom's two-sigma figure to about 0.5 SD for group-based mastery
# learning; the large-sample replication check on Bloom's claim.
10.3102/00346543060002265

# VanLehn 2011, meta-analysis finding step-based intelligent tutoring
# systems approach human-tutoring effect size (~0.75-1 SD), versus
# answer-only systems (~0.3 SD); step-based feedback is frequent
# retrieval-and-correction, the same mechanism at system scale.
10.1080/00461520.2011.611369

# Kulik & Fletcher 2016, larger, more recent intelligent-tutoring-systems
# meta-analysis (50 evaluations, ~0.66 SD average), flags that weaker
# study designs inflate the pooled estimate.
10.3102/0034654315581420

# Promoted 2026-09-10, canon intake pass two (intake/ros-canon-promotion-2),
# from the same corpus.

# Bastani et al. 2025, unrestricted GPT-4 access on math practice raised
# practice scores but lowered unassisted exam performance; a hint-only
# guardrail kept the practice gain and removed the exam harm.
10.1073/pnas.2422633122

# Vaccaro, Almaatouq & Malone 2024, systematic review and meta-analysis
# finding human-AI combination beats the better of the two alone on
# decision tasks but not on content-creation tasks; depends on the
# information-foraging foundation rather than the testing effect.
10.1038/s41562-024-02024-1

# Kestin et al. 2025, randomized within-class crossover RCT finding
# students using a pedagogically structured AI tutor learned more, in
# less time, than students in a research-validated active-learning
# session.
10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6

# Bansal et al. 2021, controlled study finding AI explanations that
# raised participants' stated trust did not raise team accuracy in step;
# depends on the information-foraging foundation instead of the testing effect.
10.1145/3411764.3445717

# De Simone et al. 2025 (World Bank, Nigeria), six-week structured
# AI-tutor access producing close to two years of typical learning
# progress on an English-proficiency measure, concentrated among
# lower-performing students and girls.
10.1596/1813-9450-11125

# Kosmyna et al. 2025, EEG study finding progressively weaker neural
# connectivity and essay-content recall in the LLM-assisted condition;
# depends on the cognitive-offloading foundation (Sparrow et al. 2011),
# not the testing effect. canon_score 30, below the informational floor;
# see CANON_INDEX.md's override note.
10.48550/arxiv.2506.08872

# Wang et al. 2024, Tutor CoPilot: large field deployment finding tutors
# with real-time AI suggestions produced higher student mastery, largest
# gains for less-certified or less-experienced tutors. canon_score 10,
# below the informational floor; see CANON_INDEX.md's override note.
10.48550/arxiv.2410.03017

# Chen and Yang 2019, meta-analysis revisiting project-based learning's
# effect on academic achievement and testing moderators of that effect;
# depends on the cognitive-load foundation.
10.1016/j.edurev.2018.11.001

# Furtak, Seidel, Iverson and Briggs 2012, 37-study meta-analysis of
# inquiry-based science teaching finding teacher-led activities
# outperformed student-led ones by about 0.40 SD; depends on the
# cognitive-load foundation.
10.3102/0034654312457206

# Lazonder and Harmsen 2016, 72-study meta-analysis of guidance in
# inquiry-based learning, finding facilitative effects on learning
# activities, performance success, and learning outcomes; depends on the
# cognitive-load foundation.
10.3102/0034654315627366

# Kirschner, Sweller and Clark 2006, the minimal-guidance argument that
# unguided instruction underperforms guided instruction for learners
# without high prior knowledge; the outcome-tier claim the new
# cognitive-load foundation (Sweller 1988) underwrites.
10.1207/s15326985ep4102_1

# Hmelo-Silver, Duncan and Chinn 2007, the direct reply to Kirschner,
# Sweller and Clark 2006, arguing problem-based and inquiry learning are
# extensively scaffolded rather than minimally guided; depends on the
# same cognitive-load foundation the paper it replies to does.
10.1080/00461520701263368

# Grinnell, Dalley and Reisch 2020, national survey finding required
# science-fair participation among uninterested students carried a
# measured research-misconduct rate; depends on the curiosity-and-motivation
# foundation (Deci and Ryan 2000, self-determination theory).
10.1371/journal.pone.0229237

# Wineburg and McGrew 2019, expert/novice study finding fact checkers
# evaluate sources by lateral reading, leaving the page to check other
# sources, while historians and students read vertically and were
# misled by superficial cues; depends on the information-foraging
# foundation.
10.1177/016146811912101102

# Breakstone et al. 2021, national assessment of 3,446 US high school
# students finding broad, consistent struggle across six live-internet
# source-evaluation tasks; depends on the information-foraging foundation.
10.3102/0013189x211017495

# Bangera and Brownell 2014, argues a course-based undergraduate research
# experience reaches a wider, more representative slice of students than
# a selection-gated independent internship; depends on the
# curiosity-and-motivation foundation.
10.1187/cbe.14-06-0099

# Sadler, Burgin, McKinney and Ponjuan 2010, review of 53 studies of
# research apprenticeships scoring which claimed learning outcomes the
# literature supports; depends on the curiosity-and-motivation foundation.
10.1002/tea.20326
