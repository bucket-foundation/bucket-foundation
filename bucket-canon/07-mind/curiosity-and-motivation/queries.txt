# Loewenstein 1994, "The Psychology of Curiosity: A Review and
# Reinterpretation." The primary statement of the information-gap
# account of curiosity: curiosity arises from a perceived gap between
# current and desired knowledge, peaks at intermediate rather than
# extreme uncertainty, and rises when the gap is named and specific
# rather than diffuse. canon_score 70.
10.1037/0033-2909.116.1.75

# Gruber, Gelman and Ranganath 2014, "States of Curiosity Modulate
# Hippocampus-Dependent Learning via the Dopaminergic Circuit." Primary
# experimental result (with fMRI) that a high-curiosity state raises
# memory for the curiosity-satisfying answer and for unrelated material
# encountered nearby, and links the effect to hippocampal and
# dopaminergic-circuit activity. canon_score 75.
10.1016/j.neuron.2014.08.060

# Deci and Ryan 2000, "The 'What' and 'Why' of Goal Pursuits: Human
# Needs and the Self-Determination of Behavior." The founding statement
# of self-determination theory: intrinsic motivation depends on three
# basic psychological needs (autonomy, competence, relatedness), and
# extrinsic motivators divide into controlled and autonomous forms
# depending on whether the person has internalized the externally set
# goal. canon_score 70.
10.1207/s15327965pli1104_01

# Gneezy and Rustichini 2000, "Pay Enough or Don't Pay at All." Primary
# field and lab evidence for a non-monotonic incentive effect: a small
# monetary incentive can perform worse than no incentive, the daycare
# late-fine experiment and a matched lab task. canon_score 70.
10.1162/003355300554917
