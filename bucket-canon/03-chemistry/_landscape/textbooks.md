# Chemistry — landscape textbooks (NOT canon)

Per maintainer decision Q1 (2026-05-01): discipline-standard textbooks that fail the README promotion rule (none is an originator work; "normative" in c3 means standards-body adoption, not popularity) but that a chemistry researcher arriving at Bucket needs to know exist.

Single-file registry. No folder explosion. One paragraph per text. Citation form, current edition, what it actually does, why it is not canon.

---

**Atkins, *Physical Chemistry* (Atkins, de Paula, Keeler, 12th ed., Oxford University Press, 2022, ISBN 978-0-19-884781-6).** The most widely adopted English-language physical-chemistry textbook of the last 40 years. Covers thermodynamics, kinetics, statistical mechanics, quantum chemistry, and spectroscopy at the upper-undergraduate level. Not canon: synthesis of pre-existing originator material, no primary derivation introduced. Useful as a teaching reference and as a way to locate the originator text behind a given concept.

**March, *Advanced Organic Chemistry* (Smith, *March's Advanced Organic Chemistry*, 8th ed., Wiley, 2020, ISBN 978-1-119-37180-9).** The standard reference for organic reaction mechanisms beyond the introductory level. Comprehensive citation index into the primary literature. Not canon: reference compilation, not originator framework.

**Cotton & Wilkinson, *Advanced Inorganic Chemistry* (Cotton, Wilkinson, Murillo, Bochmann, 6th ed., Wiley-Interscience, 1999, ISBN 978-0-471-19957-1).** The standard reference for inorganic chemistry. Builds on Werner's coordination framework (canon, `bonding/coordination/`) without itself being originator. Not canon for the same reason as March.

**Coulson, *Valence* (Coulson, McWeeny, *Coulson's Valence*, 3rd ed., Oxford University Press, 1979, ISBN 978-0-19-855145-3).** Pedagogical synthesis of pre-quantum and post-quantum bonding theory. Demoted from pass-2's tentative inclusion: Coulson is a synthesizer, not the originator of any of the bonding frameworks the book treats. Pauling 1960 is the canon entry.

**Cotton, *Chemical Applications of Group Theory* (3rd ed., Wiley-Interscience, 1990, ISBN 978-0-471-51094-9).** Discipline-standard pedagogical treatment of group theory in chemistry. Demoted from pass-2's tentative inclusion: not originator, not standards-body normative. The originator group-theory texts live in `01-mathematics/`.

**Streitwieser, Heathcock, Kosower, *Introduction to Organic Chemistry* (4th ed., Macmillan, 1992, ISBN 978-0-02-418170-3).** Mechanistically rigorous undergraduate organic textbook. Not canon: pedagogical only.

**Anslyn & Dougherty, *Modern Physical Organic Chemistry* (University Science Books, 2006, ISBN 978-1-891389-31-3).** Modern physical-organic textbook covering Hammett LFER, Marcus theory, frontier orbitals, supramolecular binding. Not canon: synthesis of canon originator works (Hammett, Marcus, Fukui, Pedersen-Cram-Lehn — all in canon proper).

**Szabo & Ostlund, *Modern Quantum Chemistry: Introduction to Advanced Electronic Structure Theory* (Dover reprint of 1989 1st ed., ISBN 978-0-486-69186-2).** The standard graduate textbook for Hartree-Fock, configuration interaction, MBPT, coupled cluster. Not canon: synthesis of the originator papers (Hartree, Fock, Slater, Roothaan, Čížek — all in `quantum-chemistry/computational/`).

**Skoog, West, Holler, Crouch, *Fundamentals of Analytical Chemistry* (10th ed., Cengage, 2022, ISBN 978-0-357-45039-0).** The standard analytical-chemistry undergraduate textbook. Not canon: pedagogical only, no originator framework.

---

**Discipline rule for this file:** entries are added when a chemist would expect Bucket to know about the text and would read its absence as ignorance. Entries are NOT added merely because a text exists or is well-cited. The standard for landscape inclusion is *researcher-discoverability-cost*; the standard for canon inclusion is the README promotion rule. Different bars.

If a future canon-tier framework first appears in one of these textbooks (rare but possible), promote that fragment to the appropriate canon sub-folder with the textbook itself remaining in landscape.
