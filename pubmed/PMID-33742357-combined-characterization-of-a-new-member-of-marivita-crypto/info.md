## Abstract

During our conveying the microbial structures of phycosphere microbiota (PM) derived from diverse marine harmful algal bloom (HAB) dinoflagellates, a new rod-sharped, white-colored cultivable bacterial strain, designated as LZ-15-2, was isolated from the PM of highly toxic Alexandrium catenella LZT09. Phylogenetic analysis of 16S rRNA gene sequence indicated that strain LZ-15-2 belonged to the genus Marivita within the family Rhodobacteraceae, and demonstrated the highest gene similarity of 99.2% to M. cryptomonadis CL-SK44
