## Abstract

Discoidin Domain Receptor 2 (DDR2) is a collagen-activated tyrosine kinase required for bone and tooth development as well as regeneration of calvarial and long bones. Although DDR2 is expressed in alveolar bone, its functions in this site have not been previously examined. The present study used global and conditional knockout approaches and lineage tracing to examine the functions of DDR2 in alveolar bone formation during tooth socket healing.
