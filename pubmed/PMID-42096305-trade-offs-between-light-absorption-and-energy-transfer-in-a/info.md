## Abstract

Purple bacteria are a diverse group of photosynthetic organisms that can capture and convert light energy with high quantum efficiency across a variety of ecological niches. They absorb light via an array of antenna proteins, primarily light-harvesting complex 2 (LH2), and rapidly transport the energy to the reaction center, where charge separation occurs.
