## Abstract

Chronic stress is associated with cardiovascular disease (CVD) risk and elevated amygdala activity. Previous research suggests a plausible connection between amygdala activity, hematopoietic tissue activity, and cardiovascular events; however, the underlying biological mechanisms linking these relationships are incompletely understood. Chronic stress is thought to modulate epigenomic modifications.
