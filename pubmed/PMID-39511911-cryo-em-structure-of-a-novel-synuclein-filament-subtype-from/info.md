## Abstract

Multiple system atrophy (MSA) is a progressive neurodegenerative disease characterized by accumulation of α-synuclein cross-β amyloid filaments in the brain. Previous structural studies of these filaments by cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM) revealed three discrete folds distinct from α-synuclein filaments associated with other neurodegenerative diseases.
