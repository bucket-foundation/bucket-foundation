## Abstract

Abnormal oxidative metabolism and tissue hypoxia could exacerbate multiple sclerosis. Research in both animal models and people with multiple sclerosis shows increased inflammation, reduced cerebral blood flow, damage to mitochondria, and loss of myelin. Understanding such abnormalities is crucial for developing effective treatments. In this study, we apply a multimodal imaging approach-combining near-infrared spectroscopy (NIRS) with 9.4T MRI-to investigate the cortical gray matter of the experimental autoimmune encephalomyelitis (EAE) mouse model of autoimmune inflammatory diseases.
