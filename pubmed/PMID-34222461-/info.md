## Abstract

Early diagnosis and appropriate antibiotic treatment are important to survival of  A 102-year-old man suffered from high fever with chill after hematochezia. Tentative diagnoses were lower gastrointestinal hemorrhage and localized peritonitis. Meropenem and ornidazole were the empirical therapy. The patient did not respond and developed multiple system dysfunction even after teicoplanin was added to the therapy.  More awareness of listeriosis should be raised. Linezolid might be an option for listeriosis in elderly people with serious complications.
