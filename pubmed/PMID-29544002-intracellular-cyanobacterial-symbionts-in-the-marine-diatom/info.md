## Abstract

The diatom Climacodium frauenfeldianum Grunow was collected in the tropical Atlantic and Pacific Oceans. Observations with epifluorescence microscopy revealed that this diatom contained coccoid symbionts (2.5-3.5 μm) with a typical cyanobacterial fluorescence in addition to that of their own chloroplasts.
