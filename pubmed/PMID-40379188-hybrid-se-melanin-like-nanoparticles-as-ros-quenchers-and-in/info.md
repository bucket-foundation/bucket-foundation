## Abstract

Hybrid selenium/melanin-like nanoparticles offer different therapeutic strategies against amyloid aggregation. In this study, novel Se-based nanostructures are synthesized, characterized, and preliminarily employed as modulators of amyloid aggregation. In detail, two types of Se NPs are tested: one containing only Se(0), named Se NPs, and the second hybrid Se/melanin structures, indicated as SeMel NPs.
