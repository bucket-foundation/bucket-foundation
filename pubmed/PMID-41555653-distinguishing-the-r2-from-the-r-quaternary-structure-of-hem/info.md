## Abstract

A problem in hemoglobin studies is separating the allosteric and affinity factors that account for differences in the oxygen partial pressure at 50% saturation among various hemoglobins. Like oxygen binding to the hemes, the reaction of Ellman's reagent, 5,5'-dithiobis(2-nitrobenzoate)-DTNB for short-with the 93β sulfhydryl groups of hemoglobin is a reversible process. However, the reaction of DTNB with Cys93β of hemoglobin is devoid of allosteric factors. We present the results of equilibrium studies of the reaction of DTNB with thirty-eight heme-liganded hemoglobins between pH 5.6 and 9.
