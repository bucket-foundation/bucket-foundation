## Description

In this podcast episode, we spoke with geologist Robert Schneiker and got to hear his thoughts on the Great Sphinx of Giza, millions of years of weathering of the limestone at Giza — not precipitation induced erosion, why Robert Schoch is wrong, debating Randall Carlson, megafloods, why there’s no evidence for a lost civilization and so much more.  This is a great conversation you don’t want to miss!
