## Description

Join legendary physicist Murray Gell-Mann — Nobel Prize winner, co-founder of the Santa Fe Institute, and pioneer of quarks — in this fascinating and intimate 1999 conversation with entrepreneur Nick DeWolf at the Aspen Center for Physics.

In this wide-ranging interview, Gell-Mann reflects on:

 • His early involvement with the Aspen Physics Center and the Aspen Institute
 • The interplay of simplicity and complexity in science
 • His thoughts on anti-ballistic missile defense and Cold War politics
 • Building a scientific community and a home in Aspen
 • The cultural and scientific shifts of the 20th century
 • The need for interdisciplinary science and unity across fields
 • His advocacy for science education and concern over anti-intellectual trends

Filled with humor, insight, and sharp commentary, this rare oral history offers a candid glimpse into Gell-Mann’s life, ideas, and his enduring love for Aspen.
