Doctoral Research Society of the John Paul II Catholic University of Lublin presents the third lecture within the series "Meet the Masters"! Our guest is prof. Robert S. Hatten (Marlene & Morton Meyerson Professor in Music at The University of Texas at Austin, USA), who presents a lecture entitled "Fruitful Intersections between Music Semiotics and Other Disciplines" (23.01.2023). 
 
----- 
Robert S. Hatten joined the Music Theory faculty at the University of Texas in Fall 2011 as Professor of Music Theory, having taught previously at Indiana University. His first book,"Musical Meaning in Beethoven: Markedness, Correlation, and Interpretation" (Indiana University Press, 1994), was co-recipient of the Wallace Berry Publication Award from the Society for Music Theory in 1997. His second book, "Interpreting Musical Gestures, Topics, and Tropes: Mozart, Beethoven, Schubert" (Indiana University Press, 2004), helped launch the book series “Musical Meaning and Interpretation,” for which professor Hatten serves as general editor. Robert Hatten served as President (2017-19) and Vice-President of the Society for Music Theory (2005-07), and President of the Semiotic Society of America (2007-08). His research interests focus on semiotic theories of musical meaning (including agency, expressive genres, gesture, style, topics, tropes, and narrativity), performance and analysis (as pianist), music and the poetic text (as poet), and twentieth-century opera (as librettist and composer). He has given invited papers and keynotes across North America and Europe, and extended lecture series in Poland, Finland, Spain, and Mexico. 
 
----- 
The series of lectures "Meet the Masters" was organized by the Doctoral Research Society of the John Paul II Catholic University of Lublin and supervised by Professor Małgorzata Gamrat. The series was aimed at all PhD students, academics, researchers, and students, especially those interested in interdisciplinary research.
