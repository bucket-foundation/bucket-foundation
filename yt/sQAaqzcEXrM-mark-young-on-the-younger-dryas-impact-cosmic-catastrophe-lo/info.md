## Description

Australian geoarchaeologist Mark Young joins Cliff Dunning on Earth Ancients to discuss one of the most controversial discoveries in modern science — the Younger Dryas Impact Hypothesis. Young, a member of the Comet Research Group, explores evidence of a cosmic impact 12,800 years ago that may have triggered global wildfires, flooding, and the sudden collapse of Ice Age megafauna and early human populations.

Mark explains how platinum anomalies, nanodiamonds, and black mat layers reveal a catastrophic event that reshaped Earth’s climate. He also delves into possible impact sites, including Lake Nipigon and the Carolina Bays, and discusses why mainstream archaeology resists this growing body of evidence.

The conversation extends to Gobekli Tepe, Atlantis, Gunung Padang, and the global signs of a lost advanced civilization wiped out by a cosmic cataclysm. Could ancient myths preserve memories of this world-changing event? Young breaks down the science, the resistance, and the implications for human history.
