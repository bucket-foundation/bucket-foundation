## Description

Professor Martin Saar discusses Adorno and Hegel, with Billie Alexopoulos, Lea Gekle, and Bernard E. Harcourt at the public seminar Hegel 13/13 at the EHESS Campus Condorcet in Paris. This lecture is part of the Hegel 13/13 series here: https://hegel1313.law.columbia.edu/

For a full length introduction to this seminar with Martin Saar, watch here on YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OrkXhXUJ_GY

For more information about the seminar with Martin Saar: https://hegel1313.law.columbia.edu/5-13/

For more information about the 13/13 seminar series, and prior seminars, please visit the website here: https://cccct.law.columbia.edu/content/13-13

*********** 
In this segment, we explore the lasting shadow of Hegel on Theodor Adorno and the Frankfurt School with Professor Martin Saar.
