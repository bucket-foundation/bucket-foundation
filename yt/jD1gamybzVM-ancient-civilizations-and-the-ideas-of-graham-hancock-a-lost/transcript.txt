The hilltop in Turkey is dark now. The
stars above it are the same stars that
the builders would have known shifted
only slightly by the slow procession
that measures the centuries in fractions
of a degree. Somewhere far to the south,
the Sphinx faces east into a night it
has faced 10 million times before, and
the plateau holds its geometry against
the sky. Let these images go where they
will as you drift. You have walked a
long way tonight, traveler, through deep
time and quiet ruins, and questions
older than any living answer. There is
nothing more required of you. The stones
will keep. The sea will keep. The past,
whatever it contains is patient enough
to wait for morning. Close your eyes and
let the stillness have you. You have
