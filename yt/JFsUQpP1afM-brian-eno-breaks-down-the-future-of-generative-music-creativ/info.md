## Description

Brian Eno joins this in-depth conversation on generative music, ambient composition, creativity, technology, and the making of the groundbreaking Scape app. In this full interview, Eno dives into the origins of generative systems, his early ambient works, and how he approaches art as both an engineer and a gardener — designing systems that evolve, react, and make music on their own.

From the history of Terry Riley, Steve Reich, and early minimalism, to the creation of apps like Bloom, Trope, and Scape, Eno explains how digital tools reshaped his artistic process and opened new possibilities for music that is “as ignorable as it is interesting.”

This wide-ranging conversation explores:
• The roots of generative and ambient music
• How Scape was engineered to create infinite compositions
• The collaboration with Peter Chilvers
• Designing creative systems vs. composing traditionally
• Why technology can expand artistic instinct rather than replace it
• How musicians, artists, writers, and developers can use ambient sound
• The philosophy behind self-organizing art and creative environments
• Eno’s approach to sound design, mood, rules, limitations, and interaction
