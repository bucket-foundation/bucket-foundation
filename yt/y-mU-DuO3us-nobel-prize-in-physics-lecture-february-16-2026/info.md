## Description

Rob Schoelkopf, Yale University, "The 2025 Nobel Prize: an Introduction to Quantum Circuits"
This year's Nobel Prize was awarded to John Clarke, John Martinis, and our colleague Michel Devoret, for their seminal work in the mid-1980s on quantum tunneling and energy quantization in superconducting circuits. Coming a decade or so before Shor's algorithm and the beginning of quantum computing research as an experimental discipline, this work was nonetheless a fore-runner of superconducting qubits and all the progress which has since ensued. I will give a simple introduction to the quantization of electrical circuits, review the results from the 1980s for which the prize was awarded, and show a few highlights of what has been achieved going forward.
