## Description

Discover the limitless possibilities that await within you.
Join us on an extraordinary 3-day adventure into the world of Holotropic Breathwork, a transformative breathing technique pioneered by the renowned Stanislav Grof. 
Follow the inspiring journey of Ângela battling anxiety and panic attacks as she embarks on an odyssey of self-discovery and healing.

Guided by a dedicated psychotherapist and a team of skilled facilitators and psychologists, she explores the depths of her psyche and taps into the transcendental realms of transpersonal psychology. Through the power of her own breath and the profound insights gained from this remarkable breathing technique, she undergoes a profound inner transformation.

Holotropic Breathwork offers not just a therapeutic approach, but a spiritual voyage, allowing participants to connect with their inner healer, confront their fears, and unlock their personal empowerment. Dive into the world of psychedelics and self-exploration as we reveal the beauty of this profound modality for healing and self-discovery.
