## Description

Graham Hancock is a British writer and journalist best known for his theories about ancient civilizations, lost knowledge, and the possibility that human history is far older and more complex than mainstream archaeology accepts. Graham Hancock has written numerous books spanning ancient history, lost civilizations, psychedelics, and alternative archaeology. Some of his well-known books include: "Fingerprints of the Gods" (1995), "The Message of the Sphinx" (1996, with Robert Bauval), "Heaven’s Mirror" (1998, with Santha Faiia), "Underworld: The Mysterious Origins of Civilization" (2002), "Magicians of the Gods" (2015), "America Before: The Key to Earth’s Lost Civilization" (2019) and "Visionary: The Mysterious Origins of Human Consciousness" (2022), A revised and expanded edition of "Supernatural", adding new material on psychedelics and consciousness. Graham also hosts 2 seasons of the popular Netflix series, Ancient Apocalypse, which was the number 1 program across the world for several weeks.
