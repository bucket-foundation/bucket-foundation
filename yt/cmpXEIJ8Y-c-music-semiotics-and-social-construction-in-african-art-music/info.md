## Description

Dr Grace Olaoluwa (University of Lagos)

Dr. Oladele Ayorinde (Project Convener) 

Music semiotics and the application of methodologies and theoretical models have been a subject of discourse in African music scholarship. There are pervasive ideologies of music as a superstructural phenomenon. Also, there is the understanding in musicological circles that music contributes actively to our social experiences and identities and is, therefore, socially relevant. How are musical meanings negotiated and understood as social constructions? What are the contradictions and tensions in constructing musical meanings in African music compositions? What might music semiotics contribute to the analytical study and understanding of African art music works? 

 In this presentation, Dr. Grace Olaoluwa draws from discourse on semiotics to explore African art music studies in Nigeria. In so doing, she argues for a pluralistic approach to music analysis, negotiating ways understanding of social meanings could render African art music compositions intelligible while also advancing music scholarship in Africa and beyond.
