Discover the history and appreciate the artistic beauty of flamenco with guest lecturer Lucy Hallman Russell as she presents “Flamenco and Guitars in Southern Spain.” A passionate cross-cultural historian, Lucy has enjoyed a long career as associate professor of music and music history at the Würzburg Conservatory and the University of Music Würzburg.
