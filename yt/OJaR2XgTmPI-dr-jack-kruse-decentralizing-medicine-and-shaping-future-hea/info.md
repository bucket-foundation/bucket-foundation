## Description

Dr. Jack Kruse, neurosurgeon, CEO of Optimized Life, and member of the American Association of Neurological Surgeons, the Congress of Neurologic Surgeons, and Age Management Medicine Group, chats with host Dr. Ted Achacoso about how the COVID-19 pandemic influenced the shift towards decentralized medicine, the impact of centralized medicine on naturopathic doctors, chiropractors, and Eastern medicine practitioners and how it can be addressed by decentralized medicine, the implications of technology on public health and how it shapes the future of healthcare, and more.
