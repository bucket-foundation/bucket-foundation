## Description

Murray Gell-Mann briefly shares with us how the fundamental laws of physics, combined with random statistical distributions can generate emergent systems which are non-random and which adapt in recognizable patterns and rules as emergent complex adaptive systems. These systems include the laws that govern the evolution of life, the processing of computers and the responses of biological systems to stimuli that, among other things, power our immune systems and ultimately allow the brain to process information into a working mind.
