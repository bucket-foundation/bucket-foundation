## Description

As explained in the yogic culture, the entire existence is seen as a complex amalgamation of sounds. The three root sounds that come out of this are “Aa”, “Oo” and “Mm”. Uttered together, these form “Aum”.  “Aum” activates and energises various sections in the body, reverberating through the entire system. Furthermore, daily utterance of this sound can bring peace and balance and is known to relieve the practitioner from numerous physical and mental ailments with regular practice. It is also effective with animals. 

This Deep Tibetan Aum Chanting series is made during the period of Coronavirus in 2020, and the Mask off image symbolizes the strength we get by chanting Aum regularly and  to prevent disease and restore health.
