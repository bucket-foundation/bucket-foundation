pyramid. It gave them a framework for
understanding what they were doing and
why. Let the stars continue their slow
processional drift. The celestial north
pole tracing its 26,000year circle
through the sky. The constellations
gradually shifting their positions
relative to the solstice points. The
great clock of the heavens ticking at a
pace that makes human history seem
instantaneous.
All of this is still happening. The
procession continues. The stars drift.
The pyramid stands. And you are here at
the end of a long night of wondering
somewhere in the world at your
particular hour in your particular life.
You are part of the long tradition of
people who have wondered about the great
pyramid. And you are part of the even
longer tradition of people who have
wondered about their place in the
universe. Who have looked up at the
stars and felt the question without
being able to articulate it. who have
built things larger than themselves in
the attempt to say something that
ordinary life cannot contain.
That tradition is what you are, not the
individual person lying in the dark. The
tradition, the long human impulse to pay
attention to the things that matter
most, to build something that will
outlast you, to send something forward
into a future you cannot see. The
builders of the great pyramid trusted
the future with something important. You
received it. That is enough. That is in
its quiet way extraordinary.
Let your breathing slow. There is
nothing you need to understand right
now. The pyramid will still be in the
desert when you wake up. The sphinx will
still be sitting. The stars will have
moved a few degrees in their nightly
ark, but Orion will still be there, or
setting, or not yet risen, depending on
the season and the hour, ready to be
found again. The questions will be
exactly where you left them. Patient,
waiting, and I will still be here in the
quiet, wondering about very old things.
Sleep well.
