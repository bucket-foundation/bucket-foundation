## Description

J. Robert Oppenheimer, the wartime Director of the Manhattan Project's laboratory at Los Alamos, talks about the legendary Danish physicist Niels Bohr.  It is believed the talk was given in Los Alamos in 1964 - not long after Bohr's death and only a few years before Oppenheimer's demise.  Oppenheimer is introduced by his successor, Los Alamos Scientific Laboratory Director Norris E. Bradbury.  

Oppenheimer typically displayed a very serious demeanor when he was recorded.  During this lecture, however, Oppenheimer is a bit more relaxed - he even turns audio technical problems into jokes. A quick glimpse at a rare side of a very complex man...
