Kosmographia Ep027 The Randall Carlson Podcast with the Snake Brothers – Kyle and Russ, Normal Guy Mike and GCREX admin Bradley, from 04/13/20. Could PGM delivery have caused the genetic changes that were discovered around the area of the 1908 Tunguska aerial blast? We look at images from the earliest expeditions to the site by Leonid Kulik, and compare some of the effects of the blast to the enigmatic Carolina Bays…
