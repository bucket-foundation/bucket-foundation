## Description

Niels Bohr didn’t just transform physics — he transformed the way we think about reality itself.
In this episode, we explore the remarkable journey of the Danish physicist who reshaped our understanding of the atom, introduced the groundbreaking principle of complementarity, challenged Einstein in one of the greatest scientific debates in history, and influenced the world during the development of nuclear science.

From his 1913 atomic model to his role in the Copenhagen Institute, from his wartime escape to his advocacy for scientific openness, this episode reveals Bohr as both a scientific visionary and a deeply human thinker.

Whether you're a student, researcher, historian, or simply curious about the origins of quantum theory, this conversation provides context, clarity, and insight drawn from authoritative research and documented historical sources.
