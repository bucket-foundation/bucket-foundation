## Description

In this full talk, Alan Watts unpacks the paradox at the heart of self-improvement: the mind cannot truly control the mind. With humor and clarity, he moves between theology, Buddhism, and modern philosophy to show how the search for grace, virtue, or a “higher self” often circles back into the very ego we are trying to transcend. Along the way, Watts explores the futility of forcing self-transformation, the dangers of righteousness, and the freedom that comes when we stop interfering and allow awareness to unfold on its own.
