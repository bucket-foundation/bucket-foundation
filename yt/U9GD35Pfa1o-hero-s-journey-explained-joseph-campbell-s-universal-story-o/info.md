Mythologist Joseph Campbell presents the "monomyth" or hero's journey motif that underlies all myths and stories no matter their epoch or culture. Dr. Miles Neale explains the stages of the hero's journey as a psychological encounter with one's shadow, in order to uncover one's treasure. This pithy explanation helps orient you like a map to the terrain, risks, landmarks, and destination of the human odyssey of healing, transformation, and self-actualization. Suitable for those interested in spiritual practice, psychedelics, and sacred pilgrimage.

Buddhist psychotherapist Dr. Miles Neale traces the steps of the universal monomyth, guiding you through the major milestones of the hero's path, from answering the call to adventure, slaying your demon, finding the treasure and returning to the world with the elixir.

Be called upon and moved to begin your own epic journey of a lifetime. Become what you were always meant to be, a hero that saves the world.
