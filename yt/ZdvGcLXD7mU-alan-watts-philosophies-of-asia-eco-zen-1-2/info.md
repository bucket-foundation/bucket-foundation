## Description

In Philosophies of Asia's "Eco Zen," Alan Watts takes us for an eye-opening exploration into Zen philosophy – a conversation that challenges conventional notions of self, existence, and reality.  He delves into the teachings of Zen Buddhism without the usual dogma.  

Alan uncovers the historical roots of Zen, its migration from China to Japan, and its profound influence on art, culture, and even contemporary life beyond its origins.  We quickly learn that Buddhism, especially in the Zen form, is not merely a religious label—it’s more akin to a transformative process.

Alan touches on the peculiar methods of Zen teaching—where rather than spoon-feeding knowledge, the focus is on self-discovery, on revealing the truth from within through a paradoxical state of purposeful non-doing.
