## Description

This session was originally offered at Grief and Death Festival 2022 in Turkey.

Filiz Telek and Charles Eisenstein engage in a conversation about how the myths of modernity shape our perception of self and a sense of separation which leads humanity's struggle to dominate nature and conquer death. As the systems of power over and control collapse, how can grief and awareness of death help us remember our interconnectedness and life thrives in a healthy web of relationships? What kind of narrative would shake us out of our cultural denial of and warfare with death? The conversation is a gentle reminder of noticing and acknowledging the felt-sense of grief in our being and to let it guide and transform us in times of crisis.
