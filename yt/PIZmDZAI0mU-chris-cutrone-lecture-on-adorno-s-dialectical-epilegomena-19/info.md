## Description

Chris Cutrone contextualizes and explains Theodor W. Adorno's dialectical epilegomena 1968-69, building upon prior lectures on Adorno's book Negative Dialectics and essays on the negative dialectic in Marxism, Georg Lukacs’s “The phenomenon of reification” and “The standpoint of the proletariat,” parts 1 and 3 of “Reification and the consciousness of the proletariat” from History and Class Consciousness (1923), and Karl Korsch’s 1923 essay on “Marxism and philosophy.” From Cutrone’s Summer 2024 course on Marx and Marxism at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago.
