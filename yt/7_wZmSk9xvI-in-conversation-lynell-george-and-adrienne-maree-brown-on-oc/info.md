## Description

In "A Handful of Earth, A Handful of Sky: The World of Octavia E. Butler," journalist and essayist Lynell George offers a blueprint for a creative life from the perspective of award-winning science-fiction writer and “MacArthur Genius” Octavia E. Butler. On March 4, 2021 George discussesed the book, her years of research, and the great resurgence of interest in Butler with writer, doula, and pleasure activist adrienne maree brown, co-editor of "Octavia's Brood: Science Fiction Stories from Social Justice Movements."
