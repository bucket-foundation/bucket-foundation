## Description

Signum Crucis—“The Sign of the Cross”—is a solemn Gregorian chant invoking the power, protection, and presence of the Holy Trinity through the most ancient and sacred gesture of Christian devotion. For the Templar Knights, the Sign of the Cross was not mere ritual—it was their shield, their vow, their battle cry.

This chant reflects that same conviction. It begins not with noise, but with stillness. It grows not in might, but in grace. It is a hymn of surrender, strength, and sanctity—marking the soul for Christ in every breath and step.

Let Signum Crucis be your morning invocation, your armor in trials, and your resting peace.

Prayer:
In nomine Patris, et Filii, et Spiritus Sancti. Protege me, Domine, signo crucis tuae. Amen.

English Translation:
In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Protect me, O Lord, by the sign of Your cross. Amen.

Latin Lyrics & English Translation:

Latin:
Signum crucis super me,
Et in corde meo in aeternum.
Domine, tu es refugium meum,
Et fortitudo mea in tentatione.

Per crucem tuam salvatus sum,
Per signum tuum confirmatus.
Non timebo mala,
Quia tu mecum es.

Spiritus Sanctus, dirige me,
Et cor meum accende in fide.
In nomine Patris et Filii,
Et Spiritus Sancti. Amen.

English:
The sign of the cross upon me,
And in my heart forever.
Lord, You are my refuge,
And my strength in temptation.

Through Your cross I am saved,
Through Your sign I am strengthened.
I will not fear evil,
For You are with me.

Holy Spirit, guide me,
And ignite my heart in faith.
In the name of the Father and the Son,
And the Holy Spirit. Amen.
