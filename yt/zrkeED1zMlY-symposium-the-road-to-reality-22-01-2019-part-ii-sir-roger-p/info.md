Lecture by Sir Roger Penrose: Three Worlds Model

On the occasion of the Dutch translation of Sir Roger Penrose's monumental book "The Road to Reality: A Complete Guide to the Laws of the Universe", the UvA Institute for Advanced Study (IAS) and Meromorf Press organised a symposium with the author, followed by the book presentation. Programme: http://ias.uva.nl/content/events/events/2019/01/road-to-reality.html.

The symposium was sponsored by the Netherlands Organisation for Scientific Research (NWO), the Korteweg-de Vries Institute for Mathematics, Platform Wiskunde Nederland, Nikhef.
