## Description

Summary of Podcast:
his podcast explores how Alfred North Whitehead and Wolfgang Smith, two influential thinkers, address the "bifurcation of nature"—a modern crisis where the scientific view of a valueless world clashes with human experience. Whitehead proposes Process Philosophy, envisioning God as a "fellow sufferer" with a dipolar nature (Primordial and Consequent), who persuades rather than coerces, integrating science with an evolving, panentheistic divine. In stark contrast, Smith advocates for the Perennial Philosophy, restoring a hierarchical view of reality where God is Esse Ipsum Subsistens (Being Itself), utterly transcendent and immutable, the ultimate vertical cause who sustains existence through an act of continuous creation. The podcast concludes by comparing these two irreconcilable paths, highlighting how Whitehead offers a relatable God for a dynamic cosmos, while Smith champions a return to a pre-modern vision of absolute divine transcendence.
