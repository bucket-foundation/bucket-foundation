## Description

In the early 90s, theories about the correlation between Giza and the stars were becoming more and more popular, but are the shaft systems in the Great Pyramid actually designed to be “star shafts” for the soul of the King? Was there a unified ground plan at Giza for a higher astronomical purpose? This video essay attempts to analyze the presented evidence and claims from 1994’s The Orion Mystery by Robert Bauval and Adrian Gilbert, as well as to address the criticisms put forward by others about this theory, particularly by Edward Krupp of the Griffith Observatory in California.
