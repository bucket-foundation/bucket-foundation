## Description

Eddy Van Hemelrijck enters into conversation with Jeremy Lent (www.jeremylent.com), author of The Web of Meaning: Integrating Science and Traditional Wisdom to Find Our Place in the Universe. Together, they explore how we can move beyond the old story of separation and exploitation toward a new narrative of interconnection, regeneration, and shared meaning.

This rich dialogue weaves fresh insights and perspectives, and is followed by thoughtful questions from the audience, deepening the exploration of how we can reimagine our place in the unfolding web of life.

This event is a co-creation of three Belgian organisations that question the dominant narrative and help nurture a new story that serves the continuing unfolding of life:

1. Regeneratief Leven (Regenerative Life) – http://droomgaard-broechem.be/
2. Re-Story – https://www.re-story.be
3. Emagine Life – https://www.emagine.life, home of Rethinking Economics Antwerpen: https://www.emagine.life/reantwerpen
