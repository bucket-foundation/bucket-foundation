What were Glenn Gould's influences? We are none the wiser after this 1959 interview, in which Gould throws out his usual red herring by naming Schnabel. I'm convinced he does this so that he can maintain the fiction that he was self-taught. Once again he fails to name his teacher, Alberto Guerrero, who he refers to dismissively as 'a Latin, from Chile'. Come to think of it, he didn't speak respectfully about Claudio Arrau either, so the Chileans are 0/2 in Gould's books. I once heard a later interview in which he acknowledges Tureck as an influence, and this seems plausible.
