# Filing Order

Fees and waits read from the linked official pages on 2026-11-16. Owner is the founder, counsel or an agent. Every step that says counsel is a legal judgment: confirm with counsel.

The IRS asks a corporation to form with its state before it applies for an EIN ([IRS EIN page](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number)). The New Jersey registration page lists the EIN before the certificate ([NJ Division of Revenue](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/revenue/gettingregistered.shtml)). Confirm the New Jersey order with counsel.

Filing Form 1023 within 27 months of formation makes the exemption effective from the formation date, and gifts become deductible back to that date once the letter arrives ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/exempt-organizations-general-issues-deductibility-of-contributions-while-application-pending)).

New Jersey lets a charity with $10,000 or less in yearly gifts and no paid fundraiser choose whether to register, and requires registration within 30 days of passing $10,000 ([NJ Consumer Affairs](https://www.njconsumeraffairs.gov/charities/Pages/charities-registration-information.aspx)). A marketplace passes that line fast, so register first.

New York requires the consent of the State Education Department for a corporation whose purposes include operating a school, a library or a museum ([DOS instructions, paragraph fourth](https://dos.ny.gov/not-profit-incorporation-instructions)). The New York draft keeps those words out of the purpose clause. Confirm with counsel.

## Public Support Test

A public charity under section 170(b)(1)(A)(vi) passes when one third or more of its support over five years comes from the general public, governments and other public charities. A charity above 10% can pass on facts and circumstances ([Schedule A instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i990sa.pdf), [IRS facts and circumstances test](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/exempt-organizations-annual-reporting-requirements-form-990-schedules-a-and-b-facts-and-circumstances-public-support-test)). A new charity keeps public charity status for its first five years when it expects to pass.

Founder gap funding and any one large donor count toward public support only up to a 2% cap per donor under that test. Many small marketplace gifts help the ratio. Royalties from the Polingual company count as support that is not public. Confirm the classification choice between 509(a)(1) and 509(a)(2) with counsel.
