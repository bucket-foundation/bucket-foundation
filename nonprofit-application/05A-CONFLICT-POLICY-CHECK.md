## Why It Matters Here

A 501(c)(3) must not operate for the benefit of its creator or of companies the creator controls, and none of its net earnings may reach a private individual ([IRS inurement and private benefit](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/inurement-private-benefit-charitable-organizations)). The founder will own the company that licenses Polingual from the Foundation. That contract is the main conflict this policy exists to manage.
