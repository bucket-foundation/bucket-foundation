### 1. Canon And Academy

The Foundation curates a canon of foundations across seven branches: mathematics, physics, chemistry, information and computation, biophysics, cosmology and mind. A named human reviewer signs off each record before it is served. Bucket Academy turns the canon into free lessons on the web and in mobile apps. The public reads and learns at no charge. Volunteers and the founder run it from the founder's home state, online.

### 2. Open Research Tools

The Foundation maintains open-source software that helps researchers search the canon and test hypotheses against it. The code is public under the MIT licence. Anyone may use or copy it.

### 3. Research Donation Marketplace

The Foundation will list research projects that its grants committee has approved. Members of the public give to the Foundation in support of a listed project. The Foundation owns each gift, decides each grant, and may redirect a fund when a project cannot go ahead.

| Rule | Practice |
|---|---|
| Who decides | A grants committee of independent directors approves each project and each payment |
| Who may receive | Public charities, and other researchers or organisations for a specific approved project |
| Grant terms | Written agreement, budget, milestones, reports, open publication of results, return of unspent or misused funds |
| Who may not receive | Directors, officers, the founder, their families and their companies |
| Donor rights | A donor picks a project fund when giving. A donor holds no account, no balance and no say after the gift. |
| Records | The Foundation keeps the application, the approval, the agreement, each payment and each report |

The control and discretion practice follows [Rev. Rul. 68-489](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-tege/rr68-489.pdf). The marketplace does not operate donor advised funds as described in the [Instructions for Form 1023](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023), Part IV line 11. Grants to foreign recipients, if any, follow the Part IV line 9 and line 10 checks. Design detail: `docs/specs/RESEARCH-DONATION-MARKETPLACE.md`.

### 4. Polingual Licence

Polingual is a language-learning app built on the same lesson engine as the Foundation's free programs. A separate for-profit company, owned by the founder, will sell Polingual as a paid app. The Foundation will license the rights it holds to that company for a royalty set at fair market value.
