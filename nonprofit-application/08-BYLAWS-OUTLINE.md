# Bylaws Outline

Outline for counsel to turn into bylaws. The board adopts the bylaws at its first meeting. Form 1023 asks for the adopted bylaws in the single PDF upload, after the articles ([Instructions for Form 1023](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023)). State law sets the defaults for anything the bylaws leave out: confirm each article with counsel.

## Articles

| # | Article | Content | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name and purposes | Legal name. Purposes repeat the articles of incorporation word for word. | Any drift from the articles draws an IRS question |
| 2 | Offices | Principal office, registered agent | No address in the public repository |
| 3 | Members | None. The board holds every member power. | Matches the articles |
| 4 | Board | 3 to 7 directors, 2-year staggered terms, election by the board | New York and New Jersey need 3 at minimum, see `10-FILING-ORDER.md` |
| 5 | Independence | A majority of directors hold no family, employment or business tie to the founder, to each other, or to the Polingual company | [IRS governance guidance](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-tege/governance_practices.pdf) |
| 6 | Meetings | 4 a year, remote attendance allowed, quorum is a majority of seated directors, written consent only when unanimous | Confirm remote and consent rules with counsel |
| 7 | Officers | President, secretary, treasurer. The treasurer is an independent director. | Confirm which offices one person may combine |
| 8 | Committees | Grants committee and audit committee, each with independent directors only | Grants committee runs the marketplace approvals |
| 9 | Grantmaking | The Foundation owns every gift, keeps control and discretion, and may redirect any fund that can no longer serve its purpose | [Rev. Rul. 68-489](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-tege/rr68-489.pdf) |
| 10 | Related parties | Any deal with a director, officer, founder or their company needs a vote of independent directors on comparability data, recorded in the minutes | [IRS rebuttable presumption](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/rebuttable-presumption-intermediate-sanctions) |
| 11 | Compensation | Directors serve unpaid. Any paid role is set by independent directors on comparability data. | Same source |
| 12 | Conflicts | Adopts `05-CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST-POLICY.md` and yearly signed disclosures | [Form 1023 Appendix A](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023) |
| 13 | Indemnification | To the limit state law allows, with insurance once the budget carries it | Confirm with counsel |
| 14 | Finance | Fiscal year end, two signers above a set amount, restricted funds tracked by project | Fiscal year is a founder input |
| 15 | Records | Minutes, document retention, whistleblower policy, public inspection of Form 1023 and Form 990 | Form 990 Part VI asks about each policy |
| 16 | Amendments | Two-thirds of seated directors. Purpose and dissolution clauses change only with an articles amendment. | |
| 17 | Dissolution | Repeats the articles clause | |

## Policies The Board Adopts With The Bylaws

| Policy | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Conflict of interest | `05-CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST-POLICY.md` |
| Grant and regrant | Project approval, recipient checks, grant agreement, reporting, recovery of misused funds |
| Gift acceptance | Cash and card gifts only at launch, no gift earmarked for a named individual, variance power stated on every gift page |
| Related-party licence | `11-FOR-PROFIT-LICENCE-TERMS.md` |

## Open Points For Counsel

| Point | Question |
|---|---|
| New York law | Which audit, related-party and conflict rules of the Not-for-Profit Corporation Law apply at this size? |
| Founder seat | May the founder serve as president and director while owning the Polingual company? |
| Grants committee | May the committee approve grants alone, or does the full board ratify each one? |
| Digital assets | Does a treasury or payout in stablecoin need its own policy? |
