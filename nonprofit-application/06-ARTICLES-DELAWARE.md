# Articles Draft: Delaware

Draft for counsel. Nothing is filed. Confirm every clause with counsel before filing.

The purpose, limitation and dissolution clauses follow the [IRS suggested language for corporations](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/suggested-language-for-corporations-and-associations-per-publication-557). The IRS organisational test needs a purpose clause limited to exempt purposes and a dissolution clause that keeps assets in charity ([IRS organisational test](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/organizational-test-internal-revenue-code-section-501c3)).

Bracketed fields are founder inputs. The repository is public, so addresses go into the state form only.

## Changes From The May Draft

| Change | Reason |
|---|---|
| Author payout percentage removed from the purposes | A fixed payout share belongs in a board policy. In the articles it reads as a private benefit commitment. |
| Named successor charities removed from the dissolution clause | The IRS clause is enough. The board picks recipients at dissolution. |
| Grantmaking purpose added | The marketplace regrants gifts, and the clause states the control and discretion rule of [Rev. Rul. 68-489](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-tege/rr68-489.pdf). |

The May memo recommends the founder's home state over Delaware. Delaware adds a registered agent, a second state filing and a second annual report.

## Certificate Of Incorporation Of [LEGAL NAME], Inc.

An Exempt Corporation.

1. The name of the Corporation is [LEGAL NAME], Inc.

2. The Registered Office of the Corporation in the State of Delaware is located at [REGISTERED AGENT STREET ADDRESS], in the City of [CITY], County of [COUNTY], Zip Code [ZIP]. The name of the Registered Agent at such address upon whom process against this Corporation may be served is [REGISTERED AGENT].

3. The Corporation shall be a nonprofit corporation. The Corporation is organized exclusively for charitable, educational, and scientific purposes within the meaning of section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or the corresponding section of any future federal tax code, including, for such purposes:

(a) publishing educational materials on the foundations of mathematics and the sciences and making them available to the public free of charge;

(b) building and maintaining open tools and open data that support scientific research and education;

(c) receiving contributions and making grants in support of scientific research and education, including distributions to organizations that qualify as exempt organizations under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, and distributions to other recipients for specific projects that further the purposes of the Corporation, over which the Corporation retains control and discretion as to the use of the funds; and

(d) any lawful activity that furthers the purposes above and is permitted to an organization described in section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

4. The Corporation shall not have any capital stock.

5. The Corporation shall have no members. The conditions of membership, if members are later admitted, shall be stated in the bylaws.

6. No part of the net earnings of the Corporation shall inure to the benefit of, or be distributable to its members, directors, officers, or other private persons, except that the Corporation shall be authorized and empowered to pay reasonable compensation for services rendered and to make payments and distributions in furtherance of the purposes set forth in Article 3 hereof. No substantial part of the activities of the Corporation shall be the carrying on of propaganda, or otherwise attempting to influence legislation, and the Corporation shall not participate in, or intervene in (including the publishing or distribution of statements) any political campaign on behalf of or in opposition to any candidate for public office. Notwithstanding any other provision of these articles, the Corporation shall not carry on any other activities not permitted to be carried on (a) by a corporation exempt from federal income tax under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or the corresponding section of any future federal tax code, or (b) by a corporation, contributions to which are deductible under section 170(c)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code, or the corresponding section of any future federal tax code.

7. Upon the dissolution of the Corporation, assets shall be distributed for one or more exempt purposes within the meaning of section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or the corresponding section of any future federal tax code, or shall be distributed to the federal government, or to a state or local government, for a public purpose. Any such assets not so disposed of shall be disposed of by a Court of Competent Jurisdiction of the county in which the principal office of the Corporation is then located, exclusively for such purposes or to such organization or organizations, as said Court shall determine, which are organized and operated exclusively for such purposes.

8. The number of directors shall be fixed by the bylaws and shall be no fewer than three.

9. The name and mailing address of the incorporator are [INCORPORATOR NAME], [ADDRESS, state form only].

Signed by the incorporator on [DATE].
