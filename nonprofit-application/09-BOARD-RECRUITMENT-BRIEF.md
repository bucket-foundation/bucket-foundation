# Board Recruitment Brief

The founder recruits two independent directors before the articles are filed. New York and New Jersey both want three names in the certificate (`10-FILING-ORDER.md`). Form 1023 Part I lists every director, and Part V asks about each one's ties to the organisation and to each other ([Instructions for Form 1023](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023)).

## Seats

| Seat | Profile | Independent |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Founder: president, technical lead | No |
| 2 | Working scientist or research librarian in a canon branch | Yes |
| 3 | Person with nonprofit board or finance experience: treasurer | Yes |
| 4, later | Nonprofit attorney or accountant, serving unpaid | Yes |
| 5, later | Researcher with open-science grant experience: grants committee chair | Yes |

## Independence Test

A director counts as independent only when every row is true. The IRS reads a board with a majority of such directors as a guard against private benefit ([IRS governance guidance](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-tege/governance_practices.pdf)). Confirm each candidate with counsel.

| Check | Rule |
|---|---|
| Family | No relation by blood or marriage to the founder or another director |
| Employment | Not employed or paid by the founder, AGFarms, DerbyFish or the Polingual company |
| Ownership | No equity, option or revenue share in any founder-owned company |
| Business | No contract with the Foundation or a founder-owned company |
| Grants | Will not apply for marketplace funding while serving, nor will family |
| Pay | Serves unpaid, expenses reimbursed |

## The Ask

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Term | 2 years |
| Time | 4 meetings a year, about 2 hours each, plus email votes |
| First-year work | Adopt bylaws and policies, approve the Polingual licence with the founder out of the room, approve the first marketplace projects |
| Duties | Care, loyalty and obedience to the mission under state law |
| Public record | Name appears in state records, Form 1023 and Form 990 |
| Protection | Indemnification in the bylaws, directors and officers insurance when the budget carries it |
| Conflict policy | Signs the yearly disclosure in `05-CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST-POLICY.md` |
