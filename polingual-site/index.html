Many languages, one daily habit

Polingual teaches a set of languages at once. Pick one language you know and the languages you want. Each word returns in every language of the set, in one mixed review queue, with shared roots shown side by side.

How it works

  • Placement: a short test in each language finds your level.
  • Spaced review: words come back right before you would forget them.
  • Speak and listen: hear each word, say it back, and get a pronunciation check.
  • Grammar in context: gender, conjugation and the past tense appear as you progress.

Start a 3-day free trial. Learn Spanish, French and Italian side by side, or build your own set from 29 languages. Your progress syncs with Bucket.
