### Inferences

- NeurIPS Atlanta is the first choice among the three sites for retained human capability and human-agent team measurement. Paris moves ahead if the main question becomes evaluation of the scientific enterprise. The programs support this judgment; no count of advisors expected onsite was verified.
- For 2027, select focused workshops once their rosters appear. A general ML meeting can offer a large candidate pool while a workshop supplies the research context for a conversation.
- Kyoto permits one trip covering IJCAI and ACL, with a gap between 13 and 17 August. Satellite attendance in Hengqin would consume that gap.
- ISWC, CPP/ITP and QIP serve distinct research branches. Prioritize them when those branches become the focus of an advisor search.

### Gaps

- No complete worldwide census is possible from these sources. This covers major relevant series plus verified current specialist meetings within the assigned AI/ETH scope.
- No booking inventory, visa eligibility or individual travel feasibility was checked.
- No count of compatible advisors attending any event was established. Publication count and venue size cannot substitute for that measurement.
- NeurIPS remaining inventory requires its registration flow. The rules allow a person to join one site waitlist.
- ACL 2027 home page lists workshops 18-19 Aug, while the shared call lists 21-22 Aug. Use the conference home page for planning and recheck the final program.
- AAAI organizer page for the AI Scientists evaluation workshop lists 22 or 23 Feb, but the parent conference workshop page failed to load. Treat workshop acceptance/slot as pending cross-check before travel.
